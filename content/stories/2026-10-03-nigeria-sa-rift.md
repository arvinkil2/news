Anti-migrant campaigns in South Africa set a September 30 "unofficial deadline" for undocumented foreigners to leave — no legal force, but real effects: Nigerian community representatives report Nigerians leaving, closing businesses, and keeping children home. Nigeria's parliament responded by suspending official visits and legislative engagements with South Africa. President Tinubu raised attacks on African nationals at the AU summit in Luanda, pushing the item onto the January 2027 Assembly agenda. Nigeria claims about 100 nationals killed in xenophobic violence since 2022.

The economics sharpen the dispute. Nigeria's Q2 GDP grew 4.43% on non-oil strength while South Africa's contracted 0.2%; their central banks are moving in opposite directions — Nigeria easing, South Africa hiking to prevent pass-through. South Africa holds local elections November 4 with Johannesburg's coalition as the stress test. A diplomatic freeze between a growing Nigeria and a stagnating South Africa lands directly on continental investment sentiment.
