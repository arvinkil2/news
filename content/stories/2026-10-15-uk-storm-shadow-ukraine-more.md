Britain is sending Ukraine additional long-range Storm Shadow cruise missiles to disrupt Russian military operations and counter attacks on civilian infrastructure, Bloomberg reported on Thursday, citing people familiar with the matter. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met Prime Minister Andy Burnham in London on Wednesday, when Britain reaffirmed its support for Kyiv and announced further military assistance following a recent deterioration in Ukraine's ties with Washington. The transfer has not been officially announced and the size of the shipment is unknown.
