Zillow Group has agreed to pay $212.5 million in cash to settle the consolidated securities class action captioned Jaeger v. Zillow Group, Inc., the company said in a Form 8-K filed on Wednesday. The settlement covers investors who bought Zillow Class A or Class C shares between August 5 and November 2, 2021, the period running into the collapse of the company's home-flipping business, and about $48 million of the payment is expected to be covered by insurance. The agreement contains no admission of liability or wrongdoing.

The money is due into an escrowed settlement fund within 20 business days after the court preliminarily approves the deal, and final approval, after notice to class members and a fairness hearing, is still outstanding. Zillow said the related shareholder derivative actions remain pending and are not affected by the settlement.
