Japanese utilities may step up spot LNG purchases after surging electricity prices made gas-fired generation profitable for the first time since the Iran war began. Japan's physical spark spread, the margin between power prices and the cost of the gas burned to produce that power, has turned positive on a seven-day average for the first time since the Middle East conflict started, according to Bloomberg data cited by OilPrice, and spot power prices jumped on Monday to their highest since August 2022 as forecast snow and colder weather lift heating demand while cloud cover cuts solar output.

The shift comes with spot LNG for North Asia at about $30 per MMBtu, roughly triple the level of a year ago, as Qatari cargoes remain disrupted by the Strait of Hormuz crisis. Analysts expect Japan to overtake China as the world's largest LNG importer this year for the first time since 2022, as high prices and comfortable storage limit Chinese buying.
