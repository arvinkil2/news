China's consumer price index rose 0.7 percent in October from a year earlier, down from 0.9 percent in September, and was flat on the month, the National Bureau of Statistics reported on Monday. Food prices fell 2.9 percent on the year, with fresh vegetables down 16.9 percent and pork down 8.6 percent, while prices excluding food and energy rose 0.8 percent and transport and communication prices rose 5.5 percent, including a 20.2 percent rise in vehicle fuel.

Factory gate prices moved the other way. The producer price index rose 5.7 percent from a year earlier and 0.7 percent on the month, with producer goods up 7.4 percent on the year as coal mining prices rose 36.6 percent, oil and gas extraction 35.0 percent and fuel processing 29.9 percent, while factory gate prices for consumer goods fell 0.1 percent. Across the first ten months of the year, consumer prices rose 0.9 percent on average and producer prices 2.7 percent.
