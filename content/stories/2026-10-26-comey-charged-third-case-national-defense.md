Former FBI Director James Comey has been charged for the third time with federal crimes, in a criminal complaint made public on Monday in the United States District Court in Washington, D.C., CNBC reported. The complaint charges him with knowingly communicating information related to national defense to a person not entitled to receive it and with conspiring with others to violate the law against sharing such information, alleging that, through one or more co-conspirators, he directed an unnamed individual to disclose national defense information to a reporter for a national news publication. Reuters, carried by Investing.com, reported that the charges accuse him of transmitting and conspiring to transmit national defense information in October 2016, according to the court documents. Comey responded in a video on Instagram that the Justice Department was back again and that he was still innocent, Reuters reported, and his lawyers have already asked a judge to dismiss the case. The complaint came to light hours after a federal judge in New York ruled that the Justice Department had unlawfully fired Comey's daughter Maurene Comey last year as an assistant United States attorney for the Southern District of New York. Comey was first indicted in September 2025 in Virginia on false statement and obstruction charges over his 2020 Senate testimony, a case later dismissed, and was indicted in North Carolina in late April over an Instagram photograph of seashells arranged as "86 47", which prosecutors treat as a threat against President Donald Trump and which he denies and is seeking to have dismissed.
