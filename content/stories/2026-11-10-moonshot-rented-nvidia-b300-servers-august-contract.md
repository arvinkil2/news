Moonshot AI, the Beijing lab behind the Kimi models, signed a contract in August to rent 32 Super Micro Computer servers equipped with Nvidia's B300 graphics processors, described as the most advanced AI processors available, according to a Bloomberg report on Tuesday cited by Investing.com and based on documents filed with Beijing regulators. No rental price or contract value was reported, and the report as relayed does not state where the rented servers are physically located.

The contract is a step beyond the talks this site reported on November 5, when CNBC said Moonshot had discussed renting Blackwell capacity from European cloud providers with no completed deal reported. Nvidia has been effectively blocked from selling its most advanced chips to China since at least 2022, and Chinese developers including Alibaba and ByteDance have been reported signing contracts for access to offshore Nvidia chips earlier this year, while Beijing promotes locally developed alternatives.
