Merck said the global, open-label TroFuse-015 study enrolled 473 patients and randomly assigned them to sac-TMT or one of four treatments of physician's choice, trifluridine-tipiracil, irinotecan, paclitaxel or docetaxel, and that at the final analysis overall survival improved statistically significantly and clinically meaningfully, with results generally consistent across subgroups regardless of TROP2 expression status. The trial did not meet its secondary endpoint of progression-free survival as assessed by blinded independent central review, objective response favoured sac-TMT but was not formally tested, and the safety profile was consistent with earlier sac-TMT studies with no new signals.

Sac-TMT is a TROP2-directed antibody-drug conjugate developed by Kelun-Biotech, which Merck licenses for development, manufacture and commercialisation outside Greater China. TroFuse-015 is the second positive Phase 3 readout in the TroFuse program, which has 18 ongoing global Phase 3 trials, and Merck said it will present the results at an upcoming medical meeting and share the data with regulatory authorities.
