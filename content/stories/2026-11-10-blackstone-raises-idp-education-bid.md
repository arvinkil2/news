Blackstone-managed funds have raised their takeover offer for IDP Education to A$3.00 a share in cash, a 20 percent increase on the A$2.50 offered in early September and the firm's third attempt after an earlier A$2.30 bid, both of which IDP rejected. The revised price values the Australian education company, co-owner of the IELTS English-language test, at about A$833.7 million (571 million US dollars) and sits roughly 36 percent above the stock's last close; IDP said its board is considering the proposal and has made no decision.

The bid lands on a business under pressure from tighter immigration and student visa policies in its main destination markets, which have cut international student flows and pushed IDP to reduce jobs, shrink its IELTS test centre footprint and guide to lower adjusted earnings for fiscal 2027. IDP shares have fallen more than 61 percent this year, and the stock rose about 23 percent on the revised offer.
