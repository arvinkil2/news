Speaking in St. Cloud, Minnesota, as part of the White House Task Force to Eliminate Fraud, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced new measures against fraud in the state and said Treasury has stopped nearly $190 million in funds tied to overseas fraud and returned more than $100 million to US victims. The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network issued an alert asking financial institutions to watch for suspicious cross-border payments involving US money services businesses, which handled more than $8 billion in potentially suspicious wire payments sent at the direction of foreign agents from 2021 to 2025, and a separate notice on detecting charities and non-profits used for fraud, terrorist financing and embezzlement. The announcement follows the sentencing this week of Salim Said, a co-conspirator in the Feeding Our Future scheme, to 30 years in federal prison for his part in defrauding a federal child nutrition program of $49 million.
