US Naval Forces Central Command issued an advisory on Wednesday through the Joint Maritime Information Center telling ships that do not need to travel between ports in the Gulf of Oman and the Strait of Hormuz to stay away from the area. The advisory, citing the current geopolitical situation, said vessels which do not need to transit the area are advised to remain well clear to avoid unnecessary delays to their voyage.

Bloomberg, the single source for the advisory in this run, gave no specific incident behind it and no timeframe for the guidance, reporting only that tensions are ratcheting up in the region.
