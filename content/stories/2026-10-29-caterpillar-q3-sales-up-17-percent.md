Caterpillar's third quarter came in well ahead of last year. Sales and revenues were $20.7 billion, up 17 percent from $17.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025, driven by $2.2 billion of higher sales volume and $663 million of favourable price realization. Profit per share was $7.13, against $4.88 a year earlier, and adjusted profit per share was $7.20, against $4.95. The operating profit margin was 20.4 percent, up from 17.3 percent.

Chairman and CEO Joe Creed said order rates were strong and the backlog is at a record. Sales rose in all three primary segments: Power and Energy up 18 percent to $8.8 billion, Construction Industries up 18 percent to $8.0 billion, and Resource Industries up 15 percent to $4.6 billion. Enterprise operating cash flow was $4.4 billion, the company ended the quarter with $7.9 billion of enterprise cash, and it returned $2.0 billion through share repurchases and dividends.
