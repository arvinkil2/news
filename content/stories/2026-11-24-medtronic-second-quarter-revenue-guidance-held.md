Medtronic reported revenue of $9.364 billion for its fiscal second quarter, ended October 30, 2026, an increase of 4.5 percent as reported and 6.5 percent on an organic basis. GAAP net income was $2.802 billion, or $2.20 per diluted share. On the non-GAAP basis the company guides to, net income was $1.700 billion and diluted earnings were $1.34 per share, down 1.4 percent from a year earlier and one cent ahead of the guidance midpoint.

Reuters reported that the revenue figure missed analyst expectations of $9.48 billion, while the adjusted profit edged past the average estimate of $1.33 per share. Cardiovascular sales, around 40 percent of the total, rose 10.2 percent to $3.79 billion, ahead of estimates. Neuroscience revenue rose 5.1 percent to $2.71 billion and medical surgical sales rose 3.9 percent to $2.25 billion, according to the same reporting.

Medtronic reiterated its fiscal 2027 guidance: organic revenue growth of 7.25 percent to 7.75 percent and diluted non-GAAP earnings of $5.94 to $6.00 per share. The company said the outlook holds despite the planned separation of its MiniMed diabetes business.

The print is steady rather than decisive. The growth engine is cardiovascular, the guidance is unchanged, and the gap between reported GAAP profit, lifted sharply year on year, and the lower non-GAAP figure is a reminder to read the reconciliation schedules before treating either number as the run rate.
