Libya’s central bank said unidentified individuals raided its headquarters in Tripoli on Thursday while Governor Naji Issa was away, and said it held all parties that participated responsible, singling out the defence ministry, which is tasked with protecting the building. Hours earlier, the parliament in Benghazi said it had accepted in principle the resignation Issa first announced in August, which it rejected at the time, and had appointed central bank board member Fauzi Abokhzam as interim governor pending a final decision.

Issa’s appointment in 2024 followed a political standoff that halted oil production during a financial crisis, and Libya remains divided between the UN-recognised government in Tripoli and a rival eastern administration backed by Khalifa Haftar, whose forces control Benghazi, where parliament sits. The Tripoli government had not commented on the reported raid.
