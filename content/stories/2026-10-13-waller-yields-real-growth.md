Speaking in Mumbai, Waller argued that AI investment, including data centre construction, is supporting demand now while its productivity payoff remains years away, and that changes in the Fed's overnight rate are not driving the 10-year yield much. His reading cuts against the market's war-driven inflation explanation for the bond selloff, one day before the September CPI print that will test which account is right.
