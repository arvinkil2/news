Qatar and the United States signed the two instruments on the sidelines of the eighth Qatar-US Strategic Dialogue in Washington, co-chaired by Qatar’s Prime Minister and Foreign Minister, Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman bin Jassim Al-Thani, and US Secretary of State Marco Rubio. The artificial intelligence acceleration partnership was co-signed at the Department of Commerce by Qatar’s Minister of State for Foreign Trade Affairs, Dr Ahmed bin Mohammed Al Sayed, and US Secretary of Commerce Howard Lutnick, and covers AI, advanced semiconductors and digital infrastructure, with reciprocal investment and private-sector partnerships.

Al Sayed also signed a memorandum of understanding on securing supplies of critical minerals and rare earth elements in the mining and processing sectors, after co-chairing the dialogue’s trade and investment session with US Under Secretary of State Jacob Helberg. Qatar’s Ministry of Commerce and Industry said both instruments sit under the Pax Silica initiative and aim to diversify critical minerals supply chains and increase joint investment in mining and processing.
