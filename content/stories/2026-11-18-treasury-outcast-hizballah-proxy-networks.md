The US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control designated the Wa Ta'awanu Association, a Lebanon based charity it says provides cover for Hizballah's activities under the guise of humanitarian services, and its founder Afif Abdel Rida Chouman. OFAC also designated Lebanese money changer Hassan Khechen, his brother Ibrahim Khechen, and Hassan Khechen's companies Gold Global Company in Lebanon and Sankari Gold in Türkiye, which Treasury says transfer funds and move gold bullion and cash for Hizballah on behalf of Iran's IRGC-Qods Force. The action was taken under Executive Order 13224, as amended, and all property of the designated persons in the United States or in the control of US persons is blocked.

Secretary of the Treasury Scott Bessent said the Iranian regime's attempts to infringe on Lebanon's sovereignty through terror proxies like Hizballah will not be tolerated. Treasury said Wa Ta'awanu provides a pretext for Hizballah's militant activities in the pilot zones established under the June 26, 2026 Trilateral Framework between the United States, Lebanon and Israel, preventing disarmament there.
