Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger will not support NextEra Energy's 67 billion dollar takeover of Dominion Energy without a six-year freeze on residential power rates and a tripling of the bill credits the companies have offered, according to comments filed with state regulators on Monday as part of the State Corporation Commission's review of the merger. She also asked for commitments to lower energy costs, protection for customers against disconnection, and more investment in energy efficiency and clean energy.

The filing raises what the companies must concede in Virginia. NextEra and Dominion first offered 2.25 billion dollars in bill credits across three states over two years, then expanded the Virginia residential credits to four years in September. Spanberger intervened in the case in August and had called herself skeptical of the deal without taking a final position. The Commission's decision is due in January under the current review clock.
