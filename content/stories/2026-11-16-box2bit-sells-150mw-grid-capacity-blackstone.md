Box2Bit, a subsidiary of Capital Energy, has withdrawn from the Epilon data centre project in Épila, in Spain's Aragón region, and is selling the 150 megawatts of grid connection capacity it obtained for the project to Blackstone, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The project had been declared of public interest by the Government of Aragon in early 2026 with a first phase scheduled for 2029, but Red Eléctrica denied Box2Bit's subsidiary CPD Edge Solutions consumption access at the Calatorao 220 kilovolt substation, and the mayor of Épila said the company missed its first payment to the owners of the roughly 100 hectares reserved for the site, whose land rights expired in May.

The capacity adds to Blackstone's Calatorao campus, which already has final planning permission for 430 megawatts in two phases. Box2Bit's two other announced Aragón campuses are also dead: the 3.4 billion euro Ebro Campus in Cariñena fell outside the government's grid investment plan, and the 1 billion euro La Charluca site in Calatayud failed to secure transmission access. No price for the transfer was disclosed.
