Nexent Capital has launched a new vehicle aimed at the physical side of the AI buildout. Nexent Capital ADGM Fund I is a closed-end fund targeting 1 billion dollars in capital commitments, expandable to a 1.6 billion dollar hard cap, dedicated to what the manager calls quantum-ready AI infrastructure and advanced energy investment.

The fund is structured as an Abu Dhabi Global Market domiciled Qualified Investor Fund, with a GIFT City, Gujarat based Alternative Investment Fund regulated by India's IFSCA acting as its feeder. Nexent Capital ADGM Limited is being established as the fund manager. The minimum investment is 500,000 dollars.

Capital is to be deployed across five strategies: infrastructure private equity in AI and data center assets, private credit and structured capital, special situations mergers and acquisitions, growth equity and private investments in public equity, and liquid public equity exposure within the AI and data center value chain. The Tyagi family office has made a 20 million dollar anchor commitment, 2 percent of the target size.

Nexent says it has line of sight to a pipeline of projects across North America and India, including data center and power assets sponsored by affiliates of Nexent Capital and the Tryfacta platform. Managing partner Adesh Tyagi said the fund is designed to finance power, land and compute at the scale the current buildout demands.

The timetable is conditional. The manager is still progressing toward authorization from the Abu Dhabi Financial Services Regulatory Authority, and targets a first close within about six months of that authorization, with a final close six months after that. Until authorization and a first close are announced, the 1 billion dollars is a fundraising target rather than committed capital available to projects.
