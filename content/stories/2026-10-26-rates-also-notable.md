The Bank of Canada decides on Wednesday, October 28 at 09:45 ET, with the Monetary Policy Report released at the same time. Nothing in Monday's pool moves Sunday's preview: a Reuters poll majority expects a 25 basis point hike to 2.50 percent, Rabobank expects a hold at 2.25 percent, and swap pricing implied about 85 percent of a hike. The full preview stands in yesterday's story and is not rewritten here.

The Bank of Japan decides on Friday, October 30, and its preview is likewise unchanged. The bank is expected to hold at 1.25 percent, to judge underlying inflation at its 2 percent target in the Outlook Report, and to leave December 18 as the meeting markets price for the next hike. Tokyo CPI for October, due Friday morning Tokyo time, is the last data check before the decision.

France and the ECB succession are unchanged on the day. Sunday's story recorded the French 10-year spread over Germany at 170 basis points at Friday's close, after 177 basis points on Thursday, and Bloomberg's framing of the rout as central to the ECB succession endgame. No new closing spread was published in Monday's pool, so the story is not updated; Thursday's ECB decision is the next event that can move it.

Britain and Canada's joint statement on defence financing, published by HM Treasury on Sunday, is covered in full as a politics story on this site. Its rates relevance is the funding channel: the two governments agreed to develop the Multilateral Defence Mechanism and the Defence, Security and Resilience Bank as two distinct but complementary institutions working in partnership, which settles the structure any future defence issuance or lending by either body will take.

The People's Bank of China published its September 2026 financial market operations report on Monday, and the release has now been read in full on the bank's own site. Government bond net financing in September was 1.54 trillion yuan, up 347.0 billion yuan year on year, while corporate bond net financing was negative 39.3 billion yuan. The bond market custody balance reached 210.8 trillion yuan at the end of September, the 10 year government bond yield stood at 1.68 percent, and the spread between the 10 year and one year yields was 46 basis points, one basis point narrower than a month earlier. Money market rates were steady, with DR001 averaging 1.38 percent and DR007 1.40 percent for the month, while interbank derivatives turnover rose 48.7 percent year on year to 7.4 trillion yuan. The yuan closed September at 6.7053 per dollar, 0.22 percent stronger than at the end of August. The release follows the bank's first three quarters data, reported on October 14, which put outstanding aggregate financing at 468.17 trillion yuan, up 7.1 percent year on year, with government bonds outstanding up 13.7 percent.

In India, the Prime Minister inaugurated the Reserve Bank of India's Data Centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, the central bank said. It is an institutional item with no policy content, recorded here because the inauguration is the bank's headline release on a day when its money market operations, covered in the full RBI story in this edition, are the rates story.

Rwanda's new central bank law, Law No. 033/2026 governing the National Bank of Rwanda, published on July 24, 2026, drew fresh analysis on Monday setting out its governance framework for monetary policy, financial stability and banking supervision. The item is single-sourced to that analysis and is recorded as background, with no rate implication claimed. The Financial Stability Board's Regional Consultative Group for Sub-Saharan Africa met in Maseru, the FSB said on Saturday, a standing forum with no policy action attached.
