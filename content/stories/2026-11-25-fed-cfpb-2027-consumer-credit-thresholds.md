The Federal Reserve Board and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau announced on Wednesday that Regulation Z (Truth in Lending) and Regulation M (Consumer Leasing) will generally apply to consumer credit transactions and consumer leases of $76,300 or less in 2027, based on the 3.9 percent annual percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers as of June 1, 2026. Private education loans and loans secured by real property, such as mortgages, remain subject to Regulation Z regardless of the loan amount, the agencies said.

In a separate joint release with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, the agencies said the 2027 threshold for higher-priced mortgage loans subject to special appraisal requirements will rise from $34,200 to $35,500, effective January 1, 2027, with loans at or below that amount exempt from the requirement for a written appraisal based on a physical visit to the interior of the home.
