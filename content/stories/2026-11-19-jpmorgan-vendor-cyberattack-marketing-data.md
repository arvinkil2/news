JPMorgan Chase said third parties who provide services to the firm were the target of a cyberattack, and that based on its investigation so far the attackers accessed certain marketing data held by those vendors, which may include some customer names and email addresses. The bank said there is no disruption to its services, that it will contact affected customers directly where appropriate and offer support including complimentary credit monitoring, and that there is no connection to the service disruption some customers experienced earlier this week, which it attributed to a software bug. The Verge reported that the confirmation followed a Cybernews report that a ransomware group had leaked email addresses belonging to about 16,000 JPMorgan customers, apparently linked to the bank's invitation-only Reserve program, and that Visa and Mastercard also confirmed attacks on a third party vendor, with Visa saying there was no indication payment data such as card numbers was affected and Mastercard saying a limited number of email addresses from a small subset of cardholders in a specific benefit program were exposed.
