Singapore based ST Telemedia Global Data Centres is in talks with banks for a syndicated loan of 3.1 billion Singapore dollars, about 2.4 billion US dollars, to finance its assets, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Standard Chartered, DBS, OCBC and United Overseas Bank are working on the proposed financing as global coordinators, mandated lead arrangers and bookrunners, on terms reported as a three year tenor at a margin of 165 basis points over the Singapore Overnight Rate Average, with an extension option of up to two years.

The talks follow the September completion of STT GDC's acquisition by a consortium led by KKR and Singapore Telecommunications, which agreed to buy the operator for 6.6 billion Singapore dollars in a deal part financed by a 5 billion Singapore dollar loan from the same Singapore banks. STT GDC, founded in 2014, operates data centres across 12 countries including India, South Korea, Japan, Malaysia, the United Kingdom, Germany and Italy.
