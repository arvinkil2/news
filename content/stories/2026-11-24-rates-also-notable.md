The Reserve Bank of India will conduct an overnight variable rate repo auction under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility on November 25, continuing the run of short operations through which it has been supplying liquidity at the start of the week. The November 23 operations were covered in full in yesterday's edition.

RBI Governor met the managing directors and chief executive officers of select non-banking financial companies, housing finance companies and industry representatives in Mumbai on November 23, and separately met chairpersons and chief executives of select urban cooperative banks the same day. The meetings are part of the central bank's regular supervisory engagement with the two sectors.

The premature redemption date for the Sovereign Gold Bond 2021-22 Series I falls on November 25, with the RBI publishing the redemption price for holders who exit early. The scheme's premature redemption windows open every six months from issue.

The South African Reserve Bank published its Composite Business Cycle Indicators for November 2026, its monthly read on the direction of the South African economy, and new deposit insurance requirements for applicant institutions under the Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI) framework.

The Central Bank of Nigeria's monetary policy decision is due today, November 24, at the end of the committee's two day meeting, with the policy rate at 23 percent going in. Yesterday's edition flagged the meeting; the decision itself had not been published when this edition closed.
