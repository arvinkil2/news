Japan's Ministry of Finance announced October 5 that Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama had signed a memorandum of cooperation with Australia establishing an annual Finance Ministers' Dialogue. The inaugural dialogue was held the same day, with economic security, supply chains and investment trends on the agenda.

A related Joint Strategic Financing Initiative document was also signed, aimed at strengthening cooperation on shared strategic economic objectives. Japan's Finance Ministry release (October 5) adds detail: the Joint Strategic Financing Memorandum of Cooperation was signed by Japan's Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Foreign Affairs and METI with Australia's Department of Finance and DFAT, explicitly framed around the growing importance of strategic finance for shared economic-security objectives and leveraging private-sector investment, expertise and innovation. It was signed at the inaugural Japan-Australia Strategic Investment Networking Forum, held as part of the 50th anniversary of the Basic Treaty of Friendship and Cooperation, bringing together around 20 Japanese and Australian policy and financing agencies to build cooperation on strategic Indo-Pacific investments (status: enacted MoC; forum inaugural session held). The move fits a pattern of Indo-Pacific financial institution-building parallel to defense cooperation, the kind of low-visibility step that later underpins coordinated export-control or critical-mineral financing action.
