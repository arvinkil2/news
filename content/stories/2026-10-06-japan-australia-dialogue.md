Japan's Ministry of Finance announced October 5 that Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama had signed a memorandum of cooperation with Australia establishing an annual Finance Ministers' Dialogue. The inaugural dialogue was held the same day, with economic security, supply chains and investment trends on the agenda.

A related Joint Strategic Financing Initiative document was also signed, aimed at strengthening cooperation on shared strategic economic objectives. The move fits a pattern of Indo-Pacific financial institution-building parallel to defense cooperation, the kind of low-visibility step that later underpins coordinated export-control or critical-mineral financing action.
