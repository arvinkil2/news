The Seoul High Court on Wednesday dismissed Yoon Suk Yeol's appeal and found him guilty on both counts of violating the Public Official Election Act, Yonhap reported. The court kept the first trial sentence of one year and six months in prison, suspended for three years, for two false statements made during the 2022 campaign: his denial at a December 2021 Kwanhun Club debate that he had introduced a lawyer to former tax office chief Yoon Woo-jin, and his January 2022 statement that he had never met the shaman Jeon Seong-bae together with his wife, Kim Keon Hee.

Under the election law, a sentence at or above the threshold for an election crime, once finalised, invalidates the election, and the candidate's party must return the campaign costs the state reimbursed. For the People Power Party that amount is 39.7 billion won, about $29.6 million. Yoon's legal team said it will appeal to the Supreme Court, and he has seven days to file.
