The Jordanian military said the State Security Court sentenced the three men, two Jordanian brothers and their relative by marriage, who fled after the October 23 attack and were arrested three days later, after finding them guilty of terrorist attacks resulting in death, weapons offences, conspiracy to commit terrorist acts and promoting the ideas of a terrorist organization. The defendants attended the closed hearing by video link from detention, and the court also sentenced two other men to six years in prison for selling weapons to the attackers, according to victims' families lawyer Amer Halaseh.

Jordan's Council of Church leaders announced the cancellation of all Christmas celebrations on Sunday as the community mourns, asking churches to make Christmas trees a symbol of hope and to light candles in memory of the dead.
