The Indonesian rupiah dropped 0.4 percent on Monday to 18,264 per US dollar, a fresh record low, after touching 18,271 in an earlier update, Bloomberg reported, as hawkish Federal Reserve expectations boosted the dollar and investors stayed sour on Indonesian assets over President Prabowo Subianto's policy uncertainty, the exits of several key policymakers, MSCI's warning it could downgrade Indonesia's equity market to standalone status, and Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka's resignation. Bank Indonesia said it would remain present in the market to maintain rupiah stability, and it has intervened regularly in the spot market and in offshore and onshore non-deliverable forwards while also buying government bonds in the secondary market to stem volatility. The currency has weakened about 9 percent this year, underperforming regional peers.
