Admiral Brad Cooper, who leads US Central Command, presented President Donald Trump with options for military action against Iran at the White House on Thursday, according to the Wall Street Journal as reported by Interfax. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and Joint Chiefs Chairman General Dan Caine took part in the meeting, and a senior US official told Axios the talks went positively, without giving details. Senator Chris Murphy, who said senators had been briefed on the administration's plan for a possible resumption of strikes, called the plan insane in a post on X and said it would lead to a new cycle of escalation without achieving Washington's goals; he also noted reports that Trump intends to resume strikes after the November 3 congressional elections.

The military track is running alongside a diplomatic one: Iran has received a letter from Vice President JD Vance and has not yet replied, Arab News reported earlier on Thursday.
