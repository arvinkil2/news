The Seoul High Court's Civil Division 18-3 ruled on Friday against Young Poong in its suit to invalidate Korea Zinc's new share issuance, finding the issuance was made within the scope necessary to achieve the company's business purpose. In August 2023 Korea Zinc issued 1,045,430 new shares to HMG Global, an overseas affiliate of Hyundai Motor Group, giving the group about 5 percent of the company; Young Poong argued the third party allotment excluded existing shareholders and served to entrench incumbent management, and the suit became the trigger for the two sides' management control dispute. The decision overturns the first instance ruling of June last year, which held that HMG Global could not be regarded as a foreign joint venture corporation and that the issuance violated Korea Zinc's articles of incorporation.
