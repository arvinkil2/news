Ford Motor Company was notified on November 17 that John Lawler, its vice chair, has elected to retire effective January 1, 2027, the automaker disclosed in a Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission. The filing, made under Item 5.02, gives no reason for the retirement and names no successor. Ford furnished a news release dated November 17 as an exhibit to the filing.
