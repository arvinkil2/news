Sanofi and Insmed. The rumored transaction covered in this beat on November 22 and November 23 is now a confirmed agreement, announced November 24: Sanofi will acquire Insmed for $175 per share in cash, about $41.0 billion in total. Insmed filed the agreement as an 8-K on November 24, accession 0001140361-26-045364. This beat does not duplicate the full story, which runs in today's infra-deals beat; for the life sciences read, the deal values Insmed chiefly on Brinsupri and its respiratory pipeline, and it removes the rumor discount that hung over both names last week.

Capricor watch. This beat's November 20 story covered the FDA's second rejection of deramiocel, and the company's conference call was scheduled for November 23. No new Capricor filing or release with a changed regulatory position was found in this run, so there is no new story today; the position stands as published on November 20 and updated on November 22.

House committee report on the US-China biotech race. Endpoints News reported that a House committee has issued a final report on the US-China biotech race. The item is noted here without figures, because no committee document was read in this run; if the report carries named company or policy consequences, it will be covered from the document itself.
