Cyclone Arnab started making landfall in India's Andhra Pradesh state in the early hours of Wednesday, bringing high winds and heavy rain that uprooted trees and disrupted power supply. The India Meteorological Department forecast winds of 125 to 135 kilometres an hour, gusting to 150, over the landfall area, and a storm surge of 1.5 to 2.5 metres above normal tide likely to inundate low lying areas of the state. Andhra Pradesh officials put evacuations at more than 84,000 people, schools were closed across coastal districts, and some train and flight services were cancelled, and the Andhra Pradesh chief minister's office said no loss of life had been reported so far.
