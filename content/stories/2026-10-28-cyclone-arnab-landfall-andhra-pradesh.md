Cyclone Arnab started making landfall in India's Andhra Pradesh state in the early hours of Wednesday, bringing high winds and heavy rain that uprooted trees and disrupted power supply. The India Meteorological Department forecast winds of 125 to 135 kilometres an hour, gusting to 150, over the landfall area, and a storm surge of 1.5 to 2.5 metres above normal tide likely to inundate low lying areas of the state. Officials said about 1.1 million people had moved into relief camps across Andhra Pradesh ahead of the storm, schools were shut in 12 coastal districts, and some train and flight services were cancelled, and the Andhra Pradesh chief minister's office said no loss of life had been reported so far.
