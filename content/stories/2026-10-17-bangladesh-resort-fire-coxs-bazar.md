A fire gutted a beach resort in southeastern Bangladesh on Saturday, killing 10 people and injuring four, an official said. The fire broke out at the Tuptup Resto and Resort at Inani in Cox's Bazar district, near the Bay of Bengal, and spread quickly through the three-storey wooden building in the morning, according to Syed Mohammad Morshed Hossain, deputy assistant director of Fire Service and Civil Defense in the district.

Children were among the dead, the official said, without giving details. Cox's Bazar is a popular destination for domestic tourists. The cause of the fire was not immediately clear.
