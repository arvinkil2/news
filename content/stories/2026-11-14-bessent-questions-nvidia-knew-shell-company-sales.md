Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC there is "a lot of leakage in the system" of US export controls, and that the real question in the Nvidia diversion case is whether the company knew its customers and checked that it was not selling to what he called obviously pass through shell companies. He said that if restricted GPUs were reaching China that way, Nvidia's internal controls were not robust, criticised general counsel Timothy Teter for pressing Commerce officials over the Singapore detention of Megaspeed's Alice Wong rather than doing more oversight, and said anyone at the company responsible should be held accountable. Bessent added that he does not favour tighter export rules over enforcing the existing ones, and that he is confident Nvidia's board will investigate thoroughly.
