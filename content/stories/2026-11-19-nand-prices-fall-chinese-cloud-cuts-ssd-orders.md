Chinese cloud service providers, grappling with GPU shortages, high inventories and fierce competition over AI token pricing, have reduced their total enterprise SSD orders for 2027 by a substantial amount, enough, TrendForce says, to outweigh the additional demand coming from US cloud operators. With manufacturers also raising output, the firm expects global NAND flash output, measured in storage capacity, to grow 25 to 30 percent in 2027 and the market to tip into oversupply.

TrendForce forecasts overall NAND contract prices falling 0 to 5 percent quarter on quarter in early 2027, with all product categories entering a downturn from the second quarter. Cheaper flash would not resolve the separate squeeze on DRAM supplies, where memory makers favouring high-margin AI products have already pushed PC prices up by double-digit percentages in Europe.
