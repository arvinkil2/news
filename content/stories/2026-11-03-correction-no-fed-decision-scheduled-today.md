This morning, Kilambi News published a preview of a Federal Reserve policy decision that it said would be announced at 2:00 PM Eastern today. That was wrong. The Federal Open Market Committee had no meeting scheduled for today, November 3, and it issued no statement. We have withdrawn the preview.

The decision the preview described, a hold in the target range for the federal funds rate at 3.75 to 4.00 percent, took place on October 28, when the committee approved it by a vote of 10 to 2, with Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack dissenting in favour of a quarter point increase. The committee's next meeting is on December 8 and 9, and the minutes of the October meeting are due on November 18.

The error came from preview coverage of the October decision that circulated again as if a further decision fell today, and we did not check it against the Federal Reserve's own calendar before publishing. We apologise for the error.
