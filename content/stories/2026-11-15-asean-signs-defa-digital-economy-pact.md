ASEAN's Digital Economy Framework Agreement was signed in Manila on Saturday, in conjunction with the 28th ASEAN Economic Community Council Meeting, and Secretary-General Kao Kim Hourn accepted the agreement from Philippine President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr., the ASEAN chair for 2026, at the opening of the 49th ASEAN Summit on Sunday. The secretariat calls the agreement a major milestone toward an open, secure, interoperable, competitive and inclusive regional digital economy. It is expected to enter into force in early 2027.
