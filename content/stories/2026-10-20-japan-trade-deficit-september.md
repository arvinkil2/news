Japan's exports rose 16.7 percent from a year earlier to 10,972,254 million yen in September 2026, while imports rose 18.9 percent to 11,508,812 million yen, leaving a trade deficit of 536,558 million yen against a deficit of 277,703 million yen in September 2025, the Ministry of Finance said in its provisional release. For the fiscal half year from April to September, exports rose 18.4 percent to 63,456,950 million yen and imports rose 20.4 percent to 66,275,384 million yen, for a cumulative deficit of 2,818,434 million yen.
