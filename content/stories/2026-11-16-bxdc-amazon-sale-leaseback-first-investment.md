Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust (NYSE: BXDC) reported third quarter net income of 15.6 million dollars, or 0.15 dollars per share, with funds from operations of 0.15 dollars per share and adjusted funds from operations of 0.17 dollars per share. In the same release the trust announced its inaugural investment: thirteen newly constructed data centers across two campuses, acquired through a sale-leaseback with Amazon.

Chief executive Nick Pell said the transaction delivers what the trust sees as compelling risk-adjusted returns and that the hyperscaler sale-leaseback channel doubles its total addressable market. A companion filing carries the contribution agreement behind the deal, structured through Jade ventures in Indiana and Mississippi with Amazon Web Services and Blackstone entities as members. The release does not disclose a purchase price, and the trust, which listed in May 2026 and is externally managed by a Blackstone affiliate, says it targets newly constructed, stabilized data centers leased to investment-grade hyperscale tenants on long-term contracts.
