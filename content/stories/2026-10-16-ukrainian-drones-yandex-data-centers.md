Ukrainian drones struck some of Yandex's biggest data centres over the past week, and strikes on just three facilities disrupted key services in major Russian cities, including shopping and taxi apps, Tom's Hardware reported. Yandex operates only a handful of major centres, while other providers supporting its cloud are mostly small, at a few megawatts each.

The analysis contrasted that concentration with Ukraine, where much infrastructure runs on distributed Western cloud hosting that can migrate abroad. It also qualified early claims about bot traffic, noting Cloudflare data showing bots largely migrated to other hosts rather than disappearing.
