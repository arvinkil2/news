ASEAN said the signing of the Enhanced Memorandum of Understanding on the ASEAN Power Grid has been completed by all member states, and the memorandum enters into force on completion of signing. The framework covers cooperation on infrastructure planning, institutional arrangements, policy and regulatory development, and access to financing, including for overland transmission lines and subsea power cables, with power market integration proceeding on a voluntary, stepwise basis.

The release links the memorandum to the ASEAN Power Grid Financing Initiative, which brings together ASEAN, the Asian Development Bank and the World Bank Group to support project preparation and mobilise financing for regional interconnections. Philippine Energy Secretary Sharon S. Garin said the Philippines, as 2026 ASEAN chair, would work with member states and partners to advance cross-border electricity trade and attract investment.
