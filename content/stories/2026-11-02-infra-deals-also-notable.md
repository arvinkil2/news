**UK housing bank anchors rental fund.** The National Housing Bank, a Homes England company, has committed up to 70 million pounds to FRESH, the Fiera Real Estate Single Family Housing fund run with Packaged Living, including a 51 million pound cornerstone investment. The fund has reached 200 million pounds of investment capacity and targets a portfolio above 1 billion pounds of professionally managed family rental homes, built through forward-funding and forward-commitment deals with housebuilders. The bank says it will invest up to 16 billion pounds over the next decade and aims to attract more than 50 billion pounds of private capital.

**South Africa counts SEZ pledges.** Trade, Industry and Competition Minister Parks Tau said 141 companies have promised investments worth more than 548 billion rand across South Africa's special economic zones, with more than 60,000 jobs attached. The Fetakgomo-Tubatse zone in Limpopo accounts for 52.6 billion rand of planned investment and a claimed 20,000 jobs over ten years. Single-sourced to Scrolla reporting carried by AllAfrica; pledges are commitments, not deployed capital.

**OPay pulls its US listing.** Nigerian fintech OPay has withdrawn its planned $304 million US initial public offering in response to a foreign regulatory inquiry, Premium Times reported. No replacement timetable was reported. The withdrawal leaves one of Africa's largest consumer fintechs private for longer and removes a test case for US listings by African platforms.

**Stanbic Bank Botswana sells 20 percent.** Stanbic Bank Botswana has launched a $94 million initial public offering to sell a 20 percent stake, BusinessDay reported. A bank listing of this size would be a material addition to the Botswana Stock Exchange and a partial exit for its Standard Bank Group parent.

**IDBI staff strike over Fairfax.** Employees of India's IDBI Bank struck over the proposed Fairfax Financial acquisition of a controlling stake, Bloomberg reported. Union opposition is now an active variable in one of India's largest bank privatisations, which this site has tracked since Fairfax's interest was first reported in October.

**Formation Bio's debut fund.** Formation Bio has raised $600 million for a debut fund targeting 10 to 15 clinical-stage drug acquisitions over three to five years, per its PR Newswire release of October 28 to 29, carried by AltAssets on November 2. The item belongs primarily to the life-sciences beat and is noted here only because the fund structure, buying drug assets rather than companies, is a capital formation story.

**Seaya reports another 600 million euro close.** AltAssets reported that Madrid-based Seaya has secured 600 million euros at the first close of a European growth fund targeting 1 billion euros, alongside a London office opening. That description differs from the 600 million euro Seaya Novo venture fund covered here on October 22, which was reported as fully raised; whether the growth fund is a separate vehicle or the same raise described differently could not be resolved from the paywalled AltAssets item, so it is flagged rather than written as a new fund.
