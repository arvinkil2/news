President Lee Jae Myung said on Thursday that Samsung Electronics' 6 trillion won ($4.48 billion) investment in a high-bandwidth memory (HBM) chip packaging plant in Asan would be followed by about 40 trillion won in investment in advanced equipment, Reuters reported. The follow-on equipment investment is worth about $30 billion at the rate implied by the report.

Reuters gave no construction or operating timeline for the plant and no jobs figure, and Samsung had published no matching statement as of this run. The announcement adds a named equipment spend to a packaging expansion that sits in the HBM supply chain for AI accelerators, where packaging capacity has been one of the binding constraints on shipments.
