Bank Indonesia launched the clearing bank at its Jakarta headquarters, where the People's Bank of China designated Bank of China (Hong Kong) Limited's Jakarta branch to provide renminbi liquidity, clearing and settlement, under a commitment signed with 12 cross currency dealer banks including Bank Mandiri, Bank Negara Indonesia, Bank Central Asia, ICBC Indonesia and China Construction Bank Indonesia. Local currency transactions between Indonesia and China reached the equivalent of 43.89 billion dollars in January to September 2026, more than three times the 13.19 billion dollars recorded for all of 2025, and China accounted for 83.49 percent of Indonesia's total local currency transaction volume. Governor Destry Damayanti said the clearing bank lets businesses settle trade with China directly in renminbi, reducing dependence on a third currency, and supports rupiah stability and the resilience of Indonesia's external sector.
