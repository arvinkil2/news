Apollo Global Management has submitted a non-binding bid for Uniper, Germany's largest gas importer, in a sale that could value the company at about 10 billion euros ($11.6 billion), Reuters reported via OilPrice. Apollo faces Equinor, Czech billionaire Daniel Kretinsky's EPH, a Brookfield and Canada Pension Plan Investment Board consortium, and a joint RWE-KKR bid; Berlin and its advisers are expected to narrow the field within weeks.

Germany seized 99.12 percent of Uniper through a 13.5 billion euro bailout in 2022 after Russia cut pipeline gas. The company now supplies about 190 terawatt-hours of gas a year in Germany with 5.9 billion cubic meters of domestic storage, and Berlin must reduce its holding to 25 percent plus one share by the end of 2028 under EU state-aid terms.
