Six Saudi citizens were injured and three homes and three vehicles were damaged after a Houthi projectile fell in a residential area in Najran on Sunday, a Civil Defense spokesman said. The spokesman said attempts to target civilian objects constitute a flagrant violation of international humanitarian law and that the established procedures for such incidents had been implemented. The agency had issued alerts of a potential danger in the city earlier on Sunday afternoon before saying the danger had passed.

Najran sits close to the border with Yemen. The projectile landed on the same day a Saudi National Guard helicopter crashed in the region, killing its two-officer crew, in the middle of a renewed Houthi missile and drone campaign against the kingdom.
