Japan intervened on three days in the third quarter and spent ¥15,399.3 billion doing it, the Ministry of Finance said in its quarterly disclosure dated November 9. The operations sold US dollars and bought yen: ¥7,975.6 billion on July 30, ¥5,552.3 billion on July 31 and ¥1,871.4 billion on August 3, 2026.

The quarterly release is the first official daily breakdown of the summer operations. It follows the ministry's monthly disclosure of zero intervention from September 29 to October 28, so the record now shows a concentrated three day defence in late July and early August, then no operations at all into the autumn.
