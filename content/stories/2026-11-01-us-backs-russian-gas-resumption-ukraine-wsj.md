The United States is backing a rapid resumption of Russian natural gas exports to Europe via pipelines crossing Ukraine, as part of talks aimed at securing an energy ceasefire before winter, the Wall Street Journal reported on Sunday, citing US, European and Ukrainian officials. Reuters, which carried the report, said it could not immediately verify it, and the report gave no volumes, prices or specific terms.
