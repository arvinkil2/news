The Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control added a group of Iran linked individuals and companies to its sanctions list on Thursday, including money exchange firms, two offshore banking companies and the Iraq based International Development Bank for Investment and Finance, and issued a general license authorizing the wind down of transactions involving that bank. OFAC also designated Cuba's state owned Banco Internacional de Comercio. In the same package, OFAC issued Russia related general licenses authorizing certain transactions involving Lukoil retail service stations located outside Russia and certain Lukoil entities in Bulgaria.
