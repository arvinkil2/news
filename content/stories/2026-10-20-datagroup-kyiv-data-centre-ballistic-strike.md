A ballistic strike overnight destroyed a Kyiv data centre owned by Ukrainian telecommunications company Datagroup, leaving the site “completely disabled”, with restoration and further operation “impossible”, the company said, as reported by Datacenter Dynamics. The wider attack destroyed a shopping and entertainment centre and damaged a medical facility and a 25-storey residential building, killing a 21-year-old woman and injuring eight other people, according to the Kyiv Post as carried in the report, and no Datagroup employees were harmed. Datagroup said it would continue to provide services to customers from backup sites.
