MSCI will remove Michael Saylor's Strategy and other companies from its global indexes after determining they more closely resemble investment funds, Reuters reported on Wednesday. MSCI had proposed last year to exclude digital asset treasury companies whose holdings represent 50 percent or more of total assets, then launched a broader review of what it called non-operating companies, which it said have fund-type characteristics that are excluded from its benchmarks.

Tokyo-listed bitcoin buyer Metaplanet and UK-based uranium buyer Yellow Cake are also on the exclusion list, and all three leave the MSCI USA and MSCI World indexes on December 1. Ethereum treasury company SharpLink is on the watchlist in the list MSCI published Wednesday. Strategy shares fell 2.5 percent in extended trading; the stock is down about 20 percent this year, against a 15 percent fall in bitcoin.
