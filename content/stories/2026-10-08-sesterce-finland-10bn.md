French artificial intelligence company Sesterce has announced plans to invest more than EUR 10 billion, about $10 billion, to create a European AI campus powered by renewable energy on the former UPM Kaipola paper mill site in Jämsä, Finland. The company said it has already secured an anchor customer for the site, which it did not name.

First-phase capacity will be 200 megawatts, expandable to 600 megawatts, and the company aims to build more than 1 gigawatt of AI capacity in Finland over the long term, reusing existing industrial sites for their grid, water, buildings and logistics connections.
