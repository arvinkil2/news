MP Materials reported revenue of $147.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 176 percent from $53.6 million a year earlier, plus $8.1 million of income under its price protection agreement with the US government. NdPr production rose 41 percent to 1,018 metric tons and NdPr sales rose 94 percent to 1,020 metric tons, Materials segment revenue reached $133.5 million, and revenue also included a $31.6 million sale of SEG+ material to a US aerospace and defense customer. Adjusted EBITDA was $41.1 million against a loss of $12.6 million a year earlier, the net loss narrowed to $20.7 million from $41.8 million, and the company said it has completed General Motors magnet qualification, with saleable magnet deliveries and revenue beginning in the fourth quarter.
