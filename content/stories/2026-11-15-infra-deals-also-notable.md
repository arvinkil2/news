The 81 Collection, a Chicago investment firm founded by the operators behind Tide Cleaners, announced on November 11 an additional close of its second fund at $82 million, double the size of its 2021 debut fund and taking total assets under management above $145 million. Fund II writes minority checks of about $8 million to $10 million into founder-owned businesses in services, manufacturing and the physical economy, targeting companies with $4 million to $20 million of revenue growing at 40 percent or more a year. It has made two investments so far, in Uptiv, a Michigan operator of technology-enabled infusion centers, and Terra, an AI-native freight brokerage.

Firmus Grid, an Australian AI and data center infrastructure company, is pursuing a $3 billion private capital raise after withdrawing its initial public offering last month amid weak investor demand, according to reporting summarised from Bloomberg and the Australian Financial Review on November 15. The plan is expected to combine new equity, debt and customer prepayments, with discussions involving Nvidia and Meta Platforms described as the most advanced element: a structure under which about 12.5 percent of contract value could be paid upfront to fund capacity. The raise is a plan reported by people familiar with the matter, not a closed financing.
