Ukraine's General Staff said its defence forces struck two facilities in Nizhnekamsk, Tatarstan, on the night of November 15: the Nizhnekamskneftekhim petrochemical complex, part of the Sibur holding and one of the largest in Europe, where explosions and a fire were reported, and the TAIF-NK refinery, where it said the plant's only primary oil processing unit, ELOU-AVT-7, was hit and a fire recorded. The extent of damage at both was still being clarified, the General Staff said, and it also claimed a strike on the Monolit aircraft and helicopter components enterprise in Trubchevsk, Bryansk region.

Russian reporting confirmed an attack on the city without confirming the plant hits. Interfax, citing Kuibyshev Railway, reported a mass drone attack on the Zakamsk zone of Tatarstan on Sunday morning in which industrial facilities and a residential house were damaged and three people were injured, and said falling drone debris set an ethylbenzene tank car alight at Biklyan station in Nizhnekamsk at 7:00 a.m. Moscow time, suspending train movement through the station; the fire was later extinguished and movement resumed, with no casualties from the tank car fire itself.
