In October 2026 the Italian consumer price index rose 1.0 percent on the month and 5.5 percent on an annual basis, up from 4.2 percent in September, Istat reported in its final release. The flash estimate was 5.4 percent. The harmonised index of consumer prices rose 1.2 percent on the month and 5.6 percent year over year, confirming the flash estimate.

The acceleration was mainly due to energy. Regulated energy product prices rose 61.6 percent year over year, up from 25.8 percent in September, and non regulated energy products rose 29.8 percent, up from 22.5 percent. Unprocessed food inflation rose to 7.5 percent from 5.5 percent, and transport services inflation doubled to 3.2 percent from 1.6 percent. Regulated energy prices alone rose 20.3 percent in the single month.

Underlying measures moved up more slowly. Core inflation, excluding energy and unprocessed food, was 2.0 percent, up from 1.6 percent in September, and inflation excluding energy was 2.5 percent, up from 2.0 percent. Grocery and unprocessed food prices rose 0.8 percent on the month and 2.3 percent on the year, down from a 1.5 percent annual pace in the previous month on Istat's presentation.

The composition matters for the policy read. Headline inflation at 5.5 percent with core at 2.0 percent is an energy shock first and a broadening process second, the same pattern Destatis reported for German producer prices this week, and it is the backdrop against which markets price a further ECB increase in December.
