Honda's operating profit for the July to September quarter fell 39 percent from a year earlier to 118 billion yen, well below the 250 billion yen median forecast in an LSEG poll of nine analysts, Reuters reported. The quarter carried 484.8 billion yen of electric vehicle restructuring charges, part of a 520 billion yen (3.29 billion dollars) total Honda still expects for this financial year, after more than 9 billion dollars of charges last year pushed it into its first annual loss in nearly 70 years. Chief Financial Officer Masao Kawaguchi said Honda wants to settle the restructuring costs as much as possible within this financial year.

The raise rests on hybrids. US hybrid sales rose 9 percent in the quarter and accounted for half of global retail sales of 823,000 vehicles, and on Monday Honda announced a 3 billion dollar plant in Ohio to build 240,000 hybrids a year from 2029. Honda cut its full year global sales forecast by 7 percent to 3.16 million vehicles, mainly on falling sales in China. Its own first half release puts revenue at 12.04 trillion yen, up 13.2 percent, and operating profit at 648.7 billion yen, up 48.1 percent.
