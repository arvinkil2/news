A protective barrier collapsed at the partially constructed Tarbela Dam T-5 hydropower project in Pakistan's Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, triggering a flood water surge, Al Jazeera reported on Monday. A worker who was swept away was later rescued, and no casualties were reported.

The report gives no indication of how far the water spread or what damage the site sustained, and no official statement accompanied the footage. Tarbela, on the Indus River, is Pakistan's largest power station, and the T-5 extension under construction there is designed to add generating capacity to the existing complex.
