President Donald Trump announced on Monday that anybody reporting voter fraud in past elections, or in the upcoming 2026 midterm election, will be paid $1,000,000 upon the arrest and conviction of those who created the fraud, The Hill reported. The offer was made in a Truth Social post about a week before the midterms, and the report named no funding source or agency that would pay or administer it. The announcement follows Trump's summer remarks claiming serious vulnerabilities in United States election infrastructure, and his statement on Friday that he ordered the Justice Department to sue to prevent certification of Alaska's Senate election if Senator Dan Sullivan loses, a suit the department filed on Thursday.
