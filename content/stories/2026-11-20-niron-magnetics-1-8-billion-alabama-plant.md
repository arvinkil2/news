Niron Magnetics said on Friday it will build a $1.8 billion, 1.6-million-square-foot manufacturing campus at the Westgate Super Site industrial park in Dothan, in southeast Alabama. The company said the 10,000-ton plant will be its first high-volume manufacturing facility and is expected to create 700 high-wage jobs within five years, after a site search covering 22 states.

The Minneapolis-based company is commercialising iron nitride permanent magnets made from abundant domestic materials, presented as an alternative to conventional neodymium-based magnets that depend on rare earth supply chains. Alabama will be its second plant: Niron said its first full-scale plant opens in Sartell, Minnesota in 2027 with capacity of up to 1,500 tons a year, and in August it received a conditional commitment from the Department of War's Office of Strategic Capital for a direct loan of up to $150 million toward the Minnesota plant.
