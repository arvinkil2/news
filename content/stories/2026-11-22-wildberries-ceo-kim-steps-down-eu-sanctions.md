Tatyana Kim, the head of Russia's largest online retailer Wildberries, has stepped down, Russian media reported on Saturday, days after the European Union added her to its Russia sanctions list, Reuters reported. The company has appointed Alexei Kulikov as its new chief executive, the TASS and RIA news agencies reported, citing a corporate filings register.

Kim faces an asset freeze in the EU, as well as travel and transaction bans, after Brussels added her to its sanctions list on November 16. Wildberries said the EU sanctions on Kim would not affect its operations, as it had no assets in the EU and did not conduct business there.

According to the agencies' reporting cited by Reuters, the change of chief executive would not affect day to day operations, and Kim will continue to oversee the strategic development of Wildberries' holding company, RWB Group.

No official connection between the sanctions listing and the leadership change has been established. The sequence is nonetheless the clearest corporate consequence so far of the November 16 EU package for a major Russian consumer business.
