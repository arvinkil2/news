Representative Jimmy Gomez, a California Democrat, will resign from Congress after the House Ethics Committee released a 19-page report on Monday finding substantial evidence that he engaged in sexual misconduct, The Guardian reported. The report found that Gomez made unwelcome sexual advances toward a House staffer on two occasions, including sexual contact and an incident in which he was forceful with her in his congressional office. Gomez had already lost his re-election bid.
