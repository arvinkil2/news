North Korea's Foreign Ministry summoned Sylvain Astier, head of the Swiss diplomatic office in Pyongyang, declared him persona non grata, ordered him to leave the country within 48 hours and demanded the office close, the state news agency KCNA reported on Wednesday. Pyongyang said the move answered Switzerland's decision to add the Songdowon International Children's Camp to its sanctions list over the camp's alleged role in the transfer of children from Russian-occupied areas of Ukraine, calling the listing an overtly hostile act. Switzerland's foreign ministry said its sanctions policy is independent of the office, which reopened last month after a closure dating to March 2020, and that Bern aims to maintain channels of dialogue.
