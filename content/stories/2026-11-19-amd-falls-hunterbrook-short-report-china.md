AMD shares fell about 8 percent on Thursday after Hunterbrook Media disclosed a short position and published a report alleging the chipmaker has, in its words, its own China problem. Hunterbrook claims that after the United States blacklisted Chinese server maker IEIT Systems, the company kept receiving newer AMD firmware, the software that lets AMD chips run in servers, and it says AMD bills over 20 percent of its revenue to customers in China and Hong Kong and depends on a Chinese partner to finish most of its chips. AMD did not immediately respond to a request for comment, and CNBC said it had not independently verified the claims.
