India signed a $5.7 billion contract with domestic companies on Monday to build eight next-generation corvettes for anti-submarine, anti-surface and coastal defence operations, replacing an aging fleet of single-role corvettes. Five of the 127-metre stealth ships will be built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers and three by Goa Shipyard, in what the Defense Ministry called the first project designed by the Indian Navy and built in domestic state-owned yards. The order sits inside a wider expansion, including a planned six advanced submarines with Germany and 26 French Rafale Marine aircraft, as the navy aims to grow from 145 ships and submarines to more than 200 by 2035.
