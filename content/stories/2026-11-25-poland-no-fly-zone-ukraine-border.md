Poland will set up a no-fly zone inside Polish territory along the border with Ukraine, Deputy Defense Minister Cezary Tomczyk told reporters on Wednesday, saying a government ordinance is already being prepared and should enter into force at the end of this year. The zone will be several dozen kilometres wide, on top of the strict limits Poland already places on air traffic at its border, and follows repeated Russian airspace incursions.

Tomczyk said Warsaw will also urge its allies, the entire NATO alliance, to establish the same principle along the whole eastern flank of NATO.
