The state-owned Shipping Corporation of India (SCI) is seeking to acquire up to 22 new vessels in a fleet expansion spanning eight very large gas carriers, six 18,000-TEU containerships, two Suezmax crude tankers and up to six 8,000-TEU containerships, according to its tender notices. The tenders steer construction to India: six of the eight gas carriers and four of the six largest containerships would be built domestically, and Indian yards hold a right of first refusal to match foreign bids on the smaller ships and tankers. Bids for the containerships and Suezmax tankers are due in December, with the gas carrier tender following in January 2027; no vessels have been ordered yet.
