The Commission said the deal as first notified would have removed an important competitor in tampon supply in Czechia, Estonia and Slovakia, where few other suppliers are present. To settle those concerns, Kimberly-Clark will divest its EEA tampon and feminine hygiene brands, Kotex including the Lidie sub-brand, Camelia and Record, together with intellectual property, know-how and key employees, to Dilek, a Turkish consumer goods distributor the Commission approved as purchaser in the same decision, with an independent trustee to monitor compliance. The transaction, notified on August 25, was cleared in Phase I, and it adds Kenvue's Listerine, Nicorette, Neutrogena and o.b. brands to a Kimberly-Clark portfolio built on Huggies and Kleenex.
