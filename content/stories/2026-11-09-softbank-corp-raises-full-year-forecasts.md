SoftBank Corp. reported revenue of 3.7987 trillion yen for the six months to September 30, 2026, up about 12 percent from 3.4008 trillion yen a year earlier, with operating income up 2.1 percent to 642.1 billion yen and net income attributable to owners up 4.5 percent to 364.5 billion yen. Enterprise revenue rose 12 percent to 538.9 billion yen, led by cloud and AI revenue up 37 percent to 163.8 billion yen on AI computing infrastructure demand, while consumer revenue rose 9 percent to 1.6025 trillion yen as mobile ARPU rose 230 yen year on year in the second quarter and smartphone churn fell to 1.31 percent.

The company raised its full-year forecasts, lifting revenue to 7.9 trillion yen from 7.5 trillion yen, operating income to 1.16 trillion yen from 1.10 trillion yen and net income attributable to owners to 610 billion yen from 560 billion yen, with the net income revision partly reflecting an expected gain from the sale of SB Energy interests. The annual dividend forecast was unchanged at 8.80 yen per share.
