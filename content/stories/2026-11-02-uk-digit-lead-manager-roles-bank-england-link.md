HM Treasury said on Monday that Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, Morgan Stanley, NatWest and RBC Capital Markets, appointed as joint lead managers for DIGIT in October, have now been allocated specific roles covering documentation, bookbuilding, investor communications and helping investors join the digital platforms through which the bond will be available. The Bank of England has confirmed regulatory approval for a link between HSBC Orion, which will act as the issuer depository, and LSEG's investor depository, which the Treasury called a world first and said would give investors a choice of infrastructure for accessing the pilot. DIGIT will be digitally native and short dated, issued on a platform inside the Digital Securities Sandbox with on-chain settlement and sitting outside the government's main debt management programme, with the pilot issuance expected by the first quarter of 2027.
