A FedEx McDonnell Douglas MD-11 freighter arriving from the company's Memphis hub overran Runway 26R at Miami International Airport at about 8:40 p.m. on Wednesday, in heavy rain and thunderstorms, Miami International Airport said. The Federal Aviation Administration issued a temporary ground stop for all flights on a busy holiday travel day; the airport said all runways were closed except the south runway, Miami-Dade Fire Rescue responded, and there were no reported injuries, with two pilots on board. The ground stop has since been lifted.

FedEx said Flight 910 from Memphis "experienced an issue upon landing" on Wednesday evening, that its crew was safe, and that it was coordinating with investigating authorities. FreightWaves reported the aircraft missed the runway's landing zone, turned slightly left after leaving the runway to avoid a localizer antenna and stopped near the perimeter fence, and that the jet will likely stay idle for an investigation and damage inspection during the peak shipping season.
