The European Commission said Presidents von der Leyen and Costa deeply regret the Libyan Parliament's ratification of the memorandum of understanding between Libya and Turkiye on delimitation of maritime jurisdictions in the Mediterranean Sea. The statement said the agreement infringes the sovereign rights of third states, does not comply with the law of the sea and is null and void, and that the European Council's 2019 position remains unchanged. It reaffirmed solidarity with Greece and Cyprus and repeated the EU position against outside interference in Libya.
