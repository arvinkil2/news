President Donald Trump told a rally in Greenville, North Carolina on Tuesday that the Department of War will invest $1.5 billion to produce semiconductor chips in the state, without providing details on how the investment would be made, Reuters and The Hill reported. At the same rally, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick announced that Sibelco, an industrial minerals mining and processing company, will build a quartz manufacturing plant in North Carolina to support semiconductor production, an investment he put at hundreds of millions of dollars, also without details. Neither announcement named a site within the state, a construction timetable, a capacity figure or a jobs total.
