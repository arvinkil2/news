Aon plc said on Monday it has completed the acquisition of USI, a provider of property and casualty, employee benefit, personal risk and retirement solutions for the middle market, for a total purchase price of $17.0 billion. The deal, first announced on August 31, establishes what Aon calls the premier platform in the US middle market segment and builds on its 2024 acquisition of NFP.

USI is the tenth largest US insurance broker, with approximately $3 billion in annual revenue and more than 10,500 team members across nearly 200 US offices. It will go to market as "USI, an Aon company," led by Mike Sicard, USI's former chairman and chief executive, who now serves as President of Aon and global chief executive of the middle market across Aon, NFP and USI. Aon chief executive Greg Case said the combination sets a new standard of capability and client service for middle market clients.

Aon funded part of the cash consideration by drawing the full $4.0 billion available under its delayed draw term loan facilities, split between two year and three year tranches of $2.0 billion each, according to coverage of its closing filing. The August announcement put the net purchase price at about $16.7 billion after roughly $278 million of tax attributes and valued the deal at about 14.5 times synergized trailing adjusted EBITDA.

The company said it will update its outlook for 2027, inclusive of USI, when it reports fourth quarter earnings in early 2027, and will report regularly on progress toward the $395 million of expected net adjusted EBITDA synergies it identified for the acquisition, which it expects to substantially realise between closing and 2029. The August terms projected the deal to be accretive to adjusted earnings per share in 2028. BofA Securities and Citi served as financial advisors to Aon on the transaction.
