Mossad director Roman Gofman, one of Benjamin Netanyahu's closest confidants, is in Washington on Friday for talks with senior Trump administration officials about the war in Iran, including a meeting with CIA director John Ratcliffe, Axios reported, citing three sources with knowledge of the matter. Gofman is also expected to travel to Miami to meet White House envoy Steve Witkoff.

The visit is a second channel for pressure Netanyahu has been applying directly. Trump has so far leaned the other way in public, telling CNN on Sunday that more strikes are "highly unlikely" if Iran signs a "very strong agreement" on its nuclear programme and the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, though he would strike "if necessary".
