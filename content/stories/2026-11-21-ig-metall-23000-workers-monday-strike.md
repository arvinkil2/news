IG Metall has called more than 23,000 workers at 19 companies in northern Germany out on a 24-hour strike on Monday, among them Mercedes-Benz, the shipbuilder Meyer Werft and the wind turbine maker Siemens Gamesa, the union announced on Sunday. The call widens Friday's warning strikes, which drew about 12,300 employees at 17 facilities. The union is seeking a 5 percent pay increase and is offering employers an exit from the sector-wide agreement in return for binding commitments on investment, production sites and job security, while employers have offered to hold pay flat for 20 months and then raise it 1.3 percent from July 2028.
