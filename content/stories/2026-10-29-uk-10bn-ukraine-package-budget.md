The UK will give a further 10 billion pounds of economic and military support to Ukraine, in what the Treasury calls the largest ever package of financial backing for the country. Prime Minister Andy Burnham and Chancellor John Healey told President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of the decision on the morning of the Budget, presenting it as vital for collective security as Ukraine heads into winter.

The composition matters more than the headline. Seven billion pounds is economic support made available over three years, including up to 5.5 billion pounds of World Bank guarantees and a 500 million pound grant contribution to the World Bank International Development Association's crisis facility, which the Treasury says mobilises 1.5 billion pounds of support. Guarantees and credit capacity are contingent backing, not cash transferred on announcement.

The military element is smaller and faster. The package includes 500 million pounds of additional military support for Ukraine's air defences this winter, in response to Russian attacks on critical national infrastructure, and a 3 billion pound increase in UK Export Finance capacity for Ukraine, prioritised for military support, taking total UKEF cover to 6.5 billion pounds. It sits on top of the 3 billion pounds a year already committed for as long as it takes.

Including this announcement, the government puts total UK commitments to Ukraine at 35 billion pounds: 16 billion in military support, up to 12.6 billion in non-military support, and the 6.5 billion pound export finance cover limit. Burnham said the package should leave no one in doubt of Britain's backing, and Healey said the commitment to Ukraine's sovereignty and defence remains unshakeable in the fifth year of the war.

For Kyiv, the structure means winter air defence help now and a financing backstop for public services over three years. For London, the fiscal cost depends on whether the guarantees are called, which is why the same announcement read as a foreign policy commitment in this beat and as a contingent liability inside the Budget in the rates beat.
