Starbucks has tapped Citigroup and Morgan Stanley to prepare for a potential sale of its Japan business for several billion dollars and has started sounding out interest from strategic and financial investors, Bloomberg News reported on Tuesday, citing people familiar with the matter. A formal process could start as soon as this year, though deliberations are ongoing and may not lead to a deal.

Reuters, which relayed the report, said it could not immediately verify it; Reuters reported in September that Starbucks was weighing the sale of a majority stake in the Japan business, its biggest overseas company-operated market, in a deal that could value it at about 3 billion dollars.
