Revolut said it has completed a fundraising process that values the company at $115 billion, above the $75 billion valuation in its earlier process, in a round co-led by Glade Brook Capital Partners, Coatue, Durable Capital Partners, accounts advised by T. Rowe Price Associates and Baillie Gifford. The company disclosed 2025 revenue of $6 billion and pretax profit of $2.3 billion, and said it launched banks in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Australia and New Zealand in 2026 and secured banking licences in France, Colombia and Argentina. A separate share sale gave employees liquidity, and the announcement follows reports of a possible dual listing in New York and London with an initial public offering pushed to 2028.
