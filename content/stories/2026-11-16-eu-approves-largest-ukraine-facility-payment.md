The Council of the European Union adopted a decision on Monday that will allow Kyiv to soon receive just under 8 billion euros under the Ukraine Facility, of which around 5 billion euros comes from resources made available from the Ukraine Support Loan in 2026. The Council called it the largest-ever single disbursement to Ukraine under the facility, which has more than 50 billion euros in grants and loans available through 2027.

The payment follows Ukraine's fulfilment of over 20 target steps covering the judicial system, the fight against corruption and money laundering, financial markets, public asset management, energy, digital transformation and other reform areas under the Ukraine Plan. Once this ninth disbursement is made, Ukraine will have received over 40 billion euros under the plan since mid-2024.
