US job openings were little changed at 7.5 million in September, a rate of 4.5 percent, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on Tuesday. Hires changed little at 5.4 million and total separations changed little at 5.3 million.

Within separations, quits increased to 3.4 million, with the quits rate little changed at 2.1 percent, while layoffs and discharges were little changed at 1.6 million, a rate of 1.0 percent. The bureau revised August openings up by 111,000 to 7.2 million and revised August layoffs up by 127,000 to 1.8 million.
