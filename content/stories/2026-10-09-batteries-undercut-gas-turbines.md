Four-hour duration batteries are now cheaper than open-cycle gas turbines in every one of the 43 markets Wood Mackenzie surveyed, and the consultancy expects battery electricity costs to keep falling while turbine-generated electrons get more expensive. The shift lands as AI data-center developers have driven gas-turbine prices up with two-to-four-year procurement waits for open-cycle units and backlogs into the early 2030s for closed-cycle ones. Solar remains the cheapest new power in every market surveyed, though North American solar is under pressure from tariffs and import restrictions.
