SQM reported net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 of $1,569.0 million, or $5.49 per share, an increase of 288.0 percent from $404.4 million, or $1.42 per share, in the same period last year, with revenue up 95.0 percent to $6,340.5 million and gross profit up 233.6 percent to $3,002.3 million. Third quarter net income was $544.2 million, or $1.91 per share, and lithium and derivatives revenue for the nine months totaled $4,481.8 million. The company cited positive volume growth in lithium and iodine and higher realized prices across its main business lines.
