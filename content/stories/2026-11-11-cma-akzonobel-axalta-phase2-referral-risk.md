The Competition and Markets Authority said on November 11 that, on the evidence currently available, it is or may be the case that the anticipated merger between Akzo Nobel N.V. and Axalta Coating Systems Ltd. may be expected to result in a substantial lessening of competition within a market or markets in the United Kingdom. The CMA launched its merger inquiry on September 16, 2026, after an invitation to comment that ran from April 16 to May 1.

The merger will be referred for an in-depth phase 2 investigation unless the parties offer an acceptable undertaking to address the competition concerns. The CMA published a summary of its phase 1 decision with the announcement.
