Ukraine's air force said Russia attacked from the evening of Saturday, October 17 with one Iskander-M ballistic missile, launched from Russia's Voronezh region, and 180 strike drones launched from Orel, Bryansk, Kursk, Shatalovo and Primorsko-Akhtarsk in Russia and from occupied Donetsk and Hvardiiske in Crimea, Ukrainska Pravda reported. The air force said air defences had shot down or suppressed 152 drones by 08:00 on Sunday, Kyiv time, including 16 jet-powered Shaheds, and that missile or drone hits were recorded at 20 locations and falling debris at four more. It said the attack was still under way at that hour, with about ten drones still in Ukrainian airspace.
