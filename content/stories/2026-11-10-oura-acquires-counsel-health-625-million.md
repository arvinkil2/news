Oura announced on Tuesday that it is acquiring Counsel Health, an AI-native primary care company, for 625 million dollars. Counsel's service, which pairs a chat-based medical AI with access to licensed physicians, was integrated into the Oura app in June, and the companies plan to expand it across preventative, acute, specialty and chronic care. Counsel chief executive Muthu Alagappan will join Oura as chief clinical AI officer along with the company's 70 employees, and TechCrunch reported the deal is expected to close before the end of 2027.
