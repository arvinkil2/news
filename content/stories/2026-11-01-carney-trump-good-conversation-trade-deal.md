Prime Minister Mark Carney said on Sunday that he had a "good conversation" with President Donald Trump on the future of Canada-U.S. trade. In a post on X, Carney said the talk built on recent constructive discussions on the outlines of a mutually beneficial trade deal that would create jobs, boost affordability for families, and fully respect Canada's languages, culture and sovereignty, and that teams from both countries "will now be working in the coming weeks to deliver that outcome." The conversation follows Canada's walkout from formal talks in August over what Carney called unreasonable last-minute U.S. demands, and a week in which Trump lifted some 50 percent tariffs on Canadian goods while adding others; Trump had not commented on Sunday's call.
