Saudi Arabia's real GDP contracted 3.8 percent year on year in the third quarter of 2026, after a 4.7 percent fall in the second quarter, according to flash estimates from the General Authority for Statistics reported by Arab News. Oil activities fell 20.4 percent from a year earlier and subtracted 4.7 percentage points from growth, while non-oil activities rose 1.9 percent, accelerating from 0.8 percent in the second quarter, and government activities fell 2.7 percent. On a seasonally adjusted basis the economy grew 1.3 percent from the second quarter, reversing a 4.8 percent quarterly contraction, as oil activities rebounded 5.7 percent on the quarter.
