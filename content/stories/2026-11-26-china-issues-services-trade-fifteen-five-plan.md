The Ministry of Commerce, the People's Bank of China and six other departments jointly issued China's services trade development plan for the fifteenth five-year period, covering 2026 to 2030 and published on November 26. The plan records that services trade first passed 8 trillion yuan in 2025, reaching 8.08 trillion yuan, with exports of 3.63 trillion yuan growing an average of 13.4 percent a year over the previous plan period, knowledge-intensive services taking 50.5 percent of exports, and services reaching 15.1 percent of China's total foreign trade.

For 2030 the plan sets qualitative goals rather than a trade volume target: a further rise in the services share of foreign trade, more balanced imports and exports, and knowledge-intensive services as a central growth engine. Its measures include orderly wider market access under the cross-border services negative list, naming value-added telecommunications and healthcare, smoother cross-border flows of data, people and funds, wider global use of the CIPS renminbi payment network, and support for artificial intelligence services and digital trade going overseas.
