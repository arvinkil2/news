Repairs to a backup transformer damaged by military activity near the Zaporizhzhia Thermal Power Plant were completed under the eighth localized ceasefire the IAEA has facilitated between Russia and Ukraine, strengthening the plant's connection to the Ferosplavna-1 330 kV line, its sole remaining external power supply. The main 750 kV Dniprovska line remains unavailable, and the IAEA said both sides confirmed this week that new severe damage had been identified on it a few kilometres from the plant, with the agency now exploring options for restoration work. The IAEA team was also informed that a plant bus was allegedly struck by a drone on October 21, killing the driver, after which the plant significantly reduced on-site staffing.
