GE Vernova reported third quarter revenue of 12,642 million dollars, up from 9,969 million dollars a year earlier, in its Form 10-Q filed on Wednesday, with equipment sales up 42 percent to 8,340 million dollars and services revenue of 4,302 million dollars. Net income attributable to the company was 1,291 million dollars, against 452 million dollars a year earlier, and diluted earnings were 4.81 dollars a share, against 1.64 dollars.

Power segment revenue was 6,067 million dollars and Electrification revenue was 3,862 million dollars, and total segment EBITDA more than doubled to 2,109 million dollars from 977 million dollars. Contract liabilities and deferred income on the balance sheet rose to 45,669 million dollars at September 30, from 25,774 million dollars at the end of 2025, and cash and restricted cash rose to 16,764 million dollars from 8,848 million dollars.
