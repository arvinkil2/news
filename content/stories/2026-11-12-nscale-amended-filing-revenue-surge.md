Nscale plc's amended IPO prospectus, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Thursday, puts revenue for the six months ended June 30, 2026 at $140.6 million, a 1,252 percent increase on the $10.4 million of the same period a year earlier, after full year 2025 revenue of $33.0 million, up 73 percent on 2024. The filing says total contracted value under its long-term take-or-pay contracts has grown from $100 million at launch to over $105 billion in two and a half years, with a weighted average contract life of about 5.7 years and a power pipeline above 10 gigawatts owned and controlled, while its largest customer accounted for 73 percent of 2025 revenue. The amendment also carries the definitive agreement, signed July 28, 2026, to acquire Anyscale, the company behind the Ray distributed computing framework, in a deal expected to close alongside the offering; the cover price range remains blank, so no offering size or valuation is yet filed.
