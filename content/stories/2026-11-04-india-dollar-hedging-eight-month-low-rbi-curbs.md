Indian companies bought $49.5 billion of forward dollars in October, down 35 percent from September's record high and the lowest total since February, according to clearing house data reported by Bloomberg, while their forward dollar sales fell to $32.2 billion, the lowest since May. The drop follows Reserve Bank of India measures last month, including a foreign exchange risk reserve requirement on large hedges, that made forward positions more expensive; activity has thinned further this month, with currency futures volumes averaging about $773 million a day last week against $3.2 billion in October and options trading down about 30 percent week on week. The rupee has recovered about 2.5 percent from the record low it hit on Monday after the central bank intervened in the spot market.
