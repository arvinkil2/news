In India, Grid Controller of India Limited has agreed to acquire 100 percent of Central Transmission Utility of India Limited from Power Grid Corporation of India for an initial payment of 73.46 crore rupees, subject to a valuation-based true-up. The tripartite share purchase agreement was signed on November 19, 2026, with closing expected within three months. The transfer separates transmission planning and grid connectivity functions from Power Grid's competitive transmission development business, and is intended to strengthen the utility's institutional independence.

The New Mexico State Investment Council has approved an initial $315 million commitment to HIG Capital infrastructure strategies, placing $150 million into HIG Infrastructure Partners Fund II and $165 million into a co-investment vehicle. Fund II is targeting $2 billion and will make equity investments of $50 million to $300 million in mid-market infrastructure, mostly in North America and Europe. The council also disclosed plans for $700 million to $1.2 billion of real estate commitments in 2027. This item is single-sourced to IPE Real Assets reporting of the council's board meeting.

The UK Competition and Markets Authority has published the interim report of its market study into private dental services, setting out emerging findings and potential remedies, and has opened a consultation for views from dental businesses, professionals and consumers. The study is a market investigation rather than a merger case, and no findings are final at this stage.
