The Central Bank of Turkey has opened its Electronic Data Delivery System to artificial intelligence applications through EVDS-MCP, a Model Context Protocol server in service since October 23, the bank's Data Governance and Statistics Department announced. Approved users sign in with two-factor authentication and an EVDS API key, after which AI agents and large language model applications can query close to 50,000 time series in 36 data groups from nine data providers, alongside the system's existing REST API and GraphQL access.

The bank frames the launch as part of its EVDS3 transparency and accessibility initiative, with user guides and a demo video published alongside the service. For data users, the change is practical rather than rhetorical: Turkish official statistics, from money and banking data to prices and the balance of payments, become directly retrievable by the software agents now doing first-pass macro work, with the central bank itself as the source rather than a third-party aggregator.
