The Central Bank of Turkey has launched EVDS-MCP, expanding access to its Electronic Data Delivery System to artificial intelligence tools, the bank announced in press release 2026-46, dated October 26. The EVDS has operated since 1993 as the bank's open data platform for economic and financial market data, and the MCP channel adds AI tools to the access routes on the EVDS 3 platform the bank launched in January 2026.

The bank is careful about the boundary of the service. Its role, the release says, is limited to providing EVDS data in a manner compatible with AI tools, and it is not responsible for the AI tools used with EVDS-MCP or for their outputs. Updated terms of use and user guides are available on the EVDS 3 platform.
