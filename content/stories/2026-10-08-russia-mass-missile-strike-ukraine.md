**Latest:** The Kramatorsk toll has risen sharply. A Russian satellite-guided glide bomb struck a street in Kramatorsk where two city buses were operating, killing at least 33 people and wounding 18, AFP reported Thursday, citing authorities; the Associated Press put the toll at 30 killed and at least 18 injured. President Zelenskyy described the site as a bus stop. Regional governor Vadym Filashkin accused Russia of deliberately targeting civilians and ordered public transport halted in Kramatorsk and the nearby city of Sloviansk. Russia has not commented; it denies targeting civilians. The overnight toll in Pryluky also rose to 22, including five children, and Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko said morning drone strikes on the capital killed three and wounded 14.

Earlier: a Russian strike on a route bus in Kramatorsk, Donetsk region, killed at least 12 people and injured 14 on Thursday, according to Donetsk governor Vadym Filashkin, who posted photos of the burned-out vehicle on Telegram. Ukraine's public broadcaster Suspilne also reported explosions in Kyiv on Thursday morning, indicating the bombardment is continuing into the day.

Russia fired 70 missiles and dozens of Shahed drones at Ukraine overnight, killing at least 28 people and injuring 100, Ukrainian officials said. The heaviest toll was in Pryluky in the northern Chernihiv region, where a missile destroyed a five-storey apartment block, killing 20 people including five children. President Zelenskyy said the barrage coincided with Vladimir Putin's birthday and called it one of the war's most vile strikes; Russia's defence ministry said the targets were military-industrial facilities in Kyiv and other regions.
