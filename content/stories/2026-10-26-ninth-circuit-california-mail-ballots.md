The Ninth U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled two to one on Monday against the Trump administration's bid to block California from counting certain mail ballots this cycle, denying its request to sequester roughly 20,000 mailed ballots ahead of the November 3 midterm election. The administration, which sued California on October 15, asserts the ballots went to people who are likely non-citizens but has not publicly revealed how it built that list, and the majority said it had not given the lower courts enough time to review the case, underscoring that election administration is the state's responsibility under the Constitution. The underlying case continues in the lower court, with a hearing scheduled for Tuesday, and the article notes the Justice Department has sued 30 states and Washington, D.C. for complete access to voter rolls and has lost 26 of those cases and won none so far.
