**German order books keep growing.** The real stock of orders in German manufacturing rose 0.8 percent in September 2026 from August, after seasonal and calendar adjustment, and stood 11.2 percent above September 2025, the Federal Statistical Office (Destatis) said in press release No. 403 on Wednesday. Machinery and equipment orders rose 1.4 percent on the month and automotive orders 1.9 percent. Unfilled domestic orders rose 1.4 percent and foreign orders 0.5 percent. Capital goods producers' backlogs rose 0.9 percent, intermediate goods 0.6 percent and consumer goods 1.1 percent.

The range of the stock of orders, the months of work on hand at constant turnover, fell to 8.9 months from 9.0 in August. It held at 12.6 months for capital goods producers, eased to 4.5 months for intermediate goods producers and stayed at 4.3 months for consumer goods producers.
