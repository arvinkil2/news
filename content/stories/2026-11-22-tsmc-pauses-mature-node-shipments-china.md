TSMC has reportedly paused shipments to Chinese chip designers and suspended deliveries of all chips made on 16-nanometre and finer processes for mainland customers while it seeks clarification from the US Bureau of Industry and Security, according to Nikkei Asia reporting carried by Tom's Hardware. Customers are reportedly being asked to sign affidavits attesting that their chips do not violate US export controls.

The affected nodes are mature ones, including 16-nanometre and 14-nanometre production used for automotive chips, television image processors, smartphone processors, smart-home devices and PC display drivers. Under the bureau's interim final rule, issued on November 16, chips made on a 16-nanometre or finer process can count as advanced computing items, a category that previously centred on high-performance AI chips.
