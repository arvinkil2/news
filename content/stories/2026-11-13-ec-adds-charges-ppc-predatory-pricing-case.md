The European Commission has sent a supplementary statement of objections to Public Power Corporation, Greece's largest electricity supplier, in its investigation into whether PPC breached EU antitrust rules by selling electricity in the Greek wholesale market below cost between 2013 and 2019, thereby excluding its main competitors at the time. The new charge sheet, which follows the statement of objections sent in February 2024, mainly adds detail on the methodology the Commission used to assess how much electricity PPC supplied below cost. The Commission said the step does not prejudge the outcome of the investigation, and PPC now has the opportunity to reply to its concerns.
