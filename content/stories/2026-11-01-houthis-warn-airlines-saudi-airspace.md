A senior Houthi military official warned international airlines on Sunday not to use Saudi airspace, except for the airspace over the holy cities of Mecca and Medina, hinting at possible attacks on aircraft, according to the Houthi-run Saba news agency as reported by Yonhap. The official also said Saudi Aramco facilities are within the strike range of Houthi forces and called investment there a dangerous gamble that would bear enormous losses.

The warning comes as Yemen's war, re-escalated since July, has repeatedly reached inside Saudi Arabia, including an October 10 strike on Riyadh's international airport that Yonhap said killed at least 13 people. No airline, Saudi authority or aviation regulator had responded to the airspace warning in the reporting available at check time.
