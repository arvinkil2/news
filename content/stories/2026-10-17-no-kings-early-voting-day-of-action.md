Thousands of No Kings protests were held across the United States on Saturday for a nationwide day of action as early voting got under way in many states, The Hill reported, with demonstrators in Washington marching from the White House to the Capitol behind a banner reading "Your vote is your voice." The organizing coalition announced the day last month as a coordinated effort to boost early voting mobilization, after earlier protest days aimed at President Trump and his administration; organizers said about five million people turned out at a nationwide day last June and seven million last October, and more than 3,000 demonstrations were held in March. No turnout estimate for Saturday was given in the reporting opened, and no incidents or arrests were reported.
