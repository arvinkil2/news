Latest (Saturday 20:45 ET): A new wave followed the Saturday evening claims. Interfax reported that Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said air defences destroyed another 25 drones flying toward Moscow early on Sunday, Moscow time, after reporting 11 downed earlier on Sunday, bringing the new wave's total to 36, and that emergency service specialists were working where debris fell. In Leningrad region, Governor Alexander Drozdenko said 11 drones were destroyed over the region, and Interfax reported that the Russian aviation authority had said St Petersburg's Pulkovo airport was not receiving or sending flights.

Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said air defences destroyed 25 drones flying toward the Russian capital on Saturday evening, RIA Novosti reported. Sobyanin said emergency service specialists were working where debris fell. The RIA account carried no report of damage or casualties.
