Latest, November 2: Marvell Technology and Lumentum have signed a definitive agreement to combine in an all-stock merger that values Lumentum at an implied enterprise value of 115 billion dollars, about 1,132 dollars a share and a premium of about 23.9 percent to the unaffected closing prices of October 28, the companies announced on Monday. Lumentum holders will receive a fixed 4.5 Marvell shares for each Lumentum share and will own about 33 percent of the combined company, against about 67 percent for Marvell holders. Both boards approved the deal unanimously. Matt Murphy stays as chairman and chief executive, three Lumentum directors including chief executive Michael Hurlston join the Marvell board, and the companies expect the deal to close in the first half of calendar 2028, subject to both sets of stockholders and to regulatory approvals. The companies put their combined addressable market at 550 billion dollars, see a line of sight to more than 1 billion dollars of annual run-rate cost savings, and expect the deal to add to Marvell's non-GAAP earnings per share in the first year after closing. Lumentum shares rose nearly 12 percent in premarket trading on Monday and Marvell shares fell more than 1 percent, according to Investing.com.

Lumentum reported its fiscal first quarter of 2027 the same morning. Revenue was 1.26 billion dollars, up 136.5 percent from a year earlier, with GAAP net income of 360.3 million dollars, or 3.54 dollars a diluted share, and the company guided second quarter revenue to between 1.575 billion and 1.625 billion dollars. The release said pump lasers and optical circuit switches led growth and that a second major near-packaged and co-packaged optics win carried purchase commitments above 5 billion dollars.

Marvell Technology is in advanced discussions to buy Lumentum Holdings, which makes lasers and optical components used to move data between servers in artificial intelligence data centres, and a deal could be announced as soon as next week, Bloomberg reported on October 28, citing people familiar with the matter. Lumentum then had a market value of about 83 billion dollars against Marvell's roughly 221 billion dollars. Lumentum shares rose 15 percent in after-hours trading after the report and Marvell shares fell 11 percent, while rival optics maker Coherent rose 4.3 percent.
