Marvell Technology is in advanced discussions to buy Lumentum Holdings, which makes lasers and optical components used to move data between servers in artificial intelligence data centres, and a deal could be announced as soon as next week, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Lumentum has a market value of about 83 billion dollars against Marvell's roughly 221 billion dollars, and the two companies have not reached a final agreement, so the terms and timing could still change. Lumentum shares rose 15 percent in after-hours trading after the report and Marvell shares fell 11 percent, while rival optics maker Coherent rose 4.3 percent.
