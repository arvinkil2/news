China's military procurement network has published a suspension list naming SF Express and tea maker Bama Tea, barring both companies from taking part in procurement of goods, engineering and services across the armed forces over suspected violations. The handling department named in the notice is the procurement service centre of the Central Military Commission's logistics support department, and the processing date is November 19.

The notice does not state what SF Express is alleged to have done, or how long the suspension lasts. SF Express is the delivery arm of Shenzhen and Hong Kong listed SF Holding and one of China's largest logistics operators, so the ban closes off the whole military as a customer for new contracts until the suspension is lifted.
