The New Zealand dollar fell 0.2 percent to US$0.5485 on Tuesday, its lowest level since March 2020, according to Sina Finance. Lower long-term inflation expectations, a weak domestic economy, worsening global risk sentiment and a widening interest-rate differential between New Zealand and the United States weighed on the currency.
