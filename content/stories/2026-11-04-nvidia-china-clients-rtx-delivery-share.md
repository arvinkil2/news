Chinese AI and cloud majors including Alibaba, Tencent and ByteDance ordered about 6 million of Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500 chips, worth about $70 billion, but expect to receive only some ten thousand chips, roughly 1 percent of the order, within the next four months, Bloomberg reported.

The reason given is production capacity: Nvidia is struggling to expand output fast enough to meet surging demand for its advanced AI chips as the data centre buildout continues.
