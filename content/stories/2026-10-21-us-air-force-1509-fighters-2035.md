The US Air Force says it needs 1,509 combat coded fighters, or jets assigned to combat units, by 2035, with F-35s making up about 40 percent of the fleet, F-22s and their F-47 replacements about 20 percent, and F-15s and F-16s splitting the rest, Defense News reported. Lt. Gen. David Tabor said the figure reflects what regional commanders need at medium risk, that it was not driven by this year's war with Iran, and that it does not count the 500 Collaborative Combat Aircraft drone wingmen the service plans to field by 2032. The fiscal 2027 request seeks 62 fighters, against the 72 a year officials have long said the service needs to buy, and Tabor said reaching the goal depends on congressional funding, multiyear contracts now pending in Congress, and Lockheed Martin and Boeing building the jets in time.
