**Latest (Monday):** Greek authorities said on Monday that 12 migrants had died attempting to cross the Aegean Sea, including six whose bodies were found after their boat collided with a police vessel off Lesbos, the coast guard said. A baby and four women were among the six who died off Lesbos. The other six dead were on a small boat that capsized off the island of Samos, the coast guard added.

A speedboat carrying foreign nationals collided with a Hellenic Coast Guard vessel in the sea area 3.5 nautical miles south of Agrielia on Lesbos, striking the left side of the coast guard vessel's bow, ERT News reported. Six of the people aboard, four men, one woman and an infant, were found unconscious, according to the Coast Guard, and eight others, four men, two women and two children, had been rescued.

A search and rescue operation coordinated by the Joint Search and Rescue Coordination Centre was still under way, with a floating ambulance, a coast guard vessel, three patrol boats and a Hellenic Air Force helicopter taking part and a private vessel of the Hellenic Rescue Team heading to the area. The report did not say how the collision happened, how many people were aboard in total, or what condition the six found unconscious were in beyond that description.
