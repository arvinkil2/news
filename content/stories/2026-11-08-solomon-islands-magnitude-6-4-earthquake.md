A magnitude 6.4 earthquake struck at 03:34 UTC on Monday about 30 kilometres southwest of Lata, in the Solomon Islands' Santa Cruz Islands, at a depth of 17 kilometres, according to US Geological Survey data revised in the first hour after the event. The survey's PAGER system issued a green alert, its lowest level, expecting little impact, and it had recorded no aftershocks of magnitude 4.5 or more nearby.

No damage or casualties were reported in the first coverage, and no tsunami warning accompanied the event. Yonhap, carrying the Korea Meteorological Administration's initial reading, placed the same quake 541 kilometres north-northwest of Luganville, Vanuatu, at an initially reported depth of 25 kilometres.
