Japan's Ministry of Finance sold 699.6 billion yen of 20 year government bonds on Wednesday, a reopening of Issue No. 198 with a 3.8 percent coupon, maturing September 20, 2046 and issued November 19. Competitive bids totalled 1,914.0 billion yen against 525.8 billion yen accepted competitively, a cover of about 3.64 times, with a further 173.8 billion yen allotted at the non-price-competitive auction for JGB market special participants.

The sale cleared at a weighted average price of 100.49 per 100 yen, an average yield of 3.756 percent, while the lowest accepted price of 100.20 yielded 3.782 percent and took a 64.9193 percent allotment. Bloomberg reported that demand was weaker than the 12 month average for the tenor, a softer reception at the long end as the Bank of Japan continues tightening.
