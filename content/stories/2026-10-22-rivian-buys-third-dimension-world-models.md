Rivian has acquired Third Dimension, a UK startup founded in 2024 by Tolga Kart and Piotr Sokólski that raised 7 million dollars in seed funding, and its 15-person team will join Rivian's autonomy and artificial intelligence team under James Philbin, expanding the company's London AI hub, TechCrunch reported. Terms of the deal were not disclosed. Rivian collects more than 400,000 driving sequences a day from customer vehicles whose owners have enabled camera sharing, and Third Dimension's technology is meant to turn that data into simulated 3D worlds where complex driving conditions can be tested without waiting for rare real-world cases.
