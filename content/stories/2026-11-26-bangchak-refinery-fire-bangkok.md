An explosion and fire broke out at Bangchak Corp's refinery on Sukhumvit Soi 62 in Bangkok's Phra Khanong district on Thursday, with the incident reported at 2.51pm local time. Three people were injured and taken to hospital, thick black smoke rose over the area, nearby schools were evacuated and office workers reported feeling a building shake from the blast.

Bangchak said in an alert issued at 3.37pm that the fire had been brought under control, while authorities still urged people in surrounding areas to evacuate as a precaution and motorists to keep the old railway road clear for emergency vehicles. The cause of the explosion is being investigated, and the company has not said whether refinery operations were disrupted.
