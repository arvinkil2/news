## Latest

Bangchak Corp said it has shut down all production units at its Phra Khanong refinery in Bangkok following Thursday's fire, which broke out at about 2.45pm local time and was brought under control and extinguished about 30 minutes later. At least seven people were injured, four of them seriously, according to Bangkok's Erawan Emergency Medical Centre, and the cause of the incident at the 120,000 barrel a day plant is under investigation.

An explosion and fire broke out at Bangchak Corp's refinery on Sukhumvit Soi 62 in Bangkok's Phra Khanong district on Thursday, with the incident reported at 2.51pm local time. Three people were initially reported injured and taken to hospital, thick black smoke rose over the area, nearby schools were evacuated and office workers reported feeling a building shake from the blast.

Bangchak said in an alert issued at 3.37pm that the fire had been brought under control, while authorities still urged people in surrounding areas to evacuate as a precaution and motorists to keep the old railway road clear for emergency vehicles.
