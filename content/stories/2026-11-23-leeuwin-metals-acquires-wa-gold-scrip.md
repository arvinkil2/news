Leeuwin Metals has entered a binding scheme implementation deed to acquire all of WA Gold, creating a Western Australian gold company with a combined mineral resource of 896,600 ounces across two projects on granted mining leases. WA Gold shareholders will receive 0.0853 new Leeuwin shares for each share held, one for every 11.72 shares, with no cash component.

At Leeuwin's closing price of A$0.21 on November 20, the offer implies A$0.0179 per WA Gold share, a 49.3 percent premium to WA Gold's A$0.012 close, and values WA Gold at about A$25.7 million fully diluted, or roughly A$45 per resource ounce on an enterprise value basis as the company framed it. Leeuwin will issue about 122.3 million new shares, leaving its existing holders with about 52 percent of the combined group and WA Gold holders with 48 percent, and a pro forma market capitalisation of about A$53 million with roughly A$5 million of cash before costs.

The assets are WA Gold's Abercromby project near Wiluna, with 518,000 ounces at 1.45 grams per tonne, and Leeuwin's Marda project near Southern Cross, with 378,600 ounces at 1.03 grams per tonne. About 70 percent of the combined resource is in the inferred category and neither project has an ore reserve, so the deal is a consolidation of exploration optionality rather than of production.

The WA Gold board unanimously recommends the scheme, absent a superior proposal and subject to an independent expert's report. Shareholders vote in late February 2027, with implementation targeted for early to mid March, and Leeuwin will lend WA Gold up to A$300,000 unsecured while the scheme proceeds.
