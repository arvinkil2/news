Tempus AI said in an SEC filing on Wednesday that it has entered a credit agreement with Ares Capital Corporation as administrative agent, providing $385 million in senior secured term loans and $115 million in revolving commitments, with the borrowings to fund part of the consideration for its acquisition of Personalis, related costs, and working capital. The facilities mature five years after the November 25 effective date, and the term loans carry interest at Term SOFR plus 5.50 percent, subject to a 1.00 percent floor.

In the same filing, Tempus said it elected on November 24 to pay approximately $600 million in cash as part of the merger consideration for Personalis, alongside approximately $900 million in Tempus Class A common stock, under the merger agreement dated July 20, 2026. Initial funding under the credit agreement is conditional on the acquisition closing or being expected to close within one business day of funding.
