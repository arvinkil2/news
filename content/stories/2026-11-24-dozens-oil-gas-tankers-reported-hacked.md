US officials suspect Iranian hackers may be behind as many as 50 cyberattacks targeting shipping vessels in recent weeks, according to a Wall Street Journal report summarised by TechCrunch. The hacked ships were carrying oil, liquefied gas and other explosive chemicals across Asia and Europe, and several oil tankers headed toward the US coast in August also had their navigation systems breached, prompting FBI and Coast Guard cybersecurity teams to board the vessels.

Officials are investigating whether Iran is expanding its recent wave of hacks against the US by targeting large vessels to sow fear among shippers, disrupt routes or cause an explosion at a port, the report said, noting that ships carry backup controls if automated systems fail. The hackers may have abused a vulnerability in Fortinet-made routers used on vessels, the subject of a joint FBI and Coast Guard security alert in September.
