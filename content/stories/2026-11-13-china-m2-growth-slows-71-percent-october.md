China's broad money supply (M2) stood at 359.06 trillion yuan at the end of October, up 7.1 percent year on year, and the stock of aggregate financing to the real economy was 469.16 trillion yuan, up 7.2 percent, the People's Bank of China reported. Yuan loans rose 11.42 trillion yuan in the first ten months of the year, but household loans fell 1.46 trillion yuan over the same period, with short-term household borrowing down 1.41 trillion yuan, while loans to companies and institutions rose 12.39 trillion yuan. The weighted average interbank lending rate in October was 1.30 percent, down 0.1 percentage points from September.
