Democrats have won at least 258 of the 435 seats in the US House of Representatives, the tally reached on Sunday after Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated Republican Representative Virginia Foxx in North Carolina, with Republicans holding at least 173 seats and four races still outstanding: one each in Minnesota and Tennessee and two in Louisiana heading to a December runoff. The result is a gain of 44 seats so far, four more than Democrats picked up in the 2018 midterms, and the party's largest House majority since 1992, bigger than the 257 seats it won in 2008.

Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries is in line to become speaker when the new Congress convenes on January 3, and Democrats are preparing a 100-day agenda on living costs alongside an oversight program aimed at President Donald Trump, who now faces divided government with Democrats also holding the Senate.
