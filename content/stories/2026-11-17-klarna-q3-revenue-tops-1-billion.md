Klarna reported third-quarter total revenue of $1,009 million, up 12 percent year over year and its fourth consecutive quarter above $1 billion, with gross merchandise volume up 10 percent to $36.1 billion and transaction margin dollars up 32 percent to $371 million. Adjusted operating income was $21 million, but the group still posted an operating loss of $53 million and a net loss of $65 million for the quarter. Klarna said it now serves 122 million active consumers and 1.35 million merchants, the latter up 59 percent, with US Fair Financing volumes up 61 percent as its fastest-growing product.
