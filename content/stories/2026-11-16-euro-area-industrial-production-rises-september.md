Euro area industrial production rose 0.2 percent in September from August, after falling 0.5 percent in August, and EU production rose 0.5 percent after a 0.3 percent fall, Eurostat said in first estimates published on Monday. Compared with September 2025, production fell 1.5 percent in the euro area and 0.8 percent in the EU.

Within the euro area, intermediate goods output rose 0.7 percent on the month and durable consumer goods rose 1.3 percent, while energy production fell 1.7 percent, non-durable consumer goods fell 0.4 percent and capital goods were flat. Ireland recorded the largest monthly fall at 5.5 percent and the largest annual fall at 19.1 percent, while Denmark posted the largest monthly increase at 3.7 percent.
