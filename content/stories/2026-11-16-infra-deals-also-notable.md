**Exus closes 345 million dollar financing.** Exus Renewables North America closed 345 million dollars in senior secured holding company financing led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives, the company announced on November 12. The financing supports construction costs and new project development across its US wind, solar and battery storage portfolio, which exceeds 6 gigawatts in total with more than 800 megawatts operating or under construction. It follows a 400 million dollar senior secured corporate credit facility closed in December and about 356 million dollars of project level construction financing in July for the Highland North and Cambria wind projects in Pennsylvania. PEI Global Partners advised Exus.

**Wollemi and Rest take majority of Redball.** Wollemi Capital and Australian superannuation fund Rest have acquired a majority stake in Redball Energy, Mercom Capital reported. No price was disclosed in the item. The deal continues the pattern of Australian superannuation capital taking controlling positions in domestic energy transition platforms.
