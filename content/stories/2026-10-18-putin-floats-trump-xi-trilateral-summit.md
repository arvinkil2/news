President Vladimir Putin proposed a three way meeting with President Donald Trump and President Xi Jinping in November to discuss ending the war in Ukraine, raising the idea in an October 9 telephone call with Trump, the Wall Street Journal reported, via Kommersant. Russia has passed the proposal to Beijing through diplomatic channels, and the Chinese side has signalled it would not object if Trump agrees to take part; Trump has not agreed, though the Journal's sources say he may be receptive. The Kremlin has proposed holding the meeting at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen on November 18 and 19, and spokesman Dmitry Peskov told the Journal there is no clear understanding yet on the meeting itself or its potential agenda, although all three sides acknowledge the hypothetical possibility of holding one.
