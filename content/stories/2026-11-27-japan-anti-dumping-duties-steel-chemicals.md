Japan's Cabinet has enacted orders that make anti-dumping duties on hot-dipped galvanized steel coil, sheet and strip from South Korea and China definitive at 29.2 to 55.3 percent, the same rates as the provisional duties in force since August 2026, the Ministry of Finance said on November 27. The orders are published on December 2 and the duties run from December 3, 2026 to December 2, 2031, after an investigation opened in August 2025 confirmed dumped imports and material injury to the domestic industry.

A second order imposes duties of 2.4 to 8.7 percent on bisphenol A, a raw material for polycarbonate and epoxy resins, from South Korea and Taiwan over the same five-year period, with products of LG Chem excluded. A third order extends the existing potassium hydroxide duties, 49.5 percent on South Korean product and 73.7 percent on Chinese product, for five years to December 1, 2031, after a review found that expiry would likely lead to a continuation or recurrence of dumping and injury.
