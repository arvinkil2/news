Israeli tanks advanced in central Gaza on Wednesday, with witnesses saying some tanks rolled onto the main Salahudeen road, west of the Yellow Line that marks the boundary of the two-thirds of the Gaza Strip still under Israeli control. Palestinian medics said an Israeli airstrike near the raid killed one person, and that separate strikes earlier in the day killed two other people in Deir Al-Balah and Gaza City. An Israeli military spokesperson said the military was carrying out an operation targeting Hamas infrastructure west of the Yellow Line and had struck a militant in southern Gaza, and Hamas has accused Israel of attempting to derail the US-brokered ceasefire.
