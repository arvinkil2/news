**Latest.** The Israeli military said on Thursday it had completed the central Gaza operation and reopened Salah al-Din Street, Gaza's main north-south road, Al Jazeera reported. About 6,000 Palestinians fled the area on Wednesday after evacuation orders covering three blocks north of the Nuseirat refugee camp, according to Gaza's Government Media Office. Some residents began returning to check their belongings, but others stayed away, saying sporadic gunfire continued and the road remained unsafe. Al Jazeera described the operation as the first major offensive publicly announced by the military since the ceasefire was agreed in October 2025; Hamas condemned the incursion as a ceasefire violation, while the Israeli military accused Hamas of violating the agreement.

Israeli tanks advanced in central Gaza on Wednesday, with witnesses saying some tanks rolled onto the main Salahudeen road, west of the Yellow Line that marks the boundary of the two-thirds of the Gaza Strip still under Israeli control. Palestinian medics said an Israeli airstrike near the raid killed one person, and that separate strikes earlier in the day killed two other people in Deir Al-Balah and Gaza City. An Israeli military spokesperson said the military was carrying out an operation targeting Hamas infrastructure west of the Yellow Line and had struck a militant in southern Gaza, and Hamas has accused Israel of attempting to derail the US-brokered ceasefire.

The BBC reported later on Wednesday that the operation followed evacuation orders for areas north of the Nuseirat and Bureij refugee camps, that a section of the Salah al-Din road was closed, and that the Israeli military described the incursion as a targeted operation with the Shin Bet to dismantle a tunnel west of the Yellow Line that it said posed an immediate threat to its troops. BBC Verify reviewed footage from about 1.5 kilometres west of the Yellow Line showing artillery fire near houses and tents, and residents of Nuseirat told the BBC that families fled the area under shelling and gunfire. Hamas called the advance a flagrant violation of the October 2025 ceasefire.
