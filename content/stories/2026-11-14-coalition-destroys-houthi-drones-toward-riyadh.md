The Coalition to Support Legitimacy in Yemen said it intercepted and destroyed two drones launched by the Iran-aligned Houthis toward Riyadh and Saudi Arabia's southern region on Saturday, according to its spokesman, Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Malki. The announcement reported no damage and no casualties, and gave no time for the interception.

The launch follows a heavier round earlier in the week, when Saudi air defences intercepted Houthi missiles and drones launched toward the capital, Makkah and other regions on Wednesday. Arab News reported that cross-border fighting between the coalition, which backs Yemen's internationally recognised government, and the Houthis, who control Sanaa, has intensified alongside the US and Israeli war with Iran.
