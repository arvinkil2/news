Taiwan's state energy company CPC has signed a memorandum of understanding with Glenfarne Group at the Gastech conference in Houston covering purchases of 6 million tonnes a year of Alaska LNG from 2032, with an option for CPC to take an equity stake in the project, Focus Taiwan reported. The signing gives last week's claim by President Trump, that Taiwan has agreed to buy 30 percent of the pipeline's gas for 20 years, its first piece of paper.

CPC's own position remains narrower than the president's. The company has repeatedly said no final procurement agreement has been signed, and an MOU is not one: it records intent on volumes and a possible stake while leaving price, and the project's final investment decision, for later. Glenfarne is targeting that decision on the 44 billion dollar, 807 mile pipeline this year.

The update is merged into the October 13 file, which remains the reference version: the claim is Trump's, the MOU is CPC's, and the distance between them is still the story. This item ships at grade C because the core commercial terms remain unconfirmed by either principal.
