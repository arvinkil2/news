President Trump said at a Republican rally in Alaska on Tuesday that Taiwan has agreed to buy 30 percent of the gas carried by the Alaska LNG pipeline over 20 years, and Clarin put the accompanying Taiwanese investment at $4.4 billion. The state energy company CPC has not confirmed a purchase: its documented step so far is a letter of intent signed in March 2025 covering possible purchases and investment, and it had not responded to requests for comment when the reports were filed.

The project, an 800 mile pipeline from the North Slope to an export terminal near Anchorage with a price tag put at $44 billion and Glenfarne Group as lead developer, has spent decades looking for exactly this kind of anchor buyer and still awaits a final investment decision.
