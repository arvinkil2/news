Police used water cannon and tear gas on antigovernment protesters in central Tirana on Saturday, as thousands gathered on Albania's independence day for a protest called by students and opposition figures. Demonstrators threw stones and petrol bombs at street cameras and government building windows, and the health ministry said eight people were injured, including a police officer and two journalists. Police also ran vehicle checks on the main roads into and out of the capital.

The protest is the latest in the movement known as the Flamingo Revolution, which began in May with rallies against a luxury hotel project in a coastal nature reserve and has since widened into demands for Prime Minister Edi Rama's resignation over alleged corruption, poor healthcare and limited prospects for young people. The European Union called for an investigation into excessive force by police and for all sides to de-escalate.
