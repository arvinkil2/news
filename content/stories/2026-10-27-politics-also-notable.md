President Donald Trump's approval rating has risen from a record low to 32 percent ahead of the November 3 midterm elections, a Reuters Ipsos poll found, Arab News reported. The number is still weak for a president whose party holds narrow majorities in both chambers, and it lands in the same closing week in which election officials told reporters they fear federal interference in next week's voting.

Trump rallied in Mississippi with a Republican Senate seat at risk, Bloomberg reported, part of a closing schedule that has already taken him to Michigan. The Mississippi appearance is campaign travel rather than a policy act, and it is noted here because the seat's risk rating is the reason for the trip.

In Serbia, the student movement celebrated for a second night after its election victory, Al Jazeera reported in a video item. The celebrations are atmospherics around a result already covered in full on this site; the material next step remains the certified count and the start of government formation talks, and no official final tally had been published in the reporting read for this roundup.

Kenya has released its first 20 Ebola contacts from quarantine after they completed monitoring, UN News reported. The release is the containment system working as designed on the Kenyan side of the regional outbreak, in the same edition that carries the killing of a nurse responding to an alert in the DR Congo, where the response is under attack.

President Trump's Board of Peace has tapped a former Biden administration aide to lead its outreach to Congress, Axios reported. The appointment is a staffing signal about how the Gaza body intends to handle a sceptical legislature, and it is carried here ahead of any formal programme announcements from the board.

The UN General Assembly's annual vote on the United States embargo of Cuba is being held under pressure, tension and crisis this year, Clarin reported in Spanish. The vote itself is a scheduled, recurring item and its result will be the news; the pressure account is single-sourced in the reporting available and is flagged as such.
