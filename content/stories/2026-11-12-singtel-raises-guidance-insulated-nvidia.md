Singtel raised its full year earnings guidance on Friday (Singapore time) as it reported first half results for the six months to September 30: underlying net profit rose 10 percent to S$1.49 billion on operating revenue of S$7.29 billion, up 6 percent, while headline net profit fell 55 percent to S$1.54 billion, from S$3.40 billion a year earlier, Dow Jones Newswires reported. The board approved an interim dividend of 9.0 Singapore cents a share.

Asked about the upheaval at Nvidia, group chief financial officer Arthur Lang told Bloomberg Television the company is "fairly insulated," with only a small share of its data centre capacity exposed to neocloud customers that may be linked to the chipmaker, though Singtel is "keeping a very close eye" on the situation.
