RBC Signals, the Redmond, Washington based satellite connectivity and data services provider, announced on November 19 a 10 million dollar strategic growth investment led by Washington Harbour Partners. The company said the money will expand its government services business and scale commercialization of intersatellite links, optical ground communications, space domain awareness and spectrum monitoring. RBC operates a network of more than 50 company-owned and partner sites, was selected in 2026 as an antenna provider for the US Space Force's Joint Antenna Marketplace, and supports Blue Origin's global ground station deployment.

ECARX Holdings, the automotive computing company, furnished a Form 6-K to the SEC on November 27 whose exhibit is a press release announcing a 20 million dollar share repurchase program. No further terms were stated in the filing cover document. The announcement is recorded here at the size the filing itself states, and no duration or authorization detail is claimed.
