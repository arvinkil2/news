The Bundestag gave final approval on Friday to Germany's federal budget for 2027, closing the plenary budget week that ran from November 24 to 27. The adopted budget sets core spending at 555.94 billion euros, about 31.4 billion euros more than in 2026, with net borrowing in the core budget of 122.97 billion euros and 61.85 billion euros set aside for investment, according to dpa-AFX reporting of the final vote and the Bundestag budget committee's own figures.

Defence is the largest single increase. The defence ministry's core budget stands at 109.75 billion euros, and with the Bundeswehr special fund counted, total defence spending reaches close to 140 billion euros, including 12 billion euros of support for Ukraine. With the infrastructure and Bundeswehr special funds added, total new debt for 2027 rises above 210 billion euros. The budget now goes to the Bundesrat before signature and entry into force at the start of 2027.
