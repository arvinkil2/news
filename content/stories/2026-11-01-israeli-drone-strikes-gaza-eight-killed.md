Israeli drone strikes killed at least eight Palestinians across Gaza on Sunday, according to hospital sources. Five people were killed in al-Mawasi, west of Khan Younis, where Gaza's Ministry of Interior said the strike hit police officers carrying out their duties, and three people, including a four-year-old boy, were killed in Gaza City's Rimal neighbourhood, according to sources at al-Shifa Hospital. The Israeli army told AFP it was targeting a terrorist in southern Gaza, and Hamas condemned the strikes as a deliberate war crime.
