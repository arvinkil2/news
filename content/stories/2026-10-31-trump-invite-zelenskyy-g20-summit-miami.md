President Donald Trump said he will invite Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to the G20 summit, which is to be held in December in the Miami area of Florida. Asked by a journalist whether he would invite Zelenskyy, Trump said there is no problem with doing so.

In the same exchange Trump said he believes the war between Ukraine and Russia will end in the not too distant future. The remarks follow Washington's decision to invite Russia to the summit, a decision Secretary of State Marco Rubio explained publicly the same day, as reported by UNIAN.
