Centrus Energy Corp said on Thursday that the first new centrifuge to support its multi-billion-dollar expansion of its uranium enrichment capacity has been completed and shipped from its manufacturing plant in Oak Ridge, Tennessee. The company disclosed the shipment in a Form 8-K filed with the SEC, which incorporates its press release of the same day as an exhibit.
