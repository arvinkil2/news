Tens of thousands of students marched in Tirana on Wednesday and the protest turned violent: demonstrators set fire to the education ministry building and two banks, including a Raiffeisen Bank branch near the ministry, hurled stones at police, overturned bins and smashed shopfronts, Reuters reported. Police answered with water cannon and tear gas. Student organisers said the protest was about university conditions, higher education costs, a lack of financial support and job prospects after graduation, and marches spread to universities in Elbasan and Shkodra.

The government condemned the violence, with Deputy Prime Minister Albana Kociu saying protest is a legitimate right but cannot excuse destruction, and police warning that anyone who burns property or attacks officers will face the law. The unrest caps a summer of peaceful protests against Rama that began over a proposed luxury resort on the southern coast backed by Jared Kushner and caught in legal disputes, and it lands amid wider student protests in France over school conditions.
