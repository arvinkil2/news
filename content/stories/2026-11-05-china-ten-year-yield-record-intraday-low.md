China's benchmark ten-year sovereign yield fell to an all-time intraday low on Friday, slipping about one basis point to 1.58 percent, the lowest level on record for the on-the-run notes according to data compiled by Bloomberg. Bloomberg attributed the move to haven demand during the Asian equity selloff, together with a recent shift in the policy rate that investors regarded as a de facto easing.
