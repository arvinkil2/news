CleanSpark swung to a net loss of $987.1 million for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2026, from net income of $364.5 million the year before, the bitcoin miner said in its Form 10-K filed Tuesday. Bitcoin mining revenue at its owned facilities fell to $576.5 million from $754.7 million, and the company booked a $281.6 million loss on the fair value of bitcoin as the price fell from about $114,100 on September 30, 2025 to about $83,600 a year later, alongside $177.1 million of impairment expense and $428.2 million of depreciation and amortisation.
