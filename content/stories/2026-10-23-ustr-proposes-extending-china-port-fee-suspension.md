USTR is proposing to extend its suspension of the Section 301 port fees on China-linked vessels for another two months. A Federal Register notice scheduled for publication on October 26 would keep the fees suspended from November 10, 2026 through January 9, 2027; the current one-year suspension expires on November 9.

During the extension, carriers would incur no liability under Annexes I, II and III of the Section 301 action, which cover Chinese-owned, Chinese-operated and Chinese-built vessels and certain foreign-built vehicle carriers, and the pause on certain duties for ship-to-shore cranes and cargo handling equipment would also continue. USTR said the United States is continuing to engage with China, and public comments on the proposal are due by 5 p.m. Eastern on October 30 under docket USTR-2026-0694.
