Two Californian customers for Nvidia's most advanced AI chips are Chinese-owned companies used to work around US export controls, CBS News reported, in an account carried by Tom's Hardware on Thursday. Airvres, listed as an elite partner on Nvidia's website and previously named Inspur after its Chinese parent, reportedly exported at least $5.6 billion of chips to Southeast Asia between April 2024 and February 2026, including top-end Blackwell silicon, with some going to Megaspeed in Malaysia, the company at the centre of the Alice Huang chip-network case. The second company, RiCloud AI, is owned by Shenzhen-based Yuxing InfoTech Investment Holdings, CBS reported.
