Nigeria: domestic gold prices fell as the naira strengthened and hawkish US Federal Reserve expectations weighed on bullion, allAfrica reported. The item illustrates how FX and Fed policy transmit into local African gold markets even while dollar gold holds near highs. (allAfrica, Oct 9; blocked at fetch; grade C.)

France: Le Monde reports the French nuclear industry is opposing moves to classify accumulating depleted uranium as radioactive waste. The classification question matters for disposal costs and long-term storage liabilities in the uranium fuel cycle, and by extension for the economics of conversion and enrichment. (Le Monde, Oct 8, in French; headline-sourced; grade C.)

Indonesia: state news agency Antara covered the potential for mineral downstreaming (hilirisasi) alongside AI investment, a reminder that Jakarta's value-added mining policy remains central to its industrial strategy as nickel and bauxite processing capacity expands.
