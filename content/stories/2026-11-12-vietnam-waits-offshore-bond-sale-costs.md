Vietnam will wait for more favourable conditions before selling bonds offshore, Finance Minister Ngo Van Tuan said on Thursday, telling reporters that conditions are currently not suitable for an offshore issuance because issuance costs are very high, Reuters reported.

Tuan said Vietnam has not abandoned its offshore bond plan and will continue engaging with investors. Bloomberg, which first carried the story in the wire pool, framed the postponement against fuel costs fanning inflation at home, a driver this item could not verify beyond Bloomberg's headline.
