Federal prosecutors have charged former OpenAI engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, with using or endeavouring to use a device to intercept oral communications, after a recording device was found plugged into a TV USB port in an executive conference room used for highly confidential technical and strategic discussions, the Justice Department said. OpenAI said it fired him, used its own AI models to help identify who planted the device, and referred the matter to the FBI; he was arrested on Friday and appeared in federal court on Tuesday. The company said there were no indications he had inappropriately accessed or exfiltrated model weights, source code or customer data, and the investigation is ongoing.
