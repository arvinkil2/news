Hurricane Isaias struck Florida's panhandle as a Category 2 hurricane, leaving two people dead and thousands without electricity, before weakening to a post-tropical cyclone. The storm made landfall along the U.S. Gulf Coast earlier in the day and moved inland, with emergency crews beginning damage assessments as conditions eased.

Isaias made landfall the same week Hurricane Simon strengthened to Category 4 off Mexico's Pacific coast, keeping both U.S. coasts under storm watch and regional power and fuel logistics under pressure.
