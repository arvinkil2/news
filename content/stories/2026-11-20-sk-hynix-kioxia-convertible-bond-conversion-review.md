SK Hynix is reviewing a plan to convert into shares the convertible bond it obtained through its 2018 investment in Kioxia, then Toshiba Memory, when it invested 395 billion yen through Bain Capital in a mix of fund and bond form, Yonhap reported, citing industry sources. The remaining bond is issued by SPC2, the investment vehicle that held a 14.53 percent stake in Kioxia at the end of September and is its largest shareholder, so conversion would give SK Hynix most of SPC2's voting rights and make it Kioxia's indirect largest shareholder. The fund portion of the 2018 investment, into which 2.6 trillion won was invested, was sold in full last June, and SK Hynix is reported to have no plan to sell after any conversion, with its position being to respect Kioxia's independent management and cooperate with it.
