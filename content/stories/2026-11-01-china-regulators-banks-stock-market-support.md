China's central bank will convene a meeting on Monday with the National Financial Regulatory Administration, the China Securities Regulatory Commission and major banks to discuss tools for supporting the capital market, Bloomberg reported on Sunday, citing a Cailian report based on people in the industry. Improving the relending program that funds share buybacks and stake increases is a specific item for the discussion. No amounts or decisions were reported, and none of the agencies had announced the meeting publicly when the report appeared.
