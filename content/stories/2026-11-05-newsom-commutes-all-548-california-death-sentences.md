Governor Gavin Newsom announced in a letter to victims' families that he is commuting the death sentence of every person on California's death row, all 548 people, to life in prison without the possibility of parole. Newsom called California's system for administering the death penalty a failure by every measure, said life without parole is the most severe punishment he can lawfully impose, and said he alone bears responsibility for the decision.

The action is largely symbolic in immediate terms, because California has not executed a prisoner since 2006, and Newsom placed a moratorium on executions in 2019 and later moved death row inmates out of San Quentin to other high-security prisons. It lands in the final months of his term, with executions rising elsewhere in the United States.
