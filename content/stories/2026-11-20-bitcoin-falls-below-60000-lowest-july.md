Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on Friday, touching $58,018, its lowest level since the beginning of July, and traded at $58,277 late in the day with its weekly loss approaching 16 percent, Anadolu Agency reported, citing CoinMarketCap data. The total crypto market fell 6.63 percent in 24 hours to $2.01 trillion, and ether dropped 10 percent on the day to $1,610. Analysts cited by the agency attributed the selling to expectations that US interest rates will stay high or rise, and to the sharp fall in US technology stocks, which has cut investors' appetite for volatile assets.
