Total oil flows through the Strait of Hormuz averaged 9.1 million barrels a day in the third quarter of 2026, up from 5.1 million in the second quarter but less than half the 21.4 million a day of the third quarter of 2025, the US Energy Information Administration said in its Global Energy Security Data release. Crude oil and condensate accounted for 7.3 million barrels a day of the third-quarter total and petroleum products for 1.8 million, while LNG flows through the strait recovered only to 1.4 billion cubic feet a day from 0.8 billion, against 10.9 billion a year earlier. The bypass pattern shifted over the quarter: flows through the Bab el-Mandeb fell to 6.3 million barrels a day from 8.1 million in the second quarter, while volumes through the Suez Canal and SUMED pipeline rose to 7.1 million from 5.8 million and flows through the Strait of Malacca rose to 19.4 million from 16.7 million.
