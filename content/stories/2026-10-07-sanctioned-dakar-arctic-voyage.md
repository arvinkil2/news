The 21,300-deadweight-ton Dakar, built in 2004 and formerly named Vulcano M, is sailing westward along Russia's Arctic coast carrying MTBE, a gasoline-blending component, after taking the cargo in a ship-to-ship transfer off South Korea. The vessel has no ice-class designation, is owned by Vulcano Marine SA and commercially managed from Dubai, and was sanctioned by the EU in July as part of Russia's shadow fleet. Russia's route rules allow non-ice-class vessels through mid-November depending on ice conditions, but October is the season's most difficult phase in the route's eastern sections, and the non-ice-class Suezmax Lynx was forced to wait days in ice at 72 degrees north on a similar run last year.
