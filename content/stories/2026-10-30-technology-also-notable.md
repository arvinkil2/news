KBRA has assigned preliminary ratings to up to 525 million dollars of notes from US Signal's issuing entities, the company's first asset backed securitisation, secured against ten owned colocation data centres spanning about 427,990 square feet and 22.5 megawatts of critical load. KBRA's release puts the pool's annualised recurring revenue at about 111.8 million dollars and annualised adjusted net operating income at about 63.8 million dollars as of July 2026, across 839 customers with a weighted average remaining contract term of 2.1 years; Datacenter Dynamics reports a preliminary A- rating and a stressed terminal capitalisation rate of 12.0 percent, above the range for other KBRA rated colocation deals, reflecting older assets in secondary markets. The ratings are preliminary and the notes have not priced, so this is recorded as a financing in preparation, not a completed raise.

Dream, founded by former Palantir executives, has launched with 17 million dollars from a16z to build AI world models for construction. The company joins a crowded field applying simulation models to physical industries, and the Palantir lineage and the a16z cheque make it the notable early stage launch of the day despite the small round.

Deepgram has acquired Daily, the company behind the Pipecat voice agent framework, to bolster its voice AI infrastructure. The deal folds a widely used open voice pipeline into a speech recognition provider, continuing the consolidation of the voice agent stack into fewer, fuller platforms. Terms were not disclosed in the reporting.

A short seller report claims Cerebras chips have shorter lifespans than the company's public statements suggest, Datacenter Dynamics reported. Chip lifespan assumptions feed directly into depreciation schedules and therefore into the reported economics of AI data centres, which is why a durability claim, contested and unverified as it stands, is worth recording rather than repeating as fact.

Alibaba has launched an Amsterdam cloud region and plans further European expansion, extending its European footprint as data residency rules push Chinese cloud providers to localise infrastructure. Akamai, meanwhile, has launched an AI inference service, the latest content delivery network operator repositioning its edge footprint for inference workloads rather than training.

Researchers have found bugs in five AI developer tools, with flaws affecting OpenAI and Microsoft tools still unpatched, The Register reported. Developer tooling is becoming a privileged layer, since AI coding tools hold repository and credential access, and unpatched flaws there reach further than the tools' user counts suggest.

President Trump has declared robot imports a national security threat, Ars Technica reported, opening a trade policy front on robotics supply chains. The declaration matters to automation vendors and manufacturers dependent on imported robots and components, though the measures that follow it have not been specified.
