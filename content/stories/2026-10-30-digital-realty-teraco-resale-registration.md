Digital Realty Trust filed a Form 8-K and a prospectus supplement on October 30 registering the resale of 3,425,031 shares of its common stock that were issued as consideration in its acquisition of approximately 16 percent of the interests in its Teraco joint venture. The shares were issued on October 20, 2026 under a share purchase agreement with Digital Titan 2 (PTY) LTD and TDE Luxco, after certain third party partners in the venture exercised a put right, and the closing lifted Digital Realty's ownership to approximately 77 percent of the ordinary shares in TDE Investments (Pty) Ltd, which owns Teraco Data Environments Proprietary Limited of South Africa. The registration does not necessarily mean any shares will be sold: the selling stockholders receive all proceeds from any resale, Digital Realty receives none, and the company pays the registration expenses under the purchase agreement.
