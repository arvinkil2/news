The Houthis have closed the only supply route linking Taiz to Aden after capturing a major road into the city, cutting off humanitarian aid and essential supplies, UN Human Rights Chief Volker Turk said on Tuesday, and the World Food Programme has warned that shifting front lines and road closures have cut off 800,000 people from life-saving assistance, the vast majority in Taiz. Local authorities on Tuesday raised the death toll from Monday's Houthi ballistic missile strikes on the al-Alam camp in Shabwah governorate to 73, an attack the Yemeni government condemned as a war crime, while President Rashad al-Alimi formally requested that the UN designate the Houthis as a terrorist organisation. The Saudi-led coalition said on Tuesday it had intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile fired by the Houthis towards Riyadh.
