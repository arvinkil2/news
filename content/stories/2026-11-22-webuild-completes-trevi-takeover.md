Webuild, Italy's largest construction company, has completed its takeover of Trevi, the specialist foundations and ground engineering group, after the Italian Competition Authority cleared the transaction and the company's mandatory tender offer closed on November 20.

Webuild said on November 19 that its holding had reached 52.53 percent of Trevi's share capital and that the antitrust clearance completed the regulatory approval process in Italy. Acceptances after that announcement lifted the stake to 60.82 percent. The mandatory offer, which carried no conditions, paid 5.165 euros per share in cash, with payment due on November 27. Webuild first offered 4.50 euros a share in July before raising the price; the final terms value the transaction at about 339 million euros.

Chief executive Pietro Salini called the deal an industrial project for Webuild, Trevi and Italy. Trevi, founded in Cesena, is among the world's leading players in specialist foundations and ground engineering, the work on which major infrastructure physically rests. It keeps its headquarters and management centre in Italy under the new ownership.

Webuild said the acquisition is fully covered by a dedicated, already committed financing facility. S&P Global Ratings rates Webuild BB+ with a stable outlook.
