Iran's Army Naval Forces said a tanker attempting to transit the Strait of Hormuz with its tracking systems turned off struck a mine a few hours earlier in the eastern part of the strait, in an area the statement called a forbidden and dangerous zone, and that the vessel was damaged and caught fire, Anadolu Agency reported, citing Iranian state television. The statement named no vessel and gave no flag, cargo or crew details, and it reported no casualties.

The navy said the strait is not safe for tankers and ships that try to pass without coordinating with the Iranian side, and that vessels wanting to pass safely must coordinate with Iran's armed forces. No independent source had confirmed the incident when this item was written.
