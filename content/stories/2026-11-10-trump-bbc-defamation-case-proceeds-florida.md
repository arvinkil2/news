US District Judge Jeffrey Kuntz declined on Tuesday to dismiss President Donald Trump's defamation claim against the BBC, finding that Trump had plausibly alleged reputational harm from the editing of a 2024 Panorama documentary about the Capitol riots, and that he had plausibly alleged a framework of actual malice in how his January 6, 2021 speech was edited. The judge dismissed a second claim accusing the broadcaster of unfair trade practices under Florida law, and the ruling did not decide whether Trump's claims are proven.

Trump is seeking $10 billion over an edit that, the BBC's chairman Samir Shah has acknowledged, gave the impression of a direct call for violent action; the affair led director general Tim Davie to resign. The BBC said it was surprised by the decision, respects the court's authority and remains confident in its position, and it has been ordered to file a response by December 1. Trump called the ruling a huge victory on Truth Social.
