ABB said it has signed an agreement to acquire Ziehl-Abegg, the family-owned German maker of electronically commutated fans and integrated electric motors based in Künzelsau, for an enterprise value of $2.65 billion and an equity value of $2.22 billion. Ziehl-Abegg is expected to generate about $1.4 billion of revenue in 2026 with an operational EBITA margin in the mid-single digit range, employs about 6,300 people, and the price implies 1.9 times expected 2026 sales and 18.6 times EBITDA. ABB expects Ziehl-Abegg to reach a margin similar to its Motion business area average within three years after closing, which is expected in the second half of 2027 subject to regulatory approvals, and said it intends to retain the Künzelsau headquarters.
