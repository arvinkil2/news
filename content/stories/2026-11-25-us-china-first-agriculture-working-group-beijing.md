The Office of the United States Trade Representative and China's Ministry of Commerce co-chaired the first meeting of the Agricultural Working Group under the framework of the U.S.-China Board of Trade in Beijing this week, USTR said on Wednesday. Joined by representatives of the relevant national regulatory agencies, the two sides held what the readout called in-depth and constructive exchanges on non-tariff barriers affecting agricultural trade, market access, and increasing trade to benefit agricultural producers.

Through the working group, the two sides agreed to continue to strengthen communication and work to address each other's concerns in the agricultural sector, USTR said. The readout said the Trump Administration will continue to pursue balanced trade with China by monitoring compliance of past commitments, while unlocking lasting benefits for American farmers and ranchers.
