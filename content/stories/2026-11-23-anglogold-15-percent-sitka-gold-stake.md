AngloGold Ashanti has agreed to invest at least C$72.8 million (US$50.8 million) for a 15 percent stake in Sitka Gold, paying 95 cents a share compared with Sitka's Friday close of 68 cents, The Northern Miner reported. Sitka rose 18 percent to 80 cents in Toronto by midday Monday, valuing the company at about C$347 million, and will retain full ownership of its RC Gold project in the Yukon, about 365 kilometres northwest of Whitehorse. At closing AngloGold would gain the right to nominate a Sitka director and preferential rights on certain transactions involving the project, and Sitka expects to raise C$83.3 million to C$101.6 million in total, with the financing expected to close in December subject to TSX Venture Exchange approval.
