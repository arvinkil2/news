South Korea's Kospi fell sharply on Thursday morning in Seoul, down 4.73 percent at 5,414.82 at 10:24 a.m. after opening down 1.82 percent at 5,580.09, while the Kosdaq was down 4.57 percent at 682.30. At 10:16 a.m. the Kospi 200 futures index, down 5.21 percent at 843.96, triggered a sell sidecar that suspended program sell orders for five minutes, the first such halt in about a week.

Samsung Electronics was down 6.65 percent and SK Hynix down 8.88 percent at the 10:24 a.m. snapshot, widening losses from the open. Yonhap attributed the fall to growing concern about tighter regulation of AI data centre construction after the Democratic Party's sweep of the US midterm elections, alongside a fall in the US semiconductor index.
