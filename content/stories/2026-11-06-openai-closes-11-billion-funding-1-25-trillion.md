OpenAI has closed on $11 billion of new funding, the initial part of a round that values the company at $1.25 trillion, Bloomberg News reported on Friday, in a one paragraph Reuters dispatch citing people familiar with the matter. The report names no investors, gives no total size for the round and states no terms, and OpenAI has not confirmed the close publicly.

The reported close lands two days into a repricing of AI assets: the Nasdaq fell more than 5 percent on Thursday, and investors in Anthropic's planned initial public offering are now talking down the valuation they will underwrite, as covered elsewhere in this edition.
