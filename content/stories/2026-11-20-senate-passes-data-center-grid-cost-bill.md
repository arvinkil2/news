The US Senate has passed the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026 by 62 votes to 33, Datacenter Dynamics reported. Sponsored by Senator Shelley Moore Capito of West Virginia, the bill would direct the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to establish ratepayer protections for data centers with capacity above 20 megawatts, requiring them to pay a greater share of the grid infrastructure costs tied to their electricity consumption.

The same package would expand FERC's authority to permit certain interstate transmission projects, remove permit requirements for onshore oil and gas drilling on non-federal land, accelerate renewable, geothermal, hydropower and offshore energy projects, and shorten deadlines for federal environmental reviews. The bill now goes to the House of Representatives, and from there to the President if passed unchanged.
