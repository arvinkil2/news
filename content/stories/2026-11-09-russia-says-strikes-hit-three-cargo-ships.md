Russia's Defence Ministry said Russian forces struck three cargo vessels in overnight attacks on Sunday, one container ship it said was delivering weapons and military equipment to the Black Sea port of Odesa, a second container ship in the port of Chornomorsk that it said had delivered dual-use cargo, and a dry cargo ship south-west of Odesa that it said was carrying weapons for Ukraine's armed forces, Reuters reported. The ministry also said it hit two electricity substations in the Dnipropetrovsk and Zhytomyr regions, which it said supplied power to the Ukrainian defence industry.

Reuters said it could not independently verify the ministry's claims, and there was no immediate comment from Ukraine. Ukrainian power company DTEK said a Russian overnight attack on energy facilities in Dnipropetrovsk had damaged important facilities and left more than 600,000 people without electricity.
