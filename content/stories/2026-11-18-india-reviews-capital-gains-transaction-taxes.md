India is examining proposals to change how it taxes stock market gains and trades, Central Board of Direct Taxes chairman Ravi Agrawal said on Monday, telling an event in New Delhi that the government is weighing industry suggestions on long-term capital gains tax and the securities transaction tax ahead of the 2027-28 Union Budget, with any decision to come through the budget, expected in early February 2027. Long-term gains on listed shares are currently taxed at 12.5 percent above an annual exemption of 1.25 lakh rupees.

Bloomberg reported on Wednesday that the options under consideration include cutting the securities transaction tax on cash equities by about half and raising taxes on derivatives trading to curb speculation, a shift aimed at reviving the cash market after foreign investors withdrew roughly 22 billion dollars from Indian equities in 2026. No rate, exemption or effective date has been announced.
