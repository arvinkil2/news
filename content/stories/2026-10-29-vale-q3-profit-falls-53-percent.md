Vale's net profit fell 53 percent year on year in the third quarter to 1.26 billion dollars, below the 1.70 billion dollar consensus compiled by Visible Alpha, Reuters reported. Adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation fell 24 percent to 3.33 billion dollars, against a 3.50 billion dollar consensus, with the decline attributed to lower iron ore prices and higher costs, including freight.

Iron ore production fell 6 percent to 88.4 million tonnes, below the 90.1 million tonne consensus, on weaker results at Serra Norte. Vale said it remains confident of reaching the lower end of its 2026 output guidance range of 335 million to 345 million tonnes.
