A drone strike hit a market in Nyala, the capital of South Darfur in western Sudan, on Monday, killing at least 21 people and injuring 40, a medical source told AFP. Nyala, one of the largest cities in Darfur, has been controlled by the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces since late October 2023, in the war the RSF has fought against Sudan's army since April 2023.

Neither the army nor the RSF had commented in the reporting read, and that reporting does not attribute the strike to either side. The war has killed tens of thousands of people, displaced millions and pushed parts of Sudan into famine, according to the United Nations.
