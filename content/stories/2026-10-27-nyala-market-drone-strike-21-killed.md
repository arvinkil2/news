Latest: more than 100 people were killed in the drone strike on a market in Nyala on Monday evening and 113 were injured, Emergency Lawyers, a Sudanese group that tracks violence in the country, said in a statement on Tuesday, adding that many of the dead and injured were women and children. The group blamed the Sudanese military for the strike and condemned the attack, saying it turned a civilian market into a scene of mass killing. The Associated Press reported the statement early on Wednesday. Nyala, the capital of South Darfur, is under the control of the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces, which have fought the Sudanese army since 2023, and a United Nations report cited in the same reporting put about 34 million people, two thirds of Sudan's population, in need of humanitarian assistance.

A drone strike hit a market in the north of Nyala, the capital of South Darfur in western Sudan, at about 8 p.m. on Monday, killing at least 21 people and injuring about 40, a medical source told AFP, with a medic at Nyala Hospital putting the injured at more than 40. Nyala, one of the largest cities in Darfur, is the de facto capital of the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces, which have fought Sudan's army since April 2023. A resident told AFP the market was frequented by armed men, particularly RSF fighters.

Neither the army nor the RSF had commented in the reporting read for the original item, and that reporting did not directly attribute the strike to either side, noting only that the Sudanese military regularly strikes Nyala. The war has killed tens of thousands of people, displaced millions and pushed parts of Sudan into famine, according to the United Nations.
