**Latest:** SK hynix disclosed on November 27 that it is doubling the pace of its ongoing treasury share buyback, raising its daily purchase order volume from about 350,000 shares to about 700,000 shares after judging that the recent share price decline had deepened the stock's undervaluation. The shares closed at 1,110,000 won on November 27, down from an intraday high of 2,987,000 won on June 25. The company said its policy of continuing buybacks is unchanged and that, as flagged at its third-quarter results, a follow-up purchase will follow a board resolution once the current acquisition is complete.

SK hynix reported the implementation status of its 2026 Corporate Value-up Plan in a Korea Exchange disclosure furnished to the SEC on Form 6-K on Wednesday, saying it is pursuing shareholder returns of approximately 100 trillion won for fiscal year 2026, made up of cash dividends of 30 trillion won or more and acquisition and cancellation of treasury shares of 60 trillion won or more.

The company said revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 237 trillion won and that it held net cash of 99 trillion won at that date, alongside credit rating upgrades from domestic and overseas agencies. The plan was reported to a meeting of its independent directors on November 23, and the company noted the return figures are forward-looking objectives subject to market and business conditions.
