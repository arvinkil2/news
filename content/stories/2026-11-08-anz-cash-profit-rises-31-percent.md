ANZ Group reported cash profit of A$7.59 billion for the year ended September 30, up 31 percent from A$5.79 billion a year earlier and in line with a Visible Alpha consensus of A$7.55 billion, as operating expenses fell 14 percent on lower restructuring charges and the absence of last year's securities regulator settlement. The Australia Retail division drove the improvement, with annual cash profit climbing to A$1.71 billion from A$771 million on higher home loan volumes, taking divisional net loans and advances to A$280 billion from A$269 billion.

The offset was margin pressure: the net interest margin slid to 1.53 percent on competition across most divisions and the timing of Reserve Bank of Australia rate changes, the common equity tier 1 ratio slipped to 12.0 percent, and the final dividend was held at 83 Australian cents a share, matching last year's payout.
