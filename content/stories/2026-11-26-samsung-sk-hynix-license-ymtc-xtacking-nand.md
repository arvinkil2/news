Samsung Electronics and SK hynix have agreed cross-licensing deals covering parts of Yangtze Memory Technologies' Xtacking technology for 3D NAND flash, Nikkei Asia reported, citing three people briefed on the matter. Xtacking builds memory cells and control circuitry on separate wafers and joins them by wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding, an approach YMTC introduced in 2018 and has used in volume production since 2019, and one that becomes harder to avoid as NAND stacking approaches 400 layers and the stress on circuitry built under the cells grows. Counterpoint Research puts YMTC's share of global NAND revenue at 14 percent in the second quarter of 2026, up from 11 percent a year earlier and level with Kioxia, behind Samsung at 28 percent and SK hynix at 19 percent. Samsung told Nikkei only that wafer bonding eases the structural limits of stacking and allows higher density, without confirming a licensing deal; SK hynix declined to comment and YMTC did not respond.
