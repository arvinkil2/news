Protesters from the Indian Youth Congress, the youth wing of the opposition Congress party, stormed into the building housing Meta's office in Hyderabad on Friday, smashing plants and clashing with police, and demonstrated outside its Mumbai and Gurugram offices over the restriction of Instagram accounts belonging to party leaders. Two accounts were restored after the protests, but the group's social media head Manu Jain told Reuters on Saturday that thousands of accounts of journalists, creators and influencers critical of the government remain restricted, and the group warned it would storm Meta offices across the country unless they are restored.

Meta declined to comment and has said it restricts content in India to comply with local laws. Its disclosures show it restricted more than 5.4 million posts or accounts in India under local laws in the first half of 2026, second only to Indonesia and up from about 70,000 in the preceding six months, after the government in February cut the deadline for removing unlawful content from 36 hours to three hours.
