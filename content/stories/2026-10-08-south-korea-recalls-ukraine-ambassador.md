South Korea said Thursday it will recall its ambassador to Ukraine, the sharpest escalation yet in the row over two North Korean prisoners of war transferred from Ukrainian custody to Seoul. The dispute dates to last month, when President Zelensky publicly announced the transfer in a UN General Assembly address; Seoul says Kyiv broke an agreement to keep the handover secret, and President Lee Jae Myung condemned the disclosure.

The two soldiers were among the thousands of North Korean troops fighting alongside Russian forces in the war. Their debriefing is Seoul's most direct window into the North's battlefield role, and the recall signals how little trust the episode left between the two governments.
