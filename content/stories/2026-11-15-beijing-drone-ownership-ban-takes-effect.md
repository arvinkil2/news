Beijing's ban on possessing drones came into force on Sunday, covering urban and rural areas across the municipality, an area of about 16,400 square kilometres. Owning or storing an unmanned aerial vehicle is now prohibited, transporting drones or their components into the city is forbidden, and anyone found in possession faces a fine and confiscation, with exemptions possible for purposes such as counterterrorism, law enforcement, emergency relief, agriculture and scientific research. Owners had been told in advance to dispose of their devices at approved buy-back points, sell them online or move them outside the enforcement area. The ownership ban, announced in September, goes beyond the ban on drone sales Beijing imposed in May, when owners were required to register devices but could keep them; authorities said the purpose is to safeguard the security of the capital.
