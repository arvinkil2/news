The European Commission has opened a formal antitrust investigation into Coca-Cola Europacific Partners, the UK-headquartered bottler that is one of the two largest European suppliers of Coca-Cola brands. The Commission says it is concerned CCEP may have abused a dominant position in the wholesale supply of carbonated soft drinks and sports drinks to off-trade customers, by making wholesale price reductions conditional on the drinks not being exported to other member states, by differentiating products between member states to restrict cross-border trade, by giving customers in Spain incentives to restrict imports, and by refusing wholesalers the chance to negotiate purchases for several countries together.

The investigation follows unannounced inspections at CCEP premises in several member states in March 2025 and will be carried out as a priority, the Commission said, under case number AT.40860. Opening the formal investigation does not prejudge the outcome.

In a Form 6-K filed with the SEC later on Monday, Coca-Cola Europacific Partners acknowledged the Commission announcement, said it takes competition law very seriously and is firmly committed to complying with all applicable laws in every market where it operates, and said it will continue to engage fully with the Commission while declining further comment at this stage.
