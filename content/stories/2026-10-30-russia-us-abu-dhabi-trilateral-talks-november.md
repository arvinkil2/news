Russia and the United States have begun coordinating the exact date of a trilateral meeting with Ukraine in Abu Dhabi, Russian presidential aide Yuri Ushakov told the Vesti journalist Pavel Zarubin, as reported by TASS on Friday. Ushakov said it is definitely clear the meeting will be held in November, that Moscow has passed its considerations to the Americans and is awaiting their answer, and that the timing will depend on factors of both a domestic and an international character. Only mid November had been mentioned before, with no concrete date discussed, and neither Washington nor Kyiv had confirmed Ushakov's account in reporting available at the time of writing.
