Five of the largest US pharmaceutical companies, Merck, Eli Lilly, AbbVie, Pfizer and Bristol Myers Squibb, have told the House Select Committee on China that they do not plan to start new clinical trial sites in the Xinjiang region of China or at hospitals affiliated with the Chinese military, and all five indicated they would not initiate new clinical studies at sites linked to the People's Liberation Army, STAT reported. The committee opened investigations into the five companies last June over concerns about intellectual property exposure, military entanglements and ethical standards at trial sites in sensitive regions of the country, and said in its letters to the companies that concern over security risks is rising.
