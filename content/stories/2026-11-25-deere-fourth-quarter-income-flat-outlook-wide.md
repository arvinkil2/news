Deere & Company reported net income of 1.051 billion dollars for the fourth quarter ended November 1, 2026, or 3.89 dollars per share, compared with 1.065 billion dollars a year earlier, as worldwide net sales and revenues fell 2 percent to 12.115 billion dollars. Production and precision agriculture, its largest segment, saw sales fall 15 percent to 4.018 billion dollars and operating profit fall 33 percent to 406 million dollars.

For fiscal 2027 the company forecast net income of 4.50 to 5.25 billion dollars, a range it said reflects continued uncertainty in key end markets and includes an estimated one-time, non-cash pension settlement charge of 0.25 to 0.40 billion dollars. Chief executive John May said the company is seeing early signs of improvement in large agriculture, led by North America.
