**Latest.** Mahanadi Coalfields will raise 22.95 billion rupees ($245.6 million) from anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering, two people familiar with the matter told Reuters, with the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority and Life Insurance Corporation of India in the book alongside HDFC Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund and Nippon India Mutual Fund. The company's anchor allotment, filed with the exchanges, allotted about 168.77 million shares at 136 rupees, the top of the price band, to 53 institutions, with Life Insurance Corporation of India the largest single anchor at 41.76 million shares for about 5.68 billion rupees. The Abu Dhabi Investment Authority declined to comment to Reuters, and neither Mahanadi Coalfields nor Life Insurance Corporation of India responded to its requests. Public subscription opens on Thursday, November 19, and closes on Monday, November 23.

Mahanadi Coalfields, Coal India's wholly owned unit in Odisha, set a price band of 129 to 136 rupees a share for its initial public offering, seeking up to 89.81 billion rupees ($953 million) at the top end and a market value of up to 90,010 crore rupees, about $9.6 billion. The sale is entirely an offer for sale, with Coal India selling up to 661.84 million shares, a 10 percent stake, and no fresh shares issued, so Mahanadi Coalfields itself receives no proceeds. Bidding runs from November 19 to November 23, with anchor investors bidding on November 18, and the company, which produced about 28 percent of Coal India's output in fiscal 2026, is the parent's third subsidiary to list this year.
