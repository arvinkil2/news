Mahanadi Coalfields, Coal India's wholly owned unit in Odisha, set a price band of 129 to 136 rupees a share for its initial public offering, seeking up to 89.81 billion rupees ($953 million) at the top end and a market value of up to 90,010 crore rupees, about $9.6 billion. The sale is entirely an offer for sale, with Coal India selling up to 661.84 million shares, a 10 percent stake, and no fresh shares issued, so Mahanadi Coalfields itself receives no proceeds. Bidding runs from November 19 to November 23, with anchor investors bidding on November 18, and the company, which produced about 28 percent of Coal India's output in fiscal 2026, is the parent's third subsidiary to list this year.
