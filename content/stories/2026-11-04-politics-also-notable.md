United States election administration. The Justice Department sent about 1,000 election monitors to all 50 states for Tuesday's midterms, Axios reported, an unusually broad deployment in a cycle already marked by federal litigation over ballots in California and Alaska. Single sourced to Axios in this pool.

Commonwealth summit. King Charles addressed the Commonwealth summit in Antigua, where Kenya's deputy president was among the leaders arriving, as campaigners pressed demands for a slavery apology. The Guardian's report is the single source in this pool; no apology or reparations commitment was announced.

European Parliament. MEPs backed tighter controls on drug precursors while protecting legitimate trade, in a press release from the Parliament on November 4. The position is a negotiating mandate, not a final law, which still requires agreement with member states.

Ghana. Parliament in Accra faces a crowded 28 sitting day agenda covering the 2027 budget, constitutional amendments and the anti LGBTQ bill, and a separate row over diplomatic passports prompted a minority walkout. Both items are single sourced to Ghanaian outlets in this pool.

Brazil. Sao Paulo's municipal chamber rejected an impeachment request against Mayor Ricardo Nunes, UOL reported in Portuguese. Single sourced; the vote ends that proceeding, with no further step announced.

Japan and the ICC. The Asahi Shimbun reported, in Japanese, that support measures for the International Criminal Court under US sanctions will become a political discussion at the state parties' annual meeting. Single sourced and read in translation; no measure has been adopted.
