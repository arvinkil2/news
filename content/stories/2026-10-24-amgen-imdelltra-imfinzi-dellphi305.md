Adding Amgen's Imdelltra (tarlatamab) to AstraZeneca's Imfinzi (durvalumab) as first-line maintenance reduced the risk of death by 58 percent (hazard ratio 0.42, p<0.001) in the 563-patient Phase 3 DeLLphi-305 trial in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer patients who had not progressed after initial Imfinzi, platinum chemotherapy and etoposide, Amgen announced at the ESMO Congress in Madrid, with the results published simultaneously in The New England Journal of Medicine. Median overall survival was 21.1 months on the combination against 12.7 months on Imfinzi alone, nearly 80 percent of combination patients were alive at one year against 53 percent, and median progression-free survival was 7.4 months against 1.9 months. Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events occurred in 20.9 percent of combination patients against 10.8 percent on Imfinzi alone, and cytokine release syndrome was reported in 49.6 percent of Imdelltra patients, predominantly grade 1 or 2 with one grade 3 event and no grade 4 or higher events.
