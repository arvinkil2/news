Five members of the same immediate family died in a shooting Friday night at 1807 Robin Lane in Douglas, Georgia, that authorities describe as an apparent domestic murder-suicide. Douglas police and Coffee County sheriff's deputies responded around 11:09 p.m. to an active-shooter call placed by one of the victims; they found four adults dead inside the home and a fifth who later died at Coffee Regional Medical Center. At a Saturday press conference, police chief Brannen Pruette said the wife had called police earlier Friday evening over a domestic complaint and been taken to a hospital before the fatal shooting; no motive has been stated and the investigation remains active.
