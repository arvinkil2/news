Joseph Wu, secretary-general of Taiwan's National Security Council, posted a map on Wednesday showing 53 Chinese ships, 34 of them navy warships, with the Liaoning carrier group positioned east of the Philippines, other formations off Taiwan's east and southeast coasts and three vessels off the southern coast of Japan, Reuters reported. Japan's Defence Ministry said it had tracked three Chinese warships and a coast guard ship a day earlier as they passed through the Osumi Strait off Kyushu and into the Pacific.

China's defence ministry spokesman Zhang Xiaogang said on Thursday that the Liaoning's training in the western Pacific was an annual routine to test long-range, open-ocean combat capability and was not directed at any particular country. Taiwan's defence minister, Wellington Koo, told reporters at parliament in Taipei that Taiwan was building its own deterrence capabilities and that China had never abandoned the use of force to annex Taiwan.
