**Codelco wins approval to run Gabriela Mistral to 2055.** The Environmental Assessment Commission (Coeva) of Chile's Antofagasta region unanimously approved the environmental study for Codelco's $868 million operational continuity and water transition project at Gabriela Mistral on Monday. The plan extends mining by 28 years, from 2028 through 2055, holding copper cathode production at about 150,000 tonnes a year, with plant capacity of 46 million tonnes a year and a maximum average mining rate of 91.1 million tonnes a year. Submitted for review in December 2024, the project shifts the mine away from its current inland water wells from the ninth year of the extension, with construction from January 2028 employing an average of about 230 workers. The approval can still face administrative or legal challenges, including a possible review by the Committee of Ministers. It lands as Codelco works to reverse the production declines of 2022 and 2023.

**Collective Mining commits $100 million, with $1 billion conditional.** Canadian-listed Collective Mining plans to invest $100 million in Colombia in 2027 on exploration and permitting at its Guayabales project in Caldas, chairman Ari Sussman said on Tuesday. Colombian Vice President Jose Manuel Restrepo said the company could invest as much as $1 billion more through 2030 if it secures the required licence. The Apollo deposit at Guayabales holds an initial indicated resource of 2.58 million gold-equivalent ounces and an inferred resource of 2.83 million ounces; Collective targets an environmental licence application in the second half of 2027 and early works in the second half of 2029. On Wednesday the company added drill results finding gold and silver in rock classed as waste inside the proposed Apollo pit, which could convert waste to mill feed when the resource is updated in late 2027 or early 2028.
