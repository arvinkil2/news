President Trump has notified Congress and the governments of Rwanda and South Africa of his intent to terminate both countries from the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA) trade preference programme as of January 1, 2027, US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said in a statement on Monday. Greer said the annual interagency eligibility review found that both governments are engaging in practices inconsistent with AGOA's eligibility criteria, including barriers that undermine American trade and investment and practices that Greer said pose a threat to US national security and foreign policy interests in the region. For Rwanda, the statement lists concerns on trade and investment barriers, rule of law, political pluralism, internationally recognised worker rights and human rights; for South Africa, it lists barriers to US trade and investment, corruption, and threats to US national security and foreign policy interests.
