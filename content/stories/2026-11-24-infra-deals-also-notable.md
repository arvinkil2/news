Sanofi's acquisition of Insmed, agreed on November 24 at $175 per share in cash for a total equity value of about $41.0 billion, is carried as a full story on this site under life-sciences, where the Brinsupri and Arikayce assets are covered in detail. From the deals side it is the day's largest transaction by value, it was documented in Insmed's Form 8-K filed November 24 (accession 0001140361-26-045364, Item 1.01), and it is not duplicated here. Closing is expected in the first half of 2027, subject to Insmed shareholder and regulatory approvals.

CrowdStrike's $43.9 billion all-stock acquisition of Zscaler, announced November 23, is carried in full under technology and is cross-referenced here only: it is the second-largest deal of the two days and the largest pure software consolidation of the year, but the asset is a security platform, not infrastructure, and one version per event applies.

Jadestone Energy's board has accused Prima Energy, its Indonesian shareholder with about 28.2 percent, of attempting to gain control without paying a takeover premium, after Prima requested a general meeting to appoint its chief executive Pieters Utomo and former Jadestone chief executive Paul Blakeley as non-executive directors. Prima rejects the claim, saying two seats on a nine-member board would be 22.2 percent of seats, below its ownership share, and that it does not intend to remove the chairman or existing directors. No offer has been made.
