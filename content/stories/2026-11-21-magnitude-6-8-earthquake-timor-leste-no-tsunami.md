A magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck offshore northeast of Timor-Leste at 09:42 UTC on Saturday, centred 103 kilometres northeast of Lospalos at a depth of about 143 kilometres, according to the US Geological Survey. The USGS issued a Green PAGER alert, its lowest impact category, estimating that 86,000 people experienced light shaking and 64,000 moderate shaking, with a low likelihood of casualties and damage.

There is no tsunami threat from the earthquake, and no casualties or damage had been reported as of this update. Indonesia's meteorology agency BMKG published its own solution rating the same event magnitude 7.2 at a depth of 125 kilometres in waters northwest of Southwest Maluku Regency, and likewise said it posed no tsunami threat while advising residents to stay alert for aftershocks.
