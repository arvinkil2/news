The Swiss Bank Employees Association has told lawmakers that a UBS relocation forced by stricter capital requirements could eliminate 10,000 to 12,000 positions from the bank's Swiss workforce of about 34,000, NZZ am Sonntag reported on Sunday, and asked parliament for a workable compromise. The warning comes before a lower house committee discussion of capital requirements for UBS's foreign subsidiaries; the upper house proposed in September that those units be backed by 90 percent common equity tier 1 capital, a level UBS has called excessive. The cuts the association describes would come on top of reductions already under way as UBS finishes integrating Credit Suisse, and the report carried no comment from UBS itself.
