Tripadvisor said on Monday that it closed the sale of TheFork on November 1, selling the legal entities behind its European online restaurant reservation and management platform to American Express Travel Related Services Company for 700 million dollars in cash, subject to certain adjustments, under the purchase agreement the two sides signed on August 2, 2026. Tripadvisor will provide transition services to American Express for 12 months, and the company said the sale counts as a significant disposition, with pro forma financial statements to be filed by amendment by November 5.

Tripadvisor said it expects to discuss the transaction on its third quarter earnings call on November 5, 2026.
