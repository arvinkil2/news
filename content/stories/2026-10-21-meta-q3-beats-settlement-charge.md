Meta's third quarter revenue rose 26 percent to $64.60 billion, above analyst expectations of $63.33 billion, with advertising revenue up 26 percent to $63.06 billion and earnings of $3.83 per share beating the $3.57 consensus. The quarter carried a $9.89 billion legal charge for the settlement with 52 state attorneys general over teen mental health litigation; without it, income from operations would have been $22.89 billion, and Meta guided fourth quarter revenue to $73 billion to $76 billion.

The company forecast capital spending of $135 billion to $145 billion as it continues building AI infrastructure, and its board approved a new publicly listed nonvoting Class C share, effective January 19, 2027, to be issued as a one-time dividend of one Class C share for each Class A and Class B share held, with the effect of a two for one split on the Class A price and no change in voting power.
