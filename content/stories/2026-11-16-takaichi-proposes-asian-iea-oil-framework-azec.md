Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi is expected to propose, at the Asia Zero Emission Community summit in Manila on Tuesday, the creation of a new framework under which countries cooperate on procuring oil in a crisis, as a measure to strengthen the resilience of energy supply, Yonhap reported, citing Kyodo News. Japanese reporting has described the plan as an Asian version of the International Energy Agency.

She was also expected to present a plan under which Japan would stockpile oil for Southeast Asian countries inside Japan in return for a fee. AZEC is a regional decarbonisation grouping launched in 2023 under Japan's leadership, with 11 participating countries including Australia and Indonesia, and the proposal is framed as strengthening cooperation with ASEAN, whose leaders Takaichi is meeting in Manila this week.
