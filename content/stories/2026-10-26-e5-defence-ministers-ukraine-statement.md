The defence ministers of France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the United Kingdom, meeting as the Group of Five, or E5, said Russia remains the most acute and long-term threat to Euro-Atlantic security, citing its war against Ukraine, violations of Allied airspace, sabotage and hybrid attacks. The five said Europe must assume greater responsibility for its own security, taking more political and military leadership inside NATO while describing the United States as an indispensable ally whose continued engagement is essential.

On Ukraine, the ministers pledged to maintain what they called unwavering support by all available means, politically, militarily and financially, and to keep tackling Russia's shadow fleet in line with international law. They said the Coalition of the Willing would contribute security guarantees once a ceasefire or peace agreement is in place.
