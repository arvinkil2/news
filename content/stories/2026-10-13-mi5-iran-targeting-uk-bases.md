In his annual speech on Tuesday, MI5 Director-General Ken McCallum said there had been a major shift in Tehran's calculus this year toward "harder targets, like military sites," and that British authorities had carried out 50 arrests over the past year to disrupt Iran-linked operations. The comments follow the September 27 incident at RAF Fairford, where three vans carrying petrol approached the base used by US bombers for strikes on Iran; the United States withdrew its B-1 and B-52 aircraft from the facility on October 4, and on Monday the UK sanctioned two Iranian nationals it accused of facilitating hostile activity. McCallum also doubted Iran's ability to mount complex attacks inside Britain, citing the limited sophistication of the operatives seen so far, and Tehran has denied any involvement in the Fairford incident.
