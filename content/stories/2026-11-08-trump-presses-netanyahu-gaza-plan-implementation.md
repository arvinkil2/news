President Donald Trump told Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in a phone call on Thursday, November 5, that he wants him to take steps to implement his Gaza peace plan, Axios reported, citing two sources with knowledge of the matter. The plan calls for disarming Hamas, demilitarising Gaza and transferring responsibility for governing the enclave to a new Palestinian technocratic government.

Axios said implementation has been on hold for months because of the Israeli elections held on October 27. The report carried no specific steps, dates or conditions, and no response from Netanyahu.
