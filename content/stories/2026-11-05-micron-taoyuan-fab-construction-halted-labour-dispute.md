Construction at Micron's Fab 11E site in Taoyuan, Taiwan, a $4.5 billion plant, has all but halted except for minor safety work, Bloomberg reported via Tom's Hardware, as Micron and its Taoyuan union have failed to agree on clearer profit-sharing terms. The union held a one-day strike last month, and the two sides are set to meet again on November 6, with no resumption timeline given.

Micron has not released a statement about the suspension itself but has informed local officials about it, and said its operations continue as normal and it is confident in maintaining business continuity for customers. The company recently announced a bonus of up to five times annual salary for some eligible workers and has reached a deal with its larger Taichung union.
