US, Ukrainian and European officials met in Miami on Friday to discuss a proposal for ending the Russia-Ukraine war, with US envoy Steve Witkoff describing constructive discussions on de-escalation and a durable peace before winter. The meeting included the E3, the European Commission, Ukraine and NATO, and covered energy resilience, grain exports, security guarantees and Ukraine's post-war reconstruction including EU accession. The diplomacy followed Witkoff and Jared Kushner's visits to Moscow and Kyiv last month, but came as Putin told Trump that talks with Ukraine are unlikely and as the US-Russia diesel deal drew criticism from Kyiv and European allies.
