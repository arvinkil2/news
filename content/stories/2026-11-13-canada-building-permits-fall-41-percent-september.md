The total value of building permits issued in Canada declined 552.8 million dollars, or 4.1 percent, to 12.9 billion dollars in September, Statistics Canada reported, though the total was still 5.7 percent higher than a year earlier. Residential intentions fell 5.8 percent to 7.8 billion dollars, as a 9.4 percent drop in multi-family permits to 5.1 billion dollars, led by British Columbia and Alberta, outweighed a 2.0 percent rise in single-family permits to 2.7 billion dollars, and dwelling units authorized fell 8.6 percent to 22,934. Non-residential permits fell 1.5 percent to 5.1 billion dollars, with institutional permits down 9.7 percent partly offset by a 7.7 percent rise in industrial permits, while Ontario was the only large province to rise, up 6.6 percent.
