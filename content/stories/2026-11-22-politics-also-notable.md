Senate leadership race. Michigan Senator Elissa Slotkin endorsed Chris Van Hollen in his challenge to Chuck Schumer for Senate majority leader, saying Senate Democrats need new leadership for the next Congress. The New York Times count reported five public endorsements for Van Hollen and public support from 15 senators for Schumer, with the secret ballot leadership vote scheduled for early December.

US sanctions notices. The Federal Register for November 23 carries formal notices of OFAC and State Department sanctions actions, the gazette publication of designations announced earlier in the week. They include the Comanchero outlaw motorcycle group and five named members and associates, designated on November 19 under Executive Order 14059 over illicit drug trafficking links, in what AUSTRAC called the first OFAC action against an Australian based organised crime group. The underlying designations were covered in full stories on November 19, so the notices themselves are a record update, not new designations.

FIFA politics. South America's CONMEBOL reiterated its support for Gianni Infantino's re-election as FIFA president, and Ghana's football association said all 54 CAF nations back him, ahead of the 2027 FIFA presidential election in which Infantino faces challengers including Diallo and Tashima.

Spain campaign calendar. Spain's pre-election ban on publishing opinion polls runs from November 24 to 28, ahead of the November 29 snap general election covered in a full story today.
