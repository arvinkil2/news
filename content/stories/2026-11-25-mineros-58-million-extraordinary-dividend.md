Mineros S.A. (TSX: MSA; BVC: MINEROS) shareholders have approved the release of occasional reserves of US$58,075,280, constituted out of profits of fiscal years ended on or before December 31, 2025, and the distribution of that amount as a one time extraordinary cash dividend of US$0.20 per common share. The approval came at a special meeting of the General Shareholders Assembly held on November 24.

The extraordinary dividend will be paid in a single instalment on December 18, 2026, to shareholders of record on December 14, 2026, a change from the December 1 record and December 4 payment dates proposed in the management information circular dated October 15. Based on 290,376,398 common shares outstanding, the aggregate amount is approximately US$58,075,280; the final amount will depend on the number of shares outstanding on the record date.

The extraordinary dividend is in addition to, and does not affect, the ordinary dividend of US$0.10 per share approved on March 27, 2026 and payable in four quarterly instalments. Chief executive Daniel Henao said shareholder returns are a central part of Mineros' strategy and that the approval confirms shareholders' support of management.
