Russia's Foreign Ministry announced on Wednesday that Alexander Darchiev, its ambassador to the United States, died suddenly on November 11 at the age of 66. The ministry called him an outstanding diplomat and an exceptional professional, and expressed profound sorrow in a statement shared on Telegram. It gave no cause of death, and the Russian embassy in Washington said he had devoted his entire life to defending Russian interests and building relations with the United States.

Darchiev had served in Washington since March 2025, after President Vladimir Putin appointed him to the post. He previously led the Foreign Ministry's North America Department and spent seven years as Russia's ambassador to Canada. No successor or interim ambassador was named in the reports read.
