Nvidia fell 14.01 percent, the largest single move in the tables, extending the AI selloff that this week's reporting has tracked through the S&P 500 correction and the SEC accounting probe story (see the November 20 Nvidia story). Broadcom fell 12.19 percent and the semiconductor index fell 10.55 percent in the same complex. No separate company news in today's reporting explains the size of the Broadcom or sector moves beyond the continuing correction.

Tesla fell 7.64 percent. Today's reporting carries positive Tesla news, the $16.8 billion Terafab groundbreaking leading October construction starts (see today's infrastructure roundup), so there is no clear single driver for the share fall in today's reporting.

Silver futures fell 7.41 percent and gold futures fell 4.62 percent to $3,515.40 an ounce, extending the precious metals slide covered in today's mining roundup, where miners' costs are still rising as prices approach $3,500. Meta fell 5.93 percent with the wider technology complex, and the Nikkei 225 fell 5.06 percent, following the Asia plunge pattern of the previous session.

Markets are closed on Saturday, so the midday tables still reflect the November 20 close and no figure in today's commodities or rates stories has moved materially since the morning edition.

The cross market read is unambiguous risk off with a flight to quality underneath it: equities, chips, metals and miners fell together, the Nasdaq 100 lost 5.61 percent and the S&P 500 lost 3.70 percent, while US Treasury yields dropped, the 5 year by 16.5 basis points to 4.70 percent and the 10 year by 14.0 basis points to 4.91 percent. Natural gas, up 4.38 percent, was the only material gainer in the tables.
