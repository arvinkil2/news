Russian strikes overnight hit energy installations in four Ukrainian regions, Dnipropetrovsk, Vinnytsia, Zhytomyr and Khmelnytskyi, striking substations, transformers and transmission lines that supply homes, President Volodymyr Zelensky said on Telegram, with repair teams working to restore service. Ukraine's air force said it shot down 371 of 433 long-range drones launched by Russia since the previous night, and Zelensky said Moscow is exploiting Ukraine's shortage of ballistic missile interceptors to maximise the intensity of the attacks. Ukraine said its own strikes hit the Kamensky factory in Russia's Rostov region and a defence industry site Zelensky valued at about 70 million dollars of air defence assets, and caused significant damage to an industrial facility in the Nizhny Novgorod region, which regional governor Gleb Nikitin confirmed; Russia's defence ministry said it shot down 235 Ukrainian drones overnight.
