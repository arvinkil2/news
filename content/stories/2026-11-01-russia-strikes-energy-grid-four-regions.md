Latest, November 1: The strikes widened on Sunday afternoon. In the Ternopil region, a Russian strike hit an energy facility in the Kremenets community, leaving 12 settlements fully or partially without electricity, the head of the regional military administration, Taras Pastukh, said, with power workers trying to restore supply; RIA Novosti carried the same 12-settlement figure. In the Vinnytsia region, a Russian drone hit the grounds of a vocational school dormitory in the Koziatyn community, killing a man at the scene and a woman who died in intensive care, and hospitalising three children, two five-year-old girls and a 16-year-old boy, with moderate injuries, the regional administration head, Natalia Zabolotna, and the State Emergency Service said, adding that two cars caught fire in the hit. Two drones also hit a civilian administrative building in Chernivtsi on Sunday, wounding a 67-year-old man with shrapnel, the Chernivtsi regional head, Ruslan Osipenko, said, and an explosion was reported in Rivne after Ukraine's air force warned that a jet-powered drone was heading for the city. All of Sunday's figures are Ukrainian officials' accounts and none has been independently confirmed.

Russian strikes overnight hit energy installations in four Ukrainian regions, Dnipropetrovsk, Vinnytsia, Zhytomyr and Khmelnytskyi, striking substations, transformers and transmission lines that supply homes, President Volodymyr Zelensky said on Telegram, with repair teams working to restore service. Ukraine's air force said it shot down 371 of 433 long-range drones launched by Russia since the previous night, and Zelensky said Moscow is exploiting Ukraine's shortage of ballistic missile interceptors to maximise the intensity of the attacks. Ukraine said its own strikes hit the Kamensky factory in Russia's Rostov region and a defence industry site Zelensky valued at about 70 million dollars of air defence assets, and caused significant damage to an industrial facility in the Nizhny Novgorod region, which regional governor Gleb Nikitin confirmed; Russia's defence ministry said it shot down 235 Ukrainian drones overnight.
