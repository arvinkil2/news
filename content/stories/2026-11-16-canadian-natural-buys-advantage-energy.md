Canadian Natural Resources has agreed to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of Advantage Energy for 13.55 Canadian dollars a share in cash, under a definitive arrangement agreement dated November 16, 2026, the two Calgary companies announced on Monday. The price values Advantage's equity at about 2.255 billion Canadian dollars on a fully diluted basis and the total transaction at about 2.53 billion Canadian dollars, including Advantage's net debt and its outstanding convertible debentures. Reuters put the total at 1.77 billion US dollars including debt.

Advantage said the 13.55 dollar price exceeds the highest trading price of its shares over the past 18 years, and Reuters reported it represents a premium of nearly 25 percent to Friday's closing price. Advantage's board has unanimously approved the transaction, which follows what the company described as a comprehensive review of strategic alternatives. The deal will be completed by way of a statutory plan of arrangement under Alberta law.

The acquisition brings Canadian Natural about 79,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day of production from Advantage's Montney and Charlie Lake assets, at a transaction value of about 32,000 dollars per flowing barrel a day, together with a light crude oil and liquids-rich natural gas development inventory that sits next to Canadian Natural's extensive Grande Prairie operations in Alberta. Canadian Natural will also satisfy the obligations on Advantage's 143.75 million Canadian dollars of 5 percent convertible unsecured subordinated debentures.

Canadian Natural said it will fund the purchase from cash on hand and its existing bank credit facilities. The transaction is targeted to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to shareholder, court and regulatory approvals and other customary conditions. Advantage's normal course issuer bid and automatic share purchase plan purchases terminate following the announcement.
