South Korea's exports rose 93.7 percent in October from a year earlier to a preliminary $115.28 billion, the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy said on November 1, the second-largest monthly total on record after September's $121.1 billion and a second straight month above $110 billion. Semiconductor exports jumped 259.4 percent to $56.44 billion, above $50 billion for a second month, and exports rose for 18 of the 20 leading items and in seven of nine destination regions, with shipments to China up 133.6 percent to $26.98 billion and to the United States up 185.8 percent to $24.9 billion.

Imports rose 36.0 percent to $72.78 billion, leaving a trade surplus of $42.5 billion, above $40 billion for a second straight month. Cumulative exports for January through October reached $930 billion, up 60.6 percent from a year earlier and past $900 billion for the first time, and Trade Minister Kim Jung-kwan said annual exports will reach $1 trillion in November if the current pace holds.
