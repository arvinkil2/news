Ecuador's Attorney General's Office said it executed arrest warrants against three people suspected of money laundering, as part of an investigation into seven people over inconsistencies in contributions and the lawful source of funds during the 2023 presidential campaign. Investigators are examining the use of a network of entities to channel approximately $500,000 for campaign advertising between May and August 2023, and allege the money may have been transferred without disclosing its sources or the identities of contributors.

Gonzalez, an ally of former President Rafael Correa who has denied wrongdoing in earlier financing probes, called the accusations absurd and compared her situation to that of a political prisoner. The Citizen Revolution Movement's president, Gabriela Rivadeneira, denounced the arrest as political persecution and demanded her immediate release, and Correa also rejected the allegations.
