The European Commission and Jordan signed a memorandum of understanding on November 18 paving the way for up to 500 million euros in EU macro-financial assistance, to be disbursed between 2027 and 2028 as the fifth such operation, bringing combined support under the fourth and fifth operations to 1 billion euros. At the same conference the two sides signed a 110 million euro multi-sector budget support agreement and a 63 million euro financing agreement, and the Commission launched negotiations on a Sustainable Investment Facilitation Agreement with Jordan.

The signings implement the EU-Jordan Strategic and Comprehensive Partnership backed by a 3 billion euro package for 2025 to 2027, made up of 640 million euros in grants, around 1.4 billion euros in additional investment and 1 billion euros in concessional loans. Team Europe is separately mobilising 1.6 billion euros for Jordan's water security centred on the National Conveyance Project, a desalination scheme the Commission says will meet around 40 percent of the country's drinking water needs.
