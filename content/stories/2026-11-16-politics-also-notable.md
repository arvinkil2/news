**Blasts in Bahrain as Iran announces attack on US base.** Blasts and sirens were reported in Bahrain as Iran announced an attack on a US base, in an item carried by five publishers in the feed pool. Bahrain hosts the US Navy's Fifth Fleet. The claim comes from a party to an active conflict and had no independent corroboration at write time; it is recorded here as Iran's announcement, not as a confirmed strike, and sits alongside today's confirmed tanker strike in Hormuz on the commodities beat.

**Estonia to close all border crossings with Russia.** Estonia moved to close all border crossings with Russia after an attack on a drone maker, in reporting carried by four publishers in the pool. The closure would sever the remaining road links between the EU member state and Russia and follows the pattern of Baltic border hardening this year.

**Jordan court sentences Karak attackers to death.** A Jordanian court sentenced the perpetrators of the Karak church attack to death, in reporting carried by five publishers in the pool. The sentences are the judicial close of one of the kingdom's most serious recent attacks on a Christian site.

**Nearly 1,500 migrants reach the Canary Islands in one day.** Nearly 1,500 migrants reached Spain's Canary Islands in a single day, in reporting carried by four publishers in the pool, one of the largest daily arrival counts on the Atlantic route this year and a renewed pressure point for Spanish and EU migration policy.
