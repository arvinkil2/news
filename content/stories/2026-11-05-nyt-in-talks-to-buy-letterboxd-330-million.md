The New York Times Company has entered exclusive negotiations to acquire Letterboxd for 330 million dollars, according to the Times' own reporting, relayed by TechCrunch. Letterboxd, a social platform for film fans founded in New Zealand, reported 28 million users in April, up from 3 million in January 2021, with more than half of its users under 35.

The platform is free with a paid tier for advanced features, and its projected earnings for this year are estimated at 15 million dollars, roughly 4.5 percent of the reported price. Film studio A24 was interested but was priced out, and Sony also showed interest, according to the report. The Times declined to comment.
