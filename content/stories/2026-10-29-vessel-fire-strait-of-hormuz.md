**Latest:** The tanker reported on fire off Khasab may be sinking. Splash247 reported that the master of a passing Panama flagged tanker saw the unidentified vessel on fire and mostly underwater, raising concern it had sunk or was sinking, and that another vessel was nearby, potentially assisting, although no rescue operation has been confirmed. Maritime security firm Vanguard described the incident as a suspected projectile strike; UKMTO has not independently confirmed that the vessel was struck by a projectile or that it is sinking, and the ship's identity, flag, ownership and cargo, and the fate of its crew, remain unconfirmed. Splash247 cited International Maritime Organization figures showing 121 confirmed maritime incidents across the Middle East this year as of October 27, with 24 seafarer fatalities.

UKMTO has received a sighting report of a tanker on fire in the Strait of Hormuz, at position 26 27.0N 056 24.6E, 9 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, with an incident time of 0205 UTC on Thursday. The advisory, categorised as fire or explosion, says the tanker's master is not responding to VHF calls and that authorities have been notified.

Vessels are advised to transit the area with caution and to report any incidents or suspicious activity to UKMTO. The tanker's name is withheld, no cause has been stated, and Reuters reported that the status of its crew is unknown, with no information yet on damage or environmental impact.
