A vessel has been reported on fire in the Strait of Hormuz, 9 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations centre said on Thursday. The report came from the master of a third-party ship that observed the incident, and the vessel itself has not been named.

No cause was given for the fire, the status of the crew is unknown, and there is no information yet on damage or environmental impact.
