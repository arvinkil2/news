The US Treasury removed Theint Win Htet from its sanctions list on Friday, ending a case that began when the Biden administration targeted her father's Shwe Byain Phyu Group in January 2024 over fuel supplies to the military. Her lawsuit argued she has no role in the family business, and her lawyer moved to dismiss the case after the delisting. The move follows a Reuters report that the Trump administration has opened talks with Myanmar's government, and drew condemnation from the advocacy group Justice For Myanmar, which called it deplorable while the military escalates attacks on civilians.
