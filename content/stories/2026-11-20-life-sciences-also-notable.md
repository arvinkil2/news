**Merck partners with Isomorphic Labs.** Merck has partnered with Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet-owned AI drug discovery company, to develop oncology drug candidates, STAT and Endpoints News reported. The deal extends the run of large pharma partnerships with AI discovery platforms into cancer.

**MegaRobo raises nearly $200 million.** Chinese AI laboratory automation company MegaRobo has raised nearly $200 million, Endpoints News reported, underlining the capital still flowing to Chinese robotics and laboratory AI even as US-China technology tensions widen.

**Lilly breaks ground in Pennsylvania.** Lilly has started work on its Pennsylvania obesity drug plant, Endpoints News reported, part of the company's US manufacturing expansion for its obesity franchise.

**UK device and gene therapy consultations.** UK regulators published guidance on the Innovative Devices Access Pathway and opened a consultation on the regulation of gene therapy medicinal products, setting out the next steps in the post-Brexit device and advanced therapy rulebooks.
