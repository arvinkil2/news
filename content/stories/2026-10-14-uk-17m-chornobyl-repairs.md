Britain is funding 17 million pounds in new support for repairs to the Chornobyl nuclear power plant, whose protective shelter was damaged by a Russian drone strike last year, the Chancellor and Foreign Secretary announced on Wednesday. The money forms part of an international programme coordinated by the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development with G7 donors, and will fund work to restore the structure's confinement and ventilation functions. The announcement followed a Downing Street visit by President Zelenskyy, and the release cited more than 200 Russian strikes on Ukrainian electricity infrastructure between October 5 and 11.
