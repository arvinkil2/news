Rackspace Technology entered a share purchase agreement on November 6 with Voss Capital, LP, acting for certain entities and managed accounts, and on November 9 issued 18,181,816 shares of its common stock under it for an aggregate cash purchase price of approximately 50 million dollars, the company reported in a Form 8-K. On November 9 it entered additional share purchase agreements with other purchasers and issued a further 5,818,181 shares for approximately 16 million dollars, taking the placement to about 24 million shares and roughly 66 million dollars in total. The shares were sold without registration under Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act to purchasers who represented that they are accredited investors acquiring the shares for investment.
