Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam announced the curfew in an address to the nation on Tuesday, covering Port Louis and major population centres, after a night of unrest that closed some schools. The protests followed the shooting of Elodie Thomas, 27, who was wounded while travelling home from work in a van in the south of the capital and underwent successful surgery to remove a bullet.

Local media reported that a police sergeant has been charged in connection with the shooting, and police said an investigation is under way, according to Reuters. The prime minister's office said the situation had returned to normal and the public sector would operate as usual, with the cabinet set to meet on Wednesday.
