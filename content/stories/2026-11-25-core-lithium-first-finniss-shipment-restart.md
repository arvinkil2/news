Core Lithium said the first shipment of spodumene concentrate produced since the restart of its Finniss Lithium Operation, in Australia's Northern Territory, was scheduled to begin loading at Darwin Port on Wednesday, November 25. The cargo of approximately 10,000 wet metric tonnes, grading about 5.1 percent lithium oxide, has been sold to a major international lithium chemicals producer under Core's five-year marketing agreement with Glencore, with provisional payment due in mid-December 2026 and final settlement after the price, weight and assay are confirmed. The shipment follows commissioning of the upgraded dense media separation plant and first concentrate production in September 2026, within six months of the restart; ore from the Grants open pit is expected to deliver about 134,000 tonnes of SC5-equivalent concentrate in total, with mining continuing into early in the fourth quarter of the 2026-27 financial year.
