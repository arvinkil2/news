Almost all participants judged that, under their baseline outlooks, further action to raise the target range for the federal funds rate would likely be appropriate to ensure a timely return of inflation to the 2 percent objective, broadly consistent with the September Summary of Economic Projections. A couple of participants noted that an increase of more than 25 basis points could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were unfavourable, and some observed that market-implied expectations of tightening steps in December and next year were broadly consistent with their own assessments. The staff estimated that total PCE inflation rose to 3.6 percent in the 12 months to September, with core PCE inflation at 3.1 percent.

The minutes also recorded an extended financial stability discussion of the AI buildout, in which the staff noted that off-balance-sheet leasing commitments and circular financing arrangements among AI-related firms could amplify losses if returns on AI investment were to disappoint, and almost all participants commented on the risks of an AI-related asset price revaluation.
