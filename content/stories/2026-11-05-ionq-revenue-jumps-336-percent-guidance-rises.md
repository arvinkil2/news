IonQ reported revenue of $173.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 336 percent from a year earlier and its sixth consecutive record quarter, with platform segment revenue up 127 percent to $90.3 million and SkyWater foundry services revenue of $83.5 million. Remaining performance obligations more than doubled to $1.2 billion, and the company raised its full year 2026 revenue guidance to between $505 million and $515 million.

GAAP net income attributable to IonQ was $564.0 million, or $1.42 per share, which the company said reflects the non-cash mark-to-market impact of warrants, while the adjusted EBITDA loss was $133.6 million. IonQ said it held $3.7 billion in cash, cash equivalents and investments at September 30 on a pro-forma basis for its October 16 equity offering, and that it is extending its quantum security platform by acquiring Elisity, a zero trust company.
