The European Commission has approved, under EU state aid rules, a €9.5 billion Dutch scheme to support renewable electricity production from two offshore wind farms in the North Sea, IJmuiden Ver Gamma-A and IJmuiden Ver Gamma-B. The two sites have a combined capacity of 2 GW and are expected to generate up to 7,441 GWh a year, with tenders to be held before July 2027 and support paid for 15 years from the start of operations, expected in 2032.

Aid will be awarded only if no bidder offers to develop a site subsidy free in return for a concession payment to the state. If aid is needed, bidders compete on the lowest variable premium over the market price, with a bid cap and a claw-back of part of the aid when market prices exceed the contracted strike price.
