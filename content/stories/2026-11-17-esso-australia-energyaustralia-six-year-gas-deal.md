Esso Australia Resources Pty Ltd, a subsidiary of ExxonMobil Australia, announced on Tuesday that it has reached a new agreement to supply natural gas to EnergyAustralia over a six-year period from 2027 to 2032, supplying the east coast domestic market with gas produced close to where consumers use it. Esso Chair Margaret Rogacki said the agreement shows the role long-term commercial arrangements play in meeting Australia's future energy needs, and EnergyAustralia Managing Director Mark Collette called the six-year deal with a long-term local supplier an essential part of delivering reliable supply while maintaining effective investment signals. The release disclosed no volumes or pricing.
