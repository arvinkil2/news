The Australian Securities and Investments Commission said it has begun civil penalty proceedings in the Federal Court against Commonwealth Bank of Australia, alleging the bank's CommBiz terms and conditions failed to accurately disclose all fees charged on direct debit and direct credit transactions, overcharging about 42,000 business customers by roughly A$8.5 million between November 2020 and May 31, 2025. ASIC is seeking declarations, pecuniary penalties and adverse publicity orders, and says the bank ceased charging the fees in June 2025 and has waived them since February 2026.

CBA said it had self-reported the matter and cooperated fully with ASIC's investigation, but does not accept some of the allegations and expects to defend certain aspects of the claim. The case is the first major action under ASIC chair Sarah Court, who took office on June 1.
