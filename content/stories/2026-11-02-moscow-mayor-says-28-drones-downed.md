**Latest:** Interfax reported at 02:39 Moscow time on Tuesday, November 3, that the number of drones destroyed on approach to Moscow had reached 41 since the start of the day. The agency said Sobyanin had reported 25 drones downed earlier in the night and then a further 16 Ukrainian drones destroyed, and that emergency services specialists were working where debris fell. The report mentioned no casualties, damage or airport closures.

Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said Russian air defences destroyed 28 drones flying toward the capital during Monday evening, November 2. Vedomosti, reporting his successive statements, put the sequence at eight drones downed earlier in the evening, a further ten reported at 23:28 Moscow time and another ten at 23:45, while Interfax reported the total reaching 28 after a final wave of ten, with emergency services specialists working where debris fell. The Defence Ministry had earlier said air defences destroyed 22 Ukrainian drones during the day over six Russian regions, including the Moscow region. Neither report mentioned casualties, and Ukraine had not commented on the claim.
