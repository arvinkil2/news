The European Medicines Agency said on Thursday its human medicines committee has recommended granting a marketing authorisation for Rasonque (daraxonrasib), an oral RAS inhibitor from Revolution Medicines, for adults with metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma whose disease has progressed after at least one previous treatment. In the open label phase 3 study of 500 patients, 92 percent of them with confirmed RAS G12 mutations, median overall survival was 12.4 months on Rasonque against 6.7 months on chemotherapy, and median progression free survival was 7.2 months against 3.6 months. The drug was reviewed under the agency's Cancer Medicines Pathfinder phased review, which cut assessment time to under 150 days, and the opinion now goes to the European Commission, which will fast track the EU wide authorisation decision.
