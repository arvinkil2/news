I-80 Gold Corp. has commenced production on schedule at its Archimedes underground mine and produced first gold. Archimedes is a high grade gold project on the company's Ruby Hill property within the Battle Mountain-Eureka gold trend in Nevada, and is the company's second underground mine to come online.

The company is mining oxide and sulphide ore at the project. The oxide material is being placed on the active heap leach pad on the Ruby Hill property, while the sulphide ore is being stockpiled for processing at the Lone Tree plant next year, executive vice president and chief operating officer Paul Chawrun said. The focus now turns to ramping up mining rates through 2027 and stockpiling refractory material ahead of the commissioning of the Lone Tree plant late in 2027. Once that plant is commissioned, the company is targeting consolidated annual gold production of 150,000 to 200,000 ounces beginning in 2028 from the Archimedes and Granite Creek underground projects.

Construction commenced in September 2025 and progressed largely in line with the capital development estimate in the March 2025 preliminary economic assessment, apart from a decision to expand and upgrade surface infrastructure to support future operations. Additional surface and underground infrastructure, including ventilation raises, a permanent mine dry and offices, will continue through 2027 to support the ramp up to planned mining rates.
