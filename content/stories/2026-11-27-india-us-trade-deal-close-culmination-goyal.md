India and the United States are close to wrapping up their long-anticipated trade deal, Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said on Friday, telling an industry conference in an online speech that the deal is close to culmination and that the process and pathway that had held back finalising it are now taking shape.

The comments followed a US Trade Representative proposal this week to impose Section 301 tariffs of up to 25 percent on Indian goods, with US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer saying Washington would cap the rate at 17 percent if a bilateral agreement is reached. India's trade ministry has said it is examining the US notice and told Washington during the investigation that India does not have structural excess capacity in any industry.
