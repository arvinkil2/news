Japan's services producer price index rose 4.2 percent from a year earlier in October, up from a revised 3.8 percent in September, and rose 1.1 percent on the month to 116.9, the Bank of Japan said in its preliminary release. Excluding international transportation, the index rose 3.2 percent from a year earlier, up from 3.0 percent in September.

Ocean freight transportation, up 120.4 percent from a year earlier, was the largest single driver, with transportation and postal activities contributing 1.53 percentage points of the headline increase. Hotels rose 12.1 percent on the year and leasing and rental rose 11.8 percent, while advertising services fell 1.2 percent.
