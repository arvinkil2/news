Indonesian police have charged former national police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo with treason after an investigation into alleged illegal wiretapping widened to activities authorities claim were aimed at undermining public trust in President Prabowo Subianto's government, Reuters reported. Six officials have now been named as suspects, the head of the police criminal investigation unit, Asep Safrudin, said late on Monday, and Mr. Listyo, who was arrested earlier this month, allegedly ordered and received wiretap reports from the other suspects on phones belonging to the president, government officials, activists and journalists.

"The suspects allegedly agreed to, prepared for, and initiated treason with the motive of maintaining the position of the police chief … by lowering the people's trust in the government of President Prabowo Subianto," Mr. Asep said. Treason charges are rare in Indonesia and carry a maximum penalty of death. Mr. Listyo, a political ally of former president Joko Widodo and unrelated to President Prabowo, faces an ethics hearing on Thursday, and Reuters could not immediately contact the suspects for comment.
