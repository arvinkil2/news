iHeartMedia shares fell about 66 percent in midday trading on Tuesday, to around $0.60 and a new 52-week low, after the company cut its full year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to $550 million to $600 million from about $800 million and trimmed free cash flow guidance to roughly $50 million. The company's own third quarter release, filed Monday evening, showed revenue of $1.03 billion, up 3.3 percent, but consolidated adjusted EBITDA of $170 million, down from $205 million a year earlier, as Multiplatform Group revenue fell 5 percent to $562 million and that segment's earnings fell 43 percent, partly offset by podcast revenue up 24 percent to $174 million. Bloomberg reported the company's debt also fell sharply in the selloff.
