NextDC, Australia's largest listed data centre operator, raised its fiscal 2027 net revenue guidance to A$485 million to A$500 million, from A$475 million to A$500 million, and its underlying EBITDA guidance to A$260 million to A$275 million, from A$245 million to A$265 million, attributing the upgrade to sales growth and cost-cutting measures. The new guidance still excludes the revenue and EBITDA of the customer contracts affected at its flagship M3 site in Melbourne, where expansion was suspended over power delays and community backlash; NextDC paused all uncontracted projects last week and had cut its forecast, including capital expenditure plans, last month.
