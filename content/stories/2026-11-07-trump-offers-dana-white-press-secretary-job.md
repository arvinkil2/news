Latest: President Donald Trump said on Sunday he was joking when he announced that he had offered Dana White the press secretary job. "I will tell you, I was only kidding," Trump told reporters, pointing to the roster of UFC fighters he had named as White's would-be assistants as the giveaway, and saying White would be in Washington on Monday for other reasons.

Earlier: Dana White is declining the offer to become White House press secretary, Reuters reported, in a brief carried by NRK. The report gave no quotation from White and no reason for the refusal.

Earlier: President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social on Saturday that Dana White, president and chief executive of the Ultimate Fighting Championship, has been offered the job of press secretary, asked him to bring named UFC fighters with him as assistants, and added, "Dana, please take the job!" White responded with an Instagram story showing himself at the White House briefing room lectern, captioned "On to the next chapter," and it was then unclear whether he had accepted the position or was acknowledging the request in jest.

The offer is the second senior White House staff move in two days, after Friday's announcement that Stephen Miller will become chief of staff. The White House has been without a permanent press secretary since Karoline Leavitt left earlier this year; Katie Zacharia, briefly tapped for the job, was dismissed in October after less than a week and one short briefing, following reports that she had marketed herself as an attorney without passing the bar exam.
