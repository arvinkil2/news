Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Russia is ready to resume trilateral negotiations with the United States and Ukraine after November 4, describing the first meeting after a resumption as a sighting-in round at which no decisions should be expected, in a pre-recorded interview with Pavel Zarubin on Rossiya-1 aired on Sunday. He said the text of a peace agreement still needs substantial work, and that preparation for a summit of Vladimir Putin, Donald Trump and Xi Jinping has begun, without giving a date, a venue or an agenda.

The timing points past the US midterm elections on November 3, and the remarks are Moscow's account alone: no US, Ukrainian or Chinese official had confirmed the talks timetable or the summit preparation in the sources read.
