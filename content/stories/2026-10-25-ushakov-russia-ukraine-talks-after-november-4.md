Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Russia is ready to resume trilateral negotiations on Ukraine after November 4, Russia's National Unity Day, telling interviewer Pavel Zarubin of Vesti that the next deadline is the holiday and that after it Moscow will be ready to resume. He said Russia had earlier been ready to resume after the September 18 to 20 Duma elections, and blamed the Ukrainian side for breaking that arrangement, a claim Kyiv has not answered in the sources read. Other Russian wires carrying remarks from the same interview round on Sunday reported him describing the first resumed meeting as a sighting-in round at which no decisions should be expected, and saying that preparation for a summit of Vladimir Putin, Donald Trump and Xi Jinping has begun, without a date, a venue or an agenda.

The remarks are Moscow's account alone: no US, Ukrainian or Chinese official had confirmed the talks timetable or the summit preparation in the sources read.
