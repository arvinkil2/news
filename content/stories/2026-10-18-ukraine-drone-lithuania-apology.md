Debris from a Ukrainian drone fell near the village of Nemunaitis in Lithuania's Alytus district on Sunday afternoon, damaging a passing car around 5:19 p.m. local time and startling its occupants, but injuring no one, Lithuanian authorities said. Ukraine's foreign ministry apologised for the violation of Lithuanian airspace, saying one of its interceptor drones had malfunctioned and gone off course because of Russian electronic warfare systems during a mass Russian drone and missile attack, and said it stayed in constant contact with Vilnius and NATO partners.

Lithuania's interior minister called the incident serious and said it showed that protecting the country's airspace takes coordinated effort, while the army said NATO air policing jets did not scramble because the object was only briefly in Lithuanian airspace. It was the tenth foreign object to violate Lithuanian airspace this year, after a series of Russian drone incursions and cigarette smuggling balloons from Belarus, and investigators are establishing whether the drone carried explosives.
