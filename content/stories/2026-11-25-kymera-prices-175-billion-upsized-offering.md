Kymera Therapeutics has priced an upsized public offering of $1.75 billion of common stock, selling 11,638,000 shares at $150.37 per share. The deal was increased from a planned $1.5 billion, and underwriters hold a 30-day option to buy up to 1,745,700 additional shares, worth about $262.5 million more at the offer price. Gross proceeds of about $1.75 billion are before underwriting discounts, commissions and expenses, and the offering is expected to close today, November 25.

The timing is the story. Kymera announced the offering on the same day it reported positive Phase 2b results for KT-621, its oral STAT6 degrader, in atopic dermatitis, covered here on November 23, and priced the deal that night at the day's closing price. The deal was priced at the day's closing price rather than at a discount, so the raise reads as opportunistic balance-sheet building at a data-driven share price rather than a response to a cash need.

The capital is aimed at the programme the data created. Kymera plans its first Phase 3 atopic dermatitis trial by mid-2027, with an asthma readout from the BREADTH study expected by late 2027. A $1.75 billion raise on top of existing cash gives the company the resources to run that Phase 3 programme and its wider degrader pipeline without returning to the market on a weaker hand.

The trade-off is dilution: roughly 11.6 million new shares, plus any exercise of the underwriters' option, against a pipeline whose value still rests on mid-stage data. Investors who bought the offering are paying the post-data price for a claim on Phase 3 execution.
