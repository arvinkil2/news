China has approved Eli Lilly's weight-loss pill orforglipron, the country's medicine regulator said on Thursday, giving the drugmaker a leg up on Danish rival Novo Nordisk in the world's second-largest pharmaceutical market, Reuters reported from Shanghai. Lilly was first to apply with orforglipron, and Novo's own oral Wegovy has not yet been approved in China.

The pill will compete with once-weekly GLP-1 weight-loss injections from Novo, Pfizer and local firm Innovent Biologics. Both Lilly and Novo believe weight-loss pills could attract patients reluctant to use injections, Reuters reported.
