Potential investors in Anthropic's planned initial public offering are raising doubts about whether the company can proceed with a roadshow next week in time for a November listing, Reuters reported, citing people familiar with investor discussions. Investors are pushing for a more attractive valuation to commit, with some viewing the recently discussed $1.5 trillion valuation as being at the high end of expectations, and the company may need to offer a bigger discount given its record of quarterly losses and its large commitments to partners in the AI buildout.

The Wall Street Journal, as reported by Investing.com, put the pressure in sharper terms, with one investor suggesting pricing may fall closer to $1 trillion and backers debating whether the company should adjust its pricing range or delay the timeline altogether. The same reporting put Anthropic's cash reserve at $146.6 billion as of September 30, giving it room to curb its capital target or postpone the debut if it chooses.
