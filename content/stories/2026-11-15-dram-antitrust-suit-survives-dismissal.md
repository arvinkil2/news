US District Judge Noël Wise of the Northern District of California has denied in full the joint motion to dismiss filed by Samsung Electronics, Micron and SK hynix in a class action brought by 17 consumers and small businesses, finding that the complaint plausibly alleges an agreement to restrict conventional DRAM supply in violation of Section 1 of the Sherman Act. The suit, filed in June, says the three companies cut conventional memory production from late 2022 and shifted wafer capacity toward high bandwidth memory, and the judge wrote that the plaintiffs' account goes beyond competitors watching each other to, in her words, hawks flying in formation.

The three companies must file their formal answers by December 7, with an initial case management conference set for January 12, 2027. The ruling decides only that the case can proceed; the court accepted the allegations as true for this stage and none of the companies has been found liable.
