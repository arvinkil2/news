South Korea's Financial Services Commission said on Thursday it will raise the maximum top-up balance for prepaid electronic payment instruments from 2 million won, a cap set in 2008, to 3 million won where identity is verified against a deposit account and 5 million won under real-name verification, with new daily use limits of 6 million and 10 million won respectively. Instruments for foreign visitors, currently capped at 1 million won, will allow verification by electronic passport and facial authentication and carry limits of up to 3 million won, barred from overseas use and from use more than 90 days after issuance. The commission plans draft legislation in the first half of 2027 and will raise the limits sooner through its regulatory sandbox.
