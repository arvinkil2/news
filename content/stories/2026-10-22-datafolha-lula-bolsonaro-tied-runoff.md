In total votes, Bolsonaro has 47 percent to Lula's 46 percent, a lead down from two points to one in a week, with blank, null or neither at 5 percent and the undecided at 2 percent. Datafolha's previous survey, published on October 15, had Bolsonaro ahead 51 percent to 49 percent in valid votes, which exclude blank and null ballots.

The poll interviewed 2,520 voters between October 20 and 22 and carries a margin of error of two percentage points. Voters choose between the two candidates in the runoff on Sunday, October 25.
