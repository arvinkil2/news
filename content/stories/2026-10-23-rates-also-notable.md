Moody's is due to review France's Aa3 sovereign rating on Friday, October 23, and no outcome had been published at the time of writing. The rating carries a negative outlook. The review was flagged by today's calendar cross-check after it was found to have no coverage in the morning's candidate pool, and a targeted check found the review still forthcoming: Dow Jones Newswires reported at 05:26 ET on Friday that Moody's will review France on Friday, quoting Danske Bank's Jens Naervig Pedersen that there is a significant likelihood of a downgrade, that a downgrade is fully priced in, and that the outlook decision is where the market impact would sit. Reuters reported on Thursday that the 10-year French yield had continued to creep higher after topping 5 percent for the first time since 2002, the move covered in full on this site on October 21, amid protests and limited progress on the budget. Moody's said on October 2 that the review will assess whether France's institutions can tackle its policy difficulties despite political fragmentation in parliament, which it called the key factor for resolving the negative outlook. This item records the scheduled review and its pricing context only; it states no outcome.

Japan's Ministry of Finance published the result of its treasury discount bill auction held on Friday, October 23, completing the week's bill supply ahead of the October 29 auctions of two-year bonds and further bills announced earlier in the week. The ministry's result table could not be extracted in this run, so this item records the publication and the calendar only, without result figures.

The US Treasury issued a notice of proposed rulemaking to implement the Comprehensive Outbound Investment Security Act, passed as part of the Fiscal Year 2026 National Defense Authorization Act. The proposed rules would require notification of certain transactions and prohibit others across semiconductor and microelectronics, artificial intelligence, quantum information, high-performance computing and hypersonic technologies, with biotechnology added by the Secretary. "The proposed regulations would strengthen the existing Outbound Investment Security Program to prevent our adversaries from using U.S. investment to develop critical technologies that threaten U.S. national security," Secretary Scott Bessent said. Comments are invited through regulations.gov before final rules set an effective date.

The Reserve Bank of India released reserve money data for the fortnight ended October 15, 2026, the statistical backdrop to the week's liquidity operations covered in full above. The International Monetary Fund published Selected Issues Paper No. 2026/103, on the structure, drivers and policy implications of Uganda's government bond yield curve, a frontier curve where the central bank's policy path and domestic bank demand set long yields with little foreign participation. Bloomberg reported that Blackstone's profit fell 9.2 percent as higher rates and inflation curbed dealmaking, a private capital read on the same rate levels that drove Thursday's Treasury rally. The CFTC forex fraud order flagged by the gap check was published as its own story on October 22 and is not repeated here.
