The Treasury Department and the Internal Revenue Service have moved to protect health care benefits for eligible Americans, publishing guidance that firms up eligibility protections in the employer and marketplace systems. The announcement, carried as Treasury release sb0658, is administrative law doing the work that statutes leave open.

The mechanics matter more than the headline. Eligibility guidance determines which workers keep employer coverage, how affordability is calculated and what happens at the seams between Medicaid, marketplace subsidies and employer plans, the seams where coverage is actually lost. Employers and insurers will now rebuild plan documents and enrolment systems around the new language.

Politically, the release is the administration putting its name on a protection rather than a cut, in a week when health policy in Washington has otherwise been about enforcement, the CVS opioid settlement in today's life sciences edition, and the standoff over the peptide rules covered there as well.
