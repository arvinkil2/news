The exclusion rule takes effect immediately upon Federal Register publication, with a 30-day comment period, while the event-contract inclusion is only a proposal subject to 30 days of comments after publication. Chairman Michael Selig framed event contracts as commodity derivatives squarely within the CFTC's exclusive jurisdiction, saying casino-style gambling products are not derivatives and sit outside the swap definition. The agency issued the rules amid conflicting appeals court decisions: the Sixth and Ninth Circuits let Ohio, Tennessee and Nevada enforce gambling laws against Kalshi, while the Third Circuit protected Kalshi in New Jersey, and the NFL has backed New Jersey's request for Supreme Court review.
