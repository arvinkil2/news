Rheinmetall, the German defence company, has reportedly acquired a stake in Rock Tech Lithium and in its German converter facility at Guben, Investing.com reported, citing a Bloomberg report. Rheinmetall is the new strategic investor in the 6 million Canadian dollar (4.2 million US dollars) private placement Rock Tech announced earlier this month, which also included an equity position in the Guben lithium hydroxide converter, a project the report said has received full permits.

No percentage stake, separate Rheinmetall amount or converter capacity was disclosed in the report. Rock Tech shares were shown up 15.25 percent and Rheinmetall shares up 6.26 percent in the market data accompanying the report.
