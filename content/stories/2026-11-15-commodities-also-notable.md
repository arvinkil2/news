**Nigeria crude theft.** The Nigerian Navy uncovered 56,800 litres of stolen crude oil in Rivers State, Vanguard reported on November 15. The seizure is a routine-sized find by Niger Delta standards, but it lands in a month when Nigeria is pressing to hold crude output gains, and every recovered litre is a reminder that theft and illegal refining still leak supply between the wellhead and the export terminal. Single sourced to Vanguard.

**USDA November outlooks.** The US Department of Agriculture published its November 2026 outlook reports for feed, wheat, oil crops and rice on November 13, the analytical companions to the November WASDE. The outlooks carry the department's own discussion of the balances WASDE set, and they are the documents to read alongside the WASDE tables before the December report.

**Delfin LNG extension.** Delfin LNG has asked US regulators for an extension of time, according to a Federal Register notice dated November 16. Delfin's floating LNG project off Louisiana has worked through a long permitting and financing path, and extension requests of this kind are the standard mechanism by which Gulf export projects keep their authorisations alive while financing is assembled.

**Brazil biodiesel fees.** S&P Global reported that Brazilian biodiesel fees have fallen, citing diesel limits and currency shifts. The item is noted here without figures, which this reader did not verify from a second source.
