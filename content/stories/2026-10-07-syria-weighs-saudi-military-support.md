Syria is evaluating military assistance to Saudi Arabia against the Houthis, according to an American official and a Syrian military official cited by Reuters and first reported by Axios. Options on the table include defensive measures against missile and drone strikes, as well as deploying Syrian forces to operate alongside Saudi-backed Yemeni troops, though Syrian officials stress domestic recovery remains the priority.

The development is a reversal in tone from mid-September, when Syrian sources said Damascus politely declined a months-old Saudi request to organize and deploy Syrian fighters to Yemen. The shift follows the sharp escalation this week: Houthi strikes on Aden airport and airports inside Saudi Arabia, including Abha and Riyadh's King Khalid, that killed three foreign nationals.
