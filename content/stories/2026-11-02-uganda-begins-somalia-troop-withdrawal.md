Uganda's army said on Monday that the first group of its soldiers deployed under bilateral arrangements with Somalia has already returned home, days after a first contingent of 109 troops left Mogadishu on Friday, the BBC reported. Uganda keeps an estimated 4,500 troops in Somalia under the African Union mission, now called AUSSOM, and about 2,000 more under bilateral arrangements, and the army said all of them will leave by the end of 2027 in a phased withdrawal that hands security responsibility to Somali forces. Army chief Muhoozi Kainerugaba announced the pullout last week, and acting army spokesperson Chris Magezi confirmed the timetable on Monday. Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud visited Kampala last week and reportedly tried to persuade Uganda to stay, without a public outcome.
