New York Attorney General Letitia James added new allegations to the state's child safety lawsuit against TikTok, accusing the platform of running secret experiments on teen users. The amended complaint escalates the legal pressure on the company as New York moves to implement its SAFE for Kids Act and related teen-account safety rules. The filing lands as states step up enforcement against addictive feeds, warning labels, and algorithmic design aimed at minors.
