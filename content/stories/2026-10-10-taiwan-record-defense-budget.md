In a National Day speech on Saturday, President Lai Ching-te said Taiwan's government has allocated NT$1.1 trillion ($34.5 billion) for defense in 2027, equivalent to more than 3 percent of GDP. The announcement followed US President Donald Trump's demands that Taiwan pay for American protection, with a $14 billion US arms package including Patriot systems still awaiting Trump's approval.
