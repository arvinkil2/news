The European Commission has greenlighted the Netherlands' fifth and final payment request worth 1.7 billion euros under the Recovery and Resilience Facility, the centrepiece of NextGenerationEU, after finding that the country satisfactorily completed the 12 milestones and 11 targets attached to the request, covering workers' rights, housing construction and nature restoration. The request, submitted on September 22, would bring total funds paid to the Netherlands to 5.4 billion euros, 100 percent of its plan allocation, with all 122 milestones and targets fulfilled.

The Commission has sent its preliminary assessment to the Economic and Financial Committee, which has four weeks to deliver its opinion, and payment can follow once the Commission adopts a payment decision.
