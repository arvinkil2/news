Witnesses and a rescue worker told AFP that a Myanmar military airstrike hit the Byar La displaced persons camp in Ponnagyun township, northeast of the Rakhine state capital Sittwe, on Friday evening, killing 12 people, among them a five year old, and injuring 18. Residents said the camp, home to people displaced by the civil war for four years, was not a combat area and that they had believed it was safe, and the rescue worker said most of the victims died in one place where they had sheltered together. AFP said it could not contact the Myanmar military for comment; Rakhine is controlled almost entirely by the ethnic Arakan Army, is blockaded by the military, and faces regular airstrikes, and the ACLED monitor estimates more than 100,000 people have been killed across Myanmar since the war began.
