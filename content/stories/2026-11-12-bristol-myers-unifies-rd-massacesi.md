Bristol Myers Squibb announced on Thursday that Cristian Massacesi, its executive vice president and chief medical officer, will lead a unified, end-to-end research and development organisation effective January 1, 2027, bringing the company's scientific and clinical work together in one organisation from discovery through development. Robert Plenge, the executive vice president and chief research officer, will remain in an advisory capacity through March 2027 to support the transition before departing the company.

The company presented the change as designed to accelerate pipeline momentum and strengthen collaboration across the R&D organisation. Massacesi joined Bristol Myers in August 2025 from AstraZeneca, where he was chief medical officer, and has held the development brief since then; the new structure adds the research organisation Plenge led to his remit.
