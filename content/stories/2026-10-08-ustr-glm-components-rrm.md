The Office of the US Trade Representative on Thursday invoked the USMCA Rapid Response Labor Mechanism against GLM Components Mexico, an auto-parts maker in Puebla, asking Mexico to review whether workers are being denied freedom of association and collective bargaining. The move follows a September 8 petition from the Mexican independent union Sindicato Regeneracion Nacional "Morenas," which alleges the company interfered with union activity and fired workers in retaliation for it. The United States has suspended liquidation of unliquidated entries of goods from the facility; Mexico has 10 days to agree to a review and, if it agrees, 45 days from October 8 to complete it.
