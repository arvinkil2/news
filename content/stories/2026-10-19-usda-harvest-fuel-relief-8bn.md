The Agriculture Department will launch a Harvest Fuel Relief program paying farmers $8 billion to offset diesel costs during this harvest season, President Trump announced in a campaign speech in Kansas on Monday, with Secretary Brooke Rollins unveiling the program alongside him. The money is authorised under the Commodity Credit Corporation Charter Act and will be run by the Farm Service Agency, modelled on the earlier Farmer Bridge Assistance program: a uniform formula pays a portion of modelled losses for the 2026 crop year, calculated from FSA reported planted acres, Economic Research Service cost of production data and WASDE yield and price estimates. Commodity specific payment rates for Title I crops are due in November, crop insurance linkage is not a condition of payment, and producers of other crops, including specialty crops and commercial fishermen, are being asked to send their fuel cost data to the department.
