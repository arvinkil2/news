OpenAI said Dan Driscoll will join the company as senior counselor for national security modernization, a newly created post advising its leadership on artificial intelligence applications in national security and cybersecurity, CNBC reported on Monday. Driscoll, an Iraq war veteran who resigned as Army secretary earlier this year after months of reported tension with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, will work with government officials, industry leaders and allied organisations on the adoption of advanced AI by US allies, building on the company's Daybreak cyber resilience program. Chief executive Sam Altman said the appointment is intended to strengthen American leadership as other countries adopt what OpenAI calls democratic AI, and Driscoll said he is joining to help the United States and its allies put the technology to work responsibly for national security staff.
