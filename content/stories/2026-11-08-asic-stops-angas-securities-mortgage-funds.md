The Australian Securities and Investments Commission has stopped Angas Securities from selling two managed investment schemes, the Angas Prime Income Fund and the Angas Direct Mortgage Fund, Bloomberg reported, citing an ASIC statement on Monday. The interim stop orders bar the Adelaide based firm from offering, issuing, selling or transferring interests in either fund.

Bloomberg described the action as the latest in a series by the regulator to prevent private credit funds from offering products that do not adequately highlight potential risks. That campaign has run through the Australian private credit sector since the Bathla Group collapse left lenders and funds exposed across hundreds of related companies.
