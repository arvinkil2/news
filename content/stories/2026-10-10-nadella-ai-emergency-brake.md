Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said Saturday it is time to step back and assess the trust architecture of AI, writing in an X post that the industry should separate the model from the harness that orchestrates its work and externalize controls and safeguards. He called for every meaningful model action to be documented with tamper-proof human-readable evidence, and said systems must be designed so an authorized person can always pause or shut down a model mid-task.

"We must assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start," he wrote. "Think of it like an emergency brake." The remarks land as leading AI companies acknowledge more incidents where they seemed to lose control of their models, and days after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a plan for more cautious AI development.
