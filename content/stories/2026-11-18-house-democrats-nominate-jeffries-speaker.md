House Democrats on Wednesday nominated minority leader Hakeem Jeffries of New York to be the next speaker of the House, giving him 251 of 265 ballots in a closed-door secret vote. Jeffries would be the first Black speaker, and he takes the gavel in January after Democrats won the House and the Senate in this month's midterms; the party will hold at least 257 House seats and 56 Senate seats, and the full House elects the speaker by roll call on January 3, 2027.

Katherine Clark of Massachusetts will serve as majority leader, the first woman in that role, and Pete Aguilar of California as majority whip, the first Latino in his. Ohio's Jim Jordan will lead the Republicans as House minority leader.
