The Philippine peso fell through 63 per dollar for the first time on Thursday, weakening to 63.15 in early trading and passing its previous intraday record low of 62.925, according to Bankers Association of the Philippines data reported by the Philippine Star. The peso had opened at 62.83 and closed at 62.87 on Wednesday, just short of the record low closing rate of 62.90 set on October 8. The Star attributed the latest slide to renewed tensions in the Middle East, and the breach lands a week before the central bank's October 22 meeting.
