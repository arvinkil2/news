Dell Technologies reported record revenue of $54.9 billion for its fiscal 2027 third quarter ended October 30, up 103 percent from $27.0 billion a year earlier, in results filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Tuesday. Diluted earnings per share were a record $8.82, up 287 percent, and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $9.45. Operating income rose 251 percent to $7.4 billion and cash flow from operations was a record $4.5 billion for the quarter.

The growth came from the Infrastructure Solutions Group, where revenue rose 198 percent to a record $42.0 billion. AI-optimized server revenue was $22.0 billion, up 291 percent, traditional servers and networking brought in $15.0 billion, up 235 percent, and storage revenue rose 24 percent to $4.9 billion. The Client Solutions Group, the personal computer business, was close to flat, with revenue up 3 percent to $12.9 billion and operating income down 6 percent to $704 million.

Dell raised its full-year guidance. It now expects fiscal 2027 revenue of $202.0 billion, up from $192.0 billion previously, AI-optimized server revenue of $78.0 billion, up from $74.0 billion, diluted earnings per share of $28.24 against $24.37 previously, and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share of $29.68 against $25.50. For the fourth quarter it guided to revenue of $56.3 billion, up 69 percent year over year. The company said it ended the quarter with a record AI backlog of $132.3 billion after booking $60.2 billion of orders in the quarter, and that more than 7,500 customers are deploying its AI factory systems.

Dell returned a record $4.9 billion to shareholders in the quarter through buybacks and dividends, and its board approved a further $25.0 billion increase to the share repurchase authorization for its Class C stock.
