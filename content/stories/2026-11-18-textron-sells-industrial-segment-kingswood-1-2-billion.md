Textron entered into a share purchase agreement on November 18, 2026 to sell the businesses that make up its Industrial segment to Meridian Buyer LLC, an affiliate of investment funds managed by Kingswood Capital Management, for $1.2 billion in cash, subject to customary closing adjustments. The segment comprises Kautex, which makes plastic fuel systems, battery enclosures and clear-vision systems for the car industry, and Textron Specialized Vehicles. Textron expects net cash proceeds of about $1.1 billion after tax and transaction expenses, and said the proceeds would fund additional share repurchases in 2027 under a new board authorisation for up to 35 million shares.

The filing says the Industrial business will be classified as held for sale and reported in discontinued operations from the fourth quarter of 2026, with a non-cash pre-tax impairment charge expected in that quarter in the range of $350 million to $400 million, including about $175 million of cumulative foreign currency translation adjustments. The transaction is not subject to a financing contingency, needs Hart-Scott-Rodino clearance and other antitrust and foreign investment approvals, and is expected to close in the first half of 2027, after which Textron will be a pure-play aerospace and defence company.
