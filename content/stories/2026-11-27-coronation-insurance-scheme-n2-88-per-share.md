Coronation Insurance Plc is moving to implement its scheme of arrangement after Nigeria's Securities and Exchange Commission granted approval on October 22, 2026 and the Federal High Court sanctioned the scheme on November 10. The final scheme consideration is 2.88 naira per share.

That price is a 336.4 percent increase over the 0.66 naira proposed in 2021 and a 269.2 percent increase over the 0.78 naira consideration in 2023. The company said the SEC directed that pricing be set on the highest trading price recorded for its shares on the NGX in the six months before the applicable notification date, so the increase reflects the share price appreciation between 2023 and 2026. The scheme resolutions were approved by 97.88 percent of shareholders present and voting at the court-ordered meeting on December 22, 2023, but implementation was delayed by legal challenges from a small number of shareholders, which have since been disposed of.

The core shareholders have deposited the funds for the scheme consideration into escrow, and Coronation Registrars Limited is expected to begin disbursement shortly. Shareholders who validly elected to remain invested will do so under the scheme arrangements. Once implementation and the applicable market processes are complete, the company is expected to be delisted from the NGX. The company said the scheme changes its shareholding structure only and does not alter its contractual obligations to policyholders, customers or business partners.
