Morgan Stanley raised its allocation to Japanese equities to overweight from equal-weight and lifted its TOPIX target to 4,800 for December 2027, from 4,300 for June 2027, implying about 22 percent upside from the November 12 level of 3,937 and a 24.4 percent total return including dividends. Its Japan strategists cited sustained nominal growth, investment initiatives under Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, corporate governance improvements and a broader domestic investor base for Japanese government bonds, with the index trading at about 14.1 times forward earnings, broadly in line with its 10-year average.

The bank forecast TOPIX earnings per share growth of 12.9 percent in 2027 and 8 percent in 2028, broadening beyond AI as capital spending growth by hyperscalers slows, and said a sharper AI slowdown, higher oil prices or more aggressive Bank of Japan tightening were the main risks to the call.
