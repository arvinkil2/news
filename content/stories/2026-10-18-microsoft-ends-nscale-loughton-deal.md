Microsoft has pulled out of its data centre deal with British cloud provider Nscale, terminating its agreement for the company's site at Loughton, Essex, according to a Telegraph report carried by Reuters on Sunday. The report gave no reason for the withdrawal, and neither Microsoft nor Nscale commented.

Nscale filed for an initial public offering in the United States last month, with Microsoft and Anthropic expected to be its major customers under its prospectus, and says its contracted value has grown from 100 million dollars to more than 103 billion dollars in two and a half years. The Loughton cancellation follows reporting this week that its Glomfjord data centre in Norway is six months behind schedule, with 6.4 megawatts of a planned 30 megawatts operational after cooling leaks and a transformer fire.
