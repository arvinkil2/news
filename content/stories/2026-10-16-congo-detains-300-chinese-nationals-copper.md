About 300 Chinese nationals working in the Democratic Republic of Congo's copper belt were detained earlier this week in a crackdown on illegal mining, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Most of the arrests were in Kolwezi, the main mining hub of the copper belt.

Some of those detained were alleged to have mined illicitly on concessions belonging to other firms, the report said. Chinese companies dominate copper output in Congo, and the detentions are the largest reported move yet against foreign nationals in the country's long running campaign against unlicensed mining.
