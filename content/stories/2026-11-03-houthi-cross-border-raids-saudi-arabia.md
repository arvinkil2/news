Houthi fighters have launched multiple small-scale cross-border raids into Saudi Arabia in recent months, targeting border posts in the kingdom's mountainous southwest, with at least one raid as recently as the end of October, Middle East Eye reported, citing a Western and an Arab official with direct knowledge of the raids. The report says the fighters appear to have captured small arms used only by Saudi border security forces, along with surveillance equipment. Saudi authorities have continued to announce interceptions of drones and missiles fired from Yemen this week, and Bloomberg reported on Tuesday that the Asian Football Confederation has postponed its flagship tournament in Saudi Arabia, citing exceptional circumstances arising from developments in the region.
