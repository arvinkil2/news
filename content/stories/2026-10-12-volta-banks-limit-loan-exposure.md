The underwritten $1.3 billion term loan covers lease payments to Bitdeer for a data centre complex in Norway, where Anthropic has signed a six year compute capacity agreement, while the ununderwritten $3.7 billion loan would fund purchases of about 36,000 Nvidia GPUs. Because the package is unrated, the usual collateralized loan obligation buyers are largely shut out, narrowing the investor base ahead of the October 14 commitment deadline.

The structure reflects lender wariness after the hung debt losses of 2022, even as banks compete to finance the AI buildout. Earlier neocloud transactions, including deals for CoreWeave and Lambda, cleared at lower borrowing costs than Volta's roughly 11 percent yield.
