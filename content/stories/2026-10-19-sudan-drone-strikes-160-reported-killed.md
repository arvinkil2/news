## Latest

The toll from Monday's strike on the Abyei market has risen sharply. Yohana Akol Ngor, the caretaker information minister for the jointly administered region, told Reuters on Tuesday that 153 people were confirmed killed, up from the 73 first reported, and that fires from the strike were still burning and slowing the recovery of bodies. About 8,000 people have been displaced, he said. Ngor blamed Sudan's army for the attack but offered no evidence; a Sudanese armed forces spokesperson did not immediately respond to a request for comment, and the RSF did not comment either. Three diplomatic sources, an aid worker and a conflict expert told Reuters the strike hit a fuel depot inside the market that had previously been used by the RSF to supply its forces, a presence in Abyei that Ngor denied and that officials in Sudan's military-led government assert. South Sudan's foreign ministry said it had summoned Sudan's ambassador in protest, and Médecins Sans Frontières said it was treating more than 20 wounded, several with burns, at Ameth Bek Hospital.


More than 160 people have been reported killed in drone strikes in western Sudan since Friday, according to medics and rights groups cited by Al Jazeera, in an escalation the United Nations condemned on Monday. The deadliest single attack hit a transport hub near a market in Al-Dibab in West Kordofan on Sunday evening, where residents and medical workers said 80 people were killed as villagers gathered after market day; the Rapid Support Forces administration and Emergency Lawyers accused the army of carrying out that strike, an attribution the army has not publicly answered.

The United Nations said drone strikes on Monday injured at least 19 people in a busy market in Abyei and targeted several locations in Khartoum, including an area near the airport, where one civilian was reportedly killed and some flights were cancelled. UN spokesperson Stéphane Dujarric said the UN was alarmed by strikes killing civilians, including children, and disrupting essential services, and condemned all of the attacks unequivocally.
