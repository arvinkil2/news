The European Commission said experts from EU countries, the Commission and stakeholder associations confirmed at Friday's Gas Coordination Group meeting that the EU is prepared for winter, with gas storage at around 70 percent providing a buffer for the heating season after storage already supplied flexibility during the season's first low wind cold spell. The Commission said the EU gas system can handle more LNG imports than in previous years if needed, that no risks to security of supply have been reported for this winter, and that the situation does not warrant recommending action by individual countries. The group also judged the EU well prepared for the full phase-out of Russian LNG imports by January 1, 2027 under the REPowerEU Regulation, and saw no immediate concern beyond this winter provided storage is not overly depleted by summer and LNG imports remain robust.
