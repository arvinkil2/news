Senator Flavio Bolsonaro has reportedly left Brazil for the United States after losing Sunday’s presidential runoff to President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva, according to reports by Folha de S.Paulo and Reuters citing people close to the senator, with Folha reporting that his flight was due to land in the United States within hours. The reports follow a Supreme Court order, which Bolsonaro said in a social media video on Monday had been served on him outside his father’s home, requiring him to surrender his passports; AFP cited a police official saying the passports were cancelled earlier on Tuesday and that there was no official record of a departure.

Bolsonaro is the subject of at least two federal investigations, one of them into corruption, money laundering and tax evasion allegations linked to money received from a banker to finance “Dark Horse,” a biopic about his father, former president Jair Bolsonaro. His Liberal Party acknowledged the runoff result on Tuesday.
