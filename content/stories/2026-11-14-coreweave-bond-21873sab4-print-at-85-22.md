CoreWeave Inc's 9.25% senior notes due 2030, CUSIP 21873SAB4, printed at 85.22 on 2026-11-13, down from a previous print of 87.8 on 2026-11-12, a move of 2.58 points. The latest fund mark is 100.65 for the period ending 2026-06-30, a gap of 15.43 points above the new print.

The bond had sat below the 15-point line earlier in the week, printing 84.375 on 2026-11-09 and 85.5 on 2026-11-10, before the 2026-11-12 print at 87.8 moved it back above the line. Friday's print crossed it again on a falling trade. The figures above are measured trade data only; the tape records trades, not reasons for them.
