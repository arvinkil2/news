China's manufacturing purchasing managers' index was 49.3 in October, down 0.8 percentage points from September, with the production index falling 1.9 points to 49.8 and the new order index falling 1.1 points to 49.4, the National Bureau of Statistics said. Large enterprises held just above the threshold at 50.2, down 0.4 points, while medium enterprises fell to 48.4 and small enterprises to 47.5. The non-manufacturing business activity index fell 0.5 points to 49.7, led by construction at 48.0, down 2.3 points, and the composite PMI output index fell 1.0 point to 49.7.
