**Latest, October 28:** The transaction the site reported as being finalised on Monday has been announced. Mubadala Capital said Apollo will take a minority equity stake in the firm and commit capital across its investment strategies, and that additional strategic investors will also join as minority shareholders, with the incoming investors together expected to commit an aggregate of $5 billion to Mubadala Capital's strategies at initial close. Apollo joins the founders of Silver Rock Financial as an equity holder, Mubadala Investment Company remains the majority shareholder, and the firm will continue to operate independently with its existing management team retaining full authority over investment decisions, the announcement said. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory and legal approvals.

Apollo Global Management is finalising a deal to buy nearly 10 percent of Mubadala Capital, the alternatives arm of Abu Dhabi sovereign investor Mubadala Investment Company, CNBC reported on Monday, citing people familiar with the matter. The transaction had not been announced when the report was published, an announcement was expected later on Monday, and financial terms were not known. Mubadala Capital, led by chief executive Hani Barhoush, says it manages, advises and administers more than $755 billion in assets, with its wholly owned core alternatives businesses managing more than $60 billion. Apollo and Mubadala already run a multibillion-dollar partnership first established in 2022, including a $2.5 billion joint venture formed in 2023 to co-invest in global private credit over five years.
