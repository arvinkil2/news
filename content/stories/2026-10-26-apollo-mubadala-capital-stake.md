Apollo Global Management is finalising a deal to buy nearly 10 percent of Mubadala Capital, the alternatives arm of Abu Dhabi sovereign investor Mubadala Investment Company, CNBC reported on Monday, citing people familiar with the matter. The transaction had not been announced when the report was published, an announcement was expected later on Monday, and financial terms were not known. Mubadala Capital, led by chief executive Hani Barhoush, says it manages, advises and administers more than $755 billion in assets, with its wholly owned core alternatives businesses managing more than $60 billion. Apollo and Mubadala already run a multibillion-dollar partnership first established in 2022, including a $2.5 billion joint venture formed in 2023 to co-invest in global private credit over five years.
