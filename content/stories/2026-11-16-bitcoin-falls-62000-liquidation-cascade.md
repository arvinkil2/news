Bitcoin fell as low as 62,000 dollars late on Saturday, according to Coin Metrics, after breaking below its 200 day moving average near 73,000 dollars set off a cascade of long liquidations. Exchanges tracked by CoinGlass saw more than 165 million dollars of bitcoin long positions liquidated on Saturday night, following 629 million dollars on Friday and nearly 700 million dollars on Thursday.

By Monday morning bitcoin had recovered to about 64,331 dollars, down about 1 percent on the day. Nansen analyst Nicolai Sondergaard put the next critical test at 60,000 to 62,000 dollars, and said a softer dollar and falling real yields would ease pressure across risk assets.
