United Airlines said on Monday that it had resolved a technology issue that delayed flight departures, after flagging a widespread system outage shortly before 5 p.m. Eastern; the disruption ended around 6 p.m., according to Reuters, and the airline said there was no indication it was a cybersecurity incident. A memo from the pilots' union, seen by CNBC and Reuters, said a companywide web-based outage affected multiple applications used by pilots, including crew communications, and left United unable to provide outbound flights with final weights. Flights already in the air and United Express flights were unaffected; as of 6:50 p.m. Eastern, United had delayed 645 flights, 20 percent of its schedule, and canceled 25, according to FlightAware figures reported by Reuters, and the airline said the outage was not related to Friday's nationwide FAA ground stop.
