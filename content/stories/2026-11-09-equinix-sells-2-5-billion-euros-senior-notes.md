Equinix Europe 2 Financing Corporation LLC, an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Equinix, Inc., issued and sold 2.5 billion euros of senior notes on November 9, in four tranches: 650 million euros of 4.150 percent notes due 2028, 700 million euros of 4.800 percent notes due 2033, 500 million euros of 5.100 percent notes due 2035 and 650 million euros of 5.450 percent notes due 2038. Equinix guarantees the notes.

Equinix intends to allocate an amount equal to the net proceeds to finance or refinance eligible green projects. Pending full allocation, the filing says the proceeds may be held in cash, cash equivalents and US government securities under its general treasury policy, or used to repay existing borrowings. Interest on the 2028 notes is payable annually each November 9, starting in 2027.
