The boards of Kenmare Resources and Abu Dhabi-based International Resources Holding (IRH) have reached agreement in principle on the key financial terms of a pre-conditional possible cash offer for Kenmare's entire issued share capital, with the price implying an enterprise value of about £487.1 million and valuing the equity at about £352.8 million. Kenmare, which operates the Moma mineral sands mine in Mozambique, said its board believes a deal would let shareholders crystallise their investment in cash at a significant premium and that IRH would be an appropriate owner for the next stage of its growth. IRH said it intends to make a firm offer only if certain pre-conditions are met, and sees the acquisition as its entry point into mineral sands through a long-life operating asset.
