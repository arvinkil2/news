Calley Means, who joined the administration as a close adviser to Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. before moving to the White House, confirmed to STAT on Thursday that he has resigned, less than two weeks before midterm elections that may serve as a referendum on the Make America Healthy Again movement. In a statement he said he leaves with tremendous respect for President Trump and Kennedy, and that the movement is in its first inning. He did not say why he was leaving.

Means had been a driving force behind the MAHA agenda, including its report on chronic disease and a White House effort to expand access to psychedelics, and a public defender of the movement against claims it was faltering. His sister Casey Means was nominated for surgeon general before that nomination was pulled over senators' concerns about her lack of medical experience.
