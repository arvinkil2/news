China escalated its defence of its stock market on Tuesday, Bloomberg reported, as state-backed funds and listed companies stepped up support. A basket of nine exchange-traded funds favoured by the so-called national team drew 76.7 billion yuan ($11.5 billion) in inflows on the day, the second largest on record and the most since April 8, 2025, when markets were hit by the US tariff announcement.

Listed companies also stepped up share buybacks, according to the report, though the portion of the article available to read named no companies or amounts. The buying follows a run of official support measures this month, including a regulators and banks meeting on capital market tools and a decision to make the buyback relending facility permanent.
