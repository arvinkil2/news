China’s gold reserves rose to 78.30 million troy ounces at the end of October, an increase of 830,000 ounces from September, the People’s Bank of China reported on Saturday. The addition, about 25.8 tonnes, is larger than September’s 740,000 ounces and makes October the 24th consecutive month of purchases since the bank resumed buying in November 2024.

The value of the holdings fell to $306.41 billion from $323.52 billion in September, because the gold price dropped over the month even as the quantity rose. China’s total foreign exchange reserves rose about $5 billion in October to about $3.405 trillion.
