India's shipping minister Sarbananda Sonowal said on X that the MT Petro Dolphin was hit by a drone attack on Sunday at the anchorage for the port of Sohar in northern Oman; nine of the 11 Indian seafarers aboard were rescued and two are missing, with search and rescue under way. Sonowal said a second vessel, the Marshall Islands-flagged M/T Valthera carrying 11 Indian crew, was struck by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz, sparking a fire, with all crew reported safe. India's foreign ministry said the "continuing incidents of attacks on shipping in the region remain a cause of deep concern" and reiterated its call for an immediate cessation, noting it had already condemned a projectile strike on a Panama-flagged vessel off Oman earlier in the week.
