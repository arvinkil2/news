The Reserve Bank of India accepted bids worth about 40 percent of the size of a shorter-tenor bond auction on Friday, declining to sell the full amount at the yields investors demanded. The bank took 3,680 crore rupees, about 396.8 million dollars, of a planned 9,000 crore sale of the 6.20 percent 2029 paper, and kept the cutoff yield below the 7 percent handle.

The three-year bond had traded at 6.9960 percent in the secondary market before the auction cutoffs, and there were no trades in it afterwards. A trader at a private bank said investors must have demanded yields above 7 percent, especially with the central bank meeting a week away, and that the RBI would not be comfortable with that level. The bank had last partially cancelled an auction of this security in September.

The restraint comes as expectations for the December 4 policy decision shift toward a larger move. A rising number of economists now call for a 50 basis point increase, and Nomura and Deutsche Bank on Friday recommended front-loaded hikes to fight elevated inflationary pressures, Reuters reported.

The rest of Friday's sale went through closer to expectations. The RBI sold a seven-year bond at a slightly higher yield than expected, while ultra-long 30-year paper was sold at yields in line with estimates. The pattern, resistance at the front of the curve and acceptance at the long end, is consistent with a market pricing a near-term policy shock rather than a permanently higher rate path.

The RBI's full results, published by the bank itself, fill in the rest of the operation. Of the 9,000 crore notified for the 2029 line, competitive bids accepted were 3,670.2 crore at a cutoff yield of 6.9960 percent, with a further 7.3 crore taken non-competitively. The 6.57 percent 2033 was sold essentially in full, 11,996.1 crore competitive plus 3.9 crore non-competitive against 12,000 crore notified, at a cutoff of 7.2736 percent. The 7.63 percent 2056 took 8,962.8 crore competitive plus 37.2 crore non-competitive against 9,000 crore notified, at 7.7996 percent, and the 7.50 percent sovereign green bond 2056 took 2,996.3 crore plus 3.7 crore against 3,000 crore notified, at 7.7721 percent. There was no devolvement on primary dealers in any line.
