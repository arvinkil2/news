Tariffs are removed from today on more than 100 products, HM Treasury said, including baked beans, garlic, chocolate, jams and marmalades, cough sweets and plant-based drinks, with fertiliser tariffs suspended until December 2027 and the rest until December 2028. The government estimates the package will cut consumer costs by up to 185 million pounds a year, with lower-income households benefiting most, and says kerosene cuts will also lower costs for households and businesses using heating oil. Chancellor John Healey said the move responds to the impact of global conflicts on the weekly shop.
