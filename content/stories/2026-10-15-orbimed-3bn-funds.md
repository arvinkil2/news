OrbiMed said on Thursday it has raised 3 billion dollars in commitments across two new private equity funds: OrbiMed Private Investments X, which raised over 1.9 billion dollars for US and European life sciences venture opportunities, and OrbiMed Asia Partners VI, which raised over 1 billion dollars for healthcare growth equity and venture capital across Asia. Both funds will back companies in biopharmaceuticals, medical devices, diagnostics and technology enabled healthcare services, with investors including medical institutions, endowments, pension funds and sovereign wealth funds. Managing partner Carl Gordon pointed to AI, new therapeutic modalities and China's emergence as a biotechnology power as expanding the opportunity set the funds will pursue.
