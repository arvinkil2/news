President Donald Trump on Thursday signed an executive order aimed at reducing the costs of single-family home ownership and mortgages, the White House said in a statement reported by Reuters. The order instructs the Federal Housing Finance Agency to launch a pilot program that would proactively cancel borrowers' mortgage insurance premiums once they meet equity requirements based on current market value, and to make permanent an FHFA pilot waiving title insurance on refinancings.

The order also creates a task force on manufactured housing innovation to expand factory-built housing eligible for federal programs, and it directs Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins and Veterans Affairs Secretary Doug Collins to reduce interest rates on single-family home loans issued by their departments. The Hill reported that the order directs FHFA Director Bill Pulte to carry out its provisions, and that an interim final rule on appraisals and reconsideration of value was announced alongside it.
