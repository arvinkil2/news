The Office of Foreign Assets Control on Monday designated five Chinese entities under the Protecting American Intellectual Property Act of 2022 and added them to the Non-SDN Menu-Based Sanctions List: Qiji Chuangtan Beijing Investment Management Co Ltd, known as MiraclePlus, its Phase I and Phase II renminbi venture capital funds, the related Zhiyuan enterprise management partnership in Beijing, and Shanghai Zhisuan Technology Co Ltd. For each entity, OFAC imposed the same set of measures: blocking of property, a prohibition on United States financial institutions lending more than 10 million dollars in any 12 month period, prohibitions on foreign exchange and banking transactions subject to United States jurisdiction, and a ban on United States persons investing in or purchasing significant amounts of the entity's equity or debt. OFAC also issued associated general licenses alongside the designations.
