Nigeria's Securities and Exchange Commission approved the share allotment plan for Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals on Monday, November 23, BusinessDay reported, ending weeks of waiting for subscribers to the refinery's initial public offering, which closed in late October with demand at roughly three times the initial 4.1 billion share offer. The approval clears the way for shares to be credited to investors through the Central Securities Clearing System, for surplus funds to be refunded, and for trading to begin on the Nigerian Exchange, with Bloomberg reporting on November 24 that the listing is set for December 1. The refinery, in the Lekki Free Zone near Lagos, has also sought approval to expand the offer by up to 30 percent to meet the demand the base offer could not fill, and Aliko Dangote has indicated a possible secondary listing in the United States within three to four years.
