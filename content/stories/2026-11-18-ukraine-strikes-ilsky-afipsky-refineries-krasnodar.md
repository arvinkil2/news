Ukraine's General Staff said Ukrainian forces carried out air strikes overnight on the Ilsky and Afipsky oil refineries in Russia's Krasnodar region, reporting that storage tanks were hit at Ilsky and an oil processing unit was damaged at Afipsky. The General Staff put Ilsky's annual processing capacity at about 6.6 million tonnes of crude and described both plants as key fuel suppliers in southern Russia, on some of the shortest supply routes to Russian forces on the front line.

Russian regional officials reported fires at two industrial enterprises in the Seversky district, where both refineries are located, and said four people were injured, without identifying the facilities. The strikes were the second this month on both plants, according to Ukrainian monitoring channels and Russian media reports, and the extent of any damage to refining operations has not been independently established.
