Saudi air defences intercepted and destroyed a drone launched by the Houthis toward the Riyadh area on Sunday, the Saudi-led coalition said, with no casualties or damage reported. The interception followed a ballistic missile launched toward Saudi Arabia's western region, on the Red Sea coast near the Yanbu export terminal and the Jazan refinery, which the coalition said it intercepted and destroyed late on Saturday.

Separately, Houthi-affiliated media reported that Saudi aircraft struck a fuel station operated by the state oil company on 60th Street in Sanaa on Sunday, injuring six employees and causing material damage. The coalition gave no immediate comment on that claim, which is uncorroborated.
