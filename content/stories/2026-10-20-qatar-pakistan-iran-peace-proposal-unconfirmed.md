Qatar and Pakistan have proposed a compromise intended to bring Washington and Tehran closer to reviving negotiations, according to an unconfirmed report in Al-Araby Al-Jadeed cited by Investing.com, including releasing 6 billion dollars in interest on Iranian funds frozen in Qatari banks and allowing confidential, unannounced visits by the International Atomic Energy Agency to Iranian nuclear sites. Stocks rose and oil prices declined as investors weighed a possible diplomatic breakthrough, with the S&P 500 up 0.6 percent in the report's account. Bloomberg reported that a diplomat involved in the US-Iran negotiations disputed reports suggesting a deal was close, while not denying that talks were continuing, and there has been no confirmed agreement to resume negotiations under the reported terms.
