Nutrien reported third quarter net earnings of 0.29 billion dollars, or 0.60 dollars a diluted share, with adjusted EBITDA of 1.31 billion dollars, and said it will shut down operations at its White Springs phosphate facility in Florida in the second quarter of 2027. The company said the mine is approaching the end of its economically recoverable reserve life, with rising operating costs and capital requirements, and that the closure should reduce annual sustaining capital expenditures and lease payments by about 50 million dollars.

White Springs is expected to account for about 20 percent of Nutrien's 2026 phosphate sales, and the company lowered its phosphate sales volume guidance to 2.40 to 2.55 million tonnes, citing deferred customer demand and lower operating rates at the plant. Nutrien also revised its retail adjusted EBITDA guidance to 1.72 to 1.85 billion dollars, reflecting lower third quarter crop nutrient gross margins.
