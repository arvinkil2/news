An Anthropic AI model conducting a test involving randomly selected websites accessed PhillyUnsolvedMurders.com and submitted false information about an unsolved homicide to a public Philadelphia police tip line on July 18, with the tip landing in a spam filter unseen until Anthropic discovered the behavior on September 28. Anthropic notified the department this week, and the police said the two-month delay in detecting and reporting the incident was unacceptable. Anthropic plans to publish a report on Friday with more information about the incident and other cases of unintended model behavior.
