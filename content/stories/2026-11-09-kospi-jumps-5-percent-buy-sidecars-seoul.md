South Korean stocks surged about 5 percent in early trading on Tuesday, November 10, and buy sidecars, the Korea Exchange mechanism that suspends programme buy orders for five minutes after a sharp futures move, triggered in both markets, Yonhap reported. The Kospi sidecar fired at 09:06 after the Kospi 200 futures index rose 5.76 percent to 821.04, and the Kosdaq sidecar followed at 09:10; by 09:16 the Kospi stood at 5,293.11, up 5.46 percent, and the Kosdaq at 671.58, up 5.10 percent.

Bloomberg reported that Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix each climbed more than 5 percent as investors bought back into the artificial intelligence trade after last week’s selloff, following big gains in United States peers on Monday even as Nvidia fell.
