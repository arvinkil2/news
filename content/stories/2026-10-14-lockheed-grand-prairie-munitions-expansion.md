Lockheed Martin is expanding its Grand Prairie operation in Texas, adding 100,000 square feet of new manufacturing space and converting 85,000 square feet of offices, in a 300 million dollar investment aimed at record production of missile components. The site feeds final assembly plants in Arkansas and Alabama and supports all of the company's Missiles and Fire Control programs.

A new partner, Hadrian, will install a 24 machine automated machining cell at the plant, due to run in early 2027, under a seven year agreement the two companies signed in 2025. Lockheed Martin said the Texas work sits inside a broader commitment to invest 8 to 9 billion dollars through 2030 to scale munitions production and expand or modernise more than 20 facilities nationwide.
