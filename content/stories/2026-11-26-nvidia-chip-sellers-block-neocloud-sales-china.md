Sellers of Nvidia chips have told neocloud providers that they cannot sell them Nvidia chips if any of the computing capacity would be rented to Chinese companies, according to CNBC reporting carried by Tech in Asia. The message followed a Bureau of Industry and Security interim final rule, announced on November 16 and effective November 19, that targets remote access to advanced AI chips and aims to stop Chinese companies from using overseas data centers to access Nvidia hardware. Sellers are also looking for new buyers for GPUs that appeared to have been meant for data centers that would remotely serve Chinese companies, and a further tightening is possible under the proposed Remote Access Security Act, which has passed the House of Representatives but not the Senate.
