Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said two oil tankers "exploded" after striking mines while travelling through an "unauthorized route" in the Strait of Hormuz, Iranian state media reported on Sunday, according to Reuters. The report names no vessel, gives no time for the explosions and carries no independent confirmation, and it is separate from the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations report earlier on Sunday of a vessel struck by an unknown projectile in the strait, covered separately today.

The claim extends a running dispute over mines in the strait. On Friday the IRGC said four tankers using unauthorized routes had been halted after incidents or mine strikes, and Oman rescued 25 crew from the burning Panama-flagged crude tanker RITI. United States Central Command chief Brad Cooper denied the mine claims the same day, saying the strait's internationally recognised primary transit routes had been successfully cleared of Iranian sea mines.
