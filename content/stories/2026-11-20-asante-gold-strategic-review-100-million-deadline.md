Asante Gold has opened a strategic review after receiving unsolicited approaches, as the Ghana gold producer faces a month-end deadline to secure at least $100 million (C$143.3 million) in new funding. The approaches are non-binding; Asante named no parties and gave no terms. Executives will present the turnaround plan for the Bibiani and Chirano mines on Friday, and the company expects to appoint advisers shortly.

The balance sheet sets the timetable. Asante held $64 million in cash at September 30 against a working capital deficit of $309.7 million, and it has disclosed a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt on its ability to continue as a going concern. Its financing partners have extended the funding deadline several times; missing the lender-approved technical action plan's performance indicators would trigger a default under the senior debt facility. Asante shares rose 4.5 percent to 35 cents on Thursday morning in Toronto, valuing the company at about C$314 million.

The strain is concentrated at Bibiani, where all-in sustaining costs reached $7,681 per ounce in the third quarter against a realized gold price of $3,873 an ounce, after power cuts, a contractor equipment shortage, resettlement delays and a January pit-wall failure. Asante withdrew its 2026 production and cost forecasts last week, reversing an August outlook of 275,000 to 300,000 gold-equivalent ounces at all-in sustaining costs of $3,200 to $3,600 an ounce. Chirano performed better, delivering 34,420 gold-equivalent ounces in the September quarter at all-in sustaining costs of $3,722 an ounce.
