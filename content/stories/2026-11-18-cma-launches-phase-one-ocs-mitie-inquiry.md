The UK Competition and Markets Authority on Wednesday announced the launch of its phase one merger inquiry into the anticipated acquisition of Mitie Group plc by OCS Group International Limited, by notice to the parties. The CMA said it has received the necessary information from the parties to commence pre-notification, and an earlier invitation to comment on the transaction's impact on competition in the UK ran from September 15 to September 29 and has closed.
