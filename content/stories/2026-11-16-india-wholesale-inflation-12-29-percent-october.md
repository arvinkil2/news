India's all-commodities wholesale price index rose 12.29 percent year on year in October 2026, up from 11.39 percent in September, with the index at 113.3 against 112.5 in September, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry reported on Monday. Fuel and power inflation climbed to 32.84 percent from 25.85 percent, and primary articles inflation rose to 9.24 percent, while manufactured products inflation eased to 9.54 percent.

The ministry named mineral oils, crude oil and natural gas, electricity and food articles among the main contributors to the higher rate; crude oil and natural gas prices were up 70.8 percent year on year and mineral oils 45.57 percent. Cumulative wholesale inflation for April to October of fiscal 2026-27 stands at 10.14 percent.
