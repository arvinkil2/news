The helicopter, an Italian contingent CH-47 Chinook with 15 people on board, came down in the Brcko District, about 180 kilometres north of Sarajevo, during a training operation on Thursday, Al Jazeera reported with AFP and Reuters. Four people were killed and 11 injured, according to EUFOR, and the injured were in stable condition in hospital; the Italian defence ministry said the dead were two soldiers and two Red Cross volunteers, and the Italian government said everyone on board was an Italian national.

Bosnian media photographs showed the helicopter overturned in a flat field. EUFOR said it is conducting a full investigation and will release more information as it is verified, and no cause for the crash has been stated.
