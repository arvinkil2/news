Hindustan Zinc's board on November 16 approved the HZL 2.0 growth programme, an expansion of about Rs 80,000 crore over the next three to five years that would raise the Vedanta group company's metal production capacity from 1.1 million tonnes per annum to 2.0 million tonnes, the company said in a filing to the BSE. The spending will be funded through internal accruals and debt, and the total includes the previously approved budget for the 250,000-tonne integrated zinc smelter at Debari in Rajasthan, exploration and related infrastructure.

The company tied the expansion to growth in zinc consumption from steel and infrastructure demand. Hindustan Zinc is the world's largest integrated zinc producer, among the top ten silver producers globally, and holds about 74 percent of India's primary zinc market; it reported a 142 percent rise in consolidated net profit to Rs 6,412 crore in the September quarter on higher metal prices and production.
