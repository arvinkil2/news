Hedera has closed a 23 million euro round with Quandela joining as a strategic investor, pairing the French photonic quantum computing company with the energy focused fund in a deal covered by Tech.eu.

Gradiant has raised 50 million dollars to expand its industrial water treatment business, the Boston company's latest round as semiconductor and mining customers price water as a process input rather than a utility.

BlackRock and HPS closed their 2.8 billion dollar purchase of Humana's CenterWell clinic stake this week; the deal was published here on October 14 and is the reference version for the life sciences beat's pointer.

Macquarie's offshore wind developer Corio Generation remains on the market, with infrastructure funds circling a platform whose development pipeline has outgrown its parent's balance sheet appetite, according to trade reporting.

Submer, the Barcelona immersion cooling company, continues to raise growth capital as data centre operators price liquid cooling as standard fit out rather than an exotic option, part of the same capital flow visible in AtNorth's credit facilities in this edition.
