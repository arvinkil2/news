Denmark will acquire two types of long-range precision missiles, the Norwegian Joint Strike Missile from Kongsberg for its F-35 fighter aircraft and the Anthem cruise missile from the American company Covenant, which can be launched from land, Defence Minister Jeppe Bruus said in a ministry statement on Thursday, adding that the purchases followed the Chief of Defence's recommendation and are meant to strengthen national and collective deterrence. Copenhagen disclosed neither quantities nor cost, and Kongsberg said in a company statement that negotiations with the customer will determine the scope and value of the Joint Strike Missile package, while Danish officials cite a NATO target for the country to offer a long-range strike capability by 2030. Russia's embassy in Copenhagen condemned the purchase in a written statement, warning of "inevitable disastrous consequences", Reuters reported.
