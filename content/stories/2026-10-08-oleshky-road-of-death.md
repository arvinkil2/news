Fewer than 2,000 people remain in Oleshky, a once-thriving resort city on the Dnipro River opposite Kherson, where residents have received almost no food or medicine deliveries for months and survive by scavenging, according to the Atlantic Council's UkraineAlert. Anyone attempting to flee must travel a 20-kilometer heavily mined, drone-patrolled highway to the nearest town that locals call the "road of death," marked by dozens of destroyed vehicles, and then journey through Russian-occupied territory and Russia itself to reach free Ukraine. Kyiv accuses Moscow of blockading the city and refusing to allow an evacuation or humanitarian aid in, and says Ukraine has begun drone deliveries of food and medicine while asking international organizations to mediate an evacuation.
