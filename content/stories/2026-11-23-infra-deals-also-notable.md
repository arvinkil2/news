Cadeler's third quarter earnings release, filed as a Form 6-K on November 23, put first nine months revenue at €663 million and EBITDA at €321 million, with a contract backlog including options of €2.4 billion at November 23. The release confirmed Cadeler completed its acquisition of foundation installation specialist Menck from Acteon in August 2026 for an enterprise value of €501 million, financed with available liquidity and a €380 million acquisition facility, and maintained full year 2026 guidance of €910 million to €1,000 million of revenue.

Net Power's Form 8-K, filed November 23, extended from November 20 to December 4, 2026 the deadline to execute a change order and limited notice to proceed under the EPC agreement for its initial project, keeping a $3.5 million termination payment deferred. The filing states the company has not entered a binding energy services or power offtake agreement for the project and the prospective customer is not obligated to enter one.

Stonepeak-Plus Infrastructure Fund LP reported in a Form 8-K that on November 2 it sold unregistered limited partnership units to third party investors for aggregate consideration of approximately $45.3 million across six unit classes, a small window on continuing retail facing infrastructure fund inflows.

Nigeria's upstream regulator NUPRC opened registration on November 20 for its 2026 licensing round covering 40 oil and gas blocks, 17 onshore, 14 shallow water and nine deep offshore. BusinessDay calculated the round could raise between $120 million and $280 million in signature bonuses at the revised $3 million to $7 million per block, down from a $10 million flat bonus in the 2024 round; registration closes January 15, 2027.

UAC of Nigeria has completed the sale of its entire 43 percent equity interest in DP World Logistics Limited to 22 Plus Invest Limited, a member of the TGI Group, following Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission approval, alongside the sale of its 73.29 percent stake in Livestock Feeds. Consideration for the logistics stake was not disclosed in the completion announcements.
