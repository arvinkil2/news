China's securities watchdog, the CSRC, has told law firm representatives in recent days that red-chip companies established after the introduction of new offshore listing rules in 2023 must be re-domiciled in China before they can list outside the mainland, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Reuters. Red-chip companies are registered abroad, mainly in tax havens, but control assets and businesses in China through equity ownership or contractual agreements, a structure favoured by Chinese startups seeking capital in Hong Kong or New York. The CSRC has approved Hong Kong listings for just 6 red-chip companies so far this year, against 50 last year, according to data compiled by law firms and Reuters, and Hong Kong's exchange said it welcomes issuers with a range of ownership structures provided they meet all applicable regulatory requirements.
