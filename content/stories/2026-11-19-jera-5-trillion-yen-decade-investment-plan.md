JERA plans to invest 5 trillion yen ($32.4 billion) over the coming decade, split at 1 trillion to 2 trillion yen each across liquefied natural gas, renewable energy and new fuels such as hydrogen and ammonia, global CEO Yukio Kani told reporters in Tokyo on Thursday. By fiscal 2035 the company targets more than 35 million tonnes of annual LNG transaction volumes, 20 gigawatts of renewables capacity and 7 million tonnes of hydrogen and ammonia handling, with consolidated net profit of 350 billion yen.

President Hisahide Okuda told the same briefing the unlisted company, co-owned by Tokyo Electric Power and Chubu Electric Power, continues to weigh options for a capital increase, including its current shareholders, a third-party allotment or an initial public offering. JERA also plans to phase out inefficient coal-fired power by fiscal 2030 and convert its remaining coal generation to ammonia by the 2040s.
