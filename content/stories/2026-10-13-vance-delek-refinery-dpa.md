Vance made the announcement at a campaign stop in Tyler, Texas, saying the administration will work with Delek US Holdings on the expansion and tap the Defense Production Act, the Cold War era law President Trump has already invoked to fund a range of energy projects. The move comes as high energy prices strain household budgets and squeeze the administration politically ahead of the midterms, with diesel costs in particular at the centre of the week's fight over the Russian fuel deal. It follows September reporting by Reuters that the White House was weighing Defense Production Act use to expand United States refining capacity, with refiners already running near full utilization.
