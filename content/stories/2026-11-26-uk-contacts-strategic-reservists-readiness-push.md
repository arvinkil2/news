Britain's Ministry of Defence is contacting thousands of former service members this week, with service chiefs writing to members of the Strategic Reserve to ask them to update their contact details and skills records, the ministry said. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting told BBC Radio 4 he wanted the country "alert, not alarmed," citing Russian incursions into Polish airspace, suspected Russian sponsored sabotage across Europe and Iran linked threats to British bases. The MoD's own guidance stresses that the contact is not a mobilisation notice: Strategic Reservists are former personnel with a legal liability to return in a national emergency, and the drive is meant to improve contact and skills information, offer training and exercise opportunities, and test mobilisation arrangements. Separately, the Royal Air Force is strengthening base security with hardened aircraft shelters and anti drone netting, and the MoD said it is investing an additional £470 million in physical protection, surveillance and counter drone capabilities at military sites.
