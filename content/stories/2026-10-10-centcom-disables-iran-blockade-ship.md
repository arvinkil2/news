**Latest (Sunday, October 11):** Senator Chris Murphy criticized the strike on X, asking "What is this all for? Why are taxpayers paying billions to hit commercial ships?" and calling the blockade strategy a "disaster." The UK Maritime Trade Operations agency separately acknowledged Sunday that it had received "a report of an incident involving a tanker and military forces in the Gulf of Oman," saying authorities were investigating. The Financial Times also carried the story under the headline "US hits commercial tanker in Gulf of Oman as tensions flare," at headline level only; the article itself is gated behind a human-verification check.

US Central Command said a US fighter jet struck the stern of the Panama-flagged M/V Ocean Molica, also known as the Arika Sun, with a precision munition, fully disabling its propulsion after the crew ignored warnings and attempted to transit regional waters after leaving an Iranian port. CENTCOM said no crew were harmed in the strike. The interception is the latest enforcement action in the ongoing US naval blockade of Iran; CENTCOM claims three months of the blockade have seen 10 shadow-fleet tankers destroyed, 4 ships disabled and 135 vessels forced to divert, figures that rest on CENTCOM's own reporting.
