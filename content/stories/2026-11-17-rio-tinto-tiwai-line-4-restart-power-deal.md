Rio Tinto will restart Line 4 at the Tiwai Point aluminium smelter in New Zealand under a three-and-a-half year, 50 megawatt electricity agreement with Contact Energy, the company said on Monday. The restart is expected to begin in October 2027 after preparations and commissioning, reach full production by December 2027, and lift output by about 30,000 tonnes a year, restoring the smelter to about 360,000 tonnes a year, roughly three percent of Rio Tinto's Australia and New Zealand smelting capacity. The contract cuts supply to 25 megawatts over winter months to protect New Zealand's wider energy security, and Line 4 has been idle since April 2020, when the pandemic hit global industrial markets.
