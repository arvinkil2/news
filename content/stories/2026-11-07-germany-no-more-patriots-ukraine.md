German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius said Germany has exhausted the possibilities for transferring Patriot air-defence systems to Ukraine, telling Focus that anything more provided now could not be replenished for the time being and would lead to gaps in Germany's own defence, for which it could not bear responsibility. Pistorius said other states must now take on a greater burden, and that the issue will be discussed in mid-November in the Ramstein-format Contact Group on Ukraine.

The statement follows a call by Ukraine's defence minister for the E5 countries, Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Poland and Italy, to consider transferring 10 percent of their stocks of interceptor missiles for Patriot and IRIS-T systems against future deliveries Kyiv expects. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said Ukraine receives on average only 12 missiles for its Patriot systems per month this year.
