Ford said on Tuesday it has held talks with the Pentagon about supplying military vehicles and other defence products to the US military. A Ford spokesperson said the discussions focused on supplying highly capable military vehicles and on working with US-based space systems contractor Revolution Space, in which Ford announced a minority investment on Monday, to build advanced satellites.

The talks sit inside a wider Pentagon effort to enlist domestic manufacturers to mass-produce military drones. The Wall Street Journal reported earlier on Tuesday that defence officials have met General Motors, Ford and other manufacturers about supplying parts including actuators, engines, sensors and lidar to drone companies, that GM is weighing whether to manufacture drone motors and has held talks with RTX and L3Harris, and that officials have also met Canadian parts maker Magna International. GM declined to comment on the report and Magna said it had nothing to add.
