A Jefferies-run fund said in court proceedings that it has evidence of alleged fraudulent conduct by iron ore traders Radiant World and Sapphire Minmetals in receivables-financing deals with a face value of almost $900 million, Bloomberg reported Saturday. The claim was made in an affidavit by a lawyer for the fund and significantly expands what is known about the scale of the alleged fraud.

The case centers on receivables Jefferies' Point Bonita trade-finance fund (LAM Trade Finance Group II) financed against invoices purportedly issued by traders including Glencore, which later said the invoices were not genuine. The fund previously obtained a roughly $499 million worldwide freezing order against Radiant and its founder, while Radiant has denied wrongdoing and countersued Glencore for $2 billion in Singapore. The U.S. Justice Department, the CFTC and Singapore police all have probes under way.
