Global Payments shares fell 9 percent on Thursday and were halted under a volatility breaker after Clayton, Dubilier and Rice and GTCR abandoned discussions about a potential acquisition of the payments processor, Reuters reported, citing two people familiar with the matter. The consortium had explored a transaction over the past several weeks, but the early stage discussions did not advance amid the complexity of arranging a take private deal for a company with a market capitalisation of about $26.4 billion. The people said the talks could resume later or another buyer could emerge; CD&R declined to comment, and Global Payments and GTCR did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
