Hassett raises the October bar. National Economic Council director Kevin Hassett said the Federal Reserve would act if the October inflation data come in high, Bloomberg reported on October 29. The remark, which this run has from the headline and pool record rather than a full read, frames the next two US inflation reports as the trigger for the December decision that futures already price at about an 83 percent chance of a hike. It is also a White House voice putting public weight on a data dependent central bank a week before the midterm elections, which is itself part of the story.

Lagarde will not run, but will campaign. Reporting on the ECB president's February 7, 2027 departure added a political dimension on October 29 and 30. A source with direct knowledge told Reuters that Lagarde resigned so she could weigh in on political matters in France and globally, freed from the neutrality expected of the ECB president. Lagarde herself has said she will not be a candidate for the French presidency but will advocate for pro-European values in her home country in whichever capacity she judges most effective. European commentary read the timing as ensuring her successor is chosen before the French presidential election, with former Dutch and Spanish central bank governors Klaas Knot and Pablo Hernandez de Cos the widely cited frontrunners. The succession process, and the parallel vacancies for the chief economist and a market operations board seat, will run through the ECB's December meetings.

SARB prudential notices. The South African Reserve Bank published notices amending the designations of Discovery Insurance Group and of LomHold (Pty) Ltd under its prudential authority framework, dated October 30. These are regulatory designation amendments published by the bank itself; the notices appear in this run's gap check as single source items, which is expected for an issuing authority's own gazette style publications, and no market figures attach to them. The bank also published its September 2026 international reserves template in the same window.

ECB research on sovereign holders. The ECB published a working paper on October 30 on the effects of geopolitical risk on sovereign debt holders in the euro area. No findings are summarised here because the paper itself was not read in this run; it is logged for the sovereign spread file, where the composition of the holder base is a standing question for French and Italian debt.

UK national accounts annual round. The Office for National Statistics published the 2026 Blue Book and Pink Book on October 30, with GDP revisions, supply and use tables and the balance of payments. The annual benchmarking round is the UK counterpart of the South African rebasing covered in a full story in this edition; any material revision to past UK growth or the current account will be picked up from the ONS releases themselves rather than from summaries.

Japan bill supply. The Ministry of Finance announced treasury discount bills to be auctioned on November 6, 2026, a routine bill sale noted for the calendar only. The Bank of Japan separately published its September payment and settlement statistics.

RBI smaller releases. The Reserve Bank of India published the premature redemption price for the Sovereign Gold Bond 2019-20 Series VI due October 30, the rate of interest on the Government of India Floating Rate Bond 2034, and its data on external commercial borrowings, foreign currency convertible bonds and rupee denominated bonds for August 2026. All three are standing statistical releases from the bank; the figures are in the releases themselves, which are the sources of record.
