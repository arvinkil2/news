The EU's plastics sector is the closest to possible new safeguard measures against surging imports, Trade Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič said on Friday after the informal meeting of trade ministers in Dublin, adding that the European Commission was assessing the legal and economic requirements for opening proceedings without specifying which products could be covered or when an investigation might begin.

The comment came as Brussels weighs further measures against Chinese industrial overcapacity, following the deal reached with Beijing on October 9 to curb imports of hybrid vehicles through country-specific quotas. Speaking after the ministers' meeting, Šefčovič said the EU was walking a tightrope in negotiations with Beijing, that whole sectors were under threat, and that rebalancing trade would require a political approach alongside traditional trade techniques, Euronews reported.
