Tesla said on Friday evening that select customers can now pre-order its autonomous Cybercab for personal use, at $29,990 plus $299 a month to operate the vehicle, with delivery estimated in December 2026. Buyers must take delivery in one of a handful of Texas cities, and the car will run unsupervised only inside the same geofenced locations as Tesla's robotaxi service, according to Tesla executive Ashok Elluswamy. Chief executive Elon Musk said the $299 monthly fee is too high and should match the roughly $115 a month Tesla charges for Full Self-Driving and connectivity on its other models, and that the Cybercab will be available nationwide as the company receives regulatory approval.
