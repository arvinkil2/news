AI server prices are being raised by as much as 25 to 45 percent between contract and delivery as suppliers pass on surging memory costs, according to Financial Times reporting carried by Yonhap, with Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro all demanding more than the agreed price at delivery. One source cited said GPU server prices have risen at least 20 to 30 percent and memory heavy CPU nodes have more than tripled, while JPMorgan Research estimates DRAM prices will have risen more than 400 percent from early 2024 to the end of this year. The increases are hitting trading firms building their own data centres, including Jane Street, Optiver and Hudson River Trading, and XTX Markets sued Dell in an Irish court in June after Dell sought an extra 62 million to 70 million dollars on a server order the two sides had fixed at 549 million dollars.
