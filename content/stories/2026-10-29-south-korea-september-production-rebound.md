South Korea's factories, shops and boardrooms all expanded in September. The National Data Agency reported all-industry production up 3.0 percent from August, the largest monthly increase since March 2011, alongside retail sales up 3.6 percent and facility investment up 5.3 percent, Yonhap News Agency reported. Mining and manufacturing output jumped 9.0 percent, led by semiconductors, up 19.2 percent, and automobiles, up 39.9 percent as the end of a strike and of summer holidays lifted factory operating rates after August's sharp fall.

It is the first month in three in which production, consumption and investment have risen together, reversing August, when production fell 1.2 percent. Retail's 3.6 percent rise was its biggest in three years and seven months, with durable goods such as cars up 4.4 percent, food and beverages up 4.2 percent and clothing up 0.6 percent, while facility investment was led by transport equipment, up 7.4 percent, and machinery, up 4.7 percent. The release follows Tuesday's third quarter GDP print, which grew 1.0 percent on the quarter and beat the Bank of Korea's August forecast.
