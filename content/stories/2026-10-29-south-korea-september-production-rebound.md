South Korea's factories, shops and boardrooms all expanded in September. The Ministry of Data and Statistics reported industrial production up 3.0 percent from August, the largest monthly increase in 15 years and six months, alongside retail sales up 3.6 percent and facility investment up 5.3 percent, Yonhap News Agency reported. Retail sales fell 0.4 percent from a year earlier despite the monthly jump.

It is the first month in three in which production, consumption and investment have risen together, reversing August, when all three fell at once on temporary factors including automaker summer shutdowns concentrated in that month. The release follows Tuesday's third quarter GDP print, which grew 1.0 percent on the quarter and beat the Bank of Korea's August forecast.
