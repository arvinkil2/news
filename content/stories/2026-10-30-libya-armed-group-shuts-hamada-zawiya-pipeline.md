An armed group closed a valve on Friday in Libya's Rayayna area on the 18-inch crude pipeline that carries oil from the Hamada fields and the Tahara field to the Zawiya refinery, the National Oil Corporation said in a statement reported by Reuters. The sudden closure raised operating pressure on the line, and technical teams halted pumping at 12:30 a.m. to protect the pipeline and production facilities.

The corporation warned that a prolonged shutdown would disrupt approximately 15,000 barrels per day of crude intended for the refinery, and it urged the Petroleum Facilities Guard to secure the pipeline, reopen the valve and allow pumping to resume. Zawiya is Libya's largest refinery, with capacity of 120,000 barrels per day, and Reuters noted this is the second time this month that oil flows to it have been halted by an armed group.
