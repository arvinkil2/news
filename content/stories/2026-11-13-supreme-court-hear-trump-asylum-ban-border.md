The US Supreme Court agreed on Friday to consider President Donald Trump's ban on asylum applications at the US-Mexico border, taking up the administration's bid to overturn lower court decisions that found the executive order illegal. The ban, imposed when Trump returned to office in 2025, has remained in place during the litigation, and the administration argues it can categorically deny asylum to migrants covered by the order rather than accept applications it expects to reject. Attorneys for the American Civil Liberties Union and the National Immigrant Justice Center counter that asylum decisions are supposed to be made on an individual basis and that the law does not allow the president to stop people from applying at all.
