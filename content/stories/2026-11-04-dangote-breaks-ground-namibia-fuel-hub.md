Aliko Dangote broke ground on Wednesday on a fuel storage site outside the port of Walvis Bay in Namibia, the first piece of a planned $2.5 billion distribution hub for southern Africa. The project includes a 1,700 kilometre pipeline to carry fuel to Botswana and South Africa, and Dangote said it is meant to let landlocked countries access petroleum products at a cheaper price. The depot will be supplied by the Dangote Group's 700,000 barrel a day refinery in Nigeria, which began operations two years ago and is expanding its distribution network across Africa.
