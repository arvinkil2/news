Saudi air defences intercepted four missiles fired at the kingdom on Saturday, the Coalition to Support Legitimacy in Yemen said in statements reported by Arab News. Two ballistic missiles aimed at the southern region and one ballistic missile aimed at al-Shuqaiq in the southwest were destroyed, and a cruise missile aimed at Riyadh province was intercepted earlier in the day. Debris fell near al-Shuqaiq with no civilian casualties, and Civil Defense alerts issued for Abha, Khamis Mushait and Jazan were lifted shortly afterwards.

The interceptions extend a week of Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia. A strike on the Jazan refinery on Thursday killed a Nepali worker and injured several others, an attack on Jazan port on Wednesday caused minor damage, and a strike on a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District earlier in the week injured 11 people.
