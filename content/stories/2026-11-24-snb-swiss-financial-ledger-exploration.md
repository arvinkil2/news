The Swiss National Bank and eight partner institutions, UBS, SIX, Zürcher Kantonalbank, PostFinance, Banque Cantonale Vaudoise, Basler Kantonalbank, Sygnum Bank and the Seturion settlement platform, have started an exploratory project for a Swiss Financial Ledger. The work sits inside Project Helvetia, the SNB's programme for settling tokenised assets in central bank money, and builds on Project Agorá, the Bank for International Settlements and Institute of International Finance initiative on tokenised cross-border payments.

The project will test whether one distributed ledger platform can serve cross-border transactions and domestic transactions alike, with central bank money, commercial bank money and securities interacting on it, and will assess technical design options alongside governance and operating models. A Helvetia Strategic Board drawn from the participating institutions, with the Swiss Bankers Association as an observer, will steer the work, and the SNB is explicit that the exploration signals neither a launch decision nor a commitment to issue tokenised central bank money.
