Three explosions were heard in Aden on Saturday, an Aden police officer and an AFP correspondent reported, and another police source told AFP the Houthis had targeted a Saudi-led coalition position near Aden airport and the coast guard. Al Arabiya reported four explosions in the city, three of them around the international airport, and IRNA, quoting Al Jazeera, put the airport and the coast guard headquarters among the probable targets, citing local sources who said three ballistic missiles had targeted the airport. Separately, the Houthis' military spokesman Yahya Saria said the movement had struck Saudi forces at the An-Nasr camp and the naval base in Aden, TASS and IRNA both reported, and no casualties or damage were reported for the city.
