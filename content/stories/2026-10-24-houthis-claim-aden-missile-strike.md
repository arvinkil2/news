Three explosions were heard in Aden on Saturday, an Aden police officer and an AFP correspondent reported, and another police source told AFP the Houthis had targeted a Saudi-led coalition position near Aden airport and the coast guard. Separately, the Houthis' military spokesman Yahya Saria said the movement had struck Saudi forces at the An-Nasr camp and the naval base in Aden, TASS reported. Neither report gave casualties or damage for Aden, and ERT reported that neither side had publicly commented on the alleged attacks.
