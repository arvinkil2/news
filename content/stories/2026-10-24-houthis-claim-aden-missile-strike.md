Explosions were heard in Aden on Saturday and Yemeni media reported a missile attack on the city's international airport and the coast guard headquarters in the Tawahi district, according to Iran's IRNA agency as carried by ERT News, which said no official statement had been issued. Separately, the Houthis' military spokesman Yahya Saree said the movement had struck a military camp and a naval base in Aden with ballistic missiles, framing the attack as a response to strikes on Yemeni cities, TASS reported. Neither report gave casualties or damage for Aden, and there was no Yemeni or Saudi confirmation of the strike.
