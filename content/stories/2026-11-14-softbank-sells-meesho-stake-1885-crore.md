SoftBank's affiliate SVF II Meerkat (DE) LLC sold 76.4 million shares in Bengaluru-based Meesho at an average price of 246.75 rupees on Friday, according to block deal data on the National Stock Exchange, taking the deal value to 1,885.17 crore rupees and its holding down to 5.19 percent from 6.84 percent. Societe Generale was the largest buyer, taking more than 14.7 million shares, a 0.32 percent stake, for 364.65 crore rupees, alongside domestic mutual funds and overseas institutions including Morgan Stanley, Fidelity Investments, BNP Paribas and Goldman Sachs, and Meesho shares rose 1.53 percent to close at 257.99 rupees on Friday. The sale follows a 1.7 percent Meesho stake sale for 1,650 crore rupees in September and a series of trims to SoftBank's other listed Indian holdings this year.
