Dropbox announced a reduction of its global workforce by approximately 9 percent on October 28, 2026, to align its investments and team structure with its top product and growth priorities, and estimated total cash expenditures of approximately $27 million to $33 million, mainly severance and employee benefits, with $20 million to $25 million of incremental expense to be recognized, most of it in the fourth quarter of 2026. The same filing said third quarter 2026 results are expected to be in line with or above the guidance ranges given on August 6, 2026 for revenue, constant currency revenue and non-GAAP operating margin, ahead of the scheduled results release on November 5, 2026. Chief technology officer Ali Dasdan will step down effective November 18, 2026, receiving a pro rata bonus of $515,000 and a lump sum payment of approximately $1.3 million under his separation agreement.
