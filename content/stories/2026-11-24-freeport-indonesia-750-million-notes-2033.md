PT Freeport Indonesia, a 48.76 percent-owned subsidiary of Freeport-McMoRan, has completed its previously announced offering of $750 million of 6.90 percent senior notes due 2033, the parent company said in a Form 8-K filed on November 24. The notes are senior unsecured obligations of the Indonesian unit and will be included in Freeport-McMoRan's consolidated debt, with proceeds used to refinance existing debt and for general corporate purposes.

The unit has also issued a notice to redeem all of its outstanding $750 million of 4.763 percent senior notes due 2027 on December 24, 2026, funded from the new offering's proceeds and cash on hand.
