Legora, which builds AI tools that automate and assist legal workflow tasks, said on Wednesday it has raised 550 million dollars in a Series E round led by Atreides Management, with Baron Capital, Bessemer Venture Partners, General Catalyst and Menlo also investing, at an 8.8 billion dollar valuation, TechCrunch reported. The company said its annual recurring revenue now stands at more than 200 million dollars, up from 100 million dollars when it extended its Series D in April at a 5.6 billion dollar valuation, and that it will use the capital to expand further across North America.

The round lands a month after Harvey, Legora's biggest competitor, announced its own 550 million dollar raise at a 15.5 billion dollar valuation.
