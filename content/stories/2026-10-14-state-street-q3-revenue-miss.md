State Street's third quarter was better than it looked in the share price. The Boston custody bank earned $3.69 a diluted share, up from $2.78 a year earlier and a cent above consensus, as servicing fees rose 9.9 percent to $1.49 billion on higher client activity and market levels. Assets under custody or administration reached $59.2 trillion, and assets under management ended the quarter at $6.31 trillion.

Revenue was the problem. At $4.004 billion it rose 12.9 percent from a year earlier but missed the roughly $4.03 billion consensus and fell 1 percent from the second quarter, while expenses rose 8 percent to $2.62 billion. The shares, which had closed at $174.10 on Tuesday, opened at $161.90 and traded lower still in early dealings, a drop of about 8 percent that left the earnings beat with little credit.
