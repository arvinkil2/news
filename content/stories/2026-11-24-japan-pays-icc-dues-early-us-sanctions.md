Japan will pay its full 2027 contribution to the International Criminal Court by year-end, Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi said on Tuesday, after the court sought early payment from its largest contributor amid US sanctions. Takaichi informed ICC President Tomoko Akane of the decision during a meeting at her office in Tokyo and announced it in a post on X, according to Reuters. Japan supplies around 15 percent of the money needed to cover the court's budget, ahead of Germany, France, Britain, Italy, South Korea and Canada, and the early payment gives the court access to funding before US sanctions take full effect in April 2027, as Washington pushes to dismantle the body through sanctions and by encouraging member states to leave.
