Albert G. White III resigned from the board of The Cooper Companies and ceased serving as president and chief executive on October 27, 2026, the company said in a Form 8-K. White remains employed full time as a strategic advisor through April 1, 2027 to assist with the transition, and the filing says the terms of additional transition compensation have not yet been determined. The board appointed Walter M. Rosebrough, Jr., an independent director since January 2026 and chief executive emeritus of STERIS, as executive chair on an interim basis, and formed a CEO search committee of Lawrence E. Kurzius, Cynthia L. Lucchese and Teresa S. Madden. The same filing appoints Shlomo Nachman to the board from December 8, 2026, and records Barbara Carbone, Colleen E. Jay and Robert S. Weiss as not standing for reelection at the 2027 annual meeting, after which the board intends to reduce its size to seven directors.
