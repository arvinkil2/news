South Korea's Fair Trade Commission has imposed a record fine of 1.63 trillion won ($1.2 billion) on more than a dozen domestic banks and brokerages for colluding in government bond auctions, Bloomberg reported, citing the commission's statement on Wednesday. The primary dealers exchanged bid yield information or agreed on yields and volumes in 150 Korean Treasury Bond auctions from January 2020 through June 2023, and the commission found agreements on specific bid yields or volumes in 145 of them, in a market of roughly 1,261 trillion won ($942 billion).

The decision ends a case the dealers had contested since the commission's staff sent them its examination report. Staff had treated 76.2 trillion won of winning bids as the sales base for the fine, a method that pointed to theoretical penalties of up to 15 trillion won; the 1.63 trillion won imposed is a record for the commission but a fraction of that exposure.
