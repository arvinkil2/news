US Central Command said its aircraft struck the Palau-flagged tanker Pacific Archer with precision munitions in the Gulf of Oman on Thursday, destroying the ship's propulsion after it departed Bandar Abbas and attempted to evade the naval blockade of Iranian ports, in force since mid-July. The civilian crew was unharmed, and CENTCOM put the running tally at 158 commercial vessels redirected and six disabled before this strike.

Tehran answered through Mohammad Mokhber, an adviser to Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei, who told Iranian state media that the Strait of Hormuz will not be reopened and that Iran will not allow the blockade to remain in place, without saying how it would be broken.
