The IRGC Navy's public relations office said Saturday that a crude oil supertanker struck a naval mine while trying to exit the Strait of Hormuz via what it called an "unauthorized and dangerous route," triggering a powerful explosion and a blaze the statement said was visible from shore. The statement added that the vessel had switched off its navigation and positioning systems beforehand, and warned that "a massive fire awaits any violating oil tanker" that ignores Iran's Strait regulations. No vessel has been named, no independent authority has confirmed an incident, and previous IRGC mine claims have been publicly denied by US Central Command, so the report is uncorroborated.
