Euro area GDP increased 0.4 percent in the third quarter of 2026 from the previous quarter, and EU GDP rose 0.5 percent, Eurostat's flash estimate showed, down from 0.6 percent and 0.7 percent respectively in the second quarter. From a year earlier, GDP rose 1.3 percent in the euro area and 1.5 percent in the EU, while the number of employed persons was unchanged in both areas in the quarter. France contracted 0.1 percent, Italy was flat, Germany grew 0.2 percent and Spain grew 0.6 percent, and United States GDP rose 0.8 percent in the quarter, up 2.1 percent from a year earlier.
