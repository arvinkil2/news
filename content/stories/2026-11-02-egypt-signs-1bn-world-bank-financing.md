Egypt signed the $1 billion agreement on Sunday at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the New Administrative Capital, with Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty, who is also Egypt's governor at the World Bank, signing alongside Alwaleed Alatabani, the Bank's division director for Egypt, Yemen and Djibouti, and Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly in attendance. The money is the second operation in a three part development policy financing series the Bank's board approved on May 8, 2026, and it pays into the budget against reforms covering state owned enterprise governance, barriers to private investment, competition, domestic revenue collection and the financial sustainability of the electricity and water sectors. The package sits inside the World Bank Group's 2023 to 2027 Country Partnership Framework for Egypt, a $7 billion envelope, and the second phase also expands the Takaful and Karama social protection programs and adds greenhouse gas monitoring and carbon market reforms.
