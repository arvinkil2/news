SK Hynix reported third quarter revenue of 104.62 trillion won, up 327.9 percent from a year earlier and above the 98.34 trillion won expected, with operating profit of 83.33 trillion won, up 632.1 percent year on year and above the 76.72 trillion won expected, as sales passed 100 trillion won in a quarter for the first time. The company expects HBM4 to overtake HBM3E in shipments and revenue in the fourth quarter, said it has secured long term agreements with 14 major customers covering more than half of its 2027 and 2028 production capacity, and expects memory demand to outpace supply through 2030. It also approved a third quarter dividend of 14 trillion won, including a special dividend funded by gains on its Kioxia stake, within a plan to return about 100 trillion won to shareholders through dividends, buybacks and cancellations.
