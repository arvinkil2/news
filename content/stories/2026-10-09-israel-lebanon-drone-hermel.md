An Israeli drone strike wounded six people in Lebanon's Hermel district near the Syrian border on Friday, the Lebanese health ministry said; five were Syrian nationals, including two women, and one was Lebanese. State media said the strike targeted a car in an area largely spared since the June ceasefire with Hezbollah. Israel's military said it struck "a Syrian terrorist" who operated under Iranian direction and planned attacks against Israeli forces in southern Syria.
