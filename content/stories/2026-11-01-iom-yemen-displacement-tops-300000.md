More than 300,000 people have fled their homes because of fighting in western Yemen, almost all of them in the past two months, the International Organization for Migration said on Sunday. More than 43,000 people have left the Al-Madaribah Wa Al-Arah district of Lahj province since the start of October, the highest figure for any district in the country, and Aden now hosts more than 56,000 displaced people, many of them arriving from Taiz province.

In the city of Taiz, clashes have cut off access to neighbouring areas and pushed the price of many essentials up as much as fivefold, the agency said, leaving families unable to afford basic food. The IOM has five mobile medical teams working across Taiz, Marib, Lahj and Aden, and it says it needs $45 million over the next six months to fund emergency assistance as displacement grows.
