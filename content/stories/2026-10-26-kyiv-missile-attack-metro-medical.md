A Russian missile attack on Kyiv on Monday evening killed at least one person, who died near a supermarket according to preliminary information, and injured seven people across the city, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said, in figures reported by Reuters, whose witnesses in the capital said a series of explosions jolted the city and power was cut in districts on the east side of the Dnipro River. Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched two Iskander-M ballistic missiles from the Kursk region at Kyiv, NV.ua reported in Russian, and the strike hit the area of the Kyiv Polytechnic Institute campus in Shuliavka, where Suspilne reported windows blown out in a student dormitory, an academic building damaged and a fire in a warehouse in Desnianskyi district, with tram tracks damaged and the entrance to Lukyanivska metro station damaged. Klitschko said two medics were wounded in Desnianskyi district when a repeat strike hit while they were aiding victims, Reuters reported that a member of an emergency crew had come under attack after rushing to a strike scene and that explosions shattered windows at two medical facilities, and the energy company DTEK imposed emergency power outages on the left bank of the city. Moscow has long denied that it targets civilians.
