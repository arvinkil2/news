A Russian missile attack on Kyiv on Monday killed at least one person, who died near a supermarket according to preliminary information, and injured seven people across the city, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said, in figures reported by Reuters. Klitschko said there was a strike next to an underground station, that explosions shattered windows at two medical facilities, and that a member of an emergency crew came under attack after rushing to the scene of one strike, while Reuters witnesses said a series of explosions jolted the city and power was cut in districts on the east side of the Dnipro River. Moscow has long denied that it targets civilians.
