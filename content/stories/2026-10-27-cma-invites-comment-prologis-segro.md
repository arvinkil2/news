The Competition and Markets Authority has issued an invitation to comment on the anticipated acquisition of SEGRO plc by Prologis, L.P., with written representations on competition issues due by November 10, 2026. The regulator said the invitation is the first part of its information gathering process and that it has not yet launched its formal phase 1 investigation into the transaction.

The CMA said it has received the information it needs from the parties to begin pre-notification. Its case page states no price or other deal terms, and no deadline for a phase 1 decision has been set.
