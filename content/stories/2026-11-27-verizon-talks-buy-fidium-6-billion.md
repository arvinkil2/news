Verizon is in discussions to buy Fidium, the fiber-optic broadband operator owned by Searchlight Capital Partners and British Columbia Investment Management Corporation, for about $6 billion including debt, according to the Financial Times. Fidium, the former Consolidated Communications, was taken private by Searchlight and BCI in a $3.1 billion deal in 2024 and operates in 21 states.

A deal could be agreed in the coming weeks, though the talks may still fail and another bidder could emerge. Fidium would add a subscriber base of 351,000 homes in northern New England, including Maine, New Hampshire and Vermont, and follows Verizon's $20 billion takeover of Frontier Communications earlier this year, which added 2.2 million fiber subscribers across 25 states.
