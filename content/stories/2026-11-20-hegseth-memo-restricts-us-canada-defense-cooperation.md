US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth imposed new restrictions on defense cooperation with Canada in a classified memo distributed last week, the Washington Post reported on Friday, citing US officials familiar with the matter. The officials said the memo's specific contents have not been confirmed, and one said Canada would receive the measure as a slap in the face.

Pentagon spokesperson Sean Parnell told the Post that the United States is taking measures to ensure cooperation with Canada advances America's substantive defense goals and ultimately supports revitalizing the bilateral defense relationship, and that the directive would not affect defense intelligence sharing or cooperation through NATO or NORAD. No Canadian government reaction was reported, and a Five Eyes official told the Post that information shared by the United States through several channels has decreased over the past year.
