The US Treasury has announced record enforcement actions against Bank of America, the largest such package it has brought against a single institution, in a release issued during the Bangkok meetings week.

Treasury enforcement of this kind typically bundles findings from its financial crime and sanctions arms, and the record scale is the news regardless of the underlying mix: it sets the compliance bar every other large bank will now measure itself against, and it arrives while the administration is simultaneously projecting financial statecraft abroad, from cartel designations to the development finance statement Secretary Bessent issued in Bangkok.

Bank of America's response and the detailed findings were not in the headline release summary available to this edition, and the story is carried at the level the primary source supports.
