Retail sales volumes in Great Britain fell 0.4 percent in September 2026, following a rise of 0.7 percent in August that was revised up from 0.5 percent, according to the Office for National Statistics. The fall was led by supermarket sales, which dropped back after growth in July and August, and by mail order retailers, where online clothing sellers said warm weather hurt sales of autumn and winter ranges, while automotive fuel volumes rose on the month. Across the third quarter as a whole, volumes rose 0.7 percent against the second quarter and were 1.5 percent higher than a year earlier, and the proportion of sales made online eased to 28.8 percent in September from 28.9 percent in August.
