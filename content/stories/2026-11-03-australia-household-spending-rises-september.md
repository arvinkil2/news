Household spending rose 0.6 percent in September 2026 in seasonally adjusted terms, the Australian Bureau of Statistics said, following a fall of 0.1 percent in August and a rise of 1.0 percent in July. Transport and health spending each rose 1.5 percent, and recreation and culture rose 0.8 percent, with fuel spending up 5.4 percent after the fuel excise relief ended on August 3, even as the volume of fuel spending fell 2.2 percent.

For the September quarter, household spending volumes grew 0.7 percent, the fourth quarter in a row of growth, with seven of the nine spending categories recording increases and transport the strongest at 1.6 percent, led by air travel.
