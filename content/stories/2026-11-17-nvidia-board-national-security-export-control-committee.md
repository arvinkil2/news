Nvidia's board has formed a standing National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, less than a week after Jensen Huang resigned from the company. The committee will provide high-level oversight of Nvidia's national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management, with the company's National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team reporting directly to it and to interim CEO Colette Kress, and it will work with independent law, national security and trade compliance experts.

Board chair Harvey Jones said Nvidia recognizes that advanced silicon technologies and the emergence of artificial and superintelligence present unprecedented challenges for national security, and that the company has a responsibility to help establish a robust framework to safeguard national security while supporting continued innovation. The move follows reporting that Alice Huang was reportedly instrumental in importing around $7.5 billion of banned Nvidia chips into China; Huang has denied any evidence of AI chip diversion, and the chairs of the House committees on China, Foreign Affairs and Intelligence have invited him to a closed-door briefing on Nvidia's security protocols.
