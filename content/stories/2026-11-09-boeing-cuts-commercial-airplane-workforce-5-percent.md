Boeing will reduce the workforce of its commercial airplane unit by about 5 percent through a combination of attrition and involuntary layoffs, with notifications beginning in December, CNBC reported. Stephanie Pope, who heads the unit, told staff in a memo that adjusting production schedules and reducing aircraft output had left the unit overstaffed against its revised production schedule and business plan.

The slower ramp centres on the 737 Max, which Boeing is building at about 47 planes a month and now targets at 52 a month in the second half of 2027. Chief executive Kelly Ortberg said late last month that Boeing does not expect to significantly increase 737 Max output until the second half of 2027, later than previously expected.
