Denmark's parliament ratified the security agreement with the United States over Greenland on Thursday, with all 109 members present in the 179-seat chamber voting in favour; Greenland's parliament had already approved the deal in early November. The agreement, signed in September by President Trump, Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen and Greenlandic Prime Minister Jens-Frederik Nielsen, expands the US military presence on the island beyond the access held since 1951, allows a Golden Dome missile defence system, and reaffirms Danish sovereignty and Greenlanders' right to self-determination. The vote closes a dispute that began in January, when Trump renewed demands for US control of the autonomous territory and threatened tariffs and military action against allies that opposed him.
