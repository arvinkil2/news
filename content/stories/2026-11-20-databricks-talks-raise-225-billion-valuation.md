Databricks is in early talks to raise several billion dollars in a new funding round that would value the company at about $225 billion, according to a Wall Street Journal report carried by Reuters. The talks are ongoing and the terms could still change.

Databricks said in August that it had raised $5 billion at a $190 billion valuation. The company, founded in 2013, sells software that helps companies store, manage and analyze data and build AI applications, and is widely viewed as a candidate for a future stock market listing.
