Anthropic is spending $3.4 billion on Caterpillar natural gas generators capable of producing up to 1.7 gigawatts of electricity, TechCrunch reported, citing the Financial Times. The generators were originally ordered a couple of years ago by equipment supplier OnePWR, which could not find financing to complete the purchase; Anthropic had planned to buy them from OnePWR and instead went directly to Caterpillar.

The purchase is a break from Anthropic's rent-don't-buy approach: its May S-1 filing disclosed payments of $1.25 billion per month through May 2029 for xAI computing power. Turbine order backlogs now stretch into the early 2030s, and grid connection queues can be just as long.
