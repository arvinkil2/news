The People's Bank of China, the National Financial Regulatory Administration and the China Securities Regulatory Commission met financial institutions on November 2 on the two tools created in October 2024 to support the capital market, and said the swap facility for securities, fund and insurance companies now carries a balance of 105 billion yuan after repeated rollovers, while stock repurchase and shareholding increase relending has signed about 440 billion yuan of loans with more than 280 billion yuan disbursed. The regulators said the repurchase relending policy will change from a phased arrangement into a long term institutional arrangement, with the relending rate cut to 1 percent from 1.25 percent, and that swap facility operations will be carried out in due course with more participating institutions, an appropriately lower swap fee rate and optimised collateral and operating arrangements.
