EU gas storage reached only 75 percent of capacity by October 21 after ending winter 2025-2026 at 28 percent, against 34 percent a year earlier, ACER said, putting the upper bound cost of the 2026 refill at 27 billion euros with the average price of injected gas the second highest in a decade. The agency estimated that the Middle East conflict added 18 to 33 billion euros to Europe's gas import bill for the same quantity of gas, with no significant volumes of Qatari LNG reaching the EU in summer 2026, US LNG at around 30 percent of total EU supply and total Russian gas at around 11 percent.

Spot EU hub prices closed near 90 euros per megawatt hour in mid October, gas fired generation rose by nearly 9 percent over the summer and by 20 percent in August on hot weather and drought, and ACER expects the EU to keep competing with Asia for flexible spot LNG cargoes through the heating season.
