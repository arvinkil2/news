Codelco said on Thursday it had dismissed eight people, including executives, amid its investigation into over-reported copper production in 2024 and 2025, Reuters reported. Those dismissed include managers at the Chuquicamata, Ministro Hales and Salvador divisions, as well as professionals from Codelco's corporate headquarters.

The probe opened in October, when Codelco said an audit of its 2024 and 2025 production had found that output at Ministro Hales and Salvador may have been inflated because the same material was recorded as production by more than one division. The company commissioned EY to run an external audit, referred its findings to Chile's public prosecutor, and had already dismissed one executive over an earlier finding that material still needing further processing was reported as finished copper at Chuquicamata and Ministro Hales.
