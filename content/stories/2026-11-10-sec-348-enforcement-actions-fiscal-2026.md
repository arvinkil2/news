The Securities and Exchange Commission filed 348 enforcement actions in fiscal year 2026, comprising 235 standalone actions and 113 follow-on administrative proceedings, and obtained orders for monetary relief totaling $1.095 billion, made up of $752 million in disgorgement and prejudgment interest and $343 million in civil penalties. The Commission returned approximately $731 million to more than 250,000 harmed investors, and two thirds of the standalone actions included charges against at least one individual. The whistleblower program received approximately 26,000 tips and paid more than $158 million to 65 individual whistleblowers, the highest number of individuals awarded since fiscal year 2023.
