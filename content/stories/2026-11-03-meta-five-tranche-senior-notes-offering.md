**Latest:** Meta priced 15 billion dollars of US dollar notes in five parts on Tuesday, Reuters reported, citing a regulatory filing. The tranches reported in detail were 3.25 billion dollars due in 2031 carrying a 5.85 percent coupon and 3.25 billion dollars due in 2033 at 6.25 percent. Moody's assigned the new debt an Aa3 rating, and Reuters put Meta's total debt at 83.67 billion dollars as of September 30, citing LSEG data. At launch CNBC had reported an expected size of 20 billion to 25 billion dollars split roughly two-thirds in US dollars and one-third in euros, and that S&P Global Ratings on Friday revised Meta's outlook to negative from stable at AA-, citing uncertainty over its heavy capital spending and negative free cash flow. Meta's full-year capital spending guidance stands at 135 billion to 145 billion dollars.

Meta Platforms is offering five series of senior notes, the company said in a preliminary prospectus supplement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Tuesday. The notes will be unsecured obligations of Meta and will rank equally with its other unsecured senior indebtedness. Interest is payable twice a year, beginning in 2027, and the notes will be issued in minimum denominations of 2,000 dollars.

The filing is a launch document, not a priced deal. Aggregate principal amounts, coupons, maturities and offering prices are blank throughout, to be completed at pricing, and the supplement is marked subject to completion, dated November 3, 2026. Meta says it intends to use the net proceeds for general corporate purposes. Citigroup and Morgan Stanley are the joint book-running managers. The notes will not be listed on any exchange and will be delivered in book-entry form through the Depository Trust Company.

Meta joins a crowded issuance calendar. Microsoft priced 25 billion dollars of notes on Monday, Oracle launched twin 10 billion dollar stock and bond offerings the same day, and Alphabet and Amazon have also raised or arranged AI-related funding in the past week, as covered in this publication's reporting on the 131 billion dollar hyperscaler financing wave. The priced terms set the size of the US dollar leg at 15 billion dollars; any euro tranche and the remaining tranche details will be set by the final prospectus supplement.
