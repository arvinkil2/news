Qatar urged Iran on Tuesday to end threats against countries in the region and reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with Ibrahim bin Sultan Al Hashmi, director of the Qatari foreign ministry's media and communication department, telling a weekly briefing in Doha that Tehran had made positive statements but still needed to show goodwill. He said messages and ideas were still being exchanged with both sides in the mediation Qatar is running with Pakistan and other regional partners, and he announced no new proposal and no breakthrough.

The call followed talks in Tehran on Monday where Saudi Arabia's defence minister, Pakistan's army chief and Turkey's intelligence chief met Iranian leaders, and Al Hashmi said Qatar and its Gulf neighbours were ready to join a regional security framework based on respect for sovereignty and a commitment not to attack one another. Euronews put crude flows through the strait at about 60 percent of normal, citing ANZ research.
