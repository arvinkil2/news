MSCI's November 2026 index review removes PT Barito Pacific Tbk (BRPT) from the MSCI Global Standard Indexes and moves PT Bumi Resources Minerals Tbk (BRMS) from the standard index to the MSCI Global Small Cap Indexes, Antara reported, and no Indonesian stock joins the standard index. Seven stocks leave the small cap index: PT Avia Avian Tbk (AVIA), PT Ciputra Development Tbk (CTRA), PT Dayamitra Telekomunikasi Tbk (MTEL), PT Elang Mahkota Teknologi Tbk (EMTK), PT Jasa Marga Tbk (JSMR), PT Rukun Raharja Tbk (RAJA) and PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (WIFI).

All changes take effect after the close of trading on November 30, 2026. Reuters reported the deletions will force funds that track the indexes to sell the shares, and that the MSCI Indonesia index currently has nine firms after the provider removed six companies in May and two more in August.
