South Korea's Kospi closed at 5,067.44 on Wednesday, up 98.96 points or 1.99 percent from the previous trading day, reclaiming the 5,000 level one day after dropping below it, Yonhap reported. The Kosdaq closed at 662.81, up 12.75 points or 1.96 percent.

Yonhap's closing flash gave no account of what drove the rise, and no sector or foreign buying figures accompanied the close.
