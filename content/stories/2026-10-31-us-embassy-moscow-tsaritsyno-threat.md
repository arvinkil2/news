Latest: Russia's Federal Security Service said on Sunday that a Russian minor has been detained over a threat to carry out a terrorist attack in a Moscow park, following the tip-off from the United States, Interfax reported. The FSB said the minor had posted photographs of himself on TikTok wearing tactical clothing and a balaclava and holding an object that looked like an automatic weapon, along with photographs taken at the Tsaritsyno Museum Reserve captioned “I found a place,” and that a search of his home found the clothes and accessories in the posts, while his phone showed a subscription to a neo-Nazi channel on Telegram and a significant number of searches concerning terrorist attacks and mass killings. “We are grateful to the American side for providing the information,” the FSB said in its statement. Russia's Investigative Committee is considering what legal or procedural action to take, and Tsaritsyno, a palace museum and park complex in southern Moscow, was temporarily closed on Saturday following the tip-off.

The US Embassy in Moscow said on Saturday that it is aware of a credible threat of a mass casualty event today, October 31, at the Tsaritsyno Museum Reserve at Dolskaya Ulitsa 1, that the US government is coordinating with Russian authorities on a response, and that Americans should avoid the location today, stay alert and avoid crowds, keep phones charged, carry identification, review their personal security plans, monitor local media and enroll in the State Department's Smart Traveler Enrollment Program. Russia's Federal Security Service said it had received information from the American side through partner channels about preparations for a terrorist attack in Moscow and that relevant work is being carried out, though it did not mention Tsaritsyno, describe the information, or say when or where an attack is planned. The museum reserve told the Telegram channel Mash it had no information about a threat and was operating as usual, and no incident at the site had been reported at check time.
