Brazilian stocks surged on Thursday after the IPCA-15 inflation preview rose 0.22 percent in November, easing from 0.32 percent in October and coming in below the 0.34 percent expected by economists in a Reuters poll, IBGE said. The 12-month rate edged up to 4.64 percent from 4.62 percent, below the 4.78 percent forecast, with food and beverages up 1.51 percent and transportation down 0.71 percent.

The Bovespa closed up 4.30 percent, its strongest day in more than seven weeks, and the real gained 1.7 percent to 5.7235 per dollar, as investors shrugged off Washington's latest tariff threats against Brazilian goods, Reuters reported.
