Sunrise Energy Metals, the Australian miner chaired by Robert Friedland, has approved the final investment decision for its Syerston scandium mine and refinery in central-west New South Wales, and is targeting first commercial production in the second half of 2028. The initial operation is designed to process 64,000 tonnes of ore a year and produce 60 tonnes of high-purity scandium oxide annually over a 32-year life, and early works are already under way, with water pipeline construction started in October. The decision was supported by a conditional commitment for up to 400 million US dollars in long-term debt from the US Department of War’s Office of Strategic Capital, which has still to be converted into drawn financing.
