Spain's transport minister, Óscar Puente, told lawmakers in Congress on Tuesday that he is stepping down, saying a transport minister in Spain cannot remain in office after two accidents of this seriousness in less than a year. The announcement follows the October 28 collision near Urda, south of Madrid, in which a Renfe high speed train struck a stationary Iryo train and at least 69 people were killed, and a second fatal crash in Andalusia in January that killed 46 people. Puente, who had earlier said he would not resign over the latest crash, presented new rail safety measures the same day, and Deutsche Welle reported he did not specify when he will leave the post and will withdraw his name from his party's candidate list for the November 29 election.
