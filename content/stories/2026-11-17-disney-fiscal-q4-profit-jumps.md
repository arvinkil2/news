Walt Disney said fourth-quarter revenue rose 16 percent to $25.9 billion from $22.5 billion a year earlier, while income before income taxes rose 71 percent to $3.5 billion and total segment operating income rose 43 percent to $5.0 billion. Diluted earnings per share rose to $1.51 from $0.73, and adjusted earnings per share rose to $1.77 from $1.11, as the company reported 4 percent more global guests at its Experiences segment and increased engagement across its streaming platforms and ESPN networks.

For the full fiscal year, revenue rose 8 percent to $102.3 billion and adjusted earnings per share rose 18 percent to $7.02, which Disney said exceeded its guidance. The quarter and the year each included a 53rd week of operations, which the company estimated contributed about $0.17 to full-year adjusted earnings per share.
