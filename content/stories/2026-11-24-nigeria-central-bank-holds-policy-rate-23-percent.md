The Central Bank of Nigeria held its monetary policy rate at 23 percent on Tuesday, at the conclusion of the Monetary Policy Committee's 308th meeting in Abuja. The committee also retained the standing facilities corridor at +50 and -300 basis points around the rate, the cash reserve ratio at 45 percent for deposit money banks, 16 percent for merchant banks and 75 percent for non-Treasury Single Account public sector deposits, and the liquidity ratio at 30 percent.

The decision was the expected outcome going into the meeting and follows the committee's 350 basis point cut in September, which took the rate from 26.5 percent to 23 percent. Headline inflation eased to 14.06 percent in October from 14.63 percent in September, according to the National Bureau of Statistics, while the naira weakened in the official market ahead of the meeting.
