Latest: North American third quarter grindings, reported by the National Confectioners Association late on Thursday, fell 2.5 percent from a year earlier to 109,978 tonnes, against expectations of no change. December cocoa closed down 3.19 percent in New York and 3.23 percent in London on Friday, and ICE cocoa inventories rose to a two and a quarter year high of 3,593,964 bags.

European cocoa grindings rose 3.2 percent from a year earlier in the third quarter to 348,215 tonnes, the European Cocoa Association reported, against market expectations for a decline of about 3.9 percent. Asian grindings rose 29 percent to 233,767 tonnes, a record for the quarter and well above expectations of about 15 percent, the Cocoa Association of Asia reported.
