European cocoa grindings rose 3.2 percent from a year earlier in the third quarter to 348,215 tonnes, the European Cocoa Association reported, against market expectations for a decline of about 3.9 percent. Asian grindings rose 29 percent to 233,767 tonnes, a record for the quarter and well above expectations of about 15 percent, the Cocoa Association of Asia reported.

December cocoa settled 1.67 percent higher in New York and 1.04 percent higher in London on Thursday after the figures. North American grindings were due after the New York market close and had not been reported in the sources checked at the time of writing.
