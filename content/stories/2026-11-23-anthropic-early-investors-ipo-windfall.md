Three of Anthropic's biggest pre-IPO backers have described, on the record, how much they committed and why, in interviews with TechCrunch published Sunday. Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC first invested in Anthropic's Series F, announced in September 2025 at a $183 billion valuation, then led the Series G alongside Coatue at a $380 billion valuation and co-led the pre-IPO round at $965 billion. Across the three rounds GIC invested more than $4.5 billion, for a stake of about 1 percent of the company after its IPO, according to a person familiar with GIC's financials. GIC's technology investment head, Chris Emanuel, said Claude Code had just been released when the fund first invested and was already visibly popular with developers.

Baillie Gifford invested a total of $1.5 billion across three rounds after studying the company for nearly two years, citing consistent execution, strong revenue and compelling gross margins. Notable Capital, formerly GGV Capital, took the earliest and riskiest position: roughly 10 percent of the US portion of its fund of more than $1 billion went into the Series E at a $61.5 billion valuation, the highest valuation the predominantly early-stage firm had paid in its 25-year history, weeks after DeepSeek's R1 release. Anthropic's IPO raised $81.3 billion, the largest in history on TechCrunch's account, at a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion, giving Notable a paper return of roughly 15 times. The figures are paper returns, reported by one outlet from investor interviews, and have not been confirmed in a filing.
