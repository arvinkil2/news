Euro area retail trade volume fell 0.7 percent in September 2026 from August and EU volume fell 0.6 percent, according to first estimates from Eurostat, reversing a 0.4 percent rise in both in August. Non-food products except automotive fuel drove the fall, down 1.2 percent in the euro area, while food, drinks and tobacco fell 0.3 percent and automotive fuel in specialised stores rose 1.0 percent. Germany recorded the largest monthly decrease among member states at 2.0 percent, followed by Romania at 1.3 percent, and euro area retail volume was up just 0.4 percent from September 2025.
