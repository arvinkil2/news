Vertiv's board of directors raised the company's regular annual dividend from 0.25 dollars to 0.40 dollars per share, to be declared and paid quarterly, the company said in its Form 8-K. The increase takes effect with the fourth quarter dividend of 0.10 dollars per share of Class A common stock, declared on November 13, 2026 and payable on December 17, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 24, 2026.
