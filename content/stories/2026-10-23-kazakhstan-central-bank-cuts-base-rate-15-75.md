The National Bank of Kazakhstan's Monetary Policy Committee cut the base rate by 50 basis points to 15.75 percent, with a corridor of plus or minus 1 percentage point, the fourth consecutive reduction after cuts in June, July and September took the rate down from 17 percent. The bank said annual inflation fell for a twelfth straight month, to 9.3 percent in September, and that falling inflation and inflation expectations created room to cut while preserving the moderately tight conditions needed to reach its 5 percent target in 2028. It also flagged that price pressure remains elevated and risks are pro-inflationary, said a further change by the end of 2026 is unlikely, and set the next scheduled decision for December 4.
