Somali pirates seized the Iranian fishing vessel Azini between the coastal towns of Bandar Bayla and Hafun in northeastern Somalia on Thursday, a Puntland Maritime Police Force officer told the Associated Press on Sunday. Twelve pirates took the vessel, which was carrying 21 Iranian crew members and two Somali security guards, and the Maritime Security Center Indian Ocean said the crew's safety was unknown and warned in an updated alert that the ship was approaching a commercial shipping lane and could be used as a mother ship to support attacks on commercial vessels, with the European Union's naval Operation Atalanta investigating.

The seizure extends a resurgence across the Gulf of Aden and the western Indian Ocean: by August, pirates had hijacked six commercial vessels since April, and in September Puntland authorities said pirates killed five crew members of the tanker MT Honour 25 before security forces recovered that vessel.
