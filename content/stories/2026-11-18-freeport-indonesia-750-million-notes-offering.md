Freeport Indonesia has agreed to sell $750 million of 6.9 percent senior notes due 2033, Mining Weekly reported. The proceeds are aimed at refinancing the company's existing debt and at general corporate purposes, and Singapore Exchange Securities Trading has given in-principle approval for the notes to be listed on the SGX-ST.

Citigroup Global Markets and J.P. Morgan Securities are the joint global coordinators. Freeport Indonesia, owned 48.7 percent by Freeport-McMoRan and 51 percent by an Indonesian state-owned enterprise, operates the Grasberg minerals district in Central Papua, one of the world's largest copper and gold mines.
