Iran negotiated buying port facilities and oil terminals in China to build a hub for sanctioned Iranian crude, according to leaked confidential correspondence obtained by Iran International and reported by OilPrice.com on Monday. The reporting says Iran bought control of port facilities at three locations in China and stakes in docks in Shandong province, home to China's independent refiners, and secured long-term leases on large storage facilities, with shadow fleet vessels linked to Hossein Shamkhani carrying oil to terminals Iran controls. The claims rest on the leaked letters and unnamed sources at the Iranian oil ministry and have not been independently corroborated.
