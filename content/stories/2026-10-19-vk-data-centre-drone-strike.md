"A drone attack targeted VK's data center in the Leningrad region. VK services are available to users as usual," VK said in a statement on its website, referring to its facility at Novosergievka in the Vsevolozhsk district near St Petersburg. The company did not detail the severity of the damage, but internet monitoring firm NetBlocks noted a 44 percent drop in VK network activity over the weekend. Datacenter Dynamics reported that Sberbank and Yandex facilities have also been struck, and that numerous data centers across Ukraine have been attacked in recent weeks.
