Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission capped the volume of intraday short-sale orders for borrowed securities at 3 percent of a stock's average daily trading volume over the preceding 30 days, reduced from 30 percent previously, according to a statement on Friday reported by Bloomberg. The curbs aim to rein in volatility after the deepening selloff in AI-related shares drove the island's benchmark equity gauge into bear market territory; Taiwan's market closed down 4.5 percent on Friday, for a weekly fall of 12.2 percent.

Separately, the Ministry of Finance said the National Financial Stabilization Fund's committee authorized its executive secretary to deploy funds as needed to stabilize the market. No size for the deployment was stated in the reporting read.
