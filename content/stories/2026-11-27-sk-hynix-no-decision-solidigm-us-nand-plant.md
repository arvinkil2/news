SK hynix has clarified a report that its US subsidiary Solidigm is pursuing an investment of about 60 trillion won to build a new NAND flash memory plant in the United States, saying Solidigm is reviewing various measures to strengthen competitiveness and expand production capacity, but that no matters have been determined as of the filing date. The company said it will disclose further either when specific details are confirmed or within three months. The original report appeared in the Maeil Business Newspaper on October 29, 2026, and SK hynix furnished a first clarification the same day.
