The CFTC on Thursday published a proposed rule that would expressly define event contracts on sports, politics, culture and weather as swaps under the Commodity Exchange Act, asserting exclusive federal jurisdiction over prediction markets like Kalshi and Polymarket, with comments due within 30 days of Federal Register publication. In a companion interim final rule effective immediately on publication, the agency codified that casino-style gambling products, including sportsbook wagers and casino games, are excluded from the swap definition. The move lands amid a federal court split — the Third Circuit held event contracts are swaps in April, but the Ninth and Sixth Circuits went the other way in August and September — with average monthly event-contract volume running about $1.5 billion, roughly $1.2 billion of it in sports.
