North Korea carried out two test-fires on October 31 to verify a cluster warhead for an upgraded tactical ballistic missile system, the Korean Central News Agency said, with Kim Jong Un attending the second launch and further test-fires planned. KCNA said the warhead separated in flight and its submunitions blanketed the target area, claiming a maximum error of 12 metres and a mean of 7 metres, figures no outside source has confirmed.

The claim, if accurate, points to an area-effect warhead for the short-range missiles Pyongyang fields against targets in South Korea. KCNA called the system an important weapons programme and said the next test-fires would again be conducted in Kim Jong Un's presence.
