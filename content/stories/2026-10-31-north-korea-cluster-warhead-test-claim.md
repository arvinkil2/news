North Korea's Missile Administration tested a cluster warhead on October 31, the Korean Central News Agency said, in a test meant to assess the warhead's combat applicability and power for the country's tactical ballistic missiles. KCNA called the warhead a standard type and said it can strike targets over an area of 12 to 13 hectares.

The claim, if accurate, points to an area-effect warhead for the short-range missiles Pyongyang fields against targets in South Korea. The RIA Novosti report of the KCNA statement gave no launch site, missile model or result beyond the stated purpose, and no South Korean or Japanese launch detection was reported alongside it.
