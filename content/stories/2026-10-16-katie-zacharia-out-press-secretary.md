Katie Zacharia is out as White House press secretary after five days in the job, the Guardian and Axios reported on Friday, citing administration officials. Axios said the tenure would be the shortest for a press secretary in modern history.

Her only week was marked by a first press briefing on Tuesday that was widely criticised, and by questions about her legal credentials after she admitted she did not pass the bar exam. No successor was named in the reports checked.
