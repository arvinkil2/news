Energy Vault subsidiaries have entered financing agreements for the approximately 150 MW / 300 MWh Sosa battery energy storage project in Madison County, Texas, part of the company's Asset Vault portfolio. The credit agreement, with First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company as administrative and collateral agent, provides a $78.0 million preferred equity bridge loan facility and an $8.7 million letter of credit facility. Bridge borrowings price at SOFR plus 2.00 percent, mature no later than April 1, 2028, and are sized at 98 percent of the tax equity investor's commitment; $27.2 million was drawn when the facility funded on November 19.

A third party investor has agreed to invest $79.6 million in Sosa Partnership, LLC for Class A membership interests, funded in installments tied to mechanical completion, project completion and tax credit conditions, with the balance due by May 30, 2028. The investor receives a 3.80 percent annual preferred return on funded capital before a distribution flip on the sixth anniversary of the placed in service date, plus tax allocations and tax credit transfer proceeds. If the project is not placed in service by December 31, 2027, either member can trigger a repurchase process that requires payment to the investor of 108 percent of funded contributions plus specified expenses.

Alongside the project financing, OIC Structured Equity Fund I funded approximately $38.3 million of its second tranche commitment to Asset Vault on November 19, and Energy Vault issued OIC warrants over 6,089,009 shares at an exercise price of $4.080375 in connection with that contribution. Asset Vault's members also restated its operating agreement to set the investment, reserve and distribution arrangements for the Sosa project.
