The foreign ministers of Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Egypt, Pakistan, Indonesia, Qatar, the United Arab Emirates, Niger, Palestine, Libya, the Maldives, Turkiye, Kuwait, Algeria, Somalia and Oman issued a joint statement on Thursday welcoming sanctions the United Kingdom announced on Tuesday against eight businesses involved in settlement expansion projects, and sanctions Australia announced on Wednesday against three individuals and four organisations. The ministers also backed the British sanctions against Tzav 9, an Israeli organisation that obstructs humanitarian aid deliveries to Gaza, and expressed support for sanctions on those responsible for settlement activities including the E1 settlement plan. They reiterated their call for Israel to halt settlement activity, condemned the military escalation in Gaza, and called for full implementation of the US plan to end the Gaza conflict.
