Demonstrators marched across France for a sixth straight Saturday, with the Interior Ministry counting 106,000 people nationwide, including 24,000 in Paris, as of about 5:00 p.m. local time, the lowest figure of the current protest wave, while organisers put the turnout at 200,000, including 100,000 in Paris, SVT reported via the TT news agency. The marches raised varied demands centred on the high cost of living, shortcomings in the school system and police brutality, with a proposed bill on police use of weapons in self-defence also a concern. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said about 70,000 police officers were on duty, and no violence had been reported on Saturday afternoon.
