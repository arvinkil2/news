The Government of India will convert or switch its securities through an auction for an aggregate amount of 25,000 crore rupees, face value, on Monday, November 16, 2026, the Reserve Bank of India announced. Holders of six source securities maturing between 2027 and 2029, including 5,000 crore rupees of the 7.38 percent GS 2027 and 2,000 crore of the 8.26 percent GS 2027, can exchange into destination securities maturing between 2034 and 2062, the longest of them the 7.40 percent GS 2062.

Bids are placed on the Reserve Bank's e-Kuber platform between 10:30 and 11:30 a.m. on November 16 in a multiple price auction, with results announced the same day and settlement on November 17. The government reserves the right to accept less than the notified amount.
