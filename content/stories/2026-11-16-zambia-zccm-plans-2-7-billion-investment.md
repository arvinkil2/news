Zambia's state-owned ZCCM Investment Holdings plans to invest $2.7 billion over the next decade to expand its mining, metals trading and power generation businesses, chief executive Kakenenwa Muyangwa told Reuters on Monday. The company expects to borrow about $700 million for the programme, with potential partners contributing up to $1.7 billion and the balance coming from equity funding and internal resources.

The plan includes 3 gigawatts of power generation projects and is tied to Zambia's target of more than tripling annual copper output to 3 million metric tons by 2031, through expansion at current mines, exploration and new mines. ZCCM-IH holds a 20 percent stake in First Quantum's Kansanshi mine, where it receives 3.1 percent of revenue in lieu of dividends, and Muyangwa said the company wants to extend that royalty model to other partners.
