The Office of Strategic Capital announced on Monday a $150 million conditional loan commitment with Rocket Lab USA, the principal operating subsidiary of Rocket Lab Corporation, to support efforts to increase the flight rate of its reusable Neutron rocket beyond first launch. The proposed loan would fund faster vehicle delivery and processing, additional maritime vessels and equipment for Neutron recovery and transport, and manufacturing upgrades for composite structures and engines, aimed at cutting turnaround time between launches.

Neutron is a reusable medium-lift vehicle designed to carry about 13,000 kilograms to low Earth orbit, built for constellation deployment and national security payloads. The commitment is conditional: Rocket Lab must complete further financial, legal, technical, investment and due diligence steps before financial close, and no rate or term was disclosed in the release.
