Japan's Ministry of Finance auctioned its 30 year issue No. 92 on Tuesday, a 4.2 percent coupon bond maturing September 20, 2056 and issued November 11. Competitive bids totaled 1,512.6 billion yen, of which 450.7 billion yen was accepted at a lowest price of 104.55 per 100 yen, a yield of 3.871 percent, with bids at that price filled at 15.1785 percent. The weighted average price was 104.90, a yield of 3.847 percent, and a further 148.8 billion yen was accepted in the non price competitive auction for JGB market special participants.
