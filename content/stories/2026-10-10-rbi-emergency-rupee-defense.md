The Reserve Bank of India unveiled a special dollar facility to meet state-run oil companies' dollar requirements and a new tool, described as a China central bank-style measure, requiring banks to set aside cash with the RBI for some foreign-exchange transactions, a reserve-requirement-style curb on FX speculation. Bloomberg called it the RBI's strongest action since the country's currency crisis in 2013, as the rupee slid toward a record low despite the central bank's multiple defenses.
