AI cloud provider Argentum AI has signed a $630 million agreement with Kyndryl covering 10 megawatts of capacity at Kyndryl's data center in Boulder, Colorado, under a 20 year contract, with deployment already underway. Argentum will use the capacity to deploy Nvidia B300 GPUs at the site, a former IBM facility that offers 15 megawatts in total. Argentum says it has 344 megawatts of contracted power and 13 sites under exclusivity, following a $4.1 billion contract with an unnamed AI company in June 2026 and a $2.5 billion infrastructure agreement in May with Boosteroid and DL Invest Group for a site in Poland.
