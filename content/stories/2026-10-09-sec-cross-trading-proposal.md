The Securities and Exchange Commission on Friday proposed amending Rule 17a-7 to restore the ability of registered funds to cross-trade most fixed-income securities, reversing a restriction imposed by the 2020 fund valuation rule. Chairman Paul S. Atkins said the changes would modernize the rule and recognize market developments that have made pricing more verifiable and transparent, while adding aggregated reporting of cross-trade activity. The proposal will be open for comment for 60 days after publication in the Federal Register.
