Brazil's Senate approved constitutional amendment PEC 221/2019 in two rounds on Wednesday, each by 75 votes to 1, with former vice president Hamilton Mourao the only senator against, ending the 6x1 schedule of six working days for one day of rest and cutting the constitutional maximum week from 44 hours to 40, with no reduction in pay. The transition is phased: two months after promulgation the limit falls to 42 hours with two paid rest days, and 12 months later it reaches 40 hours, one rest day preferably on a Sunday. The vote, on a text the Chamber of Deputies passed in May, hands President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva a popular labour win 11 days before the October 25 runoff against Flavio Bolsonaro, who switched this week to vote in favour after long opposing the change.
