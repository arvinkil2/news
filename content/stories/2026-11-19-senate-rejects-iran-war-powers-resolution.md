Senate Democrats, joined by a group of rebel Republicans, failed to pass a non-binding war powers resolution on the war against Iran, in the first such attempt since the midterm elections. The resolution, pushed by Representative Seth Moulton of Massachusetts and passed by the House in September, would have required the removal of troops engaged in hostilities not authorised by Congress; even if it had passed, it was symbolic, since it was non-binding and would not have gone to President Trump's desk.

The fight now moves to government funding, which must be extended or renewed by December 11. Majority Leader John Thune accused Democrats of preparing to use the funding process to block money for the war, while Senator Tim Kaine said Republicans were the ones willing to shut the government down to maintain what he called an illegal war, and Senator Chris Coons said his caucus was searching for responsible ways to end the war while still funding the Pentagon and restocking munitions.
