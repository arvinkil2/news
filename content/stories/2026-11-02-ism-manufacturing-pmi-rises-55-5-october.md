US manufacturing expanded for a tenth consecutive month in October, with the Institute for Supply Management's Manufacturing PMI at 55.5, up one percentage point from 54.5 in September and one point above the consensus forecast reported by Reuters. The Production Index rose 2.3 points to 59.0, its highest reading since November 2021, new orders edged up to 55.5, and employment eased 0.3 point to 52.4 while remaining in expansion for a fourth month. The Prices Index fell 2.7 points to 75.2, still an elevated level, and ISM said general sentiment among respondents worsened for a third month, with 66 percent of comments negative and pricing volatility the most cited concern.
