Apple reported revenue of 114.5 billion dollars for its fiscal fourth quarter ended September 26, 2026, up 12 percent from 102.5 billion dollars a year earlier, with net income of 30.7 billion dollars against 27.5 billion dollars and diluted earnings per share of 2.10 dollars, up 14 percent. iPhone revenue was 57.6 billion dollars, a September quarter record, Services revenue was 31.6 billion dollars, an all-time high, Mac revenue was 10.8 billion dollars, iPad revenue was 6.2 billion dollars and Wearables, Home and Accessories revenue was 8.3 billion dollars.

Gross margin was 47.9 percent, including a favourable impact of about one percentage point from tariff refunds, which also added 0.05 dollars to diluted earnings per share. Full year revenue reached a record 478.8 billion dollars, with full year net income of 132.2 billion dollars and diluted earnings per share of 8.98 dollars. Apple declared a dividend of 0.27 dollars per share, payable November 16 to shareholders of record on November 12, and gave no forward revenue guidance in the release.
