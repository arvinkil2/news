President Volodymyr Zelenskyy told journalists on Saturday that Russia is seeking to place ballistic missile launchers on Belarusian territory for attacks on western Ukraine, in addition to the relay stations that amplify signals for Russian drones and that Ukraine's border service said in September were already supporting strikes on its western regions. He called such a deployment a direct drawing of Belarus into the war and said Ukraine is strengthening air defence in the regions facing the most attacks.
