Panamanian authorities have confirmed the first death from the October 9 earthquake, a 68 year old woman, Elena Barria, who was gravely injured when a wall of her home in Macaracas, Los Santos province, collapsed, President Jose Raul Mulino said, adding that her injuries, aggravated by a preexisting condition, proved fatal despite medical efforts. The official update put the number of displaced people held in 45 shelters up by 855 from the previous day, with more than 600 public schools affected and home reconstruction already begun in Veraguas, Cocle and Chiriqui. Classes are to resume progressively, school by school, as technical inspections clear each building.
