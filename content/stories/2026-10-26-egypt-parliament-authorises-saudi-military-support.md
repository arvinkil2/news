Egypt's parliament has authorised President Abdel Fattah Al-Sisi to send military reinforcements to Saudi Arabia if requested by the kingdom, Daily News Egypt reported. The mandate, approved after secret sessions on October 18, 19 and 26 convened at the president's request, allows him to take the measures needed to defend Saudi territory, sovereignty and holy sites against any external aggression. It follows an October 18 meeting of Egypt's National Defence Council, chaired by Al-Sisi, at which Egypt pledged all necessary and possible support for the kingdom's security, stability and territorial integrity amid escalating attacks.
