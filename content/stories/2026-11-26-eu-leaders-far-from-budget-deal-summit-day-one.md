EU leaders closed the first day of the extraordinary Brussels summit on Thursday with no agreement on the 2028 to 2034 long-term budget or on financing for Ukraine, and Council President Antonio Costa's compromise of 1.78 trillion euros, about 204 billion euros below the Commission's initial proposal, did not bridge the gaps. Chancellor Friedrich Merz said the budget was still too high and that Europe must not take on more debt, Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said leaders were light-years away from a solution, and Hungary's Peter Magyar put the chance of a deal on Thursday at virtually zero, with Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Hungary and Slovakia opposing funds for military aid to Ukraine. The summit continues on Friday, and Costa aims for agreement at a further summit on December 17 to 18.
