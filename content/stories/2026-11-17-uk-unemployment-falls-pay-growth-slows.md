The UK unemployment rate fell to 4.6 percent in July to September 2026, down 0.3 percentage points on both the quarter and the year, while the employment rate rose 0.3 points on the quarter to 75.4 percent, the Office for National Statistics said on Tuesday. Annual growth in regular pay slowed to 3.5 percent from 3.7 percent, and total pay growth eased to 3.6 percent. The timelier measures were weaker: the early estimate of payrolled employees fell by 34,000 in October to 30.2 million, and vacancies fell by 22,000 to 690,000 in August to October, the lowest level outside the pandemic since 2014, with the ONS noting that smaller firms report labour and operating costs are affecting their hiring decisions.
