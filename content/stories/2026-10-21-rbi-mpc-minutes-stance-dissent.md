The Reserve Bank of India voted unanimously to raise the policy repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50 percent at its October 5 to 7 meeting and to change its stance to calibrated tightening, the minutes published on Wednesday showed, with the standing deposit facility rate at 5.25 percent and the marginal standing facility and Bank Rate at 5.75 percent. Two members, Nagesh Kumar and Ram Singh, voted for the hike but against the stance change, arguing it could signal an extended cycle of increases when inflation is mainly supply driven; the other four members backed the change, which the committee said means rate cuts are off the table in the near term and the next move can only be a hike or a pause.

The minutes kept the projections set at the meeting: real GDP growth of 7.1 percent for 2026-27 after 7.8 percent in the first quarter, and CPI inflation of 5.2 percent for the year, rising to 6.0 percent in the third quarter, with core inflation projected at 4.4 percent. The next meeting is scheduled for December 2 to 4, 2026.
