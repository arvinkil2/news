A group led by Nelson Peltz's Trian Fund Management is offering $7.35 a share in cash to take Wendy's private, a deal valued at about $1.4 billion and a premium of roughly 20 percent, the Wall Street Journal reported on October 28, 2026, and Wendy's shares rose about 15 percent on the report. Trian, Wendy's largest shareholder bloc with Peltz and Trian together holding about a quarter of the stock in earlier regulatory filings, explored a consortium take private in August 2026 and later said it had no current plans to make an offer, Reuters reported at the time. Wendy's has not yet responded publicly to the reported offer.
