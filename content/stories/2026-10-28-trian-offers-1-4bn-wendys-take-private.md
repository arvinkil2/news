**Latest, October 29:** Wendy's has confirmed that it received an unsolicited, non-binding proposal on October 28 from Trian Fund Management, Flynn Group LP, BlueFive Capital and Sixth Street Partners to acquire all of the company's shares the investor group does not already own for $7.35 a share in cash. Trian disclosed the proposal the same day in Schedule 13D/A Amendment No. 65; the Trian filing persons beneficially owned about 16.25 percent of the common stock as of October 27. The price is an approximate 20 percent premium to the October 27 close of $6.14, and the letter states the proposal would not require any new debt and would not be conditioned on external financing.

Wendy's said a committee of independent directors not affiliated with the investor group, formed after Trian's February 2026 Schedule 13D/A, will evaluate the proposal with its financial and legal advisors, and cautioned there is no assurance the company will pursue the transaction or any other strategic outcome. The committee is advised by Morgan Stanley, according to reporting of the company's statement. Shareholders are not required to take any action. At $7.35 a share on about 194 million fully diluted shares, the equity value of the whole company is about $1.4 billion.

Opposition arrived the same afternoon. Blackwells Capital, a Wendy's shareholder, issued a statement through GlobeNewswire calling the proposal a dramatic undervaluation and "an offer by the Company's longest-tenured fiduciary to purchase, at the bottom, the consequences of his own disastrous tenure," noting that Nelson Peltz has been a director of Wendy's and its predecessors since 1993 and chaired its board for seventeen years. Blackwells said it will pursue every lawful avenue to ensure the proposal does not succeed as proposed.

**Original report, October 28:** A group led by Nelson Peltz's Trian Fund Management is offering $7.35 a share in cash to take Wendy's private, a deal valued at about $1.4 billion and a premium of roughly 20 percent, the Wall Street Journal reported on October 28, 2026, and Wendy's shares rose about 15 percent on the report. Trian explored a consortium take private in August 2026 and later said it had no current plans to make an offer, Reuters reported at the time.
