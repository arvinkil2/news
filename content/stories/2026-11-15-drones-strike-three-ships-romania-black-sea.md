Three commercial vessels were struck by drones near Romania's Black Sea coast over two nights, on November 13 and 14, Romanian authorities confirmed. No casualties were reported in any of the strikes.

The first vessel hit was the Palau-flagged cargo ship Larissa 1, struck at about 9:40 p.m. local time on November 13 roughly 12 kilometres off the coast near Sfantu Gheorghe, according to Romanian broadcaster Digi24. The ship was heading for the Romanian port of Sulina, with Ukraine as its reported final destination. The drone damaged a cargo crane aboard the vessel, and the captain reported that no crew members were injured and that the impact caused no fire, according to Romanian outlet HotNews.

Two more vessels were struck the following evening, Romania's Defence Ministry confirmed, according to Romanian news agency News.ro. The Greek-flagged tanker Evia Petrol V was struck at about 9:10 p.m. local time, roughly four nautical miles east-southeast of Sulina. About 35 minutes later the bulk carrier Marsol was struck near the Sulina Canal. Both vessels caught fire and their crews extinguished the flames.

Speaking at the military port of Constanta on November 14, Defence Minister Stefan-Radu Oprea confirmed that the authorities had been notified of the first incident and that an investigation was underway. The Defence Ministry also reported detecting an aerial target east of Snake Island, which prompted the deployment of two F-16 fighter jets, and drone alerts were issued to residents of Tulcea County. Anadolu Agency, reporting the ministry's statement, said a red alert was declared in the Tulcea region and that the ministry described the Black Sea as a danger zone.

Romanian authorities have not identified the origin of the drones responsible for any of the three strikes. The incidents follow a series of drone attacks on commercial shipping in the Black Sea, including a strike on a vessel heading toward Constanta in Bulgarian territorial waters on October 28 and an attack near Constanta on October 23 that left one sailor dead and three missing. Romania is a NATO member and shares a Danube border with Ukraine.
