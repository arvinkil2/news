Japanese government bonds slumped on Friday, tracking Treasuries, which recorded a second straight session of losses as traders increased wagers on aggressive Federal Reserve interest rate hikes. The benchmark 10 year JGB yield rose 8 basis points to 2.87 percent, while the 30 year yield climbed 7 basis points to 3.99 percent.

Expectations for further Bank of Japan tightening have also rebounded, with overnight index swaps implying about an 87 percent chance of a rate increase by December, up from a low of around 50 percent last week when bonds rallied.
