President Lee Jae Myung oversaw the test-firing of a hypersonic glide vehicle on Thursday, South Korea's presidential office said, the South's answer to the intermediate-range hypersonic missile test Kim Jong Un supervised in the North days earlier. Seoul describes the weapon as a "strategic asset" able to strike with little warning on an unpredictable flight path. After the successful launch, Lee told researchers at the state-run defense institute to move quickly to deploy the missile.

The test lands in a week already tense over the two countries' rival arsenals and the diplomatic row over North Korean prisoners of war transferred from Ukraine to Seoul.
