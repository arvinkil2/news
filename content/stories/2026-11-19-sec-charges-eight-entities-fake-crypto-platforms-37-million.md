The SEC said the defendants lured investors through social media advertisements into group and private chats, where people posing as an investment adviser first advised on US stock trades and then steered victims to send as much money as possible to a supposed crypto trading platform that bought nothing real. The scheme ran in four adviser and platform pairs from August 2024 through May 2026, with the operators abandoning one pair for the next every few months, and used fake SEC certificates and filings with the SEC, FINRA and FinCEN to answer sceptical investors. The complaint, filed in the District of Colorado against Advisor WeChat Capital, Tech Brokerage Asset Alliance Ltd., HB Wealth Advisors, IdeaCrypto Exchange LLC, Convergent Wealth Advisors Inc., RealSimple Crypto Exchange Inc., Associated Wealth Advisors Ltd. and Essential Crypto Exchange, charges violations of Exchange Act Section 10(b) and Rule 10b-5 and, for some defendants, the Advisers Act, and seeks permanent injunctions, disgorgement, prejudgment interest and civil penalties.
