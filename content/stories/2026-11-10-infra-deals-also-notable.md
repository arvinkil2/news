Demea Sustainable Investment and Enovos Green Power have acquired shares in Zonnepark Lanakerveld, a project near Maastricht in the Netherlands comprising about 45 MW of solar capacity and a 25 MW, 100 MWh battery storage system. Demea, investing through its Climate Infrastructure Fund, holds a majority stake in its first Dutch infrastructure investment. The two are investing through equity and quasi-equity, with ING Bank providing debt; the transaction value was not disclosed. Construction began in September 2026 and commissioning is expected in summer 2027, with revenue supported by the Dutch SDE plus plus support scheme. Mercom Capital reported the acquisition on November 10; it is single-sourced to Mercom.

FluoRok, the University of Oxford spinout developing a fluorochemical manufacturing process that avoids hydrogen fluoride, has raised 30 million dollars in an oversubscribed Series A led by Orano Venture Fund and Supernova Invest, with Bidra Innovation Ventures and existing investors including Oxford Science Enterprises, BGF and Volta Energy Technologies participating. The money funds demonstration facilities for chemical reagents and lithium hexafluorophosphate, the battery electrolyte salt for which China produces the large majority of global supply. The round was announced on November 5 and takes FluoRok's total equity raised to about 44 million dollars, on top of more than 13 million dollars of UK and European public funding.

Italy's Open Fiber, the wholesale fibre network owned by Cassa Depositi e Prestiti and Macquarie, is reported to have begun planning an 8 billion euro refinancing. Bloomberg reported the planning on November 10; no terms have been announced and the item is single-sourced to Bloomberg.

Banks are preparing 1.8 billion pounds of debt financing to back TDR Capital's acquisition of OCU Group, Bloomberg reported on November 10. The financing supports the sale agreed on October 5, under which Triton Partners sells the UK energy and infrastructure services group to TDR funds; the sale price was not disclosed, though people familiar with the matter put the value at roughly 2 billion pounds at announcement. The financing figure is single-sourced to Bloomberg.

China Water Environment is seeking a restructuring of a 410 million dollar loan, Bloomberg reported on November 10. No terms were reported, and the item is single-sourced to Bloomberg.

Indonesia's Telkom is streamlining its subsidiaries through two internal mergers, Antara News reported on November 10, the latest step in the state controlled telecom group's multi year simplification of its corporate structure. The item is single-sourced to Antara, in Indonesian.
