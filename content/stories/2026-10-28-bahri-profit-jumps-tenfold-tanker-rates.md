Bahri, officially the National Shipping Company of Saudi Arabia, reported third quarter net income of 5.1 billion riyals (1.4 billion dollars), up from 513 million riyals a year earlier, in a stock exchange statement on Wednesday reported by Bloomberg. Sales at its oil unit increased by roughly ten times on higher tanker rates and chartering levels during the regional war that followed the US and Israeli attacks on Iran at the end of February.

Chief executive Ahmed Al Subaey said several Bahri vessels were attacked during the period and two of the company's mariners were killed, and called the operating environment exceptionally complex. Bloomberg reported Bahri has helped state producer Aramco raise crude exports through Hormuz, including ship to ship transfers outside the strait, after Aramco's East-West pipeline was attacked and temporarily put out of service in September.
