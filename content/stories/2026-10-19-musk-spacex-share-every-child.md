Elon Musk said on Monday that he will give every child in the United States one of his shares in SpaceX next year, writing on X that this will be the first of many, and he gave no details on how the giveaway would happen. CNBC put the scale at about 10.5 billion dollars, based on 72 million children under 18 in the United States as of July 1, 2025 and SpaceX's closing price of 146.03 dollars on Friday, and reported that Musk owns about 4.8 billion SpaceX shares, or 6.4 billion including unvested awards and options, a 48.4 percent beneficial stake per an August SEC filing. The pledge followed Anthropic's plan to commit 5 percent of its shares to a public benefit fund, to which Musk replied that he recommends giving the shares directly to the public, and CNBC said SpaceX had not responded to a request for comment.
