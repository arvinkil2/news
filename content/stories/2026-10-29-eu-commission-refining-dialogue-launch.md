The European Commission launched its first High-Level Dialogue with the European refining industry on October 28, to discuss energy security, defence readiness and industrial competitiveness, with representatives of refiners, emerging fuel producers, engineering and technology companies, industry associations and NATO taking part. The discussion covered the short-term response to the disruptions caused by the conflict in the Middle East and the medium-term outlook for European refineries, including high energy costs, global competition and investment in biofuels and e-fuels.

Energy Commissioner Dan Jørgensen said the Commission will work to increase output from refining assets in Europe to bring fuel prices down, and will work with the sector on a joint roadmap for its transformation. The dialogue was announced by President von der Leyen on October 6, and the Commission said its outcomes will feed into shared priorities and possible actions to support the sector.
