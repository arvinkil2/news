Canadian retail sales increased 0.8 percent to 75.3 billion dollars in September, Statistics Canada reported, with sales up in five of nine subsectors, led by gasoline stations and fuel vendors, where sales rose 6.9 percent for a second monthly gain. Core retail sales, excluding gasoline and motor vehicles, edged up only 0.1 percent, and in volume terms total sales also rose just 0.1 percent, while third quarter sales were up 1.2 percent.

The agency's advance estimate for October points the other way: it suggests retail sales decreased 1.5 percent in October, an unofficial figure calculated from early survey responses that Statistics Canada says will be revised. The October data are scheduled for release on December 18.
