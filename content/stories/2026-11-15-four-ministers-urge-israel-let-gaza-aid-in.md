The foreign ministers of Australia, Canada, Italy and the United Kingdom said on Sunday that the humanitarian situation in Gaza is dire as winter takes hold, that recent floods have brought further suffering in only the first storm of the winter, and that 150,000 tents and other vital supplies are being held at the border and prevented from entering Gaza. They urged the government of Israel to stop the delays and allow immediate, rapid and unimpeded humanitarian access throughout Gaza, with crossings opened, the tents allowed in, and dual-use items necessary for humanitarian operations and early recovery granted entry.

The four ministers also called on all parties to implement the Comprehensive Plan for Gaza, respecting the ceasefire and their obligations under international humanitarian law.
