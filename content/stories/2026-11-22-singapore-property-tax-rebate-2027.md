Singapore’s Ministry of Finance and the Inland Revenue Authority said on Monday that the government will provide a one-off property tax rebate for all owner-occupied residential properties in 2027, to cushion payments amid cost-of-living concerns. The rebate is 15 percent for owner-occupied HDB flats and 10 percent, capped at $100, for owner-occupied private residential properties, and it will be offset automatically against the 2027 bill.

After the rebate, owner-occupiers in three-room and larger HDB flats will see an average increase of about $2 to $4 per month, and most owner-occupiers in private homes will see an average increase of about $6 per month, while one- and two-room HDB owner-occupiers continue to pay no property tax. The 2027 bills will be issued by December 2026 and are due by January 31, 2027.
