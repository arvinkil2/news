UK house prices fell 0.9 percent in October on the Halifax index, the steepest monthly fall since July 2023, after a 0.1 percent rise in September. Annual growth slowed to 0.3 percent, the weakest since May 2024, and the average price dropped to 299,395 pounds from 301,780 pounds in September.

Halifax said weakening demand drove the slowdown, as rising swap rates pushed mortgage costs up: the average two-year fixed rate reached 4.35 percent at the start of November, from 4.19 percent at the start of October, after markets scaled back expectations for Bank of England cuts. Nationwide's separate index pointed the other way for the month, with prices up 0.3 percent in October and 2.4 percent over the year, and both lenders described affordability as stretched and activity as subdued.
