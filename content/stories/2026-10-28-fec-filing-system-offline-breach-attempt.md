The Federal Election Commission's electronic filing system went down at about 10 p.m. ET on Monday, and an FEC electronic filings officer told campaign compliance firms that night that a bad actor was attempting to breach FEC systems and that the commission had had to temporarily shut down its web-facing services, Reuters reported. A status update at 8:17 p.m. ET on Tuesday said the issue had been identified and a fix was being implemented, and previously filed reports remained searchable throughout.

The outage blocks the 24 and 48 hour reports that committees and outside groups must file on late spending. Super PACs and dark money groups have spent an average of about 50 million dollars a day over the past week, according to Brendan Glavin of OpenSecrets, cited by Reuters. FEC Commissioner Dara Lindenbaum said the incident has nothing to do with voter files or voter registration, which states maintain, and the FBI and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency referred questions to the FEC.
