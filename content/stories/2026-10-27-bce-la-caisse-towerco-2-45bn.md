BCE said its subsidiary Bell Canada has signed a definitive agreement under which La Caisse will acquire a 49.9 percent stake in TowerCo, a newly formed company that will own and operate Bell's wireless tower portfolio of more than 5,000 sites, for CAD 2.45 billion, valuing TowerCo at about CAD 4.91 billion. Bell will keep a 50.1 percent controlling interest, TowerCo will lease capacity on the towers back to Bell and is expected to carry no financial debt at closing, and Bell retains full ownership of its spectrum, active network equipment and owned land. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals.
