**Latest (Tuesday, October 13, night):** Zelensky said Ukraine received no early warning from its partners before a ballistic missile strike on Kyiv in which five missiles hit civilian infrastructure in the capital, and that advance intelligence on ballistic launches is one of the most critical forms of help partners provide. The strike was part of a wave of more than 100 weapons launched since Monday evening, he said: a residential building in Kharkiv was destroyed, with four people killed and 41 injured in that city in one evening, a drone strike on an educational institution in Rivne wounded 14 people according to the regional administration, and one person was killed in the Fastiv district of the Kyiv region. Zelensky said Ukraine had not struck Russian oil refineries or other energy sites in the two days since Trump announced the ceasefire, while Russia had kept hitting Ukrainian energy and critical infrastructure across 19 regions. Russia's Defence Ministry said its overnight strikes targeted Ukrainian military-industrial facilities, logistics centres and railway and port infrastructure. These are each side's accounts, reported by IBTimes Singapore from Zelensky's statement and official claims, and they have not been independently corroborated.

Zelensky said Ukrainian and American teams are in contact, that Kyiv has sent envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner its proposals for the next meetings, and that Ukraine is ready to meet in the coming days. Trump said on October 11 that an energy truce between Ukraine and Russia had begun with both sides agreeing, but Kyiv officially denied that any formal agreement existed and asked Washington for details. NV reported that after that announcement Russian forces struck the CHP-5 plant in Kharkiv and the settlement of Pesochyn, killing three people and injuring more than twenty.
