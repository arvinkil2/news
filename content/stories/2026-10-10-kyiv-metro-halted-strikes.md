Kyiv temporarily stopped train and escalator service across all three metro lines Saturday after losing voltage from external power centers, the Kyiv city administration said. Officials blamed damage to an energy facility in the Kyiv region, and the capital also reported electricity and water supply disruptions.

Electric transport was suspended in the Holosiivskyi district and parts of the Solomianskyi, Shevchenkivskyi and Pecherskyi districts. In Solomianskyi, drone debris fell on a 25-story residential building and an 8-story office building, according to city authorities. Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko confirmed the drone strikes, and UNIAN reported all three metro branches shut.
