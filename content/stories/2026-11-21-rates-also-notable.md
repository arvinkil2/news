German producer prices of industrial products were 8.1 percent higher in October 2026 than a year earlier, the largest year over year increase since February 2023, and rose 1.4 percent on the month, Destatis reported. Energy prices were up 18.7 percent year over year, with mineral oil products up 51.9 percent, naphtha up 76.7 percent and heating oil up 97.2 percent, moves Destatis attributes to the conflict in Iran and the Middle East. Excluding energy, producer prices rose 3.6 percent year over year. Intermediate goods rose 6.8 percent, with copper and semi finished copper products up 29.4 percent, while non durable consumer goods fell 1.3 percent and food prices dropped 3.1 percent.

US Treasury yields fell across the curve in the latest market tables: the 5 year yield dropped 16.5 basis points to 4.70 percent, the 10 year fell 14.0 basis points to 4.91 percent and the 30 year fell 9.8 basis points to 5.30 percent, the bond side of the same risk off session that hit equities and metals.
