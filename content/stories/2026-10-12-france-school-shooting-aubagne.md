Police detained a 14-year-old student suspected of shooting his principal and a teacher at Joseph Lakanal Middle School in Aubagne on Monday with a hunting shotgun he had hidden in a guitar case, according to Marseille Public Prosecutor Nicolas Bessone. The pupil fired on the first floor, wounding both in the legs, then fired again on the second floor, and a third person was grazed before a staff member disarmed him and police took him into custody, officials said.

Neither the principal nor the teacher was in critical condition. Education Minister Edouard Geffray condemned the attack on X, and the prime minister’s office said the interior and education ministers were travelling to the scene.
