Lucara Diamond raised its full year 2026 revenue guidance to $140 million to $160 million, from $100 million to $130 million, after selling the 2,488 carat Motswedi diamond and a 1,303 carat stone recovered from its Karowe mine in Botswana after June 30. Third quarter revenue rose 19 percent year on year to $61.1 million on sales volumes up 12 percent to 113,227 carats, although net profit slipped 11 percent to $6.6 million. Sales of stones above 10.8 carats to HB Trading rose 28 percent to $48.5 million and made up 79 percent of quarterly revenue, while prices for smaller commercial diamonds fell under pressure from weak demand and lab grown supply.
