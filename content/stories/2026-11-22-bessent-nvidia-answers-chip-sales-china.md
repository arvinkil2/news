Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that the Trump administration still needs information from Nvidia about its chip sales to China, and that it intends to establish who led compliance at the company and at what level the business knew about the matter, from the lower levels to the top. He said the administration has been in touch with Nvidia's temporary management, lead directors and lawyers, and that Nvidia's board is working to determine what happened.

The inquiry follows the resignation of former chief executive Jensen Huang and comes days after the administration issued a rule further restricting China's access to advanced AI chips from companies like Nvidia. Asked about President Trump's comment on Friday that he would like to see Huang apologize and return to Nvidia, Bessent said more information is needed first; Nvidia board chairman Harvey Jones has said the board will not rehire Huang.
