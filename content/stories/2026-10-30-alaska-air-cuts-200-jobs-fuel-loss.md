Alaska Air Group said on Friday it would eliminate about 200 non-contract positions, including 125 in Washington state and 48 in Hawaii, with no frontline, non-management jobs cut; affected employees work until November 13 and stay on pay through December 31 with severance and other support. In a memo to employees seen by Reuters, chief executive Ben Minicucci said the company could lose as much as 700 million dollars this year if current conditions persist, which would be its first adjusted pretax loss outside the pandemic since 2003, and he pointed to soaring fuel prices and geopolitical challenges alongside overhead costs that have grown faster than revenue. The cuts land while Alaska pursues an expansion into premium and international travel, where strong revenue growth has not been enough to shield earnings from the fuel bill.
