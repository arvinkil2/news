esVolta, the Texas battery storage developer, has secured $181.2 million across two financing transactions for its City Breeze and Boxcar projects, including a $126.1 million project financing facility with MUFG Bank for City Breeze in Bay City, Texas. The company has now secured more than $1 billion in funding in total. Mercom Capital Group reported the transactions in its November 25 project finance brief.

Alantra Solar has secured a 90 million euro (about $99 million) holding company debt facility from Eiffel Investment Group for N-Sun Energy, its platform with Solarig. The proceeds will complete projects under construction and fund the acquisition and construction of about 150 MW of additional solar capacity across central and southern Italy, according to the same Mercom brief.

ZE Energy has acquired the 59 MW, 472 MWh Magliano Alpi battery storage project in Italy's Piedmont region from ReFeel New Energy, its third large-scale standalone battery project in Italy, Mercom also reported.
