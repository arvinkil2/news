Rio Tinto said it will invest $1.9 billion to develop the Kangwinan bauxite project on the Western Cape York Peninsula in Queensland, replacing production from its Andoom mine near Weipa as that mine reaches the end of its life this decade. The project adds 20 million tonnes of annual bauxite production at Weipa, taking the complex to about 44 million tonnes a year from around 38 million currently, and it also helps offset the planned 2029 closure of Rio's Gove bauxite mine in the Northern Territory.

The announcement follows Rio's agreement last month to acquire the adjacent Aurukun Bauxite Project from Glencore and Mitsubishi, subject to regulatory approvals. Rio said the combined Amrun, Kangwinan and Aurukun operations are expected to support its Weipa bauxite business, which supplies ore for alumina and aluminium, well beyond the middle of the century.
