Radio Svoboda published a satellite image dated November 15, credited to Planet Labs, showing at least five petroleum product tanks completely burned out at the port of Ust-Luga, with firefighting foam residue visible beside others, Ukrainska Pravda and UNIAN reported. UNIAN said tanks were hit at the Novatek-Ust-Luga terminal, whose reservoirs hold 30,000 tons each, and at the Ust-Luga Oil terminal, with fires also recorded at the Factor terminal and Luzhskaya railway station. Leningrad Oblast governor Alexander Drozdenko said 42 drones were shot down on the night of November 11 and confirmed a hit and a fire in the port zone, but no Russian account confirms the five-tank count.
