A mudslide struck Codelco's El Teniente division near ADIT 42 in the El Rajo sector on Sunday, trapping two workers in a vehicle; a third worker escaped and was taken to a health center, the company said. Rescue teams have located the trapped workers but have not disclosed their condition, and the cause of the slide is unspecified, though the company noted unusual spring rains tied to El Nino across central Chile this month. El Teniente is the world's largest underground copper mine with 4,500 kilometers of tunnels, and the accident follows a July 2025 collapse that killed six workers and a February raid on Codelco offices over related safety investigations.
