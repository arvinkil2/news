SK Hynix plans to partner with the South Korean government on a semiconductor testing facility named Trinity Fab, a $654 million project housed at the semiconductor mega-cluster in Yongin, south of Seoul, and scheduled to open in May 2027, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The facility will include a 3,305 square metre cleanroom with lithography, etching, deposition and inspection tools, run by an independent non-profit foundation to protect suppliers' intellectual property.

The fab will let small semiconductor suppliers test materials and equipment on 12-inch wafers under conditions mimicking commercial production lines before deployment, which SK Hynix said should cut testing costs and shorten time to market. The company also plans to invest $1.05 billion over the next five years to support South Korean semiconductor materials, parts and equipment suppliers.
