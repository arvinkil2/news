China's Shenzhou-24 spacecraft lifted off aboard a Long March 2F rocket from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center at 8:18 a.m. Beijing time on Wednesday and entered orbit, carrying commander Tang Hongbo, flight engineer Wu Yi and Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to the Tiangong space station. Ali is the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong and the first Pakistani to reach orbit, and the mission is the first joint flight of Chinese and foreign astronauts in China's crewed space programme, the China Manned Space Agency said.

Ali, selected under a China-Pakistan cooperation agreement, will spend about a week aboard the station working on joint experiments, including four China-Pakistan projects in biotechnology and materials, before returning to Earth aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft with two of its crew. Tang, flying to Tiangong for the third time, and Wu, on his first spaceflight, begin a longer station rotation, and more than 90 scientific experiments and technology tests are planned for the mission.
