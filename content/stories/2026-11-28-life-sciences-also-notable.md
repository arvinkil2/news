Lupin dalbavancin approval. Lupin said the FDA approved its new drug application for dalbavancin injection, 500 mg in 5 mL single dose vials, through the 505(b)(2) pathway, for single dose treatment of acute bacterial skin and skin structure infections. The ready to dilute formulation contains the same active ingredient as Dalvance. The approval was reported on November 25 and 26 in Indian and trade coverage of the company's announcement.

UK medicines export list updated. The UK government updated its guidance on medicines that cannot be parallel exported from the UK or hoarded, the standing list used to protect domestic supply of medicines in shortage. The update, published November 27, is a routine revision of the list rather than a new export ban.

Capricor and the STAT newsletter. STAT's November 27 newsletter recapped the FDA's second complete response letter for Capricor's deramiocel in Duchenne muscular dystrophy, announced November 20 and covered in full on this site that day. No new regulatory step was reported, and Capricor has not yet announced its next move.

Clinical trial registrations. New ClinicalTrials.gov registrations in the pool included a single cell sequencing study of immunotherapy response predictors in non-small cell lung cancer (NCT07963540) and a master protocol evaluating therapeutic agents in myeloproliferative neoplasms (NCT07962045). Both are early stage study starts, not readouts.
