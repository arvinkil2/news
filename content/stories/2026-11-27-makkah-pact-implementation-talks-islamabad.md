Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkiye opened a two-day committee meeting in Islamabad on Friday, November 27, on how to implement the Makkah defence pact they signed on August 7, which commits each to treat an armed attack on one as an attack on all. A Pakistani Foreign Office official confirmed to Arab News that the committee meetings had started, without giving the agenda; Pakistan's defence minister Khawaja Asif had told AFP that the conflict between Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthis would be among the issues discussed.

Turkiye completed ratification of the pact the same day, with President Recep Tayyip Erdogan's formal approval recorded in the Official Gazette. The three countries agreed at their first committee meeting in Istanbul on August 31 to set up a permanent secretariat in Riyadh and to pursue joint defence technology development, and at emergency talks in Riyadh on October 5 they confirmed retired Pakistani Lt. Gen. Nauman Mahmood as the alliance's first secretary-general.
