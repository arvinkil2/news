HP Inc. said on Wednesday that its board unanimously appointed Emma Walmsley as chief executive officer, effective January 4, 2027. Walmsley ran the drugmaker GSK from April 2017 through December 2025, leading its separation of the consumer healthcare business, and has sat on Microsoft's board for seven years; she succeeds Bruce Broussard, who has served as interim chief executive since February 2026, when Enrique Lores departed, and who will become chair of the board on the same date, succeeding Chip Bergh.

Walmsley takes over a PC and print business working through a memory chip crunch that has driven up prices across the industry, in Reuters' framing of the announcement, and said she would focus on the AI era and new sources of growth. She has no prior record running a technology hardware company.
