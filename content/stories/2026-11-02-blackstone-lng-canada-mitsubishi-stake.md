An investor group led by Blackstone Inc. has agreed to acquire a stake in LNG Canada's liquefaction business from a subsidiary of Mitsubishi Corporation for $3.1 billion, Bloomberg reported, citing a statement. The transaction covers 49.9 percent of the LNG Canada business owned by the Mitsubishi unit, and the consortium alongside Blackstone is Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, Ontario Municipal Employees Retirement System, Investment Management Corporation of Ontario and Public Sector Pension Investment Board.

Mitsubishi holds 15 percent of LNG Canada through Diamond LNG Canada Partnership, so the sale reduces its effective project interest to about 7.5 percent. The deal follows the joint venture partners' final investment decision on September 28, 2026 to build the Phase 2 expansion, which doubles the Kitimat plant's capacity from 14 to 28 million tonnes a year.
