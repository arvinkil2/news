The Gulf Cooperation Council agreed to establish an advanced headquarters for its Unified Military Command in Kuwait and to station appropriate military forces there, the bloc's General Secretariat announced on Tuesday after a meeting of defence ministers in Bahrain. The Unified Military Command, created at a Kuwait summit in December 2013, oversees joint operations and collective defence for the six member states: Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.

The announcement gave no troop numbers, force types or timeline. It lands after Iranian missile and drone attacks struck Gulf states that host major United States military installations in the weeks after the war on Iran began in February, disrupting air travel and shipping and raising concern over the security of the region's oil and gas exports.
