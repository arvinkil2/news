Japan's Ministry of Finance put central government debt at 1,323.4 trillion yen at September 30, down 23.3 trillion yen from the end of the previous quarter and 20.5 trillion yen below the end of the last fiscal year. Government bonds accounted for 1,214.8 trillion yen of the total, up 3.1 trillion yen on the quarter, with long-term bonds rising 5.4 trillion yen while fiscal investment and loan program bonds fell.

The decline in the headline total came from the short end: financing bills fell 25.5 trillion yen on the quarter to 68.9 trillion yen, and borrowings fell 0.9 trillion yen. Government-guaranteed debt fell 1.1 trillion yen to 26.3 trillion yen.
