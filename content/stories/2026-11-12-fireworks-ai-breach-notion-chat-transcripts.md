An attacker breached Fireworks AI's cloud provider to gain access to a Fireworks development system, taking customers' billing information and some datasets, the company confirmed in a security advisory reported by TechCrunch on Thursday. Notion, a confirmed affected customer, said the hackers took chat transcripts of Notion AI customers who used its Insights feature, affecting around 1 percent of its customers, though much of the data was unattributable; Notion said Fireworks alerted it to the incident, and that it disconnected its systems from Fireworks on November 3, deleted the data stored there and stopped all connectivity between the two. Fireworks said it believes the hackers are financially motivated, focused on selling access tokens for large language models, and the incident is its second breach in as many months after one in October.
