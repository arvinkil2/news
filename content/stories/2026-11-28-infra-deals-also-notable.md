Ardian is reportedly preparing to raise about 800 million euros for a first dedicated smaller-cap fund, to be called Scale Up, sitting alongside its established mid-market Expansion strategy. The reported move follows the firm's record 3.2 billion euro sixth Expansion fund and reflects a wider pattern among large European managers: pushing into smaller deals where auctions are thinner and entry prices lower. The report rests on people familiar with the matter and Ardian has not announced the fund.

enCore Energy filed a Form 8-K on November 27 reporting a new material definitive agreement connected to the registration rights from its April 2025 sale of its New Mexico uranium projects, held through NM Energy Holding Canada, to Verdera Energy. Verdera itself changed hands in February 2026, when POCML 7 acquired its shares, and the filing works through the consequences for the 2025 transaction documents. It is housekeeping on a completed sale rather than a new transaction, but it closes a loose end on one of the larger United States uranium asset transfers of 2025.

Already covered and not rewritten: the European Commission's unconditional clearance of Deutsche Börse's acquisition of Allfunds was published as a full infra-deals story on November 27 from the Commission press release IP/26/2592 and Deutsche Börse's own announcement, and today's pool added no new development. Paine Schwartz's $315 million animal health exit in the AltAssets roundup is the Virbac acquisition of Axiota, published on November 26.
