**Capsida reports progress after gene therapy trial death (single-sourced: STAT).** Following the death of a patient in its gene therapy trial for a brain disease, Capsida has reported progress in the follow-up investigation, STAT reported on Tuesday. The update is the company's first substantive word on the path forward for the program since the death was disclosed.

**Helus psychedelic improves depression, with safety questions (single-sourced: STAT).** A psychedelic drug from Helus improved depression in a clinical study, STAT reported, while leaving safety questions that will shape the program's next steps. The readout adds to a crowded field of psychedelic mental health programs working toward pivotal trials.

**Flagship launches UK lab venture (single-sourced: Endpoints News).** Flagship Pioneering has launched a UK laboratory to build AI-driven biotechnology companies, backed by a 100 million pound investment, Endpoints News reported. The venture extends the Flagship company-creation model, behind Moderna, into the United Kingdom's life sciences cluster.
