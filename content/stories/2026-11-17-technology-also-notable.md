**US extends chip restrictions to more Asian countries (single-sourced: The Register).** The United States has extended chip sale restrictions to additional Asian countries to reduce the risk of diversion to China, The Register reported on Tuesday. The extension follows Monday's move to close the China compute loophole, covered in full on November 16, and widens the perimeter of the export control regime around advanced semiconductors.

**French postal subsidiary breach hits 22 million people (single-sourced: The Register).** Criminals attacked a subsidiary of the French postal operator and took contact details belonging to 22 million people, The Register reported. The incident is the second large French data breach in this morning's pool, alongside the Rakuten Securities disclosure covered in full today.

**PostgreSQL patches 52 security holes (single-sourced: The Register).** The PostgreSQL project has patched 52 security vulnerabilities across supported versions and sent version 14 into retirement, The Register reported. Operators running version 14 now need to upgrade to remain on a supported, patched release line.

**Luminal raises $25 million for AI cloud (single-sourced: Datacenter Dynamics).** AI cloud startup Luminal has secured $25 million in Series A funding, Datacenter Dynamics reported, adding to the run of early-stage financings for GPU cloud capacity tracked in this beat.
