Moderna's stock plunged 25 percent in Thursday afternoon trading, and was briefly halted for volatility, after data for the cancer vaccine it is developing with Merck was reportedly published by a cancer news site and then taken down, MarketWatch reported. Merck shares fell 1.4 percent. The vaccine, intismeran, is a personalised treatment made from a patient's own DNA and given with Merck's Keytruda, and the Phase 3 trial tested whether it prevents recurrence in melanoma patients.

The full data are due on Saturday at the European Society for Medical Oncology conference in Madrid. The two companies said in August that the study met its primary and secondary endpoints but released no figures at the time. A Moderna spokesperson said the company had no comment, and spokespeople for Merck and the conference organiser did not immediately respond to requests for comment, MarketWatch reported. Whether the leaked material is authentic, and what it shows, is not publicly known.
