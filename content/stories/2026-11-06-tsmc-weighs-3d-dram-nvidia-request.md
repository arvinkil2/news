Nvidia has asked TSMC to explore producing 3D DRAM, a form of memory that builds its storage cells in vertical layers, and TSMC has begun drafting plans in response, The Information reported on Friday, in an account carried by Reuters. The work is said to be led by Hsu Kuo-chin, the TSMC vice president responsible for advanced packaging technology development, who previously worked at Micron.

TSMC is weighing two routes to capacity, building new factories for memory production or acquiring an existing plant and converting it, Reuters reported. No production timetable, investment figure or capacity target has been disclosed, and neither company responded to the news agency's requests for comment.
