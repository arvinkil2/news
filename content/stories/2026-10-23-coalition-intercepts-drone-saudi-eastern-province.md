**Latest:** The Saudi-led coalition said early on Saturday that air defences intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile launched by the Houthi militia toward Riyadh, in a statement issued at 4:49 a.m. local time and carried by the Saudi Press Agency, Gulf Business reported. An AFP journalist in the capital heard two explosions in the early hours, after Saudi Civil Defense activated its early warning system to alert residents to a possible threat, Deutsche Welle reported, in Spanish, with AFP and Reuters. The coalition's initial announcements reported no casualties or damage from Saturday's interceptions, and no Houthi claim of responsibility had been reported in the sources read. Gulf Business also reported that on Friday the coalition had destroyed two ballistic missiles targeting Riyadh and Khamis Mushait, along with two drones launched toward the kingdom's southern region.

Coalition spokesman Brig. Gen. Turki Al-Malki said in a statement on X that a drone was intercepted and destroyed in the Eastern Province, in an announcement the coalition issued at 3:35 a.m. local time. Shortly past 4 a.m. local time, Saudi Civil Defense sounded alarms warning of potential incoming aerial threats across Riyadh, Najran and the southern governorate of Wadi Al-Dawasir, and two loud blasts were heard across the capital minutes after the alert, Arab News reported.

Saudi Arabia has faced sustained drone and missile barrages in recent weeks, including a strike on Riyadh's international airport, and cross-border attacks have surged since the breakdown in July of an uneasy four-year truce in Yemen, the report said.
