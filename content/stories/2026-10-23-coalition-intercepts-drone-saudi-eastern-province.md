Coalition spokesman Brig. Gen. Turki Al-Malki said in a statement on X that a drone was intercepted and destroyed in the Eastern Province. Shortly past 4 a.m. local time, Saudi Civil Defense sounded alarms warning of potential incoming aerial threats across Riyadh, Najran and the southern governorate of Wadi Al-Dawasir, and two loud blasts were heard across the capital minutes after the alert, Arab News reported.

Saudi Arabia has faced sustained drone and missile barrages in recent weeks, including a strike on Riyadh's international airport, and cross-border attacks have surged since the breakdown in July of an uneasy four-year truce in Yemen, the report said.
