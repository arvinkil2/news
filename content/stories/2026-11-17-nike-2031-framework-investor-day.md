Nike, Inc. introduced its Full Stride Ahead strategy at an investor day at its Beaverton, Oregon campus on November 17, 2026, and reaffirmed its fiscal 2027 financial guidance. The strategy concentrates resources on Performance Sports and Sportswear across the Nike, Jordan and Converse brands.

The accompanying 2031 financial framework targets approximately $5 billion of incremental revenue growth from a fiscal 2027 base, operating margins of 12 to 14 percent and adjusted earnings per share of $3.00 to $3.50 by the end of fiscal 2031. Nike expects approximately $12 billion of cumulative capital returns through dividends from fiscal 2027 through fiscal 2031.
