In transport PPPs, Halmar International has completed the accessibility upgrade at New York's 161st Street Yankee Stadium subway complex, the first station delivered under the Metropolitan Transportation Authority's 965.26 million dollar design, build, finance and maintain programme covering 13 stations. Halmar Infrastructure Development is the developer and financial sponsor through the concessionaire Elevated Accessibility Enhancements, and the remaining 12 stations are expected to finish in early 2027, Construction Dive reported from the company's October 15 release. It is the MTA's first transit PPP, which makes its delivery record a reference for every US transit agency weighing the model.

In grid regulation, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission has approved ISO New England's plan to act as formal reviewer of asset condition transmission projects from January, reporting on need, cost effectiveness and alternatives for utility refurbishment schemes that have historically had little oversight. Transmission owners expect about 5 billion dollars of such projects in New England by 2034, including about 1.3 billion dollars next year, Utility Dive reported. Commissioners Rosner and Chang urged other grid operators to adopt similar review in concurring statements.

In distributed capacity, Utility Dive's analysis of Google's 100 megawatt virtual power plant agreement with Voltus in PJM, announced in June, sets out the bring your own capacity model now spreading among hyperscalers: the large load funds distributed resources and the aggregator carries its capacity obligation. Voltus raised 225 million dollars in Series D financing this month targeting 20 gigawatts of distributed capacity by 2030, and Sunrun has agreed to supply Voltus with capacity from residential solar and storage in PJM and MISO.

In contractor ownership, Balfour Beatty completed the sale of its 50 percent stake in Hong Kong contractor Gammon Construction to joint venture partner Jardine Matheson for 250 million pounds on October 13, ending a partnership running since 2004. The consideration is payable in three equal tranches, the first paid on completion and the rest by the end of 2027 and 2028, and Balfour said its share of Gammon's operating profit was 36 million pounds in 2025 against a carrying value of 86 million pounds. The item is carried here with its October date stated because Construction Dive's report reached this beat's pool on October 19; it is not a new October 20 transaction.
