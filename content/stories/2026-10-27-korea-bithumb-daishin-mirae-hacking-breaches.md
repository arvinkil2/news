Three South Korean financial firms reported data breaches on Wednesday, the Yonhap News Agency said, in the latest cases in a hacking wave across the country's financial sector. Bithumb said its in-house mobile system had been hacked and personal information leaked, and that it was investigating whether member data was included, while Daishin Securities said about 38,000 pieces of customer information had been leaked and that it had taken emergency measures. Mirae Asset Global Investments also confirmed a hacking incident, and authorities were assessing the situation. The cases follow attacks at 16 South Korean financial firms, including banks, that the Financial Services Commission said earlier this month had produced around 100,000 cases of data leaks, and Seoul's Science Ministry and the Korea Internet and Security Agency have separately urged website operators to check for PHP flaws that let attackers run malicious commands on a server without logging in.
