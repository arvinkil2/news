Enlargement is a strategic imperative for Europe and the momentum is real, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said in a statement with European Council President António Costa after the EU-Western Balkans Summit in Brussels on Wednesday. She said the negotiations are on track to open and close over 100 chapters this year, that Montenegro is approaching the finish line of its accession negotiations, and that Albania continues to move towards it at an impressive pace, with roadmaps for both countries presented earlier this month.

On the Growth Plan for the Western Balkans, von der Leyen said partners had delivered 38 percent of the reform steps due a year ago and have now reached 63 percent, and that the reforms could unlock another €338 million for Albania, Montenegro and North Macedonia in the coming weeks. She said four Western Balkan partners are now part of the Single Euro Payments Area, that Roam Like at Home negotiations started last month with Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Kosovo, and that early next year the EU will launch tailored gradual integration dialogues with every partner to open more of the Single Market before membership.
