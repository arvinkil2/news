EU oil supply. The Oil Coordination Group, bringing together the European Commission, EU countries, the IEA, the Energy Community, NATO and industry, concluded on November 13 that the EU's oil supply remains stable for the time being. Prices of crude and products have moderated recently but remain significantly higher than pre conflict levels, overall markets remain tight, and vigilance is warranted, the Commission said. The group also took stock of EU countries' significant October contribution to the IEA March 2026 collective stock release, in particular on diesel, and discussed next steps in the new high level dialogue with the EU refining sector.

Wind shipping. The Neoliner Origin, a wind powered cargo ship, cut fuel consumption by 70 percent in its first year of operation, gCaptain reported. The figure is the operator's, reported by a single trade outlet, and it is the kind of verified in service number the wind assist segment has mostly lacked until now.

Ghana fuel floors. Ghana's National Petroleum Authority reduced the price floor for petrol to 13.69 cedis per litre while increasing the diesel floor to 16.73 cedis, MyJoyOnline reported on November 14. The split move, cheaper petrol floor and dearer diesel floor, tracks the global pattern of this autumn, in which distillates are the tight product. Single sourced to MyJoyOnline.

Egypt gold. Egypt will set up a state backed company to licence artisanal gold miners, SABC News reported on November 14. The move follows the same formalization logic as Guinea's crackdown and Ghana's GoldBod programme: bring informal production into licensed channels where the state can see it and tax it. Single sourced to SABC News.

Russian refineries. Bloomberg tallied six refineries with combined design capacity of about 1.7 million barrels a day attacked since the start of November, as Ukraine's renewed strikes keep Russian diesel exports under pressure. The tally is single sourced to Bloomberg; the renewed attack pattern itself was confirmed by Reuters reporting earlier in November.
