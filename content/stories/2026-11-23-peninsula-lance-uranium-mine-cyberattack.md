Peninsula Energy warned on Monday that a cyberattack on Friday, November 20, hit the operating server and system at its Lance in-situ uranium mine in Wyoming, about 380 kilometres north of Cheyenne, and would temporarily disrupt the operation, The Northern Miner reported. Chief executive George Bauk told the annual meeting that administrative systems at the mine and the corporate office were unaffected; the company put its recovery plan into effect and asked the Australian Securities Exchange to halt trading until it issues an update or trading opens Wednesday, and it has not said whether production stopped or how long the disruption will last.

Lance had already withdrawn its 2026 target of 400,000 to 500,000 pounds of uranium oxide in July over slow flow rates from production wells, and packed 15,588 pounds into drums in the September quarter with no sales in that quarter. Peninsula shares last traded at 16 Australian cents on Friday, valuing the company at about A$91.4 million.
