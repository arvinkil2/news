Total US construction starts rebounded 22.8 percent month over month in October to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of $1.63 trillion, according to Dodge Construction Network, after declines in September and August. The largest groundbreakings were the $16.8 billion first phase of Tesla's Terafab semiconductor plant at Anderson, Texas, the $4.9 billion Amazon Mattameade data centre campus in Woodford, Virginia, and the $3.7 billion CF Industries Blue Point ammonia complex at Modeste, Louisiana. Excluding manufacturing, total starts fell 1.1 percent, Dodge chief economist Eric Gaus said, and residential starts fell 6.9 percent, led by a 14.8 percent drop in multifamily work.

Carnival Corporation signed a contract with Fincantieri for two dual fuel cruise ships of about 130,000 gross tons and 1,650 cabins each, valued at more than 2 billion euros ($2.2 billion), for delivery in 2033 and 2035 from the Marghera yard in Italy. The ships will run primarily on liquefied natural gas and are deliberately smaller than the 230,000 gross ton Ace class vessels Fincantieri is already building for Carnival, a shift towards ports the largest ships cannot serve. Carnival chief executive Josh Weinstein said the group now has 12 ships on order for delivery through 2039.
