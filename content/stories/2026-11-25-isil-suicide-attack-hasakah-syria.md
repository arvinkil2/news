Four gunmen targeted an Internal Security Forces checkpoint in the Mushirfa neighbourhood of Hasakah on Wednesday, according to an official statement from the governorate’s media directorate reported by Al Jazeera. Two security personnel were killed and five were wounded. All four attackers died, two in clashes with security forces and two in suicide explosions, and Syrian media reported one attacker detonated a booby-trapped motorcycle at the entrance of the post.

Deputy Governor Ahmed al-Hilali said the authorities would uncover the circumstances of the attack and identify the perpetrators. In October, Syria’s Interior Ministry reported two members of the internal security forces killed and five wounded in clashes with a suspected ISIL cell during a raid on the outskirts of Damascus.
