Taiwan is expanding its list of controlled exports to include AI servers and the latest chips, in a move to align its policies with the trade bans of the United States, Bloomberg reported. Once the controls take effect, anyone caught smuggling the items will be charged under the Foreign Trade Act, which carries a penalty of up to five years in prison, a fine of around 92,500 dollars, or both.

“Going forward, some of our measures on controlled items will correspond to American ones because we have made quite some commitments in the Agreement on Reciprocal Trade,” Taiwan’s minister of economic affairs, Kung Ming-hsin, said. Under that agreement, signed earlier this year, the United States cut tariffs on Taiwanese goods to 15 percent in exchange for direct investment in the American technology industry, among other commitments.
