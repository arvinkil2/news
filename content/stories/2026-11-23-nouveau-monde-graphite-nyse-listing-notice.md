Nouveau Monde Graphite announced on November 20 that it received a written notification from the New York Stock Exchange on November 18 stating the company is not in compliance with Section 802.01C of the NYSE Listed Company Manual, because the average closing price of its shares was less than US$1.00 over a consecutive 30 trading-day period.

The company said its operations are not affected, and the notice has no immediate effect on the listing or trading of its shares, which continue to trade on the NYSE under the symbol NMG and on the Toronto Stock Exchange, where the notice does not affect its compliance status.

Under the NYSE rules, NMG has six months from receipt of the letter to regain compliance. It does so if, on the last trading day of any calendar month during the cure period, its shares close at or above US$1.00 and have averaged at least US$1.00 over the preceding 30 trading days. If the price has not met the requirement at the end of the cure period, the shares become subject to suspension and delisting procedures.

NMG is developing a fully integrated ore-to-processed-graphite value chain in Quebec, from mining to advanced graphite materials for energy and manufacturing customers. The company said it is evaluating available options in response to the notification, which in practice usually means either an organic share price recovery or a share consolidation before the cure period expires.
