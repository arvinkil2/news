Kyiv mayor Vitaliy Klitschko said a critically important infrastructure object in the capital had been damaged and that water supply had been cut in the city's left bank districts and in the Pecherskyi, Solomianskyi and Holosiivskyi districts, with the Svyatoshinsky, Podolsky and Shevchenkovsky districts on reduced pressure, nine districts affected in all. Klitschko said work was under way to fix the problems, and RIA Novosti separately noted that Ukraine's Ministry of Energy had earlier reported part of Kyiv without electricity. Neither Russian report stated what caused the damage or reported any casualties, and the claim rests on Klitschko's own channel as relayed by two Russian state agencies.
