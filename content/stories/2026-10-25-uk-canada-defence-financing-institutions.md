Britain and Canada reiterated their commitment to strengthening defence financing and said they have agreed to develop a framework of cooperation between the Multilateral Defence Mechanism and the Defence, Security and Resilience Bank, having concluded that two distinct but complementary institutions working in partnership serve both countries best. As the two are operationalised, they will be designed to work in close partnership on industrial priorities, investment opportunities and strategic financing challenges. Together, the statement said, they will provide complementary tools to strengthen the defence industrial base and enhance collective defence readiness.
