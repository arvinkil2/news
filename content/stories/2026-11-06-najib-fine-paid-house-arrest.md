UMNO, the party Najib Razak once led, said it had completed all payment processes to the Kuala Lumpur High Court on Friday morning for the 50 million ringgit fine, about $12.32 million, raising the money through a fundraising campaign. The payment was a condition of the pardon Malaysia's king granted in September, the second royal pardon in Najib's case, allowing him to serve the remainder of his sentence under house arrest.

Najib, prime minister from 2009 to 2018, was found guilty of corruption linked to 1Malaysia Development Berhad, the state fund he co-founded, and has been in prison since 2022; he denies wrongdoing. Reuters said it was not immediately clear when the transfer to his home would take place, and an aide did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
