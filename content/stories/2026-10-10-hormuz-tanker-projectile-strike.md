United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations said Saturday it received a report that the master of an outbound tanker was struck on the port side by an unknown projectile within the Strait of Hormuz at 7:20 PM UTC, causing a fire on board. The crew were reported safe; damage and environmental impact were unknown, and vessels were advised to transit with caution. In a separate advisory the same day, UKMTO said several ship captains near Ras Al Khaimah reported being ordered by VHF broadcast to move from their anchorages. Russian state outlet RT separately reported a tanker ablaze after shelling in the strait. The incident is distinct from Saturday's UAE Coast Guard rescue of 22 crew from a burning tanker in Gulf waters and from the IRGC's claim that a supertanker hit a mine in the strait.
