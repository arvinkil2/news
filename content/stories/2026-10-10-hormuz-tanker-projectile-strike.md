**Latest (Sunday, October 11):** Israeli media reported preparations for a possible resumption of war on Iran ahead of Israel's Knesset elections on October 27, with the public broadcaster reporting that Israel is bracing for the chance Trump orders a restart of the war and Channel 13 saying Trump and Netanyahu held "urgent discussions" on a possible new attack. One scenario reported is a US "green light" for a solo Israeli strike; separately, Walla reported the US had asked Israel to strike Tehran independently before the idea was dropped after IDF chief Eyal Zamir warned it would delay Israel's elections. Israeli outlet Maariv, citing a military official, said no preemptive strike is currently being prepared, Iran has not crossed the "red line" Israel set, and some parties have an interest in heating up the front - while noting Israeli forces are at maximum alert. On Thursday, Trump said Washington has "useful talks" with Tehran and does not intend to attack Iran before November's US midterms. Al Jazeera Arabic (opened and read in full, in Arabic) relayed all of these Israeli-media claims; none has been corroborated by named US or Israeli officials beyond the outlets' own sourcing.

**Latest:** Overnight, Iran's IRGC claimed through official Iranian media that its forces struck the LPG carrier NV Sunshine south of the Strait of Hormuz for attempting to transit via an "illegal route," and asserted Iranian "control of the administration of the strait." The claim is uncorroborated and it is unclear whether the vessel named is the same one UKMTO reported struck Saturday.

United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations said Saturday it received a report that the master of an outbound tanker was struck on the port side by an unknown projectile within the Strait of Hormuz at 7:20 PM UTC, causing a fire on board. The crew were reported safe; damage and environmental impact were unknown, and vessels were advised to transit with caution. In a separate advisory the same day, UKMTO said several ship captains near Ras Al Khaimah reported being ordered by VHF broadcast to move from their anchorages. Russian state outlet RT separately reported a tanker ablaze after shelling in the strait. The incident is distinct from Saturday's UAE Coast Guard rescue of 22 crew from a burning tanker in Gulf waters and from the IRGC's claim that a supertanker hit a mine in the strait.
