Meta Platforms is offering €9 billion of euro senior notes in six tranches, according to a prospectus supplement filed on Friday: €1.75 billion of 4.300% notes due 2031, €1.25 billion of 4.650% notes due 2033, €1 billion of 4.900% notes due 2035, €1.75 billion of 5.400% notes due 2038, €1.75 billion of 6.050% notes due 2044 and €1.5 billion of 6.400% notes due 2065. The notes are unsecured, rank equally with Meta's other unsecured senior debt and are not guaranteed by any subsidiary.

Interest on the 2031, 2033, 2035 and 2065 notes is payable each February 15, beginning February 15, 2027, and on the 2038 and 2044 notes each November 15, beginning November 15, 2027. Meta intends to use the net proceeds for general corporate purposes. Citigroup and Morgan Stanley are joint book-running managers, with Goldman Sachs, BofA Securities and J.P. Morgan as passive joint bookrunners and Société Générale Corporate & Investment Banking as co-manager.

**Update, November 12.** Meta completed the offering on November 12, 2026, according to a Form 8-K filed the same day: all six tranches were issued in the amounts and on the terms set out in the prospectus supplement, under an underwriting agreement dated November 4, 2026 with Citigroup Global Markets Limited and Morgan Stanley & Co. International plc as representatives of the underwriters.
