CF Industries has signed a definitive agreement to acquire The Mosaic Company's Faustina ammonia production facility in St. James, Louisiana, and related ammonia storage and logistics assets for a total transaction value of $600 million, the company said in a Form 8-K filing on Tuesday. The price comprises $360 million that CF Industries expects to fund with cash on hand and $240 million allocated to a long-term ammonia offtake agreement under which it will supply Mosaic with up to 200,000 tons of ammonia a year; Mosaic said the offtake runs for 20 years at producer economics to support its phosphate operations, and that the transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions.

The Faustina plant has recently produced about 300,000 tons of ammonia a year at an average capacity utilisation of about 60 percent over the past five years, against about 96 percent across CF Industries' own ammonia network, and CF Industries plans to invest about $100 million over three years to lift output to 480,000 tons a year within the first year after closing and to about 570,000 tons by 2030. The assets acquired include a 25,000 ton ammonia storage tank, a truck loading station, a connection to the Sunoco ammonia pipeline and access to Mosaic's deepwater dock, and the transaction is expected to preserve about 50 manufacturing jobs in Louisiana. Both companies' boards have approved the deal unanimously.
