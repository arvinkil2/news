Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority, the body Tehran set up to manage passage requests in the Strait of Hormuz, said on Tuesday that all vessels belonging to the shipping company Sinokor will face "legal action" starting on November 1. The authority said on X that Sinokor's vessels had committed the highest number of crossing violations in the strait over the past three months, and that "appropriate actions" had already been taken against at least seven of them.

The authority did not specify in the reporting read what the legal action would involve. Its non-compliance list of vessels accused of violating Iranian transit rules has been expanding through October, and ships on it have been struck in the strait, as this site's Hormuz coverage has recorded.
