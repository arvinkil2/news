Russia struck a city clinical hospital in Kharkiv overnight, injuring at least 73 people, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said. The hospital's cardiology department, where patients were receiving treatment, was damaged, and Kharkiv Mayor Ihor Terekhov said the building sustained heavy damage with more than 1,000 windows shattered.

Zelenskyy said Russia launched 289 attack drones, 17 ballistic missiles and 59 cruise missiles overnight, with power generation facilities and substations the main target across nine regions. More than 180,000 people in the Zhytomyr region were without power on Tuesday morning, according to Governor Vitalii Bunechko, and Odesa introduced scheduled outages. Russia's Ministry of Defence said it had targeted electricity facilities in the Vinnytsia, Dnipropetrovsk and Kharkiv regions, and said those sites supplied power to the Ukrainian army and military factories.
