Hafnia, the BW Group-backed tanker owner listed in Oslo and New York, has pushed back its decision on distributing its stake in Danish product tanker rival Torm, extending a November 25 deadline to December 9 to work through the mechanics of distributing the shares to its own shareholders. The company cited the number of jurisdictions involved, settlement complexities and the US Thanksgiving holiday.

Hafnia continues to favour distributing all 20.36 million Torm shares it holds rather than paying a conventional cash dividend. The proposed distribution was valued at 686.6 million dollars when announced last week, with Hafnia shareholders in line to receive one Torm share for every 26.3 Hafnia shares held. If it proceeds, the distribution replaces Hafnia's regular cash dividends for the third and fourth quarters; the alternative is a cash payout equivalent to 90 percent of third-quarter net profit.

Hafnia spent around 514 million dollars building its 19.85 percent stake in Torm before abandoning its consolidation push last week after failing to secure support from its Danish rival. The owner has since turned to other growth opportunities, including plans to exercise purchase options on eight vessels worth around 235 million dollars.
