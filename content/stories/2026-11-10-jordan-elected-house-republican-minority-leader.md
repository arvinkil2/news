House Republicans elected Representative Jim Jordan of Ohio as minority leader for the next Congress on Tuesday, defeating outgoing Majority Leader Steve Scalise of Louisiana by 106 votes to 73 in a closed-door vote in the Capitol Visitor Center. The result follows last week's midterm defeat and Speaker Mike Johnson's decision to step aside from leadership at the end of the term, and it closes a contest in which many Republican lawmakers blamed the outgoing leadership team for the loss. Jordan, a 20-year House veteran who co-founded the House Freedom Caucus and chairs the Judiciary Committee, will lead the conference against the incoming Democratic majority when the 120th Congress convenes on January 3, 2027.
