Yandex said a drone attack on its data centre in Sasovo, about 300 kilometres southeast of Moscow, caused a fire that halted operations and took part of its Yandex Cloud infrastructure offline, with its ru-central1-b availability zone now down. The company advised customers to pursue unspecified alternative service restoration plans but warned service may not be restorable in the near term; Reuters reports Yandex is uncertain it can recover the facility at all. If written off, it would be the second cloud facility lost to a drone attack this year after AWS concluded its Bahrain site could not be recovered following an Iranian strike. Yandex has not commented on the source of the drone, though Ukrainian press reports Zelensky has said Russia struck Ukrainian data centres first and his government responds in kind, a claim that remains that side's uncorroborated narrative.
