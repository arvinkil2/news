Russia has extended its ban on jet fuel exports until March 31, 2027, Anadolu Agency reported, citing a government statement. The restriction, which also covers sales through exchange trading, takes effect on December 1; the current ban, introduced in June, was due to expire on November 30, and Reuters reported that supplies made under international intergovernmental agreements are exempt.

The government said the extension is intended to maintain stability in the domestic fuel market, amid Ukrainian attacks on Russian oil refineries that have forced several facilities into maintenance and led to fuel sales restrictions in some regions. In late October, Moscow also extended a temporary ban on diesel and marine fuel exports until January 31, 2027.
