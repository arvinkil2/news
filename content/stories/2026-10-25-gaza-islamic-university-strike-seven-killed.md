An air strike on the Islamic University campus in Gaza City killed three security guards on Sunday, the second attack on the campus within a few hours after an earlier drone strike wounded seven female students, one of them seriously, Al Jazeera reported. Israeli shelling also killed two people in al-Mawasi near Khan Younis, a drone strike killed one person east of Deir el-Balah, and one person was killed on al-Rashid Street in the north, bringing the day's toll reported by Al Jazeera to at least seven. The report carried no Israeli military comment on the attacks.
