## Latest

**Update, November 23.** Poland revealed on Sunday that the same man, rapper Albert Vasiliev, who performs as Kyivstoner and holds Ukrainian and US citizenship, was allegedly targeted by Russian intelligence in Poland first, in August 2026. The Polish Foreign Ministry said the first attempt took place on Polish territory in August and was thwarted by Polish services, and that the would-be perpetrator, a Russian citizen allegedly recruited by Russia's secret services, was detained on August 7. Prime Minister Donald Tusk had disclosed a thwarted plot against a Ukrainian American citizen in Warsaw on August 13 without naming the target. A ministry spokesman, Maciej Wewior, confirmed the earlier attempt to the Associated Press, and Poland said it would support the EU measures Denmark is proposing this week. Russia's FSB has accused Vasiliev of involvement in supplying drones for an alleged Ukrainian attack on a defence enterprise in the Moscow region, which Danish broadcaster DR reported he denies.

Denmark has urged EU member states to take concrete action against Russia after the foiled plot, in a message sent to EU governments on Friday evening and confirmed by two diplomats, Reuters reported. Copenhagen proposed further restrictions on Russian nationals' access to the Schengen zone, including a halt to tourist visas for Russian citizens and Schengen-wide non-recognition of Russian non-biometric passports, and will present the proposals to EU ambassadors in Brussels on Monday. Russia's embassy in Copenhagen said on Friday that Denmark's accusations were not backed with proof.

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen on Saturday strongly condemned the assassination attempt against a US-Ukrainian citizen in Denmark, saying it was orchestrated by Russian intelligence services and calling it an attack on EU soil that is unacceptable. She said the EU stands in full solidarity with Denmark and with all member states targeted by Russian hybrid attacks, that an attack on one member is an attack on the entire Union, and that the Commission will soon propose a new European security strategy to reinforce preparedness and deterrence. European Council President Antonio Costa also condemned the attempt and said such actions will not go unanswered. Russia's embassy in Copenhagen said on Friday that the accusations were not backed with proof.

Denmark on Friday summoned the Russian ambassador and expelled Moscow's defence attache, giving him seven days to leave, after the PET intelligence agency said it suspected Russian intelligence services of planning to assassinate the Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner at a concert in a Copenhagen suburb, with help from Loyal to Familia, a Danish street gang banned since 2021. PET said three people were intercepted in a car nearby before the concert with firearms and knives, and two were placed under formal investigation for attempted murder and for assisting a foreign intelligence service.

Foreign Minister Lars Lokke Rasmussen said Denmark was also considering suspending Schengen visas for Russian nationals, and Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen called the case a clear illustration of the serious security situation facing Denmark and Europe. Russia's foreign ministry said Moscow would give an appropriate response, and its embassy in Copenhagen called the expulsion vengeful and said Denmark had presented no evidence of Russian involvement.
