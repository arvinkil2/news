**EU electricity support spending reaches 12 billion euros.** EU countries spent 12 billion euros on security of electricity supply support measures in 2025, according to ACER's monitoring report. The eight countries with capacity mechanisms spent 6.9 billion euros on them, a cost ultimately borne by consumers, and more countries introduced capacity mechanisms in 2025 and 2026, bringing the total to 11. Fossil-fuel generators still benefit the most, receiving 76 percent of state-aid approved support, including in new contracts. ACER expects EU electricity demand to rise, with data centres reaching 134 terawatt-hours by 2030 and 199 terawatt-hours by 2035, and notes that capacity mechanisms in one member state can reduce adequacy risks by up to 66 percent in neighbouring countries without them. The agency urges member states to contain rising costs through better coordination.
