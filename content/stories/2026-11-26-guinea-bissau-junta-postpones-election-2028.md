Guinea-Bissau's ruling junta announced on Thursday that it is postponing the presidential election by two years, to November 26, 2028, exactly one year after the military took power. Transitional president General Horta Inta-a said in a statement that there was an "insufficiency of the technical, material and financial conditions" to hold the vote. The military seized power on November 26, 2025, three days after a general election, deposing President Umaro Sissoco Embalo before the results were released; ECOWAS has urged the junta to hand power back to civilians.
