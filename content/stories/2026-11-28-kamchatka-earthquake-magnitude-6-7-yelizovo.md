A magnitude 6.7 earthquake struck on Russia's Kamchatka Peninsula at 13:15 UTC on Saturday, centred 62 kilometres north-northwest of Yelizovo at a depth of 188 kilometres, according to the US Geological Survey, whose reviewed solution puts the epicentre at 53.719 degrees north, 158.079 degrees east. The USGS issued a Green PAGER alert, its lowest impact category, and the event carries no tsunami flag, with no tsunami warning issued.

No casualties or damage had been reported as of this update, and the USGS record lists no felt reports, with a maximum instrumental intensity of about IV. Russian wire reports said tremors were felt at intensity 3 in Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, the regional capital south of the epicentre.
