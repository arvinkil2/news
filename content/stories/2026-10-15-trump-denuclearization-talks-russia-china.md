President Donald Trump told Newsmax on Thursday that Washington is holding talks with Moscow and Beijing aimed at denuclearizing the countries with the largest nuclear stockpiles, and that he had spoken with Chinese President Xi Jinping about it. He said he expects China to catch up with the US in nuclear capability within five years.

Russia holds the most stored warheads, followed by the US and China, according to the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute, while the US deploys more warheads than Russia. The New START treaty expired earlier this year, and any extension of its limits would now require a voluntary executive decision.
