Apollo Global Management issued $600,000,000 of 7.550% fixed-rate resettable junior subordinated notes due December 15, 2056 on November 13, closing a previously announced underwritten public offering, the company reported in a Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission. The notes pay a fixed 7.550 percent until December 15, 2036 and then reset for each period to the five-year U.S. Treasury rate plus 2.672 percent, with interest payable semi-annually on June 15 and December 15 beginning June 15, 2027, subject to Apollo's right to defer interest payments. Apollo said it intends to use the net proceeds for general corporate purposes, including to fund its previously announced investment in Mubadala Capital Asset Management.
