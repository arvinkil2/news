The Federal Reserve Board on Thursday announced an enforcement action against American Express Company, saying the firm failed to sufficiently detect and report certain suspicious activity related to money laundering. The Board also identified significant deficiencies in how the firm's enterprise-wide anti-money-laundering program was implemented, in particular at its subsidiary national bank.

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, the primary federal regulator of that bank, issued a separate enforcement action against it, the Fed said. The Board's action is intended to ensure the firm operates in compliance with all US laws and regulations.
