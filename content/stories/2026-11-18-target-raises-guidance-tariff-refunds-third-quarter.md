Target reported third quarter net sales of $27.1 billion, up 7.1 percent from a year earlier, with comparable sales up 5.6 percent on a 5.0 percent increase in comparable traffic, and GAAP and adjusted earnings per share of $2.65 against $1.51 and $1.78 respectively a year ago. The results include $301 million of pretax IEEPA tariff refunds received in the quarter, worth $0.50 per share; excluding the refunds, GAAP earnings per share would still have risen 42 percent.

The company raised its full year outlook on the strength of the quarter, guiding net sales growth to around 6 percent, a point above its prior range, and earnings per share to $10.65 to $11.40, a range that includes about $2.14 per share of tariff refund benefits recognised to date and excludes any future refunds. Target also said it expects to invest $5 billion to $6 billion in capital expenditures in 2027.
