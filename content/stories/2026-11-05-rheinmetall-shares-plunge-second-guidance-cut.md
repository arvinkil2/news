Rheinmetall cut its 2026 sales forecast to about 13.2 billion euros, from 13.7 billion to 14.2 billion euros, and lowered its operating margin target to about 18.5 percent from around 19 percent, saying revenue and advance payments linked to Germany's Arminius Boxer vehicle programme would move into next year and that slower batch approvals would delay artillery ammunition revenue. Shares fell 13 percent on Thursday, the biggest decline in the STOXX 600, in the group's second outlook cut in three months.

The warning came despite a strong third quarter, in which sales rose to 3.15 billion euros from 2.31 billion euros a year earlier and operating profit rose 62 percent to 553 million euros, while order intake of 2.89 billion euros ran below sales and operating free cash flow was negative 82 million euros.
