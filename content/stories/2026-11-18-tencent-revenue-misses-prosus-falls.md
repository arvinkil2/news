Tencent's third-quarter revenue rose 8 percent to 207.34 billion yuan, slowing from 11 percent growth in the previous quarter and missing the 210.77 billion yuan analysts expected on LSEG data, while operating profit fell 7 percent, the company's first earnings decline in years, though net profit edged up 0.8 percent to 63.61 billion yuan and beat forecasts. Games were the weak spot, with value-added services revenue up just 3 percent to 98.3 billion yuan as international games revenue fell 9 percent against an 11 percent rise at home, while fintech and business services, including cloud, rose 9 percent to 63.7 billion yuan on demand for artificial intelligence services. Prosus, the Dutch investment group that is Tencent's largest investor, fell about 8 percent on Wednesday, and Tencent also posted negative free cash flow of more than 69 billion yuan, its second straight quarterly outflow.
