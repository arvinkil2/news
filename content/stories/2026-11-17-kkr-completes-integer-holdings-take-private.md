Armstrong Parent, an affiliate of investment funds managed by Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co., completed its acquisition of Integer Holdings Corporation on November 17, 2026, under the merger agreement dated August 2, 2026. Integer shareholders received $127.00 in cash per share, and the company continues as a wholly owned subsidiary of Armstrong Parent.

The buyout was financed in part under a new credit agreement providing $2.1 billion of initial term loans and a $350 million revolving facility, with Citibank as administrative agent, and Integer repaid and terminated its existing credit agreement at closing. Integer's convertible notes remain outstanding: $116.3 million of 2.125 percent notes due 2028 and $1 billion of 1.875 percent notes due 2030, each now convertible into cash based on the $127.00 merger price.
