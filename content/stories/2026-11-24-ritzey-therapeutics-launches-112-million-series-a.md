Ritzey Therapeutics announced its launch on November 23 with a $112.5 million Series A financing led by Vida Ventures, with participation from EcoR1 Capital, Fidelity Management and Research Company, Perceptive Advisors, Montanova, Alexandria Venture Investments and FMB Research. The company is based in Towson, Maryland.

Ritzey was founded and is led by Sharon Mates as chairman, chief executive and president. Mates founded Intra-Cellular Therapies and led it through its acquisition by Johnson and Johnson in 2025, a deal reported at about $14 billion, and she is joined by former members of Intra-Cellular's senior leadership team.

The company has completed the acquisition of Perha Pharmaceuticals of France, adding a portfolio of DYRK1A inhibitors. Its lead candidate is an oral, once daily DYRK1A inhibitor that has completed Phase 1 testing. Ritzey plans Phase 2 studies of its effects on pancreatic beta cell function and tau pathology in people with Down syndrome, and on beta cell function in type 1 and type 2 diabetes. Financial terms of the Perha acquisition were not disclosed.

The thesis is intracellular signaling: preserve and restore metabolic and neurologic function and reduce inflammation by targeting dysregulated pathways inside the cell. It is the same broad scientific instinct Mates pursued at Intra-Cellular, applied to chronic metabolic and neurologic disease, and it will be judged on Phase 2 evidence that does not yet exist.
