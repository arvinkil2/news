Roivant and its spinout Priovant said on Wednesday that their immune modifying pill Lisraya was more effective than placebo in treating noninfectious uveitis, an inflammatory eye disease, in two Phase 3 trials spanning 371 patients. The companies plan to file for approval in the first half of next year, which would expand the drug beyond dermatomyositis, the rare skin disease it was cleared for in August.

Roivant estimates 70,000 to 190,000 patients in the United States have the condition, with more than 40,000 already receiving Humira or a similar therapy, STAT reported.
