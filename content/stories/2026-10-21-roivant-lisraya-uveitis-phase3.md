Roivant and its subsidiary Priovant said on Wednesday that brepocitinib, sold as Lisraya, succeeded in both CLARITY Phase 3 trials in noninfectious uveitis, an inflammatory eye disease, with steroid free clinical remission at week 24 in 44.4 percent of patients in CLARITY 1 (p less than 0.0001 against placebo) and 51.5 percent in CLARITY 2 (p equal to 0.0005). Both trials also met their key secondary endpoints, and no new safety signals were observed, the companies said.

Priovant plans to file for approval in the first half of 2027, which would expand the drug beyond dermatomyositis, the rare skin disease it was cleared for in August. Roivant estimates 70,000 to 190,000 patients in the United States have noninfectious uveitis, with more than 40,000 already receiving Humira or a similar therapy, STAT reported.
