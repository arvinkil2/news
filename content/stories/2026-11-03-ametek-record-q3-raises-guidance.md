AMETEK reported record third quarter sales of 2.22 billion dollars, up 17 percent from a year earlier, with adjusted earnings of 2.21 dollars per diluted share, also up 17 percent, and adjusted operating income up 20 percent to 594.7 million dollars. The company completed the acquisition of Indicor Instrumentation during the quarter.

For 2026, AMETEK now expects overall sales to be up in the mid teens from 2025 and adjusted earnings of 8.53 to 8.57 dollars per diluted share, up from prior guidance of 8.20 to 8.30 dollars. Chairman and chief executive David Zapico attributed the raise to the third quarter results, the fourth quarter outlook and the expected contribution from Indicor.
