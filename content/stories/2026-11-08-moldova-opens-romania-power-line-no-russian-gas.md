Moldova has launched the Vulcănești Chișinău power transmission line, which connects the country with Romania and the European energy system, President Maia Sandu announced. "For decades Moscow used energy to blackmail us. No more: Moldova does not buy Russian gas," Sandu said, adding that two more transmission lines to Romania will follow soon.

Government controlled Moldova stopped buying Gazprom gas for the right bank of the Dniester in 2023, but Russian gas continued to reach the Moldovan power station in Transnistria, from which Moldova bought electricity. Sandu also said Moldova recently bought its first gas from Ukrainian private companies and is considering purchases of 10 to 50 million cubic metres from Ukraine at below market prices.
