OpenAI is nearing a funding round of up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation led by investors from the United Arab Emirates, CNBC reported on Thursday, citing a regulatory filing connected to the deal and a person familiar with the matter. The filing lists fund vehicles tied to G42 and MGX, the Abu Dhabi artificial intelligence investment firms, and Abu Dhabi itself and Microsoft were described as close to signing on, while SoftBank, a major backer in OpenAI's earlier rounds, is not participating this time, according to the report.

The round has not closed, and neither its final size nor its investor list is settled. OpenAI last raised at a valuation well below the reported $1.25 trillion figure, and a completed round at that level would make it one of the most valuable private companies in the world.
