Secretary of State Marco Rubio met Finnish Foreign Minister Elina Valtonen on Thursday to strengthen United States and Finland economic and security cooperation, and the two signed a Critical Minerals Framework Agreement alongside a United States and Finland Technology Prosperity Deal. The State Department said the signings are meant to support technology collaboration and secure critical supply chains, and that both leaders underscored the importance of cooperation on critical minerals and continued defense investment. The readout gives no further detail: no minerals, projects, funding or implementation steps are named.
