## Latest

DoorDash is in advanced talks to acquire Instacart, The Wall Street Journal reported on Monday, in a deal that could value Instacart at about $17 billion, against a market value of about $12 billion before the sale reports began on Friday. A deal could be reached as soon as this week. Instacart shares rose 4 percent on Monday, extending Friday's gain of about 9 percent, and DoorDash shares fell 5 percent. The report said the talks remain ongoing and could still fall apart, and that a combination could face antitrust scrutiny because the two companies' businesses overlap. Neither company commented in the reporting read.

Instacart is exploring a possible sale and has held preliminary discussions with potential buyers including Uber and DoorDash, The Information reported on Friday, citing people familiar with the matter. Instacart shares closed about 9 percent higher, giving the grocery delivery company a market value of about $12 billion, while Uber closed down nearly 3 percent and DoorDash fell more than 4 percent. Reuters reported that a typical takeover premium of 30 to 40 percent would value Instacart at roughly $16 billion to $17 billion.

The talks revive an earlier attempt: DoorDash held talks to acquire Instacart in 2021, in a deal that would have valued it at between $40 billion and $50 billion, and those talks fell apart in part over whether a combination could win antitrust approval. Discussions are ongoing and there is no guarantee they will lead to a deal.
