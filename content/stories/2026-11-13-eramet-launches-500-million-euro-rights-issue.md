Eramet will issue 34.5 million new shares at 14.49 euros each, a ratio of six new shares for five existing shares, with the new shares expected to start trading on Euronext Paris on or about December 4. The French state holding agency, Agence des Participations de l'Etat, has committed to subscribe for 64 percent of the issue, or 322 million euros.

The company said the raise will improve cash generation and strengthen its balance sheet, above all by reducing debt. Net debt was 1.94 billion euros at the end of 2025, and Eramet aims to bring it down to between 1.6 billion and 1.7 billion euros, helped by asset sales that include undeveloped lithium concessions in Argentina, a minority stake in the Centenario-Ratones lithium operation and its 38 percent shareholding in Weda Bay Nickel.
