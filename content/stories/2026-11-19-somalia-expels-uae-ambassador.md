Somalia's government announced on Thursday that it had expelled the ambassador of the United Arab Emirates, accusing Abu Dhabi of interfering in the country's internal affairs. Information Minister Abdelfattah Qassem said in a statement that the ambassador has 72 hours to leave the country, and Mogadishu also recalled its own ambassador to Abu Dhabi.

The statement gave no more specific allegation against the Emirates, and the ambassador was not named in the report. The expulsion follows a period of friction between Mogadishu and Abu Dhabi over the UAE's dealings in Somalia, and no Emirati response had been reported at the time of writing.
