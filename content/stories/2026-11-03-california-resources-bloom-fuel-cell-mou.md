California Resources Corporation (CRC) and Bloom Energy have signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding to evaluate the deployment of 1 gigawatt or more of Bloom's solid oxide fuel cells in California, coupled with carbon capture systems. The partners will seek offtake customers including data centres, utilities, community choice aggregators, municipalities and other industrial users, across front-of-the-meter and behind-the-meter projects in Central and Northern California.

Bloom would supply the fuel cells and third-party carbon capture technology, while CRC's carbon management business, Carbon TerraVault, is expected to handle carbon dioxide transport and storage, with CRC managing the projects. The companies said up to three million metric tons per year of carbon dioxide could potentially be captured and stored.
