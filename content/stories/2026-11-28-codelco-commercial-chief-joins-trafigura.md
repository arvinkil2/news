Codelco chief commercial officer Braim Chiple has resigned to join commodity trader Trafigura Group, Bloomberg reported on Friday, as the Chilean state owned copper miner reshapes its leadership under chief executive Jorge Gomez. Carlos Olavarria, currently Codelco's refined copper commercial manager, will succeed Chiple, according to the report.

The commercial role at Codelco carries the sales book of the world's largest copper producer, including its long term contracts with smelters and fabricators in China, Europe and North America. A move from that desk to Trafigura, one of the largest independent copper traders and a regular buyer of Codelco metal, is the kind of producer to trader switch that tends to draw scrutiny over information flow, even when no impropriety is suggested.

The departure lands in the same week that Congo's copper exports were reported closing in on Chile's, a reminder that Codelco's commercial leadership is changing while the market it sells into is being reshaped by new supply from Central Africa.
