Forces from the European Union's naval mission EUNAVFOR MED IRINI boarded the tanker Midag in the Mediterranean on November 13, EU High Representative Kaja Kallas announced on Sunday. The vessel was suspected of belonging to the shadow fleet Russia uses to export crude oil around Western sanctions, and was sailing under the flag of Zimbabwe, according to the tracking services ShipFinder and VesselTracker. Neither report stated what the inspection found, or whether the vessel was detained or diverted.
