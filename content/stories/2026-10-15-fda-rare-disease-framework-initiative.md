The Food and Drug Administration said on Wednesday it plans to reimagine how it regulates therapies for rare diseases, reviewing existing policies and seeking input from patients and experts toward a fit for purpose framework covering everything from preclinical engagement to post marketing requirements, BioPharma Dive reported. The agency set no timeline for initial recommendations, though Bloomberg News reported it is targeting February, and it will take feedback at a meeting on October 28 at its Silver Spring, Maryland campus.

The initiative continues a reset at an agency still waiting for a permanent leader, after several earlier decisions that drew industry criticism were reversed. Leerink Partners analyst Joseph Schwartz wrote that the FDA is not lowering the bar for rare disease drugs but may make the path to approval less of a guessing game.
