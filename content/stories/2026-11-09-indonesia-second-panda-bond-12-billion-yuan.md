Indonesia has begun marketing its second yuan-denominated bond in China's domestic debt market, with a target of raising as much as 12 billion yuan (1.8 billion dollars), according to an announcement cited by Bloomberg. The offering would have three, five and seven year parts. The sale proceeds even as Southeast Asia's largest economy grapples with the threat of a double downgrade from MSCI.
