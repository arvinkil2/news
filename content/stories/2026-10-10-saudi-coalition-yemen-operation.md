The coalition's announcement of a wide-ranging operation targeting the Houthis follows days of escalation: Houthi attacks on Saudi airports earlier this week and the invocation of the Mecca pact. The reported figure of more than 2,750 strikes in 24 hours is the coalition's own count and has not been independently verified.

Yemeni Foreign Minister Afrah Al-Zouba framed the objective in an interview with Asharq Al-Awsat: the goal of the current phase, dubbed "Yemen's Dawn," is to end the Houthi coup and extend state authority over all national territory, with any future political process ending in the state having sole control over weapons and decisions of war and peace rather than power-sharing with an armed group. She said success would be measured by the number of citizens who regain security, not cities retaken, and cited UN Security Council calls for arms-embargo enforcement under Resolution 2216 to cut off Iranian supplies.
