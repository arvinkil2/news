Thailand's economy grew 2.9 percent year on year in the third quarter of 2026, accelerating from a revised 2.4 percent in the second quarter and rising 0.3 percent quarter on quarter on a seasonally adjusted basis, the National Economic and Social Development Council said on Monday, taking growth in the first nine months of the year to 2.8 percent. The result was slightly below the 3 percent median forecast in a Reuters poll of economists reported by Investing.com.

Exports of goods and services rose 18.3 percent, with goods exports worth US$106.377 billion, up 23.6 percent and the highest in 21 quarters, led by telecommunications equipment, computer parts and integrated circuits on AI-related demand. Private investment rose 16.2 percent and total investment 11.8 percent, its fastest pace in 55 quarters, while private consumption rose 2.5 percent and government consumption fell 1.6 percent, its first decline in four quarters.

The council raised its 2026 growth forecast to 2.5 percent, from a previous range of 2.0 to 2.5 percent reported by Investing.com, and projected growth of 2.0 to 3.0 percent in 2027 on a tourism recovery and a larger contribution from net exports.
