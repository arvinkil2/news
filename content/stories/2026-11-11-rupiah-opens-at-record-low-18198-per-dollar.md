The rupiah weakened 49 points, or 0.27 percent, at Thursday's open in Jakarta to 18,198 per US dollar, from a previous close of 18,149, Antara reported. Bloomberg characterised the level as a record low for the currency, attributing the pressure to MSCI index concerns and oil risks.

The move came hours after MSCI's November index review removed Barito Pacific from its global standard indexes and moved Bumi Resources Minerals to small cap, cutting Indonesia's standard index membership from nine stocks to seven, with the changes effective after the close on November 30.
