Caterpillar delivered its highest sales quarter ever in the third quarter, with revenue of 20.7 billion dollars, up 17 percent from a year earlier, and profit of 3.3 billion dollars, up 43 percent. Chief executive Joseph Creed said order rates stayed strong and the company's backlog reached 78 billion dollars, nearly double the level a year ago, with power generation sales up 30 percent on data center demand. The power and energy segment posted 8.8 billion dollars of sales, construction industries nearly 8 billion dollars, and resource industries 4.6 billion dollars, while the company absorbed 600 million dollars of tariff costs in the quarter and guided to mid to high teens sales growth for the full year.
