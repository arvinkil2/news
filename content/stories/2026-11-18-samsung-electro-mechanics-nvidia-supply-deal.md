Samsung Electro-Mechanics said on Wednesday it is expanding its partnership with Nvidia to supply advanced semiconductor package substrates and multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs), which help ensure stable power supply in high-performance computing systems. The South Korean company said it would strengthen production capabilities at its Sejong and Busan facilities to supply advanced substrates for Nvidia's next-generation graphics processing units and its high-performance computing and networking products.

Nvidia is reviewing additional funding support for the production infrastructure expansion at the two sites, building on an earlier agreement to provide large-scale funding for dedicated manufacturing facilities, the company said. Samsung Electro-Mechanics aims to establish a full-scale mass production system starting in 2029. Its Seoul-listed shares rose 6.5 percent to 803,000 won by mid-afternoon in Asia. No deal value or supply volumes were disclosed.
