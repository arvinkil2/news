Japan's trade deficit widened to ¥810,433 million in the first 20 days of October 2026, from ¥216,629 million in the same period a year earlier, according to provisional figures released by Japan Customs on November 10. Exports rose 23.5 percent year on year to ¥7,314,664 million, but imports rose faster, up 32.4 percent to ¥8,125,097 million. The release publishes totals only, with no breakdown by partner or commodity, so the drivers of the import surge cannot be separated from this release alone.
