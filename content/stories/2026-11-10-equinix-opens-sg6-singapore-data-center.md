Equinix has opened SG6, its sixth International Business Exchange data center in Singapore, announcing the launch over the weekend. The nine-story facility in Jalan Tukang will provide approximately 1,550 cabinets in its first phase and up to 20MW of IT capacity at full build-out, for an initial investment of $290 million; the company broke ground in November 2024 and had originally expected to launch in the first quarter of 2027.

SG6 was built under the Singapore government's Data Centre Call for Application process, which has rationed new capacity since the 2019 moratorium on new developments. Equinix was one of four operators allocated 80MW collectively in 2023, and one of four, with Digital Realty, Keppel Data Centers and ST Telemedia Global Data Centers, granted 50MW each in a second allocation round earlier this year. The facility is designed for a power usage effectiveness of 1.20 and targets BCA-IMDA Green Mark Platinum certification, and it will host a co-innovation facility with the National University of Singapore Energy Solutions Hub, targeted to open in 2027.
