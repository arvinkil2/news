The United States Treasury Department has targeted the Cartel del Noreste's horse racing enterprise and its wider criminal network with sanctions, freezing any US based assets of the designated parties and barring US persons from dealing with them.

Racing is not a random target. Quarter horse racing in the US southwest was at the centre of one of the largest cartel laundering cases on record, the Zetas' Tremor Enterprises prosecution, and Treasury has repeatedly returned to stables, bloodstock sales and race purses as points where drug money enters the legitimate economy. The Cartel del Noreste, the Laredo based successor faction to the Zetas, is a standing priority of that effort.

The action adds to a week of Treasury activity around the Bangkok meetings, including Secretary Bessent's statement to the World Bank Development Committee, and underlines that financial statecraft against cartels continues on its own track regardless of the state of US-Mexico political relations.
