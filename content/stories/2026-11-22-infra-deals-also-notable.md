**CESEC expands to Czechia and Poland.** Energy ministers of the Central and South-East European Energy Connectivity group, meeting in Zagreb on November 6, formally welcomed Czechia and Poland as members and endorsed updated action plans on electricity and renewable energy and on gases, including hydrogen and biomethane. The group, set up in 2015 to diversify gas supplies, now brings together 19 countries, 11 EU members and eight non-EU states including Ukraine and Moldova, and coordinates the cross-border interconnection projects on which the region's supply security depends.

**Granite wins California groundwater work.** The Coachella Valley Water District awarded Granite Construction a $26 million contract on the Whitewater River Channel and Palm Desert Groundwater Replenishment project, the company announced on November 18. It is a small award, but it sits in the fast growing class of water reuse and aquifer recharge work in the US Southwest.

**Amtrak opens Philadelphia concourse.** Amtrak has opened the renovated concourse at Philadelphia's 30th Street Station, Construction Dive reported on November 20, the latest completed piece of the passenger rail operator's station capital programme. No project cost was stated in the report.

**Routine filings from watched issuers.** Advanced Micro Devices filed an 8-K on November 20 recording a bylaws amendment (Item 5.03), and Oracle filed an 8-K the same day reporting its annual meeting vote (Item 5.07). Neither filing carries a transaction, financing or leadership change, and neither merits a story of its own; both are logged here so the filings lane's coverage is complete.
