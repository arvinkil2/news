ADT Inc. said on Thursday it has entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Thrive Holdings for $24.00 per share in cash, valuing ADT's equity at approximately $7.6 billion and the enterprise at approximately $11.7 billion including the refinancing of ADT's existing debt. The price is a premium of approximately 38 percent to ADT's closing share price on October 21, 2026, the last full trading day before media reports of a possible sale process, and of approximately 32 percent to its volume weighted average price over the 30 trading days to that date. ADT's board unanimously approved the agreement, and Thrive has obtained committed equity and debt financing, with the transaction not subject to a financing condition.

Closing is not expected until the second half of 2027, subject to ADT stockholder approval, clearance under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act and other regulatory approvals. If the deal is terminated in certain circumstances, including ADT accepting a superior proposal or its board changing its recommendation, ADT would pay Thrive a $260 million termination fee. Thrive Holdings is the permanent capital operating company controlled by Thrive Capital, the venture firm founded by Josh Kushner, and says it builds, acquires and operates businesses over long horizons, with holdings that have included Oscar Health and Cadence.
