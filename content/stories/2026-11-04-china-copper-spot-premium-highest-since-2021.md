Copper buyers in China are paying sharply more for metal they can take immediately. The spot premium in Shanghai reached 1,900 yuan, about 285 dollars, per ton over futures on Wednesday, the highest level since 2021, according to Shanghai Metals Market data cited by Bloomberg.

The squeeze is a supply story on the readable evidence: immediately available cathode is becoming scarcer as production curbs by Chinese smelters tighten supplies in the world's biggest consuming market.
