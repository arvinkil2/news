**Turkish paid employment rises 1.7 percent.** Paid employees in Turkey rose 1.7 percent year on year to 16,448,397 in September 2026, TurkStat reported, covering industry, construction and trade and services. The print adds a labour-market data point to a week in which emerging-market rate paths are being repriced alongside the dollar.

**Canadian vehicle sales climb 7.0 percent.** New motor vehicle sales in Canada rose 7.0 percent year on year in September 2026 to 180,457 units, Statistics Canada reported. The gain sits alongside the agency's October inflation release, covered in full on November 16, which showed consumer prices rising 3.1 percent year on year.

**UK government pledges to safeguard face-to-face banking.** HM Treasury committed the government to safeguard face-to-face banking services, as branch closures continue to shrink in-person access across the United Kingdom. The commitment is a consumer-access measure rather than a prudential one, but it lands in a week when UK labour and pay data, covered in full this morning, are driving the rate debate.
