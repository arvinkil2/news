Michael James Bostelle of Pennsylvania and Juan Cecilio Pena Carrillo of Texas were charged with wire fraud, money laundering and identity theft, and ten China based operators were also charged in the indictment. Prosecutors say US facilitators hosted company laptops at home and lent their names and personal information so the overseas workers appeared to be legitimate US based employees. The government has warned for years that North Korean workers use such jobs to raise money for Pyongyang and to gain access to company networks and data.
