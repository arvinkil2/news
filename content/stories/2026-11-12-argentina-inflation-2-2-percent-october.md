Argentina's consumer price index rose 2.2 percent in October from September, INDEC reported on Thursday, accelerating from 1.9 percent in September and coming in above the 1.8 percent median in the central bank's REM survey and private estimates of 1.8 to 2.1 percent. Prices were up 33.1 percent from a year earlier and 26.3 percent in the first ten months of the year.

Seasonal prices rose 5.5 percent and regulated prices 2.9 percent, led by housing and utilities, up 4.2 percent on gas tariff increases, while core inflation eased to 1.5 percent, which Economy Minister Luis Caputo noted was the lowest core reading since July 2025.
