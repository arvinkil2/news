A fire was reported at the Bashneft-Novoil refinery in Ufa, the capital of Russia's Bashkortostan region about 1,400 kilometres from the Ukrainian border, after drones attacked the city on Saturday morning. Residents reported several explosions in the industrial zone, a column of smoke and internet disruptions, and an analyst for the Russian outlet ASTRA, citing open-source analysis of eyewitness photos, said the refinery's L-35 catalytic reforming unit was likely burning; a Telegram monitoring channel reported fires at two of the three refineries in the city's northern industrial zone, which Russian authorities had not confirmed.

Ufa airport suspended operations after a drone hazard alert was declared in Bashkortostan, and there was no information on casualties. Rosneft-owned Bashneft runs three adjacent refineries in Ufa with combined capacity of 23.5 million tonnes of crude a year, and Bashneft-Novoil produces Euro-6 gasoline, diesel, jet fuel and industrial oils. The reported strike follows Ukraine's attack on the Novokuibyshevsk refinery early on Friday, which ended a two-week pause in the refinery campaign, and Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said on Friday that the resumed strikes could lead to a reduction in the diesel volumes Russia plans to export under its arrangement with the United States.
