HSBC said pretax profit rose 32 percent to 9.63 billion dollars in the third quarter, from 7.30 billion dollars a year earlier, helped by the absence of the 1.4 billion dollars in legal provisions booked in the same quarter last year. Revenue rose to 18.6 billion dollars, below an 18.93 billion dollar estimate, while net interest income rose 6 percent to 9.31 billion dollars and the bank kept its guidance for banking net interest income of at least 46 billion dollars this year.

The bank maintained its third interim dividend at 0.10 dollars per share, said its common equity tier 1 ratio stood at 13.9 percent at September 30, down from 14.1 percent at June 30, and said it remains confident of meeting the targets it set in February 2026. Chief executive Georges Elhedery said clients continue to turn to the bank in a more complex world and that simplifying HSBC is creating capacity to invest and return capital.
