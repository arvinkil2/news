Modal has secured 750 million dollars in a Series D round led by Accel, with Kleiner Perkins and existing investors including Menlo Ventures, General Catalyst and Redpoint taking part, at a post money valuation of 15.75 billion dollars. The raise comes five months after a 355 million dollar Series C at a 4.65 billion dollar valuation, and the company said usage has tripled since then and it has passed 1 billion dollars in annualised run rate. Modal sells serverless access to pooled GPU capacity across partner data centres rather than owning facilities itself.
