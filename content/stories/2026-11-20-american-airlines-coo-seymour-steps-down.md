American Airlines Group announced in a Form 8-K filed on Friday that David G. Seymour will cease serving as Chief Operating Officer of the company and of American Airlines, Inc. on December 31, 2026, and is expected to continue as a strategic advisor for up to three months afterward at a base salary of $25,000 per month. Seymour remains eligible for his 2026 annual bonus and, subject to a release of claims, for severance under his existing severance agreement.

JonCarlo Gulbranson, 49, will be appointed Executive Vice President, Chief Operations Officer effective January 1, 2027. Gulbranson currently serves as Senior Vice President, Operations, leading the airline's airport, flight and inflight operations, operations planning and analytics, product management and the Integrated Operations Center that oversees the day-to-day operation, a role he has held since August 2026. His compensation in the new role will include a base salary of $800,000, an annual incentive target of 125 percent of base salary, and a long-term incentive target award valued at $2,400,000 to be granted in 2027.
