President Donald Trump endorsed Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Sunday, writing on Truth Social that Netanyahu has been strong, brave and brilliant against tremendous opposition, that he is the kind of partner the United States is demanding of Israel, and that the post should serve as his complete and total endorsement of Netanyahu's re-election. Axios reported that most polls show Netanyahu's coalition falling short of the 61 seats needed to form a government, so the intervention lands at the point of his fight for political survival.

Netanyahu thanked Trump on social media, saying together they will complete the mission. Israel votes on October 27, its first election since the Hamas attack of October 7, 2023, and the result will either keep Netanyahu in power or replace him with the centrist rivals promising a new era.
