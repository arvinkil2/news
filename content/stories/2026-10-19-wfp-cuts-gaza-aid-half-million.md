The World Food Programme said it is being forced to reduce lifesaving assistance in Gaza because it received less than half as much funding for its Palestinian operations in 2026 as in previous years, and its Palestine director Shaun Hughes told AFP the cuts are driven by finances, not by falling need or lack of access. The number receiving flour bags and ration boxes each month falls from 1.1 million to 600,000, and hot meals from community kitchens fall from 250,000 to 150,000 a day, while the agency can for now keep supporting bakeries that provide a third of Gaza's bread and nutrition assistance to about 300,000 women and children. Hughes said the agency urgently needs 450 million dollars to maintain critical operations over the next six months.
