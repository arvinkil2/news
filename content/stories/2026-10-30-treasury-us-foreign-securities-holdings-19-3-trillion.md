US portfolio holdings of foreign securities were $19.3 trillion at year-end 2025, final survey data released by the Treasury Department on Friday showed, up from $15.8 trillion at year-end 2024. Equity accounted for $15.2 trillion of the total, against $3.6 trillion in long-term debt securities and $0.4 trillion in short-term debt, and Treasury said the increase over the year was mainly in equity.

By destination, the largest holdings were in the Cayman Islands at $3.1 trillion, followed by the United Kingdom at $2.0 trillion, Canada at $1.9 trillion and Japan at $1.5 trillion. The companion survey of foreign holdings of US securities, for end-June 2026, is still being processed, with preliminary results expected on February 26, 2027.
