Tanker rates have hit all time highs as Middle East producers tie up vessels in ship to ship transfers outside the Strait of Hormuz, with supertanker rates from the Gulf to Asia reaching 1.4 million dollars a day earlier this month. Freight from the US Gulf Coast to Europe now totals about 46 million dollars a voyage, or 24.50 dollars a barrel, against 6.5 million dollars before the war, and the Americas to Asia route costs over 80 million dollars.

Western Canadian Select fell to 74 dollars a barrel last week before recovering to 79.50 dollars, with Ecuador's Oriente and Colombia's Vasconia also down sharply, even as Russian Urals fetches close to 120 dollars. With few ships available and heavy grades in strong demand for diesel, refiners in Europe and Asia are holding back from purchases and the glut in the Americas looks set to persist.
