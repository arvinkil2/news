More than 60 people were killed in a series of attacks on Christians in three areas of the Shirka district in Ethiopia's Oromia region, according to the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, the BBC reported on Monday. The church's Holy Synod said 20 houses were burnt to the ground and many residents have fled the area, and an eyewitness told BBC Amharic that five of those killed were children. A church official described the attacks as a massacre and said security forces arrived but were unable to stop the assault.

A local official blamed a newly formed rebel coalition, the Alliance for Survival, which includes the Oromo Liberation Army; the Holy Synod statement did not say who was responsible, the army had not commented on these attacks in the BBC report, and it has denied carrying out previous such attacks. The regional government said it would take action against those responsible. The church said attacks against Christians had been increasing and that it had repeatedly raised insecurity concerns with authorities at different levels of government without, so far, a solution.
