The South African Reserve Bank and the People's Bank of China have renewed their bilateral currency swap agreement, effective from October 23, 2026 and valid for five years. The agreement allows the two central banks to exchange local currencies of up to 30 billion yuan (74 billion rand), supporting trade and direct investment between South Africa and China and mitigating short-term balance of payments pressures; interest on any rand funds used under the arrangement is referenced to the South African Rand Overnight Index Average (ZARONIA). The renminbi size is unchanged from the 2021 renewal, when the rand leg was 68 billion rand, and the Reserve Bank said the renewal builds on the 2013 agreement that allows it to invest in China's onshore bond market.
