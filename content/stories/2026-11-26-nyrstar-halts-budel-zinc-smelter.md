Nyrstar, the zinc producer owned by commodities group Trafigura, said on Thursday it will stop zinc production at its Budel plant in the Netherlands on November 30 in an operational cost-saving measure, citing significant increases in the site's energy costs against continued challenging market conditions for zinc smelters. Chief executive Matt Hadfield said the decision shows the difficult choices the industry faces and called on policymakers to act to retain strategic processing capacity, adding that a strategic review of Budel remains ongoing. The company said the site and its assets will be maintained safely and that no redundancies among permanent employees are expected as a direct result of the stop.
