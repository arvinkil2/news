Marlowe, an AI cloud startup founded in May 2026 by former Microsoft vice presidents Dan Lewis and Kutta Srinivasan, has emerged from stealth with 277 million dollars in seed funding led by Accel, Bessemer, Altimeter, Atreides, Eclipse, Journey and Valor. The company describes itself as a multi-silicon AI factory for inference, has signed compute partnerships with AMD and Etched, and says it has two projects under contract, the first expected to go live within months and the second in the first half of 2027, with three inference customers signed. It has offices in Redwood City, California, and Bellevue, Washington. This item is single-sourced to Datacenter Dynamics.

CISA published two further industrial control systems advisories on November 24 alongside the Danfoss advisory covered in full today: ICSA-26-328-02 for Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Power Build Rapsody software and ICSA-26-328-01 for AutomationDirect C-More EA9 programming software. Both are primary CISA documents and are linked in this story's sources.
