UK public sector borrowing was £17.0 billion in October 2026, £0.8 billion more than a year earlier and the third highest October since monthly records began in 1993, as central government debt interest payable jumped 33.4 percent to £11.2 billion, the highest October figure since monthly records began in 1997, the Office for National Statistics said. Of that interest bill, £4.2 billion was the inflation related uplift on index linked gilts, and October borrowing was also £0.8 billion above the Office for Budget Responsibility's March 2026 monthly forecast.

Borrowing in the financial year to October was £111.3 billion, £7.3 billion less than in the same period last year and equivalent to 3.5 percent of GDP, while public sector net debt was provisionally estimated at £3,058.3 billion at the end of October, 95.8 percent of GDP, 1.9 percentage points higher than a year earlier and at levels last seen in the early 1960s.
