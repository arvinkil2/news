Japan's Kansai Electric Power has agreed to acquire a 40 percent interest in the 390 MW BC-Wind offshore wind development in Poland from Ocean Winds, marking its first investment in the country. The share purchase agreement was signed on November 19, and the purchase will be made through Kansai's wholly owned subsidiary KPIC Netherlands. Ocean Winds, the joint venture between EDP Renewables and Engie, will retain 60 percent of the project company, C-Wind Polska.

BC-Wind sits about 23 kilometres off Poland's Baltic coast and will use 26 Siemens Gamesa turbines of up to 15 MW each. Generation is expected to start in 2028, supplying around 500,000 Polish households. Ocean Winds reached financial close on the project in December 2025 with approximately €2 billion (about $2.2 billion) in project financing.

Construction is already contracted: DEME holds the foundation and subsea cable installation work, Seaway 7 the transition piece and offshore substation installation, and Cadeler the turbine transport and installation in 2028, with foundation installation due to begin in the first half of 2027. Kansai said the acquisition takes the attributable capacity of its overseas power generation portfolio to approximately 2.65 GW on completion. No purchase price was disclosed.
