Equinor said on Tuesday it had detected an ongoing cyberattack against certain IT systems and had mobilised resources to neutralise the attack and regain full integrity. The company said there are no indications that data has been stolen, that the attack has not affected its operations and production, and that relevant authorities are being informed.

Reuters reported that the Norwegian news agency NTB, citing the company, said disruptions were affecting Equinor's use of IT applications and internal systems, and that the company's internal email system was down, according to a person at the company who spoke on condition of anonymity. The Norwegian National Security Authority said it was aware of the incident and providing assistance as needed, and noted it had raised its cyberattack risk assessment last month to four on a five-level scale.
