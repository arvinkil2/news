Amazon Web Services is planning a data-center campus of 36 buildings in Indiana County, Pennsylvania, according to Data Center Dynamics reporting on October 6. Details on the planned capacity, power sourcing and construction timeline were not in the initial report.

The project lands in a crowded week for AI-infrastructure announcements — Applied Digital's 1-gigawatt Finland campus, Google's 3.6-gigawatt Constellation nuclear deal, TeraWulf's 1-gigawatt Kentucky target — and it fits the pattern of hyperscalers assembling capacity at county scale. Pennsylvania's appeal is the usual combination: land, transmission access and a PJM market where bring-your-own-power deals are becoming the norm.
