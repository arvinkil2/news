Duration Gold Limited and China Railway Resources Group Co. Ltd (CRRG) signed a memorandum of understanding in Harare on Thursday for the joint development of Duration Gold's four Zimbabwe projects: the Vubachikwe Mine, the Gaika and Athens projects at Kwekwe, and the Filabusi project. The companies put the proposed investment at more than $600 million and said it could create more than 5,000 permanent jobs, but the memorandum sets a framework for further engagement and joint development rather than confirming the money has been committed.

CRRG is the mineral-resources arm of China Railway Group, with existing mining assets in China and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Duration Gold, a Jersey-incorporated company backed by international institutional investors, said the four projects have recorded initial gold production and are supported by recently completed resource reports under the JORC Code and positive scoping studies.
