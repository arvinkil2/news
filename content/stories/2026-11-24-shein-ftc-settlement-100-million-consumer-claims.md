Shein will pay $100 million to settle with the US Federal Trade Commission over claims it misled consumers on shipping times, product reviews and the origin of cotton in some of its goods, according to court documents filed on Tuesday, Reuters reported. The FTC said Shein marketed fast shipping while knowing packages arrived days or months late or not at all, filtered out negative product reviews under internal guidance, made unsubstantiated supply-chain claims for its evoluSHEIN label, and used cotton-origin testing that was often inaccurate or inconclusive; the settlement funds will reimburse customers. A Shein spokesperson said the company had not admitted liability and disagreed with some of the claims, and Shein agreed not to misrepresent shipping times or block negative reviews.
