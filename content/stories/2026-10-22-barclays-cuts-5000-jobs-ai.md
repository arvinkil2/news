Barclays will cut about 5,000 jobs this year as artificial intelligence takes over more back and middle office work, the bank said alongside third quarter results in which pre-tax profit rose 20 percent to 2.6 billion pounds. More than half of the 5,000 roles have already gone, with a further 2,000 to go by the end of the year, and the bank said it is targeting 500 million pounds of savings from AI by 2028, with some staff redeployed to other parts of the business.

Chief executive C.S. Venkatakrishnan said AI is making the bank more productive, and group finance director Anna Cross said the rollout is centred on back and middle office and operations functions, in areas such as fraud and financial crime.
