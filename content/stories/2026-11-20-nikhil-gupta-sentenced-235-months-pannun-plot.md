US District Judge Victor Marrero sentenced Nikhil Gupta, 55, to 235 months in prison in Manhattan federal court on Friday, at the low end of the 235 to 293 month guideline range, after Gupta pleaded guilty in February to murder-for-hire, conspiracy to commit murder-for-hire and conspiracy to commit money laundering. Prosecutors said Gupta was recruited in May 2023 by Indian intelligence officer Vikash Yadav to arrange the killing of Gurpatwant Singh Pannun, a US-Canadian citizen and Sikhs for Justice lawyer who advocates a sovereign Sikh state, and that $15,000 in cash was delivered to a supposed hitman who was in fact an undercover US law enforcement officer.

Gupta was arrested in Prague in June 2023 and extradited to the United States, and the judge said he will likely be deported after serving his sentence. India has denied any involvement in the plot and calls Pannun a terrorist, which he denies; US and Canadian authorities have linked the case to a broader effort against Sikh dissidents, including the 2023 killing of Hardeep Singh Nijjar in British Columbia, which India also denies involvement in.
