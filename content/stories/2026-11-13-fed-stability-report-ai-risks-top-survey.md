Artificial intelligence topped the Federal Reserve's survey of salient risks in its November Financial Stability Report, cited by 87 percent of the 23 market contacts surveyed in September and October, up from 50 percent in the spring survey. Respondents named three AI concerns: elevated equity valuations that could fall if earnings disappoint, cybersecurity risk from state actors or from frontier AI models themselves, and risks in complex forms of AI related financing. The next most cited risks were a correction in risk assets at 52 percent, higher long term rates at 48 percent, and cyberattacks and fiscal debt sustainability at 43 percent each, with the report also noting that hedge fund leverage reached its highest level since 2013 and stayed concentrated in the largest funds.
