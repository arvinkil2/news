Invesco Real Estate has reached the final close of Asia Fund V at $1.35 billion of total commitments, its largest closed-end raise for the region. The total comprises $950 million for the main fund and $400 million of co-investment sidecar capital, finishing $100 million above the $1.25 billion target and $200 million above the predecessor fund, which closed in 2021.

South Korea's National Pension Service anchored the raise with a reported $400 million commitment split evenly between the main fund and the sidecar. Half of the fund's limited partners had backed earlier vehicles in the series, and about a third of the capital is already deployed, including into student housing and senior living.

Launched in May 2024, Fund V targets value-add and opportunistic property in developed Asia-Pacific markets, principally Japan, Australia and South Korea. The stated focus is living sectors such as student and senior housing, with data centres and distressed credit also among its targets.

Invesco's real estate arm manages about $89 billion. The close matters less for its size than for its composition: a Korean public pension anchoring an Asia value-add vehicle, repeat limited partners supplying half the book, and deployment already a third done at final close, which is the pattern of a manager raising against a visible pipeline rather than a blind pool.
