South Korea has asked Qatar to prioritise LNG supply to South Korea when traffic through the Strait of Hormuz normalises, the country's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources said on Monday. Trade minister Kim Jung-kwan made the request in Seoul to Qatar's minister of state for energy affairs, Saad Sherida al-Kaabi, asking for priority access to Qatar's gas once Qatari LNG production and navigation through Hormuz return to normal, OilPrice.com reported.

QatarEnergy has extended force majeure on deliveries every month since April, with the latest extension running until at least the end of December 2026, and few Qatari LNG cargoes have transited Hormuz since March. South Korea, the world's third-largest LNG buyer after China and Japan, has leaned more on coal-fired generation as spot LNG prices rose, and its climate and energy minister said last month that in the short term the country must somehow reduce LNG demand.
