Eaton reported record third quarter sales of $8.9 billion, up 28 percent from a year earlier, with organic growth of 19 percent, above the high end of its guidance, record segment margins of 25.3 percent and adjusted earnings per share of $3.68, also a record. The company raised its full year guidance, to organic growth of 13 to 15 percent and adjusted earnings per share of $13.70 to $13.80, and said total backlog grew 46 percent in its Electrical business and 33 percent in Aerospace.
