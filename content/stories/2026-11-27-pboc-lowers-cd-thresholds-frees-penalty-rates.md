The People's Bank of China on Friday published revised rules for large-denomination certificates of deposit that cut the minimum subscription to 100,000 yuan for individual investors, down from 200,000 yuan, and to 5 million yuan for institutions, alongside new requirements governing the market for transferring the certificates between holders. In a second revision, covering RMB deposit and loan interest rates, the central bank removed the prescribed band for penalty interest on overdue loans, previously set at 30 to 50 percent above the contract rate, leaving the penalty rate, calculation method and grace period to be negotiated between lender and borrower. The central bank said it expects penalty rates to fall overall under the new arrangement, and the rate provisions take effect after a transition period of about four months.
