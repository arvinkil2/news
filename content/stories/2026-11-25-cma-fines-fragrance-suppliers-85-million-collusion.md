The Competition and Markets Authority has fined Firmenich, Givaudan and International Flavors and Fragrances a combined 85 million pounds for breaking competition law, with Givaudan fined 39,960,000 pounds, International Flavors and Fragrances 24,570,000 pounds and Firmenich 20,640,000 pounds. The CMA found the companies shared sensitive information through WhatsApp messages, calls and in-person meetings to coordinate pricing and responses to commercial requests from Unilever, including price decreases, and agreed not to hire each other's staff working on the Unilever account, while Givaudan and International Flavors and Fragrances also coordinated the level and timing of general price increases.

All three companies admitted breaking the law, approached the CMA for leniency and settled, so they cannot appeal the decision, the CMA said. The European Commission ran a parallel investigation and issued its own decision imposing fines for breaches of EU law the same day.
