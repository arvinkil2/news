## Latest

Rescuers will resume the search on Thursday for 13 workers still trapped underground at an illegal gold mine in Bogor regency, West Java, with 13 miners now confirmed dead, Reuters reported, citing police and rescue authorities. A total of 26 workers entered the mine on Monday when heavy rains inundated the shaft and left them trapped about 100 metres underground. West Java police chief Hengki told Reuters that six of the 13 fatalities have been identified, and that the search would continue Thursday barring bad weather. Indonesia's search and rescue agency Basarnas said rescuers are focusing on two mine shafts and using ropes to gain access, with the depth and the narrow, slippery, dark conditions complicating the work.

Rescuers recovered nine bodies on Wednesday from an unauthorized gold mine in Bogor regency, West Java, and were searching for 17 other miners believed trapped after heavy rain late Monday caused the Ciseureuh River to overflow into a shaft estimated at about 100 metres deep, the Associated Press reported. Eleven miners escaped, officials said, and police first received reports of trapped miners about 16 hours after the flooding because the operation was illegal; a search involving 325 rescuers is pumping water from the shafts, where all nine bodies were found at depths of 30 to 70 metres.

The flooded workings sit near PT Antam's Pongkor gold unit, but Antam said the incident occurred at an unauthorized mine unrelated to its operations, that all its employees were safe, and that it is supporting the rescue on humanitarian grounds.
