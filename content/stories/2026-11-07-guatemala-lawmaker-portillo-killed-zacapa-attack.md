Guatemalan lawmaker Julio César Portillo Paz was attacked on Friday in Río Hondo, in the eastern department of Zacapa, and died after being taken to hospital with serious injuries, Reuters reported, with his death confirmed by his party, the National Unity of Hope (UNE). Three National Civil Police officers were also killed, and President Bernardo Arévalo said they died in the line of duty and ordered security forces to cooperate with prosecutors investigating the killings, adding that criminal violence would not be allowed to prevail.

Authorities have not disclosed a motive or identified who was responsible. The killing comes about three weeks after former anti-corruption prosecutor Juan Francisco Solórzano Foppa was shot dead in Guatemala City, a case that heightened concern over organised crime's influence and a possible return of politically motivated assassinations, though authorities have not established a connection between the two attacks.
