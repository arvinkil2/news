The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC) has identified two suspected cases of Ebola in Nigeria's North-East region, and both individuals have been isolated while laboratory testing is conducted, the agency said in an update posted on X on Saturday. Contact identification and listing are ongoing, and the agency stressed that a suspected classification does not mean either person has Ebola, with confirmation subject to the laboratory results.

The announcement lands against a tight official timeline: Nigeria previously recorded an imported Bundibugyo strain case in a Ugandan-born woman who was evacuated to Switzerland with her husband, and the federal government is targeting a December Ebola free declaration that requires 42 days without additional confirmed or probable cases. The suspected cases also come as the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo has become that country's deadliest, with more than 5,000 deaths reported.
