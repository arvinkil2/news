Latest: The gunman acted alone, Krakow police spokesperson Katarzyna Cislo told TVN24, in remarks carried by the Press Association. She said the perpetrator was wearing military-style fatigues and used a military-type long gun, and that police found many other weapons, as well as ammunition, in his car. Police officers are among the wounded, and a tram driver reported his vehicle was shot at by a man in a moving car near the square, shattering the window of his cabin, the Krakow municipal transport company said. President Karol Nawrocki said his thoughts were with the victims, the wounded and their families.

Polish police said five people were killed and more than 10 injured on Saturday when shots were fired at random people from a car that had entered Krakow's main market square, with the first reports reaching police at about 3 p.m. local time. Police said officers shot the perpetrator, who was being taken to hospital, while France 24 reported the shooter was caught while trying to escape. Prime Minister Donald Tusk wrote on X that police are investigating the shooting and that he is being briefed by the interior ministry, and no motive has been stated.
