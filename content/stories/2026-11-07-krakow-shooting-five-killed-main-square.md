Polish police said five people were killed and more than 10 injured on Saturday when shots were fired at random people from a car that had entered Krakow's main market square, with the first reports reaching police at about 3 p.m. local time. Police said officers shot the perpetrator, who was being taken to hospital, while France 24 reported the shooter was caught while trying to escape. Prime Minister Donald Tusk wrote on X that police are investigating the shooting and that he is being briefed by the interior ministry, and no motive has been stated.
