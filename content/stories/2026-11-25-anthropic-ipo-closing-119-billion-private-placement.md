Anthropic said in an SEC filing on Wednesday that it completed its initial public offering on November 20, selling 1,127,000,000 shares of Class A common stock at $83.00 per share, including the full exercise of the underwriters' option for 147,000,000 additional shares. Of the total, 1,103,351,354 shares were sold by the company and 23,648,646 by selling stockholders, whose proceeds the company did not receive.

Immediately after the IPO closed, Anthropic completed a concurrent private placement of 313,253,007 shares of non-voting Class N common stock at the IPO price to Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners, raising approximately $26.0 billion with no underwriting discounts or commissions. Combined gross proceeds from the IPO and the private placement were approximately $119.5 billion before offering expenses, and the filing also records one Class F share issued to Anthropic Founders, LLC carrying 50.1 percent of the company's total voting power.
