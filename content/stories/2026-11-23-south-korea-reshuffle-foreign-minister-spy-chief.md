President Lee Jae Myung on Monday named Second Deputy National Security Adviser Lim Woong-soon as foreign minister, replacing Cho Hyun, and former Justice Minister Kang Kum-sil to head the National Intelligence Service, Yonhap reported. The reshuffle also named former lawmaker Hong Ihk-pyo as unification minister, Democratic Party lawmaker Han Jun-ho as interior minister and senior presidential spokesperson Kang Yu-jung as culture minister. Presidential chief of staff Lim Kwang-hyun said the changes were meant to speed up state affairs, and that the government's principles, including the South Korea-U.S. alliance and peace on the Korean Peninsula, remain unchanged.
