A magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck near Chaparral, Tolima, at 4:13 a.m. local time on Tuesday, the Colombian Geological Survey said in a preliminary bulletin, at a depth of less than 30 kilometres. The shaking was felt in Bogotá, Ibagué, Medellín and Cali, thousands of residents left their homes in several cities, and a magnitude 3.3 tremor followed at 4:16 a.m. Authorities had not reported major structural damage or casualties.

Agency readings differ while the figures settle: the European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre put the quake at magnitude 5.5, at 35 kilometres depth about 77 kilometres from Tulua, after first estimating 5.8, Reuters reported. The survey's closest municipalities to the epicentre were Rocesvalles and San Antonio in Tolima.
