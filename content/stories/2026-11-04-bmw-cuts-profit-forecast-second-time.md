BMW cut its 2026 automotive EBIT margin forecast to a corridor of -0.5 percent to 0.5 percent, from 1 percent to 3 percent, and its automotive return on capital employed forecast to -1 percent to 1 percent, from 1 percent to 5 percent, while leaving automotive free cash flow guidance unchanged at above 2.5 billion euros. The board expanded and intensified its portfolio and structural measures, which BMW said will have a non-operating effect on automotive earnings in the fourth quarter even though operating performance stayed solid in the third quarter; the restructuring includes about 8,000 job cuts, and deliveries guidance moved to a moderate decrease from a slight decrease. To counter weak demand in China, BMW has added factory shifts to speed the production ramp of its Neue Klasse models and will phase out earlier vehicle and battery generations sooner.
