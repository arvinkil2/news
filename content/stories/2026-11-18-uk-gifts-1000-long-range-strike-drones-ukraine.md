Britain will gift Ukraine more than 1,000 long range strike drones as part of a new 260 million pound package, Prime Minister Andy Burnham is to announce in Berlin, where he co-chairs a Coalition of the Willing meeting. The release says the package includes strike drones able to carry heavy warheads and fly more than 1,000 kilometres, alongside maritime, logistical and surveillance drones to resupply front line forces, and that more than 80 percent of the funding will be spent in the United Kingdom.

The announcement follows a British air defence package at the Ukraine Defence Contact Group on Tuesday and, the government says, comes after more than 2,000 Russian attacks on Ukrainian energy infrastructure, with daily outages reaching 16 hours in multiple regions and up to 22 hours in Kyiv. Twelve UK based defence firms have also been given access to Ukraine's Avengers AI Labs battlefield data platform, chosen from 92 applicants.
