The Board of Directors of the Bank of Canada announced on Monday that Governor Tiff Macklem has informed them he will not seek a second term as Governor when his seven year term expires on June 2, 2027. The board has begun a search process to select the next governor.

"It has been the highlight of my career to serve as the 10th Governor of the Bank of Canada," Macklem said. "My time as Governor has seen Canada navigate a more fragmented, shock-prone world. The Bank has faced these challenges by acting decisively, by using sound judgment in the face of uncertainty, and by serving Canadians with humility and a steadfast commitment to its mandate." He said he remains fully committed to fulfilling his responsibilities through the remainder of his term.

The bank set out the succession process in the same release. The independent directors on the board have formed a search committee to run the recruitment, assisted by the executive recruiting firm Egon Zehnder, and the process is expected to be completed by spring 2027. Subject to the approval of the Minister of Finance and the federal Cabinet, the board's independent directors will appoint the next governor for a seven year term beginning June 3, 2027.
