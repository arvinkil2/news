OpenAI filed counterclaims in California federal court on Friday accusing Apple of stealing its trade secrets, saying a former employee who spent six months at OpenAI, with access to a new device design and the company's hardware roadmap, was re-hired by Apple and that taking those documents may have been his reason for joining OpenAI in the first place. OpenAI also alleges Apple encourages staff to work on personal Apple devices and accounts, then blocks an orderly separation of work and personal data when they leave, and says 400 of its staff have come from Apple.

The filing answers Apple's July lawsuit, which accused OpenAI and two former employees of misappropriating Apple's trade secrets for its consumer hardware push, allegations OpenAI denies. Neither company immediately commented to Reuters on the counterclaims.
