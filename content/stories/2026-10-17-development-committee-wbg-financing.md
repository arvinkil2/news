The Development Committee, the joint ministerial committee of the World Bank Group and the IMF, met on October 15 in Bangkok and said the global economy has weathered the latest series of shocks better than expected, while warning that continued resilience should not be taken for granted and that wars and supply chain disruptions fall hardest on the poorest. The chair's statement noted total financing using World Bank Group resources reached $124 billion in fiscal 2026, with private capital mobilisation rising to $112 billion, and pointed to social safety net programmes benefiting 234 million people and electricity access for 59 million under Mission 300. Members reiterated support for IDA ahead of its 22nd replenishment, welcomed the refreshed fragility and conflict strategy, and thanked outgoing chair Elisabeth Svantesson of Sweden, whose successor is Japan's finance minister, Satsuki Katayama. The next meeting is in April 2027 in Washington.
