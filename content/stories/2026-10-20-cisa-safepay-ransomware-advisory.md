CISA and partner agencies in the United States, Australia, Canada, New Zealand and the United Kingdom have issued a joint #StopRansomware advisory on SafePay, a ransomware operation that emerged in 2024 and combines data theft with encryption to pressure victims into paying. The advisory says the group typically gains access with phished credentials against remote desktop and VPN services, moves laterally with standard attacker tooling, and exfiltrates data before deploying its encryptor. It urges organisations to enforce phishing-resistant multifactor authentication, patch edge devices promptly and keep tested offline backups.
