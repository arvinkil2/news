The FBI, CISA, the US Secret Service, Germany's Landeskriminalamt Rheinland-Pfalz and the United Kingdom's National Crime Agency have issued a joint #StopRansomware advisory on SafePay, a ransomware operation active since August 2024 that combines data theft with encryption to pressure victims into paying, and which the agencies say has affected at least 600 victims across critical infrastructure sectors. The advisory says the group typically gains access by stealing or guessing passwords, brute-force attacks, phishing and exploiting VPN services, moves laterally with standard attacker tooling, and exfiltrates data before deploying its encryptor. It urges organisations to require multifactor authentication, prioritise patching known exploited vulnerabilities in internet-facing systems, and maintain offline, encrypted backups that are regularly tested.
