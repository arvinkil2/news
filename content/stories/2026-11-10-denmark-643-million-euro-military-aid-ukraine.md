Denmark will send Ukraine a new package of military equipment worth 4.8 billion Danish kroner, about 643 million euros, Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen announced after a meeting of parliament's Foreign Policy Committee on Tuesday, Ukrainska Pravda reported, citing European Pravda and the Danish broadcaster DR. The announcement came during President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's visit to Copenhagen, where he was due to meet Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen and other Nordic leaders.

The package is Denmark's 27th since Russia's full-scale invasion, and it takes Copenhagen's total support for Ukraine to about 64 billion kroner, 8.6 billion euros. Rasmussen said that when Europe invests in Ukraine, it invests in its own security.
