Senator Chris Murphy of Connecticut suffered a stroke on Saturday afternoon after feeling numbness in his arm at his son's hockey game, his office announced on Sunday. He was taken to a local hospital and then transferred to Johns Hopkins for further monitoring, where he is awake and alert and responding well to treatment, according to his chief of staff, Allison Herwitt. Murphy, 53, is not up for reelection on Tuesday, but he had been campaigning for Democratic candidates in several states and was due in New Hampshire on Sunday.
