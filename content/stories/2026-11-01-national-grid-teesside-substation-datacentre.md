National Grid agreed last week to purchase 73 acres of land at Dorman Point, on a site owned by Teesworks, the local public and private partnership, in a step towards building Britain's biggest electricity substation on Teesside, the Guardian reported. The substation could supply power to a neighbouring datacentre, for which planning applications covering nearly 500,000 square metres were reported last year, and Tees Valley mayor Ben Houchen told Sky News the site could offer up to 3.2 gigawatts of capacity. Balfour Beatty was reported to be in line to deliver the substation for National Grid, with Steel River Power also heavily involved, and neither company commented in the report.
