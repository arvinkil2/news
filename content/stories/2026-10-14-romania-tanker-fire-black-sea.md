A ship anchored in Romania's territorial waters caught fire on Wednesday and its crew was evacuated, Romanian authorities said, identifying the vessel as the Comoros flagged chemical and oil tanker Yara J. Defence Minister Radu Miruta said it was unclear whether a drone had hit the ship and that an investigation was pending, on a day when multiple drones also breached Romanian airspace near the border with Ukraine. The incident follows drone attacks on two ships off Bulgaria's coast on Tuesday, one of which sank, and the sinking of a grain ship outside Romanian waters earlier this month in which two people were killed, a pattern Bulgaria's president has proposed addressing through consultations among Bulgaria, Romania and Turkiye on merchant ship security in the Black Sea.
