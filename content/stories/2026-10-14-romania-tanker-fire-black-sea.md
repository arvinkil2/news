**Latest (06:42 ET Thursday):** A second chemical tanker, the San Marino flagged Wine Trader, caught fire overnight near the mouth of the Sulina Canal, and Romania's naval authority said eight of its 12 crew were missing with four safe as rescuers searched on Thursday. Both ships were anchored inside Romanian territorial waters near the canal entrance, where vessels wait to enter the Danube route Ukraine relies on as Russia attacks its Black Sea ports. Romanian officials stopped short of explicitly linking the fires to Russian drones that had strayed into Romanian airspace, and the cause of either fire has not been established. The Yara J, in ballast and preparing to enter the Danube, put out its own fire without outside help and all its crew were reported safe; Defence Minister Radu Miruta said military radar had not detected a drone approaching the ship, and the tanker is expected to be inspected at Constanta.

The overnight fires extend a run of five reported drone attacks on merchant shipping off Romania and Bulgaria since October 5, three of them sinkings, which Splash247 noted had all occurred in the two countries' exclusive economic zones rather than inside territorial waters, as these did. Russia's defence ministry said on Wednesday its forces had struck a dry cargo ship heading for Odesa and on Thursday morning claimed attacks on two more Odesa bound cargo vessels and on infrastructure at the Danube port of Izmail; those claims have not been independently verified and the ships were not identified. Romania scrambled jets repeatedly, including Spanish F-18s and Romanian F-16s after another drone was detected over its territorial waters southeast of Sulina at 01:34 on Thursday, and Bulgaria's foreign minister Velislava Petrova-Chamova said Bulgaria, Romania and Turkiye are building an initiative to protect commercial shipping, with a planned Black Sea Maritime Security Centre operating from Varna and Constanta.

A ship anchored in Romania's territorial waters caught fire on Wednesday and its crew was evacuated, Romanian authorities said, identifying the vessel as the Comoros flagged chemical and oil tanker Yara J. Defence Minister Radu Miruta said it was unclear whether a drone had hit the ship and that an investigation was pending, on a day when multiple drones also breached Romanian airspace near the border with Ukraine. The incident follows drone attacks on two ships off Bulgaria's coast on Tuesday, one of which sank, and the sinking of a grain ship outside Romanian waters earlier this month in which two people were killed, a pattern Bulgaria's president has proposed addressing through consultations among Bulgaria, Romania and Turkiye on merchant ship security in the Black Sea.
