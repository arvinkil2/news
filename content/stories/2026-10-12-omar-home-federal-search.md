Homeland Security Investigations agents raided the Washington-area home of Rep. Ilhan Omar, D-Minn., in the early hours of Monday under a search warrant secured by the Justice Department, Fox News Digital reported in an exclusive citing a source familiar with the matter. A DOJ spokesperson confirmed that HSI "executed a judicially authorized search warrant" as part of "an ongoing federal investigation," and multiple agents wearing HSI jackets were seen outside the home.

The probe centers on a criminal fraud investigation into immigration paperwork filed by Omar's late father when she was a child, according to two sources close to the investigation. Border czar Tom Homan had said in mid-September that Omar was subject to an ongoing "criminal" investigation. Fox notes the story is developing and no charges have been announced.
