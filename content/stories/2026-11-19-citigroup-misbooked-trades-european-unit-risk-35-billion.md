Citigroup incorrectly recorded trades at a European subsidiary, understating the trading unit's risk-weighted assets by about $35 billion, Reuters reported on Thursday, citing two people familiar with the matter. The report did not name the subsidiary or say over what period the trades were misbooked, and it reported no regulator action.

Citigroup told Reuters the subsidiary remained fully compliant with regulations and that the bank had identified and resolved a capital modeling issue at the unit.
