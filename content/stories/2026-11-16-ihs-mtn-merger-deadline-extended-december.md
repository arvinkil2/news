IHS Holding and MTN Group have agreed to extend the deadline to close MTN's acquisition of the tower company by one month, moving the end date from 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on November 17, 2026 to the same time on December 17, 2026, IHS said in a Form 6-K filed on Monday. The two companies signed the letter agreement on November 14 under the merger agreement's extension provisions, saying the extra time is needed to consummate the merger once the conditions to closing are satisfied or waived.

Shareholders approved the merger agreement, first signed on February 17, 2026, at an extraordinary general meeting on August 4. Under the deal, an MTN merger subsidiary will merge into IHS, with IHS continuing as the surviving company. The filing does not say which closing conditions remain outstanding.
