Ecopetrol has sold 2 billion dollars of SEC-registered bonds due 2034 at a 7.98 percent yield, priced just inside the roughly 8 percent level at which the notes were marketed, according to IFR. The proceeds will buy back some outstanding bonds and fund general corporate purposes, including internal loans that repay part of the bridge financing used for the Brava Energia acquisition, Moody's Ratings said.

The deal is Ecopetrol's first return to the cross-border bond market in two years and the first step in the long-term refinancing the company promised when it took control of Brava in August, initially funded with a bridge facility of about 1.2 billion dollars. Brava-related financing has raised Ecopetrol's net debt by about 1.1 billion dollars since June 30, and Brava's roughly 3.7 billion dollars of debt moved onto its balance sheet on consolidation.
