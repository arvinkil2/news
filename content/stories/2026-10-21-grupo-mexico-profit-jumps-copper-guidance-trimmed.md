Grupo Mexico's third quarter net profit rose 56 percent from a year earlier to 2 billion dollars, as revenue climbed 38 percent to 6.33 billion dollars and earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation rose 50.5 percent to 3.77 billion dollars, beating the 1.78 billion dollar profit and 5.81 billion dollar revenue estimates of analysts polled by LSEG. Higher metal prices drove the result even as copper production fell 1.9 percent to 260,322 tons, with declines in Peru and at Asarco in the United States partly offset by higher output in Mexico.

The group lowered its 2026 copper production guidance to 1.026 million metric tons from a previous forecast of 1.034 million tons.
