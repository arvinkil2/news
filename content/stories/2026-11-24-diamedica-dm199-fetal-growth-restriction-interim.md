DiaMedica Therapeutics disclosed interim topline results on November 24 from an investigator sponsored Phase 2 study of DM199 (rinvecalinase alfa) in severe early onset fetal growth restriction, conducted at Tygerberg Hospital in Cape Town, South Africa. The first two cohorts covered 12 participants dosed subcutaneously at 5 and 10 micrograms per kilogram.

The company said 9 of 10 participants with known delivery dates remained pregnant longer than the published median expected time to delivery based on their baseline umbilical artery Doppler abnormalities. DM199 appeared well tolerated, with no serious adverse events reported among the 12 treated participants. DiaMedica presented the results at a key opinion leader webinar on November 24.

The design limits the conclusion. This is an open label, single arm, proof of concept study with no control group, and the comparison is against published historical medians, not against randomized patients. Twelve participants cannot establish efficacy, and pregnancy prolongation against a historical benchmark is a signal to test, not a result to price as proof.

The program continues: a further cohort is planned at 15 micrograms per kilogram, and DM199 is also in the ReMEDy2 Phase 2/3 trial in acute ischemic stroke, whose interim analysis is expected in the first quarter of 2027.
