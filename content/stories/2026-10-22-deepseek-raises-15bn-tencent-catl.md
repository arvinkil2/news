DeepSeek, the Hangzhou based artificial intelligence developer, has raised about 100 billion yuan, roughly $15 billion, after lifting its fundraising target at least twice, from an initial 50 billion yuan to 80 billion and then to the final total, Bloomberg News reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Tencent and battery maker Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., known as CATL, were among the largest investors in the round.

The raise follows a first external round of more than $6.8 billion in June and, if the reported figures hold, takes DeepSeek's total capital raised this year above $20 billion. Reports say the money is earmarked for model research and computing power, and that the company is restructuring ahead of a possible initial public offering on Shanghai's Star Market.
