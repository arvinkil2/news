France and the United Kingdom carried out a joint airborne nuclear-strike exercise for the first time, with Royal Air Force Eurofighter Typhoons escorting French Rafale jets on a simulated nuclear-strike mission over the Mediterranean on Tuesday, the French Armed Forces Ministry said on Friday. The crews, flying from Saint-Dizier air base in northeastern France with A330 MRTT tanker support in exercise Rummy, used no actual nuclear weapons, and Armed Forces Minister Catherine Vautrin said the coordination showed the two independent nuclear forces can contribute more to alliance security together. The ministry said the exercise was an important step in implementing the Northwood Declaration, last year's agreement under which the two countries said there is no extreme threat to Europe that would not prompt a response by both.
