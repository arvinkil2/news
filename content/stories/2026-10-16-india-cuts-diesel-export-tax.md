India has cut its export duty on diesel to 8.5 rupees per litre from 16 rupees for the two weeks starting October 16, and raised the gasoline export duty to 2.5 rupees per litre from 0.5 rupees, OilPrice.com reported. The jet fuel export duty was left unchanged at 10.5 rupees per litre, and duties on domestic gasoline and diesel sales were not changed.

India reviews the export levies every two weeks. The duties were first imposed in March, during the Middle East crisis that halted shipments through the Strait of Hormuz, to protect domestic fuel supply, and the lower diesel levy could encourage additional exports into a tight global diesel market.
