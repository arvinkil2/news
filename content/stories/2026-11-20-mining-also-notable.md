**Tudor rotates from copper to precious metals.** Paul Tudor Jones's firm has dumped copper miners and doubled down on gold and silver, Mining.com reported, a positioning shift that matches the week's price action, in which copper fell about 6 percent to its lowest since April while investors unwound AI-demand and tariff bets. The rotation runs against physical indicators in China, where the Yangshan copper import premium reached a record $360 per tonne this week.

**Court ruling clouds seabed auction.** A court ruling has clouded the planned US seabed minerals auction in the South Pacific, the Northern Miner reported, days after the first US offshore critical minerals lease sale was covered in yesterday's edition. The ruling is the first legal check on the programme's Pacific ambitions.

**BME opens detonator plant in Western Australia.** BME has opened Western Australia's first electronic detonator manufacturing plant, International Mining reported, localising a supply item the state's gold and iron ore mines have imported.
