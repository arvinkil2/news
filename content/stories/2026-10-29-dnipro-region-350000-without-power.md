DTEK, Ukraine's largest private energy company, said more than 350,000 subscribers in Dnipro and the Synelnykove, Pavlohrad and Kamianske districts were without power after emergency outages were introduced on Thursday evening, Ukrainska Pravda reported, citing the company's statement. DTEK said that by 21:16 local time its energy workers had stabilised the situation and switched all customers onto temporary supply schemes, though outage schedules remained in force. Ukrenergo, the state grid operator, said emergency outages were in effect in several regions of Ukraine because of the difficult situation in the energy system, and that repair work was under way where the security situation allowed.
