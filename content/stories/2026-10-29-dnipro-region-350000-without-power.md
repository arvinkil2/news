More than 350,000 subscribers in Dnipro and nearby settlements were left without electricity after Russian forces attacked the city again on Thursday, Oleksandr Hanzha, head of the Dnipropetrovsk regional military administration, said, describing the total as preliminary. Hanzha said power workers were working to restore supply, and that water supply and sewage pumping stations were being switched to backup power from generators. DTEK had earlier reported that part of Dnipro and nearby districts lost electricity as a result of an attack on trunk networks, Ukrainska Pravda said.
