TSMC reported record third-quarter revenue of NT$1.49 trillion ($46.7 billion), up 50 percent from NT$989.9 billion a year earlier and ahead of the NT$1.46 trillion LSEG SmartEstimate from 19 analysts. September alone brought in NT$511.86 billion, up 54.6 percent year on year. The company gave no forward guidance in its brief statement, and its Taipei-listed shares closed down 1.35 percent Thursday in the broader risk-off session.

**Update (Friday, October 9):** TSMC's primary release confirms the monthly figures behind the record quarter. The print lands alongside Samsung's Q3 guidance, also on October 8, which put operating profit at a record 107.4 trillion won on AI memory demand, and together the two results suggest the AI hardware cycle is still accelerating into the fourth quarter. The full Q3 earnings call with outlook guidance is due next Thursday, October 15.
