TSMC reported record third-quarter revenue of NT$1.49 trillion ($46.7 billion), up 50 percent from NT$989.9 billion a year earlier and ahead of the NT$1.46 trillion LSEG SmartEstimate from 19 analysts. September alone brought in NT$511.86 billion, up 54.6 percent year on year. The company gave no forward guidance in its brief statement, and its Taipei-listed shares closed down 1.35 percent Thursday in the broader risk-off session.
