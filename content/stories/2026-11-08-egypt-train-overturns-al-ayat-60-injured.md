Carriages of a train overturned at Al-Ayat in Egypt's Giza province on Sunday, injuring about 60 people, BBC Arabic reported, with the injured being taken to hospital and more than 15 ambulances sent to the site. No deaths were reported in that item and no cause was given.

Train movement between Giza and Upper Egypt stopped in both directions after the accident, and civil protection teams and the authorities were working to clear the site and restore services.
