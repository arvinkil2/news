The tanker Atlantic Asphalt struck the American Heritage, an American Cruise Lines riverboat docked at the Port of New Orleans, at about 12:30 p.m. local time on Friday, the port said. The riverboat, which had docked about two hours earlier to begin disembarking and carried about 129 passengers plus crew, was left on its side and taking on water, and firefighters confirmed multiple people had been rescued from the river while search and rescue operations continued.

American Cruise Lines said guests and crew were evacuated and that it was coordinating with the Coast Guard and local authorities. The Coast Guard closed the Mississippi River to all vessel traffic between mile markers 93 and 96, covering the crash area, while responders assessed the situation; no confirmed count of injuries or deaths had been reported.
