Wren House Infrastructure Management, the direct infrastructure arm of the Kuwait Investment Authority, has agreed to acquire the 80 percent stake held by Singapore's GIC in the European xScale joint venture with Equinix, Datacenter Dynamics reported on November 27. Equinix keeps its 20 percent holding and will continue to manage and operate the data centers. Completion is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

The venture, formed in 2019, holds hyperscale data centers in Dublin, Frankfurt, London and Paris, among other European sites, leased to large cloud providers. Across the wider xScale program, Equinix and GIC have invested more than 22 billion dollars since 2019, though that total covers ventures well beyond the European portfolio being sold.

Neither party disclosed financial terms. Bloomberg reported in September, when Wren House was nearing a deal, a price near 700 million euros, after February sale talk had valued the stake at 800 million to 1 billion euros. That earlier figure is the only price in circulation and predates the agreement, so the actual consideration, and the return GIC realized on a seven-year holding, remain unknown until a filing or disclosure confirms them.

The sale is a capital rotation, not an operating change: the same facilities, tenants and operator continue under a new majority owner. It lands in the same week that GIC is reported to be bidding, with Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners, for a minority stake in UK developer Pure DC, which suggests GIC is recycling capital from mature European assets into new development rather than leaving the sector.
