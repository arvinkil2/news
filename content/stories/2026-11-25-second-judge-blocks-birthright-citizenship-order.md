US District Judge Christopher R. Cooper in Washington issued a preliminary injunction on Wednesday against the Trump administration's August executive orders seeking to limit birthright citizenship and combat birth tourism, The Hill reported. In a 67-page opinion, Cooper wrote that the Citizenship Clause of the 14th Amendment promises citizenship to practically all children born in the United States, and barred federal authorities from enforcing the orders or taking other actions to deny the citizenship of class members.

The suit was filed in September by immigrants' rights groups including the American Civil Liberties Union. Cooper is the second judge to block the August effort: Judge Deborah Boardman in Maryland issued a similar injunction in September, and the Supreme Court struck down an earlier version of the policy in June by a 6 to 3 vote.
