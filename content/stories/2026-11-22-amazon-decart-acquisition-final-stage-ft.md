Amazon is in the final stage of negotiations to acquire Decart, an AI startup founded in San Francisco in 2023 by three Israeli engineers, for about $7.5 billion, the Financial Times reported, citing sources who said a deal could be completed as early as early next week but that the talks could still collapse. Decart builds software that creates and simulates 3D environments and makes AI models run more efficiently, and it was an early adopter of Amazon's Trainium 3 chip. The reported price is nearly double the $4 billion valuation Decart took in a $300 million funding round in May, and it follows a $6 billion approach by Anthropic that was abandoned after due diligence in September.
