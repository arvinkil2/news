Ukrainian media report that Ukrainian forces have struck the Samara linear oil pumping station again, the third hit on the facility in about two weeks following attacks on October 2 and October 7. Ukraine's General Staff confirmed the October 7 strike destroyed four RVS-50000 storage tanks and triggered a large fire, according to NV.ua.

The Samara node is a major gathering point where crude arriving by trunk pipeline from Tatarstan, Western Siberia and other Russian regions is received, stored and blended into the Urals export grade. The General Staff says the station also serves Russian military needs. A monitoring channel estimated damage from the October 2 strike at roughly $200 million.
