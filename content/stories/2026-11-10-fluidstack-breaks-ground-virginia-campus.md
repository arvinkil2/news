AI cloud firm Fluidstack has begun construction on its data center campus in Wythe County, Virginia, developing 99 acres at Progress Park in a project dubbed Solis Arx and first proposed in December 2025. The campus is expected to see more than 1 billion dollars of investment across property, power infrastructure, IT hardware and workforce, built in phases with the majority complete by the end of 2027, generating about 1,000 construction jobs and 75 permanent positions. Fluidstack has said it will cover the cost of power infrastructure upgrades, the site will use a closed-loop cooling system with an initial fill of roughly 400,000 gallons, and Wythe County expects about 10 million dollars in additional annual revenue by 2028.
