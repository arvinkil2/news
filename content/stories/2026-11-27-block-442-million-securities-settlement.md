Block has agreed in principle to settle a federal securities class action for approximately $442.5 million, the company disclosed in a Form 8-K filed on November 27. The agreement was reached on November 17 and remains subject to preliminary and final approval by the US District Court for the Northern District of California.

The suit, filed on January 17, 2025, alleged violations of Sections 10(b) and 20(a) of the Exchange Act on behalf of investors who bought Block's Class A common stock between February 26, 2020 and August 1, 2024. The filing does not state how much of the settlement, if any, is covered by insurance.
