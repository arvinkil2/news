Kudakwashe Tagwirei has been appointed Zimbabwe's Minister of Economic Development and Investment Promotion, the permanent secretary in the information ministry, Nick Mangwana, announced on X on Thursday. Bloomberg also reported the appointment. Tagwirei, a businessman and close ally of President Emmerson Mnangagwa who joined the Senate in August under the controversial Constitutional Amendment No. 3, has faced US and UK sanctions since 2020 over corruption allegations.
