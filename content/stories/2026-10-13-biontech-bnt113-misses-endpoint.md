BioNTech said Tuesday that BNT113, its experimental mRNA cancer immunotherapy, did not extend progression-free survival when combined with Merck's Keytruda at an interim analysis of a Phase 3 trial in first-line head and neck squamous cell carcinoma driven by the HPV16 strain. A data monitoring committee recommended the trial continue toward its second main objective, overall survival, and BioNTech said the combination showed signs of engaging its intended mechanism. Shares fell about 6 percent in early trading, extending a run of mixed oncology results as the company leans harder on pumitamig, the bispecific antibody it is developing with Bristol Myers Squibb.
