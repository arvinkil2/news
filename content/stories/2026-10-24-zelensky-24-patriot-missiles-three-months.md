President Volodymyr Zelensky said in an interview with ABC News that Ukraine received only 24 interceptor missiles for Patriot systems from the United States in the last three months, while its needs for such weapons are significantly greater, Espreso reported. "I feel what I feel. This is not support," he said, adding that he does not feel betrayed but noting the absence of American assistance, and that he cannot explain what went wrong in his relations with President Donald Trump. Espreso noted that media had earlier reported Ukraine had exhausted its stocks of Patriot interceptor missiles, leaving the country more vulnerable to Russian ballistic missile strikes.
