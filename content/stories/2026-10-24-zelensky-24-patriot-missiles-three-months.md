President Volodymyr Zelensky told journalists on Saturday that Ukraine received only 24 Patriot missiles from the United States in three months. "We received from the USA only 24 missiles for the Patriot. That is it, in three months, 24 missiles," he said, in remarks reported by Interfax-Ukraine and Espreso. Zelensky also said the United States has not yet answered regarding Tomahawk missiles, a question he said he raised again in his speech at the Hudson Institute on October 23.
