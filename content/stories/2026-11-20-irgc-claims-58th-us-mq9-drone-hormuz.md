Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps announced that its Aerospace Force intercepted and downed a US military MQ-9 drone in the skies over the Strait of Hormuz, according to Press TV, Iran's state broadcaster. The IRGC said the drone was targeted and destroyed by a newly deployed air defense system operated by its Aerospace Force, and counted the incident as the 58th MQ-9 shot down by Iranian forces.

The statement carried no detail on where over the strait the drone was downed or on any wreckage, and Press TV reported no US response. Iranian authorities said they continuously monitor military and aerial activity in the waterway and will respond to violations of the country's airspace. The claim has not been independently corroborated.
