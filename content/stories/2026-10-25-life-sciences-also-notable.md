Sac-TMT final survival presentation today, results not yet published. The final overall survival analysis of the Phase 3 OptiTROP-Lung04 trial of Kelun and Merck's sacituzumab tirumotecan in EGFR-mutant non-squamous lung cancer after EGFR inhibitor failure is presented as late-breaking abstract LBA67 at ESMO today, October 25. No results from that presentation were published at the time of this edition. The benchmark it will be judged against is the peer-reviewed data already available: median progression-free survival of 8.3 months against 4.3 months (hazard ratio 0.49) in 376 patients, with an interim survival hazard ratio of 0.60. Source: OncologyTube ESMO preview summarising the New England Journal of Medicine report, October 2026.

Rasonque survival benefit held across subgroups. Revolution Medicines presented exploratory subgroup analyses of the Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial at ESMO showing that Rasonque (daraxonrasib) maintained its overall survival benefit against chemotherapy across all evaluated tumour RAS genotype subgroups, including patients with no identified RAS mutation, and across chemotherapy regimens and prior treatment settings. In the previously reported primary analysis in 500 patients, median survival was 13.2 months against 6.7 months (hazard ratio 0.40). These are exploratory subgroup analyses, not new confirmatory results, and they sit alongside the Tango combination story already published on October 23. Source: Revolution Medicines GlobeNewswire release, October 23, 2026.
