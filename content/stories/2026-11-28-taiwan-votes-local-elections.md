Polls opened at 8am local time on Saturday across Taiwan's 22 cities and counties in the nine-in-one local elections, choosing mayors, council members and other local leaders for about 11,000 posts, with results expected early in the evening after polls close at 4pm. The governing Democratic Progressive Party's Puma Shen is challenging incumbent Kuomintang mayor Chiang Wan-an in Taipei, a traditional KMT stronghold, and analysts quoted in the report caution that local results are an unreliable guide to the presidential election due in early 2028. Taiwan's defence ministry counted about 33 Chinese PLA warplane sorties toward the island between Friday and Saturday, and the Mainland Affairs Council has warned that Beijing is trying to influence the outcome through cognitive warfare.
