Codelco has dismissed eight employees, among them Salvador general manager Patricio Viveros, who is replaced by Felipe Lagno, after new evidence emerged in the state miner's investigation into over-reported copper production across its Chuquicamata, Ministro Hales and Salvador divisions and its Santiago headquarters. Audits have identified about 20,000 tonnes included in December 2025 production records without qualifying as output, and roughly 27,000 tonnes, about 2 percent of Codelco's reported 2025 own-mine production, improperly booked as commercially ready material, though the company says its audited 2025 financial statements need no changes. A criminal investigation led by regional prosecutor Aquiles Cubillos, external reviews by Ernst & Young and KPMG, and a separate audit by Chile's Comptroller General are all still running, and an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on November 5 will examine the inconsistencies in the production figures.
