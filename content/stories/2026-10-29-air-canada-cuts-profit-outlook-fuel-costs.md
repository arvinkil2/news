Air Canada cut its full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to C$2.4 billion to C$2.7 billion, down from C$2.9 billion to C$3.2 billion set on August 11, and cut its free cash flow outlook to break-even, plus or minus C$150 million, from C$200 million to C$500 million. The airline raised its fourth-quarter jet fuel assumption to about C$1.72 per litre from C$1.29, and said it now expects to offset only about 70 percent of the incremental fuel expense in the quarter, down from 100 percent.

Third-quarter operating revenue was a record C$6.984 billion and adjusted EBITDA was C$936 million, even as fuel expense rose C$906 million year over year and fuel prices ran 10 cents per litre above the airline's expectations for the quarter.
