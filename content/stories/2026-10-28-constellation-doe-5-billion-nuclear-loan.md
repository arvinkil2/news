The Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing has announced a conditional loan commitment of up to $5 billion to finance nuclear uprates and modernization across Constellation Energy's fleet in Illinois, Maryland and Pennsylvania, work the Department says will add nearly 1.1 gigawatts of electricity across the PJM and MISO grids and support more than 10,000 project-related jobs. The planned investments cover Braidwood, Byron, Clinton, Dresden, LaSalle and Quad Cities in Illinois, Calvert Cliffs in Maryland and Limerick in Pennsylvania, with an option to finance future uprate projects at additional Constellation facilities. The commitment indicates the Department's intent to lend, but Constellation must still satisfy technical, legal, environmental and financial conditions before definitive financing documents are signed and the loan is funded.
