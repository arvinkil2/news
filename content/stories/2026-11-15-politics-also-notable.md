**UN rapporteur presses Cambodia at its own summit.** As Phnom Penh hosts the Francophonie summit, UN Special Rapporteur Tom Andrews issued a statement urging delegates to uphold the bloc's values and press the Cambodian government to release detained rights activists, saying no one should be detained for defending human rights, reporting on matters of public concern or voicing critical opinions. Local activists have treated the meeting, in a de facto one-party state, as an occasion to press for more democratic freedoms. Cambodia takes over the summit chairmanship from France at this meeting.

**OFAC's November 4 Iran designations published in full.** The Federal Register of November 9 carried OFAC's notice (FR Doc. 2026-22766) for the action issued on November 4, which blocked four individuals and ten entities under Executive Order 13902, all tagged for Iran sector exposure with secondary sanctions risk. The list centres on the Aryavart ship management network in India, a Singapore ship registry company, Eswatini Maritime Affairs and International Ship Registry, and Istanbul-based Fugo Ground Services, designated for operating in Iran's aviation sector. All property subject to US jurisdiction is blocked and US persons are generally prohibited from dealing with those named.
