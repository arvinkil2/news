Russia and Ukraine have exchanged 270 prisoners of war each, Russian state news agency TASS said on Friday, citing the Defence Ministry in Moscow. Interfax reported the same 270-for-270 formula and said Russian servicemen returned in the exchange were in Belarus, the usual staging point before they are brought back to Russia for medical and psychological support. No Ukrainian confirmation of the numbers was available in the reports read.
