The EU electricity market is suitably prepared for the winter, the European Commission said after the Electricity Coordination Group met on November 12 with national experts and stakeholders, while underlining that the market needs continued close monitoring. The ENTSO-E Winter Outlook for 2026-27, published on November 13, finds the EU is expected to have sufficient resources to meet its electricity demand this winter, even though hydro storage levels are lower than before and planned nuclear maintenance will slightly reduce plant availability.

The group said the situation in Ukraine and Moldova remains difficult, in particular in Ukraine, given continuous targeted attacks on critical energy infrastructure, and that security of electricity supply in both countries this winter will depend on weather conditions, the state of that infrastructure and the availability of interconnections with the EU.
