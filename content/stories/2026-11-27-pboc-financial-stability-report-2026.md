The People's Bank of China released its China Financial Stability Report (2026) on November 27, assessing 2025, when GDP reached 140.19 trillion yuan, up 5 percent, and foreign exchange reserves stayed above 3.3 trillion US dollars. The report records that the PBOC cut the reserve requirement ratio by 0.5 percentage points in 2025, supplying about 1 trillion yuan of long-term liquidity, and that the number of local government financing platforms and their operating financial debt have both fallen sharply compared with early 2023.

The bank's verdict is that financial risks are overall converging and controllable, with high-risk small and medium-sized banks down markedly from their peak and financial institutions' operating and regulatory indicators in a reasonable range. For the period ahead it pledges flexible use of monetary policy tools to keep liquidity ample and social financing conditions relatively loose, and to keep the renminbi basically stable at a reasonable equilibrium level.
