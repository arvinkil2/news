Titan Mining Corporation said Wednesday its subsidiary Empire State Mines has executed a term sheet with the U.S. Army establishing commercial terms for an Enhanced Use Lease to build a graphite processing facility at Anniston Army Depot. Under the framework, the company would develop, finance, build, own and operate the plant, producing purified micronized graphite in phase one and coated spherical purified graphite after customer qualification, with the Army holding a right of first offer on up to 10 percent of annual output.

The company said diligence continues at Pine Bluff Arsenal in Arkansas and Fort Drum in New York, and that the definitive lease still requires agreement on development plans, environmental and regulatory matters, project financing and board approval.
