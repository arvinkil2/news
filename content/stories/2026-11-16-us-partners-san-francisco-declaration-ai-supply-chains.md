The United States and nine partner nations, including India, Singapore and the United Kingdom, have signed a pledge to safeguard sensitive technologies and critical infrastructure amid strategic competition with China over artificial intelligence, Bloomberg reported. The pledge, named the San Francisco Declaration on the Secure Foundations of Enduring Prosperity, establishes a framework for protecting the critical goods, software, knowledge materials and components essential to AI supply chains.

The signing follows the Pax Silica initiative, the US led AI supply chain body launched in December 2025, which President Trump was expected to press ASEAN members to join at the Manila summit. The full list of the nine partners and any commitments beyond the framework have not yet been verified from an open source.
