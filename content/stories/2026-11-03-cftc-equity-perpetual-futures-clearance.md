Perpetual futures contracts on individual equity securities can begin trading on CFTC-registered exchanges for the first time, Chairman Michael Selig announced on November 3. The contracts, known as Equity Perps, were deemed approved under Commission Regulation 40.3 as security futures products under the Commodity Futures Modernization Act of 2000, and can be listed by designated contract markets that are also registered with the SEC as notice-registered national securities exchanges for security futures.

Selig said the products are widely used for risk management and price discovery and that US investors should not be forced to turn to offshore, often unregulated venues to trade them. The clearance builds on a CFTC and SEC memorandum of understanding signed in March and follows the approval earlier this year of the first bitcoin perpetual futures contract on a CFTC-regulated exchange.
