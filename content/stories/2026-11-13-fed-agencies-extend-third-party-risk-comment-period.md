The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, the Federal Reserve Board, the National Credit Union Administration and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency said on Friday that they will extend the comment period for proposed guidance on managing risks associated with third-party relationships until December 16, from an original deadline of November 16. The proposed guidance is principles based and non-binding, and is intended to help banks and credit unions tailor their risk management to individual third-party relationships; when it is finalised, the agencies plan to rescind their existing third-party risk guidance and replace it with the new text. The Federal Reserve separately extended, also to December 16, the comment period on its proposed third-party risk guide for the traditional community banks it supervises.
