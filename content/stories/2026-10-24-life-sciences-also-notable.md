Merck sac-TMT early-stop headline, flagged and unverified in detail. Endpoints News reported on October 23 that a trial of Merck and Kelun-Biotech's TROP2 antibody-drug conjugate sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT) was stopped early. The full article was not accessible in this run, so which trial, in which direction and on what data cannot be verified here, and no figure is reported. What is verifiable is the calendar: Merck presents sac-TMT late-breaking data at ESMO on October 25 (OptiTROP-Lung04 survival) and October 26 (TroFuse-005 in endometrial cancer, which met both primary endpoints at a May 2026 interim analysis), and Kelun reported Phase 2 sac-TMT plus Keytruda data in first-line cisplatin-ineligible bladder cancer at ESMO on October 24, with 63 percent grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events and support for a global Phase 3, TroFuse-055. Source: Endpoints News headline, October 23, 2026 (grade C, single source, detail unverified); Merck ESMO program release and Kelun-Biotech release, October 2026.
