India's Securities and Exchange Board of India is seeking to remove an additional disclosure requirement that applies to certain foreign funds, under a consultation paper issued on Friday, Bloomberg reported. The requirement, described as a persistent irritant for global investors, obliges the affected funds to identify every individual ultimately behind them.

The proposal is aimed at easing the disclosure rules for foreign investors in order to boost overseas inflows into India's equities. No consultation deadline, effective date or figures accompanied the proposal in the portion of the report that could be read.
