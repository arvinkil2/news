Iran's military said it attacked a US F-16 fighter with an air defence missile over the Gulf on the evening of November 20, damaging the aircraft, which aborted its mission and made an emergency landing at a nearby airport, Reuters reported, citing Iranian state media. The report named no airport, gave no extent of damage and mentioned nothing about the pilot, and Reuters said the US military had not confirmed the claim.

Separately, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said in a Telegram statement on November 21 that it had shot down a US MQ-1 drone over Siri Island in the southern Gulf, RIA Novosti reported. The Guard said the interception was its 19th of a US MQ-1. No US confirmation or denial of the drone claim was reported.
