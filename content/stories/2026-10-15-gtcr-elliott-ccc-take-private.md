CCC Intelligent Solutions, the Chicago based SaaS and AI platform for the auto insurance claims economy, said on Thursday it has signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by a consortium led by GTCR and including Elliott Investment Management, in an all cash transaction with an enterprise value of about 6.1 billion dollars. Shareholders will receive 7.75 dollars a share, a premium of about 44 percent to the unaffected close on July 9, before sale speculation surfaced, and about 59 percent to the 30 day average price to that date. The board approved the deal unanimously and it is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals, taking private a platform that connects more than 35,000 businesses across insurers, repairers and suppliers.
