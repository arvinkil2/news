China's Ministry of Commerce said on Tuesday, in Announcement No. 59 of 2026, that the second clause of its December 2024 announcement tightening dual-use export controls on the United States will stay suspended from November 28, 2026 to January 27, 2027. The existing suspension was due to expire on November 27. The suspended clause generally bars export licences for US-bound dual-use items involving gallium, germanium and antimony or superhard materials, and applies stricter checks on buyers and uses of certain US-bound graphite products; China still requires export licences for controlled products and bars dual-use exports to US military users or for US military use.
