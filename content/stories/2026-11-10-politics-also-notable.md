OFAC amended the SDN list entry for Volta Shipping Services LLC of Dubai on November 9, recording its alias as Volta Container Line FZCO while keeping its designations, licences and registration identifiers unchanged. The entry remains tagged under the IRAN-EO13902 programme and linked to Mohammad Hossein Shamkhani. It is an identifier update, not a new designation, and it matters for the unglamorous reason most sanctions maintenance does: screening systems match on names and aliases, and a stale alias is a gap.

The European Commission published an updated action plan under its Pact for the Mediterranean on November 10, bringing the plan to a total of 30 actions in its first year. The pact is the EU's framework for its southern neighbourhood, spanning migration, energy and economic ties, and the update is the document to read against next year's implementation rather than a single decision.

Israel's Shin Bet has found no evidence of foreign interference in the October 27 election, Al Jazeera reported on November 9, citing Israeli reporting of the security service's assessment. The finding, if borne out in any official publication, removes one challenge to a result that produced no majority for either bloc. It is carried here as a reported finding, single chain, not as an officially published conclusion.

In Spain, France24 reported on November 9 that the right continues to hold a polling lead over Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez's Socialists ahead of the November 29 snap election. No survey figures are stated here because the underlying poll could not be verified from the report available to this reader; the direction of the race, three weeks out, is the notable fact.
