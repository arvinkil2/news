President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the United States is sharing intelligence with Ukraine again after the flow was blocked for some period of time, speaking in an interview for NBC's Meet the Press broadcast on Sunday. He said he was hopeful that talks with the United States could be held this week. The statement follows days in which Kyiv said it had received no advance warning from partners before Russian ballistic strikes, and Washington had warned that intelligence sharing could be halted if Ukraine kept striking Russian energy facilities.
