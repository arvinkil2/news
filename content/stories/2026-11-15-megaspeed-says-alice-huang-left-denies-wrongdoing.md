Megaspeed has broken its silence on the allegations that it served as a conduit for diverting Nvidia artificial intelligence chips to China. In a company statement reported by the Associated Press on November 15 and carried by Yonhap, the Singapore firm said Alice Huang left the company on August 30, 2025 and currently holds no role there, described reports of wrongdoing as claims with no verifiable evidence, and said it is conducting a lawful and legitimate business. The company said it has appointed an outside legal advisory team and will carry out a thorough internal investigation.

Megaspeed declined to comment on the allegations concerning Huang personally, calling them her private matters. The denial leaves standing the tension at the centre of the case: Singapore authorities have investigated Huang on the basis that she was the company's chief executive, board chair and key controlling shareholder, and a research firm's account earlier this year held that employees still treated her as the de facto head of the firm after her reported 2023 step back from its directorship.
