India's Index of Core Industries rose 10.9 percent in October from a year earlier on a provisional basis, accelerating from September's final reading of 8.2 percent, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry said on Friday. The overall index stood at 123.1 in October, and cumulative growth for April to October was 6.0 percent, against 2.3 percent in the same period a year earlier.

Iron ore output rose 26.4 percent year over year, coal 23.1 percent, electricity 21.6 percent, cement 15.4 percent, steel 7.3 percent and refinery products 3.4 percent, while natural gas, crude oil and fertilisers recorded negative growth, with fertilisers down 12.2 percent. September's index was revised to 118.6 from 118.3, lifting its annual growth rate to 8.2 percent from 7.9 percent.
