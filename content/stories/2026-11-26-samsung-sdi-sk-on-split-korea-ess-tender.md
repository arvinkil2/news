South Korea's Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment announced the results of the third energy storage tender under its central contract market on November 26, awarding 1,194 MW, about 7.08 GWh of battery capacity and more than the first two tenders combined. Samsung SDI took 751 MW, 62.9 percent of the total, supplying batteries to 9 of the 14 project sites, and SK On took 443 MW, 37.1 percent, across 5 mainland sites. LG Energy Solution, the largest of Korea's three battery makers by production capacity, did not take part, saying it would focus on delivering volume it has already secured; scoring in the tender has weighted domestic industry contribution and fire safety more heavily since the second round.
