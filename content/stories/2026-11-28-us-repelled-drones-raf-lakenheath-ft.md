The US military repelled about half a dozen drones swarming RAF Lakenheath, the British base in Suffolk used by the US Air Force, earlier this month, the Financial Times reported on Saturday, citing people familiar with the matter and saying Iran was suspected of being linked to the incident. US counter-drone technology was deployed to repel the unmanned aircraft, but the drones were not retrieved by British or American personnel, the report said.

Britain's Ministry of Defence told Reuters that Ministry of Defence Police and Suffolk Police worked with USAF Security Forces to respond to reports of unauthorised drone activity at RAF Lakenheath on November 13, that the situation was quickly resolved, and that the base returned to normal security measures. A US Air Force spokesperson said at the time that no personnel, aircraft or infrastructure were affected, and the White House and Pentagon did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
