Malaysia Aviation Group, the parent of Malaysia Airlines, announced on Wednesday that it will acquire ten Boeing 787 Dreamliners directly from Boeing, with options for six more, as part of its fleet modernisation programme. The aircraft, powered by GE Aerospace GEnx engines and configured with 319 seats in three classes, are scheduled for delivery between 2032 and 2035 and will gradually replace the group's seven Airbus A350s, which are expected to be phased out over the same period. Group chief executive Nasaruddin A. Bakar called the purchase a strategic investment that would give the airline greater capacity, efficiency and flexibility on long-haul routes; financial terms were not disclosed.
