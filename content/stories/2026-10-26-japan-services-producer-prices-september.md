Japan's services producer price index rose 3.7 percent from a year earlier in September, up from a revised 3.5 percent in August, and rose 0.3 percent in the month to 115.5, the Bank of Japan said in its preliminary release. Ocean freight transportation did most of the work: its prices rose 97.7 percent from a year earlier, against 74.6 percent in August, and the transportation and postal activities group rose 8.4 percent, contributing 1.36 points of the 3.7 percent headline increase. Hotel prices rose 9.7 percent from a year earlier, after 1.6 percent in August, while advertising services fell 5.2 percent and subtracted 0.23 points.

The domestic picture was steadier. Excluding international transportation, the index rose 2.9 percent from a year earlier, the same rate as in August, and services with a high labour cost ratio rose 2.6 percent. The release lands two days before the Bank of Japan's October 29 to 30 policy meeting, at which the bank is expected to hold its rate at 1.25 percent, as this site reported on October 25.
