Concern that China may tax interest on government bonds spilled into policy bank debt on Thursday. The yield on China Development Bank's 10 year notes closed three basis points higher, matching this year's largest daily increase according to Chinabond data, and its spread over similar-tenor government bonds widened to about nine basis points, the most in over six months; yields on debt from Export-Import Bank of China and Agricultural Development Bank of China also rose.

The worry traces to soundings reported earlier this week: the People's Bank of China surveyed a number of lenders last month on how a 25 percent tax on interest income from their central and local government bond holdings would affect their appetite for those securities, according to Bloomberg reporting citing people familiar with the matter. No tax has been announced.
