Japan's largest power generator, JERA, has entered into talks with Anthropic, Meta and other US technology giants to build data centers near power plants in the Tokyo area, Nikkei reported on Friday. The talks extend a model JERA set out on October 1, when it signed a memorandum of understanding with Dell Technologies and developer RHAELM to build data centers co-located with its plants, starting with a site of up to 400 megawatts at its Chiba thermal power station east of Tokyo. No deal with Anthropic or Meta has been signed, and neither company has publicly confirmed the discussions.
