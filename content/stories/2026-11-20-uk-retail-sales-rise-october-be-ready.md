UK retail sales volumes rose 0.7 percent in October 2026, following a fall of 0.6 percent in September, revised down from a 0.4 percent fall, and were 2.3 percent higher than in October 2025, the Office for National Statistics said. Sales volumes rose 0.4 percent in the three months to October, compared with the three months to July.

Food stores rose as supermarkets reported an October uplift in long-life items such as UHT milk and tinned goods following the launch of the government's Be Ready campaign on 22 October, while non-food store volumes rose 1.1 percent on the month. Online sales values rose 1.5 percent in October, total spending rose 1.2 percent, and the share of sales made online rose to 29.2 percent, from 29.1 percent in September.
