Five more commercial vessels were struck in and around the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, gCaptain reported, after UK Maritime Trade Operations disclosed four of the incidents in time-late reports on Monday. Four vessels were struck by unidentified projectiles while transiting the strait: an outbound product tanker, an outbound crude oil tanker, another outbound tanker, and a containership sailing inbound; a fifth tanker was hit by a drone near the Sohar anchorage in Oman, where local authorities were helping evacuate the crew.

The reports land on top of the Joint Maritime Information Center's October 11 advisory rating the Hormuz security threat SEVERE, its second-highest category, with US naval figures showing roughly 29 vessel transits a day over the 72 hours to October 10 against a historical average of about 138 a day. UKMTO confirmed one outbound tanker's crew was safe, with the environmental impact unknown; the other reports gave no casualty or damage details.
