Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said it targeted a United States early-warning radar in Bahrain, in retaliation for what it described as an air attack on a telecommunications tower in southern Iran, Al Jazeera reported, citing an IRGC statement carried by the semi-official Tasnim news agency. The IRGC affiliated Fars news agency said the radar installation was destroyed, and the IRGC called the strike a serious warning against what it said were aerial provocations and oil smuggling in the Strait of Hormuz, warning that its Aerospace Force was ready to engage without limits if they recur. There was no immediate comment from Washington or Manama and no independent confirmation of a strike or of damage; Reuters noted it was the first time since early September that Iran has claimed an attack on US military assets in a Gulf country, and Bahrain hosts the headquarters of the US Navy's Fifth Fleet.
