The European Commission has created an EU Sanctions Office to bring together its expertise across the entire sanctions lifecycle, from policy design to implementation, enforcement and anti-circumvention, including outreach to third countries. The office will sit within the Secretariat-General under Commissioner Maria Luís Albuquerque, draw staff from services including DG FISMA, DG TRADE, DG TAXUD and OLAF, and support member states with faster compliance guidance, data sharing and analysis so sanctions are implemented in a harmonised way. The changes take effect on January 16, 2027.

A central part of the office's work will be supporting the sanctions envoy. The Commission appointed Lars Danielsson, a Swedish career diplomat who served as Sweden's ambassador and permanent representative to the EU from 2016 to 2023, as International Special Envoy for the Implementation of EU Sanctions. He takes up the post on February 6, 2027, when the mandate of the current envoy, David O'Sullivan, ends, and will be attached to the Secretary-General with a direct reporting line to Commissioner Albuquerque.
