Tesla chief executive Elon Musk said on Thursday the company would continue to increase output at its China factory, according to Yuyuan Tantian, a social media account affiliated with Chinese state broadcaster CCTV, in remarks made in a meeting with Ren Hongbin, head of the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade. Reuters, carrying the account, gave no specific output target or timetable for the increase.

The Shanghai plant delivered 810,889 Model 3 and Model Y vehicles in the first ten months of this year, up 21.4 percent from a year earlier, and has annual capacity of more than 950,000 vehicles. It sources 98 percent of its parts from within China and exports to markets including Europe, South Korea and Australia.
