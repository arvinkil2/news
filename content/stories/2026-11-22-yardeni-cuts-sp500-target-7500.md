Yardeni Research lowered its year-end S&P 500 target to 7,500 from 7,900, after the index closed Friday at 6,910.39, down 11.6 percent from its October 6 record high of 7,818.93. Ed Yardeni called the move a correction inside a bull market rather than the start of a bear market, saying he sees no impending recession, and noted the index's forward price-to-earnings ratio fell to 16.4 on Friday, its lowest since the 2022 bear market.

The new target still implies a gain of 9.6 percent for the year from the 2025 close of 6,845.50, and Yardeni kept his longer view intact: a 2027 range of 7,650 to 9,000, an expected 2027 finish between 8,500 and 9,000, and 10,000 by 2029. It is his second cut in about two months, after he lowered the target from 8,400 to 7,900 on September 15.
