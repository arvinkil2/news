Antofagasta's third quarter copper production rose 7 percent year on year to 174,300 tonnes, taking output for the first nine months of the year to 528,500 tonnes, and the Chilean miner backed its 2026 forecast in its quarterly production report. The quarter was the company's strongest of the year, helped by its Los Pelambres and Centinela operations.

The market sold the future, not the quarter. Antofagasta shares fell after the report as investors focused on a 2027 outlook that Mining.com described as falling flat, a reminder that at copper prices within sight of records, guidance has to surprise upward to move the stock.

Separately, the company added BHP veteran Carlos Avila to its board on Wednesday, a hire The Rio Times reported on Friday, as the group reshapes its leadership for the next phase of Chilean copper growth.
