AI minister Kanishka Narayan told the BBC it was "likely" that UK cyber defences would be breached by a major attack despite the government's National Cyber Strategy, saying it is "not a matter of if, but when." He attributed the heightened risk to AI making cyberattacks cheaper, faster and more effective for malicious actors and hostile states, including Russia.

The warning follows a string of cyber incidents in recent months affecting M&S, Jaguar Land Rover, the Ministry of Justice, the Legal Aid Agency and Heathrow, some reportedly linked to ransomware groups and teenagers from the Scattered Spider collective. Cyber minister Liz Lloyd is expected to announce 18.5 million pounds in funding for AI cyber defences on Monday.
