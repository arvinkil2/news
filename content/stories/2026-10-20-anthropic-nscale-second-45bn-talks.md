Anthropic is in talks to take space in a second data centre building at Nscale's Monarch campus in West Virginia, Bloomberg reported, in a contract that could be valued at up to 45 billion dollars for 460 megawatts of capacity running Nvidia Vera Rubin chips, with Anthropic holding a right of first refusal on a third building. The two companies signed a first agreement in August, also for 460 megawatts at Monarch, a 2,250 acre site where Nscale plans up to 8 gigawatts and where Microsoft has also committed to 1.35 gigawatts.

Neither company has confirmed the talks: Anthropic declined to comment to CNBC, and Nscale had not commented in the reports. The report lands days after Microsoft pulled out of a separate Nscale capacity deal in the United Kingdom over grid connection delays, a contrast between the American campus, powered by on-site gas generation, and British projects waiting on the grid queue.
