Australia's Defence Legislation Amendment (Accelerating Capability and Protecting Bases) Bill 2026 would criminalise making or recording an image of a defence security zone, or of a person or thing in, entering or leaving one, with penalties of up to seven years in prison for special zones. The offence does not require proof of trespass or of any risk to security, and the bill carries no defence for public-interest journalism; zones can be designated by the defence minister and include Pine Gap, the joint Australia-US satellite facility.

The bill, introduced on October 27 and passed by the House of Representatives on October 29, would also let the government authorise defence personnel, officials and contractors to act despite state and territory laws for declared capability activities, a framework the government says is needed to protect bases and deliver AUKUS. Politico reported that Labor and the Coalition backed the bill through a rapid inquiry despite civil liberties concerns, and that it is set to pass the Senate in the final sitting fortnight of the year.
