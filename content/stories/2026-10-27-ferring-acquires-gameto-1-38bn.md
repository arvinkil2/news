Ferring Pharmaceuticals has signed an agreement to acquire the fertility startup Gameto in a deal that could be worth up to $1.3825 billion, the companies said, according to Endpoints News. No breakdown of upfront and milestone payments was available in the portion of the report that could be read, and no closing timetable was given there.

Gameto's lead therapy is Fertilo, which uses ovarian support cells derived from induced pluripotent stem cells to mature eggs outside the body, with the aim of improving IVF and egg freezing. The therapy is in a Phase 3 trial in the United States and is already cleared for clinic use in several markets outside the United States.
