The Securities and Exchange Commission on Friday issued exemptive relief from its Treasury clearing rules, excluding two categories of repo transactions from the definition of eligible secondary market transactions: certain repo trades involving non-US counterparties, subject to specified limits and conditions, and certain repo trades between a direct participant of a US Treasury securities clearing agency and an affiliated entity that is not an investment company.

The relief follows the compliance-date extensions the Commission has already granted under the rules it adopted in December 2023. Commissioner Mark Uyeda, who leads the agency's clearing-transition initiative, said the targeted relief "addresses practical concerns while preserving the important benefits of central clearing," and that implementation has to account for the realities of cross-border transactions.
