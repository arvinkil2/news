Greenland's parliament has unanimously passed a resolution directing the government to introduce a ban on seabed mineral extraction, with the deep sea mining moratorium to stay in force until the end of 2029 and extensions possible in further two year periods, RMF24 reported on Friday. The legislation itself is expected to be passed in the first half of 2027.

The vote came a day after President Trump announced that large scale oil, gas and mineral exploration was set to begin in Greenland under the September security deal, a claim Denmark has said gives US investors no special resource rights. WWF Greenland welcomed the vote, citing how little is understood about deep sea mining's effects on the marine ecosystems that support the territory's fisheries.
