IMF staff and the Argentine authorities have reached a staff-level agreement on the third review of the 48-month Extended Fund Facility, the Fund said on Tuesday. Subject to Executive Board approval, Argentina would have access to about US$900 million (SDR 600 million), and the review is expected to go to the board in early December once the remaining agreed steps are completed.

The Fund said net international reserves exceeded the end-June target by about US$4 billion, the central bank has purchased close to US$16 billion so far this year, and approved investments under the RIGI incentive scheme now exceed US$76 billion. The policy package keeps the fiscal anchor at a primary surplus of 1.4 percent of GDP in 2026, with a similar effort in 2027, which the Fund said would make 2027 the fourth consecutive year of a primary surplus and an overall fiscal balance.
