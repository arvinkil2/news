Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing launched its first index tracking offshore yuan bonds on Monday, creating a benchmark for investors in the offshore yuan market. HKEX developed the index with Bloomberg and said it will build further offshore yuan bond indexes with the firm; chief executive Bonnie Chan said the launch supports greater visibility and investability of offshore renminbi assets and advances the internationalisation of the currency.

The index is one of several offshore yuan initiatives from the exchange this year: HKEX launched offshore Chinese government bond futures in August and began accepting Chinese government bonds as eligible non-cash collateral this month. The programme also includes an expansion of Bond Connect for southbound investors and an electronic trading platform for fixed-income and currency products being built with the China Foreign Exchange Trade System.
