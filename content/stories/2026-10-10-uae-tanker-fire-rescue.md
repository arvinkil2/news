The United Arab Emirates National Guard said on Saturday it had rescued 22 crew members after a fire broke out aboard an oil tanker in Gulf waters. The Coast Guard received a distress report from the crew and dispatched specialist teams, which located the vessel; all those rescued were described as being in stable condition. The National Guard did not identify the tanker or give a cause for the fire.

The incident adds to a lengthening list of Gulf tanker emergencies this month, including the Sharjah tanker drone attack and the IRGC's mine claims against a supertanker, all unfolding during the US-Iran tanker war that has raised war-risk premia for traffic through the Strait of Hormuz.
