S&P Global Ratings raised Sri Lanka's long-term foreign and local currency sovereign ratings to B- from CCC+ on Friday, with a stable outlook, and lifted the short-term ratings to B from C. The transfer and convertibility assessment was unchanged at B-. S&P said improvements in the fiscal and external positions are becoming more entrenched, with gross official reserves at about 7.09 billion dollars in October 2026 and government revenue up about 20 percent year on year.

The agency expects growth of 4.0 percent in 2026 and 4.5 percent in 2027, with GDP per capita reaching about 5,200 dollars this year. The stable outlook reflects its expectation that the economy stays resilient over the next six to 12 months despite prolonged disruption in global energy markets.
