Abolfazl Zohrevand, a member of the National Security and Foreign Policy Committee of Iran's Majlis, told a TASS correspondent that Iran had in fact ceased to be a member of the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty and of the International Atomic Energy Agency after strikes on its nuclear facilities and the killing of its nuclear scientists, and that Iranian public opinion fully supports withdrawal. He said Iran had been pushed out of both bodies and did not need to announce anything, presenting the position as his own assessment of where matters stand. The TASS report carries no formal withdrawal decision or announcement by the Iranian government.
