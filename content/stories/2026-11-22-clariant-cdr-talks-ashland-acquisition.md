Clariant and CD&R are in talks to acquire Ashland in an all-cash deal valuing the chemicals maker at about $3.39 billion, close to its share price on Friday, Bloomberg News reported on Sunday, citing people familiar with the matter. No final decision has been made, the discussions could fall through, and another suitor could still emerge, the report said. Ashland, based in Wilmington, Delaware, makes additives and specialty ingredients for pharmaceuticals, construction and personal care, and it put itself up for sale in July after pressure from activist investors, with Advent, Apollo Global Management, Carlyle Group and Standard Industries also reported to have had contact with the company.
