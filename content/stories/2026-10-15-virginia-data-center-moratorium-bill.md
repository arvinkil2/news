Democratic senator Russet Perry, who represents Loudoun County, has filed Senate Bill 843 proposing that state agencies stop approving data center planning applications for three years from July 1, 2027, giving lawmakers time to write statewide rules for the industry. Perry cited rising electric bills, transmission lines and grid strain in comments reported by the Loudoun Times-Mirror, and the proposal follows a 12 month moratorium in Loudoun County itself and tighter planning controls in neighbouring Prince William County. Governor Abigail Spanberger has stopped short of backing a moratorium, introducing a Data Center Accountability Framework last month instead.
