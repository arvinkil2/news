CrowdStrike is in talks to acquire Zscaler, the cloud network security provider, according to Reuters, citing two people familiar with the matter. No offer price, premium or payment structure has been reported, Zscaler's market value is put at about $33.5 billion, and neither company immediately responded to requests for comment.

The talks could still end without an agreement. Zscaler shares rose about 17 percent in after-hours trading on Friday and CrowdStrike shares fell about 8 percent, as investors priced a possible combination that would join CrowdStrike's endpoint platform with Zscaler's network security business.
