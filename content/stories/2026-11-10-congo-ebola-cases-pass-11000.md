Confirmed Ebola cases in the Democratic Republic of Congo have surpassed 11,000, reaching 11,042, and 5,387 people have died, according to government data reported by Reuters on Tuesday. The outbreak of the Bundibugyo strain, declared in mid-May, has affected eight of Congo's 26 provinces, and many deaths have occurred outside treatment centres.

Reuters reported that delayed detection, conflict and strained health services have driven a faster spread than in previous outbreaks, and that the disease has now been reported in East, Central and West Africa, the widest geographic spread of any Ebola outbreak on the continent. It is the second largest and deadliest on record, behind only the 2014 to 2016 West African outbreak, and there are no approved treatments or vaccines for the Bundibugyo strain.
