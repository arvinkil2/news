Jindal Power, the power arm of India's Jindal Group, has signed an offshore loan of about $470 million to help fund its power project in Botswana, according to people familiar with the matter. Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and the Export-Import Bank of India provided the nine year door-to-door facility, priced at about 300 to 325 basis points over SOFR, and the loan is being syndicated with some global institutional lenders expected to join.

Jindal Power built a 700 megawatt coal power plant in Botswana that is scheduled for commissioning next month, according to its website, and it raised about $500 million in an earlier financing round in February for its expansion abroad. The project revenues are expected to be denominated in, or linked to, foreign currency, giving the borrower a natural hedge against exchange rate swings.
