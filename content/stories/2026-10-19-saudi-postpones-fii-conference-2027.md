The Future Investment Initiative, Saudi Arabia's annual showcase for global investors, business leaders and heads of state, will not take place as scheduled from October 26 to 29, its organisers said on Monday. In a statement posted on X, FII Institute chief executive Maha Al Saud said the tenth edition would instead be held in 2027 so that it could deliver the full scale and quality participants expect, that new dates would be announced soon, and that existing registrations and partnerships would remain valid. Three sources briefed on the matter had earlier told AFP the conference would be postponed.

The decision follows repeated attacks on the kingdom by Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis. Riyadh's international airport suspended operations earlier this month after an October 10 strike killed 13 people, with no clear timeline for resuming traffic, and a consultant who advises Saudi firms told AFP that executives were hesitant to travel to Riyadh and that companies would not put employees in harm's way for an investment conference.
