A drone sank a ship in Bulgaria's Black Sea waters early Tuesday, October 6, according to media reports carried by gCaptain. It is the third vessel incident in the Black Sea in four days, following Russia's strike on a corn-carrying vessel that Ukraine's president condemned and Russia's hit on a Liberian-flagged ship at Odesa on October 3 that killed one person.

Black Sea risk is rising into harvest. USDA Crop Progress data released October 5 showed corn 23 percent harvested and soybeans 25 percent for the week ending October 4, a normal pace. The WASDE report lands Friday, October 9 at noon ET, and will reset benchmark yields and Black Sea export estimates in the middle of the disruption.

Grain loadings face tightening war risk while the physical trade continues. The Black Sea is quietly becoming the grains market's Hormuz: the incidents will price into freight and insurance first, futures second.
