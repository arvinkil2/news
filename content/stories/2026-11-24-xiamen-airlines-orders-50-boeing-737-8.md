Xiamen Airlines, a subsidiary of China Southern Airlines, has signed an agreement with Boeing to buy 50 Boeing 737-8 aircraft, China Southern said in a stock exchange filing reported by Reuters. The combined list price of the jets is about $7.4 billion, and the airline said the actual purchase price would be lower. The agreement is subject to approval by the relevant authorities, and the filing as reported gives no delivery schedule.
