IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi told the agency's Board of Governors on Wednesday that a further extension to the original Yongbyon enrichment facility has been under construction since mid-September 2026 and was externally complete by late October, and that the ongoing expansion of North Korea's uranium enrichment programme remains a cause for serious concern. Grossi said the agency has observed indications that the 5 megawatt reactor at Yongbyon likely continues to operate in its seventh irradiation cycle, and that the light water reactor there has been operating since the end of April 2026 after a shutdown. He said the continuation and further development of North Korea's nuclear programme are clear violations of relevant UN Security Council resolutions.
