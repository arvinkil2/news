## Latest

Iran's Army navy said on Tuesday that another oil tanker had caught fire after striking a naval mine in the eastern Strait of Hormuz, the fourth tanker Tehran says has been damaged by mines in and around the strait in about 24 hours. The navy said the vessel was travelling through a restricted area east of the strait with its navigation systems switched off, that it had sustained significant damage, and it warned shipping operators that uncoordinated movement through restricted waters risked "encountering mines and fires". Britain's UK Maritime Trade Operations agency separately reported an attack on a tanker seven nautical miles off Khasab, Oman, saying all crew members were accounted for and the extent of the damage was unknown. Iran named no vessel in the latest claim, and none of the mine claims has been independently corroborated.

Iran's Revolutionary Guard says more oil tankers have struck sea mines while attempting to cross the Strait of Hormuz using a route Tehran considers unauthorised, Al Jazeera reported. Several tanker explosions have been reported in the strategic waterway over the past week, according to the report.

The report names no vessel and gives no casualty figures or dates for individual incidents, and the claim has not been independently corroborated. It follows a separate report earlier on Monday that a tanker sailing out of the Gulf through Hormuz was hit by an unknown projectile, causing a fire in its engine room.
