Iran's Revolutionary Guard says more oil tankers have struck sea mines while attempting to cross the Strait of Hormuz using a route Tehran considers unauthorised, Al Jazeera reported. Several tanker explosions have been reported in the strategic waterway over the past week, according to the report.

The report names no vessel and gives no casualty figures or dates for individual incidents, and the claim has not been independently corroborated. It follows a separate report earlier on Monday that a tanker sailing out of the Gulf through Hormuz was hit by an unknown projectile, causing a fire in its engine room.
