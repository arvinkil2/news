A projectile struck the Liberia-flagged very large crude carrier MT Fujairah Energy while it was transiting the Persian Gulf from Al Ruwais to Dubai on November 10, injuring four crew members, India's Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal said in a statement on Wednesday. One of the injured was an Indian seafarer, who suffered minor injuries and was taken ashore for further medical examination; the remaining 18 crew, including the other five Indian seafarers aboard, were reported safe and accounted for, and Sonowal said the government is in close contact with the ship management company.

No party has claimed the strike and the extent of damage to the vessel has not been reported. The Fujairah Energy report is the third Gulf shipping incident dated November 10 on this site, alongside ADNOC Logistics and Services' confirmation that one of its vessels was damaged in UAE territorial waters and the UK Maritime Trade Operations report of a tanker struck by an unidentified projectile northeast of Lima, Oman, with all crew safe; no source read in this run establishes whether any of the three reports describe the same vessel.
