Novaya Gazeta said contact with its editor in chief, Sergei Sokolov, has been lost and his phones are disconnected, after officers of Sledkom, Russia's federal investigative committee, searched his home and detained him, France 24 and the TT news agency reported. No charge against Sokolov had been announced at write time, and the paper, which the Russian authorities have labelled a foreign agent, gave no indication of where he is being held. Sokolov took over the paper in 2023 from Dmitry Muratov, the Nobel Peace Prize laureate who stepped aside after being designated a foreign agent himself.
