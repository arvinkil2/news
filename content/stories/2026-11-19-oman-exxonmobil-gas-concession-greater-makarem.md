Oman has signed a $400 million gas concession agreement with Energy Oman and ExxonMobil to assess and develop gas resources in Greater Makarem, with initial investment expected to exceed $400 million, the Oman News Agency reported. The agreement includes an evaluation period in which the investors are committed to technical studies and appraisal drilling to determine the field's production capacity, and Energy and Minerals Minister Salim bin Nasser Al-Aufi said it supports Oman's efforts to strengthen energy security, expand gas reserves and increase production capacity. The concession paves the way for an integrated gas production and liquefaction project with total investment expected to reach several billion dollars, linked to a proposed LNG plant in the Special Economic Zone at Duqm targeting annual capacity of 6 million to 9 million tonnes.
