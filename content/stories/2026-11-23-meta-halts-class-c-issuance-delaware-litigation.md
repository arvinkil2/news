Meta Platforms has agreed that it will not issue any shares of Class C common stock, whether through a dividend or otherwise, during the pre-trial and trial proceedings in the Delaware Court of Chancery shareholder litigation challenging its recapitalisation, the company said in a Form 8-K filing on Monday. The lawsuits have been consolidated under the action captioned In Re Meta Platforms, Inc. Class C Reclassification Litigation, with a trial scheduled to begin April 19, 2027.

In furtherance of the agreement, Mark Zuckerberg waived the requirement in the Founder Alignment Agreement of October 20, 2026 that the previously declared Class C stock dividend be paid by no later than the fifth business day after the company's amended and restated certificate of incorporation takes effect; the waiver expires on June 30, 2027 if the dividend has not been paid by then. Because Meta will not launch its previously announced at-the-market programme until the Class C stock begins trading, it does not expect to launch the programme until after the stock dividend is paid.
