Japan's Ministry of Finance sold treasury discount bills, Issue No. 1420, on Wednesday, taking competitive bids of 5,663.70 billion yen and accepting 1,467.28 billion yen competitively, with a further 432.70 billion yen allotted at the non-price-competitive auction for JGB market special participants, for 1,899.98 billion yen sold in total. The bills are issued on November 20 and mature on November 22, 2027.

The sale cleared at a weighted average price of 98.371 per 100 yen, an average yield of 1.6469 percent, while the lowest accepted price of 98.363 yielded 1.6551 percent and took a 41.8461 percent allotment.
