The Nikkei 225 declined 2.25 percent at the close in Tokyo on Monday, with losses in the paper and pulp, transport and communication sectors leading the market lower and falling stocks outnumbering advancers by 2,226 to 1,343 on the Tokyo Stock Exchange. The heaviest falls were in semiconductor equipment: Lasertec dropped 12.84 percent, Advantest fell 11.12 percent and Taiyo Yuden lost 9.99 percent, while Credit Saison rose 16.84 percent to five year highs and Nitori added 9.15 percent.
