Philippine Vice President Sara Duterte said on Friday that resignation is out of the question, telling a year-end media gathering that stepping down would betray the 32.2 million people who voted for her. Prosecutors have narrowed her impeachment case to a single allegation of unexplained wealth, dropping three other charges, in an effort to conclude the trial by the end of this year before attention shifts to the 2028 election, in which Duterte is the polling frontrunner.

Duterte, who denies wrongdoing and calls the proceedings political harassment, said she was preparing for the worst and warned a guilty verdict would carry wide consequences, including job losses in her offices. A conviction would remove her from office and disqualify her from political posts for life.
