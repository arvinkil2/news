New Fortress Energy said it completed its restructuring and recapitalization under a UK Restructuring Plan on September 11, cutting debt principal from approximately $5.7 billion to approximately $0.7 billion, with creditors receiving its Brazil business, common stock and preferred interests in exchange for cancelled debt, and the company effecting a 1-for-50 reverse stock split. Third quarter revenue was $335.8 million and net income was $863.4 million, driven by $1.6 billion of non-cash restructuring and debt extinguishment gains partly offset by $467.9 million of asset impairments, while adjusted EBITDA was $48.4 million and cash stood at $270.5 million at September 30.

The filing also says FLNG 1, its floating liquefaction unit, is offline after a turbine mechanical issue, that the turbine is expected to be ready on November 23 with a return to service expected in the fourth quarter, and that because the timing of that return is outside the company's control there is substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern.
