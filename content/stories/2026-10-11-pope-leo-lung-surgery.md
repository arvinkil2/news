Pope Leo told pilgrims in St. Peter's Square on Sunday that a recent health check detected a localized nodule in his lung at a very early stage and that, on his doctors' advice, he will undergo surgery to remove it in the next few days. The pontiff described the growth as well-contained; the Vatican press office said his recovery was expected to take about a week.
