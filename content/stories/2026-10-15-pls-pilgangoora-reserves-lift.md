PLS has reported a substantial lift in reserves at its wholly owned Pilgangoora operations in Western Australia, taking the ore reserve to 249.2 million tonnes at 1.24 percent lithium oxide, about 3.1 million tonnes of contained lithium. The update follows completion of the P200 expansion feasibility work and an underground pre-feasibility study, and includes a maiden underground reserve of 46.5 million tonnes at 1.50 percent.

The company put the gain down to better metallurgical understanding and the new Jimmagunya plant, which lets more mineralised material be recovered economically. The larger reserve supports about 20 years of open pit operation on the P2000 schedule and more than 25 years including underground material, while the total mineral resource was put at 403 million tonnes at 1.33 percent.
