PLS Group has approved a final investment decision for the P2000 expansion of its wholly owned Pilgangoora operations in Western Australia, centred on a new Jimmagunya processing plant. Capital expenditure is put at A$1.9 billion, 46 percent above the inflation-adjusted estimate in the 2024 pre-feasibility study after changes to the mining plan and processing design, with up to A$650 million more in separately approved infrastructure. The company said the project would be funded from its cash balance and operating cash flow, with debt funding assessed if needed, and its shares rose 3 percent on the announcement.

The expansion is expected to lift production capacity to 2 million tonnes a year, with average output of 1.97 million tonnes a year over the first decade at steady state, unit operating costs near A$620 a tonne and first production targeted for the September quarter of 2029. PLS also raised its fiscal 2027 capital expenditure guidance to between A$1.11 billion and A$1.175 billion.

The decision follows the reserves update reported earlier on Thursday. PLS said the ore reserve at Pilgangoora rose to 249.2 million tonnes at 1.24 percent lithium oxide, a 23 percent rise in tonnes and a 31 percent rise in contained lithium, including a first underground reserve of 46.5 million tonnes at 1.50 percent. The company put the gain down to better metallurgical understanding and the new Jimmagunya plant, which lets more mineralised material be recovered economically, and said the mining plan now recovers about 67 million tonnes of material previously classified as waste.
