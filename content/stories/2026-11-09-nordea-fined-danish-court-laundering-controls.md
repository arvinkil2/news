Copenhagen City Court on Monday fined Nordea 155 million Danish kroner, about 23 million dollars, after finding the bank partly guilty on two of seven counts over the adequacy of its anti-money laundering processes and controls between 2012 and 2015. The court acquitted the bank on the other five counts, and Nordea stressed it was never charged with money laundering itself or with assisting it.

The fine is a small fraction of the 6.6 billion kroner, about 880 million euros, the Prosecution Service had claimed in what was the first money laundering investigation of a bank to reach a Danish court rather than end in a settlement. Nordea said the verdict has no immediate financial impact, that it will review the full judgment before deciding whether to appeal, and that the initial appeal window runs two weeks from the verdict.
