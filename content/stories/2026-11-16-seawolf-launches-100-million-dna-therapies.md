Seawolf Therapeutics, a San Diego company, emerged from stealth on November 16 with $100 million in financing to develop DNA-based therapies delivered into cells by lipid nanoparticles rather than viruses. Founding investor Versant Ventures led the seed funding and joined a Series A alongside MPM BioImpact and Alexandria Venture Investments, with Eli Lilly, CRISPR Therapeutics and Novartis's venture arm also contributing.

Seawolf also disclosed a multi-target research collaboration and exclusive licence agreement with Sanofi focused on bleeding disorders and other indications, worth up to $1.2 billion in milestone payments plus tiered royalties. Its TRIDENT platform wraps engineered DNA in a proprietary lipid nanoparticle designed to reach the cell nucleus without triggering an immune response, which the company says allows repeat dosing that viral delivery systems cannot match; its lead programme targets metabolic diseases.
