Statistics Netherlands (CBS) reported that 322 businesses were declared bankrupt in October 2026, 69 more than in October last year and one more than in September 2026. The bankruptcy rate rose to 8.5 per 100 thousand businesses, from 6.8 a year earlier, after holding between about 7 and 8 since mid-2025.

The sharpest deterioration was in manufacturing, where the bankruptcy rate rose from 19.5 to 24.8 per 100 thousand businesses year on year. Accommodation and food services recorded the highest rate of any sector in October, at 25.1 per 100 thousand businesses, up from 23.3 a year earlier.
