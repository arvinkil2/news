Genesis AI, the Silicon Valley robotics startup co-founded in 2024 by Zhou Xian and Théophile Gervet, said it has raised a $500 million Series A led by Khosla Ventures, Premji Invest and Eclipse, TechCrunch reported. The round follows a $105 million seed announced in July 2025, and the company says its physical AI model has cracked dexterous manipulation, trained in part on force data from sensor equipped gloves worn by more than 100 workers worldwide.

The company has built 30 to 40 of its Eno humanoid robots, has chosen a mass manufacturing contractor to start work next year in the United States, and is piloting the machine with a handful of undisclosed customers, TechCrunch reported. Its dexterous hands currently last only a few weeks of regular work before they need replacing, a limit the company names as its key hardware bottleneck.
