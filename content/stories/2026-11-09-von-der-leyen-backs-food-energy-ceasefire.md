European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said she is beginning a visit to the Black Sea region, meeting leaders in Romania and Bulgaria to discuss the situation on the ground and how Europe can strengthen its response, after what she called a good call with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. She said freedom of navigation in the Black Sea must be restored and preserved, and reiterated her support for Zelenskyy's call for a ceasefire covering food and energy infrastructure.

Von der Leyen said the two also discussed ongoing peace efforts and the talks in Abu Dhabi this week, where the EU will be present alongside its partners, and that she looks forward to meeting Zelenskyy in Dublin later this week to discuss the next steps towards peace with fellow leaders.
