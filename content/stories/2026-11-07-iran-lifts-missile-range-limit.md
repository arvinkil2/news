Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said on Saturday that the self-imposed limit of about 2,000 kilometres on its ballistic missile range "has been lifted for us," spokesman Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi told a press conference in Tehran marking the force's Aerospace Week. Mohebbi added that Iran would demonstrate these capabilities in actual action rather than in statements, and neither he nor the reporting around the press conference identified a missile that has flown beyond the old ceiling.

The announcement follows Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei's October 31 instruction that the military respond to threats without geographical limits and update its target lists to cover United States and Israeli assets. A 2,000 kilometre range from Iran already covers the Arabian Peninsula and parts of Greece and the Balkans; lifting the ceiling points future development toward Europe and the Indian Ocean, where Iran was reported to have attempted, and failed, a missile strike on the Diego Garcia base about 4,000 kilometres away in March.
