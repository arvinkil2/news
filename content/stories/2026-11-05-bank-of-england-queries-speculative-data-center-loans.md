The Bank of England has reached out to market participants to understand the details behind loans to UK data center projects that do not have any tenants, Bloomberg reported. The questions are not driven by a negative view of speculative lending itself, but by the Bank's effort to gauge the level of risk in a sector where project costs have ballooned as facility scale has expanded.

The Bank has been examining the interdependence of data center financing over the past year, and its Financial Policy Committee warned last month that AI linked investments' increasing leverage, opacity and, at times, circular arrangements could complicate risk assessment and amplify losses if expectations disappointed. The Bank for International Settlements has also warned that poor disclosure of deal terms means the same data center asset may be repeatedly pledged.
