President Donald Trump will present two national honors at a "Science: A New Golden Age Summit" at the White House on Thursday, October 8, according to CNBC. The National Medal of Science goes to Elon Musk, Google co-founder Sergey Brin, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and AMD CEO Lisa Su, while the National Medal of Technology and Innovation goes to Dell Technologies chief Michael Dell and Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella. A White House spokesperson said the administration is grateful for the honorees' contributions to keeping the US ahead in innovation. The summit comes just over a week after Trump hosted the same circle of executives at a White House AI lunch that produced a voluntary, "morally binding" AI-safety accord. CNBC also notes each recipient has contributed financially to Trump or Republican causes, and that Trump's most recent financial disclosure showed trades in SpaceX, Tesla, Nvidia, Microsoft, AMD and Alphabet shares as recently as July.
