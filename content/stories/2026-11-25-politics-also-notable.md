UNAIDS warned on Tuesday that decades of gains against AIDS could unravel as HIV funding dries up. Some 32.1 million of the 40 million people living with HIV are now on treatment, but the agency put the funding gap in low and middle income countries at 4.3 billion dollars. In 2025 around 446,000 fewer people gained access to pre-exposure prophylaxis than the previous year, global condom distribution fell from three billion to 2.7 billion between 2024 and 2025, and external resources account for up to 98 percent of all HIV resources in Nigeria, 94 percent in Mozambique and 80 percent in Uganda, the agency said.

The US Government Accountability Office has sent Congress its bid protest annual report for fiscal year 2026. GAO received 1,587 cases in the year, 1,500 of them protests, a 6 percent decrease in filings from the year before, and closed 1,600 cases. Of protests resolved on the merits it sustained 10 percent, with unreasonable technical evaluation, unreasonable past performance evaluation and flawed selection decisions the most prevalent grounds. GAO recorded no instance of an agency failing to implement one of its recommendations, and noted that the 43 day funding lapse at the start of the fiscal year coincided with first quarter filings of 318 cases against a prior four year average of 474.

The Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control has published a further sanctions notice in Wednesday's Federal Register, document 2026-23961, recording additions to the Specially Designated Nationals list made on November 20, 2026 under Executive Order 13224, the terrorism authority, and Executive Order 13902, the Iran authority. The names of the designees appear in the printed notice as a graphic rather than text, so they are recorded here without names; the legal effect stated in the notice is the standard blocking of property subject to US jurisdiction.
