SpaceX is in talks either to acquire Boost Mobile, the wireless service owned by EchoStar, or to gain access to Boost Mobile's network, which is leased from AT&T and T-Mobile, the Washington Post reported. SpaceX has held talks with EchoStar in recent months about launching its own wireless service through a deal with the company.

The aim, as described in the report, is a hybrid satellite and terrestrial mobile network under Starlink Mobile, combining the Starlink constellation with ground-based infrastructure to compete directly with the US wireless carriers. No price or timeline was reported, and SpaceX and EchoStar did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
