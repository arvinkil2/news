Voting in Hong Kong's 2026 Election Committee Subsector Ordinary Elections closed at 6pm on Sunday with a provisional cumulative turnout of 98.79 percent, the Electoral Affairs Commission said. A total of 5,640 registered voters cast ballots out of an electorate of 5,709, surpassing the 89.77 percent turnout recorded in the 2021 polls, according to official figures reported by China Daily Asia.

A total of 856 candidates contested 723 seats in 31 subsectors, the commission chairman, Mr Justice David Lok, said during polling. The elections fill the elected portion of the 1,500 member Election Committee: 982 seats are chosen by eligible corporate and individual voters in the subsectors, 156 are nominated by designated bodies, and the remainder are held ex officio.

The committee is a key component of Hong Kong's electoral system. Its members nominate candidates for the chief executive election and elect the chief executive designate, and the committee also elects 40 of the 90 members of the Legislative Council. The new committee's five year term begins on February 1, 2027, and its first major task is the chief executive election scheduled for March 2027.

Counting took place at the central counting station at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre after polls closed, with results to be published by press release and on the election website. The turnout figures are provisional and for reference until the Registration and Electoral Office verifies the count.
