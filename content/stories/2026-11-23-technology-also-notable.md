AMD amended its bylaws effective November 18, in an 8-K filed November 20 (accession 0000002488-26-000218). Among other changes, the amendments let the company disregard votes for a stockholder nominee who did not comply with the SEC's universal proxy rules, including the requirement to solicit holders of at least 67 percent of the voting power, and clarify nomination procedures. It is a governance tightening, not an operating development.

Oracle filed the results of its November 18 annual meeting (Item 5.07, accession 0001193125-26-470351), recording the election of directors to serve until the 2027 meeting and the other votes put to stockholders, as set out in its September 25 proxy statement. No operating or leadership change was disclosed.

Hesai Group, the lidar maker that describes itself as a physical AI infrastructure company, will join the Hang Seng TECH Index after the market close on December 4, taking effect December 7, according to its November 23 6-K (accession 0001104659-26-130777), citing the Hang Seng Indexes announcement of November 20. The index holds 50 Hong Kong-listed technology companies; Hesai has been in the Hang Seng Composite Index since December 8, 2025.

Alibaba granted awards over 3,947,118 underlying shares, about 0.02 percent of shares in issue, to employees under its 2024 Plan on November 23, at a closing price of HK$83.05 per share, with mixed vesting schedules of up to four years and no performance targets, per its 6-K of the same date (accession 0001104659-26-130757). Baidu's 6-K in the same pool was a routine Hong Kong next-day disclosure of share awards vesting and carries no new development.
