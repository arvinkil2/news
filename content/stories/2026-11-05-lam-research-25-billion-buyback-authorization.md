Lam Research said on Thursday that its board of directors approved a 25 billion dollar share repurchase authorization, supplementing the remaining balance under its prior authorization. The programme has no termination date, may be suspended or discontinued at any time, and does not oblige the company to buy any shares. The board also declared a quarterly dividend of 0.33 dollars a share, up 0.07 dollars, or 27 percent, from 0.26 dollars, payable on January 6, 2027 to holders of record on December 2, 2026.

The announcement came in a Form 8-K that also reported the results of the company's annual meeting on November 3, where all ten director nominees were elected and a shareholder proposal on the right to call a special meeting was not approved, with 42.22 percent of votes cast in favour.
