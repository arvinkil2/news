Autonomy software firm Auterion and Ukrainian drone producer Skyfall announced on November 20 that they intend to form a joint venture to manufacture drones for Ukraine at high volume in a new factory in Germany. The agreement was signed at the ninth German-Ukrainian Economic Forum in Berlin, in the presence of Economy Minister Katherina Reiche, Chancellor Friedrich Merz and Ukrainian Prime Minister Serhii Koretskyi, under the official Build with Ukraine defence cooperation framework.

The venture, Skyfall x Auterion, will start production with the Shrike first person view strike platform and later expand to Skyfall interceptors and middle and deep strike systems. Every drone pairs a Skyfall airframe with Auterion avionics and its AuterionOS operating system. The partners say they have already delivered more than 50,000 Shrike strike drones to Ukrainian units together.

Reuters reported that the German government will fund the factory and drone orders, in the range of hundreds of millions of euros, first supplying Ukraine and later the German armed forces. The companies' own announcement does not state a funding figure, a factory site or a production start date.

Auterion, based in Arlington, Virginia and Munich, said a German plant adds production capacity outside the range of air strikes while keeping a short innovation cycle: feedback from frontline units reaches Skyfall's engineers weekly and goes straight into the German line. For Germany, the factory creates domestic manufacturing jobs and a long term domestic option for drones proven in combat in Ukraine.
