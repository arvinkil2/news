The Bank of Korea kept adding to gold after resuming purchases in the second quarter for the first time in 13 years. Its report to the US Securities and Exchange Commission shows it held 956,869 shares of the SPDR Gold trust at the end of the third quarter, up 277,104 shares, or 40.8 percent, from the second quarter, with the position's value up 45.5 percent to $364.41 million.

The fourth quarter has run the other way. The fund closed at $355.45 on November 13, 6.7 percent below its third quarter end valuation of $380.84 a share, which on unchanged holdings would cut the position to about $340.12 million, a fall of $24.29 million, about 32.5 billion won. The same filing shows the bank also raised its S&P 500 index fund holdings by 14 to 18 percent in the quarter, and Yonhap reports it is pursuing purchases of domestically produced physical gold as early as next month.
