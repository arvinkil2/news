Anthropic asked the D.C. Circuit on Tuesday to rehear its appeal against the Defence Department's March decision to designate the company a supply chain risk, a label that bars defence contractors from using Claude in their Pentagon work. The company argues the designation was retaliation for its refusal to allow its models to be used for mass surveillance of Americans or lethal autonomous warfare, and for speaking publicly about those commitments.

A divided panel upheld the Pentagon in September by two votes to one, rejecting Anthropic's claim that the ban was arbitrary, unauthorised and unconstitutional, while a federal judge in San Francisco ruled in August that the designation violated the company's First Amendment rights. Anthropic had signed a 200 million dollar Pentagon contract in July 2025, but talks over deploying Claude on the department's GenAI.mil platform collapsed that September.
