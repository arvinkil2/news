Canada's Industrial Product Price Index edged up 0.3 percent in October and gained 12.6 percent year over year, its 25th consecutive month of annual increase, Statistics Canada reported. Energy and petroleum products rose 1.2 percent on the month, with diesel fuel up 93.3 percent and light fuel oils up 100.3 percent from a year earlier; excluding energy and petroleum, the index rose 0.1 percent in October. The Raw Materials Price Index fell 1.0 percent on the month, as metal ores and scrap dropped 5.4 percent, but was up 23.5 percent year over year, led by crude energy products.
