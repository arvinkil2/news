Samsung Electronics is in talks to buy Modine Manufacturing, which develops cooling systems for data centers, Bloomberg reported on Thursday, citing people familiar with the matter. The two companies are discussing a potential deal that could come together as soon as the coming weeks, and the report carried no price or other terms.

Modine shares surged more than 28 percent in after hours trading on Thursday to $136.22, after closing at $106.10, down 0.53 percent. The company, founded in 1916, has benefited from rising demand for its data center thermal management systems as AI data center construction has grown, and Samsung operates 18 global cloud centers through its SDS arm.
