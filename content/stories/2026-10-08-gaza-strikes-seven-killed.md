Israeli strikes killed at least seven people in Gaza on Thursday, according to the Gaza Civil Defense agency, whose account was carried by Greek public broadcaster ERT citing the APE-MPE wire and AFP. Drone strikes hit a car, killing two occupants, and a tent in a displaced-persons camp, killing a man; an airstrike on an apartment building in Gaza City killed a woman and a child and wounded others; one person was killed by Israeli gunfire southeast of Deir al-Balah, and health officials reported a woman shot dead in Gaza City.

The Israeli military told AFP that three of the dead were "fighters of terrorist organizations" and confirmed to AFP the reports about the four other victims. Hamas on Thursday denied Israel's claim, made Wednesday, that its fighters operated from Kamal Adwan hospital in northern Gaza, calling it a pretext.
