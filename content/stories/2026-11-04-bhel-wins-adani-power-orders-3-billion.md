Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd., the state-owned power equipment manufacturer, has secured contracts worth as much as 280 billion rupees ($3 billion) to supply equipment to Adani Power Ltd., Bloomberg reported, citing a company statement issued on Wednesday. The orders cover four projects, each with two 800-megawatt units, for 6,400 megawatts in total, and each contract is valued at between 50 billion and 70 billion rupees.

BHEL shares rose on the announcement, according to the report, though the size of the move was not in the accessible text. Adani Power, controlled by Gautam Adani, is India's largest private thermal power producer, and the orders add to a run of private-sector spending on new generating capacity.
