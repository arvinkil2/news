FinCEN, the Treasury Department's financial intelligence unit, is investigating a cybersecurity incident that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems and has taken some systems offline as a proactive measure, the agency said in a notice on its Bank Secrecy Act E-Filing website. The E-Filing website itself remains operational, though some services may be temporarily unavailable, and Treasury is working with the FBI, the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency and third-party forensic examiners to determine the scope and impact, a FinCEN official told Reuters.

In an order issued on November 24, FinCEN granted temporary, limited exceptive relief to filers hindered by the disruption: financial institutions that used Secure Data Transfer Mode as of November 18, and companies and individuals with beneficial ownership submissions due, now have until December 30, 2026 to file reports otherwise due between November 18 and December 29. While forensic analysis remains ongoing, there is no evidence of any breach of the Bank Secrecy Act or beneficial ownership databases, the official said.
