Anthropic is offering Blackstone a yield of more than 10 percent to finance its latest chip deal, the Financial Times reported on Friday, citing people familiar with the matter. Blackstone funds would invest 9 billion dollars in debt backed by Anthropic's five year lease payments on the chips. The reported pricing attaches to the junior side of the 60 billion dollar Broadcom chip financing package that banks have been syndicating since early October, and Reuters said it could not immediately verify the report.
