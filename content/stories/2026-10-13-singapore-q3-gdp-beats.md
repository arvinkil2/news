Singapore's gross domestic product grew 5.4 percent in the third quarter from a year earlier, down from a revised 6.2 percent in the second quarter, which was a four year high, but ahead of the 5 percent consensus in a Reuters poll. Manufacturing, about a fifth of the economy, declined in the quarter, partly offset by an expansion in construction.

The Monetary Authority of Singapore kept policy unchanged, pausing after tightening in April and July on inflation risks from higher energy prices, and the government maintained the full year growth forecast it raised in August, at 4.5 to 5.5 percent, the second upgrade this year after beats powered by AI related sectors and exports.
