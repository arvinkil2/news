Spain has revised its renewable energy mandate for data centers, detailed in the full story in today's edition, the latest adjustment in Europe's running negotiation between AI capacity and power rules, and another entry in the administrative constraint theme that runs through this week's editions from Oregon, Texas and Britain.

OpenAI has applied for a federal permit to build part of a data center in Effingham County, Texas, on wetlands, Datacenter Dynamics reported, a filing that puts the company's Texas expansion into the federal environmental review track and gives opponents a formal process to contest it.

Cadence and Nvidia unveiled new AI models aimed at putting electronic design automation on autopilot, The Next Platform reported, extending the pair's collaboration from simulation into the design flow itself, with Nvidia also simulating its way into quantum error correction research alongside Google in a separate Next Platform item.

Former Intel chief executive Pat Gelsinger is betting on Nagare Labs to get past the memory wall, The Register reported, a notable move because the memory bottleneck is the same constraint imec's vertical DRAM work in the research pool addresses from the device side.

Microsoft introduced new sovereignty tools across its public and private cloud offerings and acquired 37 acres in Licking County, Ohio, two Datacenter Dynamics items that pair the company's European regulatory positioning with continued land banking near its United States data center clusters.

Britain is betting on AI to defend against threats AI is helping create, The Register reported, and separately the United Kingdom government is looking at tougher prison sentences and unlimited fines for subsea cable saboteurs, Datacenter Dynamics reported, two faces of the same state response to AI era infrastructure risk.

Matiks raised $10 million to expand its math and puzzle gaming platform, TechCrunch reported, a small round that clears the pool's bar only as a marker of consumer subscription gaming still attracting growth capital. South Korea's Bae Kyung-hoon smart glasses item and the Grok Odyssey release were covered in yesterday's roundup and are not repeated.
