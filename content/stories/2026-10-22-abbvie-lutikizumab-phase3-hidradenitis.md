AbbVie said lutikizumab, its dual IL-1 alpha and IL-1 beta antagonist, met the primary endpoint and eight ranked secondary endpoints in the 1,400 patient INTREPID trial, with skin pain response at week 8 in 26.2 percent of treated patients against 15.0 percent on placebo and flares over 16 weeks in 18.4 percent against 28.1 percent. Adverse event rates were similar to placebo, at 53.4 percent against 53.7 percent for any event and 3.0 percent against 2.9 percent for serious events, and the company identified no new safety concerns. AbbVie expects data from the 36 week blinded extension in 2027 to support regulatory submissions in the second half of that year, and plans a platform study starting this year that tests lutikizumab with its long acting amylin drug ABBV-295.
