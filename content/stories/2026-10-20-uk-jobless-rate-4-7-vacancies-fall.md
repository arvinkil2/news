Britain's unemployment rate fell to 4.7 percent in June to August 2026, down 0.1 percentage points on both the year and the quarter, while the employment rate for people aged 16 to 64 rose 0.2 points to 75.3 percent, the Office for National Statistics said on Tuesday. Annual growth in regular pay held at 3.7 percent, stable for a sixth consecutive three-month period, and total pay growth also came in at 3.7 percent, its lowest reading since August to October 2020. Vacancies fell by 21,000 to 691,000 in July to September, the largest quarterly fall since January to March and the lowest level outside the pandemic since 2014, while the early estimate for September payrolled employees was broadly flat on the month at 30.3 million.
