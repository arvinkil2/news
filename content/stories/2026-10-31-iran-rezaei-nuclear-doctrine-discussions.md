Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, said about 70 percent of the Iranian population demand a change to the country's nuclear doctrine, according to remarks cited by the state broadcaster IRIB and reported by Lenta. He said discussions on changing the doctrine are underway but that no new decision has yet been taken, and he did not describe what change is under discussion.

Lenta also noted Rezaei had earlier said talks between Iran and the United States would take place only if Washington accepts Tehran's seven conditions, without listing them in this item.
