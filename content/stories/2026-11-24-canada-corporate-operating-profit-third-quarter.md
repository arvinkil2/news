Canadian corporations' operating profit declined to $236.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down $4.9 billion, or 2.0 percent, from the second quarter, Statistics Canada said in its quarterly financial statistics for enterprises. Non-financial industries recorded a $2.9 billion decrease, or 2.2 percent, to $127.9 billion, and financial industries a $2.0 billion decrease, or 1.8 percent, to $108.7 billion. Compared with the same quarter a year earlier, operating profit was up $28.0 billion, or 13.4 percent.

Oil and gas extraction was the largest contributor to the non-financial decline, with operating profit down $4.1 billion, or 19.6 percent, as crude oil prices eased from their second quarter peak. Manufacturing operating profit rose by $995 million in the quarter, led by petroleum and coal product manufacturing and by pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing.
