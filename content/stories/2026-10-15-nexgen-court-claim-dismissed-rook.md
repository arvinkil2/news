A Saskatchewan court has dismissed a legal claim against NexGen Energy, the company said, removing a litigation overhang from the Rook I uranium project, one of the world's highest grade undeveloped uranium deposits.

Rook I's path runs through federal licensing and Saskatchewan's courts of public opinion as much as its courtrooms, and each dismissed claim narrows the set of obstacles to permits, financing, and a construction decision that would make Canada the West's swing uranium supplier. The Arrow deposit at Rook I is the asset on which the domestic fuel strategy, visible in this week's uranium ETF flows, ultimately depends.

NexGen's shares sit inside every uranium basket, so legal clarity at the project level feeds directly into the policy trade described elsewhere in this edition.
