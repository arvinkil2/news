**Latest (Sunday 02:42 ET):** Russia's defence ministry said air defences destroyed 1,015 Ukrainian drones overnight, over regions including Belgorod, Bryansk, Kursk, Smolensk and Leningrad, over Krasnodar territory, Crimea and the Black Sea, and over the Moscow region. Governor Drozdenko raised the Leningrad region count to 144 drones. In St Petersburg, Governor Beglov said one person was killed and five people were injured and in hospital, revising his earlier statement that there had been no fatalities, and that 12 apartment buildings and two private houses were damaged, with evacuated residents returning home and repair work under way. Ozon said a fire broke out at one of its St Petersburg logistics centres during the attack, that all staff were evacuated and no one there was hurt, and that emergency services were working at the site.

**Earlier (Sunday 01:41 ET):** Governor Alexander Drozdenko raised the overnight count to 135 drones destroyed over Leningrad region. He said debris fell on an apartment building in Gatchina, 55 parked cars were damaged in Novoselye, and in Vsevolozhsk district debris set a residential house on fire and a woman and three children were hospitalised with symptoms of carbon monoxide poisoning. He said two transformer substations in Yanino remained shut down in an emergency outage, leaving 50 apartment buildings and about 20,000 people without electricity.

Leningrad region Governor Alexander Drozdenko said air defences destroyed 126 drones over the region in a wave that continued into Sunday morning, Moscow time, after an earlier count of 95, with debris falling on residential houses and power lines. St Petersburg Governor Alexander Beglov said drones attacked the St Petersburg Oil Terminal and civilian infrastructure objects, and that emergency crews were putting out fires in the Nevsky, Krasnoselsky, Krasnogvardeisky and Moskovsky districts and at the terminal, with Pulkovo airport operations restricted for safety and no fatalities. VK said its data centre in Leningrad region was attacked and that its services remained available to users as normal.
