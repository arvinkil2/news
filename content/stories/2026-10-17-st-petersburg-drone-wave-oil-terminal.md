**Latest (Sunday 01:41 ET):** Governor Alexander Drozdenko raised the overnight count to 135 drones destroyed over Leningrad region. He said debris fell on an apartment building in Gatchina, 55 parked cars were damaged in Novoselye, and in Vsevolozhsk district debris set a residential house on fire and a woman and three children were hospitalised with symptoms of carbon monoxide poisoning. He said two transformer substations in Yanino remained shut down in an emergency outage, leaving 50 apartment buildings and about 20,000 people without electricity.

Leningrad region Governor Alexander Drozdenko said air defences destroyed 126 drones over the region in a wave that continued into Sunday morning, Moscow time, after an earlier count of 95, with debris falling on residential houses and power lines. St Petersburg Governor Alexander Beglov said drones attacked the St Petersburg Oil Terminal and civilian infrastructure objects, and that emergency crews were putting out fires in the Nevsky, Krasnoselsky, Krasnogvardeisky and Moskovsky districts and at the terminal, with Pulkovo airport operations restricted for safety and no fatalities. VK said its data centre in Leningrad region was attacked and that its services remained available to users as normal.
