Borr Drilling's Prospector 5 rig sank at about 9:40 p.m. on Saturday in the Dussafu Marin permit area off Mayumba, Gabon, killing one worker, an Indian national, and leaving four people missing, three Gabonese nationals and one other expatriate, among the 115 people on board, Reuters reported. Borr Drilling said preliminary information indicated the rig experienced a punch-through event during preload activities, losing stability and sinking, and Gabon's petroleum ministry said it had activated a crisis unit, ordered the navy to reinforce search and rescue at sea and ordered a technical investigation. The rig was contracted to support BW Energy's drilling operations and had not yet begun drilling, Panoro Energy, which holds 17.5 percent of the licence against BW Energy's 73.5 percent, said production had been halted with no reports of damage or accidental discharges, and the ministry said no discharge of harmful substances had been observed.

Update, October 28: Search and rescue operations continued for the five individuals Borr Drilling counts as unaccounted for, the company said in an update on October 26 carried by PR Newswire and Offshore Energy. Of the 115 personnel on board, 110 have been safely accounted for and are being brought ashore in Gabon, where care and support is being provided, and extensive marine and air operations remain ongoing. Borr said its onshore emergency response team continues to work with local authorities and BW Energy, that it is in contact with the families of the missing personnel, and that a thorough investigation will be conducted. The company statement counts five unaccounted for; Reuters reporting cited in the original story reported one worker found dead and four missing, and the two counts are carried as their sources state them. Borr shares fell sharply on Monday, down up to 11.4 percent intraday in market reporting, with BW Energy and Panoro Energy also down.
