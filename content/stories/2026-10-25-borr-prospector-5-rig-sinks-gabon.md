Borr Drilling's Prospector 5 rig sank at about 9:40 p.m. on Saturday in the Dussafu Marin permit area off Mayumba, Gabon, killing one worker, an Indian national, and leaving four people missing, three Gabonese nationals and one other expatriate, among the 115 people on board, Reuters reported. Borr Drilling said preliminary information indicated the rig experienced a punch-through event during preload activities, losing stability and sinking, and Gabon's petroleum ministry said it had activated a crisis unit, ordered the navy to reinforce search and rescue at sea and ordered a technical investigation. The rig was contracted to support BW Energy's drilling operations and had not yet begun drilling, Panoro Energy, which holds 17.5 percent of the licence against BW Energy's 73.5 percent, said production had been halted with no reports of damage or accidental discharges, and the ministry said no discharge of harmful substances had been observed.
