Amazon is in talks to acquire artificial intelligence startup Decart in a deal that could be valued at around $7 billion, the Wall Street Journal reported Sunday, citing people familiar with the matter. Decart, founded in 2023 by Israeli engineers and valued at about $4 billion after a $300 million funding round in May, builds software that helps developers run AI workloads efficiently across different computing chips. No final agreement has been announced. The talks follow a reported September approach by Anthropic at around $6 billion that did not proceed, per Bloomberg.
