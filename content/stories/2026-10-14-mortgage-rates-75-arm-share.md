Mortgage applications fell 2.7 percent in the week ending October 9 as the average 30-year fixed rate edged up to 7.50 percent from 7.49 percent, more than a percentage point above a year ago and the highest since November 2023, according to the Mortgage Bankers Association. Purchase applications fell 4 percent and refinance applications were 56 percent below a year earlier, while the adjustable-rate share rose to 10.5 percent, its highest since October 2025, as borrowers traded reset risk for lower initial payments.
