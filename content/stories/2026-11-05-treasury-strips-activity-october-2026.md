The Bureau of the Fiscal Service said $633.7 billion of Treasury securities was held in stripped form in October under the STRIPS program, against $23.256 trillion held in unstripped form, out of $23.889 trillion of principal outstanding on eligible securities. Holders reconstituted $43.1 billion of stripped securities back into unstripped form during the month.

The bureau said the balances are subject to audit and subsequent revision, and that the monthly figures appear in Table V of the Monthly Statement of the Public Debt.
