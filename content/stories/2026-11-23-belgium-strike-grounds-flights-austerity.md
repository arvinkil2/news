Air traffic controllers' strike action halted all flights at Brussels Airport and at Charleroi on Monday, with no aircraft taking off or landing at Brussels between 07:00 and 21:00 local time and hundreds of flights cancelled across Belgium. More than 50,000 travellers' plans were disrupted, with more expected to be affected overnight and into Tuesday, and trains, public transport, schools and other public services were also hit.

The strike protests planned austerity measures that Prime Minister Bart De Wever has so far failed to agree with his coalition partners; King Philippe declined De Wever's resignation earlier this month and gave him more time to negotiate. The FGTB union said the government had alternatives to dismantling the social protection system, while the coalition partner Vooruit, which has stalled the budget talks, wants higher taxes on the rich and fewer cuts to pensions and health.
