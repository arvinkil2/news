A car bomb in Moscow on Friday injured Serhiy Zavorotny, a former adviser to Mykola Azarov, the pro-Kremlin former prime minister of Ukraine who fled to Russia in 2014, according to Russia's Investigative Committee, which said Zavorotny was driving the car when it exploded and was taken to hospital. A day earlier, a similar car bomb in the Pushkinsky district of St Petersburg killed a Russian colonel. No claim of responsibility for either attack had been reported, and Russian authorities characterised the blasts as the latest in a series targeting pro-Kremlin figures, against the backdrop of continuing informal peace talks in Dublin and overnight strikes by both sides that Ukrainian and Russian authorities said killed at least 12 people.
