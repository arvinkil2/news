Ferrovial said its subsidiary Webber will deliver the Mary's Creek Water Reclamation Facility for the City of Fort Worth under a $537.9 million contract, the company's largest water infrastructure project in the United States so far. The greenfield facility includes equalization and peak flow storage basins, preliminary treatment, bioreactors and advanced wastewater treatment with membrane bioreactor technology, solids handling and odor control, and once operating it will process 10 million gallons of water per day while supplying reclaimed water for industrial and irrigation use.

Construction is scheduled to begin in November 2026 and to finish by summer 2030. Ferrovial Construction chief executive Ignacio Gaston said the project would strengthen the region's water infrastructure as Texas communities grow, and Webber already holds water contracts in Austin and Fort Worth worth $721 million from last year, according to Construction Dive.
