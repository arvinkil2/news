President Trump said on Fox & Friends on Tuesday morning that US aid to Israel will be cut off at the end of this year, putting the amount at $4 billion a year and saying he and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu had agreed the change months ago, with Netanyahu raising the idea. He said Israel no longer needs the assistance and can purchase its own weapons. The current US Israel security assistance agreement provides about $3.8 billion annually through 2028, and neither government has published terms for ending it early.
