The refrigerators at three regional blood banks in France were hit by a cyberattack overnight between Sunday and Monday, at the same time hackers struck supermarkets from Sweden to Belgium, Reuters reported on Wednesday. The health ministry said several regional facilities of the French Blood Establishment were affected by an incident that impacted certain cold storage room monitoring and control systems, and an establishment spokesperson said there was no impact on blood supplies, with round the clock staff alerted by alarms and moving blood to refrigerators that were not connected. The ministry said the situation was under control on Wednesday, remote access to the affected refrigerators had been disabled, and investigations are under way into how the attack was carried out and when systems can safely return to service.
