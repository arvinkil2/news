Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said an oil tanker struck a naval mine in the southern Strait of Hormuz, causing an explosion and a fire in its engine room, Anadolu Ajansı reported. The Guards said the ship had switched off its location and navigation systems from departure and tried to cross by an unauthorized southern route in disregard of Iran's rules for the strait. The statement warned that vessels in the Strait of Hormuz and the Persian Gulf that switch off their navigation and identification systems will face a firm and decisive response.
