PepsiCo will cut about 1,000 jobs across its North America business over the next week, CNBC reported, citing a Monday letter to employees from Ram Krishnan, chief executive of PepsiCo North America, in which he said the unit needs to be faster and more connected while continuing to invest behind its people, capabilities and brands. The Wall Street Journal first reported the coming layoffs. PepsiCo employs about 306,000 people worldwide, including roughly 125,000 in the United States.

The cuts sit inside a home market turnaround that is running behind plan. PepsiCo lowered its 2026 earnings outlook in October, and in the third quarter North American beverage volume fell 2 percent while North American food volume was flat; finance chief Steve Schmitt told the earnings call that despite price cuts and added marketing investment the turnaround is taking longer than expected. Shares rose slightly in afternoon trading on Monday, and are down nearly 1 percent this year against a gain of nearly 11 percent for the S&P 500.
