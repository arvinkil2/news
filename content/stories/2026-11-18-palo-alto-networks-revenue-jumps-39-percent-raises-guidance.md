**Latest:** The Applied Compute acquisition is a $4.5 billion all-stock deal, CNBC reported, and Palo Alto Networks shares rose more than 7 percent in after-hours trading after the earnings beat. Chief executive Nikesh Arora told the earnings call the deal could become bigger than the company's cybersecurity business.

Palo Alto Networks opened its fiscal 2027 with a record first quarter. Revenue for the quarter ended October 31, 2026 grew 39 percent year over year to $3.44 billion, next-generation security annualised recurring revenue grew 66 percent to $9.73 billion, and remaining performance obligations grew 35 percent to $21.0 billion. Non-GAAP net income was $881 million, or $1.05 per diluted share, and adjusted free cash flow was $1.9 billion, a 56.2 percent margin.

Management said demand justified materially higher guidance. For the full fiscal year the company now expects revenue of $14.52 billion to $14.57 billion, up 26 to 27 percent, next-generation security ARR of $11.475 billion to $11.525 billion, and diluted non-GAAP earnings of $4.26 to $4.30 a share. The company also announced its intent to acquire Applied Compute, a platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models, which it said will let it deliver secure open weight models to customers.
