The Competition and Markets Authority published its final decisions on Thursday designating Aldi Stores Limited, Lidl Great Britain Limited and Lidl Northern Ireland Limited as Large Grocery Retailers under the Groceries Market Investigation (Controlled Land) Order 2010. The Order, which has applied to Asda, Co-op, Marks and Spencer, Morrisons, Sainsbury's, Tesco and Waitrose since 2010, stops designated chains from using restrictive covenants and exclusivity arrangements in land agreements to make it harder for a rival supermarket to open nearby.

Designation means Aldi and Lidl cannot include prohibited restrictions in new land agreements, and their existing agreements can be reviewed by the CMA at the request of landowners, developers and rival supermarkets. Juliette Enser, the CMA's Executive Director for Competition Enforcement and Markets, said the rules would stop the discounters blocking rival stores in their area and level the playing field for shoppers.
