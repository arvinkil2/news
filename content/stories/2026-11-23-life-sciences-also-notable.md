NuCana reported preclinical data on November 20 showing that NUC-7738 enhanced the anti cancer activity of carfilzomib, an approved proteasome inhibitor used in multiple myeloma. The findings were presented at the EORTC-NCI-AACR symposium in Barcelona, held November 18 to 20. The data are preclinical, with no human results claimed.

Purple Biotech reported a plenary oral presentation at the same symposium on its CAPTN-3 platform of capped tri specific antibodies, presented by Mount Sinai immunologist Dr. Amir Horowitz. The platform combines an arm that blocks the NKG2A inhibitory checkpoint with a capped CD3 arm designed to activate T cells only inside the tumor microenvironment.

Exicure said on November 20 that it had entered a strategic business expansion and investment agreement with NeuroQ Biologics and Laonz on November 19, tied to an expansion into medical device technologies designed to assist patients with Parkinson's disease. The 8-K furnishes the press release and does not, in the portion filed, set out a purchase price.

Cytosorbents priced a registered direct offering in a prospectus supplement filed November 20, offering 556,681 shares of common stock at $4.94 per share, plus 96,527 shares to directors and certain executive officers at $5.18 per share, and pre funded warrants for up to 151,822 shares. It is a small financing, not a strategic transaction.

Haleon announced on November 23 that it had purchased 11,689,398 of its own ordinary shares to hold in treasury under the buyback program announced on October 29, 2026. GSK said on November 20 that it had transferred 22,000,000 treasury shares to JPMorgan Chase Bank as depositary, to procure the issuance of 11,000,000 American depositary receipts to the GlaxoSmithKline (US) Trust. Both are capital mechanics, not operating news.

Mesoblast filed a 6-K on November 23 covering a November 20 Australian Securities Exchange announcement that it received a Deloitte Technology Fast 50 leadership and enterprise growth award, which the company links to its transition to a commercial stage business after the United States launch of Ryoncil. HCW Biologics reported that its November 17 special meeting was adjourned without business for lack of a quorum, so no vote was taken on a share issuance proposal under Nasdaq rules.
