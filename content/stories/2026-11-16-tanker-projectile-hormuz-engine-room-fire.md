A tanker was targeted by an unknown projectile while sailing out of the Gulf through the Strait of Hormuz on Monday, causing a fire in its engine room, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency said, in a Reuters report carried by Arab News. No injuries were reported, and the extent of the damage and any potential environmental impact remain unknown.

The report does not name the vessel, its flag or its cargo, and it identifies no attacker. UKMTO placed the incident amid escalating threats to shipping in the strategic waterway, where projectile strikes on tankers have recurred in recent weeks.
