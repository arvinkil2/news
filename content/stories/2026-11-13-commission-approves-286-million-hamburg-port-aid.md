The European Commission approved two German state aid measures of 143 million euros each for infrastructure works at the port of Hamburg, direct grants paid by the City of Hamburg toward investment costs of 293 million euros at the Salzgitter quay and 258 million euros at the Steinwerder quay, with the port authority financing the rest from its own funds, debenture bonds and loans. The aid pays for replacing the decayed walls of the two quays, and the Commission found it proportionate because it does not exceed the funding gap over the projects' lifetimes and does not increase the port's capacity. The decisions will be published in the State aid register under case numbers SA.118016 and SA.118022.
