A magnitude 6.6 earthquake struck the Banda Sea at 21:11 UTC on Monday (06:11 Tuesday local time), 148 kilometres west-southwest of Tual in Indonesia's Maluku Islands, at a depth of 68 kilometres, according to the US Geological Survey, whose PAGER system issued a green alert indicating a low likelihood of casualties and damage, with about 8,100 people estimated to have felt moderate shaking. Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) measured the event at magnitude 7.1 and said it had no tsunami potential, Australia's Bureau of Meteorology said there was no tsunami threat to the Australian mainland or islands, and no casualties or damage had been reported.
