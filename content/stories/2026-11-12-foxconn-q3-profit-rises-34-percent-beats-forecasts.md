Foxconn, the world's largest contract electronics maker, reported third-quarter net profit of T$77.5 billion ($2.35 billion) on Thursday, up 34 percent from T$57.7 billion a year earlier and ahead of the LSEG consensus estimate of T$69.6 billion, on continued strong AI demand. The company, formally Hon Hai Precision Industry, stuck to its forecast of strong revenue growth this year and said it expects high double-digit growth in AI rack shipments in 2027.

The result follows a 47 percent year-on-year jump in third-quarter revenue reported in October. Foxconn shares closed 0.8 percent lower on Thursday ahead of the release, and the company holds its earnings call later on Thursday in Taipei.
