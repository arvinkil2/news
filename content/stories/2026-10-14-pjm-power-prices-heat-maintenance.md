PJM issued a Maximum Generation Alert for Tuesday and kept a capacity advisory in place through Wednesday, while day-ahead prices for Wednesday rose 28 percent to $295.83 per megawatt-hour, the highest daily average since mid-July. The Department of Energy emergency order running through Friday lets PJM run plants past environmental permit limits and, as a last resort, direct data centers and other large customers onto backup generation.
