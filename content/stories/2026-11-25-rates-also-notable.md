Japan's Ministry of Finance is due to meet JGB market participants on Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27, in the regular dialogue that follows auction results. The meeting takes on added weight after Wednesday's 40-year auction drew a bid-to-cover of 2.63, down from 3.10 at the previous sale, with investors focused on next year's issuance plans.

The Bank of Japan published its accounts as of November 20 and its latest data on Japanese government bonds held by the Bank, the standing statistical record of how much of the JGB market the central bank still holds as it normalises policy. Both releases are data publications; the December 17 to 18 policy meeting remains the next decision point.

The Bank of Korea makes the week's next policy decision on November 26, with the S&P review of France's sovereign rating following on November 27.
