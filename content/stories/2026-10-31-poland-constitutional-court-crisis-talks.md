President Karol Nawrocki convened what he called urgent consultations at the presidential palace on Saturday, saying the sudden suspension on Friday of Constitutional Tribunal chief judge Bogdan Święczkowski threatened Poland's internal security. Representatives of nationalist, far right and some governing coalition parties attended, while Prime Minister Donald Tusk's Civic Coalition stayed away, and no breakthrough had emerged by Saturday afternoon. Tusk defended the removal as a painful but necessary step to restore the rule of law, the court's official account called the government's move a coup d'état, the European Commission urged Nawrocki to accept the nomination of new judges, and the US ambassador to Poland, Tom Rose, called for calm on all sides.
