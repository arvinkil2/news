Japan's Ministry of Finance auctioned treasury discount bill issue 1419 on November 13, to be issued on November 16 and to mature on February 22, 2027. Competitive bids totalled 9,335.00 billion yen and the ministry accepted 2,392.76 billion yen, at a weighted average price of 99.6598 per 100 yen for an average yield of 1.2713 percent; the lowest accepted price was 99.6575, a yield of 1.2800 percent, with 60.7960 percent of bids at that price allotted. A further 707.20 billion yen was allotted in the non-price-competitive auction for JGB market special participants, taking the total sold to about 3,100 billion yen, the amount the ministry had announced for this sale.
