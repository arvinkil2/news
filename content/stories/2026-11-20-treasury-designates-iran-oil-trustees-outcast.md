The US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control designated Mohammad Javad Bavand and Majid Taj, who Treasury calls part of a network of regime insiders tasked with obtaining cash by selling from Iran's dwindling oil reserves, under Executive Order 13902 for operating in Iran's petroleum sector. Treasury said Bavand, a former IRGC intelligence officer, entrusted personal contacts including Taj, the brother of the Iranian Football Federation's president, with recouping Iran's oil funds in third countries, and that network members owe the regime billions of dollars from previous sales while continuing to facilitate new ones.

OFAC also designated Mohammadhossein Servati, the son of IRGC Brigadier General Mohsen Servati, for materially assisting the designated oil front company Sepehr Energy Jahan Nama Pars Company, and Mojtaba Arabnejad, the son of Mahan Air's chief executive, for assisting Mahan Air, both under Executive Order 13224. Secretary of the Treasury Scott Bessent said the regime has exhausted its seaborne oil supply and may never see the revenue from oil it has already sold. All property of the designated persons in the United States or in the control of US persons is blocked.
