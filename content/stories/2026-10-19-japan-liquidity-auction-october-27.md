Japan's Ministry of Finance announced a liquidity enhancement auction for October 27, offering about 250 billion yen of off the run super long JGBs: 20 year issues 163 to 197, 30 year issues 28 to 91 and 40 year issues 1 to 18, with an issue date of October 28. The sale is half the size of the roughly 500 billion yen operation scheduled for October 22 in the 10 to 30 year sector, and it lands two days before the Bank of Japan's October 29 to 30 meeting. Liquidity enhancement auctions reopen older, cheaper lines to lift their liquidity toward on the run benchmarks, and running one in the 20 to 40 year zone keeps the long end orderly while the market prices the policy path.
