United States envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner proposed that Europe increase its purchases of Russian gas as part of a Ukraine peace plan, according to a New York Times report relayed by Yle, the Finnish public broadcaster, on Sunday. The administration's reasoning, as reported, is that gas trade would give Russia an economic incentive to end the war, and that transit through Ukraine's pipeline network could also bring revenue to Kyiv.

European officials reacted dismissively to the proposal, Yle reported. The European Union has decided to phase out imports of Russian gas completely in stages, and critics quoted in the report said that economic benefits offered to Russia could weaken pressure on Moscow to end the war and could affect Europe's energy security.
