Australia's physical energy exports fell to their lowest level in ten years in 2024-25, the Australian Bureau of Statistics said in its Energy Account release. Physical energy exports, meaning coal, liquefied natural gas, uranium and crude oil measured in volume terms, fell 3.1 percent overall. Black coal stayed the largest energy export but fell 1.1 percent, and LNG fell 2.2 percent.

Renewable energy production rose almost 10 percent in 2024-25, after a 5 percent increase in 2023-24. Solar stayed the largest renewable source, up 11 percent, and wind production rose 15 percent, the bureau said, adding that Australia produced nearly 40 percent more electricity from renewable sources than households used. Per person energy use has fallen 16 percent over ten years, while total household energy use rose 0.3 percent in 2024-25.
