Israeli authorities say no commercial goods have entered Gaza for two days. COGAT, the Israeli military body overseeing humanitarian affairs in Gaza, said local merchants halted shipments in response to what it described as a 10,000 shekel fee imposed by Hamas on each entering truck and a demand that 30 percent of sales be in cash. Gaza's government media office denied that account on Thursday, calling the fee a voluntary payment to an aid fund and saying cash payments were encouraged, not required, while the head of Gaza's private truckers union said Israel itself imposed the restrictions.
