Toronto voters reelected Mayor Olivia Chow on Monday, defeating her main rival, city councillor Brad Bradford, in a municipal election campaign dominated by the cost of living. CBC News projected Chow's win on election night and CityNews declared her reelected, giving her a full four year term after she first took office in the 2023 byelection that followed John Tory's resignation.

Chow campaigned on affordability pledges that included 10,000 additional rental homes, a cap on weekly TTC fares and expanded school meal programs, while Bradford ran on crime crackdowns and the argument that Chow had made Toronto too expensive. Final polls had disagreed on the margin, with some surveys in the last week putting Bradford level or ahead, so the projection settles a race that had tightened sharply in its final days.
