The Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) confirmed on Wednesday that it has filed two separate lawsuits against Google LLC, each seeking RM300 million in damages, for a total of RM600 million. One suit, filed at the Kuala Lumpur High Court on October 23, alleges negligence over the republication on YouTube of allegedly false and defamatory content by blogger Wan Muhammad Azri Wan Deris, known as Papagomo; the second alleges Google failed to take adequate measures against thousands of items of harmful content, including online gambling, scams and fraud.

MCMC said it submitted 74,703 reports to YouTube between January 1 and October 28, 2026, of which 99.8 percent concerned online gambling, and that 4,950 received no response, 1,468 took more than 30 days and 35 took more than 90 days. Each claim comprises RM100 million each in general, aggravated and exemplary damages, and the Papagomo case is fixed for case management on December 8.
