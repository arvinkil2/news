Oregon and Virginia are the first states to sue SpaceX, in separate complaints alleging the company failed to put safeguards in place to stop users of Grok and X from making and sharing harmful deepfake images, including alleged AI-generated child sexual abuse material, CNBC reported. The states also allege SpaceX falsely represented Grok and X as generally safe, in violation of Oregon's Unlawful Trade Practices Act and Virginia's Consumer Protection Act, and note that X allows users aged 13 and older.

Oregon filed in Multnomah County Circuit Court on Sunday evening and Virginia in the City of Richmond Circuit Court on Monday morning. Both states seek injunctions, civil penalties, disgorgement and legal costs, and Virginia also seeks restitution for consumers. SpaceX did not immediately respond to a request for comment, CNBC said.
