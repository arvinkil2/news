NRW Holdings has been selected as the preferred mining contractor by Bullabulling Operations, a wholly owned subsidiary of Minerals 260, for the Bullabulling gold project in Western Australia. The anticipated value of the first five years of mining services is about 1 billion Australian dollars, over a five-year term with an option to extend.

The scope covers mine site establishment, pre-stripping, dewatering, load and haul, and drill and blast, with early activities starting immediately ahead of full mobilisation in the September quarter of 2027 and mining from the December quarter of that year. Execution of the long-form contract is expected alongside Minerals 260's final investment decision in early 2027.
