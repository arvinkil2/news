CATL's Hong Kong shares closed down 14.93 percent at 350 Hong Kong dollars on Wednesday and its Shenzhen shares fell 9.89 percent, after the battery maker reported third quarter net profit of 21.25 billion yuan, up 14.54 percent year on year but down 5.77 percent from the second quarter, on revenue up 58.58 percent to 165.22 billion yuan. Gross margin fell to 20.69 percent, a second consecutive quarterly decline, and the company attributed the drop in profit per unit to product mix changes, a consumption tax on lithium batteries and a reduced battery export tax rebate, adding that it is negotiating how to share the tax costs with customers. China resumed a consumption tax on lithium-ion batteries on September 1 at a transitional 2 percent rate, rising to 4 percent in September 2027, and cut the value added tax export rebate for battery products from 9 percent to 6 percent in April, with the rebate eliminated entirely from January 1, 2027.
