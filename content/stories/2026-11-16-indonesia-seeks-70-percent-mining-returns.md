President Prabowo Subianto instructed ministers on Saturday in Palu, Central Sulawesi, to raise the state's share of mining returns to at least 70 percent of the benefits from mining permits, saying shares should be state controlled if necessary, and called for exports to pass through a designated agency. His office did not define whether the 70 percent would be measured against revenue, profit or another measure, gave no timetable, and left the treatment of existing operations unspecified.

Amman Mineral Internasional shares fell 3.5 percent and Merdeka Copper Gold fell 4.8 percent in Monday trading, against a 1.1 percent decline in the Jakarta Composite Index. Indonesia already holds 51 percent of PT Freeport Indonesia, operator of the Grasberg copper and gold complex, and BMO Capital Markets analysts described the remarks as an escalation in the government's rhetoric on state control.
