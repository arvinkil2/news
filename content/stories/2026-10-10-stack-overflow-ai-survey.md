Stack Overflow's latest survey of 30,903 developers found a workforce hooked on AI but unconvinced by it: among 10,926 daily AI users who answered the time-use question, about 80 percent use the tools at least an hour a day, and nearly a third spend four or more hours. Only 7 percent trust AI output for important decisions, while 48 percent trust it when they can verify it and 79 percent consider source attribution important. Coding assistants are now used by 66 percent of respondents, and more than half learned a new coding skill or language this year, with AI tools among their most common learning resources.
