Indonesia's parliament passed the 2027 state budget on September 29: a 6% growth target — the fastest since 2012 — with the deficit held at 2.40% of GDP, inside the 3% statutory ceiling. The next day, Prabowo reviewed a Q4 stimulus package: 10 kilograms of rice a month for 33.24 million households through December, a Rp300,000 monthly wage subsidy for 13.3 million workers, and bank accounts for adults in the lowest four welfare deciles. Finance Minister Nazara framed it as answering "global uncertainty" without sacrificing fiscal sustainability. Implementing regulations are still pending.

In Thailand, the Constitutional Court on September 28 dismissed the ombudsman's petition challenging the February 8 election over ballot barcodes, ruling voter secrecy was not violated — averting a re-run and securing PM Anutin's Bhumjaithai-led coalition. Senate-election fraud investigations continue separately, with calls to indict 229 implicated individuals.

In the Philippines, the flood-control corruption crisis grinds on: two legislative leaders resigned in September, an independent infrastructure commission was established, and the foreign ministry publicly rejected a Chinese embassy sovereignty claim near Batanes on October 3.
