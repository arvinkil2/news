**Latest:** Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said 630 drones flew toward the Moscow region between 8:30 p.m. Wednesday and 4:00 a.m. Thursday, Moscow time, most of them destroyed by air defences at distant approaches, Interfax reported. Sobyanin said two people were injured in the Voronovo area of New Moscow (TiNAO) and were receiving assistance. In a separate report, Interfax said air defences destroyed another 73 drones flying toward the capital, bringing the total downed on approach to Moscow itself to 163, and that Rosaviatsiya, the federal aviation agency, had imposed restrictions at all airports in the Moscow aviation hub; Aeroflot said it was shifting departure times, cancelling some flights and diverting aircraft to alternate airfields.

Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said air defences destroyed another 12 drones flying toward the Russian capital late on Wednesday, bringing the total he claims for the night's attack to 68, Interfax reported. Sobyanin said emergency service specialists were working where debris fell, and no information on damage or casualties was reported in the accounts read. Russia's federal aviation agency, Rosaviatsiya, imposed temporary restrictions on arrivals and departures at Sheremetyevo, Moscow's main international airport, Interfax reported separately.
