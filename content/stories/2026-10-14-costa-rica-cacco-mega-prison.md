President Laura Fernandez on Wednesday opened the High-Containment Centre Against Organised Crime, a mega-prison with capacity for 5,100 people, equivalent to about 20 percent of Costa Rica's current prison population, with the first transfers due at the end of October. The government presents the facility as a tool to reassert control over a prison system infiltrated by organised crime, in a country that recorded 905 homicides in 2023 and 873 in 2025 after decades of comparatively low violence. Peru's president has announced plans for a similar facility and Colombia's new president campaigned on building ten mega-prisons, even as rights groups warn the Salvadoran original has produced mass detention without charge and alleged abuse.
