South Korea's Supreme Court on Thursday overturned the 15-year prison sentence imposed on former prime minister Han Duck-soo for his role in the December 2024 martial law declaration by former president Yoon Suk Yeol, ruling that prosecutors had not sufficiently proven the case. Han had been convicted in May of helping plan the insurrection by suggesting Yoon hold a cabinet meeting to make martial law seem legitimate, and of seeking cabinet signatures on a related document and reviewing orders to cut power and water to critical media outlets. The decision is not an acquittal: the case returns to the Seoul High Court for review of the conviction.

In a related ruling the same day, the Supreme Court upheld a four-year prison term for Yoon's wife, Kim Keon Hee, on corruption charges including stock price manipulation and bribery. Yoon himself was impeached after the December 3, 2024 martial law declaration and later found guilty of leading an insurrection and sentenced to life in prison.
