Indonesia's government is formulating a plan for 70 percent of benefits from mining firms to go to the state, whether through equity or revenue, Energy Minister Bahlil Lahadalia said on Monday, reporting President Prabowo Subianto's position that the state should secure at least 70 percent of benefits from all mining permits. Lahadalia said the government will honour existing permits and that the change of equity ownership will be applied to future deals. Miners are seeking clarity on how the 70 percent figure would be calculated, and CreditSights, a research unit of Fitch Solutions, said the remarks have renewed policy uncertainty and warrant a continued risk premium in Indonesia's mining sector.
