Bailiffs acting for Jaguar Land Rover under a court order raided BYD's offices in Schiedam and Hoofddorp in August and seized digital records, as JLR sought to secure evidence it says may show the Chinese carmaker reverse-engineered technology for its Ti7 SUV. A court in The Hague hears this week whether BYD has a case to answer over claims the seven-seat plug-in hybrid infringes the Land Rover Defender's intellectual property; no finding has been made, and both companies declined to comment on the proceedings.

The Ti7, on sale in China since September 2025, is due to launch in the UK in January at £47,995, more than £20,000 below the seven-seat Defender 110's £68,465 starting price. JLR previously won a 2019 case in China stopping Jiangling Motor from selling a copycat vehicle.
