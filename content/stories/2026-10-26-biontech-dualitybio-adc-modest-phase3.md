An antibody-drug conjugate from BioNTech and DualityBio met its main goal in a Phase 3 study in HER2-low breast cancer, delaying tumor progression, but the companies described the improvement as modest and said the findings suggest the treatment's potential benefit to patients would be limited against the current treatment landscape. Treatment was associated with higher rates of interstitial lung disease than seen in earlier testing, severe and fatal events were observed in a small proportion of patients, and the trial had not run long enough to show a clear survival benefit.

The partners said they will discuss the data and determine next steps. Trastuzumab pamirtecan, obtained through BioNTech's 2023 licensing deal with DualityBio, its first move into antibody-drug conjugates, is also in a late-stage study in HER2-positive endometrial cancer and an early-stage study in advanced solid tumors.
