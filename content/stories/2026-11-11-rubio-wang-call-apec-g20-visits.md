Secretary of State and National Security Advisor Marco Rubio spoke on Wednesday with Wang Yi, director of the Office of the CCP Central Foreign Affairs Commission and China's foreign minister, in a call held at China's request, the State Department said in its readout. The two discussed President Trump's upcoming travel to China for the APEC summit and General Secretary Xi's upcoming visit to Florida to attend the G20 summit.

The readout says the two recalled the bilateral leader-level commitment to a constructive relationship of strategic stability on the basis of respect, fairness, and reciprocity. They agreed on the importance of meaningful leader-level conversations to advance goals such as peace and denuclearization, including at any potential trilateral leaders meeting with Russia.
