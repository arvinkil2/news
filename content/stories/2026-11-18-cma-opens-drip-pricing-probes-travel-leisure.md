The UK Competition and Markets Authority on Wednesday opened consumer protection investigations into Bannatyne Health Club, the holiday deals site Secret Escapes and the coach holiday provider Caledonian Travel, over concerns that mandatory charges are separated from the headline price and added later in the buying process. Bannatyne is being investigated over joining fees and facility access charges on gym memberships, Secret Escapes over resort fees, local taxes and ATOL protection fees on package holidays, and Caledonian Travel over whether it shows the total price upfront, including single-person supplements and city taxes. The CMA said it has reached no conclusions on whether the law has been broken, and that the three cases bring the number of businesses it has investigated under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 regime to 23, with more than 12 million pounds in fines levied so far.
