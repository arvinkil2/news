MPower Financing, which had been growing since 2014 by tapping bond markets to fund loans for international students, has largely shuttered after losing access to fresh financing. A person with direct knowledge of the firm's lending strategy said its concentration of loans to Zimbabwean students had grown beyond what the company intended, leaving it especially vulnerable after the Trump administration added Zimbabwe to a partial travel-ban list last year.
