The Defense Department's Office of Strategic Capital has recently announced three loans worth a total of 450 million dollars to US space firms, Breaking Defense reported on Wednesday: 150 million dollars to Rocket Lab, announced Monday, to help it increase the flight rate of its reusable Neutron rocket; 150 million dollars to Stoke Space, awarded on October 26, toward a second launch pad at Cape Canaveral for its planned Nova rocket; and 150 million dollars to LeoLabs, announced on November 4, to accelerate deployment of its Scout mobile radar and expand its radar manufacturing capacity.

Like all Office of Strategic Capital loans, the three are conditional, requiring due diligence on each company's finances and a matching investment of company capital before they close.
