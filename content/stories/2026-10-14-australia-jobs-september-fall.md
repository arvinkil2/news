Australia's labour market cooled more than expected in September, with employment falling 39,300 against a forecast gain of about 20,000, as a 73,700 drop in part-time jobs more than offset a 34,400 rise in full-time work. The unemployment rate held at 4.7 percent, its highest since October 2021, after August was revised up from 4.6 percent, with the number of unemployed up 8,900 to 732,000 and participation down 0.2 points to 66.8 percent. Moody's Analytics told HRD Australia the figures point to a genuine slowdown in hiring and, unless the quarterly CPI surprises sharply higher, point to a Reserve Bank hold in November, a read Reuters also reported in market pricing as investors trimmed bets on another hike.
