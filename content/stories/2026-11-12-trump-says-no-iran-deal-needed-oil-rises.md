President Trump wrote on Truth Social on Thursday morning that "we don't need a deal with Iran" and that "those days have long since passed," adding that the United States has Iran's money, "hundreds of billions of dollars, and we've got their Strait," a reference to the Strait of Hormuz. In the same post he said he has asked Vice President Vance to focus his complete attention on corruption and crime in Democrat-run cities and states, a day after the Wall Street Journal reported Vance was considering face-to-face talks with a top Iranian security official.

Oil rose on the comments: Brent crude traded 1.6% higher at $94.25 a barrel and West Texas Intermediate gained 1.3% to $87.62, CNBC reported, while US stock futures declined. No Iranian response to the post had been reported as of Thursday morning.
