Taiwan is drafting legislation to establish a new sovereign wealth fund with initial capital of about NT$30 billion (US$912 million), pushing ahead with the government's plan to make strategic investments overseas, Bloomberg reported. The National Development Council aims to complete a draft bill by the end of this year, the council's minister, Yeh Chun-hsien, said during a legislature session on Wednesday.

President Lai Ching-te announced the fund in May 2025 as a national level investment platform, and the council has been studying a fund set up by special act since. No funding source, governance structure or investment mandate has been published.
