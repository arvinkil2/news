The Malta-flagged chemical tanker Med Emre sank about 25 nautical miles off Ibiza in the early hours of Thursday while sailing from Algeciras to Barcelona, and may have been carrying up to 7,000 metric tons of diesel, the Balearic Islands interior department said. All 17 crew members were rescued; one died after being taken to Ibiza's Can Misses hospital, and six others were treated in hospital with light injuries, mainly hypothermia.

Spain's merchant marine authority activated the national maritime pollution contingency plan at level 2, the second-highest level, and the maritime rescue service sent the oil-spill response vessel Clara Campoamor and an aircraft to survey the area. Authorities have not disclosed the crew's nationalities or the cause of the sinking, and the Balearic government said it was assessing the risk of fuel pollution before deciding on further measures.
