Jersey Airport said at 20:05 on Saturday that it was experiencing disruption affecting inbound flights as a result of further drone activity in the vicinity of the airfield, and all late evening flights into Jersey were cancelled, the BBC reported. States of Jersey Police are investigating, and Inspector Rob McCallum said two or three drones were believed to have entered the restricted airspace, though they were not thought to be linked to terrorism. On Friday evening three flights due from London Gatwick, London Heathrow and Southampton were diverted back to the United Kingdom; the Gatwick flight eventually arrived at about 23:45, while the Heathrow and Southampton services were cancelled, and searches in the west of the island did not identify a drone operator.
