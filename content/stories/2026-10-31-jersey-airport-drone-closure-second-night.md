Jersey Airport closed from 20:35 to 21:30 on Saturday night after multiple reports of drones being flown in the vicinity of the airport, the BBC reported. A British Airways flight to London Gatwick was cancelled, and Loganair services to Manchester and Southampton were diverted to Exeter, with the Southampton passengers taken back to Jersey by bus through the night. On Friday night the airport had closed from 18:15 to 19:15 on similar reports and a Blue Islands flight from Guernsey was turned back, and States of Jersey Police are investigating the sightings.
