Qnity Electronics has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire the electronics business of Meridian Adhesives Group for $840 million. Qnity will pay the price from cash on hand and incremental borrowings under its existing revolving credit facility. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions and expiry of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period.

The business being bought makes epoxy-based optical-grade, electrically conductive and thermally conductive adhesives used for high-speed data transmission, thermal management and component reliability. Its revenue is expected to be about $100 million in calendar year 2026, with double-digit growth expected to continue in 2027, which puts the price at roughly 8.4 times that expected revenue on the company's own figures.

The unit will sit in Qnity's Interconnect Solutions segment. Qnity's chief executive Jon Kemp said the acquisition adds capabilities in optical and photonics solutions and expands its thermal management and signal integrity offering, two of the segment's core growth platforms alongside advanced packaging. Meridian's electronics business is a carve-out from a broader adhesives group, so the deal also continues the pattern of speciality materials portfolios being split between financial and strategic owners.
