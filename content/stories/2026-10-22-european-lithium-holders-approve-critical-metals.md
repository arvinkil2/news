European Lithium said shareholders and optionholders voted in favour of the schemes and the related party resolutions at the general meeting, share scheme meeting and option scheme meeting held in Western Australia on Thursday, and that each scheme resolution was passed by the requisite majorities. On the poll, the share scheme drew 452,707,512 votes in favour and 203,601 against, with 175 shareholders voting in favour and 5 against, while the option scheme drew 198,613,607 votes in favour and none against, with all 62 optionholders voting in favour. All four related party resolutions at the general meeting were also carried.

The schemes still need approval from the Supreme Court of Western Australia at the second court hearing at 10:00am Perth time on Monday, October 26, and the remaining conditions precedent. If the court approves, the company expects the schemes to become effective on Tuesday, October 27, with trading in its shares and options suspended from that day's close, a record date of October 29, implementation on November 5 and the new Critical Metals shares starting trading on Nasdaq on November 6.
