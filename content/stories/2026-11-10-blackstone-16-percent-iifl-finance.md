IIFL Finance's board on Tuesday approved a preferential issue raising 6,414 crore rupees of primary capital, anchored by Blackstone-managed funds committing 5,000 crore rupees through compulsorily convertible preference shares, for an approximately 16 percent fully diluted stake. Co-promoters Nirmal Jain and R. Venkataraman will invest a further 1,264 crore rupees and Anchorage Capital 150 crore rupees. The company said the infusion would raise its capital adequacy ratio to about 30 percent from 17 percent, on a pro forma basis; the issue is subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.
