A multi-storey building under construction collapsed at about 1 p.m. local time on Friday along Yusuf Haji Avenue on 9th Street in Nairobi's Eastleigh district, next to the Day to Day Supermarket, and several people are feared trapped under the rubble, Kenyan media reported. The Kenya Red Cross said its Nairobi response team had been dispatched and was working alongside multi-agency partners on search and rescue, and police fired tear gas to clear crowds that had blocked roads and kept emergency vehicles from reaching the site. Reports of the building's height differ, with Nairobi police commander Issa Mohamed describing a structure of about 10 floors while other local reports put it at 14 or 18 storeys, and its lower floors were reported to have been occupied by shops and rental units while construction continued above. Officials had released no number for people trapped, injured or killed, and no cause, as of the latest reports.
