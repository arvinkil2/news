US Central Command said its forces disabled a commercial vessel in the Gulf of Oman on Saturday, November 7, after the ship's civilian crew ignored repeated warnings and attempted to violate the naval blockade against Iran multiple times. A US military aircraft fired precision munitions at the stern of the St. Kitts-flagged M/V Huda, disabling the ship after Central Command forces directed the crew to abandon the targeted spaces. The cargo vessel had visited an Iranian port and was trying to depart regional waters.

Central Command said enforcement actions since July have redirected 147 commercial vessels that complied with warnings and disabled five merchant ships that refused, and that US forces previously destroyed another 10 Iranian commercial oil tankers that were part of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps' shadow network. The disabling came the same day Central Command announced Task Force Iron Gate, its new effort against Iranian weapons and sanctioned material moving over land borders and through the Caspian Sea.
