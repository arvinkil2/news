Unacademy co-founder Gaurav Munjal said on Wednesday that he is stepping down as chief executive of the Indian test preparation platform and moving to executive chairman, with chief business officer Akhilendra Kumar taking over as chief executive. In a LinkedIn post Munjal said he would stay fully involved in day-to-day operations, work alongside Kumar and help with upGrad's planned initial public offering.

The transition comes two months after upGrad completed its all-stock acquisition of Unacademy in September, in a deal valued at just over $200 million, about 94 percent below the roughly $3.4 billion valuation Unacademy carried at its 2021 peak. With Munjal's move, all of Unacademy's co-founders, Roman Saini, Hemesh Singh and Sumit Jain among them, have now left operational roles at the company he founded in 2015 as a YouTube channel.
