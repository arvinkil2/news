The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network disclosed on Tuesday that it is investigating a cybersecurity incident that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems. The Treasury Department bureau has taken some systems offline as a precaution and has given no information about the full scope of the access or its source.

FinCEN said in its alert that banks can still file suspicious activity reports online, though some services may not be immediately available, and that filers needing to expedite a report can call the Financial Institutions Hotline, which was not affected. The disclosure came hours after reporting that the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence had found a breach of an open source intelligence system exposing unclassified information; whether the FinCEN incident is related is unclear.
