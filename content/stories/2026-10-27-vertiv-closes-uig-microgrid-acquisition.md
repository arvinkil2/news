Vertiv Corporation completed its acquisition of Utility Innovation Holdings, Inc. (UIG) on October 27, 2026, paying approximately $1.45 billion in upfront cash at closing, subject to customary adjustments, with additional cash consideration of up to $1.15 billion payable in two tranches if UIG meets specified EBITDA targets, Vertiv Holdings Co said in its Form 8-K. UIG, which becomes a wholly owned subsidiary, provides microgrid solutions, advanced power controls and behind-the-meter power architecture design for data centers.

The closing completes the merger agreement dated September 1, 2026, and Vertiv announced the completion in a press release furnished with the filing.
