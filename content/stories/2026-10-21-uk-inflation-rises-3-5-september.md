UK consumer prices rose 3.5 percent in the year to September 2026, up from 3.1 percent in August, with prices up 0.4 percent on the month against little change a year earlier, and core inflation rose to 2.8 percent from 2.6 percent, the Office for National Statistics said. Transport made the largest upward contribution, as motor fuel prices rose 29.8 percent over the year, petrol averaged 169.5 pence a litre, its highest since August 2022, and diesel averaged 192.5 pence, its highest since July 2022, while restaurants and hotels partly offset the rise. The pipeline pointed the same way, with producer input prices up 8.3 percent over the year and factory gate prices up 4.6 percent, the largest rise since April 2023, both driven mainly by oil related products.
