Vice President JD Vance told a CBS News town hall on Saturday that, in some ways, the United States has decisively won its war against Iran. He qualified the claim in the same exchange, saying the enemy gets a vote and that Iran has responded in certain ways, and he framed the war's purpose as preventing Iran, which he called the largest state sponsor of terrorism in the world, from obtaining a nuclear weapon.

The Hill, which reported the remarks, set them against the war's domestic costs a few weeks before the midterm elections: the Strait of Hormuz remains closed amid the fighting, AAA put the average gasoline price at about 4.30 dollars a gallon on Sunday morning against about 3.04 dollars a year earlier, and the Decision Desk HQ polling average put President Trump's approval at 39.8 percent, with disapproval at 58.7 percent.
