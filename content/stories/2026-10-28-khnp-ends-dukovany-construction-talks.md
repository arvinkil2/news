Korea Hydro and Nuclear Power (KHNP) said it will end its negotiations with the consortium of Daewoo Engineering and Construction and Doosan Enerbility over the construction contract for the two new reactors at Dukovany in the Czech Republic, Yonhap reported. KHNP said the two sides had tried working-level talks, a dispute adjustment committee and management-level negotiations, but could not close their differences over key contract terms, and it did not name the terms in dispute.

KHNP said it is starting procedures to terminate its joint cooperation agreement with the consortium and is preparing countermeasures that include a tender to select a new construction contractor. Daewoo and Doosan were part of the original Team Korea group that won the Dukovany order with KHNP, alongside KEPCO Engineering and Construction, KEPCO Nuclear Fuel and KEPCO KPS. The project, two 1,000 megawatt class APR1000 reactors, is costed at about 407 billion Czech koruna, roughly 25 trillion won, and construction has been scheduled to begin in 2029.
