Naver's third quarter revenue rose 10.7 percent year on year to a record 3.4724 trillion won, its sixth consecutive quarterly record, while operating profit fell 20.9 percent to 451.3 billion won as the company stepped up investment in AI infrastructure, content and offline data. Growth came from commerce, payments and its global person to person trading businesses, including Wallapop and Poshmark.

Chief executive Choi Soo-yeon said Naver has signed a computing service supply contract worth about 2 trillion won for its AI Factory business, securing an initial customer for roughly 20 megawatts of capacity, with revenue to begin as commercial service starts in the first half of next year. Chief financial officer Kim Hee-cheol said the company is discussing further supply contracts with more than 10 companies covering about 180 megawatts, the balance of its 200 megawatt first phase target.
