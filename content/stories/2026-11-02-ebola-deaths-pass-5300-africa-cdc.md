The Ebola outbreak centred on the Democratic Republic of Congo has killed 5,376 people, all but four of them in Congo, and infected about 10,444 people, according to Africa CDC data released on Monday and reported by AFP and Reuters. The outbreak of the Bundibugyo strain was declared on May 20 after cases were detected in North Kivu and Ituri provinces in the east, and is Congo's 17th and largest.

The virus has crossed borders several times in recent months. Kenya recorded its first Ebola cases in October, with nine infections and two deaths, Uganda has reported 20 cases, almost all in people who had travelled from Congo, and two suspected cases in Nigeria tested negative. There are no approved treatments or vaccines for the Bundibugyo strain, and clinical trials have recently begun with the Ervebo vaccine, which is effective against the more common Zaire strain.
