Cynthia "Xanthi" Carras, the Pentagon's principal director for China, Taiwan and Mongolia, met Senior Colonel Wang Wei of the People's Liberation Army's Office of International Military Cooperation in Washington on November 2 and 3 for the annual Crisis Communications and Prevention Working Group. The two sides agreed on the importance of advancing practical communication measures to reduce risks between the United States and China, and discussed additional channels to promote effective crisis management and ways to communicate expediently. The department said it will continue to advance discussions with the PLA on key defence issues in the coming months.
