The Justice Department said on Thursday that it has obtained court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion of the cryptocurrency stolen from Binance on or about October 29, which the FBI attributes to North Korean military actors it calls TraderTraitor. United States government and private-sector efforts have frozen more than $2.7 billion of the more than $6 billion taken, and tracing and forfeiture work is continuing while the actors move the assets through bridges, mixers and exchanges.

In a separate action, the department said it seized cloud-based infrastructure used in FaceStealer, a scheme in which North Korean hackers used recorded webcam video and deepfakes of trusted contacts in fake video meetings, then installed malware through a bogus audio fix. The scheme took more than $400 million over 18 months, the FBI seized more than 900 webcam videos of victims under a federal warrant, and officials said the targets have widened from crypto workers to open-source software developers whose repository access could be used for supply-chain attacks.
