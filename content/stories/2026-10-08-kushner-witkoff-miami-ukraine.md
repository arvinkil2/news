Presidential envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will meet Ukrainian negotiators and European officials in Miami on Friday, October 9, to present "new ideas and proposals" aimed at breaking the stalemate in the Russia-Ukraine war, Axios reported, citing US and Ukrainian officials. The administration is hoping for a diplomatic breakthrough ahead of the midterm elections and before brutal winter weather grips Ukraine.
