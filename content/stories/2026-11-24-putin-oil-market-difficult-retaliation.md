Russian President Vladimir Putin acknowledged on November 24 that Ukraine's concentrated attacks on Russian oil facilities have left the country's petroleum products market in a difficult situation, and he foreshadowed retaliation, Yonhap News Agency reported, citing the Russian state agency TASS. Speaking at a Moscow meeting of the committee on fuel and energy industry development strategy and environmental safety, Putin said the market situation is still difficult, instructed officials to prepare countermeasures, and said an unavoidable response was necessary.

Putin said President Donald Trump had asked Ukraine to stop attacking Russian oil facilities but that the attacks were continuing; oil facilities in Russia's Tatarstan and Perm regions were targeted the previous day, according to the report. He also criticized Europe's decision to restrict imports of Russian energy, saying a fall in Russian oil and gas supply would create problems and adversely affect Europeans. The report carried no figures for production, exports, prices or damage.
