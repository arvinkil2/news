South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said North Korea launched more than ten short-range ballistic missiles from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea at about 12:30 p.m. on Thursday, flying about 250 kilometres, and that the projectiles were presumed to be the 600mm super-large rocket artillery, a system Pyongyang says can carry a tactical nuclear warhead. State media said the firing was the first public combat display of the system, which it calls "Sniper", staged at an unmanned aviation exhibition where drones struck mock targets representing a South Korean guard post, a K9 howitzer and a US Abrams tank. Kim Jong Un observed the event and called absolute superiority in the drone field an indispensable choice, saying strength is peace itself and peace is guaranteed only by strength.
