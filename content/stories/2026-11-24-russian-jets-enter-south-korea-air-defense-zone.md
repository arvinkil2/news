Several Russian military aircraft entered South Korea's air defense identification zone over the East Sea on Tuesday without prior notification, flying close to South Korean airspace near Ulleungdo, Agence France-Presse reported. South Korea's defense ministry said the aircraft had not violated sovereign airspace, that they departed as South Korean forces deployed fighter jets and took other military measures, and that no North Korean aircraft were involved.

An emergency alert issued on Ulleungdo on Tuesday morning first warned of a suspected North Korean aircraft and urged residents to seek shelter, before provincial authorities corrected it around 40 minutes later and said the aircraft were Russian. Seoul summoned a Russian embassy military attache, and the defense ministry's director general for international policy, Kim Hak-jo, lodged a stern protest with Col. Nikolai Marchenko, calling the repeated unauthorized entries unacceptable. Russia's defense ministry said its aircraft carried out a scheduled flight over the neutral waters of the Sea of Japan, describing a 10-hour flight by strategic bombers at times accompanied by foreign fighter aircraft. A similar Russian entry on November 17 also prompted South Korea to scramble jets.
