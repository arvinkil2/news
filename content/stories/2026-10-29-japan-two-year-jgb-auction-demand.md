Japan's two-year bond sale met firm demand on Thursday. The Ministry of Finance reported competitive bids of 8,539.2 billion yen for the 1.9 percent issue maturing November 1, 2028, against 2,118.4 billion yen accepted competitively, a cover of about 4.0 times, with a further 680.1 billion yen allotted through the non-price-competitive auction for market special participants.

The bonds cleared at a lowest accepted price of 99.975, a yield of 1.912 percent, and an average price of 99.989, a yield of 1.905 percent. The issue settles on November 2, 2026.
