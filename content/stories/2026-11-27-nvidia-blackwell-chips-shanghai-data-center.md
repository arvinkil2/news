Two Chinese firms installed Nvidia Blackwell semiconductors at the Yangtze River Delta Project, a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, Bloomberg reported on Friday. The installed gear included a GB300 NVL72 rack, among Nvidia's most powerful products, identified by a serial number in documents submitted to Chinese regulators in November.

One of the firms, Shanghai Shuoyao, offered the hardware as collateral for a 96 million yuan ($14 million) loan from Shanghai Innovation Bank, according to filings from the lender reviewed by Bloomberg. Blackwell chips cannot be sold to Chinese companies without US government permission, and the documented installation adds a hardware trail to earlier reporting on the network of companies around Huang that has moved restricted Nvidia chips into China.
