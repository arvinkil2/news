**Latest (Monday, October 12, evening):** the Houthis claimed a ballistic missile struck King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Monday evening, alongside a separate drone operation against the Khamis Mushait air base, and warned international airlines, experts, employees and travelers to avoid Saudi airports, exempting Mecca and Medina. The claim of a hit is the group's account and is not corroborated: the coalition said the missile fired toward Riyadh was intercepted and destroyed. The interception was felt across the capital: Al-Nassr's Asian Champions League match against Neftchi Fergana, scheduled for 9:15 p.m. local time, was delayed by an hour, AFP correspondents reported hearing explosions in Riyadh, and residents received phone alerts warning of a hostile aerial threat and telling them to stay home or in a safe place. The match was played after the announcer said the situation was safe.

**Latest (Monday, October 12, afternoon):** the Saudi-led Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen said its forces intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile launched by the Houthis toward Riyadh on Monday, per coalition spokesman Major General Turki Al-Malki, reported by Arab News. Yonhap separately reported three explosions heard over the Saudi capital. No casualties from the intercepted missile were reported in either account.

Speaking at the WPC Energy Congress in Riyadh on Monday, Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman said recent Houthi attacks on Saudi airports and energy facilities were serious and that the kingdom's response must be equally serious. Saudi authorities said a Pakistani man was killed in a Sunday attack on a terminal at King Khalid International Airport, a day after a Houthi missile hit another terminal, killing 13 people and forcing its closure.

The Houthis said the airport attacks retaliated for Saudi operations in Yemen, and the Houthi-run health ministry said a child was killed in a Saudi air strike on a home in Sanaa on Sunday, claims that remain each side's account pending independent confirmation.
