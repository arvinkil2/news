A further extension to the original Yongbyon enrichment facility has been under construction since mid-September 2026 and was externally complete by late October, IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi told the agency's Board of Governors on Wednesday, calling the ongoing expansion of North Korea's uranium enrichment programme a cause for serious concern.

Grossi said the agency has observed indications that the 5 MW(e) reactor at Yongbyon likely continues to operate in its seventh irradiation cycle, and that the light water reactor there has been operating since the end of April 2026 after a period of shutdown. He said the continuation and further development of the programme are clear violations of relevant UN Security Council resolutions.
