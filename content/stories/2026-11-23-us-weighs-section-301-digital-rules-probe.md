The Trump administration is reviewing, and the Office of the US Trade Representative is preparing, an investigation under Section 301 of the Trade Act into digital-sector policies in South Korea, the European Union and Australia, Yonhap reported, citing Politico reporting of November 22 based on three people familiar with the matter. The measures in scope are regulations Washington judges to work against US technology companies, including the EU's Digital Markets Act and, for South Korea, network usage fees, online-platform rules and the treatment of Coupang.

No investigation has been launched and no timetable has been given. Yonhap noted that a probe leading to additional tariffs would raise questions about the 15 percent tariff ceiling South Korea agreed with Washington, under which Seoul pledged $350 billion of investment in the United States in return for tariffs falling from 25 percent to 15 percent.
