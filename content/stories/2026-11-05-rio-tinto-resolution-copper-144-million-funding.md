Rio Tinto has approved $144 million (C$201 million) in additional spending at Resolution Copper in Arizona, advancing engineering and underground development after the venture secured the land needed for the proposed operation. Rio, which owns 55 percent of the venture alongside BHP, contributes $79 million of the programme, which covers feasibility studies, equipment purchases and water treatment work and creates about 50 additional jobs.

The spending adds to about $500 million announced when the venture completed its land exchange in March, which transferred more than 2,400 acres near Superior to Resolution Copper. The partners still face state and local permitting, further technical work and opposition from the San Carlos Apache Tribe and Apache Stronghold over the destruction of Oak Flat, a sacred site above the deposit, and no decision has been made to begin mining.
