Solidigm, the US NAND flash and enterprise SSD business SK Hynix built from Intel's storage assets, has picked Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs as lead underwriters for a US initial public offering as early as 2027, Bloomberg reported Wednesday. Reuters reported last month the listing could raise about $15 billion at a valuation of up to $150 billion, a step up from the roughly $100 billion figure Bloomberg previously floated, with a pre-IPO funding round of about $3.6 billion also in the works. Solidigm posted first-half 2026 net income of $4.3 billion on $9.0 billion in revenue, up from $97 million of profit a year earlier, as AI data-center demand drives enterprise SSD sales. SK Hynix has not confirmed the listing, saying in its September 4 disclosure that its overseas subsidiary is reviewing measures to strengthen competitiveness and setting December 3 for its next update.
