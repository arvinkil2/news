Hong Kong's Construction Industry Council said on Saturday that it detected suspicious activity on its CIC APP server on Friday and identified unauthorised access to personal data, with a preliminary investigation showing about 500,000 registered workers may be affected, about 85% of the roughly 587,000 registered as of October. The data involved names, dates of birth, gender, telephone numbers and identity document numbers, and the council said no financial data was affected. The council suspended the app, engaged an independent cybersecurity forensic specialist, and reported the breach to the police, the privacy commissioner and the Digital Policy Office, while the workers' site access system continued to operate normally.
