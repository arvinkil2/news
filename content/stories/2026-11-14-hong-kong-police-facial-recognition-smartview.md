Commissioner of Police Joe Chow Yat-ming said on Saturday that facial recognition goes live on Monday across the SmartView network, launched in 2024, and that the system can identify people even if they wear masks or take other steps to avoid detection. Officers can search recorded footage to trace a person's movements or enter a face for real-time alerts, for example to find a missing elderly person; searches require approval from an officer of inspector rank or above and must serve national security, crime prevention or detection, or public safety. Video footage is kept for no more than 31 days and facial recognition data for up to 180 days, and the city's privacy watchdog has opened a compliance check after a private housing estate in Lam Tin proposed facial recognition for access control and residents raised data protection concerns.
