France has confirmed that its military personnel are operating inside Ukraine, after Radio France Internationale reported on Monday that small French teams have been deployed there since at least the summer of 2026 under a confidential mission known as Volia. According to RFI's sources, the teams train Ukrainian soldiers and provide technical support for French-supplied equipment, including Caesar self-propelled howitzers and Mirage 2000 fighter jets, in small, short rotations led by a French general. The deployment was run under the cover of Gerfaut, the French training mission based in Poland since February 2023, and the French parliament was not informed of it, although the constitution requires the government to notify lawmakers of such operations.

Paris confirmed the presence on Tuesday while rejecting any combat role. Alice Rufo, the minister delegate for the armed forces, told the National Assembly that the personnel in Ukraine train soldiers and repair equipment, described the missions as extremely limited and far from the front line, and called Russia's claim of direct French participation absurd. Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot told France 5 that France is not a party to the war and does not intend to become one. France Télévisions, which said it confirmed the story, put the number of personnel at several dozen; Rufo gave no figure. Le Monde separately reported that small French units have worked periodically in Ukraine since the start of the full-scale invasion in February 2022, with the presence recently reinforced.

Russia's response was immediate. Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov, speaking in Manila on Tuesday, said foreign military personnel, not only French, had been in Ukraine for more than six months and had moved into direct participation in the war, including help with target selection and satellite data; that characterisation is Moscow's account and has not been independently corroborated. Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova said French soldiers in Ukraine would be legitimate targets, and the Russian embassy in Paris warned of a risk of direct confrontation between nuclear powers. Moscow had already said in February 2026 that it would treat any Western military deployment in Ukraine as foreign interference and a legitimate target.
