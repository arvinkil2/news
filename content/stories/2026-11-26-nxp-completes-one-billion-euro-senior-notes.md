NXP Semiconductors said in an SEC filing that its issuers, NXP B.V., NXP Funding LLC and NXP USA, Inc., completed an underwritten public offering on November 24 of 500 million euros of 4.625 percent senior notes due 2034 and 500 million euros of 4.875 percent senior notes due 2037, guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis by NXP Semiconductors N.V.

Interest on the 2034 notes is payable annually on February 24, beginning in 2027, and interest on the 2037 notes annually on November 24, beginning in 2027. NXP said it intends to use the net proceeds for general corporate purposes, which may include capital expenditures or debt repurchases or repayments.
