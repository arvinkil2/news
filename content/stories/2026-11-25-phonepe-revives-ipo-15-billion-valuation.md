PhonePe has resumed preparations for its initial public offering and is targeting a listing in the first three months of 2027 at a valuation of $12 billion to $15 billion, a person directly aware of the discussions told Reuters. The Walmart-backed payments company is expected to file updated draft papers with the Securities and Exchange Board of India within 30 to 45 days, having paused its listing plans in March amid global capital market volatility.

The issue will be an entirely secondary share sale raising $1.2 billion to $1.5 billion, keeping the size set out in PhonePe's January draft prospectus, under which majority owner Walmart would trim its stake by about 12 percent and Tiger Global and Microsoft would exit in full. PhonePe has more than 650 million registered users and processed nearly 11.4 billion of India's 25.4 billion UPI transactions in October.
