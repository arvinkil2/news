Kuwait's defense ministry said at least four people were injured on Thursday after Iranian drones targeting a vital facility were intercepted. In the ministry's account, falling shrapnel from the interceptions injured four people, who were taken to hospital, and the incident caused unspecified material damage. The statement did not name the facility, and no Iranian response had been reported.
