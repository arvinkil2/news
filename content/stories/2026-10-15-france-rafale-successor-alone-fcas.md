France will develop a next-generation combat aircraft, dubbed the Avion de Combat du Futur, as a national project to succeed the Rafale, Air and Space Force chief of staff Jerome Bellanger told the National Assembly's defense committee on Wednesday. The joint Future Combat Air System with Germany and Spain collapsed earlier this year after years of disputes between Dassault Aviation and Airbus over specifications and industrial control. Bellanger said the aircraft is targeted to fly conventional missions in 2040 and to carry the ASN4G hypersonic nuclear cruise missile in 2050, and that France stays open to cooperation on components such as the combat cloud, but not as a structural joint program.
