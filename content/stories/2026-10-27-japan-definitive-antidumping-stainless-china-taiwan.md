Japan’s Cabinet has enacted an order imposing definitive anti-dumping duties on nickel-added cold-rolled stainless steel coil, sheet and strip from China and Taiwan, the Ministry of Finance said on Tuesday. The rates are unchanged from the provisional duties in place since July 9, ranging from 3.6 percent to 42.1 percent, and the order will be made public on October 30, with the duties applying from October 31, 2026 to October 30, 2031.

The finance ministry and the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry have investigated the product since July 22, 2025, after an application from domestic producers, and confirmed dumped imports and material injury to the Japanese industry. The Council on Customs, Tariff, Foreign Exchange and Other Transactions reported on October 19 that definitive duties were appropriate.
