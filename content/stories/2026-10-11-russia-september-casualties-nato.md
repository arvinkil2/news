A senior NATO military official, speaking anonymously, told Bloomberg that Russia suffered more than 50,000 casualties in Ukraine in September 2026, with as many as 28,000 personnel killed, as Russian forces pushing offensive gains ran into Ukrainian kill zones of drones, mines and artillery. The same official put Ukrainian casualties for the month at about 5,000. The figure is single-sourced and unverified: no independent outlet has corroborated it, and NATO's own stake in the conflict means it should be read as the alliance's claim, not an established count.
