About 600,000 Nvidia H200 chips destined for mainland China are being held in Hong Kong awaiting import approval from the Chinese government, Datacenter Dynamics reported, citing Reuters. Small quantities have moved through intermediaries and indirect routes, and sources cited in the report said several major Chinese internet companies have each obtained about 10,000 H200 chips over the past several months.

The United States reversed its earlier ban on H200 exports to China in December 2025 and formalized the approval in January 2026, with a 25 percent export tariff attached and a bar on exporting anything more powerful than the H200. Bloomberg has reported that Chinese AI developers expect to receive only about one percent of the chips they have ordered in the coming months, a shortfall the report attributes to Nvidia's production capacity rather than to approvals alone.
