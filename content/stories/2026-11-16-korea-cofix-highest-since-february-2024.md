The October rise was COFIX's second in a row, after a 0.25 point increase in September, and the balance-based COFIX also rose, by 0.11 points to 3.26 percent. A Korea Federation of Banks official said bank time-deposit rates kept rising in October even though market interest rates fell slightly, pushing the funding index up.

KB Kookmin Bank will raise its new-transaction COFIX-based variable mortgage rate to 4.85 to 6.25 percent from November 17, and Woori Bank will raise its equivalent range to 5.04 to 6.24 percent.
