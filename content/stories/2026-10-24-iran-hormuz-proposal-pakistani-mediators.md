Iran proposed resuming shipping through the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for the United States lifting its blockade of Iranian ports, and Tehran had already transmitted the proposal to Washington through Pakistani intermediaries, Al Arabiya reported, citing its own sources, in a report relayed by TASS. Iran also notified Pakistan that it is ready to resume negotiations with the United States, the report said. The reported terms are narrower than the seven-condition reopening plan Tehran has put forward since September, which also sought sanctions relief and the release of frozen assets, and Iran had not commented on the Al Arabiya account.
