A group of financial firms including Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Banco Santander and Deutsche Bank has committed around $270 million toward a joint stablecoin venture, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Bloomberg. The money is intended to finance the new company's operations for five years; the company, called Project Faro among participants, will issue a dollar denominated stablecoin, is expected to incorporate in the United States, and is still searching for a chief executive. The commitment advances the plan a group of 21 institutions outlined in September to introduce a stablecoin in the first half of 2027, with tokens linked to other currencies, beginning with the euro, to follow.
