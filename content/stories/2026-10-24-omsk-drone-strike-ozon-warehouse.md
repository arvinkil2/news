A drone hit a logistics centre in Omsk on Saturday and the staff were promptly evacuated, regional governor Vitaliy Khotsenko said, as air defence forces continued to repel an attack in which drones reached the city's northern industrial hub and emergency services worked at the site. Ozon confirmed the centre was its own: the company said a fire broke out, all employees were evacuated, and on its preliminary account no one was injured. Ozon said goods stored at the centre would be hidden from its storefront, some orders would be cancelled or delivered from other facilities, and the centre would take in no new seller deliveries.

Ukrainian reporting, drawing on the OSINT project Exilenova+, said the Omsky Kauchuk synthetic rubber plant in the same district was also hit and a large fire covered its industrial zone, with a photograph showing flames and a column of smoke. The governor's statements do not name the plant, and Russian officials have not confirmed a fire there.
