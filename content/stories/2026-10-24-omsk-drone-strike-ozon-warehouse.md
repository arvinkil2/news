Air defence forces shot down two drones over Omsk on Saturday and one drone hit the warehouse of an Ozon logistics centre in the city's northern industrial district, regional governor Vitaliy Khotsenko said, in an attack that began at 13:45 local time and had not been given the all-clear hours later. The hit set about 1,400 square metres of the warehouse roof on fire, one middle-aged man was taken to hospital with injuries, 25 vehicles and containers were damaged and five of them burned, and staff were evacuated before the fire spread, the governor said, adding that no chemical leak resulted.

Ukrainian Telegram channels reported, and the Ukrainian outlet NV carried, that the Omsky Kauchuk synthetic rubber plant in the same district was also on fire, with one channel putting a column of smoke about 15 kilometres from the plant and residents reporting the smell of burning. Russian officials have not confirmed a fire at the plant, and their account covers the logistics centre alone.
