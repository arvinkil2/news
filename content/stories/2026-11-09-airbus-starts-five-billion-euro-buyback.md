Airbus said on Monday it is commencing the multi-year share buyback programme its board approved alongside the Airbus Business Update on July 21, 2026. The programme will be executed in the market in multiple tranches over a period ending no later than July 31, 2029, for a total amount of up to 5 billion euros, and Airbus intends to cancel the shares acquired through it.

The first tranche runs from November 9 through December 23, 2026, up to a maximum of 800 million euros, with the pace and total amount subject to market conditions; an investment firm has been mandated to manage its execution. The programme rests on the authority shareholders granted at the annual general meeting on April 14, 2026, to repurchase up to 10 percent of issued share capital, and its execution remains subject to continued shareholder approval.
