UPS and FedEx will increase their base shipping rates by 5.9 percent on average in the next round of annual rises, taking effect on 21 December for UPS and 4 January 2027 for FedEx, Supply Chain Dive reported. Lighter shipments will see larger increases than heavier ones: several UPS services will rise by more than 5.9 percent on average for packages of one to five pounds, against 5.6 percent or less for parcels of 21 pounds or more, according to an analysis by Sifted, with a similar pattern at FedEx.

Surcharges will also rise across the board, and consultants cited by the report estimate the effective increase for most shippers at 8 to 12 percent once fees are included. UPS is raising the minimum billable weight for packages requiring additional handling from 40 to 50 pounds, while FedEx will move more than 2,500 ZIP codes from its extended area surcharge of $8.80 per package to a remote area surcharge of $17.75 from 4 January.
