The Council of the European Union on Monday imposed restrictive measures on 12 individuals and 101 entities, including nearly 50 Russian banks and financial institutions, the sole shareholder of e-commerce giant Wildberries and an individual controlling the majority stake in Ozon, and energy companies that manage Russian refineries and the St. Petersburg oil terminal. The listings target Russia's shadow fleet through companies in the United Arab Emirates, China, Seychelles and Oman that buy Russian crude, and through businesspersons who own or manage vessels already under EU sanctions; gCaptain's read of the Official Journal names a Dubai-based management network around Lidoil DMCC and Spikoil DMCC, Moscow-based insurer Balance Insurance, which covered the previously sanctioned tanker GARUDA, and financing affiliate Lead Capital. Those listed are subject to an asset freeze and a prohibition on EU persons making funds or economic resources available to them, with travel bans applying to the individuals named.
