The International Energy Agency's annual Energy Efficiency 2026 report, published Monday, finds that primary energy intensity is expected to improve by nearly 3 percent this year, up from around 2 percent in 2025, while global energy demand is projected to remain at broadly the same level as in 2025, held back in part by the Strait of Hormuz crisis. Global electricity demand is still expected to grow by around 3.6 percent, and the agency said around 120 countries have introduced measures to save energy, provide financial relief or improve longer-term resilience, with more than 30 governments also adopting longer-term efficiency or electrification measures.
