India's National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority has capped the trade margin on the maximum retail price of 137 anti-cancer drugs, acting on the recommendations of an expert committee set up on October 17 by the Directorate General of Health Services. The revised prices took effect on Monday, November 9.

As of November 8, 85 pharmaceutical companies had reported revised maximum retail prices for 1,364 anti-cancer formulations covering 121 of the drugs. Of those, 679 brands cut prices by more than 50 percent, and the largest single reduction was 96.45 percent. The pricing authority estimates the cuts will save consumers about 3,540.38 crore rupees a year, and says no chemist or hospital pharmacy may charge above the revised price printed on the pack.
