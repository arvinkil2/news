ASEAN and China did not conclude their long-running negotiations on a code of conduct for the South China Sea at the bloc's summit in Manila, Philippine foreign minister Ma. Theresa Lazaro said at a press conference, despite a provisional agreement on about 80 percent of the draft text. The Philippines, as the bloc's current president, had committed to accelerate the talks and conclude the code this year.

Lazaro said several important questions remain unresolved, including whether the code would be legally binding, and that ASEAN countries and China hold different views on the situation. No new target date was given.
