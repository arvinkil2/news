Vizsla Silver said on Thursday it now targets first silver at its Panuco project in Sinaloa, Mexico, in the second half of 2028, a year later than previously planned, citing permitting, site readiness and construction timing as it prepares a phased return following the January abduction and killing of 10 workers at Concordia. Engineering is about halfway complete and about 85 percent of the planned processing plant equipment has been ordered, but Panuco still needs key federal approvals and the company has made no production decision. Shares fell 5.2 percent to $4.04 in Toronto morning trading.
