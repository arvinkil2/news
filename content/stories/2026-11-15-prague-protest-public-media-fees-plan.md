Between 350,000 and 450,000 people protested in Prague against the government's plan to abolish broadcasting fees, organizers said, with demonstrators travelling from across Czechia and public transport overcrowded. The plan would finance public broadcasters from the state budget instead of fees and cut their budgets by 15 percent, and protesters said it could bring the broadcasters under the control of Prime Minister Andrej Babis's government.

Babis defended the plan last week, saying his government is not interested in controlling or nationalizing public media, and Foreign Minister Petr Macinka accused the organizers of turning demonstrations into a business model. The European Broadcasting Union, the European Federation of Journalists and Reporters Without Borders have criticized the plan, which has drawn protests since its introduction in April, including a one-day strike by Czech TV and radio staff.
