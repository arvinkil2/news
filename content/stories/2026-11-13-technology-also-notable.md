Marvell Technology expanded its board from seven to ten members effective November 12, appointing Donna C. Morris, Douglas R. Bettinger and Sasan K. Goodarzi, its Form 8-K said. Morris and Goodarzi join the Executive Compensation Committee and Bettinger joins the Audit Committee. The filing is a governance refresh at one of the main custom silicon and networking suppliers to the AI buildout, with no executive departure involved.

Google put its Gemini 4 Hydrogen model into Search for all users worldwide, which the company calls its biggest model improvement to Search yet. TechCrunch reported the model improves handling of multi part and highly specific questions, adds more visual and structured answers in AI Mode, and that Google claims it completes tasks 2.5 times faster than its previous fast model, Gemini 3.8 Flash. Those performance figures are Google's own claims.

CoreWeave filed its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30 on November 12, the formal filing behind the third quarter results already covered on November 10. No new headline figure in the filing changes that story, and chief executive comments on capital markets volatility reported by Datacenter Dynamics add colour rather than new numbers, so no separate story is written.

CISA published a cluster of Siemens industrial product advisories in the ranked pool, covering RUGGEDCOM APE1808, Desigo and Gridscale lines and Industrial Edge Management. They are routine patching notices with no disclosed exploitation at scale, carried here from the pool's orphan lane as the primary source items they are.
