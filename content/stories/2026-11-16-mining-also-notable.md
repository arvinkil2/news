**Greenland Energy to acquire 80 Mile.** Greenland Energy has agreed to acquire 80 Mile in an all share merger valued at 74 million pounds, Mining Weekly reported. The combination consolidates two London listed explorers with Greenland exposure at a time when critical minerals interest in the island is rising.

**Perseus reaches Nyanzaga milestone.** Perseus Mining reached a significant commissioning milestone at its Nyanzaga gold project in Tanzania, Mining Weekly reported, keeping the build on the path this beat has tracked through construction. No production figures were stated in the item.

**Rio Tinto pledges $250 million for Panguna fund.** Rio Tinto has pledged 250 million dollars, 356 million Canadian dollars, to address environmental and human rights impacts from the former Panguna copper and gold mine in Bougainville, Papua New Guinea, the Northern Miner reported. The money will be paid over coming years into a fund agreed with Bougainville Copper, the autonomous regional government and community leaders, with its governance and spending priorities still to be settled by consultation, the parties said Monday. Panguna operated from 1972 until an uprising and civil war forced its closure in 1989, and Rio held a controlling interest in Bougainville Copper until 2016. An independent investigation published in December 2024 identified unstable mine structures, contaminated river areas and flooding that threatened homes, food gardens and drinking water, and the fund will support work in river and delta areas and social programmes across affected communities.
