LS Cable and System said on Monday it has begun supplying cables worth about 700 billion won through its North American subsidiary, LS Cable System America, for Propel New York Energy, the transmission project promoted jointly by the New York Power Authority and New York Transco. The roughly 145 kilometre project connects Long Island with Queens and the Bronx in New York City and Westchester County.

The company will supply 345kV extra-high-voltage submarine and underground cables and carry out the electrical construction and completion testing. The contract was signed in February, and construction is now moving into full swing after New York State authorities recently approved the construction plan, Yonhap reported.
