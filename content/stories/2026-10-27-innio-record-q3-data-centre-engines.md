INNIO N.V. reported third-quarter revenue of $1.2 billion, up 57 percent year over year and its first quarter above $1 billion, with equipment revenue up 86 percent to $777.6 million on large-scale data centre deliveries, net income of $109.2 million against $39.5 million a year earlier, and adjusted EBITDA up 70 percent to $245.4 million. Equipment order intake reached $2.2 billion in the quarter and the equipment order backlog stood at $8.0 billion at September 30, up 142 percent year over year.

The company raised its fiscal 2026 outlook to revenue of $3.9 to $4.0 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $780 to $800 million. Orders in the quarter included a 620 MW follow-on order, a 450 MW order from Liberty Energy for North American data centre projects with behind-the-meter power to be delivered by 2028, and an extension of its Aggreko partnership through 2031 covering a minimum of 450 MW of Jenbacher engine capacity.
