Rare earth miners fell 5.92 percent, the largest move in the tables, with copper miners down 5.37 percent and BHP down 4.96 percent alongside them. The mining complex is carrying the week's AI rout and China's soft October activity data, the same combination that took copper to a four-month low in Monday's trading (see Monday's copper story and today's China copper smelting story).

Semiconductors fell 5.41 percent and Nvidia fell 4.12 percent. Asia's shares slid on the tech selloff ahead of Nvidia's earnings, and Monday's reporting had Nvidia slipping premarket as Treasury Secretary Bessent demanded a shakeup; the overnight session extended both moves rather than starting a new one.

Silver miners fell 4.45 percent. The miners are following the metal, with silver futures down 7.36 percent on the week in the tables even after a 1.19 percent daily bounce, a slide that sits against the Silver Institute's sixth straight annual deficit forecast noted in Monday's commodities roundup. No fresh silver-specific news arrived overnight to explain the miners' move beyond the metal's own weekly fall.

Wheat rose 3.71 percent, the largest gainer in the commodity table. No clear single driver appears in this morning's reporting; the only wheat item in the pool pointed the other way, arguing that optimism over trilateral talks freeing up Black Sea shipments was weighing on prices.

Tanker freight rose 3.19 percent, extending the previous session's jump. The move tracks the Hormuz tanker incidents in Monday's reporting, a projectile hit that set a tanker's engine room on fire and Iranian claims of naval mine strikes, which have kept Gulf shipping risk priced into freight.

The cross-market read is risk-off but narrow. Equities and miners fell together while Treasury yields were bid, the five-year yield down 4.6 basis points to 4.93 percent on the day after a sharp weekly backup, the dollar index was nearly flat, up 0.05 percent, and Brent crude rose 0.26 percent to 92.68 dollars, held up by Hormuz risk rather than demand. Money is leaving the AI and metals complex, not chasing a macro haven.
