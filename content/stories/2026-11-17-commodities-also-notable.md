**KOGAS ordered to pay about $200 million over LNG carrier defects.** The Seoul High Court ruled on November 11 that Korea Gas Corporation must pay KRW 268.5 billion (about $200 million) in compensation over design defects in the domestically developed KC-1 cargo containment system: KRW 220.4 billion to SK Shipping, nearly double the first-instance award, and KRW 48.1 billion to Samsung Heavy Industries. The defects left the 2018-built LNG carriers SK Serenity and SK Spica unable to operate safely. The ruling remains subject to further appeal.

**Southern California import containers pass one million TEU again.** Combined imports at the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach reached 1,000,500 TEU in October, only the second time the one million mark has been passed and just below September's all-time high. Loaded volumes were up more than 20 percent year on year at Los Angeles and more than 11 percent at Long Beach, while exports fell 7 percent and 6 percent respectively. Port executives attributed the surge to AI buildout cargo, tariff uncertainty and frontloading ahead of the January expiry of the US-China trade truce; the National Retail Federation expects volumes to fall 3 percent in January.
