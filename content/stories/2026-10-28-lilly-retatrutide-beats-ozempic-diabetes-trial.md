Eli Lilly said on Wednesday that retatrutide, its once weekly experimental drug, met the primary and all key secondary endpoints in TRANSCEND-T2D-2, a Phase 3 head-to-head trial against Novo Nordisk's Ozempic in adults with type 2 diabetes. At 80 weeks, both retatrutide doses lowered A1C, a measure of blood sugar over time, by an average of 2.2 percentage points, against 1.8 points for Ozempic at 2 milligrams, the highest approved dose.

Participants on retatrutide lost an average of 19.5 percent of their weight at the 9 milligram dose and 20.9 percent at 12 milligrams, against 9.4 percent on Ozempic, and Lilly said the drug also gave larger improvements in non-HDL cholesterol, triglycerides and systolic blood pressure. Retatrutide activates three hormone receptors, GLP-1, GIP and glucagon, and Lilly said this was the sixth Phase 3 trial in which it has delivered its expected efficacy.
