Ukraine attacked Russia with a large drone barrage early on November 16, hitting the Crimean Bridge and, according to Bloomberg, likely an oil refinery as well. Crimea's Moscow-installed governor, Sergei Aksyonov, said on Telegram that traffic on the bridge would remain interrupted until about midday, that services were working on the consequences of the attack, and that an emergency coordination centre had been set up in Kerch; the ferry service between Crimea and the port of Kavkaz was still operating.

Neither report gave a verified count of drones or described the extent of any damage to the bridge, and no Ukrainian official statement was carried in either. Bloomberg reported the strike came after US-brokered peace talks ended without a breakthrough.
