Two chemical tankers caught fire in Romanian territorial waters near the mouth of the Sulina Canal, where the Danube meets the Black Sea. Seven of the 12 crew aboard the San Marino flagged Wine Trader, sailing from Tunisia to Romania, are missing and five are safe, while all crew aboard the Comoros flagged Yara J were reported safe.

President Nicusor Dan condemned attacks on commercial shipping and said he and NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte had agreed to strengthen Black Sea defences. A NATO military source told Reuters the drones were of Russian origin, an attribution Romania has not confirmed: its defence minister said investigators are still establishing whether a drone struck the Wine Trader.
