Ismail Lasim has resigned as Menteri Besar of Negeri Sembilan, Barisan Nasional secretary-general Zambry Abd Kadir confirmed, after Ismail submitted his resignation letter on Monday to the Yang Dipertuan Besar, Tuanku Muhriz Tuanku Munawir. BN chairman Ahmad Zahid Hamidi will propose a successor in accordance with the state constitution, and BN pledged cooperation to ensure an orderly transition.

The resignation follows months of controversy over an earlier attempt by several disputed chieftains to remove Tuanku Muhriz, a dispute that had paralysed the state administration. Ismail, 67, who was sworn in on August 2 after BN won 18 state seats and Perikatan Nasional seven, told reporters through a WhatsApp message to Bernama that he would not meet the press or issue a statement for now, and asked to be given space and time.
