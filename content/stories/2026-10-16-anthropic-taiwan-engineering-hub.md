Anthropic is building an engineering presence in Taiwan, the AI lab's latest international expansion and its most hardware adjacent. Taiwan is where the company's compute supply chain physically lives: TSMC fabricates the accelerators its models run on, and the island's engineering workforce is the deepest in semiconductors anywhere.

The constraint is people. EE Times reported this week that Taiwan's AI ambitions are running into a talent gap, with chip firms, the government and now incoming AI labs competing for the same engineers. An Anthropic hub adds a new bidder at the top of that market, and a new kind: software and research roles from a frontier lab, against the hardware roles Taiwan's universities have historically fed.

Anthropic's expansion follows its pattern of planting small, senior teams in strategic locations rather than mass hiring. In Taiwan the value is proximity: to TSMC's roadmap conversations, to the island's AI server industry, and to a government that treats AI capability as national strategy.
