WiseTech Global shares jumped 16.7 percent to A$54.61 in Sydney trading on Friday, their highest level since February 4, after chief financial officer Jeff Howard told the annual general meeting that fiscal 2027 trading had started well and that full-year guidance was unchanged, at revenue of A$1.48 billion to A$1.54 billion and underlying EBITDA of $725 million to $780 million, a margin of 49 to 51 percent. The core CargoWise platform grew revenue 11 percent through October on higher transaction volumes and price rises under its new commercial model, while the e2open business acquired in 2025 fell 6 percent on a comparable basis, though it remained slightly ahead of plan.
