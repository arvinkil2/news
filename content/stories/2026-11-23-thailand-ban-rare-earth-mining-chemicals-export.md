Thailand is set to ban the export and transit of chemicals used in rare earth mining and to tighten controls on mineral trade through Thailand from Myanmar, Deputy Prime Minister Yodchanan Wongsawat said on Saturday after a meeting of the National Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Command in Bangkok. The measures respond to arsenic and heavy metal contamination detected since early 2025 in the Kok, Sai and Ruak rivers, Mekong tributaries in northern Thailand, which the government links to gold and rare earth mining in Myanmar's Shan State. A 150 kilometre stretch of the Kok River is to be designated a pollution control zone from December 2.

Foreign Minister Sihasak Phuanketkaew is to take the issue to ministerial talks with China and Myanmar, the government said. Most of the mining sits in territory held by armed groups outside Myanmar government control, which limits Bangkok to indirect measures such as the trade ban, water testing and clean water supply for affected residents.
