The French navy detained the oil tanker DF Peak in the Indian Ocean on Monday morning, more than 450 nautical miles south of Reunion, President Emmanuel Macron said in a statement on X reported by Ukrainska Pravda. Macron said the ship was suspected of sailing under a false flag and of involvement in illegal fuel transport, and that the operation was carried out under international maritime law.

Ukrainian military intelligence data cited in the report links DF Peak to Sovcomflot, the sanctioned Russian shipping company, and says the tanker carries crude oil and is sanctioned by the United States, Canada, the European Union, Ukraine, Switzerland, Japan and Britain. France has acted against shadow fleet tonnage before, fining the detained tanker Tagor one million euros in May, and the EU is separately planning to extend its Mediterranean naval mission against the shadow fleet to March 2029.
