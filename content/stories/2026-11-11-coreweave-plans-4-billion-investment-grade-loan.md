CoreWeave is preparing to raise at least $4 billion through an investment-grade loan facility, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, in a transaction led by Morgan Stanley and Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group that would be syndicated in phases and could launch as early as next week. Early pricing discussions point to an anticipated yield between 7 percent and 7.5 percent, roughly half the prevailing yield on the company's existing high-yield debt.

The facility would be structured around CoreWeave's computing hardware and underlying customer commitments, mirroring an $8.5 billion investment-grade transaction completed in March that was secured against long-term contracts with Meta Platforms, with a floating-rate tranche at 2.25 percentage points over SOFR and a fixed-rate tranche priced near 5.9 percent. The plan follows Tuesday's raised full-year revenue outlook of $13 billion to $13.3 billion, above Wall Street's $12.9 billion consensus.
