Oryx Energies has been sold for about $1 billion in a management buyout, Bloomberg reported October 8. Oryx Energies is the downstream and trading business of the Addax & Oryx Group, the Geneva-based group built around energy in Africa, and operates petroleum and refined-products trading, storage, distribution, LPG, lubricants, bitumen and bunkering across sub-Saharan Africa.

The deal turns the company's management into the owners of an integrated trading-and-downstream platform in some of the most logistics-intensive fuel markets in the world. Details beyond the headline value have not been independently confirmed: the buyer-side financing, the exact assets included, and whether Addax & Oryx retains any stake are not disclosed in the available reporting. The transaction is tracked here as a reported management buyout of African fuel-distribution infrastructure until further confirmation emerges.
