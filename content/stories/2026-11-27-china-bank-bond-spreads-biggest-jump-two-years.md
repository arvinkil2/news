Chinese bank bond spreads are heading for their biggest monthly increase since September 2024, Bloomberg reported on Friday, as stress at a local state-owned borrower ripples through the broader credit market. The spread on some highly rated bonds issued by commercial banks widened by another 17 basis points.

Bloomberg linked the move to Zhuhai Huafa Group, which it said missed payments on two trust products and asked to extend some bank loans. No absolute spread level was given in the portion of the report that could be read.
