ElevenLabs, the New York company that builds realistic-sounding AI voices, is in early discussions for a funding round at a target valuation of $33 billion, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, citing people familiar with the matter. The company plans to start the fundraising formally in the first three months of 2027, though some investors are pushing to begin sooner, and its annual recurring revenue is now about $700 million, up from about $600 million in September.

The target would be the company's third valuation step in under a year: $11 billion in its February funding round, then $22 billion in a September employee tender offer. The report gives no amount sought, no lead investor and no terms, and the talks are preliminary, so the figure is a target rather than an agreed valuation.
