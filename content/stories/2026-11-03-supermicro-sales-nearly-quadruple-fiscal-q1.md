Super Micro Computer reported unaudited net sales of $19.9 billion for its first quarter of fiscal 2027, ended September 30, 2026, up from $5.0 billion a year earlier and $11.1 billion in the prior quarter, with net income of $1.59 billion, or $1.75 per diluted share, and non-GAAP earnings of $1.81 per share. Gross margin was 12.7 percent, down from 17.5 percent in the prior quarter, and operations used $434 million of cash in the quarter.

The company guided second quarter sales to $19.5 billion to $21.5 billion and fiscal 2027 sales to $72.0 billion to $82.0 billion.
