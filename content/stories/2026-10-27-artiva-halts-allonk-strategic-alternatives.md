Artiva Biotherapeutics is halting all further development of AlloNK (AB-101), its non-genetically modified, allogeneic natural killer cell therapy candidate, and has begun a process to explore strategic alternatives, the company said in a Form 8-K filed on Tuesday. The board approved the discontinuation on October 22, and the wind down includes a reduction of the workforce by about 94 percent of the current employee base, which the company expects to complete in the first quarter of 2027.

The company reported preliminary cash, cash equivalents and investments of about $319.1 million as of September 30, 2026, and said it could not yet reasonably estimate the total costs and charges of the wind down, which will cover clinical trial termination, leases and the workforce reduction, with most of the costs expected in the fourth quarter of 2026. Complete quarterly results will follow in its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30.
