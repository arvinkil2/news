The Kuwait-flagged tanker MT Kazimah III was struck by an unknown projectile while transiting the Strait of Hormuz on Friday, according to UKMTO, suffering a fire and blackout; five Indian seafarers were rescued. It was the sixth vessel hit in the strait since Sunday, per gCaptain, and a separate tanker was struck off Oman. The New York Times reported a pattern of roughly 40 attacks a week on commercial shipping as Iran pressures global trade.

The US response is a major force buildup: the Wall Street Journal reported a third US aircraft carrier and up to 10,000 additional troops are heading to the Middle East, on top of the ongoing naval blockade of Iranian ports. Trump's top aides held a secret meeting on Iran war and Yemen options, and Trump warned bombing could intensify after the Nov 3 midterms. Iran, for its part, says Hormuz will not reopen until its conditions are met.

Brent finished Friday near $102.60, down on the week — paper markets are pricing diplomacy and the G7 stock release, not a shooting war in the world's most important oil chokepoint. That divergence is the single most dangerous assumption in commodity markets right now.
