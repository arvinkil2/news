The UK government on Thursday announced the budget arrangements for Allocation Round 8, the next round of its Contracts for Difference renewables auction, during the final week of COP31. In a change from previous rounds, prices across all wind and solar technologies will be visible to ministers before they confirm which projects to procure, and initial budgets have been set at a notional £1, with the final budget to be set only after bids have been submitted and increased where projects offer value for money.

The department said previous auction rounds have secured around 24GW of offshore wind capacity since the scheme began, that auctions in the past two years have secured enough clean electricity to power more than 20 million homes, and that over £110 billion of private investment has been unlocked. It cited RWE's confirmed £12 billion investment in the Vanguard offshore wind projects from the previous round, and said more substantial reforms to the Contracts for Difference scheme are being progressed for Allocation Round 9.
