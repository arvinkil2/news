**Latest.** Intuit has entered into a definitive agreement to sell Mailchimp, its email marketing automation platform, to Bending Spoons for approximately $3.68 billion, made up of $3.31 billion in cash and $368 million in Bending Spoons stock. The transaction is expected to close in early calendar year 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions, and Intuit intends to use the cash proceeds to repurchase its own shares within fiscal 2027 following the close.

The divestiture was announced alongside first-quarter fiscal 2027 results for the quarter ended October 31, 2026, which chief executive Sasan Goodarzi said came in above the top end of the company's guidance. Total revenue rose 12 percent to $4.4 billion, Global Business Solutions revenue rose 15 percent to $3.1 billion, and Consumer revenue rose 7 percent to $958 million. GAAP operating income rose 63 percent to $868 million and GAAP diluted earnings per share rose 63 percent to $2.59, while Mailchimp revenue decreased 1 percent to $313 million.

Intuit said selling Mailchimp sharpens its focus on its AI-native platform for winning customers and managing business pipelines. The company updated its full-year fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to $23,081 million to $23,365 million, reiterated its operating income and earnings per share guidance, and said the guidance does not include any impact from the pending Mailchimp sale, which it will update at the time of close. Intuit repurchased $1.5 billion of stock in the quarter and its board approved a quarterly dividend of $1.38 per share, payable on January 15, 2027.
