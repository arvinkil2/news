A tower in Saudi Arabia's financial district was targeted in a terrorist attack, Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Malki, spokesperson for the Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen, said on Tuesday, according to Arab News. Al-Malki said competent authorities are responding at the site.

The report names no specific tower and gives no time, method, casualties or damage, and no perpetrator or claim of responsibility has been reported. The claim is uncorroborated at this stage and is carried here as the coalition's statement, not as a confirmed account of an attack.
