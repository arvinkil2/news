Two high-speed trains collided just after 9 a.m. local time on Wednesday between the towns of Mora and Orgaz in Toledo province, and rail operator Adif said traffic on the Madrid to Andalusia high-speed line has been suspended following a rear-end collision between two trains. A spokesperson for the Castilla La Mancha regional government said a Renfe train and an Iryo train travelling from Madrid to Seville crashed and two Iryo wagons derailed, adding that the cause and the number of injuries were still being determined. The mayor of Urda, near Toledo, said emergency services had asked him to prepare a sports hall to accommodate the injured, and El Pais cited witnesses describing numerous injuries, which no official source has confirmed.
