## Latest

Dubai Islamic Bank has agreed to acquire Emirates Islamic in an all-share transaction, Arab News reported on Thursday, turning this week's board-level talks into a signed agreement. Emirates NBD, which wholly owns Emirates Islamic, will receive newly issued Dubai Islamic Bank shares and become a strategic shareholder in the enlarged bank, which will have about 585 billion dirhams ($159.2 billion) in assets, making it the UAE's largest Islamic banking group and the world's second-largest listed Islamic banking group by assets, behind Saudi Arabia's Al Rajhi Bank at $278 billion. The deal pairs Dubai Islamic's corporate banking strength with Emirates Islamic's retail and deposit franchise, and remains subject to final approvals from the Central Bank of the UAE, the Capital Markets Authority and other authorities, plus shareholder approvals; no exchange ratio or closing date was disclosed.

Officials in Dubai have held preliminary talks on potentially combining Dubai Islamic Bank with Emirates Islamic Bank to create a Sharia-compliant lender better positioned to compete globally, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. No final decisions have been made and a deal may not materialise, the people said. Emirates Islamic, wholly owned by Emirates NBD, has assets of more than 158 billion dirhams ($43 billion), against 427 billion dirhams at Dubai Islamic Bank, putting a combined lender at about 585 billion dirhams ($159 billion).

Both boards met on Wednesday, November 25: Dubai Islamic Bank said its board would convene to "explore and evaluate an opportunity for a strategic acquisition," and Emirates NBD said its board would meet the same day to "consider and approve potential transaction opportunities," without naming counterparties. The Investment Corporation of Dubai holds about 28 percent of Dubai Islamic Bank and 41 percent of Emirates NBD. Citigroup analysts said the likelier outcome is a sale of Emirates Islamic to Dubai Islamic Bank, and Dubai Islamic shares posted their biggest intraday gain since December 2014 when the talks surfaced on Monday.
