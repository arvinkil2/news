The United States opened fiscal 2027 with a budget deficit of $404.1 billion in October, the Monthly Treasury Statement shows. Receipts were $368.9 billion against outlays of $773.0 billion in the month.

The gap was about 42 percent wider than in October 2025, the first month of fiscal 2026, when the deficit was $284.3 billion on receipts of $404.4 billion and outlays of $688.7 billion. Receipts fell year on year while spending rose, so the new fiscal year starts with a heavier borrowing requirement than the last one did.
