A Sunday morning outage in Delta Air Lines' automated weight-and-balance system forced the Federal Aviation Administration to briefly halt Atlanta-bound flights and reportedly delayed at least 3,500 Delta flights across major hubs, including Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport and New York's LaGuardia Airport. The ground stop was lifted after roughly an hour, and a ground delay program for Atlanta was no longer listed on the FAA's National Airspace System website shortly before noon Eastern.

Delta said its teams were using manual workarounds to get flights departed safely while work continued to restore automation. Crews must verify that an aircraft's total weight and center of gravity fall within safety limits before takeoff, a process that relies heavily on baggage and cargo data, so staff calculated weight and balance figures by hand while the software was offline.
