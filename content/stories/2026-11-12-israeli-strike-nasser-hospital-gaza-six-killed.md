An Israeli airstrike hit Nasser hospital in Khan Younis at about 12.30am local time on Thursday, killing six people and wounding 28, hospital director Dr Ahmed al-Fara said. The dead include Dr Rizk Abu Shaira, a 52-year-old general surgeon, who was killed alongside a patient he had operated on, and part of the surgery department was destroyed, with services suspended in the surgical, radiology, laboratory and inpatient departments.

The Israeli military said in a statement that it had struck a Hamas commander and other fighters at the hospital, without providing evidence. Israel has accused Hamas of using hospitals as command centres, an accusation Hamas rejects, and the Guardian notes the hospital, one of the last functioning facilities in Gaza, has been attacked seven times since the war began.
