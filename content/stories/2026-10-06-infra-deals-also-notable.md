**Amazon's data-center charm offensive.** AWS is ending secret non-disclosure pacts with host municipalities and pledging $1 billion over five years to towns that host its data centers under a "Built Together" program covering education, job training, energy affordability and 30,000 home efficiency retrofits, Tom's Hardware reported October 5. The move follows AWS CEO Matt Garman's October 2 essay warning that roughly $68 billion of data-center development across 27 states has been blocked or stalled by local opposition, with about 100 bans or moratoriums under consideration. Separately, Tom's Hardware reported October 6 that US data-center construction spending hit a record $85 billion annual pace, up 73 percent in a year.

**Solar portfolio rotation.** Exus Renewables North America acquired four solar projects totaling 715 MW from ibV Energy Partners, including 310 MW across two phases of Maple Grove Solar in Wisconsin and 405 MW across two Louisiana projects, with terms undisclosed (Mercom, October 6). Alight acquired 40 large-scale solar projects totaling about 2 GW of planned capacity in southern Sweden from Soltech Energy Solutions, lifting its Nordic pipeline above 5 GW, after Swedish strategic-products approval; terms undisclosed (Mercom, October 5).

**Clean-tech financing wave.** MaxSolar secured €155 million (~$176 million) in HoldCo debt from Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners' Green Credit strategy to refinance its holding-company financing and expand about 1.3 GW of German solar and battery storage (Mercom, October 6). Reverion raised $175 million in Series B funding, led by Kembara, to build a German factory scaling reversible solid-oxide fuel-cell output tenfold to 250 MW a year and create up to 800 jobs (Mercom, October 6). Sunrock reached financial close with Crédit Agricole CIB on a €110 million (~$125 million) green financing facility for French commercial and industrial rooftop solar (Mercom, October 6). MET Group took a majority stake in Greece's Zephiros Energy Trading, a licensed renewable aggregator, for undisclosed terms (Mercom, October 5).

**Federal and state infrastructure money.** The Department of Transportation awarded $426 million through the INFRA program to projects in 12 states, including $85.1 million for a Kentucky highway and $85 million for I-4 lanes in Florida, per an October 1 DOT release (Construction Dive, October 6). Maryland's Public Service Commission awarded 440 MW/1,760 MWh of storage in its first bulk procurement, below the 800 MW target: Flatiron Energy's 400 MW Chalk Point project and REV Renewables' 40 MW Jade Meadow III, at 15-year flat pricing costing the average residential customer 6.98 cents a month combined (Utility Dive, October 6). MISO's proposal to clear its legacy interconnection queue of about 71 GW drew support from state regulators and developers in FERC filings, though Pathway Power protested the risk shift; MISO wants approval by October 19 (Utility Dive, October 6).

**Industrial and military construction.** Iowa Governor Kim Reynolds signed House File 2801, allowing $1.36 billion in tax incentives for a $15 billion Mesabi Metallics steel mill backed by India's Essar Group and President Trump, expected to employ at least 1,750 people using direct-reduced iron and electric arc furnace technology (Construction Dive, October 6). Tutor Perini's Perini Management Services won a $315 million Coast Guard fuel pier at Base Kodiak, Alaska, and Skanska won a $271 million pier replacement at Naval Station Newport, Rhode Island, both slated to complete in 2030 (Construction Dive, October 6).

**Funds and PE add-ons.** AltAssets' October 6 deal roundup flagged Warburg Pincus buying into Ares, TDR Capital taking OCU Group from Triton after fourfold revenue growth, Antin Infrastructure investing in HP Helicopters, Niobrara Capital beating its debut target by 50 percent with a $1.1 billion close, and Dutch pension giant ABP committing €250 million to the EQT-run Scaleup Europe Fund as part of a €1 billion VC push. PE Hub's October 6 flow included add-ons and platforms: Vance Street's Fumex buying Filtrabox, Beach Point investing in Fullers Foods, Gryphon's TTDS adding Thermal Products, Bertram Capital backing Percopo Coatings, Sterling Group's Foundation Fund buying Slingmax, FFL Partners investing in Ally Technical, and Prelude Growth exiting MadeGood to the founding family.
