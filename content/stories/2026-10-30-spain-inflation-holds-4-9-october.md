Spain's annual consumer price inflation was 4.9 percent in October, the same rate as in September, according to the flash estimate the National Statistics Institute (INE) published on Friday. Prices rose 0.6 percent on the month. Core inflation, which excludes unprocessed food and energy products, was 3.1 percent, and the harmonised index also rose 4.9 percent year on year, one tenth lower than in September, with a monthly rise of 0.5 percent. The INE said falling prices for fuels and lubricants for private vehicles pulled the annual rate down, as did food and clothing prices rising less than a year earlier, while package holidays, gas and liquid fuels pushed it up. The figures are a preview of the final data, which the INE will publish next month.
