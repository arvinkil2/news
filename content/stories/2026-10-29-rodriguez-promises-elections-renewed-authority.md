Venezuela's interim president Delcy Rodríguez promised on Thursday that elections will be held in the country under a renewed electoral authority, saying the process would have the world as witness, in remarks delivered hours before opposition leader María Corina Machado was due to address the United Nations General Assembly. The commitment covers presidential and parliamentary elections and a reform of the National Electoral Council, the electoral body the opposition accuses of operating under chavista control, but Rodríguez named no date, which the council itself would have to set. The pledge follows the disputed presidential election of July 28, 2024, in which Nicolás Maduro was proclaimed the winner and the opposition denounced fraud, and it lands in Rodríguez's second period as interim president since Maduro's capture in January.
