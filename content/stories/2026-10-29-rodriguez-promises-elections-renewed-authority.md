## Latest

Rodríguez also replaced her foreign minister on Thursday, dismissing Félix Plasencia and appointing Oliver Blanco Marco, El Pitazo reported (EFE copy). Blanco had been vice minister for Europe and North America since February 2026. His background is in the opposition party Acción Democrática: he was organization secretary of its youth wing, an adviser to its leader Henry Ramos Allup, and head of communications of the National Assembly when Ramos Allup presided over it; he later founded the digital outlet El Cooperante and advised Raúl Gorrín, the Globovisión owner sought by US justice, and in recent years has worked on communications for Delcy and Jorge Rodríguez. Plasencia, who had been foreign minister since July 2026, becomes director of International Affairs of the Presidency, tasked, Rodríguez said on state television, with recovering the gold held at the Bank of England.

Venezuela's interim president Delcy Rodríguez promised on Thursday that all the elections established by the constitution will be held, organized by a renewed electoral authority and with the world as witness, but she gave no date, saying the political horizon is a sequence of objectives, among them fair conditions for all political forces, rather than a day on the calendar. She spoke shortly before opposition leader María Corina Machado, who returned to Venezuela days ago after almost a year in exile, was due to hold her first press conference; the opposition is demanding presidential elections in 2027, and Clarín reports that Washington backs a roadmap under which the Supreme Court and the National Electoral Council would be renewed in the coming months, leading Rodríguez to declare the presidency vacant and open a six month period for calling elections. Rodríguez also said she has instructed the security forces that no person be detained without a judicial order except when caught in the act, and recalled an amnesty law she says has benefited almost 9,000 people, with more than 1,000 released.
