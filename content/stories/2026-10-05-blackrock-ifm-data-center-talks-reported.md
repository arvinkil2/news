A consortium backed by BlackRock and IFM Investors has entered exclusive talks to acquire Stack Infrastructure's Asia-Pacific data centers from owner Blue Owl Capital, in a potential deal valued at $20 billion to $25 billion. The investor group includes the BlackRock-backed AI Infrastructure Partnership — the vehicle behind last year's $40 billion Aligned Data Centers take-private — alongside Australian infrastructure investor IFM, and is now conducting due diligence on the assets.

Blue Owl was initially seeking more than $30 billion for the portfolio, which spans Tokyo, Osaka, Sydney and Melbourne, so the $20-25 billion talk range already prices a meaningful bid-ask. The deliberations are ongoing and the talks could drag on or fall apart; other suitors may still be interested. The AIP's backers include Microsoft, Abu Dhabi's MGX, Nvidia, the Kuwait Investment Authority and Temasek.

A correction to weekend coverage: a low-quality trade blog reported the same $25 billion figure attached to a different portfolio — hyperscale facilities in Virginia, Oregon and Frankfurt. That version is unverified and conflicts with the Bloomberg-sourced reporting on the Asia-Pacific assets; the credible tracked item is the Stack APAC deal from Blue Owl. Until the parties or a credible outlet confirm a signing, the status stays where it has been since late September: reported, unresolved, and not confirmable.
