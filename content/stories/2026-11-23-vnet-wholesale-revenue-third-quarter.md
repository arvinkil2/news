VNET Group, the Chinese carrier-neutral data centre operator, reported third-quarter total net revenue of RMB2.96 billion ($442.0 million), up 14.8 percent from RMB2.58 billion a year earlier, in results filed with the SEC on Monday. Wholesale data centre revenue rose 27.6 percent to RMB1.22 billion ($181.8 million) and exceeded retail revenue for the third consecutive quarter, at 41.1 percent of the total. Adjusted EBITDA rose 21.7 percent to RMB923.1 million ($137.7 million), a margin of 31.1 percent, and the net loss attributable to the company narrowed to RMB83.1 million from RMB307.0 million a year earlier.

The operating build is wholesale-led. Wholesale capacity in service rose 44.1 percent year on year to 1,127MW at September 30, and utilised wholesale capacity rose 37.1 percent to 797MW. During the quarter the wholesale business took a 60MW order from a leading cloud service provider, and retail monthly recurring revenue per cabinet rose 13.6 percent to RMB10,167.

On funding, VNET said it completed the first follow-on offering under its private REITs scheme on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, added competitively priced domestic financing and set up a new joint infrastructure investment fund, and that it is deepening a compute and energy collaboration with battery maker CATL after strategic investors closed their investment in September. Management framed the strategy as capital efficiency: capacity added against signed demand, and assets recycled through the REIT channel to support free cash flow.
