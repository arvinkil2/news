Private employers added 86,000 jobs in October, ADP said on Wednesday, a second month of solid hiring that beat the 69,000 increase economists polled by Reuters had forecast. September's gain was revised down to 86,000 from 90,000, so the pace of hiring was flat rather than accelerating, and the gain was concentrated in services, which added 82,000 jobs against 4,000 in goods production, while professional and business services lost 27,000 jobs for a third straight monthly decline.

Pay growth stayed firm. Base pay rose 3.2 percent over the year and gross pay 4.7 percent, with job changers' earnings growth at its fastest pace since March 2024, according to ADP chief economist Nela Richardson. The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes the official October employment report on Friday.
