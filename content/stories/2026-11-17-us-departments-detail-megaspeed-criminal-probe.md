The US Department of Commerce began investigating Megaspeed in 2024 after proactive monitoring flagged suspected diversion of sensitive semiconductors, the Commerce and Justice departments said in a joint statement on Monday, an unusual public disclosure they said was in the public interest to correct inaccurate information. Commerce investigators requested Nvidia's records on direct and indirect sales to Megaspeed in May 2025, Nvidia placed the company on its no sell list the following month, and US agents working with Singapore law enforcement carried out an on-site end-use check at Megaspeed and interviewed its then chief executive, Alice Huang. The Justice Department then took the lead in a criminal investigation, the departments said they will bring the full extent of the law against Megaspeed for any criminal conduct that may have occurred, and Megaspeed has said it conducts legitimate and lawful business and has hired external counsel to run an internal investigation.
