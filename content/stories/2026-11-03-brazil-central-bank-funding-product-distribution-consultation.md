The Banco Central do Brasil has opened public consultation on a draft joint resolution setting criteria for the distribution of financial funding products by institutions it authorizes, with contributions accepted until 24 January 2027 under Consulta Pública 131/2026. The draft requires distributors to run a Client Best Interest Policy, prohibits minimum sales targets or quotas for specific products, provides for deferred remuneration with reversal of unpaid instalments, and requires Distribution Policies that bar exclusivity contracts and other practices restricting issuers' access to distribution channels. Suggestions from the consultation will feed a final draft to be considered by the central bank's Collegiate Board and the National Monetary Council.
