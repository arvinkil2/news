Euro area gross domestic product rose 0.4 percent in the third quarter of 2026 from the previous quarter, slowing from 0.6 percent growth in the second quarter, Eurostat said in its preliminary flash estimate, while EU output rose 0.5 percent after 0.7 percent. Year on year, euro area output was up 1.3 percent. Ireland recorded the fastest growth among the states reporting, up 5.5 percent, followed by Estonia, Spain and the Netherlands at 0.6 percent each; Germany grew 0.2 percent and Italy was flat, while Lithuania fell 1.0 percent and France contracted 0.1 percent, the only large economy to shrink.
