Spot gold fell below the 3,800 dollar support level to close at 3,724 dollars an ounce on Wednesday, down 118 dollars or 3 percent, and briefly dipped below 3,700 dollars on Thursday morning before recovering to about 3,736 dollars, according to YLG Bullion figures reported by the Bangkok Post. Analysts cited in the report pointed to expectations of further Federal Reserve tightening, with the October meeting minutes showing broad support for more rate increases and markets pricing a 95.7 percent probability of another quarter-point hike, while the dollar traded near a 19-month high. The same analysts warned bullion could fall toward 3,500 dollars an ounce if the conflict involving Iran escalates and oil prices keep climbing.
