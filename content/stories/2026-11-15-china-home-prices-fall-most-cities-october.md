China's housing prices fell in most surveyed cities in October. New home prices fell month on month in 49 of the 70 large and medium sized cities in the National Bureau of Statistics survey, rose in 14 and were unchanged in 7, and second hand prices fell in 58 cities. Against a year earlier, new home prices were lower in 63 cities and higher in only 7.

The moves were small. The largest monthly rise for new homes was Nanjing, up 0.5 percent, and the largest falls were 0.5 percent, recorded in Changchun, Haikou, Yangzhou and Beihai. The four largest cities were steadier: Shanghai new home prices rose 0.2 percent on the month and 2.7 percent on the year, Beijing fell 0.1 percent on the month, Guangzhou was flat and Shenzhen rose 0.1 percent, while second hand prices rose on the month in Shanghai, Guangzhou and Shenzhen.

The weakest readings were in smaller cities. Luzhou new home prices were down 7.1 percent on the year and its second hand prices down 8.2 percent, with Beihai, Luoyang, Haikou and Tangshan also down more than 5 percent on the year for new homes.
