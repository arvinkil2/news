**Fed discount rate minutes.** The Federal Reserve Board on Tuesday published minutes of its discount rate meetings of August 17, September 9 and September 16, 2026, the sessions at which the Board reviews and determines the discount rates charged through the discount window. The release notes that the process is distinct from the FOMC's setting of the federal funds target range. It is governance transparency rather than a policy signal, and no rate change was announced with it.

**Wunsch on the ECB.** Belgian central bank governor Pierre Wunsch addressed the ECB policy outlook in a Bloomberg video interview on Wednesday morning, ahead of the ECB's October 29 meeting, at which the deposit facility rate stands at 2.50 percent after the September hike. The item was available at headline level only in this poll, so no position is attributed to him here beyond the fact of the interview.

**Ghana fuel prices.** Petrol and diesel prices in Ghana are set to fall from October 16 while liquefied petroleum gas faces an 18.5 percent increase, MyJoyOnline reported on Wednesday. Administered and window based fuel pricing is a direct input to Ghanaian inflation, which the IMF on Tuesday projected at 4.8 percent by year end, below the Bank of Ghana's target band.

**UK and China finance channel.** HM Treasury published a readout of the Chancellor's October 8 call with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng. The call is part of the standing UK and China financial dialogue; no market moving commitment was reported in the readout.

**ECB research.** The ECB published two working papers on Wednesday, one on the heterogeneous bank lending channel of monetary policy and one on what analysts hear and why it matters for policy. Both are research inputs to the transmission debate rather than policy signals.

**RBA appointment and BoJ releases.** The Reserve Bank of Australia announced a chief risk officer appointment, a governance item with no policy content. The Bank of Japan published its October 10 accounts and its schedule of JGB holdings, routine statistical releases used as background for the money stock and auction stories in this edition.
