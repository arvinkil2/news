**Latest, October 28:** The terms are now set. PTT Global Chemical will own the remainder of the combined venture after SCG Chemicals, Siam Cement's subsidiary, takes 44 percent, which puts PTT Global Chemical's holding at 56 percent, Bloomberg reported, attributing the terms to the companies' exchange filings late on Wednesday. Both companies' boards have approved the deal, and shareholders of the two groups will consider it on December 3, 2026.

Siam Cement and PTT Global Chemical have agreed to combine their Thai olefins and polyolefins businesses in a venture valued at 187 billion baht ($5.5 billion), Bloomberg reported, as both groups seek to bolster petrochemical operations through a prolonged downturn. The agreement follows a study the two companies began under a memorandum of understanding in April 2026 and moves the combination from due diligence, where it stood at the end of September, to an agreed transaction. The $5.5 billion figure is the valuation of the businesses being combined, not a cash purchase price.

The scope set out in the companies' September filing to the Stock Exchange of Thailand covers PTT Global Chemical's Thai olefins and polyolefins assets, including its stake in HMC Polymers, and SCG Chemicals' Thai crackers and polyethylene and polypropylene plants, together with its stakes in four joint venture companies. That filing had said PTT Global Chemical was expected to become the major shareholder of the venture, with SCG Chemicals remaining a significant shareholder, which the agreed 56 to 44 split now confirms, and that the transaction would need a filing to the Trade Competition Commission of Thailand plus internal corporate approvals.
