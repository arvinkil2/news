Siam Cement and PTT Global Chemical have agreed to combine their Thai olefins and polyolefins businesses in a venture valued at 187 billion baht ($5.5 billion), Bloomberg reported, as both groups seek to bolster petrochemical operations through a prolonged downturn. The agreement follows a study the two companies began under a memorandum of understanding in April 2026 and moves the combination from due diligence, where it stood at the end of September, to an agreed transaction.

The scope set out in the companies' September filing to the Stock Exchange of Thailand covers PTT Global Chemical's Thai olefins and polyolefins assets, including its stake in HMC Polymers, and SCG Chemicals' Thai crackers and polyethylene and polypropylene plants, together with its stakes in four joint venture companies. That filing said PTT Global Chemical was expected to become the major shareholder of the venture, with SCG Chemicals remaining a significant shareholder, and that the transaction would need a filing to the Trade Competition Commission of Thailand plus internal corporate approvals.
