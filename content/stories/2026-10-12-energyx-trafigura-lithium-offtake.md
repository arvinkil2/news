Supply will come from EnergyX's two flagship projects, lithium carbonate from Black Giant in Chile and lithium hydroxide from Project Lonestar in Hooks, Texas, with a significant share directed to the US battery supply chain. Black Giant targets first commercial production in 2028 and Lonestar in 2029, following Eni's $225 million minority investment in Black Giant in July and a $690 million US EXIM Bank letter of intent for project debt.

The agreement is a financing milestone rather than production, since both projects remain pre commercial and depend on EnergyX's direct lithium extraction technology performing at scale. Trafigura said the offtake reflects its push to help bring strategic critical minerals projects online and diversify supply chains.
