NTT Global Data Centers and Thai power producer B.Grimm Power have signed a memorandum of understanding to pursue a 400MW data centre project in Thailand, Data Center Dynamics reported, citing B.Grimm's announcement. The project carries an investment value of approximately 113 billion baht, or $3.3 billion, and is described as providing digital infrastructure, an AI computing system and energy infrastructure. No site or timetable was shared.

NTT operates three data centres in Thailand around Bangkok, with a fourth, Bangkok 4, in development and due to go live in the second quarter of 2027. B.Grimm, one of Thailand's largest industrial power producers, has previously signed energy deals to power NTT facilities at its Chonburi campus.
