S&P Global Ratings on Friday revised Hungary's sovereign outlook to "stable" from "negative" and affirmed the country's rating at "BBB-/A-3". The agency said strong institutional and fiscal frameworks would ensure a steady decline in budgetary deficits over the next two years.

Bloomberg reported the move reflects planned steps by Prime Minister Peter Magyar's cabinet to cut the budget deficit and eventually adopt the euro. The S&P action follows a similar outlook lift by Moody's on November 20, which affirmed Hungary at Baa2.
