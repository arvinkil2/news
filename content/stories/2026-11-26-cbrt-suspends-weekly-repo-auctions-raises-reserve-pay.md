The Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye said on November 26 that it has decided to temporarily suspend the one-week repo auctions it conducts as part of its open market operations, giving no reason and no date for their return. In a second release the same day, the bank said that to support macrofinancial stability and the monetary transmission mechanism it has raised the remuneration rate applied to required reserves maintained for Turkish lira deposits from 86 percent to 90 percent of its weighted average funding cost.
