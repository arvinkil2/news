President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said US envoys at the trilateral meeting in Abu Dhabi asked what would happen if Ukraine joined NATO and whether Ukraine would then withdraw from Donbas, a formulation he said was raised for the first time. He repeated that Ukraine does not withdraw and does not cede its territory, but said he listened to the proposal, and that any such decision would need specific NATO and EU security guarantee details that are not yet available and the opinion of Ukrainian society, which he said he has no right to decide alone. He also rejected, in any circumstances, writing a neutral status for Ukraine into its constitution, as Moscow demands. US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held the Abu Dhabi meetings this week, then met President Vladimir Putin in Moscow before travelling to Dublin to meet Zelenskyy ahead of the European summit.
