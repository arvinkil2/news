The US Department of State is publishing the name of a person who has been added to the Department of the Treasury's List of Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons, administered by the Office of Foreign Assets Control, based on the Secretary of State's determination that the applicable criteria were satisfied. All property and interests in property subject to US jurisdiction of the designated person are blocked.

The designee is Banco Internacional de Comercio S.A., known as BICSA, of Oficina 506, Edificio Santa Clara, Centro de Negocios Miramar, Calle 3 entre 76 y 78, Miramar, Playa, Havana, Cuba. The notice records its SWIFT and BIC code as BIDCCUHH, its establishment date as October 23, 1993, and its target types as financial institution and, alternatively, state owned enterprise.

Acting under Executive Order 14404, the Secretary of State determined that BICSA met the order's criteria, and the bank was added to the SDN List as of October 15, 2026. It was designated under section 2(a)(i)(A) of the order for operating in or having operated in the financial services sector of the Cuban economy. The notice, Public Notice 13172, was signed by Hugo Y. Yon, Senior Bureau Official in the Bureau of Economic, Energy, and Business Affairs, and published in the Federal Register on November 24, 2026.

BICSA handles foreign trade and correspondent banking business for the Cuban state, so the practical effect of a blocking designation is to bar US persons from dealing with it and to expose foreign banks that use the US financial system to secondary compliance risk on any BICSA business they touch. The notice states no waiver, licence or wind down period. Status: enacted (designation in force from October 15, 2026; formally published November 24, 2026).
