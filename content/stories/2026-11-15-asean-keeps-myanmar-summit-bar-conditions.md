ASEAN leaders adopted their review of the Five-Point Consensus in Manila on Sunday, reiterating deep concern over insufficient progress in implementing the 2021 agreement and denouncing violence against civilians in Myanmar. The decision upholds Myanmar's non-political representation at ASEAN summits and foreign ministers' meetings until there is meaningful progress, while leaving participation in sectoral ministerial meetings unrestricted at all levels.

In a shift in framing, the leaders agreed to pursue a calibrated, phased and progress-based engagement, under which ASEAN may consider gradually restoring Myanmar's full political representation if progress is sustained and concrete. The statement noted the conclusion of the three phases of Myanmar's general elections, and recorded that an ASEAN monitoring mission on October 28 began a month of humanitarian deliveries to more than 11,000 households, including in four priority townships the AHA Centre had not previously reached.
