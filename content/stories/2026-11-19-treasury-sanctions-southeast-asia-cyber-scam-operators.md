The US Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) on Thursday sanctioned three individuals involved in operating and facilitating cyber scam centers in Southeast Asia, as well as two affiliated companies, in an action taken under Executive Order 13694, as amended, and in furtherance of Executive Order 14390 of March 6, 2026 on combating cybercrime and fraud against American citizens. OFAC said Zhao Hui, a Cambodian national, leased a scam center owned by the designated Prince Group TCO, paid protection fees to its leader Chen Zhi, and owned and operated a second scam compound in Cambodia that primarily targeted Americans and Europeans with virtual currency investment scams; his Phnom Penh companies Bei Fang Bo Lin Construction Decoration Co., Ltd. and ZHZ Investment Consulting Co., Ltd. were also designated. OFAC also designated Saw Shwe Wah and Saw A One, commanders of the Democratic Karen Benevolent Army, the Burmese armed group it says provides security for scam compounds along the Thai-Burmese border and participates in violence against trafficking victims held inside them. As a result of the action, the designated persons' property in the United States or in the possession of US persons is blocked and must be reported to OFAC.
