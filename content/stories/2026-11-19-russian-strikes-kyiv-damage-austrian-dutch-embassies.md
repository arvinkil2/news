A wave of Russian strikes hit Kyiv through the day on Thursday, killing one person and injuring at least four others, Prime Minister Sergiy Koretsky said, in an attack that damaged the embassies of Austria and the Netherlands and shattered a large window at St. Volodymyr's Cathedral. The Austrian embassy's charge d'affaires, Daniel Roethlin, said an explosion at about 4:30 p.m. local time damaged windows and vehicles while about 10 people were inside, with no injuries, and Dutch Foreign Minister Tom Berendsen said the Dutch embassy sustained minor damage and its staff were safe. Ukraine's foreign minister, Andriy Sybiha, said debris also fell on the grounds of the foreign ministry and the Brazilian embassy.
