ARRAY Technologies and Shoals Technologies Group said on Wednesday they have entered a definitive agreement to combine in a stock-for-stock merger, with ARRAY as the legal acquirer and Shoals shareholders receiving a fixed 1.6931 ARRAY shares for each Shoals share, valuing Shoals at about $1.32 billion, or $7.86 a share at announcement prices. Shoals shareholders are expected to own about 65 percent of the combined company on closing, and Shoals chief executive Brandon Moss will lead it, with ARRAY chief executive Kevin Hostetler moving to a special adviser role.

The combined company is targeting preliminary 2027 pro forma revenue of $2.2 billion to $2.4 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $410 million to $480 million, including about $35 million of first-year synergies, and ARRAY has committed to redeem $317.5 million of its Series A perpetual preferred stock in stages after closing. The boards of both companies have approved the deal, which is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027 subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.

The 8-K filed later in the morning fills in the structure around those headline terms. Brad Forth, who chairs both boards and did not vote on the transaction at either one, will chair the combined company, whose 10-member board will take four independent directors from each side plus Forth and Moss. Corporate headquarters will be in Albuquerque, New Mexico, with corporate offices in Chandler, Arizona, and the companies intend to announce a new corporate name and ticker symbol before closing. The presentation puts combined backlog and awarded orders at $3.4 billion as of September 30, 2026, targets more than $100 million of annual synergy benefit within three years, and estimates net leverage of 2.6 times at the end of 2026 falling to below 2 times by the end of 2027. The companies describe the 2027 ranges as preliminary and indicative, not projections.

The preferred stock commitment runs in two parts: $161.3 million redeemed at 93 percent of liquidation preference within 90 days of closing, then a further $156.3 million at 96 percent in installments of at least $52 million on each of the first three anniversaries. Termination fees are asymmetric, $40.3 million payable by ARRAY to Shoals and $22.2 million by Shoals to ARRAY in the specified recommendation-change and competing-proposal circumstances, and the outside date of May 4, 2027 extends automatically to November 4, 2027 if only antitrust and Nasdaq listing conditions remain open at that point.
