ARRAY Technologies and Shoals Technologies Group said on Wednesday they have entered a definitive agreement to combine in a stock-for-stock merger, with ARRAY as the legal acquirer and Shoals shareholders receiving a fixed 1.6931 ARRAY shares for each Shoals share, valuing Shoals at about $1.32 billion, or $7.86 a share at announcement prices. Shoals shareholders are expected to own about 65 percent of the combined company on closing, and Shoals chief executive Brandon Moss will lead it, with ARRAY chief executive Kevin Hostetler moving to a special adviser role.

The combined company is targeting preliminary 2027 pro forma revenue of $2.2 billion to $2.4 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $410 million to $480 million, including about $35 million of first-year synergies, and ARRAY has committed to redeem $317.5 million of its Series A perpetual preferred stock in stages after closing. The boards of both companies have approved the deal, which is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027 subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.
