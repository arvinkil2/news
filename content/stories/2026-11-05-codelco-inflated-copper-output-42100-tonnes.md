Chile's state owned copper producer Codelco has identified 42,100 tonnes of artificially inflated production in its reporting, board chairman Bernardo Fontaine told an extraordinary shareholders meeting on Thursday, after probes found work in process materials had been misclassified as finished output to meet targets. Codelco has ordered audits and dismissed nine employees across three mines and its corporate offices, and Fontaine said a separate irregularity affected 17,000 tonnes of copper inventory valued at between $120 million and $140 million.

Fontaine said the company expects copper production in 2026 to be closer to 1.2 million tonnes, with annual output of around 1.3 million tonnes projected over the following four years, and that a recovery plan will tighten internal controls and accountability.
