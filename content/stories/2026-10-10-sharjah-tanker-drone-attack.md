The Panama-flagged crude oil tanker MT GEM No.2 became the target of a drone attack at the Sharjah anchorage in the Arabian Gulf, with all 22 Indian crew members reported safe. India's government said on X that it is in contact with the ship's management company; no attacker has been named and no damage or casualties have been reported.

The incident lands as attacks on ships in the Gulf and Black Sea have multiplied in recent weeks. India recently co-founded a Group of Friends on Safety and Security of Shipping and Seafarers with 25 countries, co-chaired by External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar, which held its first meeting in New York.
