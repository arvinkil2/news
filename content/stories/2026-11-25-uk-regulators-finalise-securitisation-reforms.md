The Prudential Regulation Authority and the Financial Conduct Authority on Wednesday published revised securitisation rules designed to make their requirements more proportionate and less prescriptive. The changes simplify due diligence and transparency requirements, introduce more flexible risk retention options while keeping at least 5 percent of a securitisation's value at risk, and allow some simple and prudent re-securitisations while the wider ban stays in place.

The regulators estimate the reforms will cut industry costs by around 168 million pounds over the next decade. The PRA has published final rules; the FCA's rules are near-final, pending the related statutory instrument from HM Treasury, and the FCA does not expect significant changes before it makes them.
