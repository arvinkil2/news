Eldorado Gold says four tailings filter presses are now operational at its Skouries copper and gold mine in northern Greece, marking another milestone in the commissioning and ramp-up of the operation. President and chief executive Christian Milau said the additional filtration capacity positions the mine to advance throughput and build momentum across the operation.

Four operating filter presses are required to support commercial production on sulphide ore, and five are required to reach nameplate throughput. The filtration facility comprises six Metso presses in total, five operating units and one installed spare, so maintenance can be carried out without losing throughput. The remaining two presses are expected to be operational in early December.

Eldorado remains focused on progressing the ramp-up toward commercial production, which it expects late this quarter. Skouries is expected to produce on average 140,000 ounces of gold and 67 million pounds of copper a year over its life of mine.

The update extends the commissioning sequence reported last month, when the first presses entered service. With four of six units now running, the filtration constraint on the ramp is close to cleared, and the December completion of the last two units is the next dated checkpoint.
