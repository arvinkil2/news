Australia's seasonally adjusted unemployment rate rose to 4.9 per cent in October, the highest since October 2021, the Australian Bureau of Statistics said. Employment fell by 44,000 people while the number of unemployed rose by 18,000, the employment-to-population ratio fell 0.3 percentage points to 63.3 per cent, and the participation rate fell 0.2 percentage points to 66.6 per cent. In trend terms employment was steady and the trend unemployment rate rose to 4.8 per cent, while hours worked fell 0.2 per cent and underemployment eased 0.1 percentage points to 6.2 per cent.
