Nocera has signed a binding letter of intent to invest 22 million dollars for a 44 percent stake in BitRock, an Alberta developer of self-powered AI data centers planning an initial 10 megawatt build pairing compute with behind-the-meter generation. The investment is not yet a completed deal: it depends on due diligence, a definitive agreement and financing Nocera has not yet secured. (Dealroom News, November 27.)

Rendezvous Robotics, a Colorado startup building modular spacecraft tiles that assemble themselves in orbit, closed a 20 million dollar seed round in the week to November 28, the largest seed in Fundz's weekly tally of US venture rounds after Sierra's Series F. (Fundz, November 28.)

PhotonicX AI, a Chinese optical communications startup, raised 21.8 million dollars in a Series A round at a post-money valuation of 266 million dollars, with Yangtze Optical Fibre and Cable and Shenzhen Capital Group among the backers, according to November deal disclosures. The company builds optical interconnect hardware for moving data between GPUs inside AI data centers, the same bottleneck Avicena's larger round addresses. (AI News Todays, November 28, single source.)

Analysts at IO Fund estimate OpenAI's six-year, 457 megawatt capacity agreement with TensorWave, announced November 16 and powered by AMD Instinct MI455X systems, could represent 4.6 billion to 9.1 billion dollars of AMD hardware. Neither OpenAI nor TensorWave has disclosed the contract's value, and the estimate should be read as an analyst's model, not a reported figure. First capacity is expected in the first half of 2027. (Stocktwits News, November 27.)
