Microsoft will enable access to Anthropic's Claude models by default for UK Copilot tenants from December 9, The Register reported, and administrators who leave the setting untouched will be opted in, though they can disable it. Anthropic acts as a Microsoft subprocessor and processes the relevant data in the United States, according to Microsoft's documentation, and Claude is already on by default for most commercial cloud customers outside the European Union, the European Free Trade Association and the UK. UK tenants are not covered by Microsoft's EU Data Boundary, which is why the switch is being flipped for them, and Microsoft is also retiring the setting that restricts Claude access to Microsoft 365 alone.
