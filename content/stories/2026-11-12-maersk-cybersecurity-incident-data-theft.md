Maersk said in a statement published on November 11 that it had identified an IT security incident in which an attacker gained unauthorised access to one of its local legacy systems and extracted data. The company said a full investigation was launched immediately and is ongoing, that findings so far strongly suggest no sensitive data was disclosed, and that customers can keep using its platform for bookings and managing shipments.

A ransomware group calling itself WhiteArmor has claimed responsibility and threatened to release about 100 GB of data, according to the report. Maersk did not confirm those claims and did not link the incident to the group or to its LF Logistics platform by name.
