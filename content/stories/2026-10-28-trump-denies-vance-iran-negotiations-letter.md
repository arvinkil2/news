President Donald Trump denied that he instructed Vice President J.D. Vance to establish contact with Iran to start peace negotiations, rejecting a Wall Street Journal report that Vance had, on his instruction, written to Iranian security chief Mohsen Rezaei proposing that talks resume, RIA Novosti reported via Lenta.ru. Asked about the report, Trump said no, and that Iran needs help with medical supplies, mainly supplies to help people who are sick.

Bloomberg reported earlier on Wednesday, in a headline in this site's feed pool, that Trump said Vance reached out to Iran to offer medical supplies, which matches the explanation Trump gave for the contact, and oil declined during the day as traders parsed signs of fresh diplomacy on Iran.
