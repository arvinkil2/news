**Latest.** The market verdict has since landed. Baidu's Hong Kong shares fell more than 2.4 percent on Wednesday to a record low of HK$69.2, after falling nearly 6 percent in the previous session following the results, Investing.com reported. The miss was on the bottom line: adjusted earnings of RMB 6.78 per ADS came in against an analyst consensus of RMB 8.16, and the report said investors remain concerned that heavy investment in AI infrastructure will take time to turn into earnings growth while the advertising business deteriorates.

Baidu reported third quarter revenue of RMB 30.8 billion ($4.60 billion), down 1 percent year over year, as AI Cloud Infra revenue rose 94 percent to RMB 8.2 billion and GPU cloud revenue rose 191 percent, offsetting most of a 22 percent fall in online marketing revenue to RMB 11.9 billion. Baidu General Business revenue returned to year over year growth, rising 1 percent to RMB 25.0 billion, with AI-powered businesses contributing RMB 13.2 billion, or 53 percent of that total, while iQIYI revenue fell 10 percent to RMB 6.0 billion. Non-GAAP net income was RMB 2.4 billion, or RMB 6.78 ($1.01) per ADS, operating cash flow was RMB 2.8 billion, and the company said it still expects to declare its first dividend before the end of 2026.
