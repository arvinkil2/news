Turkey will take some of the diesel Russia is supplying to global markets under its agreement with President Donald Trump, Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar told broadcaster Kanal 7 on Sunday, calling the deal important for Turkey's energy security and giving no volumes. In Moscow, Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said commercial terms were still being negotiated and that, once contracts are signed, Russian diesel would reach United States markets within 15 to 20 days, without setting a signing date. The two statements put the October 9 deal's next step in contract talks, not in cargoes already moving.
