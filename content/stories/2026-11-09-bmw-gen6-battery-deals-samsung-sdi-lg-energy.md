BMW Group said on Monday that it has signed new battery cell partnerships with Samsung SDI in Europe and LG Energy Solution in the United States, expanding the supplier portfolio for its sixth generation high voltage batteries under its local for local purchasing principle. Samsung SDI will supply cells from its European facilities and LG Energy Solution from its US plants, with deliveries due to begin in the late 2020s, according to Yonhap's report of the announcement.

Purchasing board member Nicolai Martin said the partnerships are actively strengthening value creation in Europe and the US, part of a globally diversified supplier network meant to raise supply security and shorten transport distances. BMW did not disclose supply volumes, contract values or the split between suppliers.
