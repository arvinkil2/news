Energy Secretary Chris Wright announced Saturday that the Energy Department is moving quickly with an emergency exchange of up to 4 million barrels of SPR crude to keep refineries running and limit fuel supply disruptions from Hurricane Isaias, saying the aim is to keep American households and businesses supplied with affordable and reliable energy. The move comes as US crude and distillate stocks fell and gasoline stocks rose in the week to October 2, with distillates roughly flat at 105.1 million barrels and refinery utilization at 92.7 percent.
