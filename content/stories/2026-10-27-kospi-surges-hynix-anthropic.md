South Korea's Kospi rose 3.29 percent to 5,823.85 as of 9:16 a.m. on Wednesday in Seoul, extending an opening gain of 2.23 percent, while the Kosdaq rose 3.24 percent to 767.93. Samsung Electronics rose 4.73 percent and SK Hynix rose 3.13 percent after Hynix reported record third quarter operating profit of 83.33 trillion won, 9.3 percent above the Yonhap Infomax compiled consensus, alongside an additional 60 trillion won shareholder return plan. Yonhap attributed the advance to the Hynix results and to revived expectations for AI investment after Anthropic's push toward an initial public offering lifted all three major New York indices overnight, with foreign investors net buyers of Kospi shares for a second straight day.
