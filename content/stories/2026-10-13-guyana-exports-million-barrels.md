The December schedule works out to about 1.06 million barrels a day and includes the first cargoes of the new Uaru grade, due to load on December 11 to 12 from ExxonMobil's fifth Stabroek development. Guyana reached 900,000 barrels a day of production in November 2025 and passed one billion cumulative barrels in September, and the IMF says its economy grew more than 19 percent in 2025 on the back of the boom. Two further projects, Whiptail in late 2027 and Hammerhead in 2029, would lift installed capacity toward 1.3 million barrels a day and beyond.
