Morocco is positioning itself as a potential uranium supplier to France. Le Monde reported that the state owned OCP Group is in talks with French groups EDF and Orano on producing and supplying uranium concentrate recovered from Moroccan phosphate, with a formal cooperation protocol possibly announced during King Mohammed VI's planned state visit to France in January 2027. Orano has restated its commitment to diversifying uranium supply sources. The item is talks only, single origin reporting, and no agreement, volume or price has been disclosed.

SYMX.AI, the Sudbury, Ontario industrial AI company formerly known as Symboticware, has raised 6 million US dollars in a Series A led by Ascent Capital Partners, alongside a global technology and commercial partnership with Yokohama Off-Highway Tires. The company's platform covers fleet dispatch, equipment health, tyre life management and fuel optimisation for mining, construction and forestry fleets, and has been demonstrated at Glencore's Onaping Depth project in Ontario. The financing was announced on October 26, 2026 and recirculated by mining trade press on November 13.

In Liberia, the Ministry of Mines and Energy has been accused of aiding illicit mining, according to allAfrica reporting of November 13. The accusation is single sourced and the ministry's response was not in the reporting available this weekend, so it is recorded here as an allegation to watch rather than a finding.

In Nigeria, the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission has secured convictions of two people in an illegal mining case, part of a wider enforcement pattern across West Africa this month that also includes Guinea's artisanal gold crackdown, covered in full on November 13. No production or company exposure was attached to the Nigerian convictions in the reporting available.
