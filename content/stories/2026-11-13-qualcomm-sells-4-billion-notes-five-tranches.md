Qualcomm has sold 4 billion dollars of senior unsecured notes in five tranches, the chipmaker said in its Form 8-K: 750 million dollars each of 5.000 percent notes due 2028, 5.150 percent notes due 2029, 5.600 percent notes due 2033 and 5.800 percent notes due 2036, and 1 billion dollars of 5.350 percent notes due 2031. The company entered the underwriting agreement on November 9, 2026 with BNP Paribas Securities, BofA Securities, Deutsche Bank Securities, J.P. Morgan Securities and Morgan Stanley as representatives of the underwriters, and executed the officers' certificate establishing the notes on November 12, 2026. Interest on each series is payable on May 20 and November 20 of each year, beginning May 20, 2027.
