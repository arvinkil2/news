Treaty Oak Clean Energy has closed $430 million in tax equity commitments from RBC Community Investments for two solar projects in Louisiana: the 185 MWac Beekman Solar Project in Morehouse Parish and the 200 MWac Hollis Creek Solar Project in Sabine Parish, together 385 MWac. The Beekman commitment closed in September 2026 and Hollis Creek in October; Treaty Oak announced the package on November 19.

The tax equity adds to $809 million in construction financing Treaty Oak announced in January 2026, bringing combined disclosed construction financing and tax equity commitments for the two projects to $1.239 billion. Construction began in 2025, Beekman has supported about 370 peak construction jobs and Hollis Creek is expected to support a further 400 to 500. Both projects are due to reach commercial operation in the first half of 2027, generating enough electricity for approximately 80,000 homes and, by the company's estimate, more than $100 million in state and local tax revenue over their operating lives.

The sequencing is the point. The debt arrived in January, construction ran through the year, and the tax equity closed last, in the autumn, which concentrates the monetisation of investment tax credits and depreciation with a single large bank investor at the point where project risk is lowest.
