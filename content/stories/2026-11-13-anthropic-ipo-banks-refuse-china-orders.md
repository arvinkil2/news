Banks handling Anthropic's initial public offering have refused to accept orders from institutional investors in mainland China and Hong Kong, citing potential restrictions under the US International Traffic in Arms Regulations, according to Bloomberg, citing people familiar with the matter. The reported restrictions are not explicitly detailed in Anthropic's IPO prospectus, and some investors are exploring offshore structures to get into the deal, including a family office linked to a Chinese technology founder attempting to place an order through a British Virgin Islands trust, with others considering special purpose vehicles and swap arrangements.

The reported exclusion mirrors SpaceX's recent offering, where Chinese and Hong Kong investors were kept out of the initial allocation but could buy shares once trading began. Anthropic and its existing shareholders are seeking to raise as much as 90.4 billion dollars through the IPO and a concurrent private placement, with pricing scheduled for November 18 and trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT from November 19.
