Nigeria's 2027 campaign is acquiring its machinery. President Bola Tinubu has appointed Festus Keyamo to head the All Progressives Congress directorate of air mobilisation for his re-election campaign, ThisDay reported, days after the formal campaign launch in Kaduna covered on this site. Separately, BusinessDay reported that a claim of billions in bribes has sparked a fresh storm over the re-election campaign. That allegation is single-sourced in the reporting available and is carried here as an allegation only, with no response from the campaign in the item read.

In Kenya, President William Ruto has dismissed claims that his government plans to ban the livestreaming of election results, calling them propaganda, AllAfrica reported. The denial matters because results transmission has been the contested point of Kenya's last two elections, and a denial on the record now is the baseline against which any later electoral commission rule will be read.

Mexico's electoral chief, Guadalupe Taddei, files her resignation with the Chamber of Deputies today, Monday, the step she announced on Sunday as the federal auditor investigates alleged improper use of public resources at the National Electoral Institute. The developing story on this site, published Sunday night, carries the full account; today is the filing date she named, and the institute's succession process is the next step to watch.

The United Nations is warning that community fractures in Kosovo are worrying the organisation, UN News reported in French. The item is a Security Council cycle warning rather than a new incident, but it keeps Kosovo on the board in the same week Serbia's election has changed the government in Belgrade, whose Kosovo policy the incoming Student List has promised to review.

In Sudan, survivors of the RSF invasion of El Fasher face famine, displacement and a bitter wait for justice, AllAfrica reported, one year after the city fell. The account is the human record behind today's lead Sudan item on this site, the UK sanctions package against 15 commanders from both sides, which was built on investigator reporting about the same siege.

Japan's Ministry of Finance has announced 5 year climate transition government bonds to be issued in November 2026. The announcement is a primary document in the rates beat's pool and is carried there; it is noted here only because the instrument is a policy choice by the government as much as a funding operation.
