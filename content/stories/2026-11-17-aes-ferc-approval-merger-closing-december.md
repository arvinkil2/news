The AES Corporation said on Tuesday that the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission had issued an order approving its merger with Horizon Merger Sub, a subsidiary of Horizon Parent, L.P., and that the order was a condition to closing. AES said its stockholders had previously approved the merger and that it had previously received all other regulatory approvals and consents required, making the FERC order the final regulatory approval.

At closing, each outstanding share of AES common stock will be converted into the right to receive $15.00 in cash, without interest, and the shares will cease to be listed on the New York Stock Exchange. The company expects the merger to close on or about December 2, 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the remaining closing conditions, and noted there can be no assurance the closing will occur on the expected timeline.
