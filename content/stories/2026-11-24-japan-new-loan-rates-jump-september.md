The average contract interest rate on new loans and discounts at Japan's domestically licensed banks rose to 2.120 percent in September, up from 1.782 percent in August, the Bank of Japan said. Short-term rates rose to 1.846 percent from 1.427 percent, and long-term rates rose to 2.228 percent from 1.999 percent.

City banks led the increase, with their average rate on new loans rising to 2.291 percent from 1.882 percent. The average rate on the outstanding stock of loans rose more gradually, to 1.583 percent from 1.510 percent.
