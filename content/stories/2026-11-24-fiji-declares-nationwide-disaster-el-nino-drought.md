Fiji has declared a nationwide state of disaster as an El Nino-driven drought pushed water shortages to crisis levels. Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka announced the declaration on November 18, after the Cabinet approved it on the recommendation of the National Disaster Risk Management Council; it was made under Section 91 of the National Disaster Risk Management Act and can remain in force for up to 30 days.

Fiji's Meteorological and Hydrological Services reported that all monitoring stations recorded less than half their usual October rainfall, with 26 of 27 stations well below normal and Nadi, Yasawa, Viwa and Rotuma recording their lowest October totals on record. The government has allocated 20 million Fijian dollars from existing ministerial funds to strengthen emergency water supplies and support affected households, farmers and livestock owners, and more than 2 million litres of emergency water have been delivered to maritime communities over the past month.
