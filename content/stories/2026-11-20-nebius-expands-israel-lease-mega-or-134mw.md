Nebius has signed a contract to expand its data centre lease with Israel's Mega Or by 134MW, taking more capacity at a facility under construction in Beit Shemesh and a second planned site in Masmiya, in what is the third capacity agreement between the two companies. The expansion is expected to generate net operating income of NIS 550 million (about $165 million) for Mega Or, on top of the NIS 740 million (about $222 million) already signed, and the latest contract runs for seven years with a five-year extension option. Both facilities are set for completion by the second quarter of 2028; the Beit Shemesh campus will have a maximum capacity of 135MW, of which Nebius will take about half, and its first 58MW phase is already leased to Nebius under the January 2026 contract.
