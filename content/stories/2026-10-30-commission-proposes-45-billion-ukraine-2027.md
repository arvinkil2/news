The European Commission has proposed that the Council approve the remaining 45 billion euros of the 90 billion euro Ukraine Support Loan for 2027, following its positive assessment of the financing strategy Ukraine submitted on October 26. The 2027 allocation would provide up to 13.3 billion euros of budget support, split equally between the Ukraine Facility and macro-financial assistance, and 31.7 billion euros for Ukraine's defence industrial capacity, with the budget support tied to conditions on the rule of law, the fight against corruption and economic resilience. The proposal now goes to the Council for adoption, and the Commission says disbursements could then begin in early 2027.
