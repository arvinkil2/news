Netflix is cutting about 6 percent of its workforce, roughly 1,000 of about 16,000 full time staff, in its biggest layoff round since 2022, according to a Wednesday morning email from co-chief executives Greg Peters and Ted Sarandos reported by the Wall Street Journal and carried by Reuters. The bulk of the cuts fall in product, technology, legal and human resources, and Netflix did not immediately respond to a request for comment. The company has been spending on advertising, live programming and gaming after losing the contest for Warner Bros Discovery's studio and streaming assets to Paramount Skydance earlier this year.
