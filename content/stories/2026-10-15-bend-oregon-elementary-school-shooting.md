Latest: At a news conference on Friday, Bend police identified the teacher killed in the attack as Meghan Lambert, 25, and named the youngest victim as 5-year-old Rhoam Summer, Fox News reported. The man police said neutralised the shooter was identified as Kaylub Gianmarco, 36, a local carpenter whose two daughters attend Silver Rail, a law enforcement source confirmed to Fox News; his family said he intervened and shot the gunman, and Lambert's family thanked him in a statement read at the news conference.

**Earlier (11:45 p.m. ET Thursday):** Euronews, paraphrasing a Bend police spokesperson, reports the gunman was 16 years old, forced his way in by shooting at the school's locked doors, and was shot by a person at the scene who had pursued him into the school; he was found dead inside minutes after the shooting was reported. The three children killed were aged 5, 8 and 11, and the two people taken to hospital, a 7-year-old child and a 54-year-old teacher, are in critical condition.

Four people were killed at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday afternoon: three children and one adult, according to CBS News and CNN, both citing law enforcement sources, in reporting carried by AFP. The suspected shooter was also shot and killed inside the school.

Bend police said officers responded just before 1 p.m. local time after a first call reported that a person had fired into a car, and that within minutes further callers reported a person had entered the school and started shooting. Moments after officers arrived, police learned the suspected shooter had been shot and killed inside the school; officials initially said it was unclear whether he was shot by someone at the school or by a police officer, and a police spokesperson told CNN it may have been a bystander. The later Euronews account, above, attributes the shot to a person at the scene who pursued the gunman.

At least two people were taken to a hospital, and police said children were among the hurt without confirming whether they were the people hospitalised. The school was locked down and cleared room by room, and families were directed to a reunification site at Journey Church in Bend. Bend-La Pine Schools Superintendent Steven Cook said the district was "stunned and heartbroken by this senseless tragedy," and FBI Director Kash Patel said FBI Portland agents were on the scene assisting local police.

Investigators have not released the shooter's name or a motive, and no information about the weapon has been made public.
