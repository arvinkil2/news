Foreign Secretary Ma. Theresa Lazaro summoned Ambassador Jing Quan on Monday and told him his comments to media last week were provocative and unacceptable, the Department of Foreign Affairs said in a statement. Jing had told reporters that China may easily win in a confrontation with the Philippines but would not use force because it loves peace, responding to Defence Secretary Gilberto Teodoro describing China as a regional troublemaker. Lazaro told Jing the comments aggravated an already sensitive atmosphere between the two countries and that sensitive issues should go through diplomatic channels, and the department said it reserves the right to take further diplomatic action.
