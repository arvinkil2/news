FIFA confirmed on Thursday that it had been the victim of a criminal cyberattack resulting in unauthorised access to what it called a limited set of personal and business information, and said it is working closely with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement. FIFA did not say when the attack took place, how many people or organisations were affected, or what the business information included. A group calling itself the Handsome Boy Hacking Club posted a screenshot of claimed FIFA files last week and later appeared to claim more than 12 gigabytes of leaked documents; a person with direct knowledge of the matter told Reuters the affected data was less than 0.1 percent of what FIFA holds and that no files from the abandoned FIFA Forward Enterprise project were accessed.
