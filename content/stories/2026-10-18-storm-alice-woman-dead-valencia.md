A woman was found dead in a garage in Paiporta on Sunday morning after torrential rain from Storm Alice flooded towns in Spain's Valencia region overnight, the Valencia regional government said. Local media reported that her body was found by firefighters who entered the garage to pump out water, and the first indications are that she had been trapped there since Saturday night. Paiporta was the locality worst affected by the catastrophic floods of October 2024 in Valencia province.

In Catalonia, the meteorological service recorded 100 millimetres of rain in the south of the region, the Tarragona area was under a red alert, and Civil Protection restricted movement in five counties and carried out rescue operations through the night. Three people were reported missing in Catalonia. The same storm system also brought flooding to southeastern France, where one person was rescued after being trapped by rising water.
