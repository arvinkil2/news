Ukraine's General Staff said that overnight into Saturday, October 24, Ukrainian forces hit an aircraft, preliminarily identified as a Su-34, at Yeysk airfield in Russia's Krasnodar region, where a fire was recorded, and struck two enterprises involved in missile production. In Orel, the target was the Proton plant, which the General Staff said develops and produces optoelectronics and components for the Tor-M2 and Pantsir-S1 air defence systems, the Iskander-M system and Kalibr and Kh-101 cruise missiles, and in Tolyatti, in the Samara region, it was Tolyattikauchuk, described as a producer of synthetic rubber and feedstock used in solid rocket fuel. The General Staff said the results and the scale of the damage are still being clarified.
