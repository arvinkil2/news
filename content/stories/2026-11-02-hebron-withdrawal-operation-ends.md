Israeli forces withdrew on Monday from the Hebron neighbourhood they had emptied on October 24, ending the operation launched after two Palestinian gunmen killed two soldiers at a checkpoint near the city on October 23. A military official said soldiers had searched multiple locations, detained and questioned suspects, confiscated four weapons and other equipment, demolished both attackers' homes and sealed an underground space next to one of them, and AFP journalists saw residents returning on Monday to homes that in some cases had been ransacked. The Hebron municipality put the number displaced by the operation at more than 3,000.
