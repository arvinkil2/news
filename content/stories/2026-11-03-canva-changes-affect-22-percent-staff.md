Canva will merge its Product, Growth and Platform teams into a single product organisation, simplify management layers and make Sydney the primary home for more product roles, chief executive Melanie Perkins told staff on Tuesday. The changes affect 1,271 people, 22 percent of staff: some will change roles, some will be asked to relocate to centralised hubs, and some will be laid off, with those leaving offered at least six months of salary, twelve months of healthcare coverage and career support.

Perkins said small changes would not be enough and that the company needs to return to small, empowered teams to set itself up for the next decade. From January, most hybrid employees will be expected in an office two to three days a week.
