**Musk regrets the midterm spend.** Elon Musk said he felt 'pretty foolish' about the scale of his midterm spending after the Republican rout, in remarks carried by Politico, The Hill and Al Jazeera. It is the first public accounting from the election cycle's most prominent donor, and it lands as both parties reassess what the spending bought.

**GAO questions detention initiative costs.** The Government Accountability Office said Immigration and Customs Enforcement should determine the cost effectiveness of new immigration detention initiatives before continuing them, in a report published Thursday. The recommendation puts detention costs on the congressional record ahead of the next appropriations fight.
