Super Micro Computer said preliminary revenue for its fiscal first quarter, ended September 30, will be $18.5 billion to $19.5 billion, up from its previous forecast of $14.5 billion to $15.5 billion and well above the $14.99 billion analyst consensus Investing.com cited, and it raised its gross margin outlook to 12.0 to 12.5 percent, from 10.4 to 10.8 percent, on a more favourable customer and product mix. Its shares rose 12 percent in after-hours trading on Tuesday. The raise follows a July warning that fourth-quarter revenue would come in near the low end of guidance, and the company is scheduled to discuss its results on an earnings call on November 3.
