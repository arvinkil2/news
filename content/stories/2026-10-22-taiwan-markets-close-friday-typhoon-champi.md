Taiwan will suspend trading on its stock exchange on Friday as Typhoon Champi approaches, the Taiwan Stock Exchange said, with settlements scheduled for Friday postponed to the next business day and offices and schools shut in Taipei, Hsinchu, Taichung, Tainan and Kaohsiung. As of 6 p.m. local time on Thursday, 126 domestic flights and 40 international and cross-strait flights had been cancelled, according to Taiwan's Civil Aviation Administration. Champi was 713 kilometres southwest of Okinawa with top sustained winds of 213 kilometres per hour, equivalent to a Category 4 hurricane, and is forecast to pass closest to northern Taiwan late Friday into Saturday, with extreme rain over greater Taipei that could bring flash floods and landslides.
