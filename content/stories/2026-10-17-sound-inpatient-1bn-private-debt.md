Sound Inpatient Physicians, the private equity backed hospital physician services group, is nearing a refinancing of about 1 billion dollars with its existing lenders in the private debt market, Bloomberg reported. The deal would refinance existing debt that a 2024 private credit transaction put in place.

The route matters. Barclays had previously withdrawn a near 1 billion dollar financing for Sound from the syndicated debt markets in July, one of the few leveraged deals pulled this year, after investor demand fell short. Returning to the incumbent private lenders is the fallback that pull made necessary.

Sound is one of the largest US physician services groups, supplying hospitalist and related clinicians to hospitals whose own staffing economics are under pressure. Its capital structure has been restructured before, in 2024, and a second refinancing with the same lender group signals forbearance priced as a new deal rather than a market clearing.

Terms of the private refinancing were not in the report checked. This story is single sourced to Bloomberg and is graded C on that basis.
