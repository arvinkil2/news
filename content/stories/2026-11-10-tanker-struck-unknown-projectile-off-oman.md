A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile 19 nautical miles northeast of Lima, Oman, on Tuesday, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency said, in a Reuters report carried by Arab News. The crew was reported safe and no environmental impact had been reported, and the vessel was to carry out a damage assessment at first light.

The report does not name the vessel, its flag or its cargo, and it identifies no attacker. The location sits on the Omani side of the Strait of Hormuz approaches, where a series of projectile strikes on tankers in recent weeks has kept the threat level for transiting ships elevated even as LNG traffic through the strait reached its highest level since February in October.
