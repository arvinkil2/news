Norway has detained the Chinese owned heavy-lift ship Ya Qing Hai Yang after the Norwegian Maritime Authority inspected it on October 13 in Andfjorden, off the Vesterålen Islands, where it had sought shelter from bad weather, and found it had no valid flag registration or the required certificates. The authority detained the vessel the following day and said it will remain in custody until the registration and certification problems are resolved. The ship, previously named Ocean 28, carried large power generation modules built by the Chinese company Wison to the Utrenny terminal serving Novatek's Arctic LNG 2 project in October 2024, and the United States sanctioned its operator in January 2025, with the European Union and other jurisdictions later adding the vessel to their lists.
