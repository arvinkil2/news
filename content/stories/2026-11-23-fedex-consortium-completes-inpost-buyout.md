A consortium of Advent, FedEx, A&R and PPF completed its acquisition of InPost on November 20, the companies said in a release carried on the FedEx Newsroom site. InPost is now wholly owned by the consortium and has delisted from Euronext Amsterdam, and it will continue to operate as an independent company under its existing brand, headquartered in Poland and led by founder Rafał Brzoska.

Following completion, Advent and FedEx each hold 37 percent, A&R holds 16 percent and PPF holds 10 percent. FreightWaves valued the buyout at $9.2 billion. InPost runs an out-of-home network of more than 100,000 locations, comprising more than 70,000 automated parcel lockers and close to 30,000 pick-up and drop-off points across nine European countries, and the consortium said it would accelerate investment in that network and in its technology platform.
