Endeavour Mining has suspended mining at all three active underground entrances at its Mana gold mine in Burkina Faso after part of an access road sank on October 28, restricting access to the Dangouna and Aviera entrances through the depleted Wona open pit. Workers were evacuated, nobody was injured, local authorities were notified, and specialists are assessing ground stability and repairs, with no cause or restart date disclosed.

Endeavour said it remains on track for full-year group production and all-in sustaining cost targets even if no further mining occurs at Mana in the fourth quarter, and it is considering restarting through the Wona entrance, which remains accessible, alongside nearby open pit feed. The company plans to set out its assessment and repair plan with third-quarter results on November 12.
