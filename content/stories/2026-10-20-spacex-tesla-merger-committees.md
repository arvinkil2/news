SpaceX and Tesla have each established independent special committees to explore a potential merger, Elon Musk confirmed during SpaceX's third-quarter earnings call on Tuesday, saying the independent directors are actively evaluating a transaction but negotiations are in the early stages with no guarantee of an agreement. Musk, who acknowledged he is a conflicted party in the talks, said any deal would require the support of 50 percent of all outstanding Tesla shares, which he described as quite a high hurdle to achieve, and no valuation, exchange ratio or timetable was given. Tesla shares rose in after-hours trading after the disclosure, which came alongside SpaceX results showing third-quarter revenue of $14.6 billion and net income of $4.4 billion.
