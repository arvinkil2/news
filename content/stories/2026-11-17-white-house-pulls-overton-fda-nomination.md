The White House said on Tuesday it has pulled the nomination of Dr. Heidi Overton to lead the Food and Drug Administration, amid growing indications she did not have the votes to win Senate confirmation, the Associated Press reported. Overton, a physician and White House domestic policy adviser nominated in August, faced bipartisan questions about her willingness to break from President Trump, particularly on his unsupported claims about the dangers of childhood vaccines, and Senate health committee chair Bill Cassidy said earlier in the day he remained undecided ahead of a committee vote scheduled for Wednesday.

Overton would have needed the votes of all 12 Republicans on the Senate Health, Education, Labor and Pensions Committee, where Democrats hold 11 seats. The FDA has been led on an acting basis by Kyle Diamantas since Dr. Marty Makary stepped down in May, and the withdrawal follows earlier pulled Trump health nominations, including his first CDC pick and two surgeon general nominees.
