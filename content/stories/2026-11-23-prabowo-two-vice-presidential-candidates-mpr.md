President Prabowo Subianto is expected to submit a letter to Indonesia's People's Consultative Assembly (MPR) on Monday naming two candidates for the vacant vice presidency, MPR Deputy Chair Sohibul Iman confirmed. The assembly held a joint leadership meeting on Monday morning and was due to receive the letter later in the day, according to Antara.

Iman told journalists to wait for the official notification from the MPR secretariat for the names. Under Indonesia's constitution, the assembly elects the vice president from the president's nominees when the office falls vacant mid-term.
