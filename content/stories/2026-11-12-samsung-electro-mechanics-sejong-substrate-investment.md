Samsung Electro-Mechanics announced a 4.3 trillion won investment in its Sejong plant on Thursday, at the Chungcheong Mega Project groundbreaking ceremony in Asan. The company will build a new four-storey factory near its existing plant in the Myeonghak Industrial Complex to expand production facilities for high-performance package substrates, the FC-BGA boards that connect AI server and high-performance computing chips to the mainboard.

Sejong City said the spend is the first stage of a total investment plan in the region of 8 trillion won that the company had been reviewing, following the Chungcheong advanced-industry investment plan announced in July. The company aims to shorten construction to meet global IT companies' demand for rapid mass production, targeting full-scale production around September 2028. Mayor Cho Sang-ho promised prompt administrative support; the article stated no subsidy figure and no jobs count.
