The Food and Drug Administration has extended its review of Belite Bio's new drug application for tinlarebant by three months. Belite announced on November 20 that the new Prescription Drug User Fee Act action date is May 12, 2027, replacing February 12, 2027. The company filed the announcement with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Form 6-K the same day.

The agency told Belite that, if no major deficiencies are identified during its ongoing review, it plans to communicate proposed labeling requests and any anticipated postmarketing requirements by April 12, 2027. Belite said the extension gives the FDA additional time to review information submitted in support of the application. An extension of this kind is a timing decision, not a rejection, and the filing records no deficiency finding.

Tinlarebant is an oral therapy intended to reduce the accumulation of vitamin A based toxins called bisretinoids, which cause retinal disease in Stargardt disease type 1. It works by lowering levels of retinol binding protein 4, the carrier that transports retinol from the liver to the eye. The drug has Breakthrough Therapy, Fast Track and Rare Pediatric Disease designations in the United States, and Belite says its Phase 3 DRAGON trial met its primary endpoint.

Secondary coverage reported Belite shares fell sharply on November 20 after the announcement. The investment question is now timing and cash runway to a May 2027 decision, rather than a new question about the trial data itself.
