Paladin Energy has launched a fully underwritten equity raising of A$525 million, made up of a A$474 million institutional placement on the ASX and a Canadian bought deal of C$50 million on the TSX, both at the equivalent of A$7.80 a share. The price is a 10.3 percent discount to the A$8.70 close on November 11, and eligible shareholders in Australia and New Zealand will be offered a further, non-underwritten share purchase plan of up to A$50 million at the same price.

The money funds the planning and execution of the 2027 and 2028 capital works program at the Patterson Lake South project in Saskatchewan's Athabasca Basin, a possible mine life extension and resource growth at Triple R, wider basin exploration, and repayment of existing debt. Managing director Paul Hemburrow said the raising keeps the project on momentum toward targeted first production in 2031. Completion of the bookbuilds is expected to be announced on November 13, with settlement on November 18.
