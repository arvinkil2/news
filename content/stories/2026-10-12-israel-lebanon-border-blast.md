The blast occurred near Mitzpe Be’er while a group of students and teachers was on an annual school trip, and Magen David Adom said it sent 14 ambulances and three rescue helicopters to the scene. Israeli media, citing preliminary findings, reported that someone may have touched the explosive object and possibly placed it in a bag before it detonated.

The outlet Walla reported that the military and police had previously declared the area cleared of explosives, and that the blast appeared to have been caused by an old Hezbollah rocket. A Lebanese military source told Arab News that Lebanon had no army presence near the site and was monitoring Israeli media reports, and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu interrupted a live broadcast following the incident.
