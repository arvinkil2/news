Nvidia's board has created a standing National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee to provide high-level oversight of the company's national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management, less than a week after Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive. The company's National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team will report directly to the committee and to interim chief executive Colette Kress, and board chair Harvey Jones said Nvidia has both a responsibility and an opportunity to lead the industry in addressing the national security challenges posed by advanced silicon technologies. The move comes as the chairs of three House committees have invited Huang to a closed-door briefing on Nvidia's security protocols against foreign adversary espionage, and President Trump has said the FBI is looking into Huang's relationship at the centre of the chip diversion allegations.
