Ethiopia told the UN Security Council on Friday that Eritrean troops had advanced as far as 60km into Tigray, calling it "a blatant act of aggression" that engages Ethiopia's right to self-defense under Article 51 of the UN Charter. Eritrea, in its own letter on Saturday, rejected the incursion claim as a pretext for an Addis Ababa offensive and urged the Council to condemn Ethiopia's "deplorable declaration of war."

The war of letters comes as Ethiopian government and allied forces advanced across Tigray, reportedly entering Adigrat, roughly 30km from the Eritrean border, on Saturday. Analysts told Al Jazeera the confrontation has moved beyond diplomacy into a high-risk phase, with the severing of diplomatic ties removing the last formal crisis-management mechanism between the former allies.
