Germany's Ministry of Defence has formally notified Boeing that it will not directly procure the company's MQ-28 Ghost Bat drone wingman, a Boeing spokesperson told Breaking Defense, as Berlin readjusts its collaborative combat aircraft plans. Germany had targeted 2029 to field wingman drones alongside its fighter jets, which would have made it the first European country to do so, but that timeline now looks to be in doubt.

The German ministry declined to comment on the specific case, citing procurement rules, and Boeing said it stands ready to support Berlin on next steps, with rival offers expected from Helsing and the Airbus-Kratos partnership among others.
