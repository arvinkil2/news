Demant lowered its medium to long-term organic growth expectation to 5 to 7 percent a year, from 6 to 8 percent, telling investors at its Capital Markets Day in Smørum, Denmark that the shift reflects Hearing Care, enlarged by the 2025 KIND Group acquisition, becoming its largest business area with growth more closely tied to the market. With acquisitions contributing an expected 1 to 2 percentage points a year, the group sees revenue growth in local currencies of 6 to 9 percent a year, and it committed to returning at least DKK 3 billion to shareholders through buybacks from the fourth quarter of 2026 to the end of 2027. For 2026 itself, Demant said results are now more likely to reach the upper end of its outlook ranges, organic growth of 7 to 8 percent and EBIT before special items of DKK 4,700 to 5,000 million, while for 2027 it expects the global hearing aid market to grow at the lower end of its 4 to 6 percent structural range, with US managed care sales declining again and no meaningful improvement in China.
