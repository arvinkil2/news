Real gross domestic product was essentially unchanged in August after rising 0.3 percent in July, a figure Statistics Canada revised up from the 0.2 percent first reported. Goods producing industries fell 0.2 percent even as mining, quarrying and oil and gas extraction expanded, and manufacturing was unchanged after a 4.4 percent drop in July that was its largest since April 2020. Services edged up 0.1 percent, with accommodation and food services up 1.1 percent and wholesale trade down 0.4 percent.

The public sector fell 0.3 percent, its third straight decline, as federal government public administration dropped 2.0 percent with the temporary 2026 Census staffing still unwinding. Statistics Canada's preliminary estimate has GDP up 0.2 percent in September, led by mining, quarrying and oil and gas extraction, manufacturing and retail trade.
