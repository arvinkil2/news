Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said on Thursday that four vessels it described as involved in illegal oil transfers in the northern Persian Gulf were simultaneously hit and caught fire after its forces tracked them on Wednesday, and that three more vessels collided with mines west of the Strait of Hormuz. The Guards also warned the US military that continued provocations could lead to a wider response against its regional bases.

No vessel was named in the statement and no independent confirmation was available overnight. The claim extends a pattern this site has tracked all week, in which the Guards announce mine and missile strikes on unnamed ships while maritime assessors, most recently the Joint Maritime Information Center on October 18, report no substantiation for the mine claims specifically.
