Latest: a further tanker has been struck by an unknown projectile in its engine room about six nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, while sailing outbound through the Strait of Hormuz, Arab News reported on Wednesday, citing a delayed report received by UK Maritime Trade Operations. UKMTO said the crew were safe and accounted for; the vessel is not named in the report, and the extent of any damage or environmental impact was not immediately known. The position and the crew outcome distinguish this strike from the November 17 attack on the ADNOC vessel about 13 nautical miles north of Khasab.

Earlier: maritime security firm Vanguard Tech has identified the attacked vessel as the Liberian-flagged product tanker Navig8 Messi, which is commercially managed by ADNOC L&S, gCaptain reported on Wednesday. A time-late UK Maritime Trade Operations report, based on information from local authorities, said a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile about 13 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman on November 17, that a fire aboard was subsequently extinguished, and that search and rescue efforts were continuing for one missing crew member, with the extent of damage and any environmental impact unknown. ADNOC has not confirmed the identification, and Vanguard noted the same vessel was among two ADNOC ships targeted by drones in the strait on August 13, with no injuries reported then.

ADNOC Logistics and Services confirmed on Wednesday that one of its vessels was attacked while transiting the Strait of Hormuz on the evening of November 17, and that one crew member remains unaccounted for, with search and rescue operations ongoing in coordination with the relevant authorities. All other crew members were safely evacuated from the vessel, and 11 sustained injuries and are receiving medical care where required, the company said, without naming the vessel. The company said its immediate priority is supporting the search and rescue effort and its crew, and it reiterated the importance of protecting seafarers and safeguarding freedom of navigation, a week after another of its vessels was damaged in an attack in UAE territorial waters on November 10.
