**Latest:** Premier Li Qiang, who oversees China's economic field, greeted Kim on site in Dandong, Yonhap reported, citing Yoon Kun-young, the ruling party secretary of the National Assembly Intelligence Committee. Kim was expected to finish his schedule and return home the same day, and a meeting with President Xi Jinping was assessed as unlikely, with Xi preparing for the Communist Party Central Committee's fifth plenum in Beijing on October 26 to 29. The visit is Kim's sixth to China and his first in one year and one month; on each of his previous five visits he held talks with Xi, while this trip was coordinated around the meeting with Li Qiang in Dandong, with its purpose read in Seoul as inspecting the newly opened bridge and expanding economic cooperation. Yoon said the visit could also carry a positive signal on a North Korea and United States summit, which President Donald Trump has been pushing to hold within the year, possibly around the APEC summit in Shenzhen on November 18 to 19, though Yonhap said the assessed likelihood of that meeting has recently fallen.

Yonhap's Korean report, citing a government source, said Kim arrived in Dandong via the New Yalu River Bridge, the four-lane crossing between Dandong and Sinuiju that officially opened on October 15, twelve years after its completion, while Yonhap's English report described Kim entering Dandong by special train on the route of his 2018 and 2019 China trips, with a possible meeting with President Xi Jinping and Korean War memorial events in Beijing. Yonhap noted the sudden trip comes ahead of the APEC meeting in Shenzhen, at a time when President Donald Trump is pushing to resume dialogue with North Korea within the year, and that the travel itself shows Kim in good health after recent rumors of his death, which South Korean intelligence had rejected on October 23.

Yonhap reported on Saturday that North Korean leader Kim Jong Un made a surprise trip to China, arriving in the border city of Dandong in Liaoning province on a visit neither Pyongyang nor Beijing announced in advance. Kyodo News, citing diplomatic sources, reported strict traffic controls in parts of Dandong on Saturday with hotel bookings in the area unavailable, measures it linked to a visit by a senior North Korean official ahead of October 25, the 76th anniversary of China's entry into the Korean War. Neither Chinese nor North Korean state media had confirmed Kim's travel as of Saturday.
