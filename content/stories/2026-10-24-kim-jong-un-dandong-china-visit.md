Yonhap reported on Saturday that North Korean leader Kim Jong Un made a surprise trip to China, arriving in the border city of Dandong in Liaoning province via the New Yalu River Bridge, the crossing between Dandong and Sinuiju that formally opened on October 15, twelve years after its completion. Kyodo News, citing diplomatic sources, reported strict traffic controls in parts of Dandong on Saturday with hotel bookings in the area unavailable, measures it linked to a visit by a senior North Korean official ahead of October 25, the 76th anniversary of China's entry into the Korean War. Neither Chinese nor North Korean state media had confirmed Kim's travel as of Saturday, and his onward destination and the purpose of the trip had not been reported.
