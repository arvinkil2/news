**Latest:** Yonhap's Korean report, citing a government source, said Kim arrived in Dandong via the New Yalu River Bridge, the four-lane crossing between Dandong and Sinuiju that officially opened on October 15, twelve years after its completion, while Yonhap's English report described Kim entering Dandong by special train on the route of his 2018 and 2019 China trips, with a possible meeting with President Xi Jinping and Korean War memorial events in Beijing. Yonhap noted the sudden trip comes ahead of the APEC meeting in Shenzhen, at a time when President Donald Trump is pushing to resume dialogue with North Korea within the year, and that the travel itself shows Kim in good health after recent rumors of his death, which South Korean intelligence had rejected on October 23.

Yonhap reported on Saturday that North Korean leader Kim Jong Un made a surprise trip to China, arriving in the border city of Dandong in Liaoning province on a visit neither Pyongyang nor Beijing announced in advance. Kyodo News, citing diplomatic sources, reported strict traffic controls in parts of Dandong on Saturday with hotel bookings in the area unavailable, measures it linked to a visit by a senior North Korean official ahead of October 25, the 76th anniversary of China's entry into the Korean War. Neither Chinese nor North Korean state media had confirmed Kim's travel as of Saturday.
