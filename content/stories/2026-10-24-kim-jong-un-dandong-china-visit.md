**Latest:** Yonhap's English report says Kim entered Dandong on Saturday using a special train, following the route of his earlier China trips in 2018 and 2019, on a visit neither Pyongyang nor Beijing announced in advance. The timing falls a day before the 76th anniversary of the Chinese People's Volunteers' entry into the Korean War, and Yonhap said Kim could attend related events at a memorial tower in Beijing and possibly meet President Xi Jinping, with South Korea's National Intelligence Service having earlier raised the possibility of a Kim trip to China around this time.

Yonhap reported on Saturday that North Korean leader Kim Jong Un made a surprise trip to China, arriving in the border city of Dandong in Liaoning province. Kyodo News, citing diplomatic sources, reported strict traffic controls in parts of Dandong on Saturday with hotel bookings in the area unavailable, measures it linked to a visit by a senior North Korean official ahead of October 25, the 76th anniversary of China's entry into the Korean War. Neither Chinese nor North Korean state media had confirmed Kim's travel as of Saturday.
