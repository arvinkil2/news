**Latest (Sunday):** the gunman was the family's patriarch. The 45-year-old man who killed eight people in Erie on Friday was a grandfather related to seven of the victims, Erie Police Chief Rick Lorah said; the eighth victim was a friend of the pregnant woman who was among the dead. Authorities have not released any of the names.

Julius Windham, whose 1-year-old daughter Amarianna was among the killed, said Sunday the gunman never seemed distressed or out of the ordinary: "I think that was a front for him." Windham stood at a memorial to the victims, including Amarianna, her pregnant mother, the woman's 5-year-old son and her unborn baby.

Investigators are reconstructing the sequence from video and audio evidence. Muffled gunshot sounds came from inside the home just before two "distressed" women arrived and began pounding on the door, Lorah said; the gunman then came out and shot them as they ran toward him before returning inside. Responding officers found the two women dead outside, then deployed a drone and robot and went in to find the bodies of the 5-year-old boy and 1-year-old girl on the first floor, two more women and two young men in a stairwell, and the shooter dead on the second floor. Lorah declined to discuss motive but suggested mental health could be a factor, saying investigators would examine the cellphones of the women killed outside: "They knew something was going on at that house. We have to figure out what that is." Friends of the family said they had known them for more than 30 years; Nicole Eller said the shooter "was a good guy, and then he started going downhill with everything." At a Saturday news conference, Mayor Daria Devlin told the victims' families: "I am so deeply sorry."

Nine people are dead, including two children and the suspected gunman, after a shooting and lengthy police standoff Friday evening in Erie, Pennsylvania. Police were dispatched around 4:52 p.m. to reports of gunfire near a residence on Perry Street, where officers found two women fatally shot outside; the gunman barricaded himself inside, and tactical officers later found additional victims, including children, in the home. The gunman was found dead of an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound, Erie Police Chief Rick Lorah described the scene as among the worst of his 28-year career, Governor Josh Shapiro offered the state's full support to Erie Mayor Daria Devlin, and the city scheduled a 4 p.m. Saturday news conference.
