Kazakhstan's central bank is shifting from being one of the world's biggest gold buyers to a seller, Bloomberg reported Monday, citing high Treasury yields and the need to rebalance reserves. A central banker is quoted saying the gold-heavy holdings are "starting to resemble a single-asset portfolio," and the 10-year Treasury yield hit a 24-year high last week.

Heavy central-bank buying helped drive gold sharply higher in recent years, but the rally has stumbled as surging bond yields erode the appeal of non-yielding gold. The full detail of the sales program remains behind the paywall and unconfirmed.
