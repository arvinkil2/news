Four activists from Greenpeace's Rainbow Warrior scaled the 70,000 tonne Scarborough unit, about 375 kilometres off the Pilbara coast, on Saturday, and two spent the night in tents suspended from the structure before police removed them by helicopter on Sunday, Splash247 reported. In a joint statement on Monday, the Australian Border Force, Western Australia Police and the offshore regulator NOPSEMA said two men, aged 48 and 52, had been charged with interfering with operations of offshore petroleum installations, an offence carrying a maximum penalty of 10 years in prison, and were due before Karratha Magistrates Court on Monday. Woodside confirmed it paused pre-commissioning work after the unauthorised access and called the action dangerous, reckless and illegal, while Greenpeace said the protest was peaceful and aimed at pressuring the government to reject the Browse development.
