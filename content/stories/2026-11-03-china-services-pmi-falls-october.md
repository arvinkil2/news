China's services activity expanded at its slowest pace since July in October, with the RatingDog China General Services PMI falling to 50.8 from 51.6 in September, against a Bloomberg median forecast that it would rise to 51.7. The index stayed above the 50 mark that separates expansion from contraction, but business confidence eased to its lowest since July, firms cut selling prices for a second month, and input cost inflation reached a five month high on labour, raw material and oil costs. The composite PMI, which combines services with manufacturing, eased to 52.0 from 52.4.
