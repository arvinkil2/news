President Donald Trump threatened on Thursday to immediately double tariffs on Mexican imports unless Mexico stops migrant caravans traveling toward the US border, writing on Truth Social that "the Tariffs on Mexico will be IMMEDIATELY DOUBLED" if it does not. He did not say which tariffs would be doubled, whether existing exemptions would survive, or when any increase would take effect.

Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum disputed the premise at her morning news conference, saying no migrant caravan has reached the northern border since Trump returned to office in January 2025, and that two caravans in southern Mexico, put at about 1,500 people by Reuters, are being managed by Mexican officials. The threat lands on duties already at 25 percent on most non-USMCA Mexican goods, with bilateral commerce of $96.34 billion in September alone and tariff-reduction talks still under way.
