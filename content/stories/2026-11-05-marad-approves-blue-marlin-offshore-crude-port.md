The Maritime Administration has approved the deepwater port license application for Blue Marlin Offshore Port, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy announced on Thursday. The $1 billion project, developed by an affiliate of Energy Transfer, sits in West Cameron Block 509 about 99 miles off Cameron Parish, Louisiana, and is designed to load very large crude carriers at up to 80,000 barrels an hour, for exports of up to 2 million barrels a day, using existing and converted offshore platforms tied to supply hubs in Texas and southwest Louisiana. The Department of Transportation said the project would support up to 1,052 construction jobs; the application was first filed in October 2020, the final environmental impact statement was completed earlier this year, and no construction start date was announced.
