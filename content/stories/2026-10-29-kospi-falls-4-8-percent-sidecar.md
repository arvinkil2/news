The Kospi opened down 2.99 percent at 5,760.35 on Friday and the fall deepened through the morning, reaching 5,653.02, down 284.64 points or 4.79 percent, at 10:08 Seoul time, Yonhap reported. Samsung Electronics was down 5.28 percent and SK Hynix down 8.72 percent at that point, foreigners and institutions were net sellers, and the Kosdaq was down 3.32 percent at 728.25.

At 10:04:55 the Korea Exchange triggered a sell sidecar, suspending the effect of program sell orders on the Kospi market for five minutes, after Kospi 200 futures fell 5.12 percent to 887.92 and held a fall of 5 percent or more for one minute, Yonhap reported. It was the first Kospi sell sidecar since October 15, when the index plunged 7.57 percent.

Yonhap attributed the fall to the overnight US session, where the Dow Jones fell 0.25 percent, the S&P 500 fell 0.30 percent and the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.38 percent after Alphabet's third quarter revenue missed expectations and its free cash flow stayed in deficit for a second quarter, reviving concern about the returns on AI investment. Alphabet fell 12.64 percent, and Intel and Amazon were falling sharply in after hours trading after results Yonhap described as below market expectations.
