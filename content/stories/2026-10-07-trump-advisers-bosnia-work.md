President Donald Trump on Wednesday distanced himself from two of his top political advisers, James Blair and Chris LaCivita, writing that if their outside work presents a conflict, "I will terminate them, immediately," CNN reported. The warning came after the New York Times reported that Blair, who leads Trump's outside political operation, and LaCivita, Trump's 2024 co-campaign manager, traveled to the Balkans last week for paid work on behalf of a pro-Russian government in Bosnia's Republika Srpska, ahead of elections there on Sunday.

The revelation lands less than 30 days before the midterm elections, with Blair and LaCivita leading a big-money midterm advertising campaign for the Trump political operation. The New York Post characterized the work as advising a pro-Russia Bosnia party, adding to pressure on the operation over conflicts between its domestic campaign role and foreign clients.
