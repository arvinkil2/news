LSEG said New York based Octus, which sells credit intelligence on distressed companies to banks, investors and law firms, fits its fixed income data and private markets businesses and would lift Data and Analytics growth immediately, but investors sold the stock anyway, sending the shares down 4.7% by mid afternoon in London. Permira bought control of Octus from Warburg Pincus in 2022 at a $1.3 billion valuation and has been preparing a sale since last year, and Reuters reported the firm has also sounded out other buyers, so LSEG is not guaranteed to win the asset.
