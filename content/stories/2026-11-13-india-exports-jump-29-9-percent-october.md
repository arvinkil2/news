India's total exports, merchandise and services combined, rose 29.9 percent year on year to an estimated 89.99 billion dollars in October, from 69.27 billion dollars in October 2025, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry said. Imports rose to 110.07 billion dollars from 94.42 billion, and the overall trade deficit narrowed to 20.09 billion dollars from 25.15 billion a year earlier.

For the April to October period, cumulative exports are estimated at 568.84 billion dollars, up 16.66 percent from 487.59 billion dollars in the same period of the previous fiscal year.
