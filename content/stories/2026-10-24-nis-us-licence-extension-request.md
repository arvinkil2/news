Serbia's oil company NIS has filed a new request with the US Treasury for an extension of the special licence that would allow it to continue operating after the current licence expires on October 30, Interfax reported, citing the broadcaster RTRS. The request argues that NIS is important for continuity of supply to the Serbian market and that regular operation of its refinery at Pančevo, with capacity of 4.8 million tonnes a year, is key to Serbia's energy stability. NIS, the country's only oil and gas producer, was placed on the US sanctions list in early 2025 as a subsidiary of Gazprom Neft, and Hungary's MOL has agreed to buy a 56.15 percent stake from Gazprom Neft in a transaction that still requires approval from the US Office of Foreign Assets Control.
