On November 4, 2026, Donald Harrison, a Class B director of Space Exploration Technologies Corp., notified the company of his intention to resign as a member of its board of directors, effective immediately, according to a Form 8-K filed on Friday and signed by Chief Financial Officer Bret Johnsen.

The filing states that Mr. Harrison's decision to resign was not due to any disagreement with the company on any matter relating to its operations, policies or practices. The company and the board expressed their appreciation for his contributions and years of service.
