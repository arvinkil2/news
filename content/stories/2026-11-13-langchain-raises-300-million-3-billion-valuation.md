LangChain said on Friday it has raised $300 million in a Series C round at a $3 billion valuation, led by Alphabet's CapitalG, with participation from Singapore's GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP and Sapphire Ventures. The valuation is more than double the $1.25 billion at which the company raised $125 million about a year ago, in a round led by IVP.

The company began in 2022 as an open source project for tasks early large language models handled poorly, and has since shifted to managing AI agents through its LangSmith platform, which it says has 7,000 customers, including Nvidia, Toyota and Salesforce. Its pitch is that businesses can run agents without locking into a single model provider or cloud.
