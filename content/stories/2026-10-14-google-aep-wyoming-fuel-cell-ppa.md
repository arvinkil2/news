Google will buy the output of Cheyenne Fuel Cell, a 702MW plant near Cheyenne, Wyoming, in which AEP holds a 92 percent stake and funds advised by Basalt Infrastructure Partners hold the rest, with Bloom Energy supplying the fuel cells. AEP plans to start construction in 2026 and reach full operations by the end of 2028. Google is developing a 2.7GW data center campus near Cheyenne on a 716-acre site, with four data halls planned for completion by 2031.
