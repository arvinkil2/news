Cygnus Metals' merger with Central Asia Metals has concluded, and Cygnus shares will stop trading on the TSX Venture Exchange and the ASX, the companies confirmed in their closing release.

The combination folds Cygnus's Quebec lithium and copper gold exploration portfolio, centred on the Chibougamau district, into a London listed producer with operating cash flow from Kazakhstan and North Macedonia, the standard 2026 shape for junior consolidation: an explorer with good ground and no money joining a mid tier producer with cash flow and no growth pipeline.

For Cygnus holders the deal ends a long period in which the company's projects were worth more inside someone else's balance sheet than on its own register, a calculation shareholders across the junior sector have been making all year as majors and mid tiers buy growth rather than drill it.
