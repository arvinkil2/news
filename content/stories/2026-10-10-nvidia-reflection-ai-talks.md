Nvidia is in early discussions to invest further in Reflection AI or acquire the coding-automation startup outright, the Financial Times reported Saturday, citing people familiar with the matter. The deal could take the form of hiring key personnel and licensing Reflection's technology rather than a full acquisition, a structure that could avoid lengthy regulatory review. Nvidia is already a major investor, having put $800 million into the company.

Reflection AI, founded in 2024 by two former Google DeepMind researchers, builds AI for automating software development and released its first open-weight model, Beam, on October 5, positioning it against low-cost Chinese models like DeepSeek and Kimi. Neither company has commented on the talks.
