**Trump arrives in Manila, will miss most of the ASEAN summit.** President Trump arrived in the Philippines on Tuesday ahead of the ASEAN summit and the Quad leaders meeting, with the New York Times reporting he will miss most of the ASEAN gathering. The Manila meetings run into Thursday's planned talks with Presidents Xi and Putin, and allied governments are watching for signals on trade and security commitments.

**Serbia's Student List candidates to be sworn in as MPs.** Candidates of Serbia's student-led list are to be sworn in as members of parliament following their election triumph, converting a protest movement that began over the 2024 station canopy collapse into a parliamentary bloc. The swearing-in is the movement's first formal step inside the institutions it campaigned against.

**WTO counts a surge in trade-restrictive measures.** The World Trade Organization's latest trade monitoring report, published Tuesday, found that new trade-restrictive measures have surged, even as members also stepped up trade facilitation efforts. The report is the organization's own record of measures introduced across the G20 and wider membership.

**Mozambique opposition leader faces trial.** Mozambique's opposition leader faces trial over the deadly unrest that followed the disputed 2024 election, the BBC reported. The prosecution keeps the post-election crisis, in which hundreds died in protests, at the center of Mozambican politics two years on.

**China and Canada agree to further tariff reductions at Shenzhen (single-sourced: France 24).** China and Canada agreed to further reduce tariffs at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, France 24 reported. No second outlet or official readout had confirmed the agreement when this roundup was written.
