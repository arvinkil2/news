French broadcaster BFMTV reports attackers targeted outdated versions of Danish manufacturer Danfoss's refrigeration control software at numerous supermarkets, remotely forcing defrost cycles with temperatures programmed to reach as high as 50C. Around 150 Intermarche stores were affected along with Leclerc, Carrefour and Monoprix outlets, per retail trade coverage; two stores in Haguenau alone lost about €270,000 in merchandise. Cooperative U president Dominique Schelcher called the attack unprecedented and of the utmost gravity, given refrigeration's role in preserving fresh food.

Separately, Dutch market leader Albert Heijn halted online grocery deliveries and took its website, app and customer service offline after a cybersecurity incident, telling customers it cannot say when services will return. In stores, electronic shelf labels are showing last week's promotions while checkout charges current prices, and customers have been asked to keep receipts for compensation. The company has not disclosed the cause or whether data was compromised.
