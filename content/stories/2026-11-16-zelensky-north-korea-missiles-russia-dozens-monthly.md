Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said in his evening address on November 16 that Russia is receiving more ballistic missiles from North Korea, put by the Ukrainian presidential office at dozens of North Korean-made ballistic missiles supplied every month, Yonhap reported. Zelensky said the supply is further improving the weapons' performance, called it extremely dangerous and not a problem for Ukraine alone, and said it poses additional challenges to the entire region around Korea.

Zelensky also appealed for European support against Russia, saying Europe has missiles capable of effective strikes and that he is holding related discussions with Germany, France, the United Kingdom and Italy. The claim is his alone as reported: Yonhap cited no independent evidence, intelligence assessment or other corroboration for the monthly figure.
