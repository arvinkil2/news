The US State Department said on Friday it had approved the sale of 500 Patriot PAC-3 missiles to Ukraine for an estimated $2.1 billion. Ukraine will pay for the interceptors with contributions from Canada, Germany and Norway routed through the US JUMPSTART program, along with national funds financed by the European Union's Ukraine Support Loan, and Lockheed Martin is the principal contractor. The approval follows President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's statement earlier this week that Ukraine had been waiting for almost a month for the US to sign off on critical missile supplies as Russia stepped up attacks on energy and civilian infrastructure ahead of winter.
