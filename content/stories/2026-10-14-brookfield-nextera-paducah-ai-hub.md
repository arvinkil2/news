The Department of Energy has announced a $100 billion private investment, the Paducah American Energy Hub, to redevelop the former uranium enrichment site in western Kentucky as an artificial intelligence and high performance computing campus, with a phased buildout targeted for 2032 and about 8,000 construction jobs and 600 permanent jobs expected. Brookfield Asset Management has been selected to build the 1.8 GW data centre campus, while NextEra Energy will develop 2 GW of natural gas fired generation coupled with 2.6 GW of battery storage, on dedicated supplies designed to feed excess power back to the regional grid.
