**Japan sells inflation indexed 10 year bonds.** Japan's Ministry of Finance published the auction result for 10 year inflation indexed JGBs on November 16, a small but watched corner of the Japanese curve where breakeven inflation is priced directly. The result is recorded here from the ministry's own release; detailed allotment figures are in the release itself.

**RBI cancels 49 NBFC registrations.** The Reserve Bank of India cancelled the certificates of registration of 49 non bank financial companies, and separately recorded 13 NBFCs and one housing finance company surrendering their certificates, in releases in the feed pool. The cancellations continue the regulator's clean up of dormant and non compliant lenders.

**India labour participation rises.** India's Periodic Labour Force Survey monthly bulletin put the labour force participation rate for people aged 15 and over at 55.7 percent in October 2026, up 0.3 percentage points, according to the National Statistical Office press note in the pool. Single-sourced in this run's pool; the figure is the office's own headline.

**RBI to absorb 50,000 crore rupees for two days.** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct a two day variable rate reverse repo auction on Tuesday, November 17, with a notified amount of 50,000 crore rupees and reversal on November 19, after a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, the bank said in its own release. The operation is part of the bank's routine liquidity adjustment facility fine tuning.

**India to auction 33,000 crore rupees of dated securities.** The government of India will sell 33,000 crore rupees of dated securities on Friday, November 20, the Reserve Bank of India announced: 19,000 crore rupees of the 6.53 percent security maturing in 2031 and 14,000 crore rupees of the 7.71 percent security maturing in 2066, with settlement on November 23 and an option to retain a further 2,000 crore rupees against each security.

**FSB reports on cross border payments.** The Financial Stability Board published its consolidated 2026 progress report on the G20 roadmap for enhancing cross border payments, concluding that further work by authorities and the private sector is still necessary. The report is the annual scorecard for the G20's cheaper and faster payments targets.
