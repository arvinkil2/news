The Federal Home Loan Bank of Chicago said on Monday that its board and President and Chief Executive Officer Michael A. Ericson have agreed he will conclude his tenure with the bank on December 31, 2026, and that the board is starting a process to identify a permanent successor. To cover the transition, the board named Virxhini Gjonzeneli, an executive vice president who has served as chief financial officer and group head for financial accounting and markets since January 2023, as interim president and chief executive effective January 1, 2027.

The bank said compensation relating to Ericson's departure and Gjonzeneli's interim service is being finalised and will be subject to review and non-objection by the Federal Housing Finance Agency, with any material arrangements to be disclosed once available.
