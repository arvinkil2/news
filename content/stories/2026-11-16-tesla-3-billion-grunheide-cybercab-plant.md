Tesla said on Monday it plans to invest 3 billion euros, about 3.3 billion dollars, in its factory at Grünheide in Brandenburg, its only plant in Europe, so that it can start producing Cybercabs, the company's driverless taxis. Construction is set to begin in 2027 with the first vehicles due off the line in 2029, and Tesla said capacity is to reach one million units, although it gave no timeframe for that goal. The company said it would apply for the new building by the end of this year.

The plant employed 12,600 people in October, and the expansion is expected to add several hundred more jobs. Brandenburg's state premier, Dietmar Woidke, called the investment an enormously important economic decision for the state. The plan follows Elon Musk's announcement last week that Tesla was shutting down Cybertruck production, and a sales slump that had put the Grünheide expansion on hold before demand picked up.
