Moldova's Border Police and Customs Service temporarily suspended the Ocnița, Otaci, Unguri, Briceni, Grimăncăuți and Clocușna checkpoints on the Ukrainian border on Tuesday evening for security reasons, after drones entered Moldovan airspace during a mass Russian attack on Ukraine. The Defence Ministry said a Shahed-type drone crossed twice in the afternoon, first toward Tiraspol and then toward Mălăiești in Criuleni district, and the government closed Moldovan airspace after reports of another drone moving toward Chișinău.

President Maia Sandu said the drones were launched as part of Russia's war, that Chișinău residents were alarmed by drones flying directly over the city, and that Moldova lacks the defence capability to protect itself against them. No reopening time for the checkpoints was announced.
