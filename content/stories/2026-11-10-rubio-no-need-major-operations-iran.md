Secretary of State Marco Rubio told G7 foreign ministers in Paris on Tuesday that the economic campaign against Iran is so devastating that there is no need for major combat operations right now, the clearest statement yet that Washington is stepping back from renewed strikes as diplomacy gathers pace. President Trump said hours earlier that he saw positive momentum toward ending the eight-month war, and Qatar and Pakistan are leading mediation efforts, with Tehran's offer to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days, on conditions including an end to the US port blockade and the lifting of oil sanctions, still on the table. Britain's Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband called at the same meeting for an all-out diplomatic effort to end the conflict and get Hormuz properly reopened.
