Iran's Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in a statement on Wednesday that it considers Argentina's naval and air forces terrorist organisations, invoking what it called the principle of reciprocity, and said the responsibility for all consequences arising from the matter will lie with the Argentine government. The statement named the navy and the air force only, not the army.

The move answers Argentina's designation of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps as a terrorist organisation in March under President Javier Milei, a step his office tied to the 1994 bombing of the AMIA Jewish community centre in Buenos Aires, which killed 85 people and which Argentina blames on the Iran backed group Hezbollah. Argentina's foreign ministry said it categorically rejects Iran's designation, calling the measure completely baseless and an unacceptable accusation.
