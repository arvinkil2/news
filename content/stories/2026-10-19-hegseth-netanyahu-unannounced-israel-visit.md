Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth met Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in Israel on Monday on a visit that had not been announced in advance, Axios reported, citing two sources with knowledge. The meeting took place at Ovda Air Base in southern Israel, where US refueling planes and fighter jets are deployed, and Hegseth also met US troops at the base, according to the report. The Pentagon and the Israeli Embassy declined to comment, and Reuters said it could not immediately confirm the visit.

The trip comes eight days before Israel's parliamentary election and, in Axios's framing, amid US and Israeli discussions about potentially resuming major combat operations against Iran; Axios reported no agenda or statements from inside the meeting. It follows President Donald Trump's endorsement of Netanyahu on Sunday, which this site reported on October 18, while Vice President JD Vance told the outlet 2WAY on Monday that he was not getting involved in Israeli elections.
