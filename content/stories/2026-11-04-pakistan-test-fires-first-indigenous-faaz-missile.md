Pakistan has conducted a successful flight test of the Fa'az, its first indigenously developed air to air missile, the military's Inter Services Public Relations wing said on November 3, describing the weapon as fitted with a terminal guidance system able to engage aerial targets at extended ranges. Video released by the Pakistan Air Force shows a JF-17 Thunder Block 1 firing the missile in an imaging infrared seeker configuration, according to Quwa, a defence analysis publication; the Fa'az was developed by the Air Weapons Complex of the National Engineering and Scientific Commission and is marketed for export by state owned Global Industrial and Defence Solutions. The air force said the missile will enhance its operational flexibility, and Pakistan has not disclosed the weapon's range, speed or induction timeline.
