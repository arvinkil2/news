Ghana has completed the first round of its voluntary buyback of Domestic Debt Exchange Programme (DDEP) bonds due in February 2027, buying back GH¢12.60 billion of bonds tendered at the auction, 44.1 percent of the outstanding stock, the Ministry of Finance said on Thursday. Participating bondholders were paid in full and in cash, with a further GH¢289 million of accrued interest bringing the total payment to GH¢12.89 billion, financed through the Cedi Sinking Fund; Finance Minister Cassiel Ato Forson said settlement was completed at 11:15 a.m., ahead of the scheduled noon time, with no discount or haircut applied.

The reverse auction ran from November 23 to 25 and invited holders to tender up to GH¢28.58 billion of the February 2027 security, issued under the 2022 domestic debt restructuring. The ministry said the offer will reopen in two weeks for holders of the remaining bonds.
