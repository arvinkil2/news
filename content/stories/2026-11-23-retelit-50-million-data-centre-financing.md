Retelit's data centre arm has secured an extra 50 million euros ($55 million) from UniCredit, exercised through an accordion option that expands its existing 375 million euro financing agreement, split across several credit lines with ING among the lenders, to 425 million euros. The money supports a plan to add 27MW of capacity through three new data centres in Milan and Rome, on top of 18MW today. Retelit Datacenter manages 27 data centres across 17 areas of Italy on Datacenter Dynamics' count, and the wider group operates 38 facilities.

Construction has already started on a 13.6MW facility at Corsico, southwest of Milan, on a former industrial site. The financing sits inside a larger plan, announced by chief executive Jorge Álvarez in 2025, to build AI-ready capacity and more than double installed capacity from 25MW to 60MW by 2027.

The borrowing lands while the portfolio itself is in play: Italian press reports say the data centre unit is drawing non-binding bids from infrastructure investors including Ardian and Macquarie and from tower operator Rai Way, against a reported asking value of about 700 million euros. Retelit has not confirmed a sale process in the reporting read for this story.
