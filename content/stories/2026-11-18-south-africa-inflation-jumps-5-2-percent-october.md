South Africa's annual consumer price inflation was 5.2 percent in October 2026, up from 4.5 percent in September, Statistics South Africa said on Wednesday, with the index rising 0.7 percent on the month. Transport was the largest contributor to the annual rate, rising 15.2 percent and adding 2.1 percentage points, as fuel prices rose 42.9 percent from a year earlier and 11.8 percent on the month. Housing and utilities rose 5.2 percent and contributed 1.3 percentage points, and insurance and financial services rose 5.3 percent, contributing 0.6 of a point.

The pressure was concentrated in goods and administered prices rather than food. Goods inflation rose to 5.3 percent from 4.1 percent in September, services inflation was 5.0 percent, and prices for administered items rose 17.2 percent from a year earlier, while food and non-alcoholic beverages rose just 1.4 percent. Inflation excluding administered prices was 3.4 percent.
