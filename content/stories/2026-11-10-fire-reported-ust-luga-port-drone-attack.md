Ukrainian outlets NV and TSN reported overnight that drones attacked the port of Ust-Luga in Russia's Leningrad oblast and that a fire broke out on the port grounds, with a glow visible over the facility. NV based its report on social media OSINT channels and footage, and said there was no official confirmation of the consequences, including a claim in those messages that work at the port had stopped.

Russian state wires separately reported a large drone attack on the region, with TASS, RIA and Interfax each reporting that air defences shot down 146 drones over Leningrad oblast. Ust-Luga is one of Russia's largest Baltic ports and a major outlet for crude oil and petroleum product exports; neither side has published confirmed damage figures for the port itself.
