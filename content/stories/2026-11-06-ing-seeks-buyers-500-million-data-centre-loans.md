ING is reportedly seeking buyers for a portfolio of 12 data centre loans worth about $500 million through a bids-wanted-in-competition auction, with bids sized at $10 million or more due by November 19, Bloomberg reported, as carried by CNBC, citing people familiar with the matter. The portfolio is said to include a $25 million piece linked to the Stargate project in New Mexico, which involves OpenAI, SoftBank and Oracle, and other loan assets involving Vantage Data Centers and CyrusOne, and an ING spokesperson declined to comment. The reported sale joins a broader push by banks to distribute credit risk tied to the AI build-out, after the Democrats' midterm win raised the prospect of moratoriums or tighter restrictions on data centre construction in some states.
