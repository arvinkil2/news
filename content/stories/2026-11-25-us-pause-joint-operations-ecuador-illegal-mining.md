Ecuador’s Interior Minister John Reimberg said in a radio interview on Wednesday that talks last week with US Secretary of War Pete Hegseth were productive and covered operations, next steps and Ecuador’s operational needs, Reuters reported. The remarks followed reports on Tuesday by The New York Times and The Atlantic that Washington halted joint operations in mid-October while the US Department of Defense investigated alleged human rights violations during a September 23 operation by Ecuadorian forces in Manabí province that killed a suspected criminal leader.

Ecuador elevated illegal mining to a national security threat in July, and its 2026 to 2029 National Security Plan treats illicit gold extraction as a source of finance for organised crime. President Daniel Noboa and Hegseth discussed technological assistance, port security and border surveillance last week, but no new agreement was signed ahead of the November 29 local elections.
