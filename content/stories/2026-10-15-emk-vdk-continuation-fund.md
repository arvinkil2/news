EMK Capital has raised a 1.25 billion euro continuation fund for VDK Groep, co led by LGT Capital Partners and Coller EQT, PE Hub reported on Thursday. VDK, based in the Netherlands, is a group of technical installation and maintenance companies covering electrical and mechanical installation, climate technology, fire safety and building intelligence, and EMK has held a majority stake since 2020, backing a buy and build strategy. The continuation fund moves the asset into a new vehicle with secondaries investors rather than selling it, extending EMK's ownership six years into the holding period.
