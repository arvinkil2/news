Construction specialist TSL, formally Tonroe Group, is leaving Agratas's £5.2 billion gigafactory at Bridgwater in Somerset after failing to agree terms with the Tata Group battery company, according to The Guardian, with disagreements over the project's timeline and cost behind the split. TSL is expected to leave the site within two to three months with no replacement main contractor lined up, five months after Agratas dismissed its predecessor, Sir Robert McAlpine, at short notice in June.

The plant, backed by £380 million of pledged UK government support and planned to employ as many as 4,200 people, is now targeting a January 2028 start of production, already pushed back from 2026. Agratas said it is on track to meet customer requirements and that its supplier partners are supporting it well, while Jaguar Land Rover has secured batteries from other suppliers for its first electric models, so those launches are not affected.
