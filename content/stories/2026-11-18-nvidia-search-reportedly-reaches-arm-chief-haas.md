Nvidia's board has hired recruiting firm Heidrick & Struggles to oversee its search for a successor to founder Jensen Huang, and the firm has contacted Arm Holdings chief executive Rene Haas about the position and begun approaching other executives, Bloomberg News reported on Wednesday via Investing.com, citing a person familiar with the matter. Haas has led Arm since 2022. The report stresses the outreach does not mean Haas has agreed to pursue the role or that Nvidia has decided on its next chief executive, and none of Haas, Nvidia, Arm or the recruiting firm is reported to have commented.

Separately, interim chief executive Colette Kress opened Nvidia's first all-hands meeting since Huang's resignation, held Wednesday in Santa Clara, by urging employees to move forward, recapped Tuesday's earnings as a strong quarter and mentioned pay raises, CNBC reported from an attendee's account. Board chair Harvey Jones discussed the chief executive search at the meeting and had said on Tuesday's earnings call that the board unanimously accepted Huang's resignation and does not intend to reinstate him, even as some employees call for his return.
