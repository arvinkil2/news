A magnitude 6.1 earthquake struck 17 kilometres west of Rio Grande in Panama's Veraguas province at 11:20 p.m. UTC on Monday, at a depth of 11 kilometres, according to the US Geological Survey's reviewed solution. The USGS modelled very strong shaking near the epicentre and moderate shaking in Santiago, issued no tsunami statement, and a magnitude 4.9 aftershock followed 16 minutes later. The quake is the latest large aftershock of the magnitude 7.7 earthquake that hit Panama on October 9, which injured at least 27 people and damaged hundreds of buildings, and no casualties from this aftershock had been reported in the initial wire accounts.
