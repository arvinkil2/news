Guadalupe Taddei Zavala announced her resignation as president of Mexico's National Electoral Institute (INE) at Sunday's extraordinary session of its General Council, to take effect on Wednesday, October 28, saying she was stepping aside so as not to obstruct the investigations and rejecting the accusations against her administration. The institute's Internal Control Body opened an investigation on October 21 into alleged irregular contracts, unqualified appointments and a reported ghost employee under her presidency. Reforma first reported the resignation earlier on Sunday, and Bloomberg reported it on Sunday night.
