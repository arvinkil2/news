CISA added two vulnerabilities in Ivanti Connect Secure to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on October 30, each on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2026-111357, a missing authentication for critical function vulnerability, and CVE-2026-118527, a generation of predictable numbers or identifiers vulnerability. Under Binding Operational Directive 26-04, federal civilian agencies must prioritise rapid remediation of catalog vulnerabilities on publicly exposed assets that grant total control after exploitation, and must check whether threat actors compromised a system before a patch was applied. CISA encourages all organisations to adopt the same risk based approach.
