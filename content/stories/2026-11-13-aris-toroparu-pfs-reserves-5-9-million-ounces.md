Aris Mining announced positive prefeasibility study results for the Toroparu gold project in Guyana on November 12. The study outlines planned production of 5.6 million ounces of gold over a 29 year processing period, including 21 years of mining, with production of 258,000 ounces a year in years 1 to 17.

Proven and probable reserves total 5.9 million ounces, comprising 5.1 million ounces at 1.18 grams a tonne above a 0.50 gram cut off and 0.9 million ounces at 0.40 grams a tonne below it. Reserves were estimated at a gold price of 1,950 dollars an ounce. The initial funding requirement is 1,005 million dollars, and the construction schedule is 32 months for a conventional truck and shovel open pit.

Toroparu is at prefeasibility stage, one step below a full feasibility study. The milestone that now governs the project is permitting: Aris applied for a large scale mining licence in August 2026 and targets a construction decision in early 2027, subject to that licence and other approvals. The NI 43-101 technical report, dated November 12, has been filed on SEDAR+ and with the US Securities and Exchange Commission.
