Paramount Skydance's third-quarter revenue rose 5 percent to $7.0 billion with net earnings of $73 million and adjusted EBITDA of $1.0 billion, as Paramount Plus revenue grew 21 percent to $2.13 billion on 82.2 million subscribers, while television network revenue fell 7 percent to $3 billion. In its first update since closing the Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition on October 6, the company said it expects to eliminate at least $6 billion in annual expenses within three years against about $87 billion of gross debt, and warned that weaker Warner Bros. theatrical releases could reduce fourth-quarter studio profit by as much as $200 million. Skydance plans a combined Paramount Plus and HBO Max subscription early next year, and consolidated results including Warner Bros. Discovery will first appear in the fourth-quarter report due in February 2027.
