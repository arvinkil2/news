Japan and the Czech Republic signed a new convention for the elimination of double taxation in Prague on November 23, the Japanese Ministry of Finance said. The convention wholly amends the existing treaty, which entered into force in 1978 as an agreement with the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic, by revising the taxation of business profits and investment income, introducing measures to prevent abuse of the convention and reinforcing the exchange of information on tax matters. The ministry said the existing convention between Japan and the Slovak Republic is not affected.

The convention enters into force 30 days after the later of the two countries' completion notifications, which in Japan requires Diet approval, and takes effect for taxes from January 1 of the following calendar year. The information-exchange provisions apply from entry into force regardless of the tax year concerned.
