CrowdStrike said it will buy Fiddler AI, which makes observability software for monitoring AI agents inside enterprises, and Dreadnode, which automates security testing aimed at detecting flaws in AI models, apps and agents before they go live. TechCrunch reported the company is paying about $400 million for Fiddler AI and about $95 million for Dreadnode, citing the Wall Street Journal.

Fiddler AI will join the Falcon platform as Falcon Observe, evaluating agents before deployment and feeding monitoring telemetry into the wider platform, while Dreadnode will be integrated into Falcon Guardian, CrowdStrike's agent detection and response product. Fiddler AI, founded in 2018, had raised about $99 million to date, and Dreadnode, founded in 2023, about $14 million, according to PitchBook figures cited by TechCrunch.
