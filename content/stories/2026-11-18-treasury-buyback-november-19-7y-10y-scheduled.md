The US Treasury will buy back nominal coupon securities in the 7 year to 10 year maturity bucket on Thursday, November 19, in a liquidity support operation with a maximum purchase amount of $4 billion. The operation runs at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, 9 issues are eligible, and settlement is on November 20. Amounts offered and accepted will be published after the operation runs.
