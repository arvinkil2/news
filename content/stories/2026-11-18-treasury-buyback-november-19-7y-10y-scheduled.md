**Latest.** The US Treasury accepted $254 million in Thursday's liquidity support buyback of nominal coupon securities in the 7 year to 10 year maturity bucket, out of $3.078 billion offered against a maximum purchase amount of $4 billion. The operation, run at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, accepted 2 of 9 eligible issues, filled about 6 percent of the maximum, and settles on November 20. The accepted amount deviates about 68 percent from the $805 million mean of the previous four operations in the same bucket.

The US Treasury will buy back nominal coupon securities in the 7 year to 10 year maturity bucket on Thursday, November 19, in a liquidity support operation with a maximum purchase amount of $4 billion. The operation runs at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, 9 issues are eligible, and settlement is on November 20. Amounts offered and accepted will be published after the operation runs.
