F2i SGR, Italy's largest independent infrastructure fund manager, has passed 1.2 billion euros of commitments across two new vehicles: a first closing of about 650 million euros for its seventh equity fund, and a third closing of 590 million euros for its second infrastructure credit fund, ICF 2. The manager aims to raise more than 2.2 billion euros in total across the two funds, taking funds raised to date above 11 billion euros.

Equity Fund VII is a Luxembourg vehicle with a ten-year term and a 1.5 billion euro target, split between an Italy compartment and a Eurozone compartment covering Italy, Spain, France and Greece, with no more than 30 percent in any one country. Eighteen institutional investors subscribed at the first close, and 83 percent of commitments came from investors in earlier F2i funds.

Deployment has started. The fund has signed an agreement to acquire control of Luxida, a Spanish electricity distribution platform, as the first step of a buy-and-build strategy, and Snam has granted it exclusivity for Bioenerys Agri, its biogas and biomethane business, with completion of that transaction expected in early 2027 if agreements are finalised.

ICF 2's 590 million euros already stands 18 percent above F2i's debut credit vehicle, which closed at 500 million euros in 2024, and the fund has completed five financing transactions in European countries. The pairing is the point: F2i is building the credit book alongside the equity book, lending to the same class of European infrastructure assets it buys.
