Chicago-based GTCR has raised more than $14.2 billion for Fund XV, its fifteenth flagship private equity fund, according to AltAssets reporting on October 9. The raise has surpassed the $13.5 billion target reported for the vehicle and sits nearly 24 percent above the predecessor fund, as the firm moves toward a final close.

The raise adds to a run of oversubscribed flagship fundraises in the same week, including Spectrum Equity's $2.5 billion close and Bridgepoint exceeding its 28 billion euro target three months early. The pattern is consistent with PitchBook data cited this week showing global PE exit value up 65 percent in the third quarter while fundraising keeps shrinking: capital is returning to the market's largest franchises first.

The figures are single-sourced to AltAssets and not confirmed by GTCR, so the raise is tracked here at grade C pending the firm's own announcement.
