The Indonesian Navy seized the Comoros-flagged general cargo ship MV Star Line in waters off Belawan, North Sumatra, on November 7 after a patrol vessel found its cargo and papers did not match, naval officials said at a November 20 press briefing. Fifteen of the ship's 36 containers held 405 tonnes of sodium perchlorate, a strategic oxidiser used in rocket and missile propellants, and searchers also found 54 pallets of unregistered blank 4G SIM cards numbering in the millions. The ship had sailed from Lianyungang, China, bound for Mongla Port in Bangladesh, and the regional naval commander said the quantity of chemical could potentially produce up to 100 missiles.

The vessel's documents showed IMO number 8356962 with a suspended registration status, and investigators are examining its dangerous-goods compliance, navigation permits, crew and manifest discrepancies. The navy has not named the shipping company, the cargo's owner or the Bangladeshi importer.
