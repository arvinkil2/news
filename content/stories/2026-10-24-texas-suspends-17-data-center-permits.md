Texas has suspended the construction permits of 17 data center projects and disabled STEERS, the automated filing portal run by the state environmental agency TCEQ, after watchdog groups FracTracker Alliance and Public Citizen documented more than two dozen permits issued to data center projects since Governor Greg Abbott asked for new permits to be halted in September. All 17 suspended permits are stormwater construction permits, the ground raising and levelling stage that precedes building, and the agency says its electronic filing application for automatic authorisations is shut down temporarily so staff can review applications and identify data center projects. The portal was still approving projects this week, including projects for OpenAI and Amazon, FracTracker researcher Sam Law said, while Abbott's audit of 1,800 Texas data center projects over their power generation, water consumption and community impact continues.
