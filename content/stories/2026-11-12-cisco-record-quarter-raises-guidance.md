Cisco reported first quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $18.5 billion, up 24 percent year over year, for the period ended October 24, 2026, with GAAP earnings per share of $1.14, up 58 percent, and non-GAAP earnings per share of $1.40, up 40 percent. Networking revenue rose 43 percent, and the company said revenue, non-GAAP operating margin and non-GAAP earnings per share all came in above the high end of its guidance ranges.

Cisco raised its fiscal 2027 guidance to revenue of $74.8 billion to $75.8 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share of $5.47 to $5.53, and raised its expectation for AI infrastructure revenue from hyperscalers to $9 billion in fiscal 2027, more than double the total for fiscal 2026. Second quarter guidance is revenue of $18.0 billion to $18.2 billion.
