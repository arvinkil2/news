Venezuela's interim President Delcy Rodriguez authorized Starlink, the satellite internet service operated by Elon Musk's SpaceX, to operate in the country, the Venezuelan government said in a statement on Friday. The authorization is part of an agreement with the US firm, according to the statement, which did not disclose terms.

The move extends a relationship that began in January, when Starlink offered free access in Venezuela after US forces captured then-president Nicolas Maduro and power and internet went out in parts of Caracas. Rodriguez has led an interim government since the Supreme Court appointed her in Maduro's absence. The formal operating authorization suggests the two sides are settling into a working arrangement even as the country's longer-term political future stays unresolved.
