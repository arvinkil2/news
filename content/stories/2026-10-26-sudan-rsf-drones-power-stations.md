Sudan's government said Rapid Support Forces drones struck power stations overnight in Khartoum and in Dongola, Al-Dabba and Atbara, causing power outages in the capital and other areas, AFP reported. Government spokesperson Khalid Al-Aiser said the Khartoum strikes hit the Al-Jaili and Markhiyat substations and the Yarmouk area, and he accused the RSF of deliberately attacking infrastructure and endangering civilians. The statement gave no casualty figures, and no RSF comment or independent confirmation has been reported.
