Rapid Support Forces drones hit five power stations in eastern Sudan in a coordinated attack on Monday, two in Al-Jazira state, two in Gedaref and one in White Nile, causing mass outages across large parts of the three army-controlled states, an unnamed Sudanese government official told AFP, in a report carried by Arab News. An AFP correspondent in Al-Jazira said power had been out since 2 a.m., and a resident of Al-Fao in Gedaref said power there had been out since the same time. AFP reported no casualties from this attack, and the report carries no RSF comment.
