Work began on November 20 on Finger Three, the third and largest of Tuas Port's four extensions, at a groundbreaking ceremony led by Transport Minister Jeffrey Siow. The phase covers nearly 400 hectares of reclaimed land and will add 21 berths, each 23 metres deep along an 8.6 kilometre wharf, with the first berths operating from 2028 and all 21 running by the mid-2030s. Tuas now operates 15 berths and has handled 25 million TEUs since opening in 2022; when the full port is completed in the 2040s it is planned to handle 65 million TEUs a year, with the Tanjong Pagar, Keppel and Brani terminals moving to Tuas by 2027.
