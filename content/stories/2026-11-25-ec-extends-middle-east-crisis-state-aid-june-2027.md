The European Commission has prolonged the Middle East Crisis Temporary State aid Framework, known as METSAF, by six months, from its planned expiry on December 31, 2026 to June 30, 2027, after a consultation showed broad support among member states. Under the framework, member states can compensate up to 70 percent of a beneficiary's extra fuel and fertiliser costs in agriculture, fisheries and aquaculture, land transport and intra-EU short sea shipping, and a new allowance of up to 50,000 euros per beneficiary applies for the first half of 2027 under the simplified approach, so companies that received this aid in 2026 are eligible again in 2027.

For energy-intensive industries, the framework keeps the option to raise electricity price relief intensity from 50 percent to up to 70 percent of eligible consumption costs under the Clean Industrial State aid Framework. The Commission said it has so far approved 36 measures notified by 14 member states under METSAF, plus 11 amendments to existing schemes, with an estimated budget of more than 3.7 billion euros, and that it will reassess the situation in spring 2027 and stands ready to prolong the framework further if warranted.
