Anthropic used a pre-IPO investor day in San Francisco on Wednesday to report third quarter revenue of 16.8 billion dollars, up 45 percent from 11.6 billion dollars in the second quarter and 4.7 billion dollars in the first, according to an investor who attended the meeting and spoke to Reuters. The Financial Times reported separately that the company told investors it was profitable on an adjusted basis in the quarter. The listing is expected after the November midterm elections and before a rival OpenAI offering, at a valuation target above 2 trillion dollars, even as the company carries hundreds of billions of dollars in long term commitments for cloud services, computing and infrastructure.
