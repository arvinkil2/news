US nonfarm business labour productivity increased 3.6 percent in the third quarter of 2026, as output rose 3.7 percent and hours worked rose 0.1 percent, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported in its preliminary release on Thursday. Hourly compensation rose 2.9 percent and unit labour costs decreased 0.7 percent in the quarter, though they were still up 2.4 percent over the last four quarters.

The release also revised second quarter productivity up 0.7 percentage point to an increase of 2.1 percent, from 1.4 percent previously published, on an upward revision to output. Manufacturing productivity rose just 0.3 percent in the third quarter, and manufacturing unit labour costs increased 2.7 percent.
