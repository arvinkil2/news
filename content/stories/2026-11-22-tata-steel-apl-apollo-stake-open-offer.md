Tata Steel will acquire 73.86 million shares, a 26.38 percent stake on a fully diluted basis, from promoter-group member S Gupta Holding Private Limited for about 155.1 billion rupees in cash at 2,100 rupees per share, in three tranches completing by March 31, 2028, according to APL Apollo's exchange disclosure. A mandatory open offer for up to a further 26 percent from public shareholders at 2,301.40 rupees per share could take Tata Steel to about 52.38 percent and majority control. In linked transactions, APL Apollo will acquire Tata Steel's tubes business for up to 117 billion rupees and its entire holding in Tata Steel Colors for 163 billion rupees, together about $3.02 billion. Markets reacted against both names on Monday: Tata Steel shares traded nearly 5 percent lower and APL Apollo shares fell more than 10 percent in early trade, CNBC reported; CNBC also reported that ArcelorMittal had been in the running for APL Apollo.
