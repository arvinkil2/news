President Donald Trump said in a Truth Social post on Saturday that he is ordering the Department of Justice to look into what he called a criminal act, after Democrats talked of impeaching cabinet officials. He wrote that Democrats are threatening people with impeachment and other forms of punishment, calling it "extortion at the highest level" and saying those responsible should be held accountable.

The calls he was answering were specific. Representative Hank Johnson said on Thursday that the offenses of Attorney General Todd Blanche demand that he be impeached, convicted and removed, Representative Deborah Ross said removing Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth should be a top priority, and Representative Thomas Massie, a Republican, said he plans to call for a vote to impeach Hegseth. Al Jazeera reported that Trump also said, without providing evidence, that some Democrats were threatening his donors over their political views.
