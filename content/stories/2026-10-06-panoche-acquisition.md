Middle River Power, a Chicago-based independent power producer, announced October 6 that it has closed on the acquisition of Panoche Energy Center, a fully contracted 427-megawatt natural gas generation facility in Firebaugh, California. The seller was funds associated with Ares Management.

No purchase price or contract counterparty details were disclosed. Middle River said the addition strengthens its flexible capacity portfolio as demand for dispatchable resources accelerates, a concise statement of the current California capacity-market thesis: contracted gas plants are scarcity assets in a grid adding load faster than it adds firm capacity.
