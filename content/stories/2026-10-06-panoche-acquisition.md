Middle River Power, a Chicago-based independent power producer, announced October 6 that it has closed on the acquisition of Panoche Energy Center, a fully contracted 427-megawatt natural gas generation facility in Firebaugh, California. The seller was an Ares Infrastructure Equity fund. No purchase price or contract counterparty details were disclosed.

Panoche is a simple-cycle natural gas facility built around four GE LMS100 aero-derivative turbines designed for fast ramping, suited to a grid increasingly dominated by variable solar and wind. Its capacity is fully contracted, providing stable cash flows with limited merchant risk.

Middle River said the addition strengthens its flexible capacity portfolio as demand for dispatchable resources accelerates across the Western Interconnection. MRP, acquired by Partners Group on behalf of its clients in 2025, plans to apply its hybridization strategy to Panoche, pairing efficient thermal capacity with battery storage to meet rising demand from utilities, data centers and industrial offtakers.

The deal is MRP's third acquisition this year after the 240 MW Midway-Sunset facility in Kern County, California, and the 250 MW Brush plant in Morgan County, Colorado, together adding more than 900 MW of flexible capacity. Ares Infrastructure Equity head Keith Derman said Ares had supported the asset since its development twenty years ago. MRP chief executive Mark Kubow said the strategy targets rising demand across the Western Interconnection.
