Taiwan flagged nearly 2,700 accounts to Meta that officials say appear to be part of a Chinese information warfare campaign seeking to influence the island's November 28 local elections, Reuters reported, citing three government officials and internal reports it reviewed. Of 2,400 Threads accounts in one government report, 74 percent listed China as their location, about 25 percent disclosed none and 1 percent listed Russia, and the accounts fell into 27 clusters that officials assessed were batch-created in bulk by operators in China or Russia; some 260 accounts had spread more than 2,150 AI-generated videos that drew 2.35 million interactions, most targeting ruling DPP politicians and policies. Meta said it had removed the accounts flagged by Taiwan's government, many already disabled by its own systems, and described them as low-quality fake accounts, none of which had posted about election-related topics.
