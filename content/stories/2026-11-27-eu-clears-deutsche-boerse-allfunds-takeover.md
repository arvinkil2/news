Deutsche Börse said it received unconditional merger control clearance from the European Commission for its planned acquisition of Allfunds Group, following the Commission's Phase I review. The Commission concluded the transaction would raise no competition concerns in the European Economic Area, finding after its market investigation that several credible competitors remain present in each of the markets examined, including fund distribution management and fund dealing services.

The acquisition, announced on January 21, 2026, remains subject to further clearances from supervisory authorities and to sanction of the scheme of arrangement by the competent English court. Deutsche Börse now expects completion in the first quarter of 2027. Allfunds is a global fund dealing and distribution platform connecting more than 1,400 fund partners and over 900 distributors across 66 countries, with assets under administration of 1.76 trillion euros at the end of 2025.
