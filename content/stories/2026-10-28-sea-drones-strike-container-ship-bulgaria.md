**Latest, October 29:** The ship has sunk. Splash247 reported on Thursday morning that Bulgarian authorities confirmed the Contship Jet went down around midnight, after three sea drone impacts left it listing, and that at least 20 containers fell overboard, some reportedly containing flammable liquids, gases and other hazardous materials, prompting navigational warnings. The ship was attacked about 17 nautical miles east of Cape Kaliakra, according to the report, and a French naval patrol aircraft spotted the approaching drones and alerted Bulgarian forces. Russia's defence ministry separately claimed its drones had struck an unidentified dry cargo vessel heading towards Chornomorsk and, on Thursday morning, another general cargo ship bound for Odesa, alleging both carried military cargo; neither claim has been independently verified and Moscow gave no vessel identities. Bulgaria, Romania and Turkey condemned the attacks on shipping and agreed to strengthen maritime intelligence sharing and early warning arrangements.

Sea drones struck a container ship off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast on Wednesday, about 21 miles southeast of Shabla in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone, Defence Minister Dimitar Stoyanov told reporters. The Greek owned ship, sailing under the Liberian flag from Haifa in Israel to Constanta in Romania with 421 containers, was badly damaged and listing, according to a maritime security source cited by Reuters, and Bulgaria's transport ministry said four naval and police boats were sent to assist.

The owner, Contships Management, confirmed the attack and said all 18 crew, 14 Filipinos and 4 Ukrainians, were safe and ashore. Reuters reported that it was not clear who carried out the attack. At least six merchant vessels have been attacked off Bulgaria in recent weeks and government statements put missing seafarers at about two dozen, and Turkey's defence minister warned Russia and Ukraine on Wednesday against attacks on commercial vessels in the Black Sea.
