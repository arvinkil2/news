Bank of Japan board member Naoki Tamura said on Thursday the central bank must respond swiftly to signs of an inflation overshoot, including by accelerating the pace of interest rate hikes. "Given that financial conditions remain accommodative, I believe the BOJ needs to bring its policy rate to a level deemed neutral to the economy soon," Tamura said in a speech to local leaders in Yamanashi.

Tamura said rising raw material costs, including higher crude oil prices linked to the Middle East conflict, had pushed wholesale inflation up sharply, with pressure broadening to a wide range of consumer goods, and that recent price developments pointed to further upside risks to inflation. He said the case for further hikes was stronger because Japan's inflation expectations are less firmly anchored at the bank's 2 percent target than in other economies, and that within a few months of each hike the bank can assess financial conditions from company interviews and surveys.
