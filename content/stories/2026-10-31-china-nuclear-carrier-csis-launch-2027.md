An updated CSIS assessment of satellite imagery from China's Dalian shipyard puts the unfinished hull of a fourth Chinese aircraft carrier at about 322 metres long and 46 metres wide, larger than the Fujian at a comparable stage, less than two years after prefabricated hull sections first appeared in the dry dock in early 2025. CSIS assesses that the ship, widely called the Type 004, is likely nuclear powered, based in part on two large compartments resembling shielded reactor spaces, and that it could carry as many as four aircraft launch catapults. On the construction pace CSIS has documented, a launch by the end of 2027 is possible, though Beijing has confirmed neither the propulsion nor any schedule.
