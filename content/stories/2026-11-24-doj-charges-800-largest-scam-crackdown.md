The Justice Department announced on November 24 that, since President Trump's Executive Order 14390 was signed on March 6, its prosecutors have taken action in over 400 cases against over 800 defendants, including new charges, extraditions, guilty verdicts and sentences across 85 federal districts, with 30 defendants extradited. Through the FBI's Operation Blackout, the department said nearly $17 billion in illicit funds were restrained, more than 14,000 digital devices seized, over 29 scam compounds across Asia, Africa and the Middle East dismantled, and more than 2,200 trafficked workers freed from compounds.

The release, citing Gallup, said 10% of US adults experienced a scam in the past year and that Americans lost an estimated $68 billion to scams in 2025 alone.
