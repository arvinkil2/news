In shipping regulation, an Indian seafarers union has urged the International Maritime Organization to act over fake ship registries it says are endangering seafarers, Marine Insight reported on October 24. The item is single sourced in this window and is carried at headline level, with no registry count or IMO response published in the reporting read.

In Nigeria, a Rivers State community hijacked a crude oil vessel belonging to Ibeto in an incident BusinessDay on October 26 described as a return of militancy in the area. The item is single sourced in this window, with no volume, crew or production figure published in the reporting read, and it sits alongside the regulator data in today's Nigeria petrol story showing how tight domestic supply already is.

In Sudan, Al Jazeera published a feature on October 25 on artisanal gold mining as one of the few livelihoods left as the war destroys farming and livestock work, centred on the death of 16 year old miner Altayeb Alhadi. It is a reported feature, not market data, and no production or export figure is carried here from it. Its market relevance is structural: informal digging of this kind is a large, uncounted share of Sudanese gold supply.

In the United States, the Columbus, New Mexico port of entry was scheduled to reopen to cattle imports from Mexico today, October 26, under the USDA phased reopening announced on October 20, with equine imports resuming through Santa Teresa the same day. No crossing volumes for the first day had been reported as of writing, so the reopening is noted as scheduled for today, not yet as measured supply. Yesterday's cattle story carries the background and the 750 head a day starting expectation.

In Brazil, Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva won a record fourth presidential term in Sunday's runoff, a result the politics beat leads today. For commodities the relevance is continuity rather than a new policy: Brazil is a first order exporter of soybeans, coffee, sugar and iron ore, and no change to farm, mining or energy policy was in the commodities pool read for this edition, so no commodity claim is made here beyond the result itself.
