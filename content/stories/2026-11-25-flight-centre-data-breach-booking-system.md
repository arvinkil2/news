Flight Centre Travel said it is investigating unusual activity in a third-party accommodation booking system used by its Flight Centre, Travel Associates and Cruiseabout leisure brands and by some independent agents. Flight bookings were not affected. The company said an initial review showed most accessed records contained only customer names, with some also holding basic travel information, and that a very small percentage held more sensitive data such as limited passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, birth dates or, in rare cases, health or mobility information.

Flight Centre said it has found no evidence that customer credit card, payment or bank account details were accessed. Its shares fell about 2 percent on the news.
