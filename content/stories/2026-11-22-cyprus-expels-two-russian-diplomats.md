Cyprus has expelled two Russian diplomats, with local authorities saying they were engaged in espionage, Politico reported, citing Cypriot officials. The country's security services uncovered a network that allegedly tried to obtain secret information about the security of Cyprus and the European Union.

The network also allegedly sought access to information on technologies that EU sanctions bar from transfer to Russia, according to Politico's sources. The expulsions follow Denmark's decision on November 20 to declare a Russian military attaché persona non grata in a case Copenhagen links to a foiled assassination plot, and Austria's expulsion of three Russian diplomats on espionage suspicions in May.
