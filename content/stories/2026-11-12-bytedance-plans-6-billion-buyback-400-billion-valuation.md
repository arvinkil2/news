ByteDance plans to repurchase up to $6 billion in shares from current investors at a valuation of approximately $400 billion, according to The Information, citing a shareholder letter and a person with knowledge of the matter. The valuation is 20 percent higher than the figure used in its previous buyback one year ago.

The increase follows the agreement ByteDance completed with the Trump administration in January, which prevented a ban of TikTok in the United States. Under that agreement, ByteDance sold an 80 percent stake in TikTok's US data security operation to a joint venture owned by American entities and retained control of the app's revenue generating operations.
