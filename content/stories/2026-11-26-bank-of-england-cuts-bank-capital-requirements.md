The Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee has completed its review of the bank capital framework and, with the Prudential Regulation Authority, will consult on lower minimum requirements related to domestic lending, leaving system-wide capital requirements for the major UK banks consistent with an updated benchmark of around 13 percent. Taking the risk-weighted and leverage proposals together, Tier 1 requirements will fall by an amount equivalent to around 50 basis points of risk-weighted assets in normal times, with further reductions in the capital banks must hold through stress scenarios, while the UK countercyclical capital buffer stays at its neutral setting of 2 percent. The Committee warned that reducing requirements much further would risk financial instability, and flagged that AI-related financing, with hyperscaler bond issuance set to exceed $305 billion in 2026, is making stress more likely to propagate across the system.
