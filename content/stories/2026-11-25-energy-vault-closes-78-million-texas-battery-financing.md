Energy Vault Holdings has closed $78 million in construction and bridge financing for the SOSA Energy Center, its 150 MW, 300 MWh battery energy storage system in Madison County, Texas, the company announced on November 23. The financing is non-recourse. The bridge portion will be repaid on project completion with an already committed $80 million preferred equity investment, and together with a draw on the OIC preferred facility the stack is intended to carry SOSA through construction and into commercial operation.

SOSA represents an approximately $100 million total investment. Construction began in the fourth quarter of 2025, civil work is complete and a substation has been installed, with major equipment expected on site in the coming months. Commercial operations are expected in the third quarter of 2027, selling power and ancillary services into the ERCOT North region on a merchant basis, without a contracted buyer.

SOSA was the first energy storage asset formally acquired and placed into development under Asset Vault, Energy Vault's platform for owning and operating storage and AI infrastructure assets, a shift from selling storage systems to holding them. The project is estimated to generate $9.3 million in tax revenue for the local school district and Madison County over ten years.
