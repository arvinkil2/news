Iran's semi-official Mehr News Agency reported on Monday that the air defence network was activated in Isfahan, in central Iran, Yonhap reported. Isfahan hosts the facility that converts uranium ore into the uranium hexafluoride gas used for enrichment, and the International Atomic Energy Agency has said about half of Iran's enriched uranium stockpile is stored at the site. The report gave no reason for the activation and mentioned no attack, explosion, damage or casualties, and no Iranian official statement has been reported.
