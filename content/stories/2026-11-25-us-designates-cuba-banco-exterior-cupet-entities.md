The US Department of State has designated six Cuban persons, five entities and one individual, under Executive Order 14404, in an action issued on September 3, 2026 and published in the Federal Register on Tuesday. All property and interests in property of the designated persons subject to US jurisdiction are blocked.

The most consequential name is Banco Exterior de Cuba, the state bank in Havana's Plaza de la Revolucion district, established February 29, 2000, designated for operating in the financial services sector of the Cuban economy. Alongside it, Comercial CUPET S.A. of Havana, established September 18, 1991 and classified as a wholesaler of solid, liquid and gaseous fuels, was designated for operating in the energy sector, and Empresa Importadora de Abastecimiento para el Petroleo, known as ABAPET, was designated for being owned, controlled or directed by Union Cuba Petroleo.

Two further entities were designated for operating in the metals and mining sector: Empresa de Servicios Comandante Rene Ramos Latour, known as NICAROTEC, of Mayari, established January 31, 1977, and Empresa Importadora y Abastecedora del Niquel, known as CEXNI, of Moa, established December 31, 1994. The individual designated is Fidel Ernesto Castro Calis, born May 16, 1995, designated as an adult family member of Alejandro Castro Espin, whose property is blocked under the same order.

Taken with the companion notices published the same day, covering the state bank BICSA and four nickel research and services entities, the September actions move the Cuba programme from individual targets into the operating companies of the island's banking, fuel and nickel systems. Status: enacted (designations in force from September 3, 2026; published November 24, 2026).
