OpenAI has signed a six year agreement with TensorWave for 457 megawatts of dedicated, single tenant capacity powered by AMD Instinct MI455X processors in Helios rack scale systems across multiple data center locations, Datacenter Dynamics reported. Operations are expected to begin in the first half of 2027 and be fully deployed by early 2028, in what TensorWave called its largest deployment contract to date.

The contract advances OpenAI's previously announced partnership with AMD and lands weeks after TensorWave's lease at Fermi's Project Matador campus in Texas was terminated. TensorWave, which raised 350 million dollars in a Series B round in June 2026, says it has secured 2 gigawatts of long term data center capacity across the United States.
