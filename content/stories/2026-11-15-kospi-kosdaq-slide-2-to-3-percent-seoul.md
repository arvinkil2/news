South Korean stocks slid on Monday morning, with the Kospi and the Kosdaq both down between 2 and 3 percent by 10:25 a.m. in Seoul and foreign investors selling, Yonhap reported, after both indices had opened higher. At 9:25 a.m. the Kospi stood at 5,286.90, down 0.07 percent after opening up 0.28 percent at 5,305.40, and the Kosdaq was down 0.63 percent at 695.28, with foreigners net sellers of 155.8 billion won of Kospi shares at that point.

Yonhap tied the weak sentiment to the fallout from Jensen Huang's departure from Nvidia and to Friday's selloff in United States chip stocks, when the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 3.43 percent, Nvidia fell 2.01 percent and SK Hynix's United States listing fell 4.30 percent. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix were both lower in early Seoul trading.
