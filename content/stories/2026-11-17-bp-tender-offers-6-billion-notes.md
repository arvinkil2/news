BP has launched sixteen concurrent and separate cash tender offers, through BP Capital Markets America Inc. and BP Capital Markets p.l.c., to purchase for cash any and all of the outstanding US dollar notes of the series listed in its offer to purchase, subject to a maximum purchase condition capping aggregate consideration at $6 billion. Notes purchased in the offers will be retired and cancelled. The offers expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, on Monday, November 23, 2026, which is also the deadline for withdrawing tendered notes, and pricing is set off reference US Treasury securities plus fixed spreads specified for each series.
