One person was killed in a Russian ballistic strike on Kyiv on Thursday evening, the Kyiv City Military Administration said. Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko reported missile fragments burning in the courtyard of a residential complex in the Dniprovskyi district, windows blown out by the blast wave at another address, fragments falling near a second residential complex and a shop building destroyed, with the fire contained. UNIAN, reporting in Ukrainian, gave no injury count for the attack and named no missile type, and no Russian statement was reported.
