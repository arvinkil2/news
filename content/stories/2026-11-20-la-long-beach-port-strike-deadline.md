More than 110 US trade associations, including the National Retail Federation, the US Chamber of Commerce and the National Association of Manufacturers, urged the Pacific Maritime Association and ILWU Local 26 to stay at the table and keep cargo moving through Los Angeles and Long Beach. The contract extension covering about 250 port security workers expires at 12:01 a.m. Pacific time on Saturday, after more than a year of negotiations, and the two sides were due to meet again on Friday.

Local 26 members work security at terminals handling about 85 percent of cargo at Long Beach and 33 percent at Los Angeles, according to the PMA. The associations warned that a stoppage would hit holiday shipments and supply chains, and that other longshore workers honouring picket lines could widen the shutdown well beyond the security workforce.
