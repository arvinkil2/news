Abu Dhabi National Oil Company has set the December official selling price of its benchmark Murban crude at 4 dollars a barrel against Dubai quotes, down from 11 dollars a barrel above Dubai in November, according to a pricing document seen by Reuters on Tuesday. The 7 dollar reduction is the price Abu Dhabi charges Asian refiners over the Dubai benchmark, and the December price is quoted under the prompt month Platts Dubai framework ADNOC adopted from November 1, replacing its Murban futures based method.

The cut lands in a softening market. Brent crude fell below 90 dollars a barrel on Tuesday for the first time since August, covered elsewhere in today's edition, and Bloomberg reported that a jump in Middle East supply is stirring rivalry among the region's exporters. A lower Murban differential defends Abu Dhabi's market share into December loadings rather than holding the premium November buyers paid.
