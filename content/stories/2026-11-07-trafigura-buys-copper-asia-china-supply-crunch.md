Trafigura Group has been buying large volumes of copper to increase its sales in Asia as local supplies dwindle, Bloomberg reported on Saturday. The buying adds to a scramble for metal across the region that has pushed premiums for spot cargoes to record highs.

The purchases point to tightening physical supply in China, the world's largest copper consumer, where traders are competing for available cargoes rather than waiting on new mine or smelter output.
