**Latest, October 26:** Bon Secours Mercy Health announced on Monday that it plans to acquire Surgery Partners in a deal valued at $5.7 billion, STAT reported. Surgery Partners is a publicly traded chain of more than 200 ambulatory surgery centers, short-stay hospitals and multispecialty physician practices in 30 states. The deal is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, and STAT reported it would make the nonprofit Catholic system one of the largest ambulatory surgery center operators in the country and deepen its ties with Bain Capital, Surgery Partners' private equity backer. The announcement converts the late-stage talks reported on Sunday into an agreed acquisition, at a valuation below the roughly $6 billion figure attached to the talks.

Bon Secours Mercy Health is in late-stage talks to buy a majority stake in Surgery Partners, the Financial Times reported, citing people familiar with the matter. The talks value the group at about 20 dollars a share, or about 6 billion dollars on Bloomberg's headline figure. The report describes a majority stake rather than a full acquisition, and the talks are not a signed deal.
