The Élysée has unveiled the contents of the second French edition of the Choose France summit, held on Monday at the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris. In total, 273 industrial projects will be presented, 81 of them strictly new, for an aggregate €42.5 billion. The presidency specified that the total combines projects already made public during the year with brand new commitments; the 81 new projects represent €14.9 billion, up from €9.2 billion of new projects at the first domestic edition a year ago, when 151 projects worth €30.4 billion were showcased.

Two programmes account for more than 40 percent of the package: a €9.5 billion investment plan from ADP, the Paris airport operator, financed largely by fees charged to airlines, and an €8 billion programme from RTE, the electricity transmission grid operator at the centre of France's electrification effort. Digital projects account for €4.8 billion across roughly forty projects including data centers, and Lyon based Elyse Energy will present a €1.6 billion sustainable aviation fuel plant in the Pyrénées-Atlantiques, whose public inquiry must conclude in mid December, with commissioning hoped for in 2031. L'Oréal plans nearly €300 million to renovate several sites.

About 250 executives from CAC 40 groups, mid caps and start ups are expected alongside Economy Minister Roland Lescure and Industry Minister Sébastien Martin, and about 30 percent of the projects receive public support, notably through the France 2030 plan. President Emmanuel Macron separately announced €350 million of state support for civil aviation research during a visit to Airbus in Toulouse on Monday.
