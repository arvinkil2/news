UBS downgraded Japanese equities to neutral from attractive, saying risk and reward look more balanced after a strong 2026 run, with the TOPIX still up 10 percent and the Nikkei 225 up 15 percent this year despite deep losses in October and early November. The bank expects the market to trade broadly range-bound until there is greater clarity on AI capital spending, warning that fears of tighter US rules on data centre construction raise doubts over demand for Japanese semiconductor equipment, components and materials, and that a moderation in AI spending could slow earnings growth beyond 2027.

UBS still forecasts TOPIX earnings growth of 12 percent in fiscal 2026 and 10 percent in fiscal 2027, and cited a resilient US economy, stable domestic growth and ongoing corporate reforms as supports through 2027. Within Japan it favours the large banks, where Bank of Japan rate hikes should support lending spreads, and stays selective on AI-exposed names after the recent correction.
