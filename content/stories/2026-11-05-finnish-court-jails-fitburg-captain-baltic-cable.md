The Helsinki District Court sentenced the Fitburg's Russian captain to three years and six months in prison and an Azerbaijani crew member to two years and ten months on Thursday, finding that the two acted intentionally when the ship dragged an anchor chain and anchor shank along the seabed for about 130 kilometres, damaging two subsea telecommunications cables between Helsinki and Estonia. Finland seized the Saint Vincent and Grenadines flagged vessel on December 31 last year while it was sailing from St Petersburg to Israel, and the court ordered the pair to pay about 1.9 million euros in damages jointly, with the captain to pay about 85,000 euros more.

The captain will appeal, his lawyer said, and both men have denied wrongdoing and challenged Finland's jurisdiction in the case. The ship has since been released.
