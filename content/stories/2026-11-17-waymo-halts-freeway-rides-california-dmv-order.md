California's Department of Motor Vehicles issued the restrictions on November 11, made public on Tuesday, after three Waymo vehicles entered an active construction closure on US Route 101 near Burlingame and a separate incident involved California Highway Patrol vehicles on Highway 280 in San Francisco. The department said Waymo's driverless freeway operations pose an imminent hazard to public safety because of recurring incursions into active construction zones, despite a prior restriction and corrective actions after similar incidents on May 18, and the bar stays in place until Waymo demonstrates effective corrective actions. Waymo said it has temporarily restricted freeway operations in its other markets to evaluate its performance, while surface-street service continues at more than half a million trips a week.
