Contract signings to buy existing homes fell in every region, the National Association of Realtors said, led by the West, down 9.2 percent on the month and 15.0 percent on the year, followed by the South, the Midwest and the Northeast. "Market conditions weakened in September due to fast-rising mortgage rates," NAR chief economist Lawrence Yun said, adding that buyers and sellers may simply be holding back until mortgage rates move favorably, while the limited number of distressed properties is keeping prices steady.
