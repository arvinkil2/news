Germany’s ZEW expectations index fell 6.6 points to 28.1 in October, below the 30 points analysts had expected, while the current conditions index rose 11.2 points to minus 35.9, the strongest reading since May 2023. Eurozone expectations fell 7.3 points to 18.5, also below the 23.6 expected, with the bloc’s current conditions little changed at minus 14.8. ZEW president Achim Wambach said the German economy is slowly moving out of stagnation but the recovery is not self-perpetuating, as high energy prices press on real incomes, raise production costs and keep inflation higher for longer.
