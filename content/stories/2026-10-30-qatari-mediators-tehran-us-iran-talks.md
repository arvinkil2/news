Qatari mediators held talks in Tehran for more than 12 hours on Friday with the Iranian side and stayed in regular contact with the White House during the session, Axios reported, citing diplomatic sources. The mediators are pushing Washington and Tehran to agree to reciprocal steps, to be implemented over a few days, that would return them to direct talks on Iran's nuclear programme and other issues, with the talks set to continue in Doha on Saturday.

The sticking point is the order of concessions: Washington insists Iran make nuclear concessions as part of resuming talks, while Tehran insists the United States first lift its naval blockade, release frozen funds and allow Iran to sell oil. As a confidence building trade, the Qataris are trying to persuade Iran to let United Nations inspectors visit its bombed nuclear sites, with the United States responding with some sanctions relief, and Egypt, Turkey, Iraq, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia have also been urging Iran to resume negotiations before the midterms.
