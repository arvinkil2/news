Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton will also serve as acting national cyber director while Kirsten Davies awaits Senate confirmation to lead the office permanently, Axios reported, citing a White House official. The official said the strong support Davies received convinced President Trump to nominate her for the permanent role rather than keep her in an acting capacity, a nomination Axios first reported on November 19.

The arrangement gives Clayton, the country’s top intelligence official, responsibility for coordinating cybersecurity policy across the federal government on top of the intelligence role, as outages and reported cyberattacks continue to expose weaknesses in United States digital infrastructure.
