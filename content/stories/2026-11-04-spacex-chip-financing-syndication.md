Goldman Sachs and Apollo Global Management are in talks with investors to sell portions of a roughly $40 billion financing deal for SpaceX's artificial intelligence chips, Bloomberg News reported on Wednesday, in a report carried by Reuters.

The financing is the Apollo-led package first reported in October, assembled to fund SpaceX's purchases of Nvidia chips for its AI buildout. Selling portions to outside investors would move the deal from arrangement into syndication, the stage at which pricing and final allocations are set by demand.
