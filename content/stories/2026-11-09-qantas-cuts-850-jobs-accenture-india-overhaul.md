Qantas will cut 850 local jobs, most of them in back-end systems, processes and data, and transfer some services to Accenture's large India-based team under an AI-driven overhaul the airline says will make it more productive and modern. An initial group of 500 Qantas staff will be offered roles with Accenture, though the airline declined to say how many would need to move to India, and consultation with staff and unions has begun ahead of final decisions, with the first changes taking effect by the middle of next year.

The Australian Council of Trade Unions called the plan a bitter blow and said the workers losing their jobs would be expected to train their replacements, urging the government to tighten labour hire rules. The overhaul sits alongside a separate engineering restructuring announced last week that would move up to 700 workers into a new joint venture or new roles.
