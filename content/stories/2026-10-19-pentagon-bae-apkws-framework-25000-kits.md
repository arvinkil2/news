The Department of War announced on Monday a framework agreement with BAE Systems covering procurement of up to 25,000 Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System guidance sections a year for up to seven years, pending congressional authorization and appropriations. The framework also sets out a path to surge output to 35,000 kits a year for international allies, unlocked as partner nations formalize orders through the Foreign Military Sales program. APKWS kits convert unguided 2.75-inch rockets into laser-guided munitions fielded by all four services, and Defense News reported BAE will increase production while the two sides work out a multiyear contract, with no deal value announced.
