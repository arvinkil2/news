Reliance Industries plans to invest about 430 billion rupees ($4.59 billion) in two phases to build an integrated underground coal gasification complex in Andhra Pradesh, which would turn deep coal reserves into usable gas without conventional mining, according to a government document seen by Reuters. The first phase, about 30 billion rupees, covers exploration, geological assessments and pilot demonstrations to establish technical and commercial feasibility, and the second phase, about 400 billion rupees, proceeds only after the first phase succeeds and the necessary regulatory approvals are received.

The document said the project could eventually attract investments of up to 2.7 trillion rupees over its lifetime and generate around 5,000 direct and 35,000 indirect jobs. Reliance did not immediately respond to Reuters' request for comment.
