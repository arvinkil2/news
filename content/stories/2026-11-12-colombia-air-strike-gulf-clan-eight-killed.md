Eight people were killed in an air strike by Colombia's armed forces targeting the Gulf Clan in Antioquia province, in the country's north west, among them Haminton Mosquera, a senior member of the gang and one of 10 priority targets for the security forces, President Abelardo de la Espriella said. De la Espriella, who was elected in June after campaigning on air strikes against criminal gangs and said he personally authorised this bombing, said Mosquera was suspected of ordering attacks on the security forces, including the murder of a police officer in Taraza last year, and vowed that the offensive will not stop. The Gulf Clan, Colombia's largest criminal gang, traffics cocaine to the United States and Europe and was added by the United States to its list of foreign terrorist organisations last year.
