The Treasury and the Internal Revenue Service announced the qualified opportunity zone designations for all 50 states, the territories and the District of Columbia. All 6,576 eligible census tracts nominated by governors and the Mayor of the District of Columbia were designated, of which 2,594 are rural tracts eligible for increased benefits. The designations take effect on January 1, 2027 and last ten years.

The release said the Working Families Tax Cuts permanently renewed the opportunity zone incentive, added enhanced incentives for investment in eligible rural communities and set a process for redesignating zones every ten years. Treasury said more than $110 billion of private sector investment has been made since the incentives were established in 2017.
