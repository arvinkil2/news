At least eight people were killed and at least 17 injured, four of them critically and three of them firefighters, when a three-storey residential building on Flatbush Avenue in Brooklyn collapsed at about 4 p.m. on Wednesday beside a lot being excavated for a construction project. Mayor Zohran Mamdani said about 250 firefighters were working through extremely dangerous conditions, and the fire department said on Thursday morning it was still hopeful of locating survivors.

The cause is not yet known and Department of Buildings investigators have begun an investigation. The Guardian, citing NY1, reported that the department recorded multiple issues with the project next door in May, including work carried out while a stop work order was in place.
