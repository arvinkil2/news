New World Development Co. suspended trading in its Hong Kong shares on Friday morning pending an announcement to investors, a month after the developer reported a record annual loss of $3.6 billion, its worst ever performance, after a writedown on an aborted deal with the Hong Kong airport. The company is considering a rights issue to shore up its finances after Bain Capital decided against investing in the property company, according to people familiar with the situation.

The rights issue could seek as much as HK$25 billion ($3.2 billion), and the Cheng family, New World's controlling shareholder, intends to participate and inject fresh capital, the people said. The developer has been carrying a heavy debt pile that pushed it into eleventh-hour negotiations with bank lenders last year, and its shares have fallen 33 percent this year.
