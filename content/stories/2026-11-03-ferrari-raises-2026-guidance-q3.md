Ferrari raised its full year 2026 guidance after a third quarter in which net revenues rose 7 percent to 1,896 million euros, operating profit rose 12 percent to 563 million euros and net profit rose 13 percent to 431 million euros. The company now expects 2026 net revenues of about 7.65 billion euros, up from about 7.60 billion previously, adjusted EBITDA of at least 3.00 billion euros and industrial free cash flow of up to 1.65 billion euros.

Chief executive Benedetto Vigna said deliveries of the F80 supercar and personalisations, led by the Ferrari 296 Speciale family, supported the growth, and that the order book now reaches into 2028. The guidance upgrade rests on stronger personalisations than initially expected, lower than anticipated currency headwinds net of hedges, and second place in the Formula 1 championship.
