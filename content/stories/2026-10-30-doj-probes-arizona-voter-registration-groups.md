The Justice Department is investigating the Arizona voter registration work of Worker Power, Stand Indivisible AZ and When We Count, a registration program of the Democratic National Committee, the US Attorney's Office for the District of Arizona said on Friday, and it has referred the matter to the FBI. US Attorney Timothy Courchaine wrote to Maricopa County Recorder Justin Heap and Secretary of State Adrian Fontes asking for a review of registrations the groups submitted, saying they may have violated state law on registering ineligible persons, after an October 22 undercover video in which a man posing as a non-citizen asked whether he could register. The Democratic National Committee said it knew of no case of its fellows registering a non-citizen and called the allegations fabricated, and former Maricopa County Recorder Helen Purcell, a Republican, noted that registration closed weeks ago and that registrations are checked for eligibility before any ballot is issued.
