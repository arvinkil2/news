Vodafone raised its full year guidance alongside half year results on Tuesday, lifting expected FY27 adjusted EBITDAaL to 13.2 to 13.4 billion euros from 13.0 to 13.3 billion and adjusted free cash flow to 2.8 to 3.0 billion euros from 2.6 to 2.9 billion. First half revenue rose 11.4 percent to 21.9 billion euros, helped by the consolidation of Safaricom, VodafoneThree and Telekom Romania, with organic service revenue up 5.8 percent and adjusted EBITDAaL up 6.4 percent on an organic basis to 6.5 billion euros; the interim dividend rises 2.5 percent to 2.306 eurocents a share. The group still reported a 3.6 billion euro loss for the period on non-cash charges, including an impairment in Germany, where chief executive Margherita Della Valle said performance is not yet where it needs to be.
