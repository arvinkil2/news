The UN Human Rights Office said on Friday it had documented at least 306 deaths and hundreds of injuries from drone strikes in Sudan in the first three weeks of October, in Kordofan, Darfur, Blue Nile, Khartoum and other states, with strikes attributed to both the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces and hitting homes, markets, hospitals, displacement sites, power stations and water sources. The office cited strikes on displacement shelters in Ghubeish and El Nuhud in West Kordofan that killed at least 25 civilians between October 6 and 10, a strike near a displacement site in Abu Shendi in Blue Nile that killed at least 31 people on October 9, and a repeat strike on a transport truck in Dibab, West Kordofan, that killed at least 139 people on October 18. UN Human Rights Chief Volker Türk called on both parties to commit to a ceasefire and said broader peace efforts must address the continued influx of arms fuelling the conflict.
