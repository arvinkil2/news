Wallenius Wilhelmsen has reached an agreement with Jinling Shipyard (Jiangsu) in China to build four pure car and truck carriers of 9,900 car equivalent units each, with deliveries beginning in mid-2030. The agreement carries options for four more vessels in 2031 and another four in 2032; if all are exercised, total capital expenditure is expected to reach about $1.5 billion. The ships are of the company's Shaper Class design, with dual-fuel LNG engines, hoistable decks and adaptable cargo spaces for vehicles, trucks and other rolling equipment.

The order lifts the fleet renewal program Wallenius Wilhelmsen launched in 2023, which it describes as the largest in its history, to 26 vessels including options. The Oslo-based group, which operates about 130 vessels on 15 trade routes, announced it after completing a private placement earlier in October, and its shipping services chief operating officer, Xavier Leroi, said the expansion reflects confidence in the long-term fundamentals of the market it serves.
