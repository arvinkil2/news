QazaqGaz, Kazakhstan's national gas company, has signed a contract with a Chinese consortium to build a gas processing plant with an annual capacity of 2.5 billion cubic metres, processing gas from the Kashagan field. Under the turnkey arrangement the consortium, which comprises China National Chemical Engineering Sixth Construction Company, China Wuhuan Engineering Corporation, AVC Production and C-NRG, will manage every phase of the project from design to final operational testing.

The contract's value was not disclosed; in 2024 the project was estimated to cost up to $2.5 billion. Eurasianet described the award as the latest in a series of major wins for Chinese companies in the region's energy sector this year, including a $6.1 billion biorefinery project in Uzbekistan and several earlier energy contracts in Kazakhstan.
