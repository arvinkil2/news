CISA published a batch of industrial control advisories on Siemens products on October 22, covering SIMATIC S7-1500 controllers and modules, RUGGEDCOM APE1808 devices and CROSSBOW station access controllers, and Siemens Industrial Edge devices. Individually routine, the batch is a reminder that the operational technology layer beneath data centres and factories carries the same patching burden as the web layer under PHP2Shell attack this week.

CISA also published guidance titled Managing AI Token Consumption for Operational Resilience, an orphan primary item in the pool. A cybersecurity agency treating AI token consumption as an operational resilience question, rather than a billing question, marks how completely inference cost has become infrastructure risk.

Developer platform Railway has urged all users to revoke their secret keys after a cyberattack, TechCrunch reported. In a week of supply chain compromises at LiteLLM, ConnectWise and GitLab, a blanket key revocation at a developer platform is the defensive move becoming standard practice.

FCC chair Brendan Carr has warned media outlets not to run what he called fake polls, Ars Technica reported, in a warning tied to President Trump's repeated complaints about polling. The intervention puts the broadcast regulator inside an election information dispute weeks before the midterms.

Comcast lost more customers in its latest results and claimed rival internet providers' cheap prices are irrational, Ars Technica reported. The complaint is a tell: fibre and fixed wireless competition has reached the point where the largest US cable operator is arguing about price discipline in public.

Innio has signed a 620 megawatt gas engine order with an undisclosed US data centre developer, Datacenter Dynamics reported, one of the largest single behind the meter generation orders yet disclosed and further evidence that developers are buying their own power rather than waiting for grid queues.

Crusoe will develop a 106 megawatt data centre site in Paraguay, Datacenter Dynamics reported, taking the American AI infrastructure builder into South American hydro powered capacity as its international expansion accelerates beyond Europe.

Specialising the vocabulary of small language models can cut inference cost by 77 percent in real applications, according to a Hugging Face blog post from Dharma AI on its VocabX work. The claim is the publisher's, carried here at roundup length, and if it holds in production it points at vocabulary design, not just model size, as an inference cost lever.

Akashi Data Center has secured 31.2 million dollars in financing from the Eurasian Development Bank for a data centre project in Astana, Kazakhstan, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The Tier IV quality campus is planned at 100 megawatts and more than 4,200 racks across 55,000 square metres on 11 hectares, with about 10 megawatts in a first phase scheduled to launch in 2027 and total project investment of 354 million dollars.
