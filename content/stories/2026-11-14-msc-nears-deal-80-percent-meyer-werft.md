MSC, the Swiss based container shipping group, is close to securing the right to acquire the roughly 80 percent of Meyer Werft held by the German federal government and the state of Lower Saxony, and an agreement could be signed as early as next Tuesday, Der Spiegel reported on Saturday. The two governments rescued the Papenburg shipbuilder at the end of 2024, taking stakes of around 40 percent each and contributing a combined €400 million ($441 million) in equity, while the Meyer family, which founded the business in 1795, kept the remaining 20 percent.

The family is working alongside MSC on a joint ownership arrangement that would allow it to increase its stake, and a family spokesperson described the discussions as “very constructive,” Spiegel reported. Neither MSC nor either government has confirmed the report, and no price was given.
