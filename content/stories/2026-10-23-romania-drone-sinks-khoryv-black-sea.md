A drone strike sank a cargo ship off the Romanian Black Sea coast on Friday, killing at least one sailor with three still missing, and Romanian President Nicusor Dan accused Russia of escalating its attacks on NATO's eastern flank, Reuters reported. Romania's naval authority said the Comoros-flagged Khoryv, crewed by 13 Ukrainian and Azeri sailors, sent a distress signal 13 nautical miles off the port of Constanta, just beyond Romanian territorial waters, and nine sailors were rescued. The sinking extends a sudden two-week surge in attacks on commercial ships off Romania and Bulgaria, in which at least seven cargo ships have been hit and some 30 sailors reported dead or missing, around the Sulina Canal route Ukraine relies on for its grain exports.
