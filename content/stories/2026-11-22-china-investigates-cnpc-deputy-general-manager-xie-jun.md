China's Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and the National Supervisory Commission said on Sunday that Xie Jun, a deputy general manager of China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC), has been placed under disciplinary review and supervisory investigation on suspicion of serious violations of discipline and law, the standard formulation used in Chinese corruption cases. The announcement gave no detail on the alleged conduct. CNPC is China's largest oil and gas producer and the parent company of PetroChina, its publicly traded arm.
