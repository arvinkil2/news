Nyxoah said on November 23 that it has initiated enforcement of a decision issued on November 16, 2026 by the Munich local division of the Unified Patent Court in its infringement case over European Patent 2 760 534. The decision, issued by default, was against Inspire Medical Systems, Inc. and Inspire Medical Systems Europe GmbH. Inspire filed an 8-K under Item 8.01 on November 24, accession 0001104659-26-131325.

The orders are broad. Inspire must stop offering, selling, using, importing or storing products that use the patented invention in Germany, France, the Netherlands and Belgium. It must provide information on offers and sales of those products since March 31, 2021, recall, remove and destroy products placed on the market since that date, pay damages in an amount to be set in separate proceedings, and pay the legal costs.

Both companies sell neuromodulation treatments for obstructive sleep apnea. Nyxoah's Genio system has been CE marked since 2019 and was approved by the FDA in August 2025. Inspire is the larger, established competitor, which is why a four country sales bar matters even before any damages figure exists.

Two cautions belong with the news. The judgment is a default decision, and the release announcing enforcement is Nyxoah's, the winning party's, account of it. The size of any damages award, and how much of Inspire's European business the infringing products represent, are not stated in the release.
