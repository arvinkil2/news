Hanwha Defense USA has signed a 269 million dollar contract with Tote Services to build a medium landing ship for the US Navy, Reuters reported. The vessel is 2,687 tons and 100 metres long and is to be used for operations in disputed waters, including in the Indo-Pacific region, Hanwha said in a statement. The ship is to be built at the Philly Shipyard in the United States, the first order since Hanwha acquired the facility in December 2024, and the report gives no delivery timeline.
