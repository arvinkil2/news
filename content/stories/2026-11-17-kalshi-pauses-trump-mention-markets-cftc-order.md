Kalshi plans to pause indefinitely its prediction contracts tied to President Trump's public remarks, the so-called mention markets that let users wager on what the president might say on specific dates, after the Commodity Futures Trading Commission ordered the exchange on Friday to suspend the contracts temporarily, the Wall Street Journal reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Kalshi had already cut its overall mention-market catalogue by more than half, and the contracts produce modest volumes compared with its economic and political markets, but they have drawn outsized attention through viral social media streams.

The intervention reflects market integrity concerns and intensifying federal oversight of insider trading and manipulation risks on prediction platforms, following a settlement earlier this year in which a former presidential teleprompter operator was alleged to have used nonpublic information to place winning wagers on Trump's speeches. Kalshi intends to keep other administration-related markets running, including contracts on Vice President Vance's remarks, possible departures of senior officials and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's tenure, the Journal reported.
