Thailand's Securities and Exchange Commission has filed a criminal complaint with the Economic Crime Suppression Division against BG Limited, operator of the Bitget platform, alleging it operated a digital asset exchange without a licence from 2022 to 2025, charging fees and promoting its services in Thai through its website and social media, and the regulator has notified the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society to block access to Bitget's website, domains and app from December 5, 2026, advising investors to act on assets held there before that date. The complaint also names Kritsana Kritsananuwat, accused of helping promote Bitget through Facebook and YouTube, and the case is now at the inquiry stage, so no offence has been established.

The timing puts Bitget's lawful entry route in doubt: a Bitget-linked company, BTGT Management (Thailand), is due to complete its takeover of GMO-Z.com Cryptonomics, a licensed Thai exchange and brokerage, on November 30 after the seller, GMO Internet, delayed the transfer from October 31 for regulatory approval work, and the SEC has not said whether the complaint affects that deal.
