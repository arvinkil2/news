The FAO Food Price Index averaged 133.9 points in October, down 1.2 percent from its revised September level and 5.9 percent above a year earlier, the Food and Agriculture Organization said on Friday. The cereal index fell 4.5 percent to 116.8 points as wheat prices declined after four consecutive monthly increases on subdued demand and an improving production outlook in Argentina, while the rice index rose 1.6 percent. Meat fell 1.2 percent to 125.5 points and dairy fell 1.1 percent to 117.8 points, while vegetable oils rose 0.3 percent to 199.2 points.

Sugar moved the other way: the sugar price index rose 6.6 percent to 121.3 points, its fourth consecutive monthly increase and its highest level since December 2024, driven by rainfall disruption to sugarcane harvesting and crushing in Brazil's Centre-South region and concern over production in India, the European Union and Thailand.
