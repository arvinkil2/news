The FCC voted on Thursday to ban Chinese telecom firms from direct access to US phone numbers, a measure Reuters said is designed to prevent scam related robocalls. The action bars China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom, which the FCC had previously barred from providing international telecommunications services to the United States, and it also bars IP-enabled service providers that use equipment from companies on the FCC's national security risk list, including Huawei and ZTE. For voice over internet protocol providers, direct access to numbers is much cheaper than the alternatives and makes it easier to offer advanced features, and Reuters reported that other measures still under consideration include barring Chinese telecom firms from connecting to US networks and from operating data centres in the United States.
