## Latest

China's Ministry of Commerce on Sunday called on the United States to revoke the restrictions on Chinese telecom operators' direct access to US phone numbers, saying the move violates the principles of non-discrimination and fair competition. A ministry spokesperson, responding to a media query, said the FCC had abused its regulatory powers and overstretched the concept of national security, making arbitrary accusations against Chinese telecom companies without factual basis and maliciously undermining their operations in the United States. The spokesperson said the FCC has continued to push to decouple and sever supply chains between the two countries in telecommunications, communications, testing and certification, and that such moves run counter to the consensus reached by the two heads of state and undermine the stability of China-US economic and trade relations. Beijing urged Washington to rescind the measures and resolve the issue through consultations, and said China will take necessary measures to safeguard its own interests, without specifying what those measures would be.

The FCC voted on Thursday to ban Chinese telecom firms from direct access to US phone numbers, a measure Reuters said is designed to prevent scam related robocalls. The action bars China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom, which the FCC had previously barred from providing international telecommunications services to the United States, and it also bars IP-enabled service providers that use equipment from companies on the FCC's national security risk list, including Huawei and ZTE. For voice over internet protocol providers, direct access to numbers is much cheaper than the alternatives and makes it easier to offer advanced features, and Reuters reported that other measures still under consideration include barring Chinese telecom firms from connecting to US networks and from operating data centres in the United States.
