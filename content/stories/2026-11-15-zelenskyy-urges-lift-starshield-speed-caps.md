President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Sunday that operating speed restrictions on Starshield terminals along the front line and in the occupied territories are still in force, and that Ukrainian interceptor drones lose connection with their operators at 161 kilometres per hour and cannot catch the Russian jet powered drones now targeting electricity substations. He said he had asked his officials to work as closely as possible with the US government and Elon Musk’s company to have the restrictions lifted, saying the protection of lives and of Ukraine’s energy infrastructure ahead of winter depends on it. The caps on Starshield, the military grade Starlink service provided to Ukraine by the US government, were first identified in October; Musk has said the US government controls the service, while President Trump has denied responsibility and said he told Musk to restore it, and neither answered Sunday’s appeal in the reporting.
