President Donald Trump said his deportation policy was part of the reason Republicans lost the midterm elections, telling CBS's 60 Minutes in an interview that aired on Sunday: "I think it might be too tough, yeah." He said he thinks he could reach an agreement with the new Democratic majority, adding: "The deal would be we would work something out," and that he was open to a deal letting people stay if they are good people working and, in some cases, paying taxes, while people he described as killers and drug dealers should be deported.

Trump gave no specifics on what a deal would look like, and shortly after taping the interview on Friday he named Stephen Miller, the architect of his hard line immigration agenda, as his new chief of staff. Senate Democratic Leader Chuck Schumer said on social media after the interview aired that putting the man responsible for the crackdown in charge of the White House was a promotion, not a course correction.
