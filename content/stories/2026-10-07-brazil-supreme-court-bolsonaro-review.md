Brazil's Supreme Court will take up Jair Bolsonaro's request for a criminal review of his 27-year-and-three-month coup-attempt sentence only after the October 25 presidential runoff, in which his eldest son Flavio Bolsonaro faces incumbent Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva. Rapporteur Kassio Nunes Marques, who holds one of the court's 11 seats since his 2020 appointment by Bolsonaro himself, said a decision before the vote could interfere with the electoral process, and intends to bring the case to the full court on no fixed date. Bolsonaro's lawyers filed the review last week, seeking to annul the sentence he is serving under house arrest, citing alleged chain-of-custody irregularities in digital evidence, missing original phone files, and the fallout from the financial scandal touching some justices including trial judge Alexandre de Moraes.
