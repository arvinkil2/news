Stellantis said third quarter net revenues rose 1 percent to 37.6 billion euros, with Enlarged Europe and South America each up 3 percent, North America down 3 percent, the Middle East and Africa down 19 percent and Asia Pacific down 7 percent, on consolidated shipments of 1.286 million vehicles, down 1 percent. The net loss narrowed to 0.5 billion euros from a prior year quarter hit by significant items, and adjusted operating income was 0.3 billion euros, a margin of 0.7 percent, up 50 basis points from a year earlier, with every region except Enlarged Europe in positive territory.

The cash picture is slower to turn. Industrial free cash flow was negative 4.0 billion euros in the quarter, an improvement of 0.3 billion euros on a year earlier, and industrial available liquidity closed at 42.7 billion euros, 26 percent of trailing twelve month revenues and inside the company's 25 to 30 percent target range. Stellantis reaffirmed its 2026 financial guidance and said execution of its FaSTLAne 2030 plan continues, with its value creation programme ramping up.
