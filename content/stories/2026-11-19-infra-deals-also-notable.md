**Exus Renewables closes $345 million.** Exus Renewables North America, an owner, developer and operator of utility-scale renewable energy and storage projects, has closed $345 million in senior secured holding company financing led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives, with PEI Global Partners as financial adviser. The financing will support construction costs and the development of new projects across its United States portfolio.

**Calibrant Energy raises $145 million, with a data centre battery.** Calibrant Energy, which develops and finances on-site energy projects for large commercial and industrial customers, closed a $145 million debt facility led by Investec, with Citi, Liberty Bank and Siemens Financial Services participating. The facility funds solar and battery storage across several United States power markets, including a 120 MWh battery storage system serving a hyperscale data centre customer in the PJM market.

**Altus Power buys 92 Arevon projects.** Altus Power acquired 92 projects across 13 states from Arevon, adding nearly 200 MW of solar capacity and 10 MWh of battery storage. Its operating portfolio reaches 1.7 GW across 33 states and Washington, DC, with the acquired projects expanding its presence in New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts and California.
