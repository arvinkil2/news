Senior Rapid Support Forces commander Siddiq Ander says he has defected from Sudan's paramilitary group, in video footage obtained by Al Jazeera and published on Friday. Ander accused relatives of RSF leader Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo, known as Hemedti, of tightly controlling the group's finances, weapons, troop movements and military promotions, and said anyone who speaks out faces expulsion, imprisonment or worse. Hemedti's brother, Abdul Rahim Hamdan Dagalo, is the RSF deputy commander.

The defection follows several others this year, including a lieutenant colonel who left on October 8 with 300 officers and soldiers, according to the same reporting. Al Jazeera does not say where Ander went or whether he joined the Sudanese Armed Forces, and neither the RSF nor the army is reported responding.
