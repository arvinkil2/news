South Korea's safety ministry issued an evacuation warning for Ulleung Island, off the country's east coast, at 7:52 a.m. local time on Tuesday after an aircraft entered South Korean airspace, and lifted the warning three minutes later, Reuters reported. The ministry at first said a Russian aircraft had entered the airspace. It later revised that assessment, saying an aircraft suspected to be North Korean had entered and then exited a danger zone near the island, and lifted the civil defence warning for the Ulleung area.

The Associated Press account matches the sequence: residents were first told a Russian aircraft had breached the airspace and were ordered to head to shelters, the evacuation order was then lifted, and the government later described the aircraft as possibly North Korean. No operator of the aircraft had confirmed the flight in the reporting available at the time of writing.
