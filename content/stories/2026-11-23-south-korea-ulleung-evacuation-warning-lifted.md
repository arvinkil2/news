**Latest:** The protest has been delivered. Kim Hak-jo, the defence ministry's director-general for international policy, summoned Colonel Nikolai Marchenko, Russia's defence attaché in Seoul, to the ministry on Tuesday afternoon, sternly protested and urged prevention of a recurrence, Yonhap reported. Kim said the repeated unauthorised entries into South Korea's air defence identification zone and that day's close approach could unnecessarily raise tension on the Korean Peninsula and in the region, and made clear they cannot be tolerated. The foreign ministry separately expressed regret to the Russian side through diplomatic channels. Marchenko declined to answer reporters' questions on arrival, and no Russian government response has been reported. Yonhap added that South Korean fighters including F-15Ks scrambled, and that the aircraft came close enough to Ulleungdo's airspace that they could have entered it within seconds before turning toward Japan.

Latest: Several Russian military aircraft entered South Korea's air defence identification zone over the East Sea on Tuesday morning and approached Ulleung Island to the point just before violating its airspace, Joint Chiefs of Staff spokesperson Jang Do-young said, in the Guardian's account. South Korean jets scrambled, the interior ministry's shelter alert ran from 7:52 a.m. to 7:55 a.m. local time, and the defence ministry said it would summon the Russian defence attaché on Tuesday afternoon to lodge a strong protest. North Gyeongsang province, which administers the island, said its second message describing a flying object presumed to be a North Korean aircraft was sent in error: it had taken the wording from routine drill messages and changed only the time.

South Korea's safety ministry issued an evacuation warning for Ulleung Island, off the country's east coast, at 7:52 a.m. local time on Tuesday after an aircraft entered South Korean airspace, and lifted the warning three minutes later, Reuters reported. The ministry at first said a Russian aircraft had entered the airspace. It later revised that assessment, saying an aircraft suspected to be North Korean had entered and then exited a danger zone near the island, and lifted the civil defence warning for the Ulleung area.

The Associated Press account matches the sequence: residents were first told a Russian aircraft had breached the airspace and were ordered to head to shelters, the evacuation order was then lifted, and the government later described the aircraft as possibly North Korean. No operator of the aircraft had confirmed the flight in the reporting available at the time of writing.
