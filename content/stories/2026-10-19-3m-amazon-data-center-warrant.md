3M said on Monday it has expanded its strategic commercial relationship with Amazon, supplying optical connectivity and other data center products, and issued a warrant to an Amazon subsidiary to buy up to 3,162,015 shares of 3M common stock at $164.89 per share over seven years. The subsidiary is not named in the reporting.

Most of the warrant pays for volume. Eighty-five percent of the shares vest against Amazon's cumulative payments for data center products, and that portion vests in full once payments reach $6 billion; the rest vest on event-based milestones that the filing does not specify. 3M shares rose 3 percent in after-hours trading after the announcement.
