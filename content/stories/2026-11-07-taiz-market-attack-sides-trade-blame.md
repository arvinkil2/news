An attack on the al-Hadara market in al-Waziyah, a district in western Taiz province, at about 7 a.m. on Friday killed at least 30 people and wounded eight, according to Anees Alsabahi, a spokesman for the Houthi-run health ministry, who said the toll could rise. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree blamed three Saudi airstrikes, while the Taiz government media office said no airstrikes were carried out in the area and blamed Houthi missile fire, acknowledging casualties without giving a toll. The Associated Press said it could not independently verify either side's claims, and Saudi Arabia's information ministry had not responded to its requests for comment.
