Qualcomm Incorporated is offering $4.0 billion of senior notes in five tranches, according to a prospectus supplement filed with the SEC on Tuesday: $750 million of 5.000 percent notes due 2028, $750 million of 5.150 percent notes due 2029, $1.0 billion of 5.350 percent notes due 2031, $750 million of 5.600 percent notes due 2033 and $750 million of 5.800 percent notes due 2036, with interest payable each May 20 and November 20 from May 20, 2027.

Qualcomm estimates net proceeds of about $3.98 billion and intends to use the money to repay all $2.0 billion of its outstanding 3.250 percent notes due May 20, 2027 by their stated maturity, with the remainder for general corporate purposes, which may include repaying commercial paper borrowings, funding working capital and acquisitions.
