France and Sweden held their first joint exercise under the forward deterrence initiative proposed by French President Emmanuel Macron, with French nuclear-capable Rafale jets and an Airbus A330 MRTT tanker operating over northern Sweden alongside Swedish Gripen fighters on November 16 to 18, the two countries' armed forces said on Thursday. The exercise, dubbed Excalibur, aimed to improve interoperability between the two air forces, involved no actual nuclear weapons and was not directed at any country, the defence ministries said. It follows a similar exercise last month in which British Typhoon jets escorted French Rafales on a simulated nuclear strike mission, as France works with European allies on Macron's proposal to bolster European deterrence in the face of Russian nuclear threats and uncertainty about United States security guarantees.
