The Houthi movement's military spokesman, Yahya Saria, said on Tuesday that its forces carried out four operations with ballistic missiles and drones, against King Khalid International Airport and a number of pumping stations in Riyadh, Saudi Aramco facilities at Abqaiq in the east of the kingdom, concentrations of Saudi forces in southwestern Yemen, and Riyadh's financial centre, and he called on foreign companies and investors to move their assets out of Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia has not confirmed any of the claimed strikes, and the claim is uncorroborated. Saria also denied striking the East-West oil pipeline near Medina, saying the pumping stations in Riyadh were the target; Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said on November 9 that the pipeline was attacked near Medina, causing a limited fire that was put out, with no casualties and no disruption to the line.
