China's industrial firms above the designated size earned combined profits of 6,737.36 billion yuan in the first ten months of 2026, up 16.0 percent year on year on a comparable basis, the National Bureau of Statistics said on Friday, with profits in October alone up 20.1 percent. Mining profits rose 38.8 percent and manufacturing profits 17.5 percent, while profits in electricity, heat, gas and water supply fell 14.8 percent.

The sector split was wide. Profits in computer, communications and other electronic equipment manufacturing roughly doubled, nonferrous metal smelting rose 71.7 percent and coal mining 61.4 percent, while auto manufacturing profits fell 26.0 percent and non-metallic mineral products fell 46.7 percent. Revenue rose 6.7 percent to 118.41 trillion yuan and the revenue profit margin reached 5.69 percent, up 0.45 percentage points from a year earlier.
