The Philippine economy grew 2.1 percent from a year earlier in the third quarter of 2026, the Philippine Statistics Authority said on Monday, a fifth consecutive quarterly slowing that came in below the 2.7 percent median forecast in a BusinessWorld poll of economists. BusinessWorld reported the print was the weakest since the 3.8 percent contraction in the first quarter of 2021, during the pandemic.

Services grew 3.7 percent from a year earlier and carried the expansion, led by wholesale and retail trade, public administration and financial and insurance activities, while agriculture, forestry and fishing contracted 4.8 percent amid El Niño weather and industry was flat, down 0.003 percent. Household consumption grew 2.4 percent, government consumption 6.8 percent and exports 8.6 percent, while gross capital formation fell 0.2 percent and gross national income rose 2.7 percent.
