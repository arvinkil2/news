The FBI arrested Sheikhdoon Abdullahi Mohamud, 18, of Richfield, Minnesota, on October 6 after he paid $600 to an undercover agent posing as an ISIS operative for an AK-47-style rifle and 200 rounds of ammunition. He was charged October 7 with receiving a firearm and ammunition to commit a federal crime of terrorism, and made his initial court appearance the same day.

Prosecutors say Mohamud targeted the October 24 Hmong Arts Celebration at the Mall of America because "it will be a lot of people," and told investigators he hoped to kill 30 to 60. The criminal complaint says he pledged allegiance to ISIS in a video posted to Discord last month and expressed a desire to emulate attacks including Pulse nightclub. "This was a radicalized young man who needed to be watched," U.S. Attorney Daniel Rosen said. FBI Director Kash Patel said agents stopped him before he could carry out the plan.
