The Houthi health ministry said Saudi airstrikes in the Bajil district, west of the Red Sea port of Hodeidah, killed five people and injured 13 on Saturday, hitting a petrol station where a fire broke out and a house, ERT News reported, carrying the Houthi-run Al Masirah channel via AFP and Al Jazeera. ERT reported damage to houses in the area and no Saudi confirmation of the strikes.

The reported toll follows Saudi strikes on the Houthi-held capital Sanaa on Saturday morning, where the Houthis reported four killed, and an afternoon Houthi claim of missile strikes on Saudi airports and oil installations, which the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted. Neither side's casualty or damage figures for Saturday have been independently verified in the sources read.
