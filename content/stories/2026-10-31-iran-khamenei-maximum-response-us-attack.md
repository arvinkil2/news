Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei said he has instructed the armed forces to respond with the maximum possible force, and with no geographical boundaries, if the United States or Israel launches a new attack on Iran. In the written statement, carried by state broadcaster IRIB, he said he had ordered the military to keep an updated bank of American and Israeli targets and described Iran's enemies as being in their weakest position, while presenting the warning as deterrence rather than a threat to strike first.

The statement is a rhetorical escalation, not an operational announcement, and no new attack or Iranian action was reported alongside it. It lands while Brent crude approaches 118 dollars on risk around the Strait of Hormuz, where a tanker was hit by limpet mines on Thursday, and while Qatari officials are in Tehran seeking to revive a ceasefire track with Washington.
