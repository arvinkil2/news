Colombia is raising $4.2 billion in the dollar bond market in two tranches priced in final prospectus supplements filed on October 28, 2026: $2.2 billion of 7.000 percent global bonds maturing on January 29, 2032, and $2.0 billion of 7.625 percent global bonds maturing on January 29, 2037. Both series are issued on October 29, 2026, in denominations of $200,000 and integral multiples of $1,000 above that, and rank as direct, general, unconditional, unsecured and unsubordinated external debt of the Republic, backed by its full faith and credit.
