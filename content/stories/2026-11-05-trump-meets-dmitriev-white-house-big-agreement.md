President Donald Trump said he met Kirill Dmitriev, Vladimir Putin's envoy, at the White House on Thursday, but did not meet the Ukrainian officials who were also visiting, the Associated Press reported. Speaking to reporters in the Oval Office, Trump declined to say what he and Dmitriev discussed, then predicted a wider deal: "There's going to be an agreement, it's going to be a big agreement and it's going to involve more than Ukraine," adding that it might include all of Europe.

Both delegations were at the White House for an event honouring first lady Melania Trump's work brokering the ceasefire in Oleshky, the Russian-occupied Ukrainian town where a six-day truce from October 27 to November 1 allowed aid deliveries and the evacuation of nearly 800 people. Dmitriev spoke at the event and thanked her for her humanitarian role, and the White House said she gave the Russian and Ukrainian representatives personal letters for Putin and Volodymyr Zelensky.
