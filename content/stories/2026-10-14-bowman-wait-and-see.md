Federal Reserve Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman said inflation remains above target but she does not currently see an urgent need for further rate action, arguing the committee has time to see how financial conditions and the economy evolve after September's 25 basis point hike to a 3.75 to 4.00 percent range. Her remarks, at an Institute of International Finance event in Bangkok, came hours before the September CPI print and align her with the officials arguing any further move should wait for more data.
