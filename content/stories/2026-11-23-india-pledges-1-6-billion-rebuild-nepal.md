India will provide 240 billion Nepalese rupees, about $1.6 billion, to rebuild areas of Nepal destroyed by flooding last August, Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced after meeting Nepalese Prime Minister Balendra Shah in New Delhi. The floods swept away settlements and infrastructure along more than 150 kilometres of the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli river basins, destroyed 13 hydroelectric projects in the Trishuli basin, and left more than 5,000 people missing, according to EFE.

The package covers more than a third of the $4.7 billion Nepal's government estimated in September for total recovery and reconstruction. Shah, on his first official trip abroad since taking office, thanked India for being the first country to respond after the disaster and invited Modi to visit Nepal.
