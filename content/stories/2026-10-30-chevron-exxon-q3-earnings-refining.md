Chevron reported third quarter earnings of 9.9 billion dollars, or 5.06 dollars per diluted share, up from 3.5 billion dollars a year earlier, with adjusted earnings of 10.4 billion dollars and downstream earnings of 4.9 billion dollars against 1.1 billion a year earlier, on record crude throughput at its United States refineries at 98 percent utilization. ExxonMobil reported third quarter earnings of 15.4 billion dollars, or 3.72 dollars per share, with adjusted earnings of 17.5 billion dollars, or 4.23 dollars per share, cash flow from operations of 25.7 billion dollars and what it called record year to date refining operational performance.
