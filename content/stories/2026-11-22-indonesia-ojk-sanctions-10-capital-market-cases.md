Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) announced administrative sanctions and corrective orders in 10 capital market violation cases, covering stock manipulation, disclosure and financial reporting failures, and breaches on material and affiliated transactions and share transfer obligations. In the three manipulation cases, involving PT Grand House Mulia Tbk (HOMI), PT Saraswanti Anugerah Makmur Tbk (SAMF) and PT Suryamas Dutamakmur Tbk (SMDM), the OJK ordered the disgorgement of IDR 23.77 billion in illicit profits, and it imposed IDR 3.71 billion in administrative fines across the other violations. The measures also include prohibitions on activity in the capital market, derivatives and carbon exchange sectors for periods of three to 10 years, orders to close securities accounts used in the manipulation, and requirements to fulfil outstanding obligations.
