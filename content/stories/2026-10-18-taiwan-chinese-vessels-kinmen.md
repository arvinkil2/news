Taiwan's Coast Guard Administration said its cutters drove away 16 Chinese vessels that entered the restricted waters of the Kinmen islands around 3 p.m. on Saturday, after broadcast warnings and hours of shadowing, in what it called harassment and a test of Taiwan's response. The group included Chinese coast guard vessels, maritime research ships and fishing boats, and all had left the restricted zone by about 7 p.m., the coast guard said.

The coast guard called the operation part of Beijing's grey-zone and hybrid warfare pressure on Taiwan, saying such incursions aim to wear down its forces and unsettle the public. China has run frequent coast guard patrols in Kinmen's restricted waters since 2024, when two Chinese fishermen drowned fleeing a Taiwanese patrol; China's state-run Global Times, cited in the same reporting, accused Taiwan's ruling Democratic Progressive Party of using routine law enforcement to stage provocations.
