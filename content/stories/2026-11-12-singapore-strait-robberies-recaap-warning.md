Robbers boarded the bulk carrier Star Calypso on November 2, the bulk carrier APJ Priti 2 late on November 4 and the tanker Salsa in the early hours of November 10, all in the eastbound lane of the Singapore Strait Traffic Separation Scheme. One crewmember was injured and engine spare parts were stolen from two of the vessels.

The ReCAAP Information Sharing Centre has urged Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore to intensify patrols and surveillance and to share intelligence, and has advised shipmasters to maintain heightened vigilance at night and to report suspicious activity immediately.
