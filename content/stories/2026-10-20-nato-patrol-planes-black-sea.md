NATO allies have sent or will send additional maritime patrol aircraft, radar systems, drones, intelligence personnel and explosive exploitation teams to the Black Sea, alliance spokesman Colonel Martin O'Donnell said on Tuesday after the North Atlantic Council discussed the region's security in Brussels. The announcement follows a run of drone attacks on shipping near Romania and Bulgaria, NATO members on the sea's western shore: seven sailors were reported missing after drones struck two chemical tankers last week, and at least five other cargo vessels were hit over the previous 10 days, with at least 23 sailors reported missing or dead. NATO, Bulgaria and Romania have not attributed the attacks to any country; military analysts cited by both wires say Russia almost certainly carried them out, a claim Moscow has not acknowledged, and EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas called the strikes a dangerous escalation aimed at choking off Ukraine's grain export revenue.
