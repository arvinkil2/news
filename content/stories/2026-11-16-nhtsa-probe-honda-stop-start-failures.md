The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said on November 16 that it has opened an engineering analysis into 1,033,632 Honda vehicles in the United States, covering 2024 to 2026 model year Odyssey, Passport, Ridgeline and Pilot models and the Acura MDX. Drivers complained that engines shut off by the automatic stop-start system, which cuts the engine at a complete stop to save fuel, then failed to restart when the driver lifted off the brake; NHTSA said it has received 701 complaints.

The new probe runs alongside a separate NHTSA investigation into similar stop-start problems in Honda vehicles that covers the same models except the 2026 model years. The regulator said the two groups show similar failures but appear to have different root causes and respond differently to repairs, and the report carried no statement from Honda.
