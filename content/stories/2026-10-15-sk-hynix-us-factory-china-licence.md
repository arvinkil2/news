Solidigm, the US unit of South Korea's SK Hynix, wants to install additional US made equipment from suppliers including Lam Research, Applied Materials and KLA in unused space at the Dalian NAND plant in China, and is making its plan to build a new US NAND factory contingent on receiving the export licence, Reuters reported on Wednesday, citing people familiar with the matter. SK Hynix told Reuters that equipment installation for the Dalian space known as Mod B is being carried out in consultation with and under authorization from the US government, and that no capacity expansion is being sought for that space, which was completed in 2023. Commerce, which revoked the earlier licence exemptions for Korean fabs in China last year, has not yet issued a 2027 licence for the site and did not respond to a request for comment.

Update, Friday morning: subsequent reporting carried in Korean and syndicated coverage says the licence outcome has hardened against the Dalian plan, and that SK hynix is examining shifting equipment from other plants rather than relying on new US made kit for the Dalian space. SK hynix has said operations at Dalian are unaffected. The reporting remains single wire in origin and this file stays at grade C; it is updated in place rather than duplicated.
