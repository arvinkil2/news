Black Hills Corp. and NorthWestern Energy Group have completed their all-stock merger, announced in August 2025, and Black Hills has changed its name to Bright Horizon Energy Corporation, the company said on Monday. Each NorthWestern share was converted into 0.98 Bright Horizon shares, with cash in lieu of fractional shares, NorthWestern is now a wholly owned subsidiary, and the combined stock begins trading on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker HZN today.

The combined company serves 2.2 million electric and natural gas utility customers across Arkansas, Colorado, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska, South Dakota and Wyoming, is headquartered in Rapid City, South Dakota, and will keep operating under the Black Hills Energy and NorthWestern Energy names locally. Brian Bird, formerly NorthWestern's chief executive, is president and chief executive of Bright Horizon Energy.
