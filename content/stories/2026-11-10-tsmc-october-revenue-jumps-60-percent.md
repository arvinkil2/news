TSMC reported October revenue of 586.89 billion New Taiwan dollars (about 17.88 billion US dollars), up 59.7 percent from a year earlier and 14.7 percent from September, CNBC reported, extending a run that produced a record third-quarter net profit of 763.1 billion New Taiwan dollars on revenue of 1.49 trillion. The company has said capital spending from 2026 to 2028 will be significantly higher than in the previous three years to support customer demand.

The print landed with Taiwan's own trade data pointing the same way: exports rose 58.9 percent in October from a year earlier to 96.76 billion US dollars, according to finance ministry data released Monday, with electronic components exports up 61.4 percent. TSMC shares closed 2.24 percent higher on Tuesday in Taipei.
