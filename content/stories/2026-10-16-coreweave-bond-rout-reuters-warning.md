CoreWeave bonds issued in June at a 9.675 percent yield fell sharply this week, pushing the yield to 15 percent, Reuters reported on Friday. Its shares fell 15.1 percent from Monday's open through Thursday's close, despite a gain of about 6 percent on Thursday after support from partner Nvidia, and peer neoclouds Nebius and Iren also weakened.

Investors and analysts quoted by Reuters described CoreWeave as an early warning for stressed AI credit, with more leveraged names being singled out as financing costs rise. The report follows two days in which this site's bond signal measured CoreWeave paper printing far below its latest fund marks.
