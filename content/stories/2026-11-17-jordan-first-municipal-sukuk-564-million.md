Jordan has raised 400 million dinars ($564 million) through the first sukuk issuance for its municipalities, a five-year lease-to-own instrument carrying an annual rental return of 5.5 percent, issued for Amman Vision Investment and Development Co. and fully subscribed by participating Islamic banks. The proceeds will help Greater Amman Municipality repay part of its outstanding debt, and the issuance is the first tranche of a Cabinet-approved 1 billion dinar sukuk program under which the municipality's restructuring is projected to cut financing costs by about 70 million dinars over five years. The sukuk are eligible for listing and trading on the Amman Stock Exchange, subject to regulatory conditions.
