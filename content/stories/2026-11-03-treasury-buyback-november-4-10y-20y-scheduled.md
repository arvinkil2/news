The Treasury will buy back nominal coupon securities in the 10 year to 20 year maturity bucket on Wednesday, November 4, at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, under its liquidity support program. The maximum purchase amount is $6 billion, 39 issues are eligible, and the operation settles on November 5.

Offered and accepted totals are published only after the operation runs. The last liquidity support operation, in the 20 year to 30 year bucket on October 27, took the full $6 billion maximum against $10.746 billion offered.
