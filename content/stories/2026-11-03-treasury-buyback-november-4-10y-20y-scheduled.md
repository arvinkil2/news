Latest: The Treasury accepted $6 billion, the full maximum, in its liquidity support buyback on Wednesday, November 4, in the 10 year to 20 year maturity bucket. Holders offered $39.636 billion, for cover of 6.61 times the amount accepted, and Treasury accepted paper from 7 of the 40 eligible issues. The operation ran at 1:40 p.m. Eastern and settles on November 5.

The full take follows the same result in the 20 year to 30 year bucket on October 27, when Treasury also accepted the full $6 billion maximum, that time against $10.746 billion offered. Offered volume in the 10 to 20 year bucket was nearly four times that level.

The Treasury had scheduled the operation on November 3 with a maximum purchase amount of $6 billion for nominal coupon securities in the 10 year to 20 year bucket under its liquidity support program.
