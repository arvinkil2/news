Challenger, the Sydney listed retirement income provider, said on Thursday it had rejected a revised, non-binding and conditional offer of A$12.20 per share from TAL Daiichi Life Australia, a subsidiary of Japan's Daiichi Life Holdings, for the 79.5 percent of the company Daiichi does not already own. The offer values that stake at about A$6.5 billion ($4.4 billion) and was 40 Australian cents above an A$11.80 per share proposal rejected in October, but represented a premium of just 2.1 percent to Challenger's previous close.

Challenger's board said it had carefully considered the proposal with advice from financial and legal advisers but concluded it did not adequately represent fair value for shareholders in the context of a change of control. Challenger shares closed 1.8 percent lower at A$11.74, their lowest in four weeks.
