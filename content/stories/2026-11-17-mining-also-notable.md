**Perenti renews at Geita for A$1 billion.** Perenti's African Underground Mining Services (AUMS) has been awarded a new five-year underground mining contract at AngloGold Ashanti's Geita gold mine in Tanzania, worth approximately A$1 billion (about $670 million) over its 60-month term from November 2026 to October 31, 2031. AUMS has operated underground at Geita since the mine transitioned from open pit in 2016, and will continue through its joint venture with local partner Geofields, in which Perenti subsidiaries hold 80 percent. Perenti expects about A$22 million of fiscal 2027 capital spending on the contract, already inside guidance.

**Ottawa backs Mactung tungsten with up to C$14 million.** Natural Resources Canada has conditionally approved up to C$14.0 million through its Global Partnerships Initiative for Fireweed Metals' Mactung tungsten project on the Yukon and Northwest Territories border, one of the world's largest undeveloped high-grade tungsten deposits. The funding, subject to final due diligence, supports metallurgical testing and engineering designs to a tender-ready stage as the project advances toward a final investment decision, and follows up to C$12.9 million from the First and Last Mile Fund announced in August 2025 and a US$15.8 million United States Defense Production Act award in December 2024. Fireweed targets an updated feasibility study in early 2027.

**ACG Metals completes $325 million bond placement (single-sourced: Mining Weekly).** ACG Metals has completed a $325 million senior secured bonds placement, according to Mining Weekly. No second outlet had confirmed the terms when this roundup was written.

**Guinea deports 1,557 West Africans in gold mining crackdown (single-sourced: Vanguard).** Guinea has deported 1,557 West African nationals in a crackdown on informal gold mining, Vanguard reported. The figure had not been independently confirmed when this roundup was written.

**Barrick tops up Hercules Metals stake (single-sourced: Mining Weekly).** Barrick has invested C$5.7 million to maintain its shareholding in Hercules Metals, according to Mining Weekly.
