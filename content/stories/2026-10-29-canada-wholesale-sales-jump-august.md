Canadian wholesale sales rose 3.4 percent in August to 87.4 billion dollars, Statistics Canada reported, the largest monthly increase since November 2020 and a rebound from a revised 0.4 percent decline in July. Five of seven subsectors rose, led by motor vehicle and motor vehicle parts and accessories wholesalers, up 13.7 percent to 16.7 billion dollars, and sales excluding petroleum, petroleum products and other hydrocarbons, and excluding oilseed and grain, rose 3.7 percent.

Wholesale inventories rose 0.8 percent to 132.4 billion dollars as six of seven subsectors built stock, leaving the inventory-to-sales ratio unchanged at 1.52 in August. Ontario drove the provincial picture, with sales up 4.3 percent to 45.4 billion dollars, while Alberta fell 1.3 percent to 10.0 billion dollars on weaker petroleum sales.
