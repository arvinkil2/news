Thailand will issue a ban on exports of chemicals used in mining on Monday, November 30, Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul said on the weekly government television programme on Saturday, with the order to be issued by the Ministry of Defence. The measure targets rare earth mining in Myanmar: the government said last week it planned to block exports of chemicals used in rare earth mining to Myanmar and other relevant countries, and the National Security Council is preparing the list of chemicals to be controlled, which has not yet been published.

Deputy Prime Minister Yodchanan Wongsawat told Reuters on Friday that existing data showed mines in Shan State were polluting rivers flowing into northern Thailand, though Myanmar's government had asked for more evidence. Tests in August found arsenic above standard levels at multiple points on the Kok, Sai and Ruak rivers in Chiang Rai and Chiang Mai provinces, and the two countries have agreed to set up 12 joint water sampling locations, with sampling expected to begin between December and January.
