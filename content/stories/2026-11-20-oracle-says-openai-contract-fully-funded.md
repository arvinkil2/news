Oracle said on Friday that its infrastructure contract with OpenAI is fully funded following a final capital raise earlier this month, posting on X: "The financing we outlined at Oracle Investor Day, including vendor financing, is in place." Reuters reported the raise totalled $20 billion, consisting of about $10 billion in stock and $10 billion in debt, and that Oracle and OpenAI plan to develop 4.5 gigawatts of additional data center capacity in the United States on top of the initial Stargate site in Abilene, Texas. The statement follows investor scrutiny over the amount of capital Oracle needs to fund its AI data center expansion and its growing exposure to OpenAI; Oracle shares rose as much as 3.3 percent in after-hours trading.
