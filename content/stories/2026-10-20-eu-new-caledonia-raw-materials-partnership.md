The European Commission and the government of New Caledonia signed a memorandum of understanding in Nouméa establishing a strategic partnership on sustainable raw materials value chains, signed for the Commission by Stéphane Séjourné, Executive Vice-President for Prosperity and Industrial Strategy, and for New Caledonia by its president, Milakulo Tukumuli, and Alcide Ponga, the government member responsible for energy and mining. The partnership covers five areas: integration of critical raw materials value chains and access to finance under the Global Gateway strategy, sustainable mining with local value creation and site rehabilitation under EU environmental, social and governance standards, infrastructure for raw materials development, skills training, and research on exploration, processing and recycling. The two sides will now draw up a joint roadmap with EU member states, the European Investment Bank and industry, and the Commission said New Caledonia, which produced 3.1 percent of the world's nickel in 2024, is its 17th raw materials partner since 2021.
