Sri Lanka coordinated the rescue of 40 Iranian sailors from multiple commercial ships in international waters off its coast after its foreign ministry received a request from the Iranian embassy through diplomatic channels, ministry spokesman Thushara Rodrigo said on Sunday. The sailors, drawn from 19 ships, had been suffering unspecified medical emergencies, were brought ashore at Galle Harbour and given medical treatment, and returned to Iran early on Sunday morning. Local media, including the Indian newspaper The Hindu, reported the sailors had been aboard vessels that were US-sanctioned oil tankers, Reuters reported, and Washington has imposed a blockade on Iranian shipping as part of its war on Iran.
