**Latest.** The violence widened on Thursday and Prime Minister Tarique Rahman publicly blamed the country sheltering Hasina's exiled leaders, without naming India. Police said two members of his ruling Bangladesh Nationalist Party were killed on Wednesday night, the houses of two BNP lawmakers in Hasina's home district of Gopalganj were vandalised and set alight, and Awami League supporters attacked markets and shops in Gopalganj town. At least six buses and pickup vans and a section of railway line were set on fire and crude bomb blasts were reported across six districts overnight, and a bus and a truck were torched on the Dhaka to Chattogram highway on Thursday, their passengers unharmed. Dhaka police said they arrested 107 people in 24 hours, including former education minister Nurul Islam Nahid. Speaking in the northern district of Lalmonirhat, Rahman said the exiled League leaders had not looked at the interests of Bangladesh and that the country across the border, where they have fled to, is their master, and that to satisfy those masters they have again started conspiring against Bangladesh. India's foreign ministry did not immediately respond to a request for comment, Reuters reported. Separately, garment workers demanding higher wages blocked a highway into Dhaka in protests Reuters described as unrelated, forcing factories to close.

Parts of Bangladesh shut down on Thursday as supporters of former prime minister Sheikh Hasina clashed with police in a nationwide lockdown called by her Awami League party, after the government rejected her request for a new passport and identity card to support her planned return from exile in India in December. Police said the ancestral home of former interim leader Muhammad Yunus in Chattogram was set on fire late on Wednesday, and Grameen Bank, which Yunus founded, said at least 37 of its branches and offices have been targeted in arson attacks or attempted fires since November 10.

Two people have been killed in the unrest, a Bangladesh Nationalist Party activist near Hasina's home district of Gopalganj on Tuesday and a bus company helper burnt to death in Pirojpur on Wednesday after two buses were set on fire, authorities said. Police reported dozens of crude bomb blasts and vehicles set alight in at least 28 districts over ten days of protests, and 97 platoons of the Border Guard Bangladesh were deployed across seven districts.
