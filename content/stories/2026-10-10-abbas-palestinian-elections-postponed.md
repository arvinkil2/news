Palestinian Authority President Mahmoud Abbas issued a decree cancelling the legislative elections scheduled for November 28, 2026 and setting September 11, 2027 as the date for both presidential and legislative elections. The move followed a PLO Executive Committee decision and consultations with Palestinian factions that produced what was described as a national consensus on the obstacles Israeli measures pose to holding the vote.

No reactions were quoted in the brief announcement. Palestinians have not held national elections in two decades.
