India's existing customs duty exemption on cotton imports, which was available until October 31, 2026, has been extended by two months to December 31, 2026, the Ministry of Textiles announced on October 31, through Notification No. 35/2026-Customs dated October 30 from the Department of Revenue in the Ministry of Finance. The government said the aim is to increase cotton availability for the domestic textile industry, meet specialised cotton requirements, and promote value addition across the textile and apparel value chain. It expects the extension to give manufacturers more flexibility on raw materials, reduce cost pressures, support micro, small and medium enterprises, and strengthen the competitiveness of Indian textile products at home and abroad.
