Tenstorrent, a chip startup focused on inference, has raised 1.5 billion dollars at a 6.2 billion dollar valuation, more than doubling the 2.6 billion dollar valuation it fetched in late 2024. The round was led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, with Cadence Ventures, Eclipse, Fidelity, SanDisk and T. Rowe Price also taking part, and the new capital will go toward scaling chips tailored for serving open-weight AI models and video generation. Chief executive Jim Keller told Bloomberg the company decided to remain independent and raise more capital after its Galaxy servers showed they could run DeepSeek models, five months after Intel and Qualcomm reportedly showed acquisition interest at valuations of as much as 5 billion dollars.
