StarHub and Keppel, M1's parent, have entered into definitive agreements to combine StarHub and M1 in an all-share transaction that values M1 at S$1.36 billion and gives the combined group an enterprise value of about S$5 billion, with StarHub issuing 651.7 million shares. The companies expect annual cost savings of S$110 million by 2030, and StarHub has separately exercised its call option to acquire Circles.Life's Singapore mobile business.

The merger needs approval from the Infocomm Media Development Authority and the Ministry of Digital Development and Information, plus shareholder votes at StarHub and Keppel, and is expected to complete in the first half of 2027. Both operators will keep competing independently until then, and it is the second attempt to consolidate M1 this year, after its proposed acquisition by Simba Telecom collapsed in May.
