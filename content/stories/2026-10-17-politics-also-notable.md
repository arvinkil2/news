Japan's finance minister Katayama Satsuki used her statement as Japan's governor of the IMF and World Bank in Bangkok to call for the withdrawal of arbitrary export restrictions on critical minerals, free and safe navigation through the Strait of Hormuz, and a government debt to GDP ratio on a firm declining path at home. Japan also pledged 40 million US dollars to the Global Financing Facility for maternal and child health and backed the World Bank guarantee push for critical minerals projects.

China's Ministry of Commerce published Announcement No. 46 of 2026, the implementation rules for the 2027 import tariff quotas on sugar, wool and wool tops, the annual administrative act that sets how much of each commodity enters at the lower in quota tariff rate. UN Secretary General Guterres urged faster action and financing to halt biodiversity loss, in remarks carried by UN News.

In Washington, the Government Accountability Office's October 16 batch also covered textile circular economy policy options for the United States, a call for the IRS to build a comprehensive strategy against ghost tax preparers who file returns without signing them, the use of new occupancy data to shed unneeded federal office space, and future land use planning at Department of Energy nuclear cleanup sites. FIFA sanctioned Spain over Islamophobic chants by fans during a friendly against Egypt, a sports governance decision noted here only for the record.
