Microsoft has rejected an option to more than double the size of its existing agreement with Nscale to rent computing power at a Texas data centre, the Financial Times reported on Thursday, citing people familiar with the matter. The rejected expansion was a potential deal worth tens of billions of dollars.

The report comes weeks after Microsoft pulled out of a deal to anchor Nscale's flagship data centre project in Loughton, Essex, after power connection delays pushed its expected supply date into the 2030s. Nvidia backed Nscale filed for a US initial public offering in September, with Microsoft and Anthropic expected to be among its major customers, and Reuters reported last month that Nscale was in talks to sell computing power to Anthropic in a deal that could be worth about $45 billion.
