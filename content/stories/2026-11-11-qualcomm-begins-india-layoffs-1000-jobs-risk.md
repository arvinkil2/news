Qualcomm has begun laying off employees in India as part of a global workforce restructuring, with the first round starting at its Bengaluru facility on Monday and further cuts expected in phases at its Hyderabad, Delhi-NCR and Chennai operations. The Financial Express, citing people familiar with the matter, reported that the global exercise could affect around 7 percent of Qualcomm's roughly 52,000 employees, and Pareekh Jain of EIIRTrend estimated that a similar ratio in India, where Qualcomm employs about 20,000 people, would put around 1,000 or slightly more jobs at risk.

Chief executive Cristiano Amon told employees in an October memo that the cuts would follow the company's annual reviews in November as part of a push for a stronger performance culture, and affected staff have reportedly been offered severance linked to tenure. The restructuring follows a 26 percent year on year fall in fourth quarter handset revenue to $5.14 billion, even as automotive revenue rose 89 percent and the company targets $5 billion of data centre revenue in fiscal 2027.
