Diamondback Energy is nearing a deal to acquire Double Eagle V, the EnCap Investments-backed Permian producer, for about $5 billion, the Financial Times reported, citing people familiar with the matter. The consideration is expected to be a mix of cash and stock, final terms are still under negotiation, and an announcement could come as early as next week, according to the report.

It would be Diamondback's second purchase from the Double Eagle founders: it closed the acquisition of Double Eagle IV assets for $4.08 billion in cash and stock in 2025, after its $26 billion merger with Endeavor Energy Resources in 2024 made it one of the largest independent producers in the Midland Basin. Neither Diamondback nor Double Eagle has commented on the reported talks.
