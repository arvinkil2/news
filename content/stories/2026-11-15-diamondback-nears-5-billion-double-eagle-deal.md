## Latest

Diamondback Energy has entered into a definitive purchase agreement to acquire certain subsidiaries of Double Eagle IV Midco, LLC and Double Eagle V Midco, LLC, the company announced on Monday. The consideration is approximately 15.56 million shares of Diamondback common stock and $2.15 billion of cash, subject to customary adjustments, and Reuters valued the cash and stock deal at $5.03 billion. The cash portion is expected to be funded through a combination of cash on hand, borrowings under the company's credit facility and proceeds from term loans.

The assets cover approximately 176,000 net acres in the core of the Midland Basin, with estimated 2027 oil production of approximately 35,000 barrels a day (92,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day, 38 percent oil). Diamondback said 68 percent of the asset is undeveloped, with over 1,200 estimated gross (495 net) horizontal locations in primary development targets, including 101 gross (46 net) drilled but uncompleted wells. The company valued the transaction at approximately 4.0 times estimated 2027 EBITDA, said it is immediately accretive to cash flow, free cash flow and net asset value per share, and expects it to be leverage neutral. Closing is expected on December 21, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions.

Chief executive Kaes Van't Hof said the deal buys out the 40 percent of the partnership Diamondback did not already own, a step the company calls netting up, and that Diamondback does not need to add a rig or materially alter its development plan as a result. Double Eagle co-chief executives Cody Campbell and John Sellers said the team plans to continue its multi rig development program on its remaining assets in Reagan County.

## Earlier

Diamondback Energy is nearing a deal to acquire Double Eagle V, the EnCap Investments-backed Permian producer, for about $5 billion, the Financial Times reported on November 15, citing people familiar with the matter. The consideration was expected to be a mix of cash and stock, final terms were still under negotiation, and an announcement could come as early as next week, according to the report.

It would be Diamondback's second purchase from the Double Eagle founders: it closed the acquisition of Double Eagle IV assets for $4.08 billion in cash and stock in 2025, after its $26 billion merger with Endeavor Energy Resources in 2024 made it one of the largest independent producers in the Midland Basin. Neither Diamondback nor Double Eagle commented on the reported talks at the time.
