Frame's mandate is to identify investment opportunities and address bottlenecks in materials the bank treats as essential to manufacturing, energy and defence, within a ten year initiative aimed at industries vital to US economic and national security. The appointment comes as Western governments and companies work to diversify mineral supplies and cut exposure to concentrated overseas production and processing capacity. Jamie Dimon launched the initiative with a warning that the United States had become too reliant on unreliable sources of critical minerals and manufacturing.
