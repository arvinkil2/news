Normet. Finland based Normet has acquired Western Australia based mining equipment and engineering business Total Rockbreaking Solutions, or TRS, strengthening its rock breaking capability and adding established expertise in surface mining applications. TRS serves mining operators, contractors and engineering firms in some of Australia's most demanding surface mining environments, and will continue to operate under its existing brand with its current team, Normet said. President and chief executive Ed Santamaria said TRS's application expertise complements Normet's global technology platform and its investments in digitalisation and automation of the rock breaking process. No financial terms were disclosed.
