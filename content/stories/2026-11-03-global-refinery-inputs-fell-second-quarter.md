Global crude oil refinery inputs fell by 4.8 million barrels per day in the second quarter of 2026, a 6 percent drop that followed the start of conflict in the Middle East in late February, the Energy Information Administration said on Tuesday, using Kpler data. Inputs in China fell by 2.1 million barrels per day in the quarter, inputs in Kuwait and the United Arab Emirates each fell by 0.7 million, and Russian inputs fell by 0.6 million as attacks on its oil infrastructure intensified.

Throughput recovered by 1.8 million barrels per day in the third quarter as refiners adjusted to new trade flows and more Middle Eastern crude reached the market, and US inputs rose by 0.5 million barrels per day in each quarter. Third quarter global inputs still averaged about 5 percent below the same period of 2025.
