## Latest

Turkish authorities have arrested Yunus Arinci, who led Takasbank, the country's settlement and custody bank, before resigning earlier this month, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, citing the state-run Anadolu Agency. Ali Pasli, a co-founder of the digital Islamic lender Adil Katilim Bank, and Omer Murat Gulkan, chairman of OMG Capital Advisors, were also arrested as police expanded the market manipulation investigation, according to the same report, which said the charges were not specified.

Sahap Kavcioglu, chairman of Turkey's Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BDDK), said on Wednesday he had submitted his resignation to President Tayyip Erdogan. Kavcioglu said on X that he had asked for permission to step down "in light of the recent allegations concerning the Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency," Reuters reported.

The resignation follows the arrest of three senior BDDK officials, including a deputy head, in the investigation into investment funds, according to Turkish media reports summarised by Le Private Banker. Kavcioglu denied any link to the fund affair. The probe began after fund fraud came to light on September 17, and regulators have since ordered the liquidation of 131 funds run by seven asset managers.
