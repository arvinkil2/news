Steel Dynamics said on November 9 that it had authorized an additional $3.0 billion of share repurchases and declared a fourth quarter 2026 cash dividend, in a press release the company filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission as Exhibit 99.1 to a Form 8-K on Tuesday under Item 8.01. The filing, signed by Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Theresa E. Wagler, incorporates the release by reference and does not restate the dividend amount in the report text itself.
