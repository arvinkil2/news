The Reserve Bank of India conducted a second overnight variable rate repo auction under the liquidity adjustment facility on Thursday November 26, with a notified amount of 50,000 crore rupees, a one-day tenor and reversal on November 27. A second such auction in the current sequence signals the central bank is actively supplying overnight funds rather than letting money market rates drift above the policy corridor into month-end.

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the December 10-year JGB auction for December 3, with an offering amount of about 2,600 billion yen, issue date December 4 and maturity September 20, 2036. The announcement lands in the same week the ministry meets JGB market participants after the soft November 25 40-year auction, so any change to super-long supply in the December calendar remains the point to watch.

Federal Reserve Vice Chair Philip Jefferson, in a speech delivered in Brasilia on November 19 and posted by the BIS on November 26, set out how the Fed distinguishes demand shocks from supply shocks and warned that energy, trade and AI shocks are now unfolding simultaneously. Jefferson said that where an energy price shock pushes inflation up while inflation is already above target, the risk of expectations becoming unanchored argues for a stronger reaction on inflation, a framing that fits the market's pricing of a further increase at the December FOMC meeting.
