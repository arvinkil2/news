Kymera Therapeutics reported positive 16-week results from BROADEN2, a randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 2b trial of KT-621, its once-daily oral STAT6 degrader, in 244 adults and adolescents with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis. All three doses met the primary endpoint of percent change in Eczema Area and Severity Index score and the secondary endpoints; the mid dose produced a 72 percent mean EASI reduction against 27 percent on placebo, a placebo-adjusted difference of 45 percent, and 62 percent of mid-dose patients reached EASI-75 against 22 percent on placebo, with significant itch improvement from week 1.

The company said KT-621 was well tolerated with a favourable safety profile; STAT reported conjunctivitis was the only side effect more common on the drug than on placebo. Kymera plans to start a first Phase 3 atopic dermatitis trial by mid-2027, subject to regulator alignment, while the BREADTH Phase 2b asthma trial continues toward a topline readout by late 2027.
