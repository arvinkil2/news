Speaking at the Boston Fed's annual conference for regional and community banks, Collins said her most likely scenario is healthy growth with the labour market near full employment and inflation gradually returning to 2 percent, helped by efficiency gains lifting productive capacity. She flagged fuel costs from the Middle East war, including New England heating oil, and AI data centre construction costs as pressures to watch, and said the question is whether price rises stay confined to energy and AI related categories or broaden. Her timing will depend on the incoming data, she said, starting with Wednesday's CPI print.
