PayPal said its board has appointed Claire Bramley, 49, as executive vice president and chief financial officer effective February 3, 2027, succeeding Jamie Miller, whose departure as chief financial and operating officer on February 2, 2027 is, the filing states, not the result of any dispute over the company's reported financial statements. Bramley has been Xero's chief financial officer since April 2025 and was previously Teradata's chief financial officer from June 2021 to April 2025, after 14 years at HP Inc. Her package carries an $875,000 base salary, a $5 million cash signing bonus in two instalments, and restricted and performance stock units with a combined targeted grant value of $26 million; the board also appointed Brian J. Lee, Goldman Sachs's former chief risk officer, as a director from November 30, 2026.
