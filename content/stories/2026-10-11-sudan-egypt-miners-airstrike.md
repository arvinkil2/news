At least 72 Sudanese artisanal gold miners were killed in an airstrike near the Sudan-Egypt border, the Sudanese rights group Emergency Lawyers said, per a Reuters relay. The group blamed the Egyptian air force and said such a cross-border strike would violate international law; Egypt did not comment. Unverified images circulating online showed dozens of bodies in rubble, and the strike comes as artisanal gold has become a key cash source for both sides of Sudan's war, much of it smuggled into Egypt.
