Foreign outflows from Taiwanese stocks hit a record in October, with overseas investors selling 31.78 billion dollars of shares on a net basis, surpassing the previous all-time high set in March, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. The selling came as a global selloff in artificial intelligence stocks pushed the island's benchmark closer to a technical correction. The figure makes October the heaviest month of foreign exit on record from the market that hosts the chipmakers at the centre of the AI buildout.
