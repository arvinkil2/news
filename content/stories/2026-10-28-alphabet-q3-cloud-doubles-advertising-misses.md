Alphabet's third quarter results, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission after the close on Wednesday, delivered a split verdict. Total revenues rose 25 percent to 127.6 billion dollars, and Google Cloud revenues rose 103 percent to 30.7 billion dollars, which the company called record growth, led by enterprise artificial intelligence infrastructure and solutions and by sales of its TPU systems, with Cloud operating income more than tripling to 11.1 billion dollars. Against that, investinglive reported that Search and other revenue of about 63 billion dollars and YouTube advertising of about 11.2 billion both missed expectations, and that total revenue came in just below the roughly 127.9 billion dollars analysts had expected.

The cost of the build-out is now visible in the cash flow statement. Consolidated operating income rose 30 percent to 40.6 billion dollars, a margin of 31.8 percent, but net income available to common stockholders fell to 26.2 billion dollars and diluted earnings per share to 2.12 dollars, from 2.87 dollars a year earlier, a decline investinglive attributed to a loss of about 8.7 billion dollars on equity securities. Purchases of property and equipment reached 51.4 billion dollars in the quarter, ahead of the 42.3 billion the operations generated in cash, leaving free cash flow at minus 9.1 billion dollars. Chief Executive Sundar Pichai said the company is now on a half-trillion dollar revenue run rate. Investing.com reported the shares tumbled 7 percent in reaction to the miss.
