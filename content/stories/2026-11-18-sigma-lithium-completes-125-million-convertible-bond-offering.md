Sigma Lithium Corporation has completed its previously announced private placement of senior unsecured convertible bonds in an aggregate principal amount of US$125 million. The bonds were issued at par, bear interest at 7.0 percent per annum payable quarterly, and mature five years from closing at 100 percent of principal, with a conversion price of US$6.51 per common share, a 25.0 percent premium over the US$5.21 closing price of the shares on Nasdaq on November 2, 2026; BofA Securities acted as sole global coordinator and sole bookrunner, and an insider of the company took US$10 million of the offering.

The company said the net proceeds will be used to repay the outstanding amounts under its senior secured debt agreement with Synergy Capital maturing in June 2027, with any remaining balance invested in its mining operations as growth capital expenditures and used for general corporate purposes. Holders may require the company to redeem the bonds at par on the third anniversary of closing, in November 2029.
