Kim Yo-jong, the sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong-un, said in a statement carried by the Korean Central News Agency that North Korean tactical units are taking up new positions and that loudspeakers for psychological warfare against South Korea will be in operation along the whole border within October, calling the move the most legitimate measure and an appropriate response. She said she had visited border positions on Wednesday with Army Marshal Pak Jong Chon, accused South Korean forces of loudspeaker warning broadcasts and warning shots at at least two locations that day, and warned that if South Korean warning fire harms North Korean territory or soldiers, border troops will show no mercy.

A Yonhap breaking flash later on Wednesday reported a follow-on statement in which Kim said new countermeasure means are on standby for their mission along the whole border line and the loudspeakers too will be on standby within a few days.
