Hubert Aiwanger, 55, Bavaria's deputy premier and economy minister and the chairman of the Free Voters party, was found dead on Wednesday evening in a hotel on Friedrichstrasse in Berlin, according to Berlin's public prosecutor's office as reported by Deutsche Welle. He had been feeling unwell during the day and had cancelled a scheduled speech at the opening of his party's Berlin office for health reasons.

Berlin police and prosecutors have launched an investigation to determine the specific cause of death and the circumstances, and to rule out foul play; no cause has been stated. Bavarian premier Markus Söder, whose CSU governs Bavaria in coalition with Aiwanger's Free Voters, said he was deeply shocked and called the sudden death an absolute tragedy.
