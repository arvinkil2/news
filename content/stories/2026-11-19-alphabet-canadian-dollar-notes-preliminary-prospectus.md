Alphabet has filed a preliminary prospectus supplement with the SEC for a Canadian dollar note offering in five series, one floating rate tranche priced off Daily Compounded CORRA and four fixed rate tranches, with RBC Capital Markets, Scotiabank and TD Securities as joint book-running managers. The supplement, dated November 19, 2026 and marked subject to completion, leaves the aggregate size, coupons and maturities blank, and says the proceeds will be used for general corporate purposes, which may include repaying outstanding debt. The notes will be senior unsecured obligations of Alphabet, will not be listed on any exchange, and are expected to be delivered through CDS in Canada on a T+3 settlement cycle.
