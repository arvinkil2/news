President Trump told radio host Hugh Hewitt on Thursday that he wants to "get Cuba done" before he leaves office and would "certainly consider" a short US military operation, describing Cuba as a "failed" state his administration is monitoring very closely, CNBC reported. He said Secretary of State Marco Rubio "would love to do it," and CNBC, citing Politico, said the comments marked the first time Trump has put a timeline on possible military intervention to topple Cuba's authorities. Analysts cited in the report were sceptical that a military campaign would follow and judged further economic pressure more likely, while Cuba's embassy in Washington said US steps deepen an energy siege that already leaves hospitals without reliable power and families facing blackouts.
