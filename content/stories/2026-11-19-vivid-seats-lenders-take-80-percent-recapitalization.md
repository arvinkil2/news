Vivid Seats has entered into a transaction support agreement with an ad hoc group holding a substantial majority of its term loans to recapitalize the company, the ticket marketplace announced on Thursday. About 273 million dollars of secured debt will be exchanged for about 45.8 million newly issued Class A shares, about 80 percent of the common equity after completion, while existing shareholders keep their about 11.5 million shares, about 20 percent.

The deal cuts net debt by about 232 million dollars, from 342 million dollars to about 110 million dollars on the company's pro forma figures, provides 66 million dollars of new first lien, first out term loans, and extends the remaining term loan maturity from February 2029 to January 2032. It is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to definitive documentation and other customary conditions, and chief executive Lawrence Fey said it gives the company a durable capital structure.
