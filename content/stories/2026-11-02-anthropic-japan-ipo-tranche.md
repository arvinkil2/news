**Latest, November 2:** Anthropic reported updated financials on Monday in an amended Form S-1 filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission, CNBC reported. Revenue was about 17 billion dollars in the third quarter and 33.24 billion dollars in the nine months to September 30, up roughly 1,200 percent from the same period a year earlier. The net loss was 23.29 billion dollars in the quarter and 104 billion dollars over the nine months, against a 25.1 billion dollar loss in the same nine months of 2025, with most of the nine month loss coming from changes in the fair value of convertible notes and derivative liabilities. The amended filing puts Anthropic's purchase commitments and lease agreements at about 895.4 billion dollars in aggregate committed spend, substantially all for compute and infrastructure, and says compute and infrastructure expenses rose by 14.4 billion dollars in the nine months, up 329 percent year on year.

Anthropic will sell up to 552.9 billion yen, about $3.5 billion, of new shares to investors in Japan as part of its United States public listing, according to a filing that Reuters reported on Monday. The filing, as reported, puts the proceeds toward working capital, operating expenses and capital expenditure, and states neither the overall size nor the valuation of the offering and no pricing or listing timetable. Reuters reported that some investors have discussed a valuation of about $1.5 trillion for the company, and that Japanese investors secured $2.2 billion of the $75 billion SpaceX raised in its June listing.
