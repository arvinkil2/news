A Saudi National Guard AH-6i reconnaissance helicopter crashed inside Saudi territory in the Najran region, near the border with Yemen, because of a technical malfunction, killing its two-officer crew, Saudi-led coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Maliki said in a statement on Sunday. Al-Maliki offered condolences to the families of the two officers.

The crash came hours after coalition air defences intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched toward Khamis Mushayt and a drone targeting the Riyadh region early on Sunday, following civil defence alerts warning residents of a hostile aerial threat. Saudi Arabia has faced a renewed wave of Houthi missile and drone attacks since Yemen's conflict reignited in July.
