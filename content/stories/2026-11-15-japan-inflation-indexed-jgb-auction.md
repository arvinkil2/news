Japan's Ministry of Finance accepted 249.8 billion yen of bids for the 10-year inflation-indexed JGB Issue No. 31 on November 16, out of 673.5 billion yen in competitive bids, a bid-to-cover ratio of about 2.7. The lowest accepted price was 96.00 per 100 yen, for a yield of 1.072 percent, with 44.6666 percent of bids at that price allotted.

The bonds carry a nominal coupon of 0.6 percent, are issued on November 17 and mature on March 10, 2036. The indexation coefficient on the issue date is 1.01169.
