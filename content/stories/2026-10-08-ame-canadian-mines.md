The AME report puts a headline figure on what the next wave of Canadian mine builds would contribute to the economy, at a moment when the industry is lobbying for faster permitting and clearer federal support.

The estimate is an industry-association number, not an independent economic forecast, and the underlying assumptions on mine count, timing and commodity prices were not visible in trade-press coverage. Treat the C$23 billion as advocacy arithmetic until the report itself is reviewed.
