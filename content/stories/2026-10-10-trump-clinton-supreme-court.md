Trump's 2022 suit alleged Clinton, Comey, Christopher Steele, the DNC and others ran a racketeering conspiracy to discredit him with a false narrative of collusion with Russia in 2016. US District Judge Donald Middlebrooks dismissed the complaint as a political manifesto built on frivolous legal theories, fined Trump and his lawyer Alina Habba nearly $1 million in sanctions, and the 11th Circuit upheld both rulings in 2025. In the Supreme Court papers, Trump's lawyers argue the lower-court decisions are having a profound impact on the presidency and the nation as a whole.
