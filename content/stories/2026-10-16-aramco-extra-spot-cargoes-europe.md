Saudi Aramco contacted oil traders this week and offered additional crude cargoes for immediate, spot delivery to Europe, Reuters reported, citing three well informed trading sources. The delivery window given was late October to mid November; the report stated no number of cargoes, no total volume and no crude grades or pricing.

The offer follows Aramco telling European refiners it would meet their full requested quantities for November after the restart of the kingdom's main east west pipeline, which had previously been attacked, with at least three European refiners expected to receive their full allocations. Saudi Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman said earlier this week that the kingdom plans to raise oil pipeline capacity to about 10 million barrels a day within two to three years.
