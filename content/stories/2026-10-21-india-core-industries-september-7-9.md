India's Index of Core Industries grew 7.9 percent year on year in September 2026 on a provisional basis, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry said, with the overall index at 118.3 against 109.6 a year earlier. Iron ore output rose 18.4 percent, electricity 11.4 percent, coal 10.7 percent, refinery products 10.3 percent, cement 9.8 percent and steel 4.9 percent, while fertilizers fell 11.7 percent, natural gas 4.6 percent and crude oil 1.4 percent.

August's final reading was revised up to 5.9 percent growth from the 4.8 percent provisional print, with the index revised to 120.4 from 119.2. Cumulative core output growth for April to September 2026 was 5.1 percent, against 2.5 percent in the same period a year earlier.
