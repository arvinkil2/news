ZTO Express, China's leading express delivery company, reported revenue of 14,361.6 million yuan (US$2,142.1 million) for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 21.0 percent from a year earlier, in a Form 6-K filed on Wednesday. Parcel volume rose 7.0 percent to 10,241 million parcels, taking the company's market share to 20.1 percent, and gross profit rose 19.5 percent to 3,532.4 million yuan.

Profit growth lagged revenue. Net income rose 1.4 percent to 2,573.4 million yuan and adjusted net income rose 2.6 percent to 2,571.1 million yuan, while net cash from operating activities rose to 3,720.7 million yuan from 3,211.0 million a year earlier.
