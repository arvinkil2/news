Rockstar Games, a label of Take-Two Interactive Software, announced that Grand Theft Auto VI reached 3.79 billion dollars in day one retail sales, based on company estimates through the end of November 19 worldwide, setting a record for entertainment across any medium and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history. The game sold 32.5 million units before release, a record across all platforms, and holds a Metacritic score of 99.

The release, furnished to the SEC as Exhibit 99.1 to Take-Two's Form 8-K on Friday, measures retail spending by consumers rather than revenue recognised by the company, and gives no breakdown by platform or region. Grand Theft Auto VI is available through the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Games Store and global retailers, and is rated M for mature.
