Asian technology stocks fell sharply on Wednesday, reversing the rally that followed Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang's reinstatement. Taipei sank about 8 percent, Tokyo fell more than 3 percent and Seoul dropped more than 5 percent, a day after the same markets surged 9 percent, 3.9 percent and more than 6 percent respectively.

Taiwan's TSMC and MediaTek led the decline, alongside Japan's Kioxia and Advantest and South Korea's SK Hynix and Samsung, while Shanghai, Sydney and Singapore also sold off and Hong Kong edged higher. The move followed Wall Street, where Nvidia shed almost 6 percent after Monday's 31 percent gain, Anthropic lost 6.5 percent to fall below its $83 IPO price, and the Nasdaq fell 1.5 percent.
