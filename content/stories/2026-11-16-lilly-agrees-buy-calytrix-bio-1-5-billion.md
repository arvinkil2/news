Eli Lilly has agreed to acquire Calytrix Bio, a Wellesley Hills, Massachusetts company developing precision therapies for myeloproliferative neoplasms, for up to $1.5 billion in cash, Calytrix said on Monday. The price includes an upfront payment and later payments tied to clinical and regulatory milestones, and the company did not disclose the size of the upfront payment.

Calytrix's lead asset is a preclinical biparatopic antibody aimed at mutant calreticulin, the driver of essential thrombocythemia and myelofibrosis in the patients it targets, designed to deplete the mutant clone while sparing normal tissue. Calytrix was founded in 2025 by J.P. Morgan Life Sciences Private Capital, with a Series A co-led by Westlake BioPartners and a Series B led by Avoro Ventures.
