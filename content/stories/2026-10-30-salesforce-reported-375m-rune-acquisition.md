Salesforce has agreed to acquire Rune Technologies for $375 million in cash and stock, TechCrunch reported, citing Resilience Media. Rune, founded in 2024, builds AI software that helps the US military predict supply needs and manage logistics from the field, and it has raised $30 million from investors including Andreessen Horowitz, Point72 Ventures and IQT, the CIA-backed venture firm formerly known as In-Q-Tel. The acquisition would sit inside Missionforce, Salesforce's national security and defense unit, which the company says has grown 80 percent year over year and holds a new 10-year, $5.6 billion contract with the US Army. Salesforce did not respond to a request for comment, and neither company has announced the deal.
