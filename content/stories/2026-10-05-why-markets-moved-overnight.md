Tanker freight rose 5.67% to 873 on the day, extending a 33% weekly surge. The move tracks the war-risk repricing across energy shipping: Gulf crude exports are recovering under convoy and ship-to-ship logistics after the Hormuz attacks, while the Black Sea corn-carrier sinking, which killed the captain of the Royad Mammadov and ended with the vessel lost to fire, keeps conflict premiums in hull and cargo rates. See today's Gulf exports recover and Black Sea corn stories.

Copper miners gained 3.01% and copper itself rose 2.27% to $6.64 a pound, with LME copper at $14,340 a tonne, the highest since May. The trigger was supply-side: mandatory mediation failed at Antofagasta's Centinela mine in Chile, and two unions covering more than 700 workers can strike from October 7, the first strike in the company's history. See today's Centinela and miner-equities stories.

Silver rose 2.1% to $61.24 an ounce, extending a run that has the metal near its highs. The bid combined safe-haven demand from the weekend's escalation in the Black Sea and Gulf theaters with the rate backdrop: traders cut October Fed-hike odds from 70% to 20% in a week even as the long end kept rising. See today's precious metals story.

Dry bulk freight fell 2.2% to 14.2, down 7.3% on the week, with no clear single driver in this morning's reporting. The slide is consistent with China's Golden Week demand lull: Beijing halted fuel exports through the holiday, and the market waits on Thursday's reopening and the fuel-export quota decision around it.

The technology sleeve of today's feed still carries Friday's closes, so its table moves, Tesla up 4.65% and Broadcom up 3.35%, are not scored against today's reporting. Monday's tech tape was led by records: Nvidia rose 2.12% to $238.90 and the Nasdaq closed at 27,477, with Broadcom up 2.08% to $362.51 after Anthropic's IPO prospectus revealed the chipmaker will lend it up to $42 billion, and TSMC up 2.75% to a record on Musk's confirmation of early Terafab talks. See today's Broadcom-Anthropic and FTC probe stories.

Across markets, the read is a split screen. Metals and freight are repricing for conflict logistics and supply disruption, with copper and silver bid and tanker rates surging, while sovereign rates keep tightening from the long end, the 10-year closing at 5.31% and the 30-year at 5.66%. Tech equities pushed to records on AI-financing and demand headlines even as the FTC's new AI-lab probe introduced the first real federal enforcement overhang for the frontier labs. The day ahead turns on Wednesday's double bill of the RBI decision and FOMC minutes, and on whether the freight spike reads as a supply story crude can look through or the start of a broader transport-cost pass-through.
