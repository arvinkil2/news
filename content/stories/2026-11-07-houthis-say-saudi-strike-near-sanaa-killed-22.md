Latest: The Houthi health ministry raised the death toll from the Saudi-led coalition bombing of al-Shariya village near Sanaa to 33, with 39 people wounded, Deutsche Welle reported, citing AFP and Reuters. Health ministry spokesman Anees Alasbahi, posting on X, gave an almost definitive breakdown of 12 men, eight women and 13 children among the dead; the Houthi-run Saba agency had earlier reported 27 dead and said aircraft bombed emergency teams twice while they were recovering victims from the rubble. Saudi Arabia gave no statement on al-Shariya in the report.

Separately, the Saudi-led coalition said its air defences intercepted and destroyed a Houthi ballistic missile launched toward Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province, ERT News reported, citing Reuters. Saudi Civil Defence had issued warning alerts for the city of Dammam and for the al-Ahsa and Abqaiq areas. The Eastern Province holds the kingdom's main oil infrastructure; this interception is separate from the Riyadh-bound missile the coalition reported on Saturday, which is covered below.

The Houthi health ministry said on Saturday that a Saudi airstrike on al-Shariya village near Sanaa killed 22 people and wounded 30, with spokesman Anis al-Asbahi saying most of the victims were women and children, according to the Houthi-run Saba news agency as reported by Yonhap. No independent source has verified the strike and Saudi Arabia gave no immediate response. The Saudi-led coalition said separately on Saturday that it had intercepted and destroyed a Houthi missile fired toward Riyadh, and that an alert it had issued for the southern Najran region had been lifted.
