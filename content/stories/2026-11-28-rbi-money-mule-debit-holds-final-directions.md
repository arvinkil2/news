The Reserve Bank of India has issued final Amendment Directions setting a standard operating procedure for banks to place temporary debit holds, on their own initiative, on amounts or accounts suspected to be linked to money mule activity and cyber-enabled financial frauds, and to remove those holds in coordination with law enforcement agencies. The directions follow a draft published on September 11, 2026, and incorporate modifications from stakeholder feedback.

The procedure applies across commercial banks, small finance banks, payments banks, local area banks, regional rural banks and urban co-operative banks through parallel amendments to each sector's Know Your Customer directions. The directions take effect on July 1, 2027, or earlier if a bank chooses to implement the procedure sooner, and are designed to protect genuine customers from inconvenience while holds are in place.
