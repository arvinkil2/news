Albertsons argues Kroger breached the merger agreement by failing to use its best efforts to win regulatory approval, including refusing to change a store divestiture plan that a federal court found insufficient when it blocked the deal in December 2024. Kroger has said it did everything it could to secure approval and has accused Albertsons of undermining the divestiture proposal with regulators.

The filing turns on contract language under which Kroger agreed to remove antitrust obstacles, short of divesting more than 650 stores. The trial before Judge Lori Will will decide whether that commitment was honoured or whether Kroger held to its own profit targets at the expense of approval.
