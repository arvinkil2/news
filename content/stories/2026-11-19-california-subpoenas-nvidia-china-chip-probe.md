California Attorney General Rob Bonta said on Thursday that his office has served an investigative subpoena on Nvidia, related to the news of former chief executive Jensen Huang's affair with an alleged Chinese spy. The state Department of Justice is asking whether Nvidia's advanced chips or confidential information may have gone to China, and whether Nvidia misled investors and the public; the information received will help the department evaluate whether the company's actions violated California law. "The conduct of Nvidia and its former CEO raises bright red flags, including about the ways in which the company may have misled investors," Bonta said, adding that under California's corporate securities law companies cannot mislead their investors.
