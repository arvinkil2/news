WeRide reported total revenue of RMB321.5 million (US$48.0 million) for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 88.0 percent year over year and 38.8 percent quarter over quarter, with gross margin reaching 43.0 percent, up 10.1 percentage points from a year earlier. Revenue from overseas operations was RMB146.9 million, up 171.5 percent, and accounted for 45.7 percent of the total.

Robotaxi revenue exceeded RMB100 million in a quarter for the first time, up 204.0 percent year over year, with overseas markets contributing more than 90 percent of it, and the company said its Guangzhou robotaxi operations achieved positive unit economics. Its global Level 4 fleet stood at about 4,100 vehicles as of October 31, including more than 2,300 robotaxis. The quarterly loss was RMB310.0 million, against RMB307.3 million a year earlier, and the company held RMB4,663.0 million in cash, time deposits and wealth management products at September 30.
