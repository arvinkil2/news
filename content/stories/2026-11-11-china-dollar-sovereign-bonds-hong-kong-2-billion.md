China's finance ministry will issue US dollar-denominated sovereign bonds of up to $2 billion in Hong Kong in the week of November 16, the ministry announced on Wednesday. The announcement as reported gave the size cap, the currency and the venue, but no tenors or pricing guidance.

The sale adds a dollar deal to a heavy run of offshore sovereign issuance by the ministry through Hong Kong, most recently renminbi sales, and follows its 4 billion yuan green sovereign issue in Canada earlier this month. A dollar sovereign curve point from Beijing is used by bankers as a reference for pricing other Chinese borrowers in the offshore dollar market.
