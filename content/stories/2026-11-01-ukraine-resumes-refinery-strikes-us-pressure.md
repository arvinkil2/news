**Latest (Sunday evening):** President Volodymyr Zelensky has now stated terms for a pause. Speaking to Fox News on Sunday, he said Ukraine would cease attacks on Russian refineries for one month if Washington provided Patriot interceptor systems for one month, that Ukraine would find the money rather than rely only on aid, and that the other route to a pause was Russia stopping its attacks on Ukraine’s energy system. He said Ukraine could not refrain from responding while Russia attacked its energy infrastructure and killed its people, and, asked whether he was concerned the offer would be seen as an attempt to pressure President Trump, said he had no concerns about sharing the truth between partners. The Trump administration on Friday approved the sale of 500 Patriot missiles to Ukraine, according to the State Department, but Zelensky warned that deliveries could take years, and last week he proposed that US and European allies supply missiles from their own stockpiles, to be replenished by the new purchase.

Ukraine has resumed attacks on Russian oil refineries after a pause under US pressure. Russian state media said Ukrainian long-range drones set fire overnight to the Kstovo refinery in Nizhny Novgorod oblast, days after a major strike on the Novokuybyshevsk refinery in Samara oblast, owned by the state energy company Rosneft. The Wall Street Journal had reported that Kyiv had accepted White House pressure to stop bombing Russian energy infrastructure, but Ukrainian officials told the Kyiv Independent they never agreed to a demand from Jared Kushner to pause refinery strikes for 30 days, and Kremlin officials said the raids restarted on Friday and complained Kyiv was “simply ignoring” US wishes.

In that earlier reporting, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy had not acknowledged the refinery attacks. He said Ukrainian forces hit a defence industry enterprise in Russia’s Rostov region and destroyed a Pantsir missile system as part of Ukraine’s “long-range sanctions”, and that Kyiv was obliged to retaliate for Russia’s overnight campaign against energy facilities in four Ukrainian regions, which cut electricity in hundreds of thousands of households.
