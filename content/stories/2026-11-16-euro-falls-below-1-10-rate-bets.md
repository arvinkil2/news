The euro fell to a fresh 19 month low on Monday, dropping as much as 0.2 percent to 1.0998 dollars and trading below the 1.10 dollar threshold for the first time since April 2025. Bloomberg attributed the move to expectations of further interest rate increases in the United States supporting the dollar, which strengthened against nearly all of its Group of 10 peers.
