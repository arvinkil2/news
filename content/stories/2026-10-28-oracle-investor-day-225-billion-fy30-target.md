Oracle set its fiscal 2030 targets at its Investor Day in Las Vegas on October 28, 2026: revenue of $225 billion or more, growth of more than 35 percent a year compounded from fiscal 2026, a non-GAAP operating margin of 40 percent or more, and non-GAAP earnings per share of $21 or more, with fiscal 2027 guidance reaffirmed at revenue of at least $90 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share of $8.10. On funding, the company capped additional capital through fiscal 2030 at $20 billion excluding refinancing, $10 billion of equity and $10 billion of gross debt, on top of a $20 billion at-the-market equity raise already completed in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, and said growth will be self-funded from fiscal 2029, when net debt to adjusted EBITDA is to fall below 3.5 times and free cash flow is to reach at least $30 billion. Capital spending peaks in fiscal 2027 and 2028 and steps down in fiscal 2029; the release gives no cloud revenue or remaining performance obligations figure.
