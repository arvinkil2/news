Kim Jong Un received the credentials of new ambassadors from ten countries, including Russia, India, Vietnam and Sweden, at the Workers' Party headquarters in Pyongyang on Sunday morning, according to the Korean Central News Agency as reported by Yonhap. In his speech, Kim said the world faces a global security instability crisis and that North Korea's core foreign policy values are peace, development and prosperity, adding that it pursues neither hegemony nor expansion and that power and coercion have no place in its foreign policy. He also said Pyongyang does not accept attempts to restrict the independent rights of sovereign states and will do everything to guarantee its own rights and interests, and Yonhap noted it is the first time in more than 30 years that a North Korean supreme leader has received ambassadors' credentials directly.
