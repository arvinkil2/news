Lucid Group said on Monday that the Public Investment Fund, its anchor shareholder, has increased the company's delayed draw term loan facility by $1.75 billion, taking total liquidity to $3.75 billion on a pro forma basis from $2.0 billion at the end of the third quarter. Third quarter revenue was $350 million, up 4 percent year on year, and free cash flow consumption fell 30 percent sequentially to $1.04 billion under the operating reset announced with the second quarter results.

The company said the expanded facility and its cash flow improvement plan, which has delivered $0.8 billion of a $1.4 billion target so far this year, should fund operations and key investments into the fourth quarter of 2027, bridging the period through the start of production of its midsize platform in the second half of 2027. Lucid's second Saudi plant, AMP-2, has entered the final stages of commissioning and is expected to be inaugurated in early 2027.
