Indonesia's sovereign wealth fund Danantara reported a consolidated net profit of Rp259.6 trillion for the full year of 2025 in its first audited results, presented in Jakarta on Wednesday, with total consolidated assets of Rp11,607 trillion and equity of Rp2,980 trillion at the end of the year. Operating revenue was Rp3,073.0 trillion and EBITDA Rp704.7 trillion on the full-year figures, and the fund received an unqualified audit opinion, with Indonesia's Supreme Audit Agency accompanying the audit.

Chief executive Rosan Roeslani said net profit for the nine months from April to December 2025 was Rp203.4 trillion, and stressed that accuracy and integrity of the data mattered more than speed in publishing the first audited accounts. The fund, launched in 2025 to consolidate and manage Indonesia's state-owned enterprises, has been watched by foreign investors for exactly this disclosure.
