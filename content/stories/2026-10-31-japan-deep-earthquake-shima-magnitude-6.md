A magnitude 6.0 earthquake struck at 22:15 UTC on October 31, 58 kilometres south-southeast of Shima, on the southeastern coast of Honshu, Japan, according to the US Geological Survey. The event was deep, with its focus 352.4 kilometres below the surface, which spread the shaking widely but kept it weak at ground level.

The USGS issued a green PAGER alert, indicating a low likelihood of casualties or damage, put the maximum estimated intensity at III on the Modified Mercalli scale, and reported no tsunami threat from the event. No damage or casualties had been reported in the first coverage of the quake.
