Poland's Operational Command said military aviation was operating in Polish airspace on Sunday because of Russian drone strikes on western Ukraine, with ground air defence and radar systems brought to readiness in what it called preventive measures to protect Polish airspace near the threatened areas. The Polish air navigation agency temporarily suspended operations at Lublin and Rzeszow airports because of the military activity, and a search was under way near Lublin for an object that may have violated Polish airspace.

In the Baltic states, Lithuania's army and crisis management centre told residents in central and northern regions to go to shelters over an unrecognized flying object, NATO air policing fighters were sent to the area, and Kaunas airport was closed for about an hour under a red air warning. Latvia issued a yellow air warning in regions bordering Lithuania, and European Pravda reported that debris from a suspected drone was later found in the Lithuanian village of Juodzionys, in the Pasvalys district, where officials thought it may have flown in from Poland.
