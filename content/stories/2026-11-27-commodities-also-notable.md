France has released the tanker DF Peak after six weeks of detention off Reunion. The French Navy boarded the 2006 built vessel on October 12, about 450 nautical miles south of the island, and found its claimed Benin flag registration was not valid. On November 24 a court in Saint Denis convicted the owning company, under a guilty plea procedure, of failing to prove the vessel's nationality under the French Transport Code. The prosecutor's office said a financial confiscation penalty has been paid and the detention lifted; local media put the penalty at about 1 million dollars. The ship, formerly operated by Russia's Sovcomflot and sanctioned by the United States in 2024 and later by the European Union and others, will be supervised until it leaves French waters, and its owner is seeking a new flag registration.
