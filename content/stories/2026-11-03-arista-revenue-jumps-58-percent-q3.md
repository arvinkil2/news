Arista Networks reported revenue of $3.66 billion for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 58.5 percent from $2.31 billion a year earlier and 20.5 percent from the second quarter, with net income of $1.56 billion and diluted earnings of $1.22 per share on a GAAP basis and $1.20 on a non-GAAP basis. The company said the growth rate was its highest since its initial public offering, with a GAAP operating margin of 44.9 percent.

For the fourth quarter Arista expects revenue of $3.9 billion to $4.0 billion, a non-GAAP operating margin of 48 to 49 percent, and non-GAAP diluted earnings of $1.26 to $1.30 per share.
