The Government Accountability Office published five reports on November 10 that belong on the oversight record. GAO-27-108037 examines housing construction innovations and the technologies and policy options that could increase supply and affordability. GAO-27-108667 finds the Treasury should improve the consistency and transparency of its decisions on multilateral bank projects affecting US producers.

GAO-27-108701 covers challenges in measuring the benefits of the IRS's taxpayer service investments. GAO-27-108450 says the office's fraud risk framework could provide a roadmap for states' oversight of grant subrecipients. GAO-27-108908 warns that early planning is critical to assessing the Veterans Affairs Department's new foreclosure avoidance programme. Each is a recommendation document for the incoming Congress rather than a decision, and none is carried as a full story.
