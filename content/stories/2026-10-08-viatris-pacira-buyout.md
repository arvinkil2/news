Viatris said it will pay Pacira investors $36.50 per share in cash and expects the deal to close by the end of 2026. Pacira's two main products, the post-surgical pain drug Exparel and the osteoarthritis knee-therapy Zilretta, booked $692 million in 2025 sales, up about 4 percent. Leerink analyst Daniel Clark called the transaction "immediately accretive" to revenue and a source of margin expansion into the 2030s, though generic erosion of Exparel is likely to begin in early 2030 under patent settlements with Fresenius Kabi and a Hengrui Pharma subsidiary.
