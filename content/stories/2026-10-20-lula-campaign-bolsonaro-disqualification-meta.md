Lula's campaign team will request the disqualification of Flavio Bolsonaro on Tuesday over alleged algorithmic manipulation by Meta aimed at harming Lula's chances, a campaign source told AFP, in a petition to the Superior Electoral Court first revealed by Folha de S. Paulo on Monday. The court on Monday gave Meta 24 hours to prove it was complying with an order to treat both campaigns equally, threatening a daily fine of around $200,000, and Meta said on Tuesday it had completed implementing the measures required; the company denies taking any action to favour one side and has said some Lula campaign ads were mistakenly deactivated because of technical errors. Bolsonaro took 47 percent of the first round vote against Lula's 45 percent, and polls show a technical tie ahead of the runoff on Sunday, October 25.
