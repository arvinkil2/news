Home Depot said Richard McPhail, its executive vice president and chief financial officer since 2019, will become chief executive and join the board on November 23. Ted Decker, who has returned from a temporary medical leave of absence that began in August, will step down as president and CEO and continue as chair of the board. Ann-Marie Campbell was named president and chief operating officer and Jordan Broggi executive vice president and chief financial officer, both also effective November 23.

The leadership change came with third-quarter results: sales of $43.4 billion, up 4.9 percent from a year earlier, with comparable sales up 2.2 percent, net earnings of $3.7 billion or $3.72 per diluted share, and adjusted earnings per share of $3.86 against $3.74 a year earlier. The company raised its fiscal 2026 guidance to total sales growth of about 4.5 percent, the top of its previous range.
