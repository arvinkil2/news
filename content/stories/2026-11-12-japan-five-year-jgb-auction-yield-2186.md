Japan's Ministry of Finance sold five-year government bonds on Thursday at a weighted average yield of 2.186 percent, with the lowest accepted yield at 2.190 percent, in an auction that drew 6,964.6 billion yen of competitive bids for 1,897.2 billion yen accepted, a bid to cover ratio of about 3.7. A further 602.4 billion yen was allotted at the non-competitive auction for JGB market special participants.

The bond, issue number 189 with a 2.2 percent coupon maturing in September 2031, cleared well below October's five-year auction, which printed a 2.379 percent average yield, as the front end of the JGB curve continues to settle around the Bank of Japan's 1.25 percent policy rate.
