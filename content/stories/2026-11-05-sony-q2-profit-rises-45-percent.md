Sony's operating profit rose 45 percent in the second quarter to 622.3 billion yen (3.95 billion dollars), beating market estimates as its games and movies units performed more strongly, Reuters reported on Thursday. The company raised its full year profit outlook by 9 percent, helped by the hit film Spider-Man: Brand New Day, and its shares rose about 7 percent in Tokyo.

The games business is expected to benefit from the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19, but Sony is grappling with higher memory chip prices driven by the AI boom, the same cost pressure Apple flagged this week for its holiday quarter margins.
