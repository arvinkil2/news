Moldova said on Friday that its airspace was violated by Russian unmanned aircraft and that five drones crashed on Moldovan territory within a 17-hour period between Thursday and Friday, in areas of the north and east, ERT News reported, citing APE-MPE and Reuters. One person was injured and limited material damage was caused, and Moldova's foreign ministry said the incidents threatened citizens' safety and showed the direct consequences for regional stability of Russia's war against Ukraine.
