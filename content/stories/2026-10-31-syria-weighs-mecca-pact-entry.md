Syria is evaluating whether to join the Mecca defence pact, Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani said at the Manama Dialogue in Bahrain on Saturday, telling the conference that discussions are currently under way on Syria's entry into the alliance. He said Damascus is an integral part of regional security, would support any framework aimed at protecting regional stability, and stressed that the region needs joint deterrence and defence systems more than ever.

The Mecca Joint Defence Agreement, signed by Saudi Arabia, Turkiye and Pakistan in August, treats an attack on one member as an attack on all, and Pakistan and Turkiye have pledged military support to Riyadh against Houthi attacks, though neither has announced any attacks on the Houthis. Al-Shaibani also said Syria backs Yemen's Saudi-backed internationally recognised government in its fight against the Houthis, and said Israel has carried out more than 1,000 attacks on Syria in less than two years.
