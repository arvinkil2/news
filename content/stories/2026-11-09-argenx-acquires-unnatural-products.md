Argenx said on Monday it has agreed to acquire Unnatural Products, Inc., the Santa Cruz, California biotech developing orally delivered macrocyclic peptides, for an upfront payment of $550 million in cash on hand plus up to $595 million contingent on development and regulatory milestones, for total potential consideration of up to about $1.15 billion. Argenx already holds a minority stake in the company and will acquire all remaining shares; both boards have approved the transaction, which is expected to close by the end of the year subject to customary closing conditions.

The acquisition builds on a two-year multi-target research collaboration between the companies, and Unnatural Products' team and platform will continue in Santa Cruz as argenx's dedicated macrocyclic peptide discovery hub. Morgan Stanley is acting as exclusive financial advisor to argenx, with DLA Piper as legal advisor and Latham & Watkins advising Unnatural Products.
