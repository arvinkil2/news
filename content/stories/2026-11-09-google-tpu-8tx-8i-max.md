Google added two systems to its eighth-generation Tensor Processing Unit line on November 9. TPU 8tx is built for secure and confidential inference: it uses the same silicon as the TPU 8t training system with fifth-generation AMD EPYC host processors, and keeps sensitive data and model weights protected in hardware while they are in active use, a step aimed at regulated enterprises and governments. TPU 8i Max combines two TPU 8i chips on a single package, delivering double the compute, double the on-chip SRAM and double the high-bandwidth memory capacity of the 8i, for workloads that run across very large context windows.

The additions make 2026 the first year Google has introduced four TPU systems, following the 8t and 8i in April, and Google said its TPU business now serves nearly 100 customers. The announcement, made at Google Cloud Next in Tokyo according to Yonhap, carries no availability date or pricing for either new system.
