The European Medicines Agency said its Accelerating Clinical Trials in the EU (ACT EU) advice pilot will become a permanent initiative from January 2027. The procedure, coordinated by the Heads of Medicines Agencies through its Clinical Trials Coordination Group, gives sponsors consolidated technical and regulatory feedback from the Member States concerned before they submit a clinical trial application through the Clinical Trials Information System. Since its 2024 launch the pilot has handled roughly 30 applications, and a final lessons-learned report is due before the end of 2026, with updated guidance and templates landing on the CTCG website in January 2027.
