The Council of the EU adopted the AGILE regulation in under seven months after the Commission tabled it in March 2026, with Parliament thanked for the swift passage. The 115 million euro funding tool will back between 20 and 30 projects through two tracks, mission-driven development of disruptive defence products and market delivery to get solutions into actual markets, and lets firms claim costs retroactively up to three months before a call closes. Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen said AGILE shows Europe is eager to lead in defence innovation, while Defence Commissioner Andrius Kubilius said Ukraine shows modern warfare is won by those who innovate fast; the programme becomes fully operational in early 2027.
