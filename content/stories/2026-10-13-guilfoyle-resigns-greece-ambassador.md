White House press secretary Katie Zacharia told reporters on Tuesday that the administration had asked Guilfoyle to resign, a day after President Trump said he was looking at the allegations against her and was not happy. The reports, led by the Wall Street Journal, alleged that shortly before her confirmation hearing she asked a Trump donor to cover a $100,000 credit card bill in exchange for access, and that in Athens she promoted a Greek construction and energy company's interests and spoke of using US influence against the Greek government. Guilfoyle denied wrongdoing in a statement after the announcement, saying the allegations are false and that she intends to defend her name and reputation.
