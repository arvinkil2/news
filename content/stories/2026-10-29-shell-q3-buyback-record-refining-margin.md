Shell plc reported third quarter adjusted earnings of $10,814 million, up from $9,836 million in the second quarter, with cash flow from operations of $21,043 million, the company said in its results release on Thursday. Reuters reported that the result beat the $10.33 billion average estimate in a company-provided analyst poll and was almost double the $5.43 billion earned a year earlier.

Chemicals and Products, the segment that includes refining, earned $3,855 million as Shell's global indicative refining margin rose to $42 a barrel from $24 in the second quarter, a level the company described as a record, while Integrated Gas earned $3,037 million and Upstream $3,189 million. Shell announced a $5 billion share buyback programme for the next three months, up from the $3 billion pace Reuters reported for the prior quarter.
