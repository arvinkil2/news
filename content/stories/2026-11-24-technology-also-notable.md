Arm chief executive Rene Haas has signed an agreement to remain in his role until 2033, and will also become vice chairman of SoftBank Group from December 1, 2026. The announcement follows reports that Nvidia's board had approached Haas about its then-vacant chief executive position after Jensen Huang's November 12 resignation, a search that ended with Huang's reinstatement. SoftBank owns almost 90 percent of Arm, which it acquired in 2016 for 32 billion dollars.

Vodafone and AST SpaceMobile have switched on what the companies call Europe's first Earth-space station in Malaga, Spain, part of a satellite centre run with the University of Malaga. The centre will validate direct-to-device smartphone broadband over AST SpaceMobile's low Earth orbit constellation in areas with little or no terrestrial coverage, with initial activations already running near Granada, and planned testing for crisis communications, inspection robots and connected vehicles.

CrowdStrike's 43.9 billion dollar all-stock acquisition of Zscaler, announced November 23 alongside record CrowdStrike results, is covered in full in Monday's edition.
