Planet One Group, a UAE headquartered investment company, is committing $933 million to advance Senegal's Falémé iron ore project, its chairman Sanjeev Mansotra told Reuters on Thursday. Planet One holds an 80 percent stake in the project through a special purpose vehicle, with Senegal's government and state mining company MIFERSO holding 10 percent each, and the commitment is to be funded on a 40 to 60 equity to debt ratio.

The first phase will cost about $200 million and cover mining infrastructure and equipment as well as road and railway upgrades over 12 to 18 months, targeting initial output of 3 million to 5 million tonnes a year and peak capacity of 10 million tonnes. Falémé, in south east Senegal, is estimated to hold up to 600 million tonnes of iron ore, with China, India and Africa as potential export markets.
