Russian forces carried out a mass strike on Kharkiv on Monday evening, preliminarily with ten Banderol missiles, injuring eight people, two of them seriously, Kharkiv regional military administration head Oleh Syniehubov said, in an account reported by TSN that has not been independently corroborated. In the Kholodnohirskyi district a strike destroyed an entire entrance section of a four-storey residential building and damaged at least ten private houses, while in the Shevchenkivskyi district a five-storey block was hit, a bank premises on the ground floor caught fire, and six cars burned.

Rescue work was continuing at the sites and people may remain under the rubble of the destroyed buildings, Syniehubov said. The strike is separate from the overnight Russian attack on Naftogaz gas facilities in the Kharkiv region reported earlier on Monday.
