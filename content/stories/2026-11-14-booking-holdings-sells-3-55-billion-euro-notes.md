Booking Holdings has sold 3.55 billion euros of senior unsecured notes in four tranches, the travel company said in its Form 8-K: 1.25 billion euros of 3.950 percent notes due 2029, 800 million euros of 4.375 percent notes due 2032, 750 million euros of 4.750 percent notes due 2035 and 750 million euros of 5.000 percent notes due 2038. The company entered the underwriting agreement on November 9, 2026 with Banco Santander, BNP Paribas, Deutsche Bank's London branch, Goldman Sachs and HSBC Bank as representatives of the underwriters, and executed the officers' certificates establishing the notes on November 13, 2026. Interest on each series is payable on November 13 of each year, beginning November 13, 2027, and the notes rank equally with the company's other senior unsecured obligations.
