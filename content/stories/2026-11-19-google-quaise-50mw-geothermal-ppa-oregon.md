Google has signed an agreement to purchase 50 MW of electricity from Project Obsidian, a geothermal project Quaise Energy is developing near Newberry Volcano in central Oregon, the companies announced this week. The project is intended to supply firm, carbon-free power to Google's data centre operations in The Dalles, Oregon, possibly as early as 2030, and a Quaise spokesperson put the deal's value at more than $500 million. The first phase will use conventional drilling, while a later phase will use Quaise's millimeter-wave drilling technology to reach superhot rock far below the depths of conventional geothermal wells, with the two-stage buildout expected to cost about $350 million.
