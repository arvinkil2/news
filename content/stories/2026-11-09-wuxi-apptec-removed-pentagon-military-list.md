The US Department of Defense has removed WuXi AppTec from its Section 1260H list of Chinese military companies, according to a government posting reported by Reuters on Monday. The deputy secretary of defense made the determination in light of a federal court in Washington enjoining the department from enforcing the designation, which WuXi AppTec had challenged as arbitrary, capricious and the product of political pressure.

The removal complies with the US District Court for the District of Columbia's August 7 order granting WuXi AppTec's motion for a preliminary injunction, and the department said it was acting without prejudice to relisting the company on additional or supplementary grounds. WuXi AppTec, a Shanghai-headquartered contract research and manufacturing group heavily used by Western drugmakers, was designated in June 2026, when the Pentagon expanded the list to 188 entities.
