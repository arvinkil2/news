Anthropic expects to have more than 3.5 gigawatts of computing capacity by the end of this year and aims to double that figure in 2027, to about seven gigawatts, according to New York Times reporting citing people familiar with the matter. The company has been sourcing capacity from neocloud providers including Fluidstack, Lambda and Nscale alongside its major agreements with large technology firms, as it races to secure computing resources for surging demand for its models. Seven gigawatts is roughly enough electricity to power all the homes in Chicago.
