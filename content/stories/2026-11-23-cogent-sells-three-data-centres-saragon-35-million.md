Cogent Communications, through its subsidiary Cogent Fiber, LLC, has entered into a definitive agreement to sell data centre facilities in Omaha, Nebraska, Tacoma, Washington, and Cheyenne, Wyoming to Saragon Infrastructure for an aggregate $35 million in cash. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, and Cogent did not disclose facility-level details.

Saragon was launched by I Squared Capital earlier this year, seeded with ten data centres across nine US markets previously acquired from Cogent for $225 million. The sites trace back to switching facilities built for Sprint's long-haul fibre network in the 1980s and 1990s, which Cogent acquired in 2022 and has been converting to colocation space.
