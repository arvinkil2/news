A tanker travelling from the Persian Gulf through the Strait of Hormuz was hit by an unknown projectile seven nautical miles northeast of Limah, Oman, RIA Novosti reported on Friday, citing the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations centre, which said it had received the report late. No casualties were reported and the tanker continued on its way, according to the report, which named no vessel, flag or cargo and described no specific damage beyond the hit. No independent outlet had corroborated the incident as of writing.
