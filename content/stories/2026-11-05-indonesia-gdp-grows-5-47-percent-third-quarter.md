Indonesia's economy expanded 5.47 percent from a year earlier in the third quarter of 2026, Statistics Indonesia (BPS) announced on Thursday, above the 5.18 percent median forecast in a Reuters poll and faster than the 5.29 percent recorded in the second quarter. Growth from the previous quarter was 1.59 percent, and cumulative growth through the first three quarters of the year was 5.45 percent.

Manufacturing was among the sectors supporting growth, in particular the processing of palm oil into fuel as Indonesia expanded its mandatory biodiesel programme. GDP at current prices reached 6,741 trillion rupiah in the quarter. The result keeps the government's 5.4 percent growth target for 2026 in view, against President Prabowo Subianto's stated goal of lifting annual growth to 8 percent by 2029.
