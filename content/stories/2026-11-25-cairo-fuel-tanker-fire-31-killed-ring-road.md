A fuel tanker overturned on the Ring Road in Giza's Waraq district on Wednesday evening and caught fire, killing 31 people and injuring 21, Egypt's health ministry said. The fire left at least 27 vehicles, including cars and motorcycles, charred, and the injured were taken to nearby hospitals with burns and smoke inhalation.

The health ministry said 39 ambulances were sent to the scene, and Health Minister Khaled Abdel Ghaffar warned the death toll could rise further. Egypt's public prosecutor has opened an investigation, with DNA testing under way to identify badly burned victims, and President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi directed authorities to support the families of the dead and injured.
