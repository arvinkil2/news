China's State Taxation Administration announced on Wednesday that the transition period for taxing offshore trust income has ended smoothly, with cumulative individual income tax declarations from offshore trusts exceeding 160 billion yuan. More than 120 billion yuan of that total was declared in October alone, contributing to a nearly 90 percent increase in national individual income tax revenue for that month.

The figures close the 90 day window opened on July 24, 2026, when the Ministry of Finance and the State Taxation Administration jointly required taxpayers with offshore trust income to declare and pay within the transition period without late fees. An official in the administration's Income Tax Department said taxpayers who fail to declare, or declare inaccurately, after the deadline will be urged to comply and will face late fees, with investigations and public exposure in serious cases, and action against intermediaries who induce or assist tax evasion.
