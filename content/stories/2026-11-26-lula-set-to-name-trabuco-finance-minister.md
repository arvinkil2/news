Latest: Bradesco chairman Luiz Carlos Trabuco Cappi has accepted President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's invitation to be Brazil's next finance minister, a person close to the matter told Reuters on Thursday, confirming reporting first published by the magazine Veja. Neither Bradesco nor the presidency responded to Reuters requests for comment, and Lula is expected to announce a first group of ministers for his new government next week, after he returns from the Mercosur summit in Montevideo, Uruguay. Reuters reported that Brazil's benchmark stock index rose 6 percent on Thursday as the news circulated.

President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva is set to announce Bradesco chairman Luiz Carlos Trabuco Cappi as finance minister, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Bloomberg on Thursday. The report said Lula was turning to one of Brazil's best known bankers to restore investor confidence in the nation's finances.

No announcement had been made by the government at the time of writing, and Bloomberg gave no date for one in the portion of its report that could be read. Folha de S.Paulo had reported a day earlier that Trabuco was an option for the post, noting unease in parts of Lula's Workers' Party at the prospect of a banker in the finance ministry.
