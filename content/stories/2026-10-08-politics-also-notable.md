European Parliament adopted three human-rights resolutions on October 8: on Iran, denouncing the death sentences of Taraneh Rahimi and Leila Abolhasani and calling for gender apartheid to be recognised as a crime against humanity (adopted 541-11-25); on Georgia, condemning Georgian Dream's attempts to ban opposition parties and naming Ivanishvili, Kobakhidze, Papuashvili and Kaladze for targeted sanctions (445-37-97); and on Azerbaijan, making any future EU partnership conditional on human-rights improvements including the release of political prisoners (492-23-60). Sources: European Parliament press releases, October 8.

Parliament's Legal Affairs Committee proposed amendments to the members' statute on proxy voting on October 8, a procedural item that could tighten how MEPs cast votes on behalf of absent colleagues. Source: European Parliament press release, October 8.

Michigan's Senate candidates, Republican Mike Rogers and Democrat Abdul El-Sayed, faced off for the first time in a debate on October 7, in a race Cook now rates as one the GOP hopes can limit Democratic gains. Source: The Hill (via feed), October 7.

The Holborn and St Pancras parliamentary by-election got under way on October 8, the first electoral test of the UK government's standing since the general election cycle. Source: BBC News, October 8.

More than 300 hopefuls signed up for Hong Kong's Election Committee poll as nominations opened, the first step in the city's tightly managed electoral cycle. Sources: SCMP, October 7.

Kenya's electoral commission (IEBC) stepped up election-security preparedness ahead of the 2027 polls. Source: KBC Kenya, October 8.

In Zambia, opposition figure Mbita Chitala declared the August election not credible and called for a unity government and an audit of 13,529 polling stations, a dispute with potential to unsettle the country's political settlement. Source: Lusaka Times, October 8.

In Nigeria, Kano governor Abba Yusuf reconciled his deputy and Senate President Barau with former lawmaker Bichi ahead of 2027 positioning, while the Chief Justice of Nigeria warned tribunal judges their conduct will be monitored in election cases. Sources: Vanguard NG, October 7-8.

Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni attended the MED9 Summit in Split on October 7, the Mediterranean leaders' format, days after pushing her electoral reform through the Chamber. Source: Governo Italiano, October 7.

Malaysia's 2027 budget is tabled tomorrow, October 9, at 3:30pm MYT, with the fuel-subsidy bill the hard fiscal constraint on a pre-election package. Covered in the existing story 2026-10-07-malaysia-budget-2027.md; no new delta.
