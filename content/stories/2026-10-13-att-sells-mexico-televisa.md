AT&T said the sale fits its plan to prioritise its US fibre and wireless networks, and that it will report the Mexico business as held for sale from its third quarter filing. Televisa brings a large Mexican fixed line broadband operation and Fintech Holdings brings capital and wireless operating experience, a combination AT&T said is well placed to run the business through its next phase. The company said US customers who roam or do business in Mexico should see no change to their service before or after closing.
