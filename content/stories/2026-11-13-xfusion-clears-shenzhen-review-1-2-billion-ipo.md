The Shenzhen Stock Exchange listing committee said in a filing dated November 12 that xFusion Digital Technologies met the requirements on share issuance, listing and disclosure, and that there were no further matters for the company to address, Reuters reported. xFusion plans to raise 8 billion yuan (1.2 billion dollars) in the offering on ChiNext, the exchange's growth board, according to its May prospectus, with CITIC Securities as sponsor.

The committee asked xFusion to explain the trend in its gross margin and whether growth in its operating performance could be sustained, citing changes in demand for computing capacity, capital spending by downstream customers, its technology, net operating cash flow and concentration among customers and suppliers, and whether the related risks had been adequately disclosed. xFusion makes computing infrastructure, including AI servers, according to the prospectus.
