**Bundesbank stability review.** The Deutsche Bundesbank published its 2026 Financial Stability Review on Thursday, the German central bank's annual assessment of risks in Europe's largest financial system, released into a week in which the STOXX 600 has fallen 5.18 percent.

**Ueda addresses the Diet.** Bank of Japan Governor Ueda delivered the Bank's statement on its Semiannual Report on Currency and Monetary Control to the Senate Committee on Financial Affairs on Thursday, a day before the Nikkei 225 closed 5.06 percent lower in the Asia selloff.

**Long end steady.** The US 30-year Treasury yield eased 1.1 basis points to 5.40 percent in this site's morning tables, down 3.2 basis points on the week, even as equity markets sold off for a second session. Yesterday's Treasury buyback result, $254 million accepted in the 7 to 10 year bucket, is covered in the November 18 buyback story, updated with the operation's outcome.
