President Lee Jae-myung visited the SK AI Data Center construction site in Ulsan on Saturday, said the centre is nearing completion, thanked SK for the speed of the build and promised government support, casting AI data centres as core infrastructure and a major source of semiconductor demand. He flagged a severe shortage of construction workers for advanced facilities, noting that semiconductor fabs still have to be built, and said ministries would think hard about preparing the workforce because speed is decisive in global competition. The visit was Lee's second consecutive weekly inspection of a mega-project site, after the Samsung Onyang campus groundbreaking on November 12, and he cast the Ulsan project, built outside the capital region, as a test of whether such projects can create growth momentum in the regions.
