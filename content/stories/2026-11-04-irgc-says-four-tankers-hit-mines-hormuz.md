Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said four oil tankers were destroyed after striking naval mines while trying to traverse a route it described as prohibited south of the Strait of Hormuz, and that the tankers were still ablaze, Reuters reported on Wednesday. The Guards said two other vessels had been seized in the same waters. The statement named no vessel and carried no independent confirmation, and it follows the Guards' November 1 claim that two tankers had exploded on mines in the strait, which United States Central Command had already disputed as a threat to the strait's main transit routes.
