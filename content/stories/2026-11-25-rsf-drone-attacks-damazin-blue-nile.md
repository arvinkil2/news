Drone attacks by the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces hit multiple sites across Damazin, the capital of Sudan's Blue Nile state, and the neighbouring town of Roseires late on Tuesday, killing at least 19 people and wounding at least 20, Al Jazeera reported, citing the Emergency Lawyers rights group. Six women were among the dead, the group said, and the targets included electricity facilities in Damazin and a large dam in Roseires; a Sudanese military source told Al Jazeera that three families were killed in the bombing of a residential area in Damazin.

The attacks come amid a sharp escalation in drone use by both the RSF and the Sudanese Armed Forces in recent days, following November 22 drone attacks around Khartoum and Omdurman that killed at least four people and damaged a United Nations building. Blue Nile, in Sudan's southeast, neighbours some of the country's most unstable regions and is home to some of the army's most experienced fighters.
