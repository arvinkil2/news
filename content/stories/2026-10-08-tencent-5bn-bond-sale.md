Tencent is weighing an offshore bond sale of as much as $5 billion to fund its AI push, Bloomberg reported Thursday, citing people familiar with the matter. The notes could be denominated in dollars and offshore yuan, with a sale possibly as early as this month. The move would follow Tencent's nearly $4.7 billion June issuance, its largest debt deal since 2020, and last month's $11.1 billion SoftBank junk-bond offering for AI plans.
