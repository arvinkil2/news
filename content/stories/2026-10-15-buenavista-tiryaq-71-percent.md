Buenavista Equity Partners has agreed to buy a 71 percent stake in Tiryaq Medical, a Spanish trauma care group, PE Hub reported on October 15. Terms were not disclosed in the reporting available at write time, and no company or adviser release confirming the price, structure or closing timetable was located.

Buenavista is a Madrid based independent manager across private equity, infrastructure and venture capital, with about 1.3 billion euros under management and a dedicated healthcare growth strategy. A controlling stake in a trauma care provider fits that strategy's focus on Spanish healthcare services with consolidation potential.

Because the item rests on a single trade report behind a paywall, it ships at grade C. The 71 percent figure and the identity of buyer and target are as reported by PE Hub; valuation, sellers and regulatory conditions are unverified and are not stated here.
