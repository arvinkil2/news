Mineros (TSX: MSA) has secured 250 million dollars (C$356.11 million) in credit facilities through two subsidiaries to support its projects in Nicaragua and Chile. Its Swiss subsidiary has a 150 million dollar syndicated facility arranged by Banco BTG Pactual's Cayman branch, and Hemco Mineros Nicaragua has a 100 million dollar facility with Banco de América Central. Both facilities run for five years at Term SOFR plus 4.975 percent a year, can be drawn in whole or in part during the first year, and are repaid in eight instalments beginning 18 months after closing.

The financing advances the Porvenir polymetallic project in Nicaragua, where Mineros is preparing for a potential construction decision by early 2027, and the La Pepa gold project in Chile's Maricunga belt, where a 7,000 metre exploration campaign is expected to begin in December. Mineros shares rose 2.72 percent to C$7.19 in Thursday afternoon trading in Toronto.
