A magnitude 7.2 earthquake struck offshore in northern Vanuatu at 02:31 UTC on Saturday (13:31 local time), centred 200 kilometres west of Sola on Vanua Lava island at a depth of 10 kilometres, the US Geological Survey said. It came about 25 seconds after a magnitude 6.9 earthquake at almost the same place, 197 kilometres west-northwest of Port-Olry, also at 10 kilometres deep, in what seismologists call a doublet.

The US Tsunami Warning Centers listed a tsunami threat for the magnitude 7.2 event. The Geological Survey's solution for the larger quake is automatic and may be revised as more data arrive, and its reviewed assessment of the magnitude 6.9 quake carried a green alert, indicating a low likelihood of casualties and damage. No reports of casualties or damage had emerged in the first minutes after the quakes.
