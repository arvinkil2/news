The Reserve Bank of India announced four regulatory measures for the foreign-exchange market, citing evolving conditions and the need for orderly market functioning. Authorized dealers may no longer let users rebook cancelled INR foreign-exchange derivative contracts, though rollovers at maturity remain permitted. The threshold for taking FX derivative positions without establishing underlying exposure was cut from $100 million to $5 million equivalent, and dealers must obtain undertakings that the same exposure is not hedged elsewhere.
