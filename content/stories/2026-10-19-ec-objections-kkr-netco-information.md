The European Commission said on Monday that it had sent KKR a Statement of Objections setting out its preliminary view that the firm, at least negligently, submitted incorrect or misleading information on the terms of FiberCop's existing long-term wholesale access agreements, including that their terms would continue to apply and could only be changed by mutual agreement. Brussels cleared KKR's acquisition of NetCo, Telecom Italia's primary and backbone network together with FiberCop, unconditionally on May 30, 2024, and says its clearance relied on those submissions. The Commission opened a formal investigation on July 24, 2025 after rival operators complained that FiberCop had unilaterally raised the prices they pay for network access, and KKR can now inspect the file, reply in writing and request an oral hearing.
