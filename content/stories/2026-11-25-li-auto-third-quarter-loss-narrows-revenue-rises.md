Li Auto reported a third-quarter net loss of 309.7 million renminbi (46.2 million dollars), half the 624.4 million renminbi loss of a year earlier and down 81.8 percent from the second quarter, as total revenues rose 5.4 percent year on year to 28.8 billion renminbi. Deliveries rose 7.2 percent year on year to 99,964 vehicles, and gross margin recovered to 16.7 percent from 11.0 percent in the second quarter.

Vehicle margin rose to 15.3 percent from 9.4 percent in the second quarter, while the operating loss narrowed to 699.6 million renminbi. The company ended September with 485 retail stores in 160 Chinese cities and 4,188 super charging stations in operation.
