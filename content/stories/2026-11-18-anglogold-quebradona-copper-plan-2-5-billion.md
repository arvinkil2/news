AngloGold Ashanti plans to spend $2.5 billion to build its Quebradona copper project in Colombia if it secures an environmental licence that has eluded the company since its first application in 2019. The underground project, about 60 kilometres southwest of Medellín in Antioquia, could produce about 80,000 tonnes of copper a year and generate about $1.5 billion in annual exports, the company said at an investment event in New York.

The timetable is not in the company's hands. A 2025 order created a temporary protected area covering the project and bars new environmental licences for mining in the zone into mid-2028, and Colombia's licensing agency shelved the application in 2021 over gaps in studies on groundwater, tailings storage and ground subsidence. Chief executive Alberto Calderón said he expects the licensing process to take 12 to 18 months, with construction possible from 2028 and about $2 billion spent in the first two years if the licence is granted.
