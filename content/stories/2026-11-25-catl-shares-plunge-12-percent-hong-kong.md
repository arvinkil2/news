CATL's Hong Kong-listed shares plunged 12.2 percent to HK$295.6 on Wednesday, the second-worst performance among blue chips, as Bloomberg reported that investors were selling on fears over the battery maker's weak earnings outlook and signs of forced selling by leveraged holders. The fall came as the Hang Seng Index dropped for a seventh straight day, closing down 253 points, or 1.14 percent, at 21,966 on turnover of HK$229.976 billion, according to Longbridge's close report.
