India's bank unions have announced a nationwide strike for December 3 in protest at the planned sale of IDBI Bank to Canada's Fairfax Financial Holdings, the second such action in two months after a two-day strike on November 2 and 3, as unions press the government to cancel the transaction. Separately, the United Forum of IDBI Officers and Employees has asked Reserve Bank of India Governor Sanjay Malhotra to delay approval of the deal and order an independent review of Fairfax's banking credentials, the valuation, and its holdings in other lenders, including its 40 percent stake in CSB Bank. Fairfax agreed last month to buy a 60.72 percent stake in IDBI, which employs more than 19,000 people, and a senior official at the Department of Investment and Public Asset Management told CNBC-TV18 that employees' service terms and pension benefits would remain protected.
