The Food and Drug Administration has accepted for review uniQure's biologics license application for ifezuntirgene inilparvovec, also called AMT-130, an investigational gene therapy for Huntington's disease filed under the accelerated approval pathway, and granted it priority review with a target action date of May 1, 2027. In its acceptance letter the agency said it tentatively plans to discuss the application at an advisory committee meeting, with the date still to be set. The application rests on 36 month Phase I/II results compared with a matched external control group from the Enroll-HD natural history study, and a confirmatory trial of about 200 patients is expected to start screening by the end of 2026.
