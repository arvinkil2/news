The FDA disclosed the schedule in a Wednesday court filing, saying the agency's review
of mifepristone's safety would produce a report in March 2027, with the underlying study
results expected in mid-December. The agency also said no new restrictions on the drug
would be imposed before the review concludes.

Separately, the FDA is reviewing mifepristone's REMS safety program, a process the agency
said would take longer than the safety review itself.
