Powerful explosions were heard in Kyiv on Sunday evening after Ukraine's Air Force warned at 22:57 local time of a first ballistic missile heading for the capital and the city military administration declared a ballistic threat alert, Ukrainska Pravda reported. Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko reported a hit in the Desnianskyi district, with preliminary reports pointing to a medical centre building, and a hit on a non-residential building in the Dniprovskyi district, TSN reported. The Kyiv City Express ring railway suspended services in both directions while the alert lasted, and no casualty figures had been reported in the reporting opened.
