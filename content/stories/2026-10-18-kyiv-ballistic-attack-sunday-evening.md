**Latest (Sunday 17:44 ET):** One person was killed and two others were injured in the attack, the Kyiv city military administration said, and educational institutions were damaged in the Dniprovskyi district, Ukrainska Pravda reported. The injured were receiving medical care.

Powerful explosions were heard in Kyiv on Sunday evening after Ukraine's Air Force warned at 22:57 local time of a first ballistic missile heading for the capital and the city military administration declared a ballistic threat alert, Ukrainska Pravda reported. Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko reported a hit in the Desnianskyi district, with preliminary reports pointing to a medical centre building, and a hit on a non-residential building in the Dniprovskyi district, TSN reported. The Kyiv City Express ring railway suspended services in both directions while the alert lasted.
