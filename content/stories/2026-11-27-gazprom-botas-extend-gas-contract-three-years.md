Gazprom and Turkey's state gas importer Botaş have signed a three-year extension of their gas supply contract, Russian Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak told journalists at the Russian-Turkish Business Forum in Istanbul on Friday, Interfax reported. Novak said the terms of the deal were as before and volumes were the same.

Russia supplied 21 billion cubic metres of gas to Turkey by pipeline in 2025 and almost 15 billion cubic metres in the first ten months of this year, Interfax said. Turkey imports almost all of the roughly 50 billion cubic metres of gas it consumes each year, receiving most of it by pipeline from Russia, Azerbaijan and Iran.
