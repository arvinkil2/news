MTN Group has revived talks over a possible takeover of rival carrier Telkom, confirming in a stock exchange filing on Monday that discussions are at an early stage, Datacenter Dynamics reported. Telkom said in a separate filing that there is no guarantee the talks will lead to an agreement, and no price or structure has been disclosed.

The approach comes four years after MTN walked away from merger talks with Telkom, a process that was also disrupted by rival operator Rain's proposal that Telkom acquire it instead. If a deal succeeds, it would combine MTN's 39 million South African mobile customers with Telkom's 25.28 million subscribers, surpassing Vodacom's 46.28 million to create the country's largest mobile carrier, and would give MTN Telkom's fibre infrastructure and enterprise operations.
