Mirae Asset Securities reported a consolidated operating loss of 502.3 billion won in the third quarter of 2026, against an operating profit of 1,622.3 billion won a year earlier, with a net loss of 405.6 billion won and revenue of 7.5005 trillion won, down 7.1 percent from a year earlier, Yonhap reported. The operating loss was smaller than the 596.4 billion won loss in the Yonhap Infomax consensus.

The swing came from a weak domestic stock market and valuation losses on investment assets, above all SpaceX, whose share price fell from $170.86 at the end of June to $150.86 at the end of September. Mirae Asset put the SpaceX related pre-tax loss at about 514 billion won in the quarter and said that, excluding proprietary investment losses, recurring profit was about 380 billion won; brokerage commission revenue fell 47 percent from the previous quarter to 330.6 billion won as trading value dropped. The firm also announced a 2027 to 2029 shareholder return plan under which it will cancel 65 million treasury shares, worth 1.4 trillion won at the November 11 close, after next year's shareholders' meeting.
