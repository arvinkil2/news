Bank of Canada Governor Tiff Macklem said the federal government and the Bank renewed Canada's monetary policy framework earlier this month for another five years, keeping the 2 percent inflation target at the midpoint of a 1 to 3 percent control range. Total CPI inflation stays front and centre in the Bank's communications, he said, with core measures treated as indicators rather than targets, and the Bank will lean a little more on CPI-trim and CPIX in judging underlying inflation.

On how policy should handle supply shocks, Macklem said inflation is likely to be pushed off course more frequently and for longer in a more shock-prone world, and that the key judgment is whether price pressures will fade or persist. If inflation goes up but is expected to come down on its own, the Bank generally does not need to respond, he said, but if it stays too high for too long the policy rate would need to rise.
