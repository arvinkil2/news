Kuznetsov, 50, was arrested in Italy in August 2025 and is the only defendant in custody, with prosecutors saying they have identified seven suspects and alleging in the indictment that Ukrainian state bodies knew of the operation, a claim President Volodymyr Zelensky has denied. His defense argues the pipelines, which funded Russia's war, were a legitimate military target and has asked the court to dismiss the case. Hearings are scheduled through January with a verdict expected next year, and the Kremlin said it expects the trial to name who ordered the attack.
