India's coal, power and railway ministries are working jointly to raise the country's coal stockpiles to 55 million tons by the end of the fiscal year on March 31, from 18 million tons now, Bloomberg reported, citing unnamed sources. Stocks are at their lowest since 2021 and cover only about six days of coal fired power generation, against a government requirement of at least three weeks, with half of India's coal plants critically low last month after monsoon disruptions tightened supply and transport. Indian Railways raised coal carriage capacity by 20 percent last month and has been told to add a further 7 percent by March, while the International Energy Agency expects India's total coal demand to rise 4.2 percent this year to 1.353 billion tons.
