Japan's Government Pension Investment Fund lost 5,253.3 billion yen in the three months to September 30, a return of minus 1.65 percent, reversing the June quarter's gain of 24,089.5 billion yen. Foreign bonds accounted for nearly the whole loss, falling 6.52 percent, or 5,230.1 billion yen, as the US 10 year Treasury yield climbed to 5.28 percent from 4.47 percent and the yen strengthened to 157.14 per dollar from 162.53, cutting the yen value of overseas holdings. Domestic bonds lost 1.80 percent as the 10 year Japanese government bond yield rose to 3.07 percent from 2.68 percent, and foreign equities fell 1.86 percent, while domestic equities gained 3.71 percent.

Total assets stood at 314,387.6 billion yen at the end of September, down from 317,759.6 billion yen three months earlier. For the fiscal year to date the fund is still ahead by 18,836.3 billion yen, a return of 6.42 percent.
