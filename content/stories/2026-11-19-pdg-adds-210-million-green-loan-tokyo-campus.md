Princeton Digital Group has secured an additional $210 million in green financing for its TY1 campus in Saitama, Greater Tokyo, taking the total raised for the 96MW campus to $327 million. The loan, provided by BNP Paribas, ING, OCBC and Société Générale under PDG's Green Finance Framework, funds the campus's development and its energy-efficiency work.

The Singapore-headquartered operator, backed by Warburg Pincus, Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Mubadala and Stonepeak, says the raise sits inside a plan to lift up to $5 billion in debt financing across its Asia-Pacific buildout, where its portfolio exceeds 1.8GW. TY1 already carries LEED v4, OCP Ready v2 for Hyperscale and Nvidia DGX-Ready certifications.
