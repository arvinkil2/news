The European Commission published a Eurobarometer survey on Wednesday highlighting growing use of artificial intelligence in European companies. The release, IP/26/2160, arrived through the official feed; its detailed figures were not stated here because the release text did not render in full in this run.

South Africa's deputy president Paul Mashatile was scheduled to address the eighth national congress of the FEDUSA trade union federation on Wednesday, with SABC carrying the address live. Union congresses in South Africa feed directly into the governing alliance's labour politics ahead of next year's budget cycle.

In Ghana, the Speaker of Parliament told parliamentary counsel that their client is Parliament, not a party, and that they should remain honest even when the advice is unwelcome. The remark, reported by MyJoyOnline, is a small institutional signal in a legislature where legal advice to the chair is politically contested.

Nigeria's electoral commission INEC said political parties have 14 days to notify it over any omission of candidates from the final list for a governorship election. The notice starts a short correction window; omissions left unchallenged after it closes become much harder to reverse.

Nigeria's Senate and House of Representatives began consideration of President Tinubu's request for a 100.5 billion naira reimbursement connected to Yobe airport spending. The item is at consideration stage; no approval was reported.

Former Niger Delta militant leader Government Tompolo mobilised supporters in Lagos for Tinubu's 2027 re election, pleading against a repeat of the 2023 outcome in the city. The event is part of the same cross regional incumbency build out covered in today's Nigeria campaign story.

Australia's ambassador to the United States, Kevin Rudd, warned of tariff risks while describing US China ties as stable, in Bloomberg video remarks on Wednesday. No new policy accompanied the comments; they are carried here as a marker of allied messaging on trade ahead of the G20 finance meetings.
