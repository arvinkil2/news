Chinese regulators have told SoftBank Group that they will not allow its $5.4 billion acquisition of ABB's robotics division, the Financial Times reported, citing multiple people familiar with the matter. SoftBank was informed that China's National Development and Reform Commission would block the deal, and the company is now trying to determine what went wrong and whether it can seek approval again, according to the report.

China is the biggest market for ABB's robotics unit, and robotics is a major part of chief executive Masayoshi Son's push into physical artificial intelligence. SoftBank shares fell 5 percent in Tokyo on Thursday, against a 0.5 percent fall in the Nikkei 225, with the stock also pressured by an overnight fall in Arm Holdings after the chip designer's current quarter guidance underwhelmed.
