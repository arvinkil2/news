Switzerland's Consumer Price Index increased by 0.2 percent in October 2026 compared with the previous month, reaching 101.7 points on the December 2025 equals 100 base, the Federal Statistical Office said in Neuchatel on Tuesday. Inflation was 1.5 percent compared with the same month of the previous year.

The monthly increase was led by energy and clothing. Prices rose for heating oil, petrol and diesel, for women's and men's footwear, and for outpatient medical services in hospitals, the office said.

Working the other way, prices for medical services at local surgeries and for international package holidays decreased, as did prices for hotels. The composition matters for the read through to policy: the October gain was concentrated in fuel and a seasonal clothing category rather than in domestic services, even as hospital outpatient charges added to the services side.
