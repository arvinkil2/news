Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said air defences destroyed another 13 drones approaching the capital in the early hours of Tuesday, taking the overnight tally to 23, after he reported 41 drones attempting to attack the city on Monday. Emergency crews were working at debris fall sites, Interfax reported. Russia's aviation authority Rosaviatsiya said Vnukovo airport had suspended arrivals and departures, with Zhukovsky and Domodedovo airports handling flights only by coordination, the second consecutive night of restrictions on the capital's airports.
