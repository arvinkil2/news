Natural gas rose 2.35 percent to 3.49 dollars at Friday's close in the estate market tables, extending the cold forecast rally covered in yesterday's edition, in which the December contract settled up 4.38 percent at 3.411 dollars against a fund net short reported at about 235,983 contracts for the week ended November 10, the largest in almost seven years. The December contract expires on November 25, and the January contract's premium over December is the market's price for the first full winter month.

The European Union is supporting the interconnection of Greece's Dodecanese islands to the mainland grid with a 228.9 million euro grant under the Public Sector Loan Facility, the largest award under that facility to date, alongside a 1.53 billion euro European Investment Bank loan and a 534 million euro grant from the Greek Decarbonisation Fund for a first phase budgeted at 2.54 billion euros. Phase one, implemented by transmission operator IPTO, builds a high voltage direct current submarine link between Corinth and Kos with two converter stations; a second phase adds alternating current links to Rhodes and Karpathos. CINEA said phase one alone is expected to cut carbon dioxide emissions by around 7.7 million tonnes over 25 years by replacing oil-fired island generation.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday that Ukrainian forces sank the Russian corvette Merkury off Libya with naval drones, claiming the Benghazi attack that Libyan officials had described on Sunday without naming a perpetrator, and naming a second target, the dry cargo ship Angara. The full story is carried and updated in this site's politics coverage of the Benghazi explosion; for commodity markets the significance is the extension of the sea drone campaign into the Mediterranean and the risk it adds to Russian-linked shipping far from the Black Sea. In the Gulf of Finland, the sanctioned cargo ship Sparta II remained at anchor in Finnish waters after Finnish vessels fought its fire through Saturday night, a story also carried in the politics coverage and updated on November 22.
