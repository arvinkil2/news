Intersnack Group has acquired all outstanding Class A shares of Utz Brands for $14.25 a share in cash, a premium of about 91 percent to the unaffected closing price on July 20, 2026, completing the previously announced take-private after stockholder approval at a special meeting on November 13. Utz's Class A stock has ceased trading on the New York Stock Exchange and will be delisted, and the business is now held by Utz Brands Holdings, LLC, owned equally by Intersnack and affiliated entities of the Rice and Lissette family. BofA Securities advised Intersnack and provided committed debt financing for the transaction, the companies said.
