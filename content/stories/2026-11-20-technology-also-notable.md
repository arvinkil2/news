**Alibaba Cloud expands in Malaysia.** Alibaba Cloud has launched its sixth data centre in Malaysia, Datacenter Dynamics reported, continuing the buildout of Southeast Asian cloud capacity by Chinese providers as regional AI demand grows. The launch follows a week in which the sector's US-listed names sold off sharply.

**Meta's Muse Charm may cost $300.** Meta's new Muse Charm device may cost $300, TechCrunch and The Verge reported, giving the first price indication for the company's next consumer hardware device.

**CISA issues ICS advisories.** The US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency published industrial control system advisories covering Elecbrakes EB2, VM1 and ELBC2000 products, Autonomy Logic's OpenPLC Runtime and the libumqtt library, the latest in its running series of operational technology vulnerability notices.
