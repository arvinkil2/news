Xiaomi posted a 57 percent fall in third-quarter adjusted net profit on Tuesday, reporting 4.88 billion yuan against an analyst average of 5.2 billion yuan compiled by LSEG, according to Reuters. Revenue came in at 107.24 billion yuan, down 5.2 percent year on year but above the average estimate, while profit for the period fell 68.4 percent to 3.88 billion yuan. The company pointed to rising memory component costs and intensified smartphone competition as the pressures on margins.
