**Latest (Sunday evening):** Alberta Premier Danielle Smith said on Sunday that 39 people were hospitalised after the fire, including at least 19 in critical condition, with patients transferred from Wainwright to four hospitals across Alberta, and some suffering severe burns that could face weeks or months of intensive treatment. Prime Minister Mark Carney confirmed that four service members were killed in the fire, which broke out on Saturday during military training.

**Latest (Sunday 14:30 ET):** Four service members died and dozens were wounded in the fire, which ignited in the base's training area late on Saturday afternoon and spread rapidly in high winds and dry conditions, beyond the base toward the Municipal District of Wainwright. Edmonton hospitals declared a Code Orange, the alert for an external disaster or mass casualty incident, and the local health facility served as the first reception point for casualties. Prime Minister Mark Carney called it one of the worst tragedies for Canadian forces on home soil in peacetime and praised the coordinated response; Defence Minister David McGuinty said he could not recall an incident like this and travelled to the region, and the military said on Sunday there was no indication the fire was an act of terrorism. The cause is not yet known and an investigation is under way, with fuller detail expected after next of kin are notified.

A major fire broke out at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright, about 180 kilometres southeast of Edmonton, on Saturday after what Prime Minister Mark Carney, in a post on X, called a serious incident at the base, and Defence Minister David McGuinty later confirmed there had been fatalities and injuries, without giving numbers. The Municipal District of Wainwright said the fire, spreading rapidly in high winds, had crossed the base fence and that emergency crews were focused on protecting lives, homes and other structures. Alberta Premier Danielle Smith called the fire significant and tragic in a social media post, and Saskatchewan and British Columbia offered resources and assistance.
