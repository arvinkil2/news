A major fire broke out at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright, about 180 kilometres southeast of Edmonton, on Saturday after what Prime Minister Mark Carney, in a post on X, called a serious incident at the base, and Defence Minister David McGuinty later confirmed there had been fatalities and injuries, without giving numbers. The Municipal District of Wainwright said the fire, spreading rapidly in high winds, had crossed the base fence and that emergency crews were focused on protecting lives, homes and other structures. Alberta Premier Danielle Smith called the fire significant and tragic in a social media post, and no cause has been stated.
