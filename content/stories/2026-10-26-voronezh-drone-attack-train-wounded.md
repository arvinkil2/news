Ukrainian drones attacked the Russian city of Voronezh overnight, and 17 people were wounded, most of them passengers on an Anapa to Moscow train standing at Pridacha station, where regional governor Alexander Gusev said most of the wounded were hurt and two were in serious condition, according to NV, a Ukrainian outlet reporting Russian official statements. Russian media reported two people killed. A drone came down by the tracks, the train was damaged and a power line was cut, delaying some trains, and fires were reported at the Galeria Chizhova business centre in the city centre, at warehouses and in garages, with power cuts in parts of the city. Russia's defence ministry said 112 drones were intercepted and destroyed over the region.
