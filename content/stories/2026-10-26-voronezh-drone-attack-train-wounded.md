Two people were killed and 17 wounded, including a child, in an overnight drone attack on the Russian city of Voronezh, regional governor Alexander Gusev announced, in Interfax's report of his statement; ERT News, carrying APE, Reuters and AFP reporting, gives the same toll. Gusev said air defences destroyed 113 drones over the city and districts of the region, more than 70 of them over Voronezh itself, and that a significant part of the wounded, including two seriously wounded people, were injured in a train moving through a left bank suburb when drone debris fell on it. Russian Railways, in RIA Novosti's report, said a carriage of train No. 11, Anapa to Moscow, was damaged at Somovo station with five passengers injured on preliminary information, and that traffic on the section through Somovo was suspended after power was lost in the contact network. Fires broke out at industrial enterprises, a business centre, an apartment building, dacha settlements, a school stadium and a garage cooperative, Gusev said; NV, a Ukrainian outlet citing the Astra channel's OSINT analysis, identified the business centre as the Galeria Chizhova in the city centre. No Ukrainian official comment on the attack has been reported.
