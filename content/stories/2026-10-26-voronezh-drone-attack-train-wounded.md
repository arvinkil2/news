Ukrainian drones attacked the Russian city of Voronezh overnight. A carriage of passenger train No. 11, Anapa to Moscow, was damaged at Somovo station and five passengers were injured on preliminary information, Russian Railways said, in RIA Novosti's report; power was lost in the contact network on the section through Somovo and train traffic on it was suspended. Fires started in residential areas and one drone hit the Galeria Chizhova business centre in the city centre, NV reported, citing the Astra channel's OSINT analysis and local residents. Russian state wire headlines separately report two people killed and 17 wounded in the attack and 113 drones shot down over the region; those articles have not been opened in full, and no Ukrainian official comment has been reported.
