Anthropic prices its initial public offering this evening, ahead of a first trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Thursday under the ticker ANT. Amendment No. 3 to its Form S-1, filed on November 17, kept the marketed range at $70 to $78 a share for 825 million Class A shares, of which 801,351,354 are new shares sold by the company and 23,648,646 are sold by existing holders, with a separate $26 billion private placement of Class N shares to close at the same price.

The reports around the book point higher. The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday that Anthropic expects to price above $80, which on the Journal's figures would value the company at roughly $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion, and Bloomberg News reported on Wednesday that the offering is close to seven times oversubscribed. The final price is set after the US market close and would be locked in by a Form 424B4 filing, which had not appeared as of this morning.
