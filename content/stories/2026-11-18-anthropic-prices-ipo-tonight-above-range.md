**Latest:** Anthropic registered 178,250,000 additional Class A shares with the SEC on Wednesday evening, in a Form S-1MEF filing under Rule 462(b) against its earlier registration statement (No. 333-299633). All of the additional shares are sold by the company, and 23,250,000 of them are shares subject to the underwriters' option to purchase additional shares. The filing says the additional shares are in an amount and at a price that together represent no more than 20 percent of the maximum aggregate offering price in the earlier registration. The filing is the first primary-source confirmation of the upsizing Reuters reported without a share count, and it takes the registered Class A total to about 1,003 million shares against the 825 million shares in the marketed offering.

Anthropic has priced its offering at $83 a share, above the $70 to $78 range in its filing, Reuters reported, citing a person familiar with the matter, and TechCrunch and The Guardian reported the same price. TechCrunch put the fully diluted valuation at $1.5 trillion. Reuters said the size of the offering has increased from the initially planned 825 million shares, without giving a final share count, and that Anthropic declined to comment. Trading is expected to begin on Thursday on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

Anthropic prices its initial public offering this evening, ahead of a first trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Thursday under the ticker ANT. Amendment No. 3 to its Form S-1, filed on November 17, kept the marketed range at $70 to $78 a share for 825 million Class A shares, of which 801,351,354 are new shares sold by the company and 23,648,646 are sold by existing holders, with a separate $26 billion private placement of Class N shares to close at the same price.

The reports around the book point higher. The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday that Anthropic expects to price above $80, which on the Journal's figures would value the company at roughly $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion, and Bloomberg News reported on Wednesday that the offering is close to seven times oversubscribed. The final price is set after the US market close and would be locked in by a Form 424B4 filing, which had not appeared as of this morning.

**Earlier:** Bloomberg reported on Wednesday afternoon that Anthropic is weighing a price of $80 to $85 a share, above the $70 to $78 range it initially marketed. At the top of that range the base offering would raise roughly $68 billion to $70 billion, and together with the previously arranged $26 billion private placement the transaction would exceed $96 billion, which Bloomberg's reporting carried by Investing.com described as potentially the largest IPO on record. Pricing discussions were still ongoing and no final decision had been made.
