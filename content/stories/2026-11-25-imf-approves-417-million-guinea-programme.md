The IMF Executive Board on November 23, 2026 approved 38-month arrangements for Guinea under the Extended Credit Facility and the Extended Fund Facility totalling SDR 310.59 million, 145 percent of quota or about $417 million, split between SDR 103.53 million (about $139.1 million) of concessional ECF resources and SDR 207.06 million (about $278.1 million) under the EFF. The decision allows an immediate disbursement of SDR 47.19 million, about $63.4 million, which the authorities intend to use for budget support, with the rest to follow in six equal instalments subject to semiannual reviews.

The Fund said Guinea’s economy grew an estimated 6.9 percent in 2025 and is projected to accelerate as Simandou iron ore production ramps up, while the authorities’ programme aims to raise domestic revenue, especially from the mining sector, phase out fuel and electricity subsidies gradually, and rebuild reserves to above four and a half months of imports by 2029.
