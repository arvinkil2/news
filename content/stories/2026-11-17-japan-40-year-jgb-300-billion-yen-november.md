Japan's Ministry of Finance said it will auction about 300 billion yen of 40 year government bonds on November 25, for issue on November 26. The bonds mature on March 20, 2066, and the sale is a reopening of the May 2026 issue, adding to the outstanding line rather than creating a new one, sold by Dutch style yield competitive auction at intervals of 0.5 basis points.

The sale is the ministry's scheduled super long offering for November and will show how much yield investors demand to hold Japan's longest maturity while the Bank of Japan continues tightening.
