TSMC has instructed that orders from Chinese chip designers using manufacturing processes of 16 nanometres and below be halted unless the customers obtain US export licences, according to Reuters, after new US Commerce Department rules took effect on Thursday removing the route under which TSMC could verify a design's technical characteristics itself and treat it as outside export controls. Affected Chinese designers will generally be limited to older 28 nanometre and 40 nanometre processes, provided their chips use no high-bandwidth memory and need no advanced packaging, and the US list of approved chip designers includes Nvidia, AMD, Apple, MediaTek and Realtek but no mainland Chinese firm.

The guidance also tightens scrutiny of customers' locations, parent companies, shareholders, packaging providers and shipping routes, including orders routed through overseas subsidiaries, Reuters said. The Financial Times first reported that TSMC and Samsung Electronics had suspended some chip shipments to Chinese customers under the new restrictions.
