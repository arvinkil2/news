China Oilfield Services Limited (COSL) has signed contracts worth 10.928 billion yuan including taxes, about $1.64 billion, to build 59 offshore support vessels, the company disclosed in a Hong Kong Stock Exchange filing on November 16. The order covers 46 liquefied natural gas powered platform supply vessels and 13 conventionally powered anchor handling tug supply vessels, with deliveries scheduled in batches between 2028 and 2030.

Construction goes to a consortium of three Fujian province shipyards led by Fujian Southeast Shipbuilding, alongside Fujian Mawei Shipbuilding and Fujian Funing Shipbuilding Heavy Industry, all under Fujian Shipbuilding Heavy Industry Group. COSL, controlled by state-owned China National Offshore Oil Corporation, said the vessels will work mainly in Chinese coastal waters supporting offshore oil and gas production and marine engineering, and that the LNG-powered ships make up nearly 80 percent of the order.
