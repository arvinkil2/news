Rio Tinto said it will invest 1.9 billion dollars in the Kangwinan bauxite project, a new production centre 15 kilometres southeast of its Amrun mine on the Western Cape York Peninsula, with a 15 kilometre conveyor to expanded stockyards and the Amrun wharf, which will be enlarged to take capesize vessels. The project adds 20 million tonnes of annual capacity, taking Weipa to about 44 million tonnes from around 38 million tonnes today, replacing output from the Andoom mine as it reaches the end of its life and offsetting the planned end of production at Gove in the Northern Territory in 2029. First production is targeted for 2029 and full production for 2032, construction ramps up in 2027 with more than 1,300 construction jobs expected, the capital estimate includes 150 million dollars of early works pre-approved in 2025, and environmental permits were already secured under the South of Embley assessment.
