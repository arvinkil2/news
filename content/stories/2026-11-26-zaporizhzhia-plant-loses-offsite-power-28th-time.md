The Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant lost its connection to its sole remaining external power line, the 330 kV Ferosplavna-1 line, on November 25 at 05:28 local time, reportedly due to military activity, the International Atomic Energy Agency said on Thursday. The plant is relying on emergency diesel generators for the 28th time during the conflict, with two generators running, on-site heating suspended to conserve fuel despite temperatures near 0°C, and enough diesel to hold the current configuration for more than ten days. Director General Rafael Mariano Grossi said the agency is engaging Russia and Ukraine on arrangements for a ninth localized ceasefire to repair the Ferosplavna-1 line and called for an end to military activity against electrical infrastructure.
