China is considering selling sovereign bonds denominated in dollars and in offshore yuan as early as this month, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Bloomberg, as Beijing seeks to deepen its presence in international debt markets. Officials at the Ministry of Finance have been working with investment banks on the possible sales, the people said.

The discussions are ongoing and the details could still change, and the report gave no size for either sale.
