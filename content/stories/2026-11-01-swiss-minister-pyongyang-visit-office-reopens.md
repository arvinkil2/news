Swiss Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis has concluded a two day visit to Pyongyang, the first by a Swiss government minister to North Korea since Micheline Calmy-Rey went in 2003, aimed at re-establishing political dialogue with Kim Jong Un's regime, swissinfo reported. In Pyongyang, Cassis met Foreign Minister Choe Son Hui and Jo Yong Won, a secretary of the ruling Workers' Party Central Committee, for talks on normalising political relations, resuming political dialogue and the security situation on the Korean Peninsula, and said he was delighted to mark the official reopening of the Swiss diplomatic mission, calling it a concrete step towards strengthening direct dialogue. The diplomatic office, closed in March 2020 during the Covid pandemic and operational again since October 19, reports to the Swiss embassy in Beijing, which remains responsible for diplomatic and consular relations with North Korea; Cassis crossed into South Korea for the second leg of his trip.
