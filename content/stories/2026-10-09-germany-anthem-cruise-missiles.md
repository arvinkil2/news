German Army Inspector Major General Christian Freuding said the decision to buy new mass-produced cruise missiles with a range of about 1,500 km has been made, with the government intending to buy them from the United States, according to RND via UNIAN. Two market-available systems are in focus, both still requiring Bundeswehr certification, with first delivery targeted for 2027. US defense firm Covenant, which opened a cruise-missile plant in Leipzig this week, plans to build its Anthem missile there from 2027, claiming a unit cost under $1 million against several million for comparable systems.
