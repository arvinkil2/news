The Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control removed Jorge Rodríguez from its sanctions list on Wednesday, in a Venezuela related designation removal notice that gave no reason for the move. Rodríguez has led the National Assembly since 2021 and was first listed in September 2018, alongside his sister Delcy, who is now acting president and was herself delisted in April. Reuters reported that the siblings are expected to lead changes to Venezuelan oil legislation to admit more foreign companies, under the access deal Washington and Caracas cut after the ouster of Nicolás Maduro.
