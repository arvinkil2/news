Merck KGaA said it has signed a definitive agreement to divest its contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) business, part of its Life Science sector, to Lonza for a mid-triple-digit million-euro amount. The transaction is expected to close in mid-2027, subject to regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions, and the business will continue to operate inside Merck's Life Science sector until then.

The operations covered are the sites in Carlsbad, California; Madison and Verona, Wisconsin; the bioconjugation operations in St. Louis, Missouri; and Indianapolis, Indiana, which provide development and manufacturing services for complex drug modalities. BioReliance contract testing services are not part of the sale and will remain in Merck's Life Science portfolio.
