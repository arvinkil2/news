Former Hungarian central bank governor Gyorgy Matolcsy was detained on Wednesday morning along with other people related to an ongoing probe into operations at the central bank under his governance, the news site Telex reported. Citing his lawyer, Pal Batki, Telex said Matolcsy would be questioned later in the day over fraud causing significant financial losses and using a forged private document, Reuters reported via Devdiscourse. Prosecutors declined to comment and Batki was not immediately reachable, the report said.

Status discipline is essential on this item. Detention for questioning is not a charge, and no charge has been reported. The allegations as reported are the subject of the questioning, fraud causing significant financial losses and use of a forged private document, not findings by any court. The probe concerns operations at the Magyar Nemzeti Bank during Matolcsy's governorship; he led the central bank until his second six year term expired in March 2025.

This file is the morning brief's update in place for the story. It remains graded C because the detention rests on a single original source, Telex, with prosecutors declining to comment. The next verifiable step is a prosecutor's statement or a court filing; either would change the status of this item from reported detention to a formal proceeding.
