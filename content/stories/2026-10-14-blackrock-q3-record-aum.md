BlackRock reported third quarter adjusted earnings of $15.93 a share, up 38 percent, ahead of consensus near $14.30, on revenue up 19 percent to $7.75 billion, with GAAP net income up 25 percent to $1.66 billion and the adjusted operating margin widening to 47.8 percent from 44.6 percent. Net inflows were $205 billion in the quarter and $869 billion over twelve months, including $175 billion of long term inflows and $15.3 billion into private markets, taking assets under management to $15.49 trillion, up 15 percent from a year earlier. The shares rose about 3 percent in premarket trading.
