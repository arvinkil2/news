France published a decree on Saturday setting the economic conditions for the release of strategic diesel stocks announced in early October by Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu. The decree authorizes the sale of Sagess security stocks up to a cumulative maximum of 1.6 million cubic metres, about 10 million barrels, until January 31, 2027, sold at weighted average acquisition cost and allocated among distributors in proportion to the volumes they place on the market, with the sale price and allocations to be fixed by ministerial order.

Distributors must pass the full economic benefit through to the final customer and inform customers before the sale, and the pass-through may begin early, from November 1. The government aims for a cut of 12 to 18 centimes per litre in diesel, which has averaged 2.3 to 2.4 euros per litre since early October on government figures. A separate decree published the same day extends the aid scheme for off-road diesel used in farm work until December, at 15 centimes per litre.
