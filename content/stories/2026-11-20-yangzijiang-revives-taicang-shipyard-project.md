Yangzijiang Shipbuilding's wholly owned Jiangsu Yangzijiang Shipbuilding Group has signed an investment agreement with the Taicang Port Economic and Technological Development Zone for the Yangzi Runze project, a RMB3.38 billion ($504 million) manufacturing base for clean-energy vessels to be financed entirely from the group's internal resources. The project was first announced in February 2025 and stayed on hold pending further negotiations and regulatory clearance, and Yangzijiang said construction still cannot proceed until the necessary approvals are obtained, with no timetable announced. The new yard would sit alongside Jiangsu Yangzi-Mitsui Shipbuilding, the group's joint venture with Japan's Mitsui, and the company said the agreement would not materially affect its earnings or net tangible assets for 2026.
