The Securities and Exchange Commission announced on Monday that it will host a roundtable on machine-understandable data in the age of superintelligence on November 9, from 9 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Eastern at its headquarters in Washington. The session is open to the public and will be streamed live on the Commission's website, with a recording to follow. Joshua White, the Commission's chief economist and director of the Division of Economic and Risk Analysis, is the only official named in the release, which seeks feedback from market participants on how the market and the investing public use machine-understandable data for superintelligence solutions, on the importance of high quality information, and on how the Commission can better facilitate such use.
