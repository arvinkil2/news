Secretary of State Marco Rubio, speaking to reporters in the Philippines, said the administration has no indication or reason to believe the recent US technology outages are related to one another, stating that the Microsoft incident has nothing to do with the FAA's problems and that the FAA's have nothing to do with the interruptions at the banks a few days earlier. The remarks address a run of incidents that includes Friday's nationwide FAA ground stop and the bank outages covered on this site on November 16.

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin has separately blamed rapidly developing artificial intelligence systems, telling Fox News that self-fixing code can overwhelm systems not designed to run at that speed, a theory Rubio's remarks did not endorse.
