BHP said it has found an economic path to raise annual copper output from its South Australian operations to 650,000 tonnes by 2040, roughly double current production, through an expansion of Olympic Dam, Carrapateena and Prominent Hill costed at $14.2 billion to $18.1 billion, with an internal rate of return of 14 to 17 percent based on block caving and two-stage smelting. The plan also roughly doubles uranium processing capacity to 5,000 to 6,000 tonnes a year and relies on a planned 260-megalitre-a-day seawater desalination plant at Cape Hardy. BHP has twice before shelved Olympic Dam expansions on cost grounds, and this plan stays at the study stage until a final investment decision in the first half of 2027.
