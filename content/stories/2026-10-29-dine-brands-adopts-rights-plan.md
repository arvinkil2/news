Dine Brands Global adopted a stockholder rights agreement on October 28 and declared a dividend of one right for each outstanding common share to holders of record on November 9, 2026, the company said in a Form 8-K filed on Thursday. A person or group becomes an acquiring person at 10 percent beneficial ownership, or 20 percent for a passive investor, and on a flip-in event each right held by anyone else buys common stock worth twice its 210 dollar exercise price, which would heavily dilute the acquirer. The rights expire on October 27, 2027, and the board can redeem them for 0.001 dollars each before anyone crosses the threshold.

The company said the plan is intended to reduce the likelihood that anyone gains control without paying all stockholders an appropriate control premium, and that it is not expected to interfere with a merger or business combination the board approves. The filing names no investor or approach that prompted the adoption.
