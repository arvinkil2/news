The Securities and Exchange Board of India is exploring a separate exchange for pre-IPO shares and plans to issue a consultation paper soon, Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said at CNBC Awaaz's Market Mahakumbh event in Mumbai on Saturday. The venue would be limited to companies preparing to launch an initial public offering, not the wider unlisted market, and would be designed with investor safeguards and additional disclosure requirements.

The proposal follows SEBI's November 23 advisory cautioning investors against grey market premium figures for upcoming IPOs, which the regulator described as unofficial, unverified and unregulated, and outside its grievance redressal mechanisms. Pandey gave no timeline for the platform beyond the consultation paper. He also said SEBI is looking to cut foreign portfolio investor onboarding from several months to as little as five business days.
