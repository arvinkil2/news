The Commodity Futures Trading Commission on Monday approved a final rule amending Parts 15, 16 and 17 of its regulations to set an alternate reporting framework for certain fully collateralized event contracts, which have been covered by staff no-action letters since 2017. Under the rule, reporting markets, futures commission merchants, clearing members and foreign brokers will report these contracts under Parts 15 through 18 rather than under sections of Parts 38, 39, 43 and 45, and a new section 16.03, titled "Covered Event Contracts", is added to Part 16.

Compliance is staggered: some provisions take effect 60 days after the final rule is published in the Federal Register, others on the later of that date or July 26, 2027, and section 16.03(g) 12 months after publication. Chairman Michael S. Selig said the CFTC "will no longer regulate market participants through a patchwork of no-action letters, which serve as band-aids for unworkable regulations."
