Volkswagen's India unit told the Mumbai High Court in a November 10 filing that tax authorities wrongly singled it out over parts imports, arguing that every comparable carmaker follows the same model of importing components at the 10 percent duty rate rather than the roughly 30 percent charged on car kits. India alleges Volkswagen deliberately shipped nearly complete cars as separate part consignments to evade the higher rate and is demanding $1.2 billion, a record; the filing supports Volkswagen's case with shipment counts for Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Kia, Isuzu and JSW MG Motor, including 390,360 major component shipments by BMW in 2024.

Indian authorities told the court other carmakers pay the rightful duty and have described Volkswagen's conduct as fraud and subterfuge. The court is expected to rule on Monday, initially on the narrower question of whether authorities could look back 12 years in scrutinising the imports.
