The Reserve Bank of India said Wednesday that Paytm Payments Bank Limited has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the RBI Act, 1934, under a notification dated July 31, 2026 that was published in the Gazette of India on September 7. The announcement came in RBI press release 2026-2027/1273, signed by chief general manager Brij Raj.

The move is the formal endpoint of the central bank's supervisory action against the payments bank, which has faced RBI business restrictions since 2024. Exclusion from the Second Schedule means the institution no longer holds scheduled-bank status under the Act.
