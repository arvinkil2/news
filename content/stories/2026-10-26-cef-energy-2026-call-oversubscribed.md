The European Commission has received 67 applications for cross-border energy infrastructure projects requesting €2.88 billion in co-funding under the Connecting Europe Facility Energy programme, CINEA said on Monday, against a budget of €600 million under the 2026 calls for Projects of Common Interest and Projects of Mutual Interest. The proposals, submitted by the 30 September close and covering more than 20 countries, are now being checked for admissibility and eligibility before evaluation by independent experts, the Commission and CINEA. Applicants are expected to learn the results in January 2027, with grant agreements for successful projects expected by July 2027.
