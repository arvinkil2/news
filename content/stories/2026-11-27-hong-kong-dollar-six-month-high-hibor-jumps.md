The Hong Kong dollar extended its rally to a six-month high on Friday, rising as much as 0.1 percent to 7.8267 per US dollar, its strongest level since May 8, Bloomberg reported. Bloomberg attributed the move to surging local borrowing costs and a rally in Chinese stocks.

One-month Hong Kong Interbank Offered Rate jumped 20 basis points to 3.13 percent, the highest since December, in the portion of the report that could be read. No Hong Kong Monetary Authority action was mentioned in that portion.
