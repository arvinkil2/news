Trump told reporters Saturday that Russian President Vladimir Putin "would like to see a settlement" of the war with Ukraine, recounting a phone call between the two on Friday. Trump accused Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky of resisting a compromise, said Zelensky "disobeyed" US orders not to strike Russian refineries, and argued Ukraine should hold an election, which has not happened since Russia's 2022 invasion. The exchange escalated a war of words after Zelensky called Trump's diesel deal with Moscow a "weak decision."
