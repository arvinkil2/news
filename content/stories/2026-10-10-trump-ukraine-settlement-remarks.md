**Latest (Sunday):** President Zelensky said Ukraine is ready to halt its attacks on Russian oil refineries if Moscow ceases its own strikes on Ukrainian energy infrastructure, Reuters reported Sunday. Speaking on Telegram, Zelensky said any steps to de-escalate the conflict must be mutual and come with real guarantees, and said he would continue conversations with European leaders on Sunday about additional air defence packages. The offer answers Trump's Saturday demand that Ukraine stop hitting Russian refineries, redirecting the decision to Moscow. Zelensky also said the Zaporizhzhia death toll had risen to 23 people, including four children.

Trump told reporters Saturday that Russian President Vladimir Putin "would like to see a settlement" of the war with Ukraine, recounting a phone call between the two on Friday. Trump accused Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky of resisting a compromise, said Zelensky "disobeyed" US orders not to strike Russian refineries, and argued Ukraine should hold an election, which has not happened since Russia's 2022 invasion. The exchange escalated a war of words after Zelensky called Trump's diesel deal with Moscow a "weak decision."
