BMW is recalling 720,796 vehicles in the United States because an improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump may be exposed to water and short circuit, increasing the risk of a fire while the car is parked and turned off, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said on Friday. The agency told owners to park outside and away from buildings and other vehicles until the repair is completed, and the campaign, number 26V733000, replaces the 2024 recall 24V608 for the same issue, so owners whose cars were fixed under the first recall must return for the new remedy.

The recall covers 2012 to 2016 3 Series and 5 Series cars, the Z4, X1, X3 and X4, several 2 Series and 4 Series variants and the 2016 to 2018 X5 xDrive40e. BMW said it is still developing the fix, is aware of about 10 fire incidents that may relate to the problem and that occurred after vehicles were repaired under the 2024 recall, and expects owner notification letters and VIN lookups on NHTSA.gov to start on December 25, 2026.
