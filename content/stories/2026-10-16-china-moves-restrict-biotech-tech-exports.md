China's Ministry of Commerce is moving to restrict exports of select biopharma technology by domestic companies, according to Endpoints News, with the proposed limits covering cell and gene therapy, siRNA and pathogen-related technology. Ordinary drug licensing deals would be preserved under the approach described, leaving the main channel for China-West life sciences deals open.

Shares in WuXi AppTec fell 2.19 percent on the report. The measure was described as a proposal being pursued, not an enacted ban, and no timeline was reported.
