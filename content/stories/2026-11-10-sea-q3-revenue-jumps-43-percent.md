Sea Limited, the Singapore based owner of Shopee, Monee and Garena, reported third quarter revenue of 8.6 billion dollars, up 43.4 percent year on year, and net income of 502.5 million dollars, up 34.0 percent, with adjusted EBITDA up 28.9 percent to 931.3 million dollars. Shopee gross merchandise value rose 23.3 percent to 39.7 billion dollars and its revenue rose 41.3 percent to 6.1 billion dollars, while Monee revenue rose 63.9 percent to 1.6 billion dollars as consumer and SME loan principal outstanding reached 12.6 billion dollars, up 60.0 percent.

The board on November 9 approved an additional 1.0 billion dollars to the company's share repurchase authorization, with no expiration. Dow Jones reported the profit figures beat market expectations.
