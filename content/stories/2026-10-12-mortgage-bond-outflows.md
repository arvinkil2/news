Investors fled US mortgage bond funds at historic levels over the past three weeks, Bloomberg reported Monday, with outflows ranking among the seven worst since the start of 2021. The Bloomberg US Mortgage Backed Securities Index posted its second-worst performance on record in September. The selling followed a spike in Treasury yields to multi-decade highs, fueled by surging oil prices, inflation concerns, and expectations of Federal Reserve rate hikes.
