An explosion tore through a furnace in the carbon dioxide filling workshop at the Habas Demir-Celik steel plant in Aliaga, in Turkey's western Izmir province, on Monday, and a fire then broke out in one of the plant's workshops, according to the DHA news agency and the NTV channel, both carried by TASS. Fire crews and ambulance teams were sent to the plant, and firefighting was under way.

NTV reported that there were dead and wounded in the explosion, but their number was not yet known. No cause for the blast had been reported in the accounts read.
