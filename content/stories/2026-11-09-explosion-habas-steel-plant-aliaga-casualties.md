Latest: Turkish authorities have ordered the detention of 11 people, including the chairman of Habas, after the explosion at its Aliaga plant killed at least 17 workers, Deputy Justice Minister Can Tuncay said on Tuesday. The detention orders cover managers and engineers as well as the chairman and followed preliminary findings by experts examining the blast, and the investigation remains underway. Tuncay gave a revised account of the accident: a crane support beam gave way while workers were moving an electric arc furnace vessel, causing the crane and vessel to fall, and the accident occurred during planned maintenance, as Habas itself also said. One worker remained unaccounted for and one of the injured was in serious condition at the time of his briefing. The Aliaga mill has operated since 1987 and has annual production capacity of 4.5 million tonnes of liquid steel, according to Habas. The company said it was working with authorities to establish the cause.

Earlier: Health Minister Kemal Memisoglu said the death toll has risen to 17 after one of the seriously injured died, TASS reported. Erdogan had earlier put the injured at 13, two of them seriously. TASS also reported a cause for the first time in the accounts read here: an overhead crane broke and fell into an electric arc furnace in one of the workshops, spilling liquid metal, and the building almost completely collapsed.

Earlier: President Recep Tayyip Erdogan said the death toll from Monday's explosion at the Habas Demir Celik plant in Aliaga, about 60 kilometres north of Izmir, has risen to 16, AFP reported, raising an earlier toll of 10. AFP described the blast as apparently an industrial accident and gave no figure for the injured.

An explosion tore through a furnace in the carbon dioxide filling workshop at the Habas Demir-Celik steel plant in Aliaga, in Turkey's western Izmir province, on Monday, and a fire then broke out in one of the plant's workshops, according to the DHA news agency and the NTV channel, both carried by TASS. Fire crews and ambulance teams were sent to the plant, and firefighting was under way.

NTV reported that there were dead and wounded in the explosion, but their number was not yet known.
