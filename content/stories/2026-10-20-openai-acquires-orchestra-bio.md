OpenAI has bought Orchestra Bio, a startup that built AI agents for drug development, Axios reported on Tuesday in an exclusive later reported by other outlets. Orchestra's team and technology will join Rosalind, OpenAI's suite of models and tools for biology and drug discovery, and OpenAI said the platform was designed to let researchers hand off tasks such as monitoring studies, tracking development timelines and spotting possible delays to AI agents.

The acquisition deepens OpenAI's push into life sciences, where it introduced GPT-Rosalind in April and has partnerships with drugmakers including Novo Nordisk and Moderna, and it puts the company in closer competition with Anthropic, which bought Coefficient Bio earlier this year. OpenAI did not disclose financial terms.
