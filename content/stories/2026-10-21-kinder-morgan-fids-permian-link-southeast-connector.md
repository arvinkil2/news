Kinder Morgan said Southern Natural Gas, its joint venture with Southern Company, had reached a final investment decision on the Southeast Connector after securing long-term take-or-pay agreements, a project of up to 2.6 billion cubic feet a day over about 470 miles of new pipeline and compression for Southeast markets, with a Kinder Morgan share of cost of 3.1 billion dollars and phased service from the fourth quarter of 2030. Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America reached a final investment decision on Permian Link, a 4.2 billion dollar project, of which Kinder Morgan's share is about 1.6 billion dollars, moving about 2.8 billion cubic feet a day over about 434 miles to serve new power generation for an A-rated hyperscaler under long-term take-or-pay agreements, with service expected in the third quarter of 2030. The two decisions, announced with record third-quarter results, add 4.7 billion dollars of Kinder Morgan-share projects aimed at power and data centre gas demand.
