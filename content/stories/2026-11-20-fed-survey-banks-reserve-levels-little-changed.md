The Federal Reserve's September 2026 Senior Financial Officer Survey drew responses from 91 banks holding around three-fourths of total banking system reserve balances. More than half of respondents who also answered the March 2026 survey reported no change in their bank's lowest comfortable level of reserves, and those reporting a change were equally split between an increase and a decrease.

About three-fourths of respondents said their bank prefers to hold additional reserves above that lowest comfortable level, and over half reported taking actions in recent months intended to maintain their current reserve holdings. Almost half said they would expect their bank's reserves to decline if the secured overnight financing rate averaged 5 basis points above the interest rate on reserve balances.
