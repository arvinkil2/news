The Department for Work and Pensions said on Friday that it had taken some of its online services offline as a precaution after identifying a cyber incident affecting its systems. The department is working with the National Cyber Security Centre and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre to investigate and restore services, and said regular benefit and pension payments are not affected and will be paid as normal.

Services reported unavailable on Friday morning included signing in to universal credit accounts, applying for universal credit, sending a fit note for Employment and Support Allowance, and pension and Personal Independence Payment application services. The DWP gave no timetable for restoration and has not said how the incident occurred or whether personal data was accessed.
