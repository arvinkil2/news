Brazil's Superior Electoral Court (TSE) ordered Lula's campaign to pull electoral ads that linked senator Flavio Bolsonaro to alleged dealings with Banco Master and tied his running-mate Alfredo Gaspar to a criminal investigation. TSE president Kassio Nunes Marques granted a preliminary injunction on October 10, finding the spots omitted relevant information and made criminal imputations without a minimal factual basis, in violation of the electoral law's ban on calumny and defamation.

The ads cited leaked messages pointing to a US$24 million negotiation between Flavio Bolsonaro and former Banco Master owner Daniel Vorcaro, and a statutory-rape investigation against Gaspar that the Supreme Court archived after the prosecutor's office cited lack of evidence. The court ordered the material removed from the free electoral airtime until the merits are judged. Lula faces Flavio Bolsonaro in the presidential runoff.
