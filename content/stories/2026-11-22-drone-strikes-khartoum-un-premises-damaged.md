Explosions and anti-aircraft fire were heard across Khartoum between about 2:00 a.m. and 4:30 a.m. on Sunday as drones struck homes and a UN premises in a civilian neighbourhood, and the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs said UN colleagues were inside the premises when it was damaged. UN Resident and Humanitarian Coordinator Denise Brown condemned the attack, saying humanitarian personnel and facilities are protected under international humanitarian law.

Al Jazeera reported medical sources saying three civilians were killed when a drone struck a house in Karari, in Omdurman, and a Sudanese human rights group said a child was killed when a drone fell on a home in Al-Assal, near Jebel Aulia, south of the capital. Sudanese military sources alleged the drones were launched from Ethiopia; the UN named no perpetrator, and Sudanese media attributed the strikes to the Rapid Support Forces, which has fought Sudan's army since April 2023.
