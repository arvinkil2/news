Titan Mining said Wednesday its subsidiary Empire State Mines signed a term sheet with the U.S. Department of the Army establishing commercial terms for an Enhanced Use Lease to develop a graphite processing facility on Army property. The framework targets roughly 97 acres at Anniston Army Depot in Alabama, with diligence continuing at Pine Bluff Arsenal in Arkansas and Fort Drum in New York.

The plant would produce purified micronized graphite in phase one and coated spherical purified graphite, a lithium-ion anode material, after customer qualification. The lease runs 50 years with two 25-year renewal options. Titan, the only end-to-end U.S. producer of natural flake graphite, says the facility answers America's full reliance on foreign-controlled graphite supply chains under the administration's March 2025 critical-minerals executive order.
