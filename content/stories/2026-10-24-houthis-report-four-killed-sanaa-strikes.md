The Houthi-run al-Masirah television channel reported on Saturday that four people were killed and several wounded in airstrikes in the west of Sanaa, and the Houthis blamed Saudi Arabia for the attack, ORF reported. Eyewitnesses also reported airstrikes west of the city, with clouds of smoke seen, and al-Masirah reported about 20 airstrikes in the port city of Mokha. There was initially no confirmation of the attacks from the Saudi side, ORF said.
