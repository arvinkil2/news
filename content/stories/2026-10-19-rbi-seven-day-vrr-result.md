The Reserve Bank of India allotted Rs 1,00,014 crore in a seven-day variable rate repo on Monday, against Rs 1,00,000 crore notified and Rs 1,50,735 crore of bids received, at a cut-off rate of 5.51 percent and a weighted average rate of 5.52 percent, its published result showed. The cut-off sits one basis point above the 5.50 percent policy repo rate set on October 7. The RBI had announced the auction on a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions in the near term, and it ran three days after its daily statement for Friday, October 16 showed banks parking Rs 1,58,474 crore in the standing deposit facility at 5.25 percent, with Rs 8,316 crore taken at the marginal standing facility at 5.75 percent the same day.

The weekend statement underlines why the injection was needed on one side of the market only. On Sunday, October 18, banks parked 144,678 crore rupees in the standing deposit facility at 5.25 percent while taking 10,410 crore at the marginal standing facility at 5.75 percent, and outstanding operations showed net liquidity absorption of 185,872.95 crore rupees, the Reserve Bank's money market statement for October 18 showed. Aggregate surplus and pockets of deficit are coexisting, which is the condition variable rate operations exist to smooth.
