Nigeria's national oil company NNPC Limited and the Central Bank of Nigeria signed a memorandum of understanding at the central bank's headquarters in Abuja on Wednesday, creating a structured framework to strengthen foreign exchange inflows, improve financial discipline and attract investment into the energy sector. NNPC group chief executive Bashir Bayo Ojulari said the partnership would strengthen the flow of export proceeds and improve financing for energy projects, while group chief financial officer Adedapo Segun said improved coordination would help attract the investment needed to raise production from the current 1.8 million barrels a day to 3 million. Central bank governor Olayemi Cardoso described the agreement as formalising an existing relationship, and the two institutions agreed to set up working groups and implementation timelines, with NNPC treasurer John Etukudo overseeing implementation for the company.
