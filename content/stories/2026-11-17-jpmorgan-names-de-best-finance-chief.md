JPMorgan Chase announced on Tuesday that Bregje de Best, 43, will succeed Jeremy Barnum as chief financial officer following the firm's first-quarter 2027 financial reporting. At that point de Best will join the firm's Operating Committee, and Barnum will become a vice chairman of the firm.

De Best has been chief financial officer of the Commercial and Investment Bank since June 2025. She was previously co-head of Global Corporate Banking from March 2024, and before that co-head of Global Corporate Derivatives for the investment bank from 2019 to 2024, having joined the firm in 2006 and held senior investment banking and markets roles in the United Kingdom and Asia.
