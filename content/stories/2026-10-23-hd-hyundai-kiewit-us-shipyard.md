HD Hyundai and US construction group Kiewit signed the agreement in Houston on Thursday to form a joint venture, HD Kiewit Shipbuilding, expanding a strategic partnership announced in July, Splash247 reported. The yard, at a US industrial site still to be announced, will use HD Hyundai's automated production model and target US Navy auxiliary vessels and commercial contracts, while also exploring ship blocks and modules for other American yards. The project sits inside South Korea's $150 billion commitment to the US shipbuilding sector, part of the $350 billion of US investment funds Seoul agreed to in return for US tariff cuts last year, Reuters reported.
