Vir Biotechnology said its Phase 3 ECLIPSE 1 trial of elebsiran plus tobevibart in chronic hepatitis delta met its primary endpoint, with 44 percent of patients on the monthly combination reaching undetectable hepatitis delta virus RNA plus ALT normalisation at week 48, against 0 percent in the delayed treatment arm (p below 0.0001). The key secondary endpoint was also met, with 79 percent of treated patients reaching undetectable virus, and all treated patients achieved a virologic response by week 24.

The trial enrolled 124 patients randomised 2 to 1 against delayed treatment. Vir said the regimen was well tolerated, with one Grade 3 treatment related adverse event, a neutrophil count decrease that resolved in 15 days, and no treatment interruptions or discontinuations. Data from the ECLIPSE 2 and ECLIPSE 3 trials are expected in the first quarter of 2027, and Vir plans a United States FDA submission in mid 2027; the regimen already holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy and Fast Track designations.
