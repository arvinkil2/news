A suspected Russian drone briefly entered Polish airspace on Monday night during strikes on western Ukraine and stayed for eight minutes before flying back into Ukraine, Polish authorities said. The drone was believed to be carrying surveillance equipment, and authorities described the flight as intentional activity by Moscow. Deputy Defence Minister Paweł Zalewski told RMF24 the military decided against shooting it down because of the risk to civilian property in the area.

The breach came days after three Russian cruise missiles fell in Poland during a major attack on Ukraine, and hours after NATO held emergency talks over that earlier breach and authorised the deployment of an AWACS surveillance aircraft to Poland.
