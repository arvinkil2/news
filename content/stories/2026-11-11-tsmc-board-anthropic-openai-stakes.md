TSMC's board, meeting on Tuesday, approved investments of up to $500 million in Anthropic, PBC, buying common shares at the per share initial public offering price, and up to $500 million in OpenAI Group PBC preferred stock, the company reported in a Form 6-K filing. The Anthropic leg matches the commitment Anthropic's amended registration statement lists for TSMC Partners in its $26 billion concurrent private placement, covered on this site on November 10; the filing gives no price, valuation or timetable for the OpenAI preferred stock.

The same meeting approved capital appropriations of approximately $40,926.70 million for advanced technology capacity, advanced packaging, fab construction and 2027 research and development capital, up to $500 million to establish an investment company for strategic investments in the semiconductor industry and related ecosystem, and a third quarter cash dividend of NT$9.0 per share, payable on April 8, 2027. The board also approved the third quarter statements, with consolidated revenue of NT$1,494.24 billion and net income of NT$763.06 billion.
