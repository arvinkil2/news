A car loaded with explosives detonated on Monday night outside the Meta department governor's office at Plaza Los Libertadores in downtown Villavicencio, southeast of Bogota. Villavicencio's security adviser, Colonel German Ayala, put the preliminary toll at 12 injured, most of them security staff and none in serious condition, with damage across a perimeter of about 500 metres, two commercial establishments destroyed and at least three vehicles wrecked; later Colombian reporting put the injured at 23. President Abelardo De La Espriella confirmed the attack on social media, said there were no known deaths, blamed drug trafficking structures and said his government would not enter talks with what he called narco-terrorism.
