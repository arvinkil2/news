China's Ministry of Commerce said on Saturday that the United States and China held the first meeting of the trade committee under their economic and trade negotiation mechanism by video on November 13. The two sides exchanged views on implementing the outcomes of their trade negotiations and discussed tariffs, agricultural trade and other issues, including a plan to lower the tariffs applied to about $30 billion worth of each country's imports from the other, the ministry said.

The Office of the United States Trade Representative confirmed on social media that USTR and the Treasury Department held the first official US-China Trade Committee meeting with their Chinese counterparts on November 13, and said the Trump administration looks forward to continuing consultations toward fair and balanced trade. The meeting comes ahead of a bilateral summit on the margins of the APEC summit in China on November 18 and 19.
