Swire Pacific announced on Monday night that it had sold 252.65 million shares in Cathay Pacific Airways, about 4.15 percent of the airline, at HK$14.23 per share, an 8.5 percent discount to Monday's closing price of HK$15.55. The placing, run by J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley and HSBC, gives Swire net proceeds of HK$3.57 billion for general working capital and an expected one-off gain of about HK$870 million, and it cuts Swire's holding to 35 percent from 39.15 percent, leaving it Cathay's largest shareholder.

Cathay's shares had risen to their highest level since July 2015 on Monday and have gained nearly 20 percent in the past month. A Swire spokeswoman said the company was taking advantage of market conditions to strengthen its balance sheet and add financial flexibility, and that it has no plans to sell down further.
