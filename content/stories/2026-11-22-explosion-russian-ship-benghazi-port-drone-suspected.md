An explosion struck a Russian ship docked at the Benghazi naval base on Sunday, and Libya's eastern-based government said a preliminary investigation indicated a suspected drone attack. The vessel had stopped at the port to refuel and resupply on its way back to Russia, under maritime cooperation agreements between the two countries. Two medical sources said there were injuries but no deaths reported, with the number and nationalities of the injured not immediately known, and the government said port and naval base facilities, including warehouses, were safe.

The eastern-based government condemned the suspected attack as a violation of Libya's sovereignty and a threat to maritime safety, and said it would take legal and diplomatic measures against anyone found responsible. There was no immediate reaction from Russian authorities and no claim of responsibility.
