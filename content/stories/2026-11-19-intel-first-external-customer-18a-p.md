Intel has secured its first external customer for its 18A-P process node, Chief Executive Lip-Bu Tan told attendees at the TomorrowX Summit in Austin, Texas, and Chief Technology Officer Pushkar Ranade confirmed on X that the customer has signed up for the 18A-P node, which went into risk production in June. Intel has not named the customer; Tan said on the company's third quarter earnings call that it was engaged with "a couple of highly strategic customers" on 18A chips, and the Wall Street Journal reported earlier this year that Intel and Apple were looking at a deal for Intel to manufacture a share of Apple's device chips.

The 18A family is among the last nodes on Intel's "5 Nodes in 4 Years" roadmap and the first with its PowerVia power delivery system and RibbonFET gate-all-around transistor architecture; 18A-P is a performance variant designed to extend the node's life and is fully compatible with 18A designs. Tan said yields on Intel 3 and Intel 18A rose again in the third quarter, with 18A improving at a double-digit rate and wafer output on both nodes above expectations, and that 18A-P remains on track to launch by year-end across Intel's own product portfolio.
