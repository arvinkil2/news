Hong Kong pro-democracy activist Joshua Wong, 30, was sentenced to ten additional years in prison on Thursday after pleading guilty to foreign collusion under the national security law. The Guardian reported he is the only person to have been prosecuted twice under the law, and that he had already spent six years in detention and was due for release in January 2027 under his previous conviction.

A UK Foreign Office spokesperson said the sentence showed the law being used to target individuals for political expression and participation, and called for an end to unjust prosecutions under it. The US consulate in Hong Kong strongly condemned the additional sentence and called for Wong's immediate release. China warned relevant parties to stop making irresponsible remarks about Hong Kong judicial cases.
