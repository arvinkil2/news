Yemen's Southern Armed Forces said on Sunday that they were redeploying troops from Dhubab Airport, in the Bab al-Mandab area of Taiz province, to Aden, as part of a broader reorganisation to reinforce front lines and secure the interim capital and neighbouring governorates. Government military spokesman Majed Al-Nuzaili welcomed the move, said defending Aden and lifting the Houthi siege of Taiz were linked responsibilities, and called for international support to secure the Bab al-Mandab Strait for international navigation. In a separate statement, Al-Nuzaili said Yemeni armed forces had carried out 906 strikes against Houthi targets on Sunday, a figure no independent source had confirmed.
