The Financial Stability Board published its annual list of insurers reported as subject to resolution planning standards consistent with its Key Attributes on Monday. Seven new Japanese insurers were added, raising the number of reporting jurisdictions from eight to nine and the total number of insurers on the list from 17 to 24. The FSB said no other changes were made to the list.
