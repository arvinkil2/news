South Korea's exports totalled 61.6 billion dollars in the first 20 days of October, up 105.4 percent from the same period a year earlier and the largest October 1 to 20 tally on record, according to provisional Korea Customs Service figures reported by Yonhap. The period had 13 working days against 10.5 a year earlier, and on a daily average basis exports rose 65.9 percent to 4.74 billion dollars, also a record and above September's 4.6 billion. Semiconductor exports drove the increase, surging 222.5 percent to 27.5 billion dollars and taking a 44.7 percent share of the total, while imports rose 42.8 percent to 47.1 billion dollars, leaving a trade surplus of 14.5 billion dollars for the 20 days and cumulative 2026 exports of 876.3 billion dollars.
