SigraFi, a UK-headquartered gold offtake company, secured a $35 million credit facility from a consortium of British and UAE investors led by UAE-based Ronin Commercial Brokers LLC to finance gold supply from artisanal and small-scale (ASGM) cooperatives. The company is working with partner PeaceGold to source from 11 cooperatives representing 25,000 miners in the Democratic Republic of Congo's Ituri Province, with plans to expand in the DRC, Zambia, and South America. SigraFi offers guaranteed offtake at competitive pricing, giving cooperatives a larger share of the gold's value.
