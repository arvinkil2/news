Mexico's real gross domestic product rose 0.9 percent in the third quarter of 2026 from the previous quarter and 3.3 percent from a year earlier, INEGI said in its final quarterly release, Boletín de Indicador 699/26, published on November 23. Tertiary activities led the quarter, rising 1.2 percent from the second quarter and 3.6 percent from a year earlier, while secondary activities rose 0.4 percent and 2.7 percent and primary activities rose 0.3 percent and 1.5 percent on the same bases.

Over the first nine months of 2026, GDP rose 1.9 percent from the same period of 2025. The quarterly gain matches INEGI's October 30 flash estimate, while the annual rate was revised up from the flash reading of 3.2 percent.
