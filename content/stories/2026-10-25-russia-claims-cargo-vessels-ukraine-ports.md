Russia's Defence Ministry said its drones struck a cargo vessel bound for the Black Sea port of Chornomorsk in an overnight attack and another cargo vessel en route to Odesa during the day on Sunday, and that both vessels were being used to supply the Ukrainian army, Reuters reported. The ministry also said Russian forces hit a logistics centre, a distribution centre and a logistics terminal in Kharkiv overnight, which it said were used for storage and distribution of dual-use cargo for the Ukrainian military. Reuters said it could not independently verify the ministry's information, and no Ukrainian confirmation had been reported.
