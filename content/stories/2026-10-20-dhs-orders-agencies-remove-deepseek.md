The Department of Homeland Security published notice on Tuesday of an order excluding information technology products and services developed by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd, including its affiliated entities, from federal civilian agency procurements, and requiring the removal of all DeepSeek and affiliate products from civilian agency information technology. The removal duty also covers third-party products and services developed using DeepSeek implementations, and the order rests on the Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security Act of 2018, following a Federal Acquisition Security Council recommendation. CNBC reported the order was originally issued on July 24 and made public on Monday, with the department saying DeepSeek's models present multiple threat vectors, including the risk that use by government employees or vendors could transfer sensitive data to China.
