Galaxy Digital's affiliate Galaxy Helios II LLC has filed with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation for a new building at its Helios campus outside Lubbock, Data Center Dynamics reported. Helios 5, part of the campus's Phase III development, is a one-story data center of 349,595 square feet at 1003 W FM 193 in Afton, Dickens County, with a stated project cost of 1.3 billion dollars and construction scheduled from November 2026 to June 2028.

CoreWeave has committed to 526 MW of critical IT load across three phases at Helios under 15-year leases. Phase I delivered 133 MW over the summer and the 260 MW Phase II build is under way, so the Helios 5 filing is the first permitted step of the third phase.
