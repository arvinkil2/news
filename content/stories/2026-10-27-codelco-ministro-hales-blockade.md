Workers at Codelco's Ministro Hales division near Calama occupied the mine's gates from 6 a.m. on Monday, bringing production and vehicle movements to a standstill for about six hours, Mining.com reported. The dispute is over bonuses the union says were withheld after a haul truck caught fire in December 2025, seriously burning its operator and taking 13 trucks out of service for several days; the union argues workers are being made to bear the cost of the company's safety failures.

Alberto Muñoz of the Federation of Copper Workers warned the protests could spread to Codelco's Chuquicamata, Radomiro Tomic and Gabriela Mistral mines unless senior management addresses the concerns. The blockade itself has ended, and no production loss figure has been published.
