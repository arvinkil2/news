DraftKings reported third quarter revenue of 1.33 billion dollars, up 16 percent, and a net loss of 468.7 million dollars, or 94 cents a share, against a 39 cent loss expected by analysts polled by LSEG. The company cut full year 2026 revenue guidance to 6.35 billion to 6.55 billion dollars and adjusted EBITDA guidance to 175 million to 275 million dollars, down from 700 million to 900 million, mainly to spend 650 million to 700 million dollars this year on DraftKings Predictions, its CFTC regulated event contracts business, which management said will bring in no meaningful revenue in 2026 because new customer promotions offset most of what it generates. The shares fell 24 percent in after hours trading.
