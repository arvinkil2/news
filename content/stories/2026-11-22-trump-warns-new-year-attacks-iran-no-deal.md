President Trump said in a Fox News interview on Sunday that he is willing to order additional military attacks on Iran after the new year if no agreement is reached with Tehran. He accused Iranian officials of stalling the negotiations, saying they "always just talk," and said Iran has become more aggressive since the US midterm elections.

Trump also said he hopes Iran tries to break the US maritime blockade, warning that its navy would end up at the bottom of the sea, and he urged Iranians taking part in renewed anti-government protests to keep protesting. The remarks follow comments a day earlier in which he said he would reach a permanent agreement with Iran and would not allow it to obtain nuclear weapons.
