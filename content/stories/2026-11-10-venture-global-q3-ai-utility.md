Venture Global reported a record third quarter on Tuesday. Revenue was $4.8 billion, up 43 percent from a year earlier, net income was $2.5 billion, up 493 percent, and consolidated adjusted EBITDA was $2.6 billion, with Plaquemines Phase 1 entering commercial operations on October 31. The company raised the midpoint of its full year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance, now set at $10.0 billion to $10.3 billion, and declared a dividend of $0.04 a share.

Alongside the results, the company set out a new line of business: an off-grid AI utility offering island-mode power, land, closed-loop water and its own natural gas supply to data center customers. Frame agreements signed in October with GE Vernova and Baker Hughes cover up to 16 new 720 megawatt power island units, about 11.5 gigawatts in total, plus transformer and grid equipment.
