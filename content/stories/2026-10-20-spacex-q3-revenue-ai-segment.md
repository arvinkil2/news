SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.6 billion in a regulatory filing on Tuesday, a figure Investing.com put at 161 percent above a year earlier, and net income of $4.4 billion against a $1 billion loss in the same quarter last year, TechCrunch reported. Its renamed Super Intelligence segment, covering the recently acquired Cursor coding tool, the Grok chatbot and a neocloud business now offering 1.7 GW of compute, generated $7.5 billion of the total and $431 million in profit, after $1.2 billion in one-time stock compensation tied to the Cursor acquisition. The company spent $19.6 billion on capital projects in the quarter, guided to $35 billion to $40 billion more in the fourth quarter and forecast revenue of $33 billion to $37 billion for the first quarter of 2027, while Starlink subscribers reached 13.6 million.
