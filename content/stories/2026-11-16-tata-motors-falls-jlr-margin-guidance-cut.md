Tata Motors shares fell 8.4 percent on Monday after Jaguar Land Rover lowered its fiscal 2027 adjusted core earnings margin forecast to between 2 and 3 percent, from about 4 percent, citing market challenges and exceptional costs including a one-off charge of 530 million pounds. JLR now expects a free cash outflow of around 1.25 billion pounds in fiscal 2027 on revenue of about 25 billion pounds, and announced a 1.7 billion pound cost-cutting programme over two years covering manufacturing, materials, logistics and warranty costs, including a voluntary redundancy programme affecting about 4,000 employees that accounted for 295 million pounds of the exceptional charges.

In the July to September quarter JLR reported an adjusted core margin of 1.1 percent and a free cash outflow of 540 million pounds, while Tata Motors' consolidated revenue rose to 969.20 billion rupees from 723.49 billion rupees a year earlier. The domestic passenger vehicle business reported a core earnings margin of 4 percent, down 180 basis points on the year despite a 51 percent rise in revenue, as higher commodity costs and adverse foreign exchange movements weighed on margins.
