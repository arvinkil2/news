Compensation costs for civilian workers increased 0.8 percent in the three months ending in September 2026, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported, down from 0.9 percent in the second quarter. Wages and salaries rose 0.7 percent, easing from 0.9 percent, and benefit costs rose 0.9 percent, easing from 1.0 percent. Private industry compensation also rose 0.8 percent on the quarter.

Over the 12 months ending in September, compensation costs rose 3.4 percent, the same annual rate as in June, with wages and salaries up 3.1 percent and benefit costs up 3.9 percent. Real wages and salaries for private industry workers fell 0.6 percent over the year.
