Trump rallied in Ohio on Saturday with 30 days until the November 3 midterms, vowing a "big surprise." The electoral map is uncomfortable for the White House: Reuters analysis finds Democrats competitive in ten states Trump won by double digits in 2024, with Democrats needing a net four Senate flips from 47 seats. Prediction markets price a Democratic House near 86 percent but the Senate as a coin flip.

The stakes go well beyond the usual midterm referendum. Trump says the Iran war will end "very quickly", "immediately", after the midterms, putting a political-economy timeline on an active conflict. Congress only funded the government through December 11 via continuing resolution, so the lame duck will carry a shutdown fight, Ukraine aid decisions, Iran-war escalation authority, and tariff policy.

Market transmission is direct: divided government reshapes fiscal, trade, and defense spending; the Iran escalation-authority question moves with the composition of the next Congress; and the December 11 funding cliff is already the market's next scheduled political risk. The Ohio rally's "big surprise" promise is campaign rhetoric, but the thirty-day countdown to the pivot is a hard calendar.
