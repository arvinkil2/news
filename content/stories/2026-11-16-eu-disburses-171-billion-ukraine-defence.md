The European Commission disbursed 1.71 billion euros to Ukraine on Monday under the 90 billion euro Ukraine Support Loan, to fund drones, ammunition and air defence systems, including early-warning radars and missiles, for Ukraine's most urgent military needs. Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said the EU would continue its steadfast support for Ukraine and work for a just and lasting peace.

This is the eighth payment under the defence component of the loan, and it will support procurement from Ukrainian and European manufacturers, with about 40 percent of the disbursement funding contracts with manufacturers established in the EU. Under the defence leg the Commission has now disbursed 18.1 billion euros, and for the rest of 2026 a further 23.6 billion euros in EU financial support remains available to Ukraine, including about 19.2 billion euros under the Support Loan.
