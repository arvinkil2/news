A North Korean party and government delegation led by Jo Yong-won, a secretary of the Workers' Party Central Committee whom Yonhap describes as Kim Jong Un's closest aide and the party Politburo's No. 2 figure, left Pyongyang by special plane on November 14 and arrived in Moscow, according to the Korean Central News Agency as reported by Yonhap. The visit was made at the invitation of Dmitry Medvedev, chairman of Russia's ruling United Russia party, and KCNA said the two countries' alliance relationship is being consolidated day by day, but it gave no purpose or itinerary for the stay. Analysts cited by Yonhap speculate Jo may be acting as an envoy carrying Kim's message ahead of a US-China-Russia trilateral meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen; no meeting with Medvedev or President Putin and no agreement has been reported.
