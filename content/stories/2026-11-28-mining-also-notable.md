Giyani Metals has secured a further $2.5 million in funding from South Africa's Industrial Development Corporation for its K.Hill battery grade manganese project in Botswana. The third addendum to its convertible loan agreements, announced on November 23, lifts the combined facility limit from 329.9 million rand to 372.4 million rand and extends key project milestones, including the final investment decision, to December 31, 2027. The money funds a pilot plant campaign and offtake qualification work from the July 2026 definitive feasibility study, which put K.Hill's post tax net present value at $482 million.

Gemfields' 56th auction of higher quality rough emeralds from the Kagem mine in Zambia raised $35.3 million, with all 45 lots sold at an average of $160.47 a carat between November 12 and 26. The result, announced on November 27, took Gemfields' 2026 emerald auction revenue to $91.7 million, its third highest calendar year total since auctions began in 2009. Kagem is 75 percent owned by Gemfields and 25 percent by Zambia's Industrial Development Corporation, and proceeds are repatriated to Zambia with royalties paid on the full sales prices.

Ampcontrol has agreed to acquire 100 percent of POWINS, an Australian electrical engineering and manufacturing business with operations in Brisbane and Perth, the companies said on November 27. POWINS delivers turnkey power distribution, switchgear and switchroom solutions across the resources, renewables and heavy industry sectors. The acquisition, whose terms were not disclosed, remains subject to regulatory approval, with completion targeted for early 2027.
