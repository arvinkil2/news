Three smaller stories from the week's file describe the same market from different ends. In Chicago, lithium market participants are building the city out as a pricing and trading hub for the battery metal, the financial layer the Pilgangoora expansion and the Thacker Pass financing in today's mining edition will eventually sell into. In South America, a soybean biofuel plant project under the Parane name is advancing, adding crush and renewable diesel capacity in the region that sets the world's soybean balance.

In California, the physical market is speaking loudest: diesel prices remain above 8 dollars a gallon, Supply Chain Dive reported, with truckers calling for relief, a state market where refinery capacity, regulation and the Pacific supply chain have detached retail fuel from the national benchmarks entirely. WTI at 95.71 dollars does not describe what a California haulier pays, which is the delivered cost problem again.
