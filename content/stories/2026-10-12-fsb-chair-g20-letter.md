FSB Chair Andrew Bailey submitted the letter to G20 finance ministers and central bank governors ahead of their October 15 meeting, calling for continued vigilance as the pace of change in the financial system accelerates. Alongside the letter, the FSB published its report on cross-border vulnerabilities and financial-stability monitoring of global stablecoin arrangements, and flagged forthcoming reports on sound practices for responsible AI adoption, regulatory and supervisory modernisation, and G20 implementation monitoring.
