ILWU Local 26, which represents maritime security employees and watchmen at the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach, has warned fellow maritime unions that its members may strike when the latest extension of their labor agreement with employers and the Pacific Maritime Association expires on November 1, gCaptain reported. Local 26 President Luisa Gratz wrote on October 23 that negotiations running since 2025 have been unsuccessful, that federal mediation requested by the association has produced nothing of value, and that the union is asking other maritime unions for support and solidarity.

The eight affected locations are APM Terminals, Long Beach Container Terminal, Total Terminals International, SSA at C-60, A-90 and LB 206, Pacific Container Terminal, and OH 55. The notice does not confirm that a strike will take place, and the extent of any disruption remains unclear; the International Organization of Masters, Mates and Pilots said it would give further guidance to members working at affected berths as the deadline approaches.
