The Food and Drug Administration announced the Rare Disease Framework Initiative on Wednesday, a project led by its Rare Disease Innovation Hub with the drug and biologics centres to make regulatory advice and decisions consistently tailored to the evidence that can realistically be generated in small patient populations. The agency committed to clearer and more predictable decision making, patient input at defined milestones, and risk based approaches to chemistry, manufacturing and controls review and pre approval inspections. A public meeting on October 28 at the White Oak campus, facilitated by the Reagan-Udall Foundation, opens the input process, with roundtables and a request for information to follow.
