Iran's parliament, the Islamic Consultative Assembly, gave a vote of confidence on Sunday to Mehrdad Akhlaghi, the nominee for minister of defense and armed forces logistics, state television reported. Akhlaghi replaces the previous defense minister, who was killed on the first day of the war with the United States, nearly eight months after the initial US and Israeli strikes that began the conflict.
