ValOre Metals has agreed to buy Bemisa Agua Azul Mineracao and an associated landholding entity from the Opportunity Holding investment fund for $135 million, the company said in a statement on Tuesday. The price is more than 20 times ValOre's market value before trading in its shares was halted: the stock last closed at 3.5 Canadian cents, valuing the Vancouver developer at C$8.9 million.

The terms call for $90 million at closing, $20 million after one year and $25 million after two years. ValOre holds a one time option, exercisable within four months of closing, to settle all $45 million of deferred payments for $30 million adjusted for US inflation, which would cut the nominal price to $120 million. The deal needs TSX Venture Exchange acceptance and is expected to close on or before January 15, 2027. ValOre said advanced financing discussions covering the purchase, working capital and initial development spending are under way, but the funding is not yet secured and the halt is expected to last until closing.

Agua Azul, about 1,900 kilometres northwest of Sao Paulo, would be ValOre's first operating asset alongside its Pedra Branca platinum group elements project in Ceara state. The 206 square kilometre property hosts the Abelhas, Juliano and Campos Altos areas along a 15 kilometre structural trend, and the Abelhas open pit feeds a plant processing about 120,000 tonnes a year.

A preliminary economic assessment dated July 28 studies expanding the plant to 2.85 million tonnes a year after a three year development period, at an initial capital cost of $297 million. At a 5 percent discount rate and a long term gold price of $3,609 an ounce, the study puts after tax net present value at $927.6 million with a 48 percent internal rate of return, a 13 year mine schedule and all in sustaining costs of $1,540 an ounce. The property holds 22.8 million indicated tonnes grading 1.27 grams per tonne gold, containing 931,920 ounces, plus 26.4 million inferred tonnes at 1.29 grams for 1.1 million ounces. The assessment is preliminary and includes inferred resources, which cannot support mineral reserves.
