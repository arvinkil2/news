The Saudi-led coalition said its forces struck operational capabilities at the Houthi-run Red Sea port of Hodeidah and destroyed several targets in Yemen's northern governorates of Saada and Amran, in a brief statement reported by Al Jazeera. The strike came hours after the coalition ordered civilians to evacuate the port for their safety.

Yemen's Saba news agency, cited by Iran's IRNA, reported a wider set of targets in the early hours of Wednesday, including Hodeidah's international airport, a honey production centre in the province's Al-Zahra district and the Harf Sufyan district of Amran province, hit in three rounds. Al Jazeera's reporting does not mention an airport strike, and neither source reported casualties or damage from the port attack. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree had warned before the strike that Jeddah port and Saudi ports along the Red Sea coast would be targeted if Hodeidah was bombed, and called on shipping companies to move their vessels out of those ports.
