Apollo Global Management is preparing to invest $2.5 billion in Focus Financial Partners, according to a Bloomberg report citing people with knowledge of the matter. The transaction could be announced as soon as Monday and would value Focus at about $21 billion including debt.

Focus was taken private by Clayton Dubilier and Rice and Stone Point Capital in a $7 billion deal in 2023, and both firms would remain controlling shareholders after the Apollo investment, alongside a significant employee ownership stake. Focus provides wealth management, family office and business management services, and oversaw more than $550 billion in advised billable assets at the end of June.
