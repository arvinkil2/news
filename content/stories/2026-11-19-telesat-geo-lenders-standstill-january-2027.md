Telesat said it and an ad hoc committee of holders of Telesat GEO Inc.'s term loans, secured notes and unsecured notes agreed standstill arrangements that include the lenders taking no enforcement actions through and including January 18, 2027, and Telesat continuing to make interest payments during the standstill period. The company's term loans and certain secured notes mature on December 6, 2026, and Telesat said its goal remains reaching a fair and consensual resolution and that the arrangements give it additional time to address the maturities. Datacenter Dynamics reported that Telesat's geostationary revenue fell 21 percent year over year to C$22 million in the third quarter of 2026, on a broadcast customer non-renewal and reduced contracted capacity, and that GEO EBITDA fell 43 percent to C$36 million, reflecting lower revenue and higher expenses related to its debt financing process.
