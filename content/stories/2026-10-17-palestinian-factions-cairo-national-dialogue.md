Palestinian factions concluded a round of talks in Cairo on Friday evening and agreed to hold a comprehensive national dialogue conference under Egyptian auspices, with Egypt to send invitations to 14 factions, including Hamas and Islamic Jihad, Arab News reported. Fatah delegation head Jibril Rajoub said his movement presented a seven-point initiative built on the PLO political program, a central government to manage the West Bank and Gaza Strip, and one authority, one law and one weapon, and that Fatah is open to bringing the legislative and presidential elections forward if the conference reaches common agreements. A Hamas official called the discussions fruitful and serious, and Rajoub warned that continued settler attacks in the West Bank could lead to an explosion.
