Boosteroid, Hong Kong based private equity firm Gaw Capital and AI cloud startup Firebird AI have signed preliminary agreements to establish 480 megawatts of data center capacity in Kazakhstan, expandable to 620 megawatts. The project is phase two of the country's Data Center Valley development in the Pavlodar region, which targets up to 1 gigawatt of capacity by 2029, and the plans were revealed at a meeting between Prime Minister Olzhas Bektenov and Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of AI and Digital Development Zhaslan Madiyev.

Phase one is already underway, with three data centers totalling 304 megawatts: a first 68 megawatt site has moved to foundation construction and is scheduled to open in the second quarter of 2027, a second Firebird AI site of 164 megawatts is 70 percent excavated for launch in the third quarter of 2027, and a third of 72 megawatts is due in the fourth quarter of 2027. Negotiations are also underway with Aleria, Energy Capital Global and China Energy for a further 240 megawatts, expandable to 420 megawatts, and talks on a third phase have begun.
