KT Cloud has broken ground on a 50MW AI data center in Ansan, in Gyeonggi Province near Seoul, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The facility totals 36,880 square metres across nine stories with two basement floors and is due live in 2029. KT Cloud chief executive Kim Bong-kyun said the design incorporates power and cooling infrastructure optimised for high density AI servers.

The Ansan project sits inside a larger build programme. KT plans to offer up to 1GW of AI data center capacity by 2031. A further 50MW facility in Cheongna, Incheon, is set to start construction in the fourth quarter of this year for completion in the first quarter of 2030, and construction has begun in Gunsan with SGC Energy on a site offering 60MW initially, live in the first quarter of 2028, with potential to scale to 300MW. Its 40MW Gasan facility, with liquid cooling and 26MW of IT capacity, opened in November 2025.
