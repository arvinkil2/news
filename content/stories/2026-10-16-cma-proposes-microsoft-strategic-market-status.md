The Competition and Markets Authority said it is proposing to designate Microsoft with strategic market status in its business software ecosystem, covering products including Word, Excel, Teams and Copilot, and published a roadmap of areas it may examine for possible interventions. Those areas include software bundling, choice of AI tools alongside Copilot, security product integration and the cloud on which customers deploy Microsoft licences.

The regulator said its provisional findings show Microsoft holding a strong position across five areas it investigated, with switching barriers limiting customer choice. The proposal is open for consultation, with a final designation decision required by a February 2027 statutory deadline, and the CMA said designation does not assume wrongdoing.
