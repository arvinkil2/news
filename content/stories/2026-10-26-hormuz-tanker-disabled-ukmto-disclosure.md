UK Maritime Trade Operations said it received a verified, time-late report of a tanker struck by an unknown projectile while transiting outbound through the Strait of Hormuz on October 23 at 0839 UTC, an impact that disabled the vessel while all crew were reported safe, gCaptain reported on Monday. In a separate warning issued Monday, UKMTO said it had received multiple third party reports of a vessel on fire approximately 63 nautical miles southeast of Al-Faw, Iraq, in the northern Persian Gulf, with the vessel's identity, crew status and the cause of the fire unknown, and classified the report as suspicious activity rather than a confirmed attack. The Joint Maritime Information Center kept its threat assessment for the strait at severe in its October 25 advisory, warning further deliberate attacks were highly likely, and counted 156 confirmed maritime security incidents across the region since March 1, a figure published before the latest tanker disclosure.
