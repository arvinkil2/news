Euro area banks reported a further net tightening of credit standards for loans to firms in the third quarter of 2026, at a net 9 percent of banks against the 5 percent tightening they had expected, and a net 14 percent tightening for consumer credit, while standards for housing loans were unchanged and banks expect further tightening across firm and household lending in the fourth quarter. Loan demand fell in every segment, with demand from firms down a net 6 percent against an expected increase of 4 percent and housing loan demand down a net 27 percent on higher interest rates, falling consumer confidence and worsening housing market prospects, and banks expect housing demand to fall by a net 36 percent in the fourth quarter. Banks also reported that their access to retail funding deteriorated by the most since the survey series began in 2011, and that several banks expect a gradual but sustained increase in loan demand to finance digital infrastructure, particularly data centres.
