American reported record third quarter revenue of $16.34 billion, up 19.3 percent from a year earlier and ahead of the $16.22 billion analysts surveyed by LSEG expected, with an adjusted loss of 28 cents a share against 35 cents expected and a net loss of $269 million. The top line did not cover the fuel bill, which rose 67.5 percent from a year earlier to $4.64 billion as jet fuel prices climbed after the start of the Iran war.

For the fourth quarter the airline expects an adjusted loss of 50 cents to $1.10 a share, with revenue up as much as 22.5 percent and capacity up between 5.5 and 6.5 percent. Delta Air Lines, United Airlines and Southwest Airlines have also cut their 2026 outlooks this month, and airfares have risen more than 20 percent from last year as carriers pass part of the fuel increase on.
