Alphabet closed its underwritten public offering of C$6 billion of Canadian dollar senior notes on November 24, the company said in a Form 8-K filed with the SEC. The issue has four tranches: C$2.0 billion of 4.100% notes due 2029, C$1.25 billion of 4.800% notes due 2034, C$1.5 billion of 5.325% notes due 2038 and C$1.25 billion of 6.000% notes due 2066, issued under Alphabet's February 2016 indenture with The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company as trustee.
