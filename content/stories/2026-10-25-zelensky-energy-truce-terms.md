Volodymyr Zelensky told TSN's weekly programme that Ukraine's strikes on Russian oil refineries answer Russian attacks on Ukrainian electricity, and set a direct condition for stopping them: if Russia does not hit Ukraine's electricity, Ukraine will not hit Russian diesel and oil infrastructure, which he called a fair energy truce. He said he had put three options to partners: the energy truce, Starlink access over Russian territory so Ukraine can act against ballistic missile launchers, or, if that access is not opened, additional Patriot missiles that depend on neither Elon Musk nor Vladimir Putin. Zelensky said Ukraine had so far received none of the three, and that Starlink speed had instead been reduced from the level Ukraine already had.
