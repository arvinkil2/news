The Treasury will buy back nominal coupon securities in the 1 month to 2 year maturity bucket on Thursday, November 5, at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, under its liquidity support programme. The maximum purchase amount is $4 billion, 65 issues are eligible, and the operation settles on November 6.

Offered and accepted totals are published only after the operation runs. A 10 year to 20 year liquidity support operation runs earlier today, November 4, with a maximum of $6 billion; its results are also published after the operation.
