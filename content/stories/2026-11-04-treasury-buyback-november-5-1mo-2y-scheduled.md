Latest: The Treasury accepted $4 billion, the full maximum, in its liquidity support buyback on Thursday, November 5, in the 1 month to 2 year maturity bucket. Holders offered $47.319 billion, for cover of 11.83 times the amount accepted, and Treasury accepted paper from 4 of the 65 eligible issues. The operation ran at 1:40 p.m. Eastern and settles on November 6.

The accepted amount is 61 percent below the $10.27 billion average accepted in the previous four operations in this bucket, because the maximum for this operation was set at $4 billion. Offered volume was the heavy side of the result: holders put up nearly twelve times the amount Treasury took.

The Treasury had scheduled the operation on November 4 with a maximum purchase amount of $4 billion for nominal coupon securities in the 1 month to 2 year bucket under its liquidity support programme.
