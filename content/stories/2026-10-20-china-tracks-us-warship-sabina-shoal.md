China's military said on Tuesday it had sent forces to track and monitor the USS Charleston, which the Southern Theatre Command said entered waters adjacent to Sabina Shoal in the Spratly Islands in the early hours without Chinese government approval, and it called on the United States to cease such activities. A Philippine military spokesperson said the US ship did not trespass into Chinese territory and that no nation has authority to require prior permission for lawful transit through international waters, and Reuters reported the US Navy's last known freedom of navigation operation in the South China Sea was more than a year ago. Further north, Taiwan's Coast Guard said a Chinese coast guard ship entered restricted waters near the Taiwan-controlled Pratas Islands on Tuesday and that a standoff, with both ships broadcasting claims of sovereignty, was continuing.
