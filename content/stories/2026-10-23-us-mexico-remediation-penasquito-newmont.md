The United States and Mexico announced a course of remediation at Minera Peñasquito, Newmont's gold and silver mine in Zacatecas, under the USMCA rapid-response labour mechanism, the Office of the United States Trade Representative said. Under the plan, Mexico will ensure the company reinstates two workers with full backpay and benefits, distributes its neutrality statement on freedom of association to all personnel, trains staff in person on those rights, and publicly acknowledges its unlawful and interfering practices, while Mexico keeps an anonymous reporting line open for workers; both governments set an October 28, 2026 deadline to complete the plan. The case began with a May 13, 2026 petition from the Los Mineros union alleging the company retaliated against workers for union activity, including unlawful dismissals, and Mexico's own review preceded the agreement.
