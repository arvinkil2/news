Alcoa has entered definitive agreements to sell portions of its former Massena East smelter site in Massena, New York, to an unnamed data center firm for up to $315 million, the company said alongside its third quarter results. Alcoa expects to receive $50 million to $65 million at closing, expected within six months, and a further $60 million to $250 million over the following seven years as data center capacity is approved and commissioned, with most proceeds in the first five years.

The 1,600 acre former Reynolds Metals site, closed in 2014, is initially permitted for 180MW and could reach 635MW in phases, executives said on the earnings call, and chief executive Bill Oplinger said two further deals from Alcoa's portfolio of ten former smelter sites could follow quickly. Datacenter Dynamics reports, without confirmation from the buyer, that planning filings for an 88 acre, two-building project on the former campus name North Country Colocation Services, an affiliate of bitcoin mining firm NYDIG.
