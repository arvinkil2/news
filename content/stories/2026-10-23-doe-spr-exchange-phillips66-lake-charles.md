The US Department of Energy on Friday announced the authorization of an emergency exchange of up to 400,000 barrels of crude oil from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to address a crude oil supply disruption affecting the Phillips 66 Lake Charles Refinery in Louisiana. The exchange is authorized from the West Hackberry SPR site for immediate delivery, and under its terms all borrowed crude will be returned with additional premium barrels. The department did not say what caused the disruption, saying only that the supply would offset temporary disruptions to crude deliveries and support continued production of transportation fuels serving Louisiana and surrounding markets.
