Indonesia is cutting its natural gas exports to Singapore by 31 billion British thermal units per day from Saturday, split between 15 from Natuna and 16 from the Jambi Merang field in Sumatra, according to comments by SKK Migas chief Djoko Siswanto on October 23 carried in Indonesian media and reported by The Straits Times. Gas Supply, a Temasek-owned company and one of Singapore's largest gas suppliers, said its delivery of gas to customers has been affected by temporary reductions from its Indonesian supplier and that it is in direct communication with affected customers.

The cuts were framed in Indonesian media as part of the Prabowo government's move to prioritize domestic energy needs. David Broadstock of the Lantau Group estimated the reduction at about 2.25 percent of Singapore's gas usage and said he did not expect massive market disruptions, though replacement cargoes, potentially from the United States or the Middle East, would presumably cost more.
