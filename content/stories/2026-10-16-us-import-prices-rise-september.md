US import prices rose 1.1 percent in September, following a 0.5 percent rise in August, as fuel import prices increased 4.8 percent and nonfuel import prices rose 0.7 percent. Over the year to September, import prices advanced 8.1 percent, the largest 12 month increase since the year ended July 2022.

US export prices rose 2.2 percent in September after rising 1.2 percent in August, and were up 11.6 percent over the year.
