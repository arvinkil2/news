Ukraine's General Staff said its forces struck Kombinat Kamensky, a military industrial plant in Kamensk-Shakhtinsky in Russia's Rostov region, in the night leading to October 31, in a combined strike using drones and missile troops, including the Ukrainian Navy's Neptune coastal missile systems. The plant produces solid rocket fuel for ammunition for the Uragan, Smerch and Tornado-S multiple launch rocket systems, and for other missile systems and aviation munitions, and the General Staff said a finished products warehouse caught fire and four production workshops were damaged.

The same General Staff report said four Russian logistics warehouses were also hit, near Khartsyzk and Dokuchaievsk in Donetsk region, Novovasylivka in Zaporizhzhia region and Novoaydar in Luhansk region. UNIAN carried no Russian response to the claims.
