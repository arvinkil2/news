US industrial production rose 0.7 percent in September after a revised 0.1 percent rise in August, the Federal Reserve reported on Friday, above a consensus expectation of 0.4 percent. Manufacturing output rose 0.3 percent, mining output rose 0.2 percent, and utilities output rose 3.5 percent, with total production 2.2 percent above a year earlier and up at a 2.8 percent annual rate in the third quarter.

Capacity utilization rose to 76.9 percent, above the 76.5 percent expected and still 2.5 percentage points below its long run average. Motor vehicles and parts output fell 3.0 percent, while durable manufacturing was lifted by gains of 1 percent or more in primary metals, machinery, and electrical equipment, and semiconductor output drove a strong third quarter for durable goods.
