Johnson & Johnson is in advanced talks to buy Ollin Biosciences for up to $3.4 billion, the Financial Times reported on Sunday, citing people familiar with the matter. The structure under discussion is $2.2 billion in upfront cash and up to $1.2 billion in milestone payments, and the report said the discussions could still fall apart.

Ollin, backed by Arch Venture Partners and Mubadala Capital, is developing OLN324 for wet age related macular degeneration and diabetic macular oedema, two leading causes of severe vision loss, and the treatment is expected to enter late stage clinical trials. Neither company commented in the report.
