A tanker travelling southeast through the Gulf was struck by two unidentified projectiles on its port side on Monday morning, 72 nautical miles east of the Saudi coastal city of Ras al-Khafji, Britain's UK Maritime Trade Operations agency said. All crew members were accounted for and no environmental impact had been reported, and the agency gave no name, flag or cargo for the vessel and no indication of who fired the projectiles.

The strike follows Friday's hit on the Qatari LNG carrier Al Mayeda and Sunday's report of another vessel struck in the Strait of Hormuz, and it moves the pattern north, into waters off the Saudi and Kuwaiti export coast rather than the strait itself.
