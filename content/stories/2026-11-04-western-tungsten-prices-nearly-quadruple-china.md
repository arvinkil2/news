Ammonium paratungstate in Rotterdam traded at $278.22 a kilogram in October, against $74.43 a kilogram ex-works in China, according to a MetalMiner report cited by Mining.com. The 3.74 times multiple averaged 1.24 times from September 2021 to June 2025 and broke out only in April 2026; between March and September 2026, monthly average China prices fell 58 percent while Rotterdam prices rose 39 percent.

MetalMiner attributed the split to China's February 2025 export licensing, which restricted tungsten exports, and to US measures that include tariffs raised to 16.2 percent in December 2025, an August 2026 scrap allocation order, and defence procurement restrictions taking effect on January 1, 2027. The report said the Western constraint is conversion capacity rather than scrap supply, so scrap can accumulate unless US processing capacity grows.
