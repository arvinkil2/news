Italy's seasonally adjusted industrial production index rose 1.2 percent in September compared with August, ISTAT reported, while the calendar adjusted index fell 0.1 percent compared with September 2025 and third quarter production fell 0.4 percent from the second quarter. In a companion release, ISTAT put September's trade surplus at 2,083 million euros, with seasonally adjusted exports down 0.2 percent on the month, imports up 3.3 percent, and import prices up 1.9 percent on the month and 9.6 percent on the year.
