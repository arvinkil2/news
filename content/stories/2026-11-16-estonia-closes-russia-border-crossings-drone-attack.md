Estonia will close its road border crossings with Russia from December 1, the government announced, in response to an attempted arson attack in the early hours of November 14 on Threod Systems, a drone maker in Viimsi near Tallinn whose launchers are used by the Ukrainian military. Eight Latvian citizens were detained on suspicion of trying to set fire to the facility and cause an explosion, and the Internal Security Service said its assessment is that the attack was carried out at the behest of the GRU, Russia's military intelligence service; the facility sustained no significant damage and production was not disrupted.

Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said closing the crossings will reduce Russia's ability to conduct hostile activities against Estonia and limit sanctions circumvention risks, and that he would raise further measures at today's EU foreign ministers meeting in Brussels. The attempt follows an August fire at Milrem Robotics in Tallinn that Estonian officials also attributed to Russian security services.
