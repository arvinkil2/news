Moscow's air defences destroyed 41 drones flying toward the capital on Tuesday, Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said, after 12 more were shot down overnight, taking the two-day total to 53, according to the Defence Ministry figures reported by Interfax. Sheremetyevo airport operated under temporary restrictions that were lifted the same evening, with some flights held for up to two and a half hours, and TASS reported more than 30 aircraft bound for Moscow were diverted to other cities, including St. Petersburg, Nizhny Novgorod and Kazan. Emergency services were working where debris fell, Interfax reported; neither account reported casualties or damage, and Ukraine has not commented in the reports read.
