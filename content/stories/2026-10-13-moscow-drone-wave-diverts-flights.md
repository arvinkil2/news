**Latest (Wednesday, October 14):** A second overnight drone wave on Moscow was larger than Tuesday's, with Mayor Sergei Sobyanin putting the count shot down on approach to the capital since midnight at 116 and the last batch of 20 reported at 5:37 a.m. Moscow time, according to Vedomosti, while all four Moscow airports were restricted again before reopening in the morning, Interfax, TASS and RIA reported. Hours later, Russian ballistic missiles struck energy facilities in Kyiv, where Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported one person killed in the Holosiivskyi district and one person injured and hospitalised in the Darnytskyi district, according to NV, and President Zelensky said the previous day's strike had hit the CHP-4 heating plant, one of the capital's key energy facilities. Both sides' figures are their own officials' accounts, with no independent corroboration in the reports read.

Moscow's air defences destroyed another 10 drones late on Tuesday, Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said, taking the count shot down on approach to the capital to 63 for the day, according to Interfax, with emergency services working where debris fell. Sheremetyevo airport operated under temporary restrictions that were lifted the same evening, with some flights held for up to two and a half hours, and TASS reported more than 30 aircraft bound for Moscow were diverted to other cities, including St. Petersburg, Nizhny Novgorod and Kazan. Neither account reported casualties or damage, and Ukraine has not commented in the reports read.
