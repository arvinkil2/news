New Zealand's government bond tender on Thursday drew the strongest demand in more than a decade, Bloomberg reported, with the sale of April 2037 bonds attracting bids for 7.46 times the amount on offer, the highest cover for any tenor since February 2013. Bloomberg attributed the demand to preliminary election results that eased concern over government finances.

The benchmark 10-year yield extended its drop after the tender, falling four basis points to 4.64 percent.
