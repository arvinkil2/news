Leaders of the United States, Japan, Australia and India are expected to meet in Manila on Tuesday, November 17, shortly after President Trump arrives on the last day of the ASEAN summit, two people familiar with the planning told Reuters. It would be the first Quad leaders meeting since the four met in Delaware in September 2024, and the first since President Trump returned to office. Critical minerals supply is expected on the agenda, along with free and unimpeded commercial shipping lanes for energy supplies and attacks on commercial ships, and the meeting follows the Quad foreign ministers' October 31 talks in Atlanta, where they agreed to respond jointly to China's control of critical minerals supply chains.
