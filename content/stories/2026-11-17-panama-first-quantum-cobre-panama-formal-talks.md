Panama will commence formal discussions with First Quantum Minerals on a new agreement for the Cobre Panama mine, President Jose Raul Mulino announced, and First Quantum said on Tuesday it welcomes the government-led process. Mulino set five principles for the talks: the subsoil minerals belong to Panama, the country must obtain the maximum economic benefit, no company may decide on Panamanian territory, waters or airspace, the highest environmental standards will be required with independent oversight, and any agreement must fully comply with Panama's constitution and laws. A public participation process will run in parallel with the discussions and before any agreement is reached.

Cobre Panama has been in preservation and safe management since November 2023, when Panama's Supreme Court ruled its operating contract unconstitutional after nationwide protests. First Quantum said a reactivation could generate 6,000 direct and 30,000 indirect jobs and business for 2,000 registered suppliers, and that it will engage on the basis of the Interministerial Commission's report and the president's criteria.
