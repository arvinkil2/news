ExxonMobil Holdings Corp. has formalised negotiations with Venezuela about operating one or more oil fields, Bloomberg reported on Monday. The company said in a statement that it signed a memorandum of understanding with the Venezuelan government and state-owned Petróleos de Venezuela SA for “potential upstream opportunities”; memoranda of this kind are typically non-binding.

The step comes months after chief executive Darren Woods called Venezuela “uninvestable” given its condition at the time, Bloomberg reported.
