Ethiopian government forces and an allied militia entered Adwa, about 40 kilometres from the Eritrean border, on Sunday, a day after Tigray People's Liberation Front fighters withdrew from the town, residents told AFP via Al Jazeera. One resident said shops were closed and government institutions, including Adwa Hospital, were looted overnight, while another said federal forces had deployed in the town but had not yet reached its centre.

The move follows the federal capture of Mekelle last week and of Adigrat days ago, and Ethiopian Airlines resumed flights to Mekelle on Sunday for the first time since September 23. Ethiopia has accused Eritrea, Sudan and Egypt of backing the armed alliance fighting with the TPLF; Khartoum and Cairo have denied involvement, and Eritrea severed diplomatic ties with Ethiopia over the accusations last week.
