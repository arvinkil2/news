US October employment report due today. The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes the October employment report at 8:30 AM Eastern today, Friday, November 6. Market consensus reported ahead of the release looks for payroll growth of about 65,000, up from 29,000 in September, with the unemployment rate expected to stay at 4.2 percent. The report had not been released at this edition's write time, so no outcome figures appear here. Statistics Canada publishes Canadian employment data this morning as well.

Bank of England and Banxico decisions, already published. The Bank of England's rise to 4 percent, voted 8 to 1 on November 5, and Banxico's surprise quarter point cut to 6.25 percent, voted 3 to 2, were both published as full stories on this site on November 5 from the central banks' own documents, and are updated, not duplicated, in today's edition. Banxico's new rate takes effect today, November 6. The next Bank of England decision is on December 17.

Treasury buyback settles today. The Treasury's November 5 liquidity support buyback in the 1 month to 2 year bucket, in which it accepted the full $4 billion maximum against $47.319 billion offered, settles today, November 6. The existing story for the operation has been updated in place with the results; see the buybacks disposition in today's rates brief.

Japan 10 year auction, closed. The Ministry of Finance's November 5 auction of 10 year JGBs, carried in yesterday's roundup with a lowest accepted yield of 2.800 percent and cover of about 3.2 times, is closed as a dedup item today; the Bank of Japan's September minutes, published the same day, get a full story in this edition.

Dutch businesses shelve investment. Almost a quarter of Dutch businesses postponed or cancelled investment plans over the past twelve months, according to the Netherlands Business Survey of October 2026 published by Statistics Netherlands and partner organisations on November 5. Over 48 percent of businesses reported barriers to investment, with financial constraints the most cited at 17 percent, and business confidence was unchanged at the start of the fourth quarter at minus 5.3. Single-sourced to the CBS release.

Turkey inflation report briefing set for November 12. The Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey has announced a briefing on its Inflation Report 2026-IV for November 12, 2026, the next scheduled set piece for Turkish rate expectations.

Ueda speaks in Washington. Bank of Japan Governor Kazuo Ueda speaks at an IMF seminar in Washington on Friday, November 6, the first leadership remarks since the September minutes showed the board debating a faster pace of increases.
