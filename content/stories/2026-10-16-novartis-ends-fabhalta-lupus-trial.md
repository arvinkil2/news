Novartis has terminated a mid-stage trial of Fabhalta (iptacopan) in lupus nephritis, according to Endpoints News, which cited a federal clinical trials database entry giving strategic considerations as the reason. No further Novartis statement was visible in the material available.

Fabhalta is an oral factor B inhibitor already marketed for other indications. The report did not state any implication for those uses, and no safety or efficacy finding was reported.
