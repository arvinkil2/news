A Vulcan rocket operated by United Launch Alliance, the joint venture of Boeing and Lockheed Martin, failed shortly after launching from Florida on Sunday, Reuters reported. The failure destroyed the payload of 40 satellites for Amazon's Leo broadband internet unit, and Reuters said it was the launch company's first mission failure in its two-decade history. The wire gave no cause for the failure and no statement from the company or from Amazon.
