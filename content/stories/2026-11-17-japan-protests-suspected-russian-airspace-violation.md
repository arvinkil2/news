Two aircraft believed to be Russian entered Japanese airspace off Okinoshima Island in Fukuoka Prefecture early on Tuesday, prompting the Air Self-Defense Force to scramble fighter jets and issue warnings, Japan's defence ministry said. Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara called the violation a serious infringement of Japan's sovereignty and completely unacceptable, and said Tokyo had lodged a strong protest with Russia and urged it to prevent a recurrence. The aircraft then transited the Tsushima Strait, passed between Yonaguni Island and Taiwan and continued into the Pacific; Taiwan's defence ministry separately said it detected two Russian military aircraft off its east coast on Tuesday.
