Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said air defences destroyed another 15 drones flying toward the Russian capital overnight, bringing the total he claims for the attack to 44, Interfax reported. Sobyanin said emergency service specialists were working where debris fell, and Ukrainian outlet TSN, reporting the same statements, said there was as yet no information on damage or casualties and no independent confirmation of the Russian claims.
