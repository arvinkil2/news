**Latest, October 28:** The Reserve Bank's money market operations release for Tuesday, October 27 shows the marginal standing facility draw down sharply to 7,022 crore rupees at 5.75 percent, from 17,385 crore on Monday, while banks placed 172,235 crore in the standing deposit facility at 5.25 percent. Net liquidity on an outstanding basis, including the day's operations, stood at minus 30,419 crore, improved from Monday's minus 34,082 crore. Call money averaged 5.75 percent and triparty repo 5.62 percent. The 150,007 crore four day repo allotted Monday remains outstanding and reverses on Friday, October 30.

**October 27:** The Reserve Bank's money market operations release for Monday, October 26 shows the marginal standing facility draw down to 17,385 crore rupees at 5.75 percent, from 28,311 crore on Saturday and 29,479 crore on Sunday, while banks placed 182,261 crore in the standing deposit facility at 5.25 percent. Net liquidity on an outstanding basis, including the day's operations, stood at minus 34,082 crore, an improvement on Sunday's minus 39,083 crore. Overnight money traded firm regardless: call money averaged 5.81 percent and triparty repo 5.74 percent. The 150,007 crore repo allotted on Monday reverses on Friday, October 30, the same day the bank auctions 33,000 crore of Government of India dated securities.

**October 26:** Monday's auction drew bids of 186,666 crore rupees against the 150,000 crore on offer, and the Reserve Bank allotted 150,007 crore at a cut-off rate of 5.55 percent and a weighted average rate of 5.59 percent, with bids at the cut-off allotted at 85.07 percent. The queue is shorter than Friday's, when banks bid 106,949 crore for a 50,000 crore operation, more than twice the amount notified, and the cut-off has come down from 5.58 percent after rising through last week. The larger supply relieved the marginal premium without clearing the demand for term money into month-end.

The weekend figures show why cash stays unevenly held. Banks drew 28,311 crore at the marginal standing facility on Saturday and 29,479 crore on Sunday at 5.75 percent, while other banks placed 199,912 crore and 201,337 crore in the standing deposit facility at 5.25 percent on the same two days. Net liquidity on an outstanding basis, including the day's operations, stood at minus 39,083 crore rupees in Sunday's release, close to Friday's minus 39,422 crore.

The Reserve Bank of India is stepping up its liquidity support. On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions in the near term, the bank said it will conduct a variable rate repo auction on Monday, October 26 with a notified amount of 150,000 crore rupees, a tenor of four days and reversal on Friday, October 30. The operation is three times the 50,000 crore notified for Friday's three-day repo and a day longer.

The escalation answers Friday's queue. Banks bid 106,949 crore for the three-day operation, more than twice the amount on offer, and the bank allotted 50,003 crore at a cut-off of 5.58 percent and a weighted average rate of 5.60 percent, up from cut-offs of 5.54 percent on Thursday and 5.52 percent on Wednesday. Demand for central bank money has risen through the week at rising prices, and Monday's larger, longer operation is sized to meet more of that queue rather than ration it at the margin.

The week's pattern is why the size matters. The Reserve Bank rejected every bid at Wednesday's treasury bill auction, surplus banks placed 161,742 crore in the standing deposit facility on Thursday while others drew at the marginal standing facility, and Friday's dated government auction still cleared in full. Cash exists in the system but is unevenly held, and a 150,000 crore four-day repo is the instrument for moving it across banks and across the month-end window. The cut-off rate on Monday will show whether the larger supply relieves the premium banks have been paying to term out their funding.
