Singapore-based DayOne Data Centers has put its plans for a multibillion-dollar US initial public offering on hold, Bloomberg News reported, citing people familiar with the matter, as investor concerns over lofty valuations and volatility in AI-related stocks weigh on the sector. DayOne had been targeting an offering raising as much as 5 billion dollars as early as the fourth quarter and filed for the listing in October, working with Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citigroup and BNP Paribas; it is now considering delaying the listing or pursuing a smaller offering, and could still pursue an IPO in 2027 if conditions improve, though no final decision has been made. DayOne operates hyperscale data centres across Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, and its largest customer is TikTok owner ByteDance.
