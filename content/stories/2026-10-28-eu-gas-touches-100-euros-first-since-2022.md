Europe's benchmark natural gas contract briefly traded at 100 euros per megawatt hour at about 13:35 GMT on Wednesday, up around 4 percent from the previous session and the first time at that level since December 2022, according to Intercontinental Exchange data reported by Reuters. The rise comes as disruptions to liquefied natural gas shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, a route for about 20 percent of global supply, leave Europe and Asia competing for fewer cargoes, with prices now about three times their levels before the war on Iran. Europe has begun the heating season with storage nearly 75 percent full and Germany's at about 60 percent, both below last year, and the German front-year power contract rose 1.7 percent to 159.30 euros per megawatt hour, its highest since June 2023.
