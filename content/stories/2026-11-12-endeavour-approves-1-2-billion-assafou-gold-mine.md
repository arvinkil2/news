Endeavour Mining has approved construction of its $1.2 billion Assafou gold project on the Tanda-Iguela property in Côte d'Ivoire, with an initial mine life of 16 years, a processing plant designed for 5 million tonnes of ore a year, and first production targeted for the first half of 2029. The development budget is up $139 million, or 13%, from the $1.06 billion in April's definitive feasibility study, mostly on higher land and crop compensation, and the company has awarded the engineering, procurement and construction management contract to Lycopodium and reached an agreement in principle on its mining convention with the Ivorian government.

The decision came with third-quarter results showing record free cash flow of $1.02 billion for the first nine months of 2026, up 50% year on year, and $263 million of net cash at the end of September after $412 million of gross debt reduction.
