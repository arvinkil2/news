Malaysia has made an exception to its policy against exporting raw minerals for France, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said at a joint press conference with President Emmanuel Macron in Kuala Lumpur on Tuesday, after the two leaders witnessed the exchange of a memorandum of understanding on critical minerals cooperation. "We do not export raw materials, but we have made an exception for France because you are committed to processing them and working with Malaysians as a team," Anwar said, citing local processing, technology transfer and training as the conditions. Macron said the industrial partnership between Malaysia's Malaco Mining and French rare earth specialist Carester, agreed during Anwar's visit to Paris last year, is expected to become operational in early 2027, with a proposed separation plant in Perak starting at 3,000 tonnes a year and expanding to a planned 13,000 tonnes, and further refining in France possible.
