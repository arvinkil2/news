BHP and Rio Tinto have reached supply agreements with China Mineral Resources Group, the state-backed buyer created to consolidate China's iron ore purchasing, according to Investing.com, while Fortescue remains in negotiations and has warned that a prolonged dispute could affect its operations. During this year's talks the group restricted purchases of certain products from all three miners as it pressed for more favourable supply terms.

Executives from both miners, including Rio Tinto iron ore chief Matt Holcz and BHP's Australia head Geraldine Slattery, are among 11 senior Australian business executives due to meet Premier Li Qiang and Chinese business leaders in Beijing on Friday, November 20, alongside Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, at the ninth Australia-China CEO Roundtable, co-hosted by the Business Council of Australia and China Development Bank. China accounts for more than a quarter of Australia's international trade.
