Vietnam's National Assembly adopted a revised penal code on Friday that removes the death penalty for drug trafficking, crimes against humanity, war crimes, rebellion and the rape of a juvenile, replacing the maximum sentence for those offences with life imprisonment. Capital punishment now remains available for only four crimes: treason, murder, terrorism and the illegal production of drugs. Public Security Minister Luong Tam Quang told deputies that some traffickers had been lured, cheated or forced into their actions and could still be rehabilitated, after several deputies had resisted removing drug trafficking from the capital list.
