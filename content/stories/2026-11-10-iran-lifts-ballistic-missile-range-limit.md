Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has lifted its self-imposed limit of 2,000km on the range of its ballistic missiles, its spokesman Brigadier-General Hossein Mohebbi announced at a news conference in Tehran on Saturday, saying the limitation "has been lifted for us" and that Iran would show its capabilities through action rather than statements as it shifts towards an offensive doctrine. No new maximum range was stated, and analysts cited by Al Jazeera say the capability to exceed 2,000km already existed, pointing to Iran's space launch rockets and to the March launch towards the US-UK base at Diego Garcia, about 4,000km away, which Tehran denied. The announcement follows a warning from Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei that any threat from the United States or Israel would be met with a response unlimited by geography, and it withdraws an assurance Europe had been given for years that Iranian missiles would be kept too short-ranged to reach it.
