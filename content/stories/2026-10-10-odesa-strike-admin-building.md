Russian forces struck central Odesa on the evening of October 10, hitting an administrative building and the premises of a local internet provider, according to Ukrainian media citing the Odesa military administration. Odesa regional administration head Oleh Kiper said the building's facade, roof and parked cars were damaged, that the fire caused by the strike has been extinguished, and that no information about dead or injured had been received. The extent of the destruction is still being established.

The strike follows Russian attacks on Odesa on October 6, which damaged an enterprise and started a fire, and on Odesa-region infrastructure, industry, ports and dacha areas on October 5.
