Samsung Electronics disclosed provisional third quarter results on October 29 with operating profit of 107.4405 trillion won, up 783.1 percent from a year earlier, on revenue of 195.2532 trillion won, up 126.9 percent, Yonhap reported. Net profit was 75.1361 trillion won, up 514.6 percent, a figure the October guidance did not carry.

The operating profit came in 1.7 percent above the 105.6764 trillion won market forecast compiled by Yonhap Infomax, and it matches the guidance Samsung issued on October 8, which put operating profit at about 107.4 trillion won on sales of about 195 trillion won. The flash carried no division breakdown, so how the profit splits between memory, foundry, mobile and displays waits for the detailed report.
