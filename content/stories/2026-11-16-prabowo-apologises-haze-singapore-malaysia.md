Prabowo conveyed the apology during a session of the ASEAN summit in Manila, according to the Indonesian presidential office, after haze from fires burning mainly on Sumatra, Borneo and Papua blanketed Singapore and Malaysia for months; both neighbours offered firefighting help rather than public criticism. He said Indonesia had revoked 17 company concessions, had 11 companies charged with 23 more listed to be charged, and had 104 companies in total under police investigation, with 71,000 firefighters deployed and 16 people killed battling the blazes.

Jakarta also plans a new body to strengthen wildfire prevention. The apology came as the haze began to clear: the ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre issued a no-transboundary-haze notice for southern Southeast Asia on Thursday, after rain reduced hotspots in Sumatra and Kalimantan.
