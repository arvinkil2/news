Encounter Resources has extended high-grade niobium mineralisation beyond the existing resource boundary at the Green deposit in Western Australia's West Arunta, with drilling returning 25 metres at 3.8 percent niobium pentoxide from 80 metres and 19 metres at 2.5 percent from 78 metres, outside the current high-grade footprint. The results add to Green's inferred resource of 100 million tonnes at 0.71 percent, including 19 million tonnes at 1.6 percent, and will feed an updated resource estimate in the first half of 2027.

Forrestania Resources has begun wet commissioning at its Edna May gold mine in Western Australia after delivering first ore into the primary crusher, about 11 weeks after taking operational control on September 4. Combined run-of-mine stockpiles at Edna May and Lake Johnston have passed 250,000 tonnes, the company has expanded its resource inventory from about 300,000 ounces to more than three million ounces over the past year, and the first gold pour remains targeted for December 2026.

In Vietnam, Khanh An Industry has broken ground on a rare earth materials plant in Haiphong costing 1.58 trillion dong, about $61 million, on a 14-hectare site in the Lach Huyen non-tariff industrial zone, targeting 10,000 tonnes a year of high-purity materials, according to VnExpress and Vietnam News Agency reporting. The plant adds processing capacity outside China at a small but growing scale.

In Rwanda, the Mining Association says unlocking bank financing is the key to pushing mineral exports past $2 billion, after earnings reached $1.75 billion in 2024, up from about $373 million in 2017, against a national target of $2.17 billion a year by 2029. The sector centres on tin, tungsten and tantalum alongside a large gold trade, and the association's case is that mechanisation and on-site beneficiation cannot scale without lender access.

Elevra Lithium used its November 19 annual general meeting to report that the staged expansion of its North American Lithium operation in Quebec is fully funded stage by stage, after a package combining an institutional placement, a convertible note investment from the Canada Growth Fund and a retail share purchase plan. Elevra was formed by the August 2025 merger of Sayona Mining and Piedmont Lithium, and its growth case rests on the staged NAL expansion and the Moblan project.
