**Budderfly's 500 million dollar municipal push.** Energy-as-a-service company Budderfly will invest 500 million dollars in energy efficiency upgrades at public facilities under its Modernizing American Cities programme, announced in October and detailed this week. The programme targets municipalities of about 10,000 to 30,000 residents, offers up to 5 million dollars of up-front capital each, and could reach about 100 clients over 12 to 18 months, an executive told Utility Dive. Municipalities repay from the savings achieved over 15-year contracts, with Schneider Electric supplying energy management technology. The first project, in Cadillac, Michigan, is a 4.4 million dollar upgrade across seven facilities including city hall and the wastewater treatment plant.

**Nigerian diesel imports jump 917 percent.** Nigeria's diesel imports rose to 6.1 million litres a day in October from 0.6 million in September, a 917 percent increase, according to the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority factsheet reported by BusinessDay. Total diesel supply averaged 16.8 million litres a day, up 93 percent, as consumption rose 7 percent to 15.5 million litres a day. Petrol imports also rose 47 percent to about 15 million litres a day, even as the Dangote refinery lifted its local petrol supply to 31.89 million litres a day.

**Nuclear-powered LNG carrier planned.** South Korea's Samsung Heavy Industries and US firm Tesser plan what would be the world's first nuclear-powered LNG carrier, according to gCaptain and Marine Insight reports this week. The design pairs a liquefied natural gas carrier with nuclear propulsion, a combination no commercial vessel has yet operated, and it remains at the planning stage with no delivery date reported.
