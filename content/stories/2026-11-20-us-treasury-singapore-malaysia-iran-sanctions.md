US Treasury Department officials met authorities in Singapore and Malaysia this week, including regulators and national security bodies, to warn of the risks of non-compliance with US sanctions on Iran and to urge banks, law firms and companies to stay alert for Iranian front companies, with added scrutiny of maritime transport, aviation services and procurement networks. An unnamed Treasury official said Washington would target Iran's lifelines as they are found and that doing so would not be a negotiation. The officials also raised an Iranian airline flying to Malaysia: Reuters identified the carrier as Iran Airtour, whose Tehran to Kuala Lumpur flight last operated on November 14, while a November 17 departure did not leave. Singapore's government said it is firmly committed to preventing bad actors from abusing its corporate vehicles; Malaysia's government did not respond to requests for comment.
