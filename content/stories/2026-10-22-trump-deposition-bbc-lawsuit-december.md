U.S. Magistrate Judge Enjoliqué Lett ordered President Trump on Thursday to sit for a deposition by December 11 in his $10 billion defamation lawsuit against the BBC, after BBC lawyers argued he was delaying his appearance. His lawyers had proposed January 11 to 22, 2027, saying presidential duties prevented an earlier date, and Lett also warned that the complaint could be dismissed if Trump does not comply with orders to hand the BBC's lawyers records relevant to the suit.

Trump was first scheduled to be deposed on October 16 and canceled, with his lawyers citing matters affecting national security and the economy. The BBC's lawyers told the judge he spent that day campaigning in Georgia. The suit, filed last December, claims a BBC Panorama documentary, "Trump: A Second Chance," spliced clips of his January 6, 2021 speech to make it appear he had directly called for violent action; the BBC has apologized for the edit, said the programme will not air again, and maintains there is no basis for a defamation claim.
