The American Petroleum Institute estimated on Wednesday that US crude oil inventories fell by 11.12 million barrels in the week ending October 9, after a drop of 2.09 million barrels the week before, while no barrels were released from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, which the report put at 283 million barrels. Gasoline inventories rose 1.1 million barrels and distillate inventories fell 1.5 million barrels, with stocks at the Cushing delivery hub rising 1.823 million barrels. Brent traded near 106 dollars a barrel late on Wednesday and WTI near 95.75 dollars, both well above their levels a week earlier, as the draw landed on a market already short of diesel and watching renewed attacks on Saudi and Hormuz infrastructure.
