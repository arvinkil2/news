Australia has announced another tranche of sanctions on individuals and organisations involved in illegal settlement expansion and human rights abuses against Palestinians in the West Bank, Foreign Minister Penny Wong said. The measures prohibit Australians and Australian businesses from trading with, or providing financial or other support to, three individuals and four organisations, and they target a construction company involved in building settlements for the first time in an Australian package.

The Guardian named settler figures Daniella Weiss and her Nachala Movement, settler Eliav Libi, and the Eyal Hari Yehuda construction company, which it said helped develop settlements and outposts and demolished Palestinian structures. Wong said the government acted because the rapid expansion of settlements and settler violence are extinguishing the possibility of a two-state solution, pointing to the E1 settlement, where demolitions and displacement have occurred in recent weeks, and said she had outlined Australia's position to her Israeli counterpart, Gideon Sa'ar, on Tuesday night. The announcement was coordinated with the United Kingdom, which sanctioned eight West Bank settlement firms earlier on Tuesday.
