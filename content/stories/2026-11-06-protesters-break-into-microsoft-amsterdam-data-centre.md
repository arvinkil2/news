More than 100 protesters linked to the Dutch climate and justice collective Geef Tegengas broke into a Microsoft data centre construction site in Amsterdam on Friday, while hundreds more protested peacefully across the street as part of a wider anti-data centre march planned across Europe this weekend. The facility is a 78 megawatt Pure Data Centres site with Microsoft as sole tenant, and Amsterdam police said one person was arrested.

The group, which said 500 people entered the site, pointed to Microsoft's work with US immigration enforcement and the Israeli military, AI surveillance, climate change and water use, and has accused the project of circumventing the national ban on large hyperscale developments by splitting into three smaller facilities. Microsoft said it respects the right to protest peacefully but does not condone unlawful actions that put safety at risk.
