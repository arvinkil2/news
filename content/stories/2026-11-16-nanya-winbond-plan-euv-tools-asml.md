Taiwan's two second-tier DRAM makers plan to adopt extreme ultraviolet lithography. Nanya Technology will deploy EUV at its Fab 5A in northern Taiwan in the first quarter of 2027, with the fab expected to begin production by the end of that year, Datacenter Dynamics reported, citing local media; Nanya's board in August approved up to US$10.53 billion of phase-one equipment spending for the fab between 2026 and 2029, including a budget for EUV tools, and the fab is planned at 30,000 wafers a month in 2028, rising to 50,000 by 2030. Winbond expects to receive its first EUV tool in about two years and to use EUV at its Kaohsiung fab from 2030 for its second-generation 14-nanometre DRAM process. ASML is the sole supplier of EUV tools, and neither company has disclosed what it will spend on them.
