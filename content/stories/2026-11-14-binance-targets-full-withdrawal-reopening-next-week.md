Binance said on Saturday that it aims to reopen withdrawals for all remaining networks and assets by the end of next week, as operations normalise after a security breach linked to North Korea saw $6.3 billion stolen from the platform last month. The exchange said it reopened withdrawals for 87 networks this week, in a phased resumption that began earlier this month after it had covered 95 percent of the stolen tokens.

Binance and the FBI have attributed the October 29 theft to North Korea, which Pyongyang has denied, and blockchain investigation firms have attributed it to a group known as TraderTraitor. Binance has vowed to repay customers in full.
