Algeria's General Directorate of National Security (DGSN) announced Sunday that authorities had arrested 61 Moroccan nationals earlier this month on suspicion of planning "acts of sabotage" undermining security and public order, per Arab News. The group, described as an "international Moroccan criminal network" and said to include a former soldier in the Moroccan army, allegedly entered Algeria via mountain trails from neighboring Tunisia. Relations between the two countries have been strained for years, their shared land border closed since 1994, with Algiers severing diplomatic relations in 2021; the two remain at odds over Western Sahara.
