**Latest (Sunday, October 11, midday):** a man has been detained. Poland on Sunday detained a man accused of plotting to assassinate Prime Minister Donald Tusk and Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, Deutsche Welle reported. Tusk confirmed the arrest on X, writing that "the man threatening an attack has been detained" and praising the police for "swift and decisive action"; the Interior Minister also confirmed it. The arrest followed an emergency meeting of Poland's security services convened after the morning's tip-off. No details of the man's identity have been made public.

Polish police received information about a possible attack on Prime Minister Donald Tusk and Deputy Prime Minister and Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz, Interior Ministry spokesperson Karolina Galecka said on X on Sunday. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński and the minister-coordinator of special services, Tomasz Siemoniak, ordered action in response, and an emergency meeting of police, the State Protection Service and the Internal Security Agency (ABW) was set for 11:00 a.m. (0900 GMT) at the ABW Counter-Terrorism Centre.

The announcement comes as Polish authorities are on high alert after three school attacks in four days in Mazovia province, in which a school caretaker was killed on Thursday. In September, Tusk warned that Russia was likely intensifying hybrid attacks against NATO's eastern flank, including possible drone or rocket strikes on European countries supporting Ukraine; Moscow has denied any such plans. No further details of the reported threat have been made public.
