Southwest Airlines reported third quarter adjusted earnings of 66 cents a share, above the 58 cents analysts expected, on revenue of $8.42 billion, also above the $8.25 billion consensus, with net income of $270 million, up from $54 million a year earlier. The airline still cut its full year 2026 adjusted profit forecast to $2.60 to $2.85 a share from the $3.25 to $4.25 range it gave in July, citing higher fuel costs, and guided fourth quarter profit to 55 to 80 cents a share, below the 82 cents analysts expected.

Chief Executive Bob Jordan said demand remained strong as the airline lapped last year's bag fee and other commercial changes, and Reuters reported Southwest expects fourth quarter fuel to average $4.55 a gallon on the October 15 forward curve. Average fares rose almost 27 percent in the quarter to $231.69, while revenue passengers carried fell 3.2 percent to 33.47 million.
