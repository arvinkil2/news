Statistics Canada's advance indicator shows total manufacturing sales increased 2.3 percent in October 2026, with the largest increases in the petroleum and coal product and primary metal subsectors. The percentage change is calculated using seasonally adjusted data in current dollars, and the release gives no dollar value for October sales.

The estimate is based on a weighted response rate of 69.9 percent, against an average final response rate of 92.6 percent over the previous 12 months, and Statistics Canada notes it is subject to a higher revision rate than the regular monthly release. The official October estimate, based on a more complete set of survey and administrative data, is scheduled for December 14, 2026.
