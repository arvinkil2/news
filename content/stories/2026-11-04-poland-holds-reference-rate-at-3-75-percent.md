The Monetary Policy Council kept the National Bank of Poland's interest rates unchanged at its November 3 to 4 meeting, with the reference rate at 3.75 percent, the lombard rate at 4.25 percent and the deposit rate at 3.25 percent. The council noted that October CPI inflation fell to 3.5 percent year on year from 4.1 percent in September on the Statistics Poland flash estimate, mainly on lower fuel prices, and that inflation excluding food and energy prices also fell.

The meeting carried the staff's November projection, which puts inflation at 3.1 to 3.2 percent in 2026 but raises the 2027 range to 3.1 to 5.3 percent, against 1.5 to 4.0 percent in the July projection, with a return toward target in 2028 at 1.4 to 4.4 percent. The council said further decisions will depend on incoming information on the outlook for inflation and economic activity, and listed fiscal policy, regulated energy prices and wage growth among the risk factors.
