Deliveries of some U.S. weapons systems to Poland are set to be delayed, including interceptors for the Patriot air defense system, Polish army operational commander Gen. Ireneusz Nowak said Monday. Speaking at a panel at the Warsaw Security Forum, Nowak said the delay would hit PAC-3 MSE Patriot missiles "for sure," with additional delays likely to deliveries already scheduled or under contract.

The slippage traces back to April, when Reuters reported U.S. officials had warned some European counterparts that previously contracted weapons deliveries were likely to be delayed as the Iran war draws on American stocks. Nowak's remarks are the first on-record confirmation that Poland, a frontline NATO buyer, is among those affected.
