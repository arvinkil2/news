The Ministry of Defence said on Tuesday that RAF Typhoon fighter jets will fly defensive counter-air missions to protect Saudi cities and critical national infrastructure against aerial attack, in additional support given escalating Houthi attacks in the region. The aircraft will operate in a defensive role and will not conduct offensive strikes against the Houthis, and Britain will also provide an RAF A400M to move defence materiel within Saudi Arabia, military advisers to support Saudi staff officers, and an extension of air-to-air refuelling support for Saudi defensive air operations. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting said he had approved the further support after Houthi attacks that included the targeting of King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, building on the air defence equipment and personnel Britain already has in the country.
