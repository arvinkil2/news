UnitedHealth Group's third-quarter net income rose to $4.11 billion, or $4.56 a share, from $2.35 billion a year earlier, on revenue of $112.7 billion, and the company raised its full-year adjusted earnings outlook to at least $20.50 a share, above the $19.80 analysts had pencilled in. The medical care ratio came in at 88.4%, better than the 89.7% consensus, helped by $610 million of favourable reserve development, while Optum Health's operating earnings recovered to about $735 million from $41 million a year ago. Enrollment continued to shrink as the group exits unprofitable business, with 48.3 million people served against 50.1 million a year earlier.
