Defence Minister Israel Katz said Sunday that the Israeli army has eliminated Hamas's leaders in Gaza, and that Khalil al-Hayya, the movement's political bureau chief, will be next. The statement came as a breaking flash with details to follow, and no independent confirmation of the claim was available.
