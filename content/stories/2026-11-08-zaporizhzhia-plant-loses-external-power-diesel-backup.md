Latest: The IAEA said on Monday that it had been informed an employee of the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant was killed in a drone attack that day, and that an employee of the nearby Zaporizhzhia thermal power plant was killed in a separate drone attack. Director General Rafael Grossi strongly condemned the attacks as unacceptable attacks violating nuclear safety principles, and said all attacks targeting nuclear power plant workers must stop immediately. The agency said another plant employee was also reported killed in a drone attack a few days earlier. The IAEA post did not attribute Monday's attacks; both sides have repeatedly accused each other of putting the plant in jeopardy.

Earlier: The International Atomic Energy Agency said the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant lost connection early on Sunday to its only remaining external power line, the 330 kV Ferrosplavnaya-1 line, the 27th time the plant has lost external power during the conflict, and that the causes of the outage are still unknown. The plant switched to emergency diesel generators to cool its reactors and provide other nuclear safety functions, and IAEA experts at the site were told the plant has enough fuel for a limited number of the generators for about two weeks. Director General Rafael Grossi stressed the importance of restoring the grid connection and of increasing the diesel fuel stocks on site.

The plant's Russian-installed management said the line had been shut down by automatic protection because of damage, that backup diesel generators are supplying the equipment required for the station's safe operation, and that radiation levels at the plant and in the surveillance zone are normal, Reuters reported. That is the management's own account; what damaged the line has not been independently established.

All six of the plant's reactors have been shut down since 2022, but their fuel still needs continuous cooling, which is why the external lines matter. The second line supplying the plant was cut in March, Sweden Herald reported, leaving Ferrosplavnaya-1 as the last working connection until Sunday.
