The International Monetary Fund said Australia's financial system has remained broadly sound and resilient since its 2019 assessment, in a Financial Sector Assessment Program review published on November 26 alongside the conclusion of the 2026 Article IV consultation. The review describes a sector of large, concentrated banks focused on residential mortgage lending alongside a growing superannuation sector, and says external risks have risen, with the war in the Middle East, tighter global financial conditions and slower growth in key trading partners posing challenges. Bloomberg reported that the Fund flagged a series of emerging risks in the pension system, including the challenge of keeping more assets liquid as a wave of workers enters retirement.
