Nvidia was down about 2.7 percent in premarket trading on Monday as of 8:07 AM ET, CNBC reported, extending a slide that erased hundreds of billions of dollars in market value last week after the board announced on Thursday that Jensen Huang had resigned as chief executive. Longtime finance chief Colette Kress is interim chief executive while the board considers internal and external candidates for the permanent role.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia needs a dramatic shakeup, that the board should look outside the company for its next chief executive, and that other executives should face scrutiny over compliance with US export controls. An online petition calling for Huang's reinstatement has drawn more than 170,000 signatures, and US lawmakers are pushing for comprehensive investigations into the scandal.
