South Korea's Defense Acquisition Program Promotion Committee approved the basic strategy for the Jangbogo N (KSS-III Batch-III) nuclear-powered submarine project on Friday, and the Defense Acquisition Program Administration said the project had been exempted from the feasibility study normally required for large weapons programmes, on security grounds. The programme covers three 8,000-ton class boats built with domestic technology, with the hull and system integration put out to competitive tender among domestic shipbuilders and the small modular reactor and key weapons supplied by the government.

The project is now set to start in 2027, with contractor selection after a tender notice in the second half of that year, the lead ship launched in the mid-2030s and entry into service in the late 2030s. DAPA did not disclose the total cost, citing the project's security characteristics; Budget Minister Park Hong-geun has previously put the possible total at roughly 28.9 trillion won, and the specific cost is to be settled early next year.
