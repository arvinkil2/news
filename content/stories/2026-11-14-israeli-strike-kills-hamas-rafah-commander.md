Hamas's armed wing, the Al-Qassam Brigades, said on Sunday that Samir Abu Younis, 53, commander of its Rafah brigade, was killed in an Israeli airstrike on Saturday, and the Israeli military confirmed it had struck a senior commander in Hamas's military wing without identifying him. Medics said Abu Younis was killed and others wounded in a strike that targeted him in a street in the Mawasi area of Khan Younis, in the southern Gaza Strip. Hamas said Abu Younis joined its armed wing in 1991, held senior operational roles in Rafah before taking command of the brigade, and helped plan and oversee the October 7, 2023 attack on Israel; his funeral is set for Sunday afternoon in Khan Younis. The October 2025 ceasefire halted major fighting but has not stopped Israeli attacks or secured Hamas's disarmament; Gaza health authorities say at least 1,600 Palestinians have been killed since it took effect, against five Israeli soldiers in the same period.
