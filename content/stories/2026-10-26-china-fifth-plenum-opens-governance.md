**Latest, October 29:** The session has now closed, and its closing resolution is a discipline document. Deutsche Welle reported that the committee published a resolution praising the leadership and Xi Jinping for, in its words, enormous political courage and a strong sense of responsibility in tackling corruption and the weakening of the party's governance, and emphasizing the need to consistently advance comprehensive and strict party leadership. Xi signaled he would deepen the anti corruption effort and that officials at all levels should show more loyalty and more initiative. The article named no successor, and analysts cited by DW said the succession question was left unanswered, with Xi likely to be confirmed as general secretary at next year's congress and able to stay until 2032. On the evidence available in this run, the communique carried no shift in economic, property or technology policy language.

**Latest, October 29:** The plenum has also promoted Zhang Shengmin, a vice chairman of the Central Military Commission, to the Politburo, Bloomberg reported, and the party plans its next congress next year. Zhang Shengmin is not Zhang Youxia: Shengmin is the military's discipline chief who rose to CMC vice chair last year, while Youxia is the former vice chairman whose expulsion the same session ratified, in the update below. One general promoted and another expelled at the same plenum is the personnel story of the session in miniature: the purge and the rebuilding of the military leadership are proceeding together, a year before a congress at which Xi Jinping is expected to seek a fourth term.

**Latest, October 29:** The fifth plenum has ratified the expulsion of Zhang Youxia from the Communist Party, Yonhap News Agency reported in a breaking news flash on Thursday. Zhang is a former vice chairman of the Central Military Commission, the military's top body, and his ratified expulsion makes the purge of the armed forces' senior ranks a formal outcome of the session. The flash carried no other personnel decisions and no reasons, and the plenum's closing communique has not yet been published.

China's Communist Party began a four day meeting of its Central Committee in Beijing on Monday, the fifth plenum of the cohort formed in 2022, targeting concrete measures for full and rigorous party self-governance as President Xi Jinping seeks to reinforce his authority and enforce ideological uniformity, Reuters reported. Xi briefed the committee on a draft document that the state news agency Xinhua said addressed the challenges the party faced, including growing its governing capacity, guarding against complacency and combating corruption, his signature campaign.

A Xinhua commentary carried in the report said the implementation of major decisions and plans of the Central Committee must not be empty rhetoric, and that institutional rules must be refined to close loopholes and strengthen safeguards, and then actually implemented. The plenum sits in closed session from Monday to Thursday, with a communique expected at the close.

The emphasis differs from the framing this site's week ahead preview carried, which pointed readers to economic, technology and security policy direction and to personnel signals in the communique. The opening reporting instead centres discipline. Xi has overseen an anti corruption campaign that has purged hundreds of senior officials and top generals, including one time allies. Critics argue the purges, particularly those in the highest ranks of the armed forces, are destabilising and undermine combat readiness; the party portrays the campaign as necessary and regenerative, and Reuters reported analysts' view that rooting out corruption is also meant to make policy more predictable for markets.

Analysts cited in the report say this could be the last major party gathering before the 2027 leadership reshuffle, at which Xi is expected to hold the top job for a fourth term. What would change the read is Thursday's communique: whether its economic language shifts the growth, property or technology stance materially, and whether any senior personnel change is announced with it. Until then, the verified facts are the opening, the dates and the theme, all on a single wire account of the official agency's reporting.
