Lynas Rare Earths reported gross sales revenue of A$310.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, up from A$200.2 million a year earlier, a rise of 55 percent. Its average selling price rose 88 percent from a year earlier to a record A$102.1 per kilogram, helped by stronger market pricing, sales of bundled light and heavy rare earth mixes, and floor-price contracts that now cover more than half its output.

The company also flagged lower volumes ahead. Its Malaysian plant is set for a month-long planned maintenance shutdown in the December quarter, which it expects to reduce sales volumes against the September quarter, and its Kalgoorlie site in Western Australia is due a planned two-week outage in fiscal 2027 while Western Power upgrades the network.
