A bomb found at the BioUrja Renewables ethanol plant in Peoria, Illinois was rendered safe by bomb technicians, Police Chief Brad Dixon said at a news conference early on Saturday, and investigators were still searching the facility for other devices. Police announced a reported bomb threat at the plant at about 7:05 p.m. on Friday and ordered everyone who lives or works within a one mile radius to evacuate, a zone covering parts of Peoria and East Peoria that remained in place on Saturday morning, with Manual Academy school offered as a temporary shelter. Mayor Rita Ali said the device was real and not a hoax, the FBI said its Springfield field office is investigating, and no injuries or arrests were reported.
