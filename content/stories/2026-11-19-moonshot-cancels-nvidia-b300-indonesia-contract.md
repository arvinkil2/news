China's Moonshot AI, the Beijing lab behind the Kimi models, has cancelled a contract worth $150 million to use a cluster of Nvidia Blackwell Ultra B300 chips through a data centre in Indonesia from next year, Yonhap reported, citing a November 19 report by The Information. Moonshot had agreed to pay a 50 percent advance under the contract with an unnamed Indonesian cloud company, well above the usual 15 percent advance rate cited in the report. The cancellation followed a new US rule under which data centres providing more than a set amount of computing power to Chinese customers must obtain US government approval within 90 days, and Moonshot has also temporarily suspended discussion of other new contracts while it reviews the rule's effect.
