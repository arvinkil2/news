NOAA's Office for Coastal Management said the application from Eco Minerals, Inc. is fully compliant with the information requirements of the Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act and its implementing regulations. As part of the review, NOAA will hold one virtual public hearing on the application and accept written comments submitted electronically.

The hearing is scheduled for November 16, 2026, from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern Time, and written comments must be received by December 14, 2026. The docket is 260930-0006. The notice does not disclose the target minerals or the proposed exploration area in its summary; those details sit in the application file.
