Brookfield Infrastructure Partners Inc. completed its previously announced plan of arrangement on November 19, reorganizing Brookfield Infrastructure Partners L.P. and Brookfield Infrastructure Corporation into a single Canadian publicly traded corporate entity, according to the business acquisition report the company filed with the SEC on November 20.

Under the transaction, each of BIP and BIPC became a subsidiary of the new corporation. All non-voting limited partnership units of BIP, class A exchangeable subordinate voting shares of BIPC and the various exchangeable units and shares of the group's holding entities were exchanged, directly or indirectly, for newly issued class A subordinate voting shares of the corporation on a one-for-one basis. The common shares of BIP's general partner were exchanged for class B multiple voting shares, and the special general partner units of Brookfield Infrastructure L.P. were exchanged for class I non-voting incentive shares.

Before completion, BIP and BIPC were Brookfield Corporation's flagship listed vehicles for its global infrastructure business, held in parallel so that investors could choose between a partnership unit and a corporate share with equivalent economics. The arrangement collapses that choice into one security.

The filing attaches unaudited pro forma financial statements showing the estimated effect of the transaction, which the company says are based on preliminary estimates and assumptions management believes are reasonable. The corporation said it currently has no plans or proposals beyond those set out in the report.
