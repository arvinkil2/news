Energy Transfer LP completed its previously reported underwritten public offering of senior notes on November 20, 2026, issuing $900 million of 5.450 percent senior notes due 2032, $900 million of 5.900 percent senior notes due 2037 and $900 million of 6.550 percent senior notes due 2057, a total of $2.7 billion.

The notes were issued under the partnership's December 14, 2022 indenture with U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as trustee, as supplemented by a Thirteenth Supplemental Indenture dated November 20, 2026. The offering was registered on the partnership's Form S-3ASR shelf and priced under a prospectus supplement dated November 5, 2026.

The equal sizing across six, eleven and thirty one year maturities keeps Energy Transfer's maturity ladder smooth rather than concentrated, and the 110 basis point spread between the 2032 and 2057 coupons is the market's current price for three extra decades of midstream credit exposure. The filing does not state a specific use of proceeds beyond the offering itself.
