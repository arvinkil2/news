The White House announced on Friday that First Lady Melania Trump had secured a local ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine in Oleshky, the Russian-occupied city on the east bank of the Dnipro River opposite Kherson, with both sides to suspend military action there from October 29 so aid can enter and civilians can leave through a designated safe passage, the BBC and Al Jazeera reported. Melania Trump said in a White House statement that the situation in Oleshky was dire, with residents living with limited electricity, mined roads and regular military attacks, and the White House estimated that 1,700 to 2,000 of the city's pre-war 24,000 residents remain, including about 50 children. The first lady said she initiated the talks and that she and a representative worked directly with Russian and Ukrainian leadership on the terms, that civilian representatives from both countries would visit residents at home to assess what help they need, and that residents who wish to leave would be taken to their preferred destination in Russia or Ukraine. Neither government had commented on the announcement as of the reports.
