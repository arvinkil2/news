Nvidia is no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China, interim chief executive and chief financial officer Colette Kress said on the company's earnings call on Tuesday, telling analysts its guidance includes no licensed Hopper data center products. The decision reverses the position Nvidia took under former chief executive Jensen Huang, who won White House approval in December 2025 to sell H200 chips to approved Chinese customers, with 25 percent of the sales value allocated to the US budget.

Kress gave no reason for the halt and did not say whether Nvidia would pursue other business in China. Antara, reporting her remarks via Sputnik, said Nvidia sold $2.4 billion of chips to Chinese customers in the previous quarter. The halt comes a day after Nvidia's board formed a committee to review export control compliance amid an ongoing investigation, and it is consistent with the fourth quarter outlook in Tuesday's results, which assumed no data center compute revenue from China.
