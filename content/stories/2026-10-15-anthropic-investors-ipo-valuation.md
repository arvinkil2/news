Some investors at Anthropic's investor day in San Francisco on Wednesday discussed a valuation of around $1.5 trillion for the company's planned November share sale, a step down from the roughly $2 trillion previously expected, Reuters reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Anthropic told prospective investors that third quarter revenue rose 45 percent to $16.8 billion and that it was profitable on an adjusted basis.

The disappointment, according to the report, was the slowing sequential growth rate: revenue had more than doubled from the first quarter to $11.6 billion in the second. Investors are also weighing the borrowed money cost of financing the AI buildout, which has committed Anthropic to spend at least $518 billion over a decade on cloud services, computing and infrastructure.
