QXO, the building products distributor, said in a Form 8-K filed on November 9 that preliminary third-quarter net sales were approximately 4.90 billion dollars, up 79 percent from 2.73 billion dollars a year earlier, and that adjusted EBITDA was approximately 557 million dollars, up 85 percent. The preliminary GAAP net loss was approximately 123 million dollars, narrower than the 139 million dollar loss a year earlier.

The company stated that the quarter includes Kodiak Building Partners from April 1, 2026 and TopBuild from July 1, 2026, neither of which was in the prior-year quarter, so the growth is acquisition-driven rather than a like-for-like comparison. QXO said the figures are preliminary and unaudited and that it will report full third-quarter results on November 12.
