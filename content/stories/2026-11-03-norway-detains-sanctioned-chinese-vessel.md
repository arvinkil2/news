Norwegian inspectors detained the Ya Qing Hai Yang after it sought shelter from bad weather in Andfjorden, off the Vesteralen Islands, on October 13, and the vessel remains held there as of November 3. The Norwegian Maritime Authority said the ship had no valid flag-state registration and lacked required certificates, and it must establish valid registration and obtain those certificates before inspectors can complete their work and consider release.

The vessel, which sailed as Ocean 28 in October 2024, carried power-generation modules from China to the Utrenniy terminal serving Novatek's Arctic LNG 2 project, and the United States sanctioned its operator in January 2025 before the European Union added the ship to its own lists. A second Chinese-owned heavy-lift ship inspected in the same fjord, the Jian Yang Cheng He, was not detained.
