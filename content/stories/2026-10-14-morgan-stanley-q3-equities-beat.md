Morgan Stanley reported third quarter profit of $4.91 billion, up 6.5 percent, or $3.03 a share, beating the $2.91 a share LSEG estimate, on revenue up 12 percent to $20.34 billion against $19.95 billion expected. Equities trading did the work, with revenue up 41 percent to $5.79 billion, about $909 million above the StreetAccount estimate, while fixed income trading fell 7 percent to $2.01 billion on weaker credit and commodities, about $260 million below expectations.
