American Electric Power, Amazon and X-energy announced on Monday a joint development plan to explore a 960 MW advanced nuclear project at the former site of Indiana Michigan Power's Breed Generating Station in Sullivan County, Indiana, using 12 of X-energy's 80 MW Xe-100 small modular reactors. If the project is built and enters commercial operation, Amazon would purchase the power under a long-term contract covering the cost of the facility and its operations, and AEP said the agreements give it the capital protections and financial safeguards it requires for any nuclear project to proceed. The companies gave no timing for construction or operation.
