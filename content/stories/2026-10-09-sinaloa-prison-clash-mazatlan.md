Ten people, nine inmates and one family visitor who was a minor, were killed and 16 inmates injured in a fight at the El Castillo Penitentiary Centre in Mazatlan, Sinaloa, on Thursday, the Sinaloa Public Security Secretariat said.

An inter-institutional group of security forces moved in and the prison is now "under control," according to the secretariat; the injured were taken for medical care and prison visits have been suspended while social-work staff contact victims' families. Officials have not said whether the violence was linked to the warring Sinaloa cartel factions that make the state one of six the US State Department advises Americans not to visit, and no escape was reported.
