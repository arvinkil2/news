Oracle has scuttled a $7 billion chip leasing contract with Tencent after US officials raised concerns with Oracle about the deal, the Financial Times reported on Wednesday. The five-year lease, signed earlier this year, would have given Tencent access to as many as 100,000 advanced artificial intelligence chips in data centres across Southeast Asia.

Tencent was also scrutinised by Beijing over the deal and asked to seek domestic chips, the FT reported. ByteDance and Alibaba still have chip leasing deals in place, and Oracle is a major supplier to ByteDance in Asia, with several data centres in Malaysia.
