**Latest:** The Crimea bridge was hit overnight, and traffic on it will be limited until about noon, Serghei Aksyonov, the Russian-installed governor of Crimea, said on Telegram, according to ANSA. Aksyonov said emergency services had been deployed and an emergency response centre set up in Kerch; the report gives no weapon used and no extent of damage to the bridge. Russia's defence ministry said air defences intercepted and shot down 1,336 fixed-wing Ukrainian drones overnight over regions including Moscow, Crimea and the waters of the Sea of Azov and the Black Sea, a total that would be the largest single-night count reported in the war. Ukraine's General Staff said Russia launched at least 158 drones at Ukraine over the same night, including 77 jet-propelled Shahed drones, of which Ukrainian air defence neutralised 131, with impacts reported in 19 locations.

Russian authorities reported a sustained drone attack on Moscow from Sunday evening into Monday, November 16. Ukrainska Pravda, summarising Mayor Sergei Sobyanin's posts, said 82 drones flying toward the capital had been reported downed from Sunday evening, with a further 42 announced later, and RIA Novosti reported another batch of 10 destroyed late on Sunday night; the batches appear to overlap and neither report stated a single cumulative total. Sobyanin said emergency services were working where debris fell, no casualties or damage were reported in either account, and Yaroslavl region governor Mikhail Yevraev said the highway exit from Yaroslavl toward Moscow was closed while the attack was repelled.
