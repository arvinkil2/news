Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi met Russian President Vladimir Putin for about 30 minutes immediately after the APEC leaders' dinner in Shenzhen on Wednesday, according to Japan's foreign ministry, the first face-to-face contact between the two countries' leaders since Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Takaichi conveyed Japan's position on Russia's detention of Hokkaido Shimbun journalists and on Moscow's de facto seizure of management control over Japan Tobacco's local subsidiary, and the two leaders agreed to continue communication at various levels.

The contact was a brief sideline exchange rather than a formal summit, and it came a day after aircraft believed to be Russian entered Japanese airspace near Fukuoka and hours ahead of Thursday's US-China-Russia trilateral meeting, from which Japan is excluded. Tokyo has said its sanctions on Russia over the Ukraine war remain unchanged.
