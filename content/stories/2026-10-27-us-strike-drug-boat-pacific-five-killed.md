The US military said on Tuesday that it attacked a boat it accused of smuggling drugs in the eastern Pacific Ocean, killing five people. US Southern Command said the vessel was travelling along a known smuggling route and that intelligence had revealed its active involvement in narcotrafficking, and the military posted a video on X showing a small boat before an explosion engulfed it; it provided no evidence that the vessel was carrying drugs.

The attack brings the reported toll from the administration’s boat strike campaign to at least 240 people killed in more than 70 strikes since it began targeting people it calls narcoterrorists in September 2025. President Trump has said the United States is in armed conflict with cartels in Latin America, a rationale critics in Congress and among military legal scholars continue to question.
