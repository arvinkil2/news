Reliance Industries said in a stock exchange filing on Wednesday that its digital arm Jio Platforms had set a price band of 1,065 to 1,119 rupees a share for an initial public offering of up to 270 million new shares, seeking as much as 3.2 billion dollars and valuing the company at about 10.3 trillion rupees, roughly 108.3 billion dollars, at the top of the band. The offer would overtake Hyundai Motor India's 2024 listing as India's biggest, and Jio plans to use up to 275 billion rupees of the proceeds to repay debt at its telecom subsidiary Reliance Jio Infocomm, with the rest for general corporate purposes. Jio is India's largest telecom operator, with more than 524 million subscribers at the end of March, and reported June quarter revenue up nearly 12 percent year on year to 391.7 billion rupees, with net profit up 9 percent to 77.6 billion rupees, according to its prospectus.
