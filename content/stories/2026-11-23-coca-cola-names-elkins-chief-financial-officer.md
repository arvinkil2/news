The Coca-Cola Company on Monday announced that David V. Elkins will succeed John Murphy as executive vice president and chief financial officer, effective April 2, 2027. Murphy will continue as president of the company and intends to retire at a later date in 2027, the company said in a Form 8-K filing.

Elkins, 58, has been Bristol Myers Squibb's chief financial officer since 2019, joining through its acquisition of Celgene, where he held the same job, after senior finance roles at Johnson & Johnson, Becton Dickinson and AstraZeneca. Coca-Cola set his base salary at $1,180,000 with a target annual incentive of 135% of salary, plus one-time awards of about $5,400,000 in restricted stock units and $5,400,000 in deferred cash to replace compensation he forfeits at Bristol Myers Squibb.
