Salesforce said on Tuesday it has entered a definitive agreement to acquire Braze, the customer engagement platform, for approximately 5.66 billion dollars in enterprise value, with holders of Braze Class A common stock to receive 47.50 dollars in cash per share. The transaction is expected to close in Salesforce's fiscal 2028, subject to approval by Braze shareholders and required regulatory approvals, and Salesforce said there is no anticipated change to its fiscal 2027 guidance. Patrick Stokes, Salesforce's president for applications and marketing, said Braze turns live customer behaviour into personalised engagement at scale and will be combined with Marketing Cloud, CRM and Data 360, while Braze chief executive Bill Magnuson said the company will keep building as Braze; Salesforce shares fell about 3 percent after the announcement, Investing.com reported.
