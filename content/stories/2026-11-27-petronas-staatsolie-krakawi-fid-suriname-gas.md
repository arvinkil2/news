Petronas, through its subsidiary Petronas Suriname E&P, and Staatsolie have taken the final investment decision to develop the Sloanea gas field in Block 52 offshore Suriname, Staatsolie said. The project, named KrakaWi, is expected to require an investment of $1.6 billion and to produce first gas in mid-2030, making it Suriname's first offshore gas development.

The development comprises four offshore production wells, subsea infrastructure and a floating liquefied natural gas facility with capacity of approximately 1.5 million tonnes of LNG per year. The field holds an estimated 97 billion cubic metres of recoverable gas, and the project is expected to run for 25 years. Petronas leads the partnership with an 80 percent participating interest; Staatsolie holds 20 percent and will invest in proportion to its interest, a share Splash247 put at about $320 million, to be financed through an existing loan facility.
