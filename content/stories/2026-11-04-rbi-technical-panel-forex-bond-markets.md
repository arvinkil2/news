The Reserve Bank of India said on Wednesday it has constituted a Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets, headed by Rohit Jain, the deputy governor in charge of the Financial Markets Regulation Department, and meeting at least once a quarter. Its terms of reference cover the functioning, efficiency, liquidity and resilience of the money, government securities, foreign exchange and related derivative markets, measures to deepen and broaden those markets, and the trading, clearing, settlement and reporting infrastructure underneath them, including the identification of unintended frictions from RBI measures already in force.

The seven external members named in reports of the statement are Ananth Narayan, a former whole time member of the Securities and Exchange Board of India, Shamsher Singh of the State Bank of India, Neeraj Gambhir of Axis Bank, Shailendra Jhingran of ICICI Bank, Aditya Bagree of Citibank India, Nand Kishore of the Clearing Corporation of India, and Ridham Desai of Morgan Stanley India. The committee follows an October policy announcement and lands in the same weeks in which RBI measures have raised the cost of corporate dollar hedging, with forward purchases down 35 percent in October to their lowest since February, as reported earlier today.
