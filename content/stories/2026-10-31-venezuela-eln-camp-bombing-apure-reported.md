Venezuela's armed forces bombed a camp of Colombia's ELN guerrilla group in Apure state on Friday, according to Juan Francisco García Escalona, the Apure coordinator of the human rights organisation Fundaredes, who said 19 people were killed. The attack took place at Buena Vista del Meta, in Pedro Camejo municipality near the Colombian border; nine bodies were taken to San Fernando de Apure for forensic identification, ten remained at the site, and an unspecified number of wounded crossed into Colombia, García said, citing residents of Puerto Páez.

The defence analyst Rocío San Miguel relayed the same local reports and said an official statement was still awaited. No Venezuelan authority had confirmed the operation or the death toll in the reporting, and the figures rest on the Fundaredes account alone.
