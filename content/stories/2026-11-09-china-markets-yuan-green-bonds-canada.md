China's Ministry of Finance began selling yuan-denominated green sovereign bonds in Canada on Monday, the first time it has marketed such notes to investors in the country, Bloomberg reported. Initial price guidance was around 1.8 percent for the three-year notes and around 1.9 percent for the five-year notes, according to people familiar with the matter, and the notes may be priced later on Monday. Royal Bank of Canada is a joint bookrunner for the sale, Bloomberg said. No issue size was disclosed in the portion of the report that could be read.
