Pfizer said on Wednesday that Blackstone will provide up to 1.5 billion dollars through 2028 to help fund development of berobenatide, an experimental monthly injectable obesity treatment it picked up last year in its acquisition of Metsera, a deal worth up to 10 billion dollars. Pfizer said it will keep full control over the development programme and that the funding gives it more flexibility to invest across its wider pipeline.

Blackstone will be eligible for milestone based payments but will receive no rights or ownership in the medication. The payments depend on United States regulatory approval of monthly dosing of berobenatide as a monotherapy for chronic weight management and on the drug reaching certain commercial sales milestones, and their size was not disclosed.
