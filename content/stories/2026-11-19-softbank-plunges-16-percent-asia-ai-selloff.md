SoftBank Group shares plunged 16.16 percent in Tokyo on Friday, CNBC reported, leading a broad selloff in Asian technology stocks a day after Anthropic's debut on the Nasdaq. Japan's Nikkei Stock Average fell 4.7 percent, with memory chipmaker Kioxia Holdings down 12.6 percent and chip-testing equipment maker Advantest down 12.3 percent, Dow Jones reported, while Japanese semiconductor names Tokyo Electron, Lasertec and Renesas Electronics fell 7.19 percent, 6.75 percent and 6.71 percent respectively, according to CNBC. South Korea's Kospi also fell 4.7 percent, with SK Hynix down 6.3 percent and Samsung Electronics down 4.8 percent, and Australia's S&P/ASX 200 was down 0.9 percent.

The selling followed Anthropic's first day of trading in New York, in which the Claude maker raised about $81 billion in the largest IPO on record and closed 17 percent above its $83 offer price at $97.17, as covered on this site. Commerzbank Research said the listing appeared to trigger rotation out of existing AI-related names as investors redeployed capital into the new issue, and OCBC strategists said investors continued to unwind crowded positions as the record IPO sharpened scrutiny of stretched AI valuations and financing risks. Short-dated Asian and US government bonds rose as the equity selloff bolstered safe-haven demand, Dow Jones reported.
