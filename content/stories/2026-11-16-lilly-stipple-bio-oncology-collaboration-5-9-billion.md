Eli Lilly has entered a strategic research collaboration and licence agreement with Stipple Bio, a Cambridge, Massachusetts startup, to develop new cancer treatments across multiple targets and modalities, the companies said on Monday. Stipple will use its Pointillist Platform to discover tumour-specific cell surface epitope binders, which Lilly will combine with its own oncology technologies, and Lilly gains exclusive worldwide development and commercialisation rights.

Stipple receives an upfront payment and an equity investment, and could receive up to $5.9 billion in aggregate milestone payments plus tiered royalties on sales, making this, on Endpoints News' count, Lilly's second-largest collaboration this year by total deal value. The agreement is Lilly's second oncology deal announced on Monday, after its agreement to acquire Calytrix Bio for up to $1.5 billion, covered separately in this edition.
