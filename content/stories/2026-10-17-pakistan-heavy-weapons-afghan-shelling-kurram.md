Pakistan's army gave what state broadcaster PTV called an effective response with heavy weaponry to unprovoked shelling by the Afghan Taliban in the Kurram sector, destroying several Afghan armed forces fortifications, TASS reported on Saturday, citing PTV's post on X; no casualties from the exchange were reported in the account. The exchange follows earlier clashes in the Dand-Patan district of Afghanistan's Paktia province, where the Afghan channel TOLO News, citing a security source, reported seven Pakistani service members killed and six wounded. Neither side's figures had been independently confirmed in the reporting checked.
