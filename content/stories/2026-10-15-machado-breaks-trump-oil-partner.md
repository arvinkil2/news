Exiled Venezuelan opposition leader Maria Corina Machado has publicly broken with the Trump administration over Alejandro Betancourt, the businessman who recently entered a deal with Washington to control roughly a fifth of Venezuela's oil reserves. In her first public comments on him, Machado said Betancourt was responsible for serious corruption and would not be a viable business partner if she took power. Washington passed Machado over in January in favour of Delcy Rodriguez to lead the country, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio has said the ultimate goal remains a democratic transition.
