Initial claims for state unemployment benefits rose 9,000 to a seasonally adjusted 200,000 in the week ended November 7, the Labor Department said on Thursday, above the 195,000 economists polled by Reuters had forecast, with the four-week moving average unchanged at 195,250. Continuing claims, a proxy for hiring, fell 20,000 to 1.697 million in the week ended October 31, and the insured unemployment rate held at 1.1 percent. Claims have stayed at or below 200,000 since mid-September and remain close to the 189,000 touched in mid-July, the lowest since 1969, Reuters reported.
