Russian forces attacked Kramatorsk three times on Tuesday, killing at least four people and wounding six, Donetsk regional governor Vadym Filashkin said, with one strike killing three employees of the city's heating utility as they checked the network's readiness for the heating season and a fourth person killed by an FPV drone strike. The Donetsk regional prosecutor's office said the morning attack wounded a woman and a 38-year-old man with shrapnel and blast injuries, and Filashkin said 15 apartment buildings, two educational facilities and other buildings were damaged across the city.
