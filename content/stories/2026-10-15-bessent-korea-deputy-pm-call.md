US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has held an introductory call with South Korea's deputy prime minister, the Treasury said in a readout, a first formal contact that comes with the won among the world's weakest currencies this year and Seoul deep in the Bangkok meetings.

Introductory calls of this kind are diplomatic plumbing, but the pairing matters: Korea's exporters sit at the centre of the semiconductor and shipbuilding trades Washington is trying to reshape, and its central bank governor is in Bangkok this week publicly concerned about capital flows and the exchange rate. Currency language in any readout from this channel is read tick by tick in Seoul.

The call also fills in the picture of Bessent's week. Absent from the G20 session itself, where deputies led the US delegation, he has worked the bilateral track and issued the US statement to the World Bank Development Committee.
