Reinova Partners, the London-based energy transition investment firm founded by former Brookfield managing partners Ralf Rank and John Stinebaugh, has reached first close on its debut fund with commitments of about 400 million. With Intelligence reported the figure in euros and AltAssets in dollars; both agree on the scale, the fund and the team. Reinova Energy Transition Fund I targets 800 million euros with a 1 billion euro hard cap, on a mid-market value-add strategy across clean electricity, clean fuels and industrial decarbonisation. A further closing is expected before the end of the year, with final close in early 2027.

The firm has already begun deploying: it has just completed the acquisition of Vattenfall's UK district heating business, giving the debut fund an operating anchor asset in British heat networks.

Reinova is part of a wider pattern this year in which senior teams leaving the largest infrastructure managers raise first-time funds quickly, on the strength of track records built inside the big platforms. Whether those funds can source mid-market transition assets without bidding against their former employers is the open question for the strategy.
