Procter & Gamble said on Thursday that higher raw material, energy and freight costs will cut fiscal 2027 profit by about 1.4 billion dollars, up from its previous estimate of 1 billion dollars, with finance chief Andre Schulten warning that commodity pressure will increase over the next two quarters. The warning came alongside a first-quarter beat: net sales rose 4 percent to 23.28 billion dollars against estimates of 22.80 billion dollars, core earnings were 2.00 dollars a share against 1.88 dollars expected, and volumes rose 2.4 percent as beauty and home-care demand held up.

The company kept its annual guidance unchanged, at organic sales growth of 1 to 3 percent and core earnings of roughly 7 dollars a share, and its shares rose about 5 percent in early trading.
