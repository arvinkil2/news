Kim Jong Un said North Korea should focus on deploying tactical nuclear weapons across multiple domains for different missions, describing diversified tactical use as an important and urgent task, state media KCNA reported via Al Jazeera and Reuters. Speaking during an inspection of nuclear material production and weapons research facilities, which KCNA did not identify, Kim said nuclear material production capacity was being systematically expanded and that any attempt to deny the country's nuclear status, enshrined in its constitution in 2023, would be regarded as an open declaration of war.

The remarks come amid a border dispute over a September 21 landmine explosion in the Demilitarized Zone that injured three South Korean soldiers; Seoul demanded an apology after a joint South Korea-UN investigation found North Korea had planted the mines, while Pyongyang accused Seoul of fabricating the incident. South Korea estimates North Korea holds between 80 and 120 nuclear warheads.
