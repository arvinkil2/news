SK hynix said on Thursday that no decision has been made on a new United States NAND flash plant for its Solidigm unit, in a Form 6-K filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. The filing responds to a Maeil Business Newspaper report that Solidigm is pursuing an investment of about 60 trillion won to build a new NAND production facility in the United States.

The company said its overseas subsidiary under the Solidigm brand is reviewing various measures to strengthen its competitiveness and expand its production capacity, but that no matters have been determined. It said it will make a further disclosure when specific details are confirmed or within one month of the filing.
