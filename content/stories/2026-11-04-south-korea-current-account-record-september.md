South Korea's current account surplus was 61.46 billion dollars in September, a record and the first monthly reading above 60 billion dollars, the Bank of Korea's provisional balance of payments statistics showed. The goods surplus was also a record, at 62.75 billion dollars, as exports rose 92.4 percent from a year earlier to 130.72 billion dollars, with semiconductor exports up 260.3 percent and computer peripheral solid state drive exports up 392.3 percent on a customs basis.

The cumulative surplus for January to September reached 340.66 billion dollars, more than four times the same period a year earlier, and the central bank expects the annual surplus to exceed the 450 billion dollar forecast in its August outlook. Foreign investors were net sellers of Korean stocks in the month, with foreign securities investment in Korea falling 13.81 billion dollars, even as Korean investors' overseas securities investment, at 16.63 billion dollars, was the second largest on record.
