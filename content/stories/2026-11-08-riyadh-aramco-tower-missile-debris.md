Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Energy said on Sunday that an administrative building belonging to Saudi Aramco in Riyadh was subjected to an attempted attack, and that falling debris caused minor damage to the building, with no injuries or deaths reported. The Saudi-led coalition said it had intercepted a missile fired by Yemen's Houthi rebels targeting Riyadh.

Yonhap, reporting Reuters and AFP, said witnesses and sources reported a high-rise in the King Abdullah Financial District was targeted and the Aramco Tower in the district was damaged, and one witness told AFP it was certain the tower had been targeted. The reporting does not establish whether the building was hit directly or damaged by debris from the intercepted missile, and Saudi authorities gave no figure for the scale of the damage.

The attack came hours after Saudi Arabia's Education Ministry, late on Saturday, announced the resumption of in-person classes in Riyadh after weeks of remote schooling imposed because of earlier strikes.
