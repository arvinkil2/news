Carles Puigdemont, the former Catalan regional leader, said at a televised news conference in Brussels on Wednesday that he will return to Spain on December 12, ending nearly a decade of self-imposed exile after the Supreme Court this week applied the 2024 amnesty law and shelved the criminal case against him. "I will return to my country, to Catalonia, without having to ask their permission or forgiveness, and without having renounced anything," he said. He added that he will unveil his proposal for a renewed Catalan independence push on December 17, after this weekend's national election, in time to take part in any post-ballot alliance negotiations.
