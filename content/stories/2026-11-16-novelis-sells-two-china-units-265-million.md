Novelis has signed a definitive agreement to sell Novelis (China) Aluminum Products and Novelis (Shanghai) Aluminum Trading to China's Shenzhen Hongzhi Industrial Investment or its affiliates for $265 million, according to a Hindalco exchange filing reported by Reuters. The price includes a potential earn-out of up to $10 million tied to specified conditions, and the sale is expected to close within 12 months, subject to regulatory approvals.

The report gave no stated reason for the sale. It noted the deal comes days after the announcement of Novelis chief financial officer Dev Ahuja's departure, and months after Hindalco reported a profit decline it partly attributed to costs from a fire-related disruption at a United States unit.
