Naver has closed the funding for its AI factory buildout. Datacenter Dynamics reported that the South Korean cloud provider has signed a $9 billion agreement with Brookfield, under which Brookfield will set up a special purpose vehicle, tentatively named Asset Co., to invest up to $9 billion acquiring AI infrastructure such as GPUs and data centers and then supply the computing power to Naver Cloud. Naver said the closing meets the precondition for Nvidia's previously announced $1 billion strategic equity investment.

The first 55 megawatts under the agreement is set to go live in the first half of 2027, scaling to 200 megawatts in 2028, centered on Naver's GAK Sejong data center with overseas expansion in phases. The three companies describe the programme as a 1 gigawatt scale global AI factory business now entering full-scale execution.
