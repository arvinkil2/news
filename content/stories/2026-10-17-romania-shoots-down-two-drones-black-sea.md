Romania said on Saturday that two drones were engaged and shot down by Romanian military pilots over the Black Sea, moments after they had illegally entered the country's airspace, defence minister Radu Miruta wrote on Facebook. Arab News, reporting AFP, described the incident as the latest in a recent spate of such events believed to be spillover from Russia's invasion of Ukraine. The report did not identify the drones' origin, type or any damage on the ground.
