The Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) announced on Monday that negotiated prices for 13 Medicare Part B and Part D drugs, covering cancer, psoriatic arthritis and other serious chronic conditions, will produce an estimated net saving of 45 percent, or $9.2 billion, when the maximum fair prices take effect on January 1, 2028. The 13 drugs accounted for about $22.3 billion in gross Part D drug costs in 2025, roughly 6 percent of the total, and were used by about 1.4 million Part D patients, while the four Part B drugs among them accounted for about $3.3 billion and were used by about 120,000 patients. Reuters reported that the individual cuts range from 34 to 84 percent, with Eli Lilly's diabetes drug Trulicity cut by 84 percent and Gilead's HIV treatment Biktarvy by 37 percent.
