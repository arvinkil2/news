The Justice Department is seeking a criminal indictment of former White House aide Cassidy Hutchinson for lying to Congress, according to a person familiar with the matter, with the department's civil rights division pursuing the indictment. A DOJ spokesperson said the department does not confirm or deny investigations.

Hutchinson, then an assistant to White House Chief of Staff Mark Meadows, testified before Congress in June 2022, giving a closely watched account of Trump's conduct in the final weeks of his first term. The move follows a March 2026 criminal referral from Representative Barry Loudermilk, who accused her of false testimony, and a DOJ investigation opened in April. In September, a federal judge quashed a BBC subpoena seeking Hutchinson's deposition, referring to her as "presently the subject of a criminal investigation."
