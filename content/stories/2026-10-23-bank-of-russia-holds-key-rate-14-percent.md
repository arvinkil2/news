The Bank of Russia kept its key rate at 14 percent at its October 23 board meeting, a decision Interfax described as expected after the bank paused in September following ten consecutive cuts. In its updated medium-term forecast, the bank raised its 2026 inflation forecast to 7.1 to 7.6 percent, narrowed its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 0.5 to 1.0 percent, lifted its 2026 Russian oil price assumption from $60 to $72 a barrel, and raised its average key rate forecasts for 2027 and 2028. The bank linked the main pro-inflationary risks to an imbalance between supply and demand, and the ruble was little changed against the yuan after the decision.
