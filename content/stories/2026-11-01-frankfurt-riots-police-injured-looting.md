Demonstrations across more than 30 German cities on Saturday turned to rioting in the evening, and Frankfurt am Main saw the worst of it, with more than 100 police officers injured, Roman Poseck, the interior minister of the state of Hesse, told a press conference, as reported by dpa. Police detained 54 people in Frankfurt, aged 13 to 29, and rioters damaged 17 shops, looting five of them. In Berlin, 27 officers were injured after pyrotechnics, bottles and eggs were thrown at police during a march, barricades were set on fire in Hamburg, and police used water cannon in Cologne.
