A Learjet 60 business jet crashed in a residential area near Cresthaven, in the Greenacres area of Palm Beach County, Florida, at about 12:50 p.m. local time on Sunday, erupting in a fireball beside a major roadway and leaving a crater in the pavement just short of homes. The aircraft had departed Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was bound for North Eleuthera Airport in the Bahamas, the Federal Aviation Administration said. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy confirmed that all nine people on board were killed; whether anyone on the ground was hurt was not yet known, and the National Transportation Safety Board will lead the investigation into the cause.
