Commerzbank is reviewing options to scale back its US Treasury trading operations as new US rules requiring Treasury securities and Treasury-backed repo to be cleared through central counterparties threaten to raise costs, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. The alternatives under consideration include withdrawing from the US repo market and using a broker-dealer to handle its cash Treasury trading; the review is ongoing and no final decision has been reached. Commerzbank acts as a market maker in Treasuries rather than a primary dealer, and its US subsidiary Commerz Markets LLC reported $16.2 billion in gross repo obligations at the end of 2025, about 97 percent of them in US government obligations. Cash Treasury trades must meet the clearing requirement by the end of 2026 and repo trades by mid-2027.
