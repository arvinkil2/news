The Children's Place and certain subsidiaries agreed on November 22 to sell their intellectual property to Mithaq Capital SPC for a base purchase price of $31.0 million in cash, subject to adjustment, plus applicable contract cure costs, according to the company's Form 8-K. The assets include the brand names The Children's Place, Gymboree, Sugar & Jade, PJ Place and Crazy 8, related websites, domains and social media assets, specified customer data and operating information, and TCP Brands' equity interests in Gym-IPCO, LLC. Mithaq will make a $3.1 million earnest money escrow deposit, creditable against the price at closing.

Mithaq is designated stalking horse bidder in the company's chapter 11 cases in the US Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Jersey, so the sale remains subject to higher or better bids, an auction if one is held, bankruptcy court approval and a Canadian recognition order from the Ontario Superior Court of Justice. The sellers keep a royalty-free licence to use the brands for liquidation and wind-down activities through April 30, 2027, and must cease using the names, and change their own names to exclude them, by that date.
