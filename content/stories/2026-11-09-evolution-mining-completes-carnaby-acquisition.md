Evolution Mining has completed its $213 million acquisition of Carnaby Resources under a court-approved scheme of arrangement implemented on November 10, with Carnaby shareholders receiving 0.0682 new Evolution shares for each share held and the new shares expected to trade on the ASX from November 11, when Carnaby is delisted. The deal, first announced in July, brings the Greater Duchess copper-gold project in north-west Queensland into Evolution's Ernest Henry operations near Cloncurry, where the company estimates the project could contribute about 10,000 tonnes of additional copper a year. Greater Duchess carries a mineral resource of 30.8 million tonnes grading 1.3 percent copper and 0.3 grams per tonne gold, and Evolution plans an updated feasibility study on integrating it into Ernest Henry's mining and processing operations.
