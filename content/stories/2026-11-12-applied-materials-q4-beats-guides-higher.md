Applied Materials reported fiscal fourth-quarter revenue of $10.41 billion, up about 53 percent year over year, and adjusted earnings per share of $4.16, up 92 percent, against consensus of $10.3 billion and $4.06. Semiconductor Systems revenue was $7.996 billion, Applied Global Services $1.896 billion and other revenue $517 million; adjusted gross margin was 50.6 percent, adjusted operating income a record $3.68 billion and free cash flow $2.62 billion. Full-year revenue rose 21 percent to $34.45 billion, with adjusted earnings per share up 37 percent to $12.89.

For the first quarter of fiscal 2027 the company guided revenue to $11.1 billion, plus or minus $500 million, against estimates of $10.84 billion, and adjusted earnings per share to $4.35, plus or minus 20 cents, against expectations of $4.25. The shares gained in after-hours trading.
