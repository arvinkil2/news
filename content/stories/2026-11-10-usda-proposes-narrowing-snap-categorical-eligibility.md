The US Department of Agriculture published a proposed rule on Tuesday that would limit categorical eligibility for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program to households that receive Temporary Assistance for Needy Families assistance as defined in federal regulation, ending the broader route under which states have used non-cash TANF-funded benefits and services to qualify households automatically. The department estimates 6.3 million people in 3.2 million households, about 18 percent of participants, would lose benefits by fiscal 2029, with federal benefit spending falling by about $44.6 billion over fiscal 2027 to 2031 and about 1.78 million children losing automatic eligibility for free school meals through their SNAP enrollment. Comments on the proposal, docket FNA-2026-0661, are due December 10, and no household's eligibility changes unless the rule is finalised.
