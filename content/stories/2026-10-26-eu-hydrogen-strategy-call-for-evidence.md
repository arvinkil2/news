The European Commission on Monday launched a four-week call for evidence and a four-week targeted stakeholder consultation to gather input for a forthcoming Hydrogen Strategy, inviting stakeholders, citizens and organisations to respond by November 23, 2026. The Commission said the new strategy will seek to support the development of a resilient and competitive European hydrogen value chain while providing stability and predictability for investors. It will be developed in parallel with the renewable energy framework for the period to 2040, to ensure renewables contribute to the EU's 2040 climate target while addressing geopolitical pressure to reduce dependence on imported fossil fuels.
