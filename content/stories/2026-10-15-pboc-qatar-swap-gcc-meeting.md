China's central bank governor Pan Gongsheng met his Gulf Cooperation Council counterparts in Bangkok on October 14 for their fourth governors meeting, where the two sides discussed investment flows, cross-border payments and deeper cooperation between their central banks, the People's Bank of China said. On the sidelines Pan met the central bank governors of the United Arab Emirates, Qatar and Oman, and with Qatar's governor renewed the two countries' bilateral local currency swap agreement. The PBOC statement said both sides will keep advancing local currency cooperation, payment links and financial market connectivity, without disclosing the size of the renewed Qatar line.
