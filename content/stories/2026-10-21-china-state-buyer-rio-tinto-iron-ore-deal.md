China Mineral Resources Group, the state buyer Beijing created in 2022 to centralise iron ore purchasing, has agreed a new annual supply contract with Rio Tinto and verbally notified some steel mills of the deal on Wednesday, Reuters reported. The group plans to start collecting each mill's required volumes on Thursday, steering orders through one negotiating book rather than dozens of mill-by-mill talks.

The agreement follows the pressure campaign Reuters reported in early August, when the state buyer directed some mills to halt their own negotiations with Rio Tinto over shipments from September while annual contract talks were under way. Contract terms, including total volume, were not disclosed, and mills have been squeezed by weak domestic steel demand and thin margins.
