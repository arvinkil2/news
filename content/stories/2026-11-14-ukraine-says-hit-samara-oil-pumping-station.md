Ukraine said it struck a major crude pipeline hub in Russia's Samara region on Friday, about 860 kilometres southeast of Moscow. The attack on the Samara pumping and dispatch station near the settlement of Prosvet set several storage tanks on fire, Ukraine's General Staff said in a post on X, as reported by Bloomberg.

The report gave no casualty count, no count of tanks beyond several, and no figure for oil volumes affected. Russia had not commented on the incident as of Saturday morning.
