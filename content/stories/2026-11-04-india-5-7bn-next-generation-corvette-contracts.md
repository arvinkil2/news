India's Ministry of Defence signed contracts worth 53,866.50 crore rupees on November 2, 2026, awarding five Next Generation Corvettes to Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers and three to Goa Shipyard Limited. The ministry said the project is the first in which the Indian Navy will design the ships itself and state owned shipyards, selected through a competitive process, will build them. The corvettes will carry advanced missiles, weapons and sensor suites for air, surface and sub-surface missions, and the ministry expects the work to generate about 72 lakh person-days of employment in the shipyards and 460 lakh person-days in ancillary industries over the next ten years.
