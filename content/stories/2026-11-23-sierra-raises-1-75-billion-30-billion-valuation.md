Sierra said on Monday it is raising $1.75 billion in a Series F round at a $30 billion valuation, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with all of its previous lead investors, Benchmark, Sequoia, GV and Tiger Global, investing again. The valuation is double the mark of more than $15 billion set by its $950 million round in May, six months earlier.

The San Francisco company, co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, sells AI agents that handle customer service for large enterprises and says it has passed $400 million in annual recurring revenue in just over two and a half years.
