Brazil's IPCA inflation rate slowed to 0.14 percent in October 2026 from 0.82 percent in September, the national statistics institute IBGE reported on Thursday. Prices rose 4.10 percent in the first ten months of the year, and the 12-month rate edged up to 4.63 percent from 4.58 percent in the 12 months to September.

Food and beverages posted the largest increase of any group, rising 1.04 percent and contributing 0.22 percentage points, as food at home accelerated to 1.31 percent on a 19.57 percent jump in tomato prices and an 11.29 percent rise in onions. Transport fell 0.59 percent, as airfares dropped 9.21 percent after rising 9.66 percent in September, and housing fell 0.51 percent, as residential electricity prices fell 1.92 percent with the move to the green tariff flag, which carries no surcharge on bills.
