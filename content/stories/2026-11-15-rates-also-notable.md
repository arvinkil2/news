Saudi farm output rose across the board in 2025, the General Authority for Statistics reported on November 15. Table egg production increased 8.2 percent year on year to more than 9 billion eggs, broiler chicken production rose 4.8 percent to 1.3 million tonnes, and raw milk production rose 1.6 percent to about 2.8 billion litres. Sheep numbers exceeded 22 million head, goats were about 7.3 million and camels about 2.2 million, and lending to livestock projects was about 2.6 billion Saudi riyals. The release is a food supply indicator rather than a price release, and it is carried here as context for the kingdom's contained food inflation, which ran at 1.4 percent year on year in September.

Bank of Canada Deputy Governor Toni Gravelle speaks on Wednesday, November 18, in a scheduled address listed by the central bank. The speech falls three weeks before the Bank of Canada's December 9 rate decision and a day after Canadian factory data showed flat nominal sales over falling volumes in September. Speeches do not set policy, but Gravelle's remarks will be read for any signal on whether the bank shares the Federal Reserve's tightening bias or the domestic data's softer message.

The week's central bank paper trail is otherwise minutes. The Federal Reserve publishes the minutes of its October 27 to 28 meeting on Wednesday, November 18, and Banxico publishes the minutes of its November 5 meeting, at which it cut its policy rate by 25 basis points to 6.25 percent, on Thursday, November 19. Both documents will be read against record short positioning in the peso and market pricing that treats a December Federal Reserve increase as certain.
