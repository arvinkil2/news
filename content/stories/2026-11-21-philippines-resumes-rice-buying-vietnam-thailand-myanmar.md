Philippine importers have resumed buying 15 to 25 percent broken rice from Vietnam, Thailand and Myanmar, after a July suspension of 5 percent broken white rice imports and a September halt to sanitary and phytosanitary clearances for shipments from the three origins. The resumed buying has held Asian export prices firm: Vietnamese fragrant 5 percent broken rice was assessed at 559 dollars a tonne free on board on November 17, up 3 dollars on the day, against 472 dollars for Thai 25 percent broken white rice and 458 dollars for Myanmar 25 percent broken. Vietnamese supply is limited through February, and some Philippine buyers are already shifting part of their demand to India and Pakistan, whose 25 percent broken grades were assessed near 389 dollars a tonne.
