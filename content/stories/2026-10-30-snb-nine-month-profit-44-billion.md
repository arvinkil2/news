The Swiss National Bank reported a profit of 44.2 billion francs for the first three quarters of 2026, before the allocation to its provisions for currency reserves. The profit on foreign currency positions was 43.3 billion francs, made up of exchange rate gains of 29.8 billion francs, price gains of 23.7 billion francs on equity securities and interest and dividend income, partly offset by price losses of 22.6 billion francs on interest bearing paper. Gold holdings, unchanged in volume, added a valuation gain of 1.2 billion francs as gold traded at 112,118 francs per kilogram at the end of September, and Swiss franc positions contributed 0.1 billion francs. The bank cautioned that its result depends largely on gold, foreign exchange and capital market developments, that strong fluctuations are to be expected, and that only provisional conclusions are possible for the annual result.
