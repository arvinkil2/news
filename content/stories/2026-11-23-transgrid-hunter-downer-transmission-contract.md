The New South Wales government, through the Energy Corporation of NSW (EnergyCo), has awarded delivery of the Hunter Transmission Project, appointing Transgrid as network operator for the 110 kilometre, 500kV line between Bayswater and Olney. Downer has been appointed to build the transmission line and UGL to deliver the switching stations and substation upgrades.

Downer said its transmission line works are valued at approximately A$1.1 billion over a three year construction period, covering about 110 kilometres of double circuit 500kV line and associated steel towers between the Bayswater South and Olney switching stations. Planning, design and procurement start in December 2026, with on site mobilisation in 2027, subject to final approvals. The project received state planning approval in July 2026; Commonwealth approval remains pending, and construction is expected to begin in 2027 with commissioning targeted for late 2029.

The line connects the Central-West Orana and New England Renewable Energy Zones to the wider network, the route by which new wind, solar and storage capacity replaces retiring coal fired generation. Transgrid will manage delivery and then operate and maintain the infrastructure long term, while EnergyCo retains its planning and oversight role as infrastructure planner.

The project is expected to support an average of up to 210 direct jobs a year during construction and 140 ongoing operational jobs from 2030.
