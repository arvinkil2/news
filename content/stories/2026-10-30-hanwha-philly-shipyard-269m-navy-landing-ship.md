TOTE Services said on Friday it has selected Hanwha Defense USA as the first shipbuilder under its $2.2 billion Vessel Construction Manager contract for the Navy's Medium Landing Ship program, a $269 million award to build one vessel at Hanwha Philly Shipyard with options for three additional ships. Construction is expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2027, with the first vessel scheduled for delivery in 2030, and TOTE said Hanwha was chosen on competitive pricing and acceptance of subcontract terms after a bidding process launched in July. The landing ships are intended to move Marine Corps personnel, equipment and supplies between islands and coastal locations, particularly in the Indo-Pacific, under a planned fleet of 35 vessels of which TOTE's initial contract covers up to eight.
