Security firm Calif said on Tuesday that it had discovered a critical vulnerability in Java with the help of OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber model, one of several serious flaws the firm says it has found with AI models in recent months. Researcher Bruce Dang said the flaw will affect practically all large companies, and that he and colleagues have spent recent days coordinating a response with agencies in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the United Kingdom, alongside companies including Google and Microsoft. Java is used by public bodies and about 90 percent of Fortune 500 companies, including banks, hospitals, airlines and technology giants.
