Australian private new capital expenditure rose 12.2 percent in the September quarter of 2026, seasonally adjusted, the largest quarterly rise since the September quarter of 2011 during the mining investment boom, according to the Australian Bureau of Statistics. Capex was 18.6 percent higher than a year earlier, driven by businesses investing significantly in equipment for data centres, with new equipment and machinery investment up 23.1 percent to a record high.

Information media and telecommunications recorded the largest industry rise, up 86.7 percent to a record level, and its equipment spending rose 183.5 percent. Victoria, up 31.0 percent, and New South Wales, up 9.6 percent, accounted for the bulk of data centre investment, and businesses revised their expected capex for 2026-27 up by 11.8 percent from the previous estimate.
