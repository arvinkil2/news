South Korea's real gross domestic product grew 1.0 percent in the third quarter from the previous quarter, according to the Bank of Korea advance estimate reported by Yonhap, well above the 0.3 percent the central bank forecast in August and following growth of 0.6 percent in the second quarter. Growth from a year earlier was 3.4 percent, down from 3.7 percent in the second quarter.

Facility investment rose 4.1 percent, led by machinery including semiconductor manufacturing equipment, and government consumption rose 1.9 percent, the two together contributing 0.7 percentage points of the 1.0 percent growth. Exports rose 2.3 percent on semiconductors, machinery and electrical equipment, while private consumption fell 0.2 percent as spending on both goods and services declined. Real gross domestic income rose 18.8 percent from a year earlier, its fastest pace since the fourth quarter of 1977, which Yonhap attributed in part to improved terms of trade as semiconductor prices rose.
