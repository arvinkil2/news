A magnitude 6.7 earthquake struck central Peru at 00:09 UTC on Saturday (7:09 p.m. local time on Friday), with its epicentre 9 kilometres northeast of Carhuamayo in the Junin region, the US Geological Survey said. The quake occurred at a depth of 108 kilometres, an intermediate depth that typically spreads shaking over a wide area while reducing its intensity at the surface, and the USGS solution was marked reviewed.

The USGS PAGER system issued a green alert, indicating a low likelihood of casualties and damage, and the first data carried felt reports with light reported intensity. No casualties or damage had been established in the initial reports.
