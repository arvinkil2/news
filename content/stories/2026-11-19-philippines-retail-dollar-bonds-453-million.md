The Philippines sold $453.4 million of a new three-year retail dollar bond on Thursday at a coupon of 5.65 percent, Metrobank reported, more than double the $200 million minimum the Bureau of the Treasury had set for its third retail dollar tranche. The bond, designated RDB 5.65 29, matures on November 27, 2029, its public offer runs from November 19 to November 24 or earlier, and it issues on November 27.

The sale comes as the peso rebounds from the record lows it set in October and after a run of weak peso bond auctions earlier this month, and Metrobank put the new coupon about 41 basis points above the older retail dollar bond maturing in 2029. For the full year the government aims to raise 2.733 trillion pesos, with 815.5 billion of it sourced externally, BusinessMirror reported.
