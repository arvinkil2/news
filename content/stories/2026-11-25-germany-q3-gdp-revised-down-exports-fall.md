Germany's gross domestic product rose 0.1 percent in the third quarter of 2026 from the previous quarter, the Federal Statistical Office (Destatis) said in its detailed release on November 25, revising the increase down by 0.1 percentage points from the first release of October 30 because September exports and retail turnover were weaker than initially assumed. GDP was 1.2 percent higher than a year earlier after price adjustment.

Exports fell 1.0 percent from the second quarter, with goods exports down 0.8 percent and services exports down 1.7 percent, while imports fell 1.4 percent. Gross fixed capital formation fell 1.2 percent, led by a 3.5 percent drop in machinery and equipment, while total final consumption expenditure rose 0.1 percent, with household consumption up 0.1 percent and government consumption up 0.3 percent.
