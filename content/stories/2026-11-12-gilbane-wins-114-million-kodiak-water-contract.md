The United States Coast Guard awarded a $114 million contract on October 29, 2026 to Gilbane Federal, a division of Gilbane Building Co., to modernize the water distribution system at Base Kodiak, Alaska. The contract covers all design, labour, material, equipment and site work, with completion scheduled for October 2031.

The scope replaces and upgrades more than 8 miles of main water distribution lines across the base and replaces four large scale water storage tanks. It also delivers water supply and pressure reliability for fire suppression systems protecting two operational aircraft hangars and key shore facilities, and potable water for the personnel and families living and working on the base, the Coast Guard's largest operational installation.

The contract is funded through the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. The Coast Guard says it has received nearly $25 billion under the act, the largest capital investment in its history, and has already ordered more than $20 billion in new assets, capabilities, depot maintenance and shore facilities. The Kodiak water work sits alongside a new fuel pier, cargo wharf upgrades, 30 additional family housing units and a child development centre at the same base.

The strategic logic is Arctic. Base Kodiak is the designated future home port for the service's first two Arctic Security Cutters, the core of its icebreaker fleet, and the shore buildout is intended to support an expanding polar fleet and long term mission readiness in Alaska and the Arctic approaches.
