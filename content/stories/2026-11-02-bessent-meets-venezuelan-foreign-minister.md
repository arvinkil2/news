Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent met Venezuelan Foreign Minister Oliver Blanco in Washington on Monday, congratulating him on his appointment, and the two discussed developments in Venezuela and opportunities to advance the administration's three-phase approach of stabilization, recovery, and transition, according to the Treasury readout.

Bessent underscored the administration's commitment to supporting Venezuela's economic recovery and revitalizing its critical sectors, including boosting energy production and reconnecting the Venezuelan financial sector, with the goals of restoring confidence, attracting investment, and laying the groundwork for a more prosperous Venezuela. The readout announced no sanctions change, agreement, amount or timeline.
