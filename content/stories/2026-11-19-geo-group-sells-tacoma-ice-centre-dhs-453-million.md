GEO Group subsidiaries CSC of Tacoma, LLC and Correctional Services Corporation, LLC have agreed to sell their Tacoma property, which holds the Northwest ICE Processing Center and its buildings and improvements, to the United States of America, acting through the Department of Homeland Security. The purchase and sale agreement, dated November 17, 2026 and filed with GEO Group's Form 8-K on November 19, sets the purchase price at $453 million, payable at closing by wire transfer or immediately available funds.
