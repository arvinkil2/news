The Reserve Bank of India released its 2026 list of Domestic Systemically Important Banks on November 23 and changed nothing. State Bank of India, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank continue to be identified as D-SIBs under the same bucketing structure as in the 2025 list, based on data collected from banks as on March 31, 2026.

State Bank of India stays in bucket 4, which carries an additional Common Equity Tier 1 requirement of 0.80 percent of risk weighted assets. HDFC Bank stays in bucket 2 at 0.40 percent, and ICICI Bank stays in bucket 1 at 0.20 percent. Buckets 3 and 5 remain empty, and no bank joined or left the list.

The surcharges sit on top of the capital conservation buffer and are the price the framework puts on systemic importance: the larger and more interconnected the bank, the more loss-absorbing capital it must fund itself with. An unchanged list is still information for funding markets, because a bucket move would have raised or lowered a bank's cost of capital at the margin.

The RBI has published the list annually since the framework began, scoring banks on size, interconnectedness, substitutability and complexity. The 2026 outcome says the hierarchy of systemic importance in Indian banking, at least as the central bank measures it, did not move over the year to March.
