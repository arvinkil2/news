President Donald Trump on Thursday demanded an immediate probe into what he alleged was a deliberate decision to release fake growth numbers before this month's midterm elections, Reuters reported. The Bureau of Economic Analysis, part of the Commerce Department, on Wednesday revised third-quarter annualized growth up to 4.3 percent from the advance estimate of 3.4 percent it announced on October 29, five days before the November 3 election, in which Trump's Republican Party suffered heavy losses.

"It was just revealed that the REAL Numbers on the Economy, published before the Election, were FAKE," Trump said in a Truth Social post, without providing evidence. "Why wasn't the correct number issued before the Election? I am asking for an immediate investigation of this situation. This is not a small error, this is a massive, and purposeful, error," he said. The BEA has previously said its estimates can differ from the final numbers because advance estimates lack complete source data on inventories, trade, and consumer spending on services. Last year Trump fired a top Labor Department official, accusing her without evidence of manipulating the figures, adding to concerns about the quality of economic data published by the federal government.
