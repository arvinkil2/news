Entain Group Pty Ltd, the Australian operator of Ladbrokes and Neds, has reached a preliminary agreement with the Australian Transaction Reports and Analysis Centre to pay a civil penalty of A$185 million plus AUSTRAC's costs, resolving the civil penalty proceedings the regulator began in the Federal Court in December 2024 over contraventions of the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act between December 2018 and August 2024. The two sides propose to file a statement of agreed facts and admissions, and Entain has applied to vacate the trial dates that were due to begin on November 30.

The penalty and the admissions still need final approval from Entain's board and AUSTRAC, and the Federal Court must approve the penalty, so Entain said there is no certainty the preliminary terms will stand. Subject to those approvals, the company expects to pay the penalty in full in late December 2026, and said it has completed a programme of enhancements to its Australian anti-money laundering systems and controls.
