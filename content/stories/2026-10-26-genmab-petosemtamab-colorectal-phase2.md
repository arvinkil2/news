Genmab shares rose as much as 13 percent in Copenhagen trading on Monday after the company presented updated Phase 2 results for petosemtamab at the ESMO Congress in Madrid over the weekend, Investing.com reported. In Genmab's own release, petosemtamab combined with FOLFOX or FOLFIRI chemotherapy produced confirmed and unconfirmed response rates of 75 percent in first-line and 67 percent in second-line metastatic colorectal cancer among 79 treated patients, with disease control rates of 95 and 93 percent and most earlier unconfirmed responses confirmed at median follow-ups of 5.9 and 7.1 months. The company said the data support its ongoing global Phase 3 LiGeR trials in first and second-line disease, and that treatment-related events led to discontinuation in 5 percent of the FOLFOX group and 3 percent of the FOLFIRI group, with no treatment-emergent events leading to death.
