The Federal Reserve on Thursday announced a revised tentative schedule of Federal Open Market Committee meetings in 2027. The Committee plans to meet on January 26-27, March 16-17, April 27-28, June 8-9, July 27-28, September 14-15, October 26-27 and November 16-17, followed by January 25-26, 2028, and will announce its policy decision on the second day of each meeting as usual, with one exception.

That exception is the October 26-27 meeting, which will be dedicated to discussion of broad monetary policy strategy issues rather than a policy decision. The schedule lists no December 2027 meeting.
