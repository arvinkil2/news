South Africa's mining production fell 10.7 percent year on year in September 2026, Statistics South Africa reported in release P2041. The largest negative contributors were iron ore, down 23.7 percent, coal, down 13.4 percent, platinum group metals, down 9.9 percent, and diamonds, down 63.1 percent.

The annual fall came despite a monthly recovery: seasonally adjusted production rose 2.8 percent month on month in September. Mineral sales at current prices fell 4.1 percent year on year.
