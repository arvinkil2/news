President Donald Trump said at a White House press conference on Friday that US companies will either be banned from building data centers in other countries or will face a tremendous tax penalty if they do so, adding that his administration is going to fight very hard for the data centers. He gave no specifics on the scope, rate, timing or legal basis of the policy, and said he had not raised it with his own staff before announcing it. CNBC reported no company response and no market move attributed to the remarks.
