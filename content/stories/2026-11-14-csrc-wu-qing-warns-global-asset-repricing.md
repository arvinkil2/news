Wu Qing, chairman of the China Securities Regulatory Commission, told the opening of the first Chinese Capital Market Academic Annual Conference in Shanghai on Saturday that global capital markets are undergoing a new round of asset repricing and rebalancing in the face of geopolitical tensions and policy uncertainty. He called for deeper research and regulatory cooperation to strengthen markets' resilience to unpredictable "black swan" and "gray rhino" events, and said China's capital market has never been as interconnected with global markets as it is today, requiring stronger work on cross-border, cross-market and cross-industry risk transmission.

On domestic rules, Wu said the CSRC will research and evaluate optimizing the institutional rules governing quantitative trading and shareholding reductions, alongside fair and reasonable regulatory arrangements to protect investors. He put total A-share market value above 100 trillion yuan and the bond market above 210 trillion yuan, and listed long-term stability mechanisms, market microstructure and the two-way effects of artificial intelligence on capital markets among the research priorities for the 15th Five-Year Plan period.
