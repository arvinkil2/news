Jordan's armed forces said their fighter jets destroyed terrorist positions in southern Syria on Monday, killing or wounding many members of groups they link to Friday's attack on a Greek Orthodox church in the Jordanian village of Hamoud, which killed nine people and injured 34. Syrian authorities said the strikes killed eight civilians, among them three children and one elderly person, and injured four others, and health officials said the dead were taken to hospitals in Daraa province.

Damascus said there had been no coordination with the Syrian government over the operation, and Syria closed the Nasib border crossing with Jordan after the strikes. Jordan has not, in the reporting read, addressed the civilian toll, and Syria has not, in that reporting, addressed Jordan's description of the targets.
