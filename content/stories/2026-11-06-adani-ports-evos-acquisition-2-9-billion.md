Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone has agreed to acquire 100 percent of Netherlands-based Evos B.V. from Igneo Infrastructure Partners for 2.56 billion euros (2.88 billion dollars), with an equity value of 1.18 billion euros financed through debt and MUFG financing commitments of up to 2.8 billion euros, subject to regulatory approvals and works council consultation.

Evos operates eight port-based terminals in the Netherlands, Germany, Spain, Belgium and Malta with 6.3 million cubic metres of storage across 452 tanks, handled 37 million metric tons of liquid cargo in 2025, and generated 392 million euros of revenue and 219 million euros of EBITDA last year at average commercial occupancy of 99 percent. No expected closing date was announced.
