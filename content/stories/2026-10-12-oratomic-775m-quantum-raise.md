Quantum computing startup Oratomic has raised $775 million in total funding for the development of fault-tolerant quantum systems, Datacenter Dynamics reported Monday. The announcement comes three months after the company raised $300 million in a Series A, with both rounds co-led by Arch Venture Partners, Spark Capital and Khosla Ventures, and Bezos Expeditions, Index Ventures and General Catalyst joining as additional co-leaders in the latest round.

Oratomic, based in Pasadena and emerged from stealth in March 2026, builds its machines with lasers that hold individual atoms in place and claims it can reach a useful fault-tolerant computer with 10,000 to 20,000 qubits, far fewer than rivals working on the same modality have assumed necessary. Founder Manuel Endres has said the company has already trapped arrays of 6,000 atomic qubits. The new funding will go to growing the scientific and engineering team, laboratory infrastructure, and the systems needed to build the machines.
