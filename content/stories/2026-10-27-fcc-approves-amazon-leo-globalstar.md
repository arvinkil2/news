The Federal Communications Commission said on Tuesday that its Space Bureau has approved Amazon Leo's application to acquire about 25 megahertz of Globalstar's internationally harmonised mobile-satellite service spectrum, concluding the agency's review of Amazon Leo's $11.5 billion purchase of Globalstar. The commission said the transaction will expand direct-to-device competition, strengthen emergency communications, and improve service in unserved and underserved areas.

Amazon agreed the purchase in April, and the deal is expected to close in 2027. Globalstar's network carries satellite connectivity for Apple's iPhones, and Amazon Leo, Amazon's low Earth orbit broadband network formerly called Project Kuiper, has committed to honouring that capacity arrangement, according to earlier reporting on the transaction.
