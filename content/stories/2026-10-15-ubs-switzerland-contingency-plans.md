UBS is preparing contingency plans that could include relocating its headquarters out of Switzerland if the country's planned capital rules are tightened, the Wall Street Journal reported on Wednesday, citing people familiar with the matter, with the options said to include acquiring a US financial institution, merging with a European rival, or selling itself to a US bank. Under the Swiss government plan, drawn up after the 2023 rescue of Credit Suisse, UBS would need to hold about 20 billion dollars in additional Common Equity Tier 1 capital, mainly by backing its foreign units at 100 percent rather than the current minimum of about 60 percent. UBS has called the requirement excessive, while its chairman said last month the bank would have to consider its future in Switzerland if the rules became too stringent, though its preferred outcome remains a compromise that lets it stay.
