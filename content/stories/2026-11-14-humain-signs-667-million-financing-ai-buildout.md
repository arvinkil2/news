Humain and the National Infrastructure Fund (Infra) have signed two financing agreements totaling SAR 2.5 billion ($667 million) to support Humain's AI buildout in Saudi Arabia, the first transactions under Infra's AI Data Center Financing Program. A GPU financing facility of up to SAR 1.1 billion ($293.3 million) will support procurement and deployment of GPUs with approximately 25MW of IT load, and a second facility of up to SAR 1.4 billion ($373.3 million) will finance substations, transmission infrastructure and grid connections for Humain's Saudi data centers.

Humain, in which Saudi Aramco acquired a 49 percent stake earlier this month and the Public Investment Fund holds the remaining 51 percent, is targeting 1GW of IT capacity in total. Infra chief executive Esmail Bin Mohammed Alsallom said the agreements aim to prepare a broader pipeline of data center projects for investment and to attract a wider pool of private and institutional capital.
