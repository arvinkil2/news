INSEE's household confidence indicator fell to 80 in October from 85 in September, its lowest reading since June 2013, against a long-term average of 100. Households' view of their future financial situation dropped nine points and of their past financial situation six points, and the balance on whether it is a good time to make major purchases fell four points.

The savings climate index eased two points to 118, still above its long-term average, so the deterioration sits in spending intentions and personal finances rather than in the capacity to save.
