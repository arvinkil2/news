Teva Pharmaceutical Industries said in an SEC filing that Dr. Eric Hughes, its executive vice president for global research and development and chief medical officer, notified the company on November 17 of his intention to resign. Hughes steps down from the role immediately and will continue to assist with the transition over the next few months.

Teva announced the departure in a press release on November 18, furnished with the filing. The 8-K names no successor and gives no reason for the resignation.
