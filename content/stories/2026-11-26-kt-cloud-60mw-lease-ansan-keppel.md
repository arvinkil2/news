KT Cloud, the cloud unit of KT Corp, has signed a 60MW lease as anchor tenant for the whole of Keppel's greenfield data center in Ansan, in the greater Seoul metropolitan area, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The facility, held under Keppel Data Centre Fund III, will span 52,620 square metres over eight floors above ground and two below, with liquid cooling for high density AI compute, and construction is expected to begin this month for service by 2030.

KT Cloud broke ground on a separate 50MW facility in Ansan in October and plans to link the two sites as an integrated campus, its chief executive Bong-gyun Kim said.
