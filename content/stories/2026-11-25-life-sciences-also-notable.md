**UK medicines recalls.** The MHRA's Defective Medicines Report Centre issued a Class 3 recall today for Mercury Pharmaceuticals' bexarotene 75 mg soft capsules, batch 5GJ0551, after several capsules were found with black and blue spots during quality-limit testing at a pre-wholesaler, following a pharmacy complaint. The recall is at pharmacy and wholesaler level, no patient action is required, and no patient harm has been reported. Bexarotene is used in cutaneous T-cell lymphoma. The same alert series carried a Class 2 recall of Quadrant Pharmaceuticals' parallel-imported Brintellix (vortioxetine) 5 mg tablets and a Class 4 defect notification for Atnahs Pharma's Toradol (ketorolac) 30 mg/mL injection, both dated November 24.

**AstraZeneca director purchase, not a development.** AstraZeneca's only filing this morning (Form 6-K, accession 0001654954-26-010111) records non-executive director Anna Manz buying 550 ordinary shares at GBP 135.519 on November 23. It is a routine market-abuse-regulation notification and carries no company news; it is noted here only because the filing was in this morning's pool and was checked.
