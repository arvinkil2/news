Virbac, the French animal health group, has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Axiota Animal Health, a portfolio company of Paine Schwartz Partners, in an all-cash transaction valued at 315 million dollars, the parties announced on November 25. Virbac will acquire 100 percent of Axiota's shares.

Axiota sells preventive cattle health products for beef and dairy producers, principally Multimin, an injectable trace mineral product, and Lactipro, a rumen-native probiotic. Total fiscal 2026 revenue was 65.0 million dollars under Axiota's accounting methodology, of which about 47 million dollars is US revenue, alongside a specialised dairy, beef and feedlot team that Virbac described as a transformative step for its US presence.

Virbac expects the acquisition to be accretive to revenue growth from the first year and to lift its adjusted EBIT margin by about 30 basis points in 2027 and about 50 basis points by 2030, underpinning its target of a 20 percent adjusted EBIT margin by 2030. Reuters put the value at 287.78 million euros at current exchange rates.

Closing is subject to customary Hart-Scott-Rodino antitrust clearance and other conditions and is anticipated by the end of January 2027. Rabobank and Morrison and Foerster advised Paine Schwartz; Deloitte and White and Case advised Virbac.
