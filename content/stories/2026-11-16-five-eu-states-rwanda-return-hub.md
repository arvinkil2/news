Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Greece and Denmark agreed in Vienna on Monday to set up a return hub in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers who have exhausted their legal options in the EU and cannot be returned to their home countries, Deutsche Welle reported. The group hopes the scheme is running in 2027, starting as a pilot housing about 20 people from each country; Rwanda's interior minister Vincent Biruta said the site would be a welcome centre where people can live and work, not a detention facility, and Austrian Chancellor Christian Stocker called the decision a historic paradigm shift in migration policy.

About 45 NGOs from 15 countries condemned the plan as a new phase of harmful externalisation, and Human Rights Watch pointed to Rwanda's record of repression and arbitrary detention; fewer than 30 percent of people ordered to leave the EU are currently returned to their country of origin.
