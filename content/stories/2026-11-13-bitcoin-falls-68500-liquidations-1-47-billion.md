Bitcoin traded at 68,529.96 dollars as of 2:30 p.m. on Friday in Seoul, down 6.82 percent from the previous day according to CoinMarketCap figures reported by Yonhap, while on the domestic Upbit exchange it changed hands at 93 million won, down 3.72 percent, at a Korea premium of 1.80 percent, and ether, BNB, XRP and other major tokens also fell. CoinGlass data cited in the report put liquidations in the previous 24 hours at about 1.47 billion dollars, of which 1.31 billion dollars was in long positions, with about 175,000 traders liquidated and the largest single order, on Hyperliquid, worth 31.09 million dollars. Yonhap attributed the fall to dried up new money and the interest rate burden, quoting Tiger Research's Yoon Seung-sik that with the United States 10 year yield above 5 percent bitcoin is struggling to hold up, and that forced liquidations of repeated long bets are widening the decline.
