**Latest, November 23.** The death toll rose to at least 14 as rescuers recovered another woman's body in Dymer on Monday, Kyiv regional official Tymur Tkachenko said, taking the Dymer toll to at least 10. In Vyshneve, the supermarket strike killed three people and wounded 15, including three children, the town's mayor, Illya Dikov, said; one person was killed in Bila Tserkva. Emergency teams were still searching for 11 people, including two children, who could not be reached.

Overnight Russian attacks on the Kyiv region killed at least 11 people, Ukrainian officials said. In Dymer, about 45 kilometres north of the capital, a strike hit a five-storey apartment building and partially collapsed it, killing at least eight people; Kyiv regional official Tymur Tkachenko said rescuers had recovered an eighth body on Monday and that search and rescue operations were continuing, with 11 people, including two children, earlier reported unaccounted for.

Two more people were killed and eight wounded when a strike hit a supermarket in Vyshneve, and one person was killed when a strike set a private home ablaze in Bila Tserkva, Tkachenko said. Ukraine's prime minister, Sergiy Koretsky, said Russia also struck a power substation in the Dnipropetrovsk region.
