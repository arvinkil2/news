South Korea is considering issuing about $3 billion of dollar-denominated bonds as early as January 2027, according to Bloomberg reporting citing finance ministry officials. The government solicited proposals from banks earlier this month and will hear presentations this week to select underwriters. Seoul is seeking parliamentary approval to issue up to $8 billion of such bonds by 2027, with the proceeds intended to strengthen foreign exchange reserves.
