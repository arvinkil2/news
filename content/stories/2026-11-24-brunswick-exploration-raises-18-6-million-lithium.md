Brunswick Exploration has closed its previously announced best efforts private placement for aggregate gross proceeds of $17,844,500, from the sale of 21,050,000 common shares at $0.19 per share and 46,150,000 common shares qualifying as flow-through shares, sold to charitable purchasers at $0.30 per share. Canaccord Genuity Capital Markets acted as lead agent and sole bookrunner, with Red Cloud Securities and Integrity Capital Group, under an agency agreement dated November 23, 2026.

The company also closed a first tranche of its concurrent non-brokered private placement for gross proceeds of $718,018, from the sale of 2,564,350 traditional flow-through shares at $0.28 per share, taking total gross proceeds to about $18.6 million. A second tranche of the concurrent placement remains to come, and both offerings remain subject to final approval of the TSX Venture Exchange.

Gross proceeds from the charity flow-through and traditional flow-through shares will be used to incur eligible Canadian exploration expenses qualifying as flow-through critical mineral mining expenditures on the company's properties in Quebec, to be incurred on or before December 31, 2027 and renounced to subscribers effective December 31, 2026. Net proceeds from the common shares will be used for exploration at the company's properties and for working capital and general corporate purposes.

Brunswick will use the funds for exploration activities in Canada, Greenland and Saudi Arabia, including its flagship Mirage project in Quebec, which the company describes as one of the largest undeveloped hard rock lithium deposits in the Americas. The closing follows an upsizing of the offering earlier in November on strong investor demand.
