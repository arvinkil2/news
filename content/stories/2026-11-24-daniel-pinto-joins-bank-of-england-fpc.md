The Chancellor of the Exchequer has appointed Daniel E. Pinto as an external member of the Financial Policy Committee, HM Treasury said. Mr Pinto, who has more than 40 years of experience in financial services and most recently served as vice chairman of JPMorgan Chase, will serve a three-year term beginning on 4 January 2027, filling the position held by Jonathan Hall, who steps down on 31 December 2026.

Mr Pinto previously served as JPMorgan's president and chief operating officer and as chief executive of its corporate and investment bank. The appointment follows an open competition and is subject to pre-commencement scrutiny by the Treasury Select Committee on 15 December 2026. Governor Andrew Bailey said Mr Pinto brings an exceptional breadth of international experience to the committee, the UK's macroprudential regulator, which has thirteen members including five external appointees.
