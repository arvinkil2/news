Polling stations opened at 9am on Saturday across New Zealand and close at 7pm, closing a voting period that began on October 26. More than 1.6 million voters, out of nearly 3.8 million in total, had cast ballots by the end of Thursday, according to the Electoral Commission, with cost of living and the state of the economy the backdrop to the final day.

Opinion polls ahead of the vote put the governing centre-right National Party and the opposition centre-left Labour Party neck and neck, with neither on course to win enough seats to govern alone under the mixed-member proportional system. The Electoral Commission aims to release preliminary results from 95 percent of polling stations by the end of election night on Saturday, with the final result, including ballots cast abroad, expected by November 27.
