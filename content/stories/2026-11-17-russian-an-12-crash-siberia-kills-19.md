A Russian military Antonov An-12 transport aircraft crashed on Tuesday in an uninhabited area near the village of Yagunovo in the Kemerovo region of western Siberia, about 3,000 kilometres east of Moscow, killing all 19 people on board, Russia's defence ministry said. The aircraft was on a scheduled flight, and the ministry said its preliminary finding was a technical malfunction.

Regional governor Ilya Seredyuk said emergency crews quickly extinguished a blaze at the site, police sealed off the area, and a helicopter and medical facilities were put on standby. A commission from the Russian Aerospace Forces is working at the site, and the Investigative Committee has opened an inquiry into possible violations of flight safety rules. The An-12, a Soviet-built four-engine turboprop in service since the late 1950s, is widely used by the Russian military to move weaponry and personnel; a Russian Tu-95 crashed in the Amur region in September, killing six crew.
