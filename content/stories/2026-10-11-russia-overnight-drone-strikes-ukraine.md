Russian drones struck an industrial enterprise in Bucha district, Kyiv region, overnight, setting fire to warehouse buildings, Ukrainska Pravda reported, citing the regional military administration and emergency services. Kyiv region head Timur Tkachenko said firefighters had localized the blaze and that there were no dead or injured, according to the preliminary report.

Espreso reported the overnight wave also hit Dnipro and Odesa, with children among the wounded, and said Ukrainian air defenses neutralized 94 drones. The strikes came the same night Kyiv offered Moscow a mutual halt to attacks on energy infrastructure.
