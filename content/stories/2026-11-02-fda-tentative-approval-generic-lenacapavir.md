The Food and Drug Administration has given tentative approval to Dr. Reddy's Laboratories to make a generic version of lenacapavir, the HIV prevention medicine given by injection once every six months, according to STAT. The company plans to start shipping the medicine next month, at an annual cost of 40 dollars per person, comparable to daily prevention tablets, as part of an effort backed by global health organisations to supply dozens of low and middle income countries. The brand name version, Yeztugo, is sold by Gilead Sciences and was approved by the FDA last year.
