A consortium led by private investment firm RRJ Capital has agreed to buy 619 million dollars of bonds from GM3, the Australian coking coal miner, according to Bloomberg, citing people familiar with the matter. GM3 will use the proceeds to refinance a 600 million dollar private credit loan.

The notes are to be issued on December 8, mature in five years, pay a coupon of 9.75 percent, were sold at a price of 97 and can be called in 2028, Bloomberg reported.
