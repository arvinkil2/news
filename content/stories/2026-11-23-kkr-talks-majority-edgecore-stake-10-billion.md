KKR is negotiating to acquire a majority stake in EdgeCore Digital Infrastructure from Partners Group at a valuation of between $8 billion and $10 billion, Bloomberg reported, in what would be the first deal for Helix Digital Infrastructure, the AI vehicle KKR launched in June with $10 billion of committed capital, backing from Nvidia and the Kuwait Investment Authority, and former AWS chief executive Adam Selipsky in charge. Discussions are ongoing and could still end without an agreement, the report said.

EdgeCore, founded in 2018 and bought by Partners Group in 2022, says its portfolio exceeds 2 gigawatts across operating and development sites in Silicon Valley, Phoenix, Reno and Virginia, with $8 billion of capital committed and more than $16 billion of planned investment. KKR already holds data centre positions including CyrusOne, ST Telemedia Global Data Centres and Global Technical Realty.
