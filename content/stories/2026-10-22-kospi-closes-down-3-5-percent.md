South Korea's Kospi closed down 201.89 points, or 3.52 percent, at 5,533.77 on Thursday, after opening 1.14 percent lower, briefly turning positive and then sliding through the session, Yonhap reported, citing Korea Exchange figures. The Kosdaq closed down 28.66 points, or 3.62 percent, at 764.10.

Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, together about 51 percent of the Kospi's market value, fell 4 to 5 percent and dragged the index lower. Yonhap, citing Seoul analysts, attributed the fall to profit taking after the two stocks rose more than 5 percent the previous day, to Meta reporting strong third quarter results before the open but giving no specific capital spending guidance for next year, with its free cash flow turning negative, and to a demand gap after both companies' large share buyback programmes recently ended. The Bank of Korea held its base rate at 3.00 percent the same day in what analysts described as a hawkish hold, with Governor Shin Hyun-song signalling that further increases remain possible.
