Chilean lithium production will grow 3.1 percent in 2027 to 335,000 tonnes, according to a November 12 report by the state copper commission Cochilco, reported by Reuters. Production in 2026 is expected to close at 325,000 tonnes, after 311,000 tonnes in 2025.

Between January and July this year, 74 percent of Chilean lithium exports were lithium carbonate, 21 percent sulfate and 5 percent hydroxide. Chile holds 25 percent of the world available lithium reserves.

Cochilco expects the global market to close this year with a deficit of 79,000 tonnes and to move to a surplus of 42,000 tonnes in 2027. On those figures, Chile is growing output steadily from the Atacama brine operations into a market that its own state forecaster expects to be oversupplied next year, which limits the revenue benefit of the volume growth unless prices respond to the earlier deficit.
