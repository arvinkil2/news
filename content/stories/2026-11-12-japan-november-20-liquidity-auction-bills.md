Japan's Ministry of Finance announced on Friday (Tokyo time) a liquidity enhancement auction for November 20, reopening off the run issues across five sectors: 2 year issues 478 to 490, 5 year issues 155 to 187, 10 year issues 349 to 364, 20 year issues 99 to 131 and 30 year issues 1 to 5. The same day the ministry will auction about 3,100 billion yen of treasury discount bills, to be issued on November 24 and to mature on March 1, 2027; the ministry notes the offering amount may be changed, with any revision announced the day before the auction.
