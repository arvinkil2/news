Jodi L. Richard notified U.S. Bancorp on October 23, 2026 of her resignation as vice chair and chief risk officer, effective immediately, to take a position at another publicly traded company, the bank said in a Form 8-K filed on Thursday. The filing states that her resignation was not due to any disagreement with the company on any matter relating to its operations, policies or practices. Troy W. Remington, executive vice president and chief credit officer, will serve as interim chief risk officer while the company undertakes a process to select and appoint a successor.
