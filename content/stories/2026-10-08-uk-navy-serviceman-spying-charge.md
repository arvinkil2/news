Teddy Young, 24, was arrested at his home on Wednesday and charged with engaging in conduct to prepare "for committing an offense of disclosing protected information," the Metropolitan Police Service said in an emailed statement cited by Bloomberg. Counter-terrorism police said he allegedly spied for a foreign country, which the statement did not name.

Young's rank was not given beyond "serving member of the Royal Navy." Bloomberg's report was brief and carried no bail or court-date details.
