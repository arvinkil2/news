Enel, through its wholly owned subsidiaries Enel Green Power North America and EGPNA Project Holdco 7, has signed an agreement with energyRe to acquire a 317 MW solar portfolio in South Carolina. The portfolio is expected to generate more than 540 GWh of electricity a year once in service.

Enel said the acquisition is expected to increase the group's net financial debt by approximately $255 million after accounting for the projects entering service, and to contribute approximately $30 million a year to group EBITDA once closed. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary conditions including US antitrust approval and US government foreign direct investment clearance.

After closing, Enel expects its installed renewable capacity in North America to reach approximately 13.5 GW across wind, solar and storage. The purchase follows its September completion of a 625 MW US solar portfolio acquisition from Excelsior Energy Capital for approximately $760 million, and forms part of the strategy presented at its February capital markets day to grow renewable capacity through acquisitions of operating or developed assets in Tier 1 countries.
