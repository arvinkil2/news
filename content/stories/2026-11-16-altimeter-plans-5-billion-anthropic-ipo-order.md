Altimeter Capital plans to invest $5 billion in Anthropic's initial public offering, founder Brad Gerstner told CNBC's Halftime Report on Monday, calling the company "a cornerstone compounder for a long time" and saying the order is five times the most he has ever put into an IPO. Anthropic is expected to list on Nasdaq on Thursday, the first major AI lab to go public, ahead of OpenAI, which has said it is targeting an offering next year.

Gerstner is investing alongside the large chipmakers and cloud providers, many of which count Anthropic as a customer: AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC are collectively investing $26 billion at the IPO price, currently expected at $70 to $78 a share, according to CNBC. Anthropic plans to offer 825 million shares at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion.
