The Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye said on Thursday it has decided to temporarily suspend the one-week repo auctions it conducts as part of its open market operations, giving no end date for the suspension. In a separate release the same day, the bank said it will raise the remuneration rate applied to required reserves maintained for Turkish lira deposits from 86 percent to 90 percent of its weighted average funding cost, a step it said supports macrofinancial stability and the monetary transmission mechanism.
