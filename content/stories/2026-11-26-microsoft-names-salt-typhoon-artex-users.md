Microsoft said this week that Chinese and North Korean state-sponsored hacking groups are among the crews using ARTEX, the open-source AI-powered penetration-testing framework released in July, in live attacks, The Register reported. The named users include China's Salt Typhoon, Crystal Typhoon and Storm-0940, and North Korea's Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet, and Microsoft said campaigns using the tool have reached privileged access in under three hours against cloud environments, IT software and financial institutions. ARTEX chains known attack techniques with AI agents rather than introducing new ones, and the UK National Cyber Security Centre warned this week that such agentic offensive frameworks are fast and thorough but noisy and easier to detect.
