Australia's consumer price index rose 4.9 percent in the 12 months to October, accelerating from 4.3 percent in the 12 months to September, the Australian Bureau of Statistics said. The trimmed mean, the underlying measure, rose 3.8 percent on the year, up from 3.6 percent in September.

Housing was the largest contributor, rising 6.4 percent on the year, as electricity prices rose 25.2 percent after Commonwealth rebates that had cut household costs last year ended, and new dwelling prices rose 5.1 percent. Transport was the second largest contributor, rising 10.6 percent on higher automotive fuel prices, which rose 6.3 percent in October alone on higher world oil prices.
