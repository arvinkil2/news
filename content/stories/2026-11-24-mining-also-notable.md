EcoGraf has secured approval for up to 1.4 million euros (A$2.3 million) of community and environmental funding for its Epanko graphite project in Tanzania, with Germany's Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development contributing 700,000 euros through the develoPPP initiative, administered by DEG Impulse, and EcoGraf matching the amount over two years. The programme funds skills training, employment, environmental protection and local enterprise in the Ulanga District. It is not mine construction financing: the separate KfW IPEX-Bank mandate to arrange up to $105 million of project debt remains subject to approvals and due diligence.

Kropz's Elandsfontein phosphate operation in South Africa has agreed two related party demand loan facilities totalling 535 million rand (about $32.5 million): 370 million rand from Ubuntu-Botho Investments for cash flow and operations, and 165 million rand from ARC Fund for capital expenditure. Both carry interest at the South African prime overdraft rate plus 6 percent, compounded monthly, are repayable on demand with at least 15 business days' notice, may be drawn until March 31, 2028, and had not been drawn when announced. Elandsfontein is still ramping up and has yet to achieve break-even production.

Codelco has offered Asian buyers annual contract premiums of $250 to $280 per tonne for 2027, compared with $350 for Chinese buyers this year, Reuters reported, citing people familiar with the discussions. China imported just over 140,000 tonnes of Chilean cathode in the first nine months of 2026, down 38 percent from a year earlier, a sign of how much the tariff driven reshaping of copper flows has weakened the traditional Chile to China contract channel.

Traders are preparing to ship an estimated 30,000 to 100,000 tonnes of refined copper from the US West Coast to China, BMO Capital Markets said, reversing the flow of metal into American warehouses ahead of possible US import tariffs. The shipments follow reports that tens of thousands of tonnes of US held cathode have been booked for direct dispatch to China, and they would return metal to the market where the Yangshan import premium, though easing from its record, still signals tight physical supply.
