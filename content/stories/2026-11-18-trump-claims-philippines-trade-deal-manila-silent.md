President Donald Trump said at a state luncheon at the presidential palace in Manila on Wednesday that the United States had just concluded a trade deal with the Philippines. "We've just consummated a very big trade deal, the biggest trade deal we've ever made so far, and it is a fair deal for both countries," he said, adding that it "took place very quickly." Negotiations on a trade deal between the two allies had not been mentioned in any schedule released by either country.

No terms were provided, and Manila has not confirmed the claim. There was no immediate comment from the office of Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., and the Philippines' ambassador to the United States did not immediately respond to a request for comment, Reuters reported. Trump separately said on Tuesday that Thailand would eliminate all tariffs on imports from the United States under a deal expected to be finalized in the coming weeks.
