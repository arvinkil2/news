OpenAI will bring its GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 Pro and GPT-5.5 models and its Codex coding agent to Google Cloud, rolling out over the next few months starting with international markets outside the United States, CNBC reported on Wednesday, citing Google's announcement. OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman said customers had asked for the company's products in more countries and on the platforms they already use, and Google Cloud's Thomas Kurian said the arrangement gives enterprises one place to build with the models alongside Google's own AI services and security controls.

The deal extends OpenAI's distribution beyond Microsoft, its principal cloud partner, and no financial terms were disclosed in the report.
