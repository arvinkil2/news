OpenAI will make its frontier artificial intelligence models and its Codex coding agent available through Google Cloud in the coming weeks, the companies said on Wednesday, with its latest models such as GPT-6 Astra offered in Google Cloud's Model Garden. Customers will be able to buy the models through Google Cloud Marketplace and apply existing cloud spending commitments to the purchases, and customers in regulated industries will be able to use them under Google Cloud's access controls and data governance tools, Reuters reported, citing the company blog post announcing the arrangement.

Google is the latest major cloud provider to distribute OpenAI's models after its exclusive distribution arrangement with Microsoft expired in April, with Amazon Web Services added in between, CNBC reported. No financial terms for the arrangement were disclosed in either report.
