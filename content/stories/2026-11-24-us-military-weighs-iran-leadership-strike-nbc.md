The US military is reviewing an operation to remove Iran's regime leadership as President Donald Trump considers resuming attacks to end the war, NBC reported, in a report relayed by Yonhap on November 25. Supporters inside the government present the option as an exit strategy that would remove hardliners and let Washington declare victory, with Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei, who succeeded his father Ali Khamenei after he was killed on the war's first day and has not appeared in public since, the likely first target. Opponents cited in the same report warn that Mojtaba's removal would make him a martyr, push the regime further toward hardliners, undermine trust in any later ceasefire negotiation and expose senior US officials to retaliation.
