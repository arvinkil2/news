Hansoh Pharma said on Wednesday it has signed a licence agreement granting Bayer an exclusive licence to develop, manufacture and commercialise HS-20122 globally, excluding the Chinese mainland, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan. Hansoh will receive an upfront payment of $150 million and is eligible for up to $3.1 billion in development, regulatory and commercialisation milestone payments, plus tiered royalties on future net sales, a total potential value of $3.25 billion; the agreement takes effect after antitrust clearances.

HS-20122 is a bispecific antibody-drug conjugate targeting EGFR and c-Met, designed for advanced solid tumours including non-small-cell lung cancer. Hansoh said encouraging efficacy and safety results from its phase 1 study were recently presented at the European Society for Medical Oncology 2026 congress.
