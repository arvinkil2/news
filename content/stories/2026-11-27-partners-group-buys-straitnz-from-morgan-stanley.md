Partners Group, acting on behalf of its clients, has agreed to acquire StraitNZ from Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners. StraitNZ operates two vessels providing daily freight and passenger services across the Cook Strait between New Zealand's North Island and South Island, under the Bluebridge brand, and also runs a road freight business offering haulage and freight forwarding nationwide. It is one of only two operators on the route.

Neither side disclosed financial terms. Partners Group said freight customers are served under inflation-protected contracts while passengers are carried under a dynamic pricing model, giving the company recurring income with strong infrastructure characteristics. It plans to work with management on a value creation plan to improve the customer offering and grow the recurring revenue base, and said client demand for income-oriented infrastructure assets is rising.

Morgan Stanley Infrastructure Partners has owned StraitNZ since March 2022, when it bought the business from Champ Private Equity and Macquarie in a deal reported at the time at about NZ$500 million. During its ownership the company refreshed its ferry fleet and secured long-term port lease extensions. The sale to Partners Group is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions and approval from New Zealand's Overseas Investment Office.
