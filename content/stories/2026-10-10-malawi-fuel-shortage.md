Malawi's fuel shortage has brought daily life in the capital Lilongwe to a halt, Al Jazeera reported Saturday. Motorists are waiting up to three days for petrol, with some sleeping in their cars to hold their place in the queues. Businesses are losing customers as residents struggle to get to work and take children to school, and the queues show no sign of clearing.
