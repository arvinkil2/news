Ukraine said it attacked Russia's largest oil refinery for a third time on Wednesday. The Special Operations Forces said its Deep Strike units hit the Omsk plant in western Siberia, giving no details of damage, Bloomberg reported, and the city spent more than eight hours under a drone alert on November 11, with residents reporting a fire near the refinery and the independent Russian channel Astra's analysis confirming a blaze near the site, The Kyiv Independent reported.

Omsk governor Vitaly Khotsenko confirmed Ukrainian drones were attacking the region and said emergency workers were sent to sites hit by falling drone debris, without naming the facilities struck. The Gazprom Neft refinery, which processes over 21 million tonnes of crude a year, was previously hit in July and October 2026, and the precise extent of damage from this strike is not yet clear.
