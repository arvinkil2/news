South Korea's Financial Services Commission will hold an emergency financial sector meeting on Monday, November 30, chaired by Chairman Lee Eok-won with the heads of the five major financial holding companies, to review market conditions after weeks of sharp stock swings and the Bank of Korea's rate hike to 3.25 percent. The stabilization fund's investment committee meets a day earlier, on November 29, to review the conditions for activating the fund, which now stands at about 10.76 trillion won in pledges from financial firms and securities institutions.

Korea Development Bank has written to participating banks and securities firms asking whether they can pay their pledged contributions immediately and whether they could add more if the fund is expanded, with some reports pointing to a doubling beyond 20 trillion won. Banks are pushing back, citing year-end capital ratios and board approval constraints, and officials say nothing specific has been decided.
