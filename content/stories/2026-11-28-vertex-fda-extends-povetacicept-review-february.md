The US Food and Drug Administration has extended its review of povetacicept, Vertex Pharmaceuticals' candidate for IgA nephropathy, by up to three months, Vertex announced on November 25. The agency informed the company on November 24. The original target action date was November 30, 2026, and the revised date is no later than February 28, 2027.

The extension followed an information request the FDA sent late in the review cycle. Vertex said it responded promptly, the agency classified the response as a major amendment to the biologics license application, and it needs more time to complete the review. Vertex did not disclose what the agency asked for. The company said it remains confident in the data supporting the application.

A major amendment extension is not a rejection. Vertex noted that the FDA generally uses this option when new information has the potential to resolve outstanding questions and allow approval within the existing review cycle. The application remains under review, and the agency can act on or before the new date.

Povetacicept is a dual inhibitor of the BAFF and APRIL cytokines, built on an engineered TACI domain, and is being studied in RAINIER, which Vertex describes as the largest contemporary trial in IgA nephropathy. Zacks reported that Vertex shares fell 7.3 percent in after-hours trading after the announcement, a sign investors read the delay as added risk rather than routine scheduling.

For Vertex, povetacicept is the lead asset in its push beyond cystic fibrosis into kidney disease. The three month delay moves a potential first approval and launch in that franchise into 2027, and the undisclosed information request will be the question hanging over the stock until the agency acts.
