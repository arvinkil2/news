Coalition spokesman Major General Turki Al-Malki confirmed the interception in a statement carried by Saudi state media, without identifying the missile's origin or intended target and with no word on casualties or damage. The statement follows a week in which Houthi missiles and drones have struck Riyadh's airport and an Aramco building in Dhahran, and a coalition drone interception over the Eastern Province earlier on Thursday. Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport remains closed until at least October 17, with more than 3,100 Saudi departures cancelled or not operating over the past week, according to Cirium data reported by Gulf Business.
