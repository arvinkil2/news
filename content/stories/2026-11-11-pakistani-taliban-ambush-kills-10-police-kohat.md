Militants ambushed vehicles carrying police officers near Kohat, a district in Pakistan's northwestern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, on Wednesday as the officers returned from an operation against criminals involved in illegal gold mining, local police official Faisal Khan said. At least 10 officers, including two senior officers, were killed, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa police said 18 militants were killed in a gunbattle that continued for hours.

The Pakistani Taliban, or Tehrik-e-Taliban Pakistan, claimed responsibility in a statement hours later. Police said the attackers had acted in collusion with criminal elements, a claim the AP report notes they offered no evidence to support, and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi condemned the attack and paid tribute to the officers killed.
