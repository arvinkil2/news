Shares of China's Kuaishou Technology fell as much as 13 percent in Hong Kong on Friday to HK$19.97, their lowest level on record, after the short-video platform operator reported disappointing third-quarter results. For the three months ended September, Kuaishou swung to a net loss of 959 million yuan, against a profit of 4.49 billion yuan a year earlier, while adjusted net profit fell 84.2 percent to 790 million yuan and revenue fell 11.1 percent to 31.59 billion yuan, with online marketing revenue down 11.1 percent and livestreaming revenue down 19 percent. Research and development expenses rose 41.9 percent to 5.2 billion yuan on increased AI investment, including its Kling video-generation platform, and the company warned that revenue and profitability could remain under pressure in the fourth quarter.
