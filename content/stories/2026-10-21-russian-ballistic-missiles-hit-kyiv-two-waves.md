Russian forces struck Kyiv with ballistic missiles on Wednesday evening and again overnight. In the evening wave, a missile hit a transport infrastructure site in the Darnytskyi district and six people were injured, four of whom were hospitalised, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said, with hits also recorded in the Dniprovskyi district. In the overnight wave, missiles damaged the building of a medical facility in the Pecherskyi district and set a restaurant building on fire in the Sviatoshynskyi district, the Kyiv City Military Administration said.

Ukrainska Pravda, citing the monitoring Telegram channel Monitor, reported that Russian forces launched at least 25 missiles of the Iskander, Oniks and Zircon types at Kyiv and its suburbs across the evening and night, a count no official source in the reporting read has confirmed. The strikes landed on the same day Moscow signalled readiness to resume peace talks with Ukraine and the United States, a track covered separately on this site.
