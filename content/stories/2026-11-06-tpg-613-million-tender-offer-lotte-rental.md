TPG is moving to take Lotte Rental fully private. Lexicon Korea Holdco, its acquisition vehicle, is offering 59,000 won in cash a share for the 14.1 million shares it does not already own, the remaining 38.83 percent of the South Korean car rental company, in an offer running from November 6 to December 3 with settlement on December 7, no minimum acceptance threshold, and a stated intention to make Lotte Rental a wholly owned subsidiary and pursue a voluntary delisting.

Lexicon built its controlling stake only a week ago, acquiring 22.2 million shares, 61.17 percent, from Hotel Lotte and Busan Lotte Hotel for 1.31 trillion won on October 30, and a full take-up of the tender would lift TPG's total investment to about 2.14 trillion won. The offer price is an 11.5 percent premium to Lotte Rental's November 5 close of 52,900 won, and the shares rose 10.4 percent in early Friday trade to a 52-week high of 58,400 won, against a Kospi that fell sharply the same morning.
