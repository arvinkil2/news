## Latest

Mayor Zohran Mamdani said a 5-year-old boy was sitting in the back seat of the car when ICE agents fired on the driver, who was shot in the neck and hospitalized; the child was unhurt. Mamdani called President Trump to demand an end to all ICE operations in New York City and called for the agency to be abolished, while Governor Kathy Hochul demanded a full, transparent federal accounting of the incident, the first ICE shooting in the city's history. A New York Post video showed the wounded man seated on the ground beside a gray SUV, a federal agent nearby with a rifle, and several bullet holes in the vehicle including one in the windshield; the Post also reported, unconfirmed, that agents were checking whether the man is a gang member linked to a 2025 shooting of an off-duty federal agent, and his nationality had not been disclosed. ICE did not respond to Le Monde's request for comment.

A person was shot during an incident involving federal law enforcement agents in the Marble Hill neighborhood of the Bronx around 4 p.m. Thursday, the New York Police Department said in a statement on X. The person was taken to a nearby hospital, conscious and receiving medical attention.

The NYPD said its officers were not involved in the incident and were not at the scene when the shooting occurred. Bloomberg identified the federal personnel as ICE agents; no federal agency has yet issued a statement on the shooting.
