Latest: Iranian state television suggested that explosions heard in southern Iran on Saturday evening, including on Qeshm Island in the strait, were related to Iranian military activities rather than US strikes, and said officials had not confirmed any missile strikes on Iranian territory, Reuters reported. The semi-official Tasnim news agency said two missiles had struck Qeshm Island, with no official information on possible damage, and neither Iran nor the United States has commented on that report. Reuters said it was not able to confirm any of the reported strikes on vessels. Qatar, which is mediating talks to end the conflict, on Saturday denied reports that the United States and Iran had failed to reach a deal, and its prime minister affirmed that both sides were continuing to work toward an agreement.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said in a statement on Sunday that three non-compliant oil tankers had exploded and caught fire in the early hours of the morning while sailing in what they called the illegal channel in the Strait of Hormuz, AFP reported. The Guards did not name the tankers or state their nationalities.

Britain's UKMTO agency separately reported that a tanker had caught fire off Khasab, Oman, a day earlier, with its crew evacuated, and that another tanker was struck by an unknown projectile on Saturday, while Emirati state media said 22 people had been rescued from a burning vessel in the Gulf of Oman and were in stable condition. AFP said it was unable to confirm whether those reports and the Guards statement referred to the same incidents.
