AMD reported third quarter revenue of $13.38 billion, up 45 percent from $9.25 billion a year earlier and above the $13.09 billion consensus estimate, with adjusted earnings of $2.09 per share against $1.95 expected. Data center revenue nearly doubled from a year earlier, and the segment now accounts for 64 percent of total revenue, up from 47 percent a year ago. The shares rose 7 percent in extended trading.

For the fourth quarter AMD guided to about $17 billion in revenue, above the $16.2 billion average analyst estimate, with gross margin of about 55 percent. The PC and gaming segment fell 8 percent to $3.7 billion, with gaming revenue down 40 percent, leaving the result carried almost entirely by AI data center demand.
