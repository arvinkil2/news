**Global and Alight Media.** The Competition and Markets Authority has issued an invitation to comment on the anticipated acquisition by Global Media and Entertainment Limited of Radiant Holdco Limited, which trades as Alight Media. The CMA has received the information needed to begin pre-notification but has not yet launched a formal phase 1 investigation. Comments are invited from November 25 to December 10, 2026.

**Empire AI, Buffalo.** Gilbane has broken ground on the Empire AI supercomputing centre at the University at Buffalo in Amherst, New York, announced by Governor Kathy Hochul on November 19. The facility has a 74.5 million dollar construction budget, will initially use up to five megawatts, and targets substantial completion in late 2027 with operations in 2028. It is exempt from the state's data center moratorium as a research and education facility, will run on hydropower, and will recover heat from computing to warm campus buildings.

**Union Pacific and Norfolk Southern.** J.B. Hunt's November 18 filing urging the Surface Transportation Board to reject the merger has been merged into the existing November 22 story on the comment round, which was updated in place today rather than duplicated.
