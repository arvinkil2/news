The new group, known as the Front pour la Refondation de la Nation, will be headed by Kabila, with rebel leaders Corneille Nangaa, Bertrand Bisimwa, Freddy Kaniki and Sultani Makenga as deputies, adviser Claude Ibalanky announced at a press conference, and Jeune Afrique reported the AFC/M23 itself dissolved into the new front. Congolese authorities have accused Kabila for months of supporting the AFC/M23, which captured large areas of eastern Congo in an offensive last year, and he had denied involvement.

Kabila, who travelled to rebel held Goma in 2025, was sentenced to death in absentia by a military court after being convicted of treason, war crimes and crimes against humanity over the alleged support; he has denied the accusations and called the case politically motivated. In a speech on Friday he accused the government of corruption and poor governance and criticised its minerals deal with the United States, while the United Nations and Western governments accuse Rwanda of backing M23, which Rwanda denies.
