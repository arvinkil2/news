Taiwan announced on Friday, November 6 that it will close its representation office in Papua New Guinea and withdraw all personnel posted there, and attributed the decision to pressure from China on the South Pacific country. Its foreign ministry said the office would close proactively, that bilateral cooperation relations would be reviewed and adjusted, and that the resources involved would in future go to friendly countries and other regions with cooperation potential.

The announcement follows months of dispute that began when Papua New Guinea decided in July to close Taiwan's office in Port Moresby, a step Taipei rejected at the time. Papua New Guinea maintains official diplomatic relations with China, as most countries do, but had allowed Taiwan to keep a representation office until now.
