A federal judge in Washington on Tuesday converted his emergency order restoring White House access for CNN, MS NOW and Politico into a preliminary injunction, so the ban President Trump announced on September 18 cannot be enforced while the outlets' lawsuit proceeds, unless an appeals court intervenes. Judge Timothy Kelly, whom Trump appointed in 2017, wrote that the president's own remarks showed the outlets were barred because he considered their reporting biased and inaccurate, which the judge called unlawful viewpoint discrimination, and he rejected the Justice Department's argument that national security motivated the decision, finding no evidence it played a role from the start.

Trump announced the ban on social media in September, saying the three outlets reported fiction and lies, and their reporters' hard passes were deactivated the next day. The outlets sued on First Amendment and due process grounds, and Kelly's September 24 emergency order had already forced the White House to restore access for dozens of reporters and staff; that order was due to expire on Tuesday.
