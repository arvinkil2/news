BYD confirmed that it has entered exclusive negotiations to acquire Ineos Automotive's plant at Hambach in France, a site of about 1,000 employees that builds about 30,000 Grenadiers a year, Electrek reported. Neither side disclosed a price or a closing date, the sale requires consultation with employee representatives, and BYD gave no commitment on how many employees it would keep or which models it would build there.

Alongside the talks, Ineos said it plans to move Grenadier and Quartermaster production to the United States, which accounts for about 70 percent of its sales, with United States production aimed to start in 2028 at a site not yet named, and it would keep building both models at Hambach for the medium term if the sale goes through.
