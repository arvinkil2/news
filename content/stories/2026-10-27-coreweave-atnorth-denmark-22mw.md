CoreWeave has brought the majority of its planned 22.5 megawatts of IT capacity online at atNorth's DEN01 data centre in Ballerup, in greater Copenhagen, its first deployment in Denmark. The liquid cooled facility offers 30 megawatts across 27,730 square metres, densities of 100 kilowatts per rack, and a heat reuse agreement to supply surplus heat to the area's district heating network.

Alongside the lease, CoreWeave said it is extending its global AI network backbone with new points of presence in the United Arab Emirates, India, Indonesia, Singapore, Australia, Korea and Japan, going live from later in the fourth quarter of 2026 to a full loop in mid-2027, with multiple 400 gigabit per second connections at each point. The company said it operated 51 data centres with more than 1.5 gigawatts of active power as of June 2026, with about 4.2 gigawatts contracted as of its August earnings call.
