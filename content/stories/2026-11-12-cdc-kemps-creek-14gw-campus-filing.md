CDC this month filed an updated application with the New South Wales government for its Kemps Creek site in Sydney, expanding the project from the single four-story, 144 megawatt building proposed in August to six two-story buildings, KC1 to KC6, totalling 1.4 gigawatts plus an on-site substation, across 117.5 hectares at 859-901 Mamre Road, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The first three buildings are set to offer 168 megawatts across five data halls, with the remaining three offering 144, 120 and 240 megawatts. CDC, founded in 2007 and backed by Infratil, has nearly 20 sites in operation and development across Sydney, Canberra, Melbourne and Auckland.
