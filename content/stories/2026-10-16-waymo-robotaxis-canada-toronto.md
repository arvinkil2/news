Waymo, Alphabet's self-driving unit, said on Friday it will begin manually driven mapping in Toronto after receiving approval to take part in Ontario's automated vehicle pilot program. Testing with a human safety operator will follow, before any public robotaxi service.

Toronto-based Element Fleet Management will manage the local fleet, in line with Waymo's recent pattern of using third-party operators in new cities. No launch date for paid rides was given.
