AirTrunk has signed a memorandum of understanding with Andhra Pradesh chief minister Nara Chandrababu Naidu for two proposed data center campuses in Anakapalli district, outside Visakhapatnam: 400 megawatts at Rambilli and 1.4 gigawatts at Tootipala. The company, acquired by Blackstone and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board for $16.1 billion in 2024, said full details of either project have not been shared, and it gave no investment figure or timeline. AirTrunk entered India earlier this year by acquiring Lumina CloudInfra and already lists projects around Mumbai, Chennai and Hyderabad.
