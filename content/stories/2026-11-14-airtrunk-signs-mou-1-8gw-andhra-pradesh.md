**Latest.** The proposal now has a price attached. AirTrunk has submitted a detailed project report to the Andhra Pradesh government for the two campuses, putting the proposed investment at about 1.03 trillion rupees, about $11 billion, for combined capacity of as much as 1.8 gigawatts, according to an emailed statement from the company reported by Bloomberg. The proposal remains under consideration by state authorities, and the company said the campuses would use low-water, air-cooled technology and industrial water supplies rather than municipal sources. A filed project report is the document a developer submits to seek land, power and approvals; it is not evidence of approved or funded capital, and no anchor tenants or construction timetable have been disclosed.

AirTrunk has signed a memorandum of understanding with Andhra Pradesh chief minister Nara Chandrababu Naidu for two proposed data center campuses in Anakapalli district, outside Visakhapatnam: 400 megawatts at Rambilli and 1.4 gigawatts at Tootipala. The company, acquired by Blackstone and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board for $16.1 billion in 2024, said full details of either project have not been shared, and it gave no investment figure or timeline. AirTrunk entered India earlier this year by acquiring Lumina CloudInfra and already lists projects around Mumbai, Chennai and Hyderabad.
