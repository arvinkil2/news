Vietnam has made very strong commitments in its trade negotiations with the United States and has essentially concluded them, Prime Minister Le Minh Hung said in Hanoi on Friday. Both sides are finalising technical details for the signing of the agreement, he said, and the applicable tax rates will be significantly lower than the maximum levels recently announced by the US.

No rate has been published yet, and the prime minister gave no signing date. The statement is the clearest signal yet from Hanoi that a reciprocal trade agreement is close, after months in which Vietnamese exporters faced the prospect of the maximum US tariff levels.
