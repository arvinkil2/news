**Europe's critical technology funding reaches almost 24 billion euros.** Almost 24 billion euros has been mobilised and 614 projects awarded in support of critical technologies in Europe, according to a European Commission report published Thursday. The tally is the Commission's own scale marker for the bloc's effort to fund strategic technologies domestically.

**GAO finds savings in Commerce Department offices.** The US Government Accountability Office found the Department of Commerce could save tens of millions of dollars by consolidating underused office space, in a federal real property report published Thursday. The finding adds federal real estate to the savings targets the watchdog has been cataloguing across agencies.
