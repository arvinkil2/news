Syrian Petroleum Co. plans to more than double crude production to about 250,000 barrels a day by the end of 2027 from about 110,000 now, investing in existing fields, CEO Yousef Qiblawy said in an interview published October 7. The state company also aims to ramp up oil refining as foreign operators return to the country, with further expansion possible by the end of the decade if exploration efforts succeed.
