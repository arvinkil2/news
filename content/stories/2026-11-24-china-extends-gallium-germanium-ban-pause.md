China's Ministry of Commerce announced on Tuesday that clause 2 of its Announcement No. 46 of 2024 will stay suspended from November 28, 2026 to January 27, 2027. That clause bars, in principle, the licensing of gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard material dual-use exports to the United States and imposes stricter end-user and end-use review on US-bound graphite, and its previous suspension, under Announcement No. 72 of 2025, was due to end on November 27.

The move keeps US-bound shipments of the four materials under ordinary licensing for two more months, past the January 10 expiry of the separate suspension covering the October 2025 rare earth package. MLex notes that the ban on dual-use exports to US military users or for military purposes, set out elsewhere in the 2024 announcement, remains in force.
