The Competition and Markets Authority issued its final determination on Monday in the appeal brought by Gwynt y Môr Offshore Wind Farm Limited, RWE Renewables UK Limited and RWE Renewables UK Swindon Limited, ordering that the appeal not be allowed and that the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority decision of May 1, 2026 be confirmed, with costs reserved. The appeal challenged GEMA's rejection of CMP344, a proposal to amend the Connection and Use of System Code so that allowed revenue increases reflecting income adjusting events would be recovered under the Transmission Demand Residual Tariff only, and thus not passed on to generators. Ofgem, whose governing body is GEMA, had rejected the proposed modification.
