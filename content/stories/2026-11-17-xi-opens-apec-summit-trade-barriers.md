**Latest.** Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Wednesday, beginning talks a day ahead of Thursday's trilateral summit with Donald Trump on the sidelines of the APEC meeting, AFP reported; Chinese and Russian news agencies reported the meeting around noon on Wednesday, the fifth between the two leaders this year including one by video link. Russian officials said the two were expected to discuss energy and finance, and then, in a smaller format, foreign policy including Ukraine and North Korea. Addressing the APEC gathering, Putin said he expected the trilateral to be highly useful, calling for unity rather than division and dialogue rather than ultimatums, and said Western sanctions on Russia had failed. China said only that the three leaders would hold in-depth exchanges on safeguarding world peace and promoting strategic stability in major-power relations; Washington has said Ukraine, the Iran war and nuclear disarmament are also up for discussion on Thursday. Trump, still in the Philippines on Wednesday, said he was eyeing one-on-one talks with both Putin and North Korean leader Kim Jong Un at his next stop in Shenzhen, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio stopped short of confirming a Kim meeting.

Chinese President Xi Jinping opened the APEC meeting in Shenzhen on Wednesday with a call to remove trade and investment barriers and foster an open Asia-Pacific economy, speaking at the opening ceremony of the APEC CEO Summit. Xi said China would not only cut tariffs and expand market access but go a step further, reforming rules and regulations to give foreign businesses full manufacturing access, and that China is expanding imports of quality products and services. No specific tariff rates or dates were given. The speech comes ahead of Xi's meetings this week with US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin alongside the summit.
