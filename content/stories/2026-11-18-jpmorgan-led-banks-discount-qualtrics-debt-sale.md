Banks led by JPMorgan Chase are meeting investors to sell nearly $2 billion of debt for software firm Qualtrics International in the high-yield bond market, with initial indications of a coupon of at least 8 percent and a price of about 85 cents on the dollar, Bloomberg reported. The sale is part of about $5 billion in financing for Qualtrics's $6.75 billion acquisition of Press Ganey Forsta that 11 banks committed and then had to fund themselves in March, after a software selloff driven by AI disruption fears stalled the original sale and left the group facing paper losses put at more than $500 million at the time.

Qualtrics told lenders its third quarter revenue slipped to $711 million from $718 million in the second quarter, with growth slowing to 2 percent from a year ago, and the banks waited for those results before reviving the deal, Bloomberg said. A sale could price before the US Thanksgiving holiday, with more of the debt to be offered later to leveraged loan investors.
