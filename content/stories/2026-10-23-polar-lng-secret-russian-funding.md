Polar LNG planned to buy liquefaction technology from Russia's Novatek, tap the large natural gas supply in northern Alaska, liquefy it and export it abroad, The New York Times reported. The plan was discussed last month by President Vladimir Putin with President Donald Trump's peace envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, as one of a series of energy deals US officials see as a route to smoother relations with Moscow and an eventual end to the war in Ukraine, the report said.

The funding did not match that American framing. Joel Riddle, Polar LNG's chief executive until he resigned in September, said he had been misled about the sources of the start-up funding, which ostensibly came from a company in the United Arab Emirates, while the Times reported that the money in fact came secretly from the Russian company that was to provide the technology.
