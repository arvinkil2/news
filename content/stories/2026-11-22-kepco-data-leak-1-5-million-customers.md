Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) said on Sunday that it had confirmed a leak of personal information affecting more than 1.5 million people across two systems: 650,487 users of its HanJeonON customer platform, whose leaked items included resident registration numbers, addresses, bank account numbers and card numbers, and about 870,000 people in its electronic procurement system, including company representatives and employees. KEPCO said it became aware of the leak on November 20, blocked external access and suspended the affected services, and is investigating with the National Intelligence Service, the environment ministry's Cyber Safety Center and outside experts while notifying those affected.
