Google announced a 10 million dollar Energy Impact Fund for Oregon on November 19, tied to the 20th anniversary of its first data center in The Dalles. Nine million dollars goes to Energy Trust of Oregon for energy efficiency improvements in homes and schools in The Dalles and surrounding Wasco County, with the remaining 1 million to be distributed later. Google also announced a "20 for 20" program of twenty 20,000 dollar grants to local organizations through 2027. The announcement is a company release carried on Business Wire and on Google's own blog.

Exaion, the French AI cloud provider owned by MARA Holdings and EDF Pulse Ventures, has signed a four-year GPU-as-a-service contract valued at 79 million dollars with its first US customer, on 100 percent take-or-pay terms, supplying compute on Nvidia HGX B300 servers. The contract was disclosed in MARA's third-quarter shareholder letter on November 9 and was reported by Datacenter Dynamics on November 25. Exaion says prospective customers have expressed demand for more than 30 megawatts of capacity for delivery by the end of 2027; MARA describes the wider pipeline figures as indications of demand, not contracted business.

President Trump reportedly told the New York Post that pardoning Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang is "always an option," while saying he does not think it will be necessary. The remark was reported by Bloomberg and TechCrunch on November 24 and 25, citing the Post. It is a reported remark from a single original interview, not a policy statement, and no pardon process has been announced.

TSMC filed a Form 6-K on November 25 adjusting its second-quarter 2026 cash dividend to NT$7.00000137 per common share, from the NT$7.0 approved by its board on August 11, after the reclamation of shares from 2024 restricted stock awards changed the share count slightly. The total distribution is NT$181,526,590,469, payable January 7, 2027. The adjustment is arithmetical and has no material effect on holders.
