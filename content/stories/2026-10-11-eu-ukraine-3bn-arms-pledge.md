Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Sunday that European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen confirmed the EU will allocate about 3 billion euros in military support to Ukraine next week, writing on Telegram after their call, per Espreso. Zelenskyy said he thanked her for a recent 1.24 billion euro defense tranche that he said has already arrived in Ukraine. He also said Russia struck a power plant in Kyiv with a Shahed drone just before the call, blacking out districts of the capital, and later hit energy facilities in the Kharkiv region, all after Donald Trump's claimed energy ceasefire. The teams agreed to discuss next steps in detail, and Ukraine is preparing to open EU accession clusters with the rest to follow by year-end.
