Ethiopia has announced that a six-month interim government will take control of Tigray, after federal forces retook the northern region following an insurrection launched last month by the Tigray People's Liberation Front. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed said five officials, including the mayor of the capital Mekelle, will run the state under directives from the federal government, and pledged that free elections will follow.

The TPLF, whose fighters surrendered in Mekelle in early October, has yet to respond to the announcement.
