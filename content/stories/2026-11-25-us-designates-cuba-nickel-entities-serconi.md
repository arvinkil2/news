The US Department of State has designated four Cuban state owned entities in the nickel sector under Executive Order 14404, according to a notice published in the Federal Register on Tuesday. The Secretary of State determined on September 17, 2026 that each entity operates in the metals and mining sector of the Cuban economy, and each has been added to the Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List, blocking all property and interests in property subject to US jurisdiction.

The four entities are all based in Holguin province, the centre of Cuba's nickel industry. They are Empresa de Servicios Tecnicos de Computacion Comunicaciones y Electronica, known as SERCONI, of Moa, established September 30, 1996; Empresa de Ingenieria y Proyectos del Niquel, known as CEPRONIQUEL, of Moa, established May 31, 2001; Centro de Investigaciones del Niquel Capitan Alberto Fernandez Montes de Oca, known as CEDINIQ, of Moa, established December 31, 1986; and Pinares S.A., of Nicaro, established January 31, 2001. The notice classifies each as a state owned enterprise whose type is support activities for other mining and quarrying.

The designations continue the build out of the Cuba programme under Executive Order 14404 sector by sector. The State Department designated the state bank BICSA for operating in Cuba's financial services sector in an action published the same day, and a companion notice published Tuesday covers a further set of energy, mining and banking entities and one individual. Nickel is among Cuba's principal export earners, and the Moa complex named in the notice is the industry's hub.
