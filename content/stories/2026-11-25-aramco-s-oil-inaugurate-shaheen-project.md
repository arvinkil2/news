Aramco and S-OIL inaugurated the $7 billion Shaheen Project at a ceremony in Seoul on Wednesday, coinciding with S-OIL's 50th anniversary, Aramco said in its own release. The Ulsan complex, integrated with S-OIL's existing refining and petrochemical facilities, features the world's largest single steam cracker and the first major commercial deployment of Aramco's thermal crude-to-chemicals technology, and is expected to add about 3.2 million tons of annual petrochemical production capacity, raising petrochemicals from 12 percent to 25 percent of S-OIL's overall portfolio. Commercial operations are scheduled to begin in 2027 after commissioning; Aramco holds more than 63 percent of S-OIL and called Shaheen its largest ever investment in Korea.
