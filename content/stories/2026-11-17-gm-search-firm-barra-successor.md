General Motors is working with an executive search firm to identify potential external candidates to succeed Mary Barra as chief executive, the Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday, citing people familiar with the matter, while the company also considers several internal candidates. Barra, 64, has led GM since 2014, no successor has been publicly named and no timeline for a transition was reported, and GM declined to comment. The Journal's report is the first indication that the search has moved beyond internal planning, and any successor inherits the task of funding GM's electric vehicle programme from the margins of its profitable pickup trucks and SUVs against lower cost Chinese competition.
