Ardian has raised 1 billion euros for Ardian Semiconductor, a private equity platform dedicated to the semiconductor value chain, the Paris based firm announced on November 9. Close to 700 million euros of the total is fund commitments, with the balance in co-investments alongside the fund. Reuters put the total at about 1.12 billion dollars.

The investor base is the story inside the raise. Investors from the Middle East and North Africa supplied 41 percent of the capital and French investors 24 percent, and 58 percent of commitments came from investors new to Ardian. The limited partners include government agencies, sovereign wealth funds, semiconductor companies, insurers and private wealth clients. The fund has been selected under France's Tibi initiative and is a member of the French Ministry of Armed Forces' Defense Investor Club.

Ardian runs the strategy with Silian Partners, a team of former senior semiconductor executives, and has research partnerships including with CEA-Leti. It targets scalable mid-market companies in specialty chips such as power, analog and photonics, and in the intellectual property, software, equipment, materials and consumables used to design, make, package and test chips, mainly in Europe and also in North America and Asia.

Four investments are already done: VSORA, a French designer of AI inference accelerators for data centers; centrotherm, a German maker of thermal processing equipment including for silicon carbide power devices; Synergie CAD, a French test interface company; and Ion Beam Services, a French ion implantation equipment and services firm.

Ardian's case rests on the breadth of AI driven chip demand. It cites Semiconductor Industry Association data showing global semiconductor sales above 1 trillion dollars in the first eight months of 2026, a level not previously expected before 2030, with the market forecast at about 1.6 trillion dollars in 2026 and about 2.5 trillion dollars by 2030.
