A large fire broke out on Saturday at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright in Alberta, one of the country's busiest military training bases, killing and injuring several people, Defence Minister David McGuinty said, describing the situation as very active and delicate without confirming a cause or a casualty count. Global News reported that at least seven people were killed and dozens more injured when a gas line or gas well was hit during a live-fire training exercise, citing unnamed military sources, while the New York Times put the death toll at four with dozens injured.

The Royal Alexandra Hospital in Edmonton declared a code orange, its disaster protocol for a high-casualty incident, and the municipal district said high winds, gusting to 100 kilometres an hour, spread the fire rapidly through the base fences before crews contained it. Prime Minister Mark Carney called it a serious incident creating a major fire, Alberta Premier Danielle Smith called the fire significant and tragic, and Saskatchewan and British Columbia offered resources and assistance.
