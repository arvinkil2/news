A group led by Exiro Minerals Corp., alongside Orion Resource Partners and the Canada Growth Fund, has taken control of Vale Base Metals' Thompson nickel operations in northern Manitoba, holding 81.1 percent of a newly formed company, Exiro Nickel, while Vale retains 18.9 percent. The complex covers two operating underground mines, an adjacent mill and exploration ground across the 135 kilometre Thompson Nickel Belt, and the group plans to invest up to 200 million dollars in its long-term future.

The transaction has closed, ending a strategic review Vale launched in January 2025. Vale has also signed a multi-year agreement to buy nickel concentrate produced at the Thompson mill, keeping a commercial link to the operation after giving up majority control.
