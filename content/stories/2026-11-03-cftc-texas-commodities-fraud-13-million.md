The Commodity Futures Trading Commission said the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas entered a default judgment against Walter Dunning Larrick III and Cambridge Financial Advisors LLC, ordering a 9,767,625 dollar civil monetary penalty and 3,255,875 dollars in restitution, with permanent trading and registration bans. The court found the defendants fraudulently solicited and misappropriated more than 3 million dollars from participants, falsely saying the funds would trade futures and options involving natural gas, oil and gold, and issuing false account statements to conceal the misconduct. The court also found participant funds were paid to existing participants in a manner akin to a Ponzi scheme, used for Larrick's personal expenses, and sent to offshore accounts in Costa Rica, and the judgment resolves the CFTC's 2023 complaint.
