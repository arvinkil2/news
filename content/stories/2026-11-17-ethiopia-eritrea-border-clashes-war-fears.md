Ethiopian government forces, backed by pro-government militia, have pushed Tigrayan forces toward the border with Eritrea after a few weeks of relative stability, Deutsche Welle reported, citing the ACLED conflict tracker. Skirmishes were reported from several locations, and Ethiopian forces fired heavy artillery toward dwellings close to the Eritrean border town of Badme, the flashpoint of the 1998 to 2000 border war.

Eritrea and Ethiopia allied against the Tigrayan forces in the 2020 to 2022 Tigray conflict, but Eritrea has since shifted toward the Tigrayan side and the two governments cut diplomatic ties in October. Analysts cited by Deutsche Welle said Addis Ababa's immediate aim may be to consolidate control of Tigray and cut supply lines from Eritrea, though direct conflict with Eritrea remains possible.
