President Trump wrote on Truth Social that massive oil, gas and mineral exploration is getting ready to begin in Greenland, saying experts believe the island's easily available oil could exceed that of almost any other land in the world, and casting Greenland as a defensive shield for the United States and Europe against future attack. He gave no details on who would explore, where, or when. The post follows the September 22 security agreement among the United States, Denmark and Greenland, which expanded the American military presence on the island and mentioned resource extraction and minerals cooperation without specifics, and the reports read carried no confirmation from Copenhagen or Nuuk.
