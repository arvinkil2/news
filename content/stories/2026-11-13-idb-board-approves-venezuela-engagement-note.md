The Inter-American Development Bank's board unanimously approved a country engagement note for Venezuela on Friday, Reuters reported, the bank's first formal roadmap for renewed operations in the country since it halted new lending in May 2018. The note, a shorter transitional form of the bank's country strategy, prioritises rehabilitating the electricity sector, social services for vulnerable groups and reconstruction after June's earthquakes, but it is not itself a loan and does not clear Venezuela's arrears, which stood at 2.3 billion dollars in overdue principal and interest as of September 30 and bar new lending until paid. Separately, Bloomberg reported that the US Treasury is weighing a short term bridge loan to help Venezuela clear those arrears, citing people familiar with the matter.
