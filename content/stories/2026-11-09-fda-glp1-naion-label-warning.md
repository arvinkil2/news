The FDA said it is requiring application holders to add information about the risk of non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION) to the Warnings and Precautions section of the labelling of all GLP-1 receptor agonists, after a comprehensive evaluation of clinical trial data, animal data and postmarketing data. Its meta-analysis of 126 trials covering 134,253 patients found 6.4 NAION events per 100,000 patient-years on GLP-1 drugs against 3.8 on comparators, and its Sentinel cohort study in adults with type 2 diabetes found a 43 percent higher risk than DPP-4 inhibitors and no statistically significant difference against SGLT-2 inhibitors. The agency said NAION cases have been reported for all currently marketed GLP-1 products, that its review of animal data found no mechanism for the risk, and that the condition is a common cause of sudden, painless vision loss that can leave permanent impairment.
