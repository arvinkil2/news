The European Medicines Agency's human medicines committee recommended marketing authorisations for Aumseqa (aumolertinib) and Zybrivo (sintilimab), both for non-small cell lung cancer, and for Baxfendy (baxdrostat) for hypertension that is not sufficiently controlled, alongside two insulin aspart biosimilars for diabetes and two generics. The committee also backed wider uses for 11 authorised medicines, including Gazyvaro for moderate to severe active systemic lupus erythematosus, and confirmed after re-examination its refusal of KemSu for acute pain in children and of Meplyffa for Niemann-Pick disease type C. Two applications, Amlypave for cardiac light chain amyloidosis and Qezzaqar for soft tissue sarcoma, were withdrawn.
