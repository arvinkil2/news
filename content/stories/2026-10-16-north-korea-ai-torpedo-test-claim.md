North Korea's Korean Central News Agency said on Saturday that defence science institutes test fired a new torpedo weapon system, the Jam-2.0, underwater on October 15, and that artificial intelligence has been introduced into its target search and guidance functions. The agency said a rocket propulsion unit and core propulsion technology, developed over 180 tests in five years, were proved in the firing, and South Korean experts quoted by Yonhap said the rocket propulsion claim and the high speed name suggest a supercavitating torpedo.

No specifications or photographs were released, Kim Jong Un did not attend, and the report ran only in the external facing KCNA, not in the domestic Rodong Sinmun. Yonhap noted the test follows an October 14 firing of two strategic cruise missiles from a submarine, part of a push to close the naval gap with South Korea.
