Magdalena Andersson reported back to Speaker Andreas Norlen on Tuesday and said the conditions are not in place to form a government, her second failed attempt since the September 13 election, in which her left bloc won 176 seats to 173 for the outgoing right bloc. The blockage is unchanged: the Center Party will not accept a government with Left Party ministers, and the Left Party will only back a government it sits in. Norlen said parliament will still vote on Andersson as prime minister on Thursday, a vote he concedes she will likely fail, after which he is expected to turn to caretaker prime minister Ulf Kristersson; the speaker has four attempts before a snap election is required.
