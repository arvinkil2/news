The Securities and Exchange Commission on Friday proposed rescinding Rule 613 of Regulation NMS, which obligates the national securities exchanges and associations to establish and maintain the Consolidated Audit Trail, and replacing it with new Rule 17a-26 under the Securities Exchange Act, requiring exchanges, associations and broker-dealers to record and electronically report substantially the same information to the Commission or its designee. The Commission said it intends to use funds appropriated by Congress to pay its costs of using, operating and maintaining the system, and that the change would bring the audit trail under its direct control with minimal disruption.

Chairman Paul Atkins said the proposal addresses persistent concerns with cost overruns, funding and governance, and encompasses changes already made, including exemptive relief and amendments that have cut the audit trail's projected annual operating costs by over $100 million and permanently eliminated the reporting of personally identifiable information. The public comment period will remain open for 60 days after publication in the Federal Register.
