Samsung SDI said on Monday that its Americas subsidiary, Samsung SDI America, has signed a contract to supply a total of 16 gigawatt-hours of lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries to Jupiter Power, the BlackRock-owned US energy storage developer and operator, over three years from 2029, taking the two companies' total contracted volume to about 27 gigawatt-hours; the company put the value only in the trillions of won, with no exact figure disclosed. The batteries will be produced at the StarPlus Energy plant in Indiana, where Samsung SDI has just begun LFP mass production, and the company presented itself as the only non-Chinese maker of prismatic storage batteries in North America with a materials supply chain secured outside China. Jupiter Power chief executive Andy Bowman said securing stable US-made equipment is central to the company's project planning and risk management.
