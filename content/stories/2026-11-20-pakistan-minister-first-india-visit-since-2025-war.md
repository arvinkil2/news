Pakistan's climate change minister Musadik Malik arrived in New Delhi on Friday to attend the first Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Civilisational Dialogue Forum, a three-day gathering that Prime Minister Narendra Modi was due to inaugurate on Friday evening and that Malik is expected to address on Saturday. No bilateral meeting has been announced: Pakistan's Foreign Office framed the trip as active SCO membership, and India's foreign ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said on October 28 that India's Pakistan policy "remains in place; there has been no change" since the April 2025 Pahalgam attack, in which gunmen killed 26 civilians and which India blames on Pakistan, a charge Islamabad denies. The visit is the first by a Pakistani minister since Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari travelled to Goa in May 2023, and analysts quoted in the report frame the open question as whether contact confined to multilateral forums could eventually bring Modi to the SCO leaders' summit Pakistan hosts in Islamabad in August 2027.
