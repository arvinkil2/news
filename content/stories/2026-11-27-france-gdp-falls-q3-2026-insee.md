France's economy contracted 0.2 percent in the third quarter of 2026, INSEE said in its detailed national accounts, after flat growth in the second quarter, and the figure was revised down 0.1 points from the first estimate. Exports fell sharply, down 1.6 percent after a 2.9 percent rise, making foreign trade a 0.6 point drag on growth, while gross fixed capital formation fell 0.9 percent, its third consecutive decline, led by construction and manufactured goods. Household consumption rose 0.3 percent and purchasing power per consumption unit rebounded 0.2 percent, but the general government borrowing requirement widened to 5.6 percent of GDP from 5.3 percent, and the 2026 growth overhang stands at 0.1 percent.
