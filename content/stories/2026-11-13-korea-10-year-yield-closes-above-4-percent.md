Korean treasury yields closed higher across the curve on Friday, tracking the rise in United States yields and foreign selling of treasury bond futures. The 3 year yield rose 3.0 basis points to 3.759 percent, the 5 year rose 3.8 basis points to 3.841 percent and the 10 year rose 6.4 basis points to 4.001 percent, its first close above 4 percent in ten days. The 3 year and 10 year yields are each up about 15 basis points on the week.

Yonhap said foreigners net sold 3,254 contracts of 10 year treasury futures on the day, and that the first 50 year auction held since the Fair Trade Commission fined dealers for collusion in treasury auctions drew a bid to cover ratio of 160 percent on 700 billion won, down from 246 percent last month.
