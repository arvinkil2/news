Optimum Communications, the cable operator formerly known as Altice USA, is nearing an agreement with creditors to cut its roughly $26 billion debt load by more than half and reduce leverage from about eight times earnings to about three times, Bloomberg reported on Tuesday, with lenders receiving at least 90 percent of the reorganized company's equity and existing shareholders including founder Patrick Drahi retaining the remainder. The company's CSC Holdings unit skipped about $102.5 million in scheduled interest payments on Monday, starting a 30 day grace period, a step Bloomberg reported was taken to finalize creditor terms ahead of more than $6 billion of debt maturing next year even though the company holds about $1.1 billion in cash. S&P Global Ratings has downgraded the company to CCC- and warned that a default, distressed exchange or bankruptcy filing appears likely within the next six months, and the amount of existing debt to be reinstated under the deal remains under negotiation, while a JPMorgan Chase financing facility would be left intact.
