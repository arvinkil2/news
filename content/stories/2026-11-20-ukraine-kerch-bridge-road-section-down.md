Ukraine carried out its fourth and most complex operation against the Kerch Bridge over three days of strikes this week, and its Security Service said Palyanytsia drones collapsed the roadway section on November 17 after a month spent destroying air defences along the approach. President Zelenskyy said Neptune and Flamingo missiles and Ruta drones struck the separate railway span, and Ukraine said rail service had been suspended, though it remains unclear whether the rail bridge was permanently disabled. President Putin dismissed the operation as a strike on a purely civilian facility and said Russia had not transported oil or petroleum products across it, while Ukraine's General Staff said the bridge was a vital artery supplying fuel, ammunition, equipment and personnel to Russian forces in Crimea.
