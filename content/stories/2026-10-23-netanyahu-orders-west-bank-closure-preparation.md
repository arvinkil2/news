**Latest:** Suspected Palestinian gunmen wounded five Israelis in a shooting at the Judea Junction checkpoint outside Hebron on Friday before Israeli troops killed both gunmen in a prolonged shootout, Reuters reported, with an AFP journalist at the scene witnessing the exchange of fire. Magen David Adom, Israel's emergency medical service, said two of the wounded were in critical condition, and the Israel Defence Forces said the attackers were carrying significant ammunition. Facing a tight election race on Tuesday, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu cancelled all political meetings, instructed the military to impose a cordon around Hebron and said he had told security forces to prepare for a full lockdown of the whole West Bank starting today, while National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir demanded the complete dismantling of the Palestinian Authority, though officials presented no evidence linking the Palestinian leadership to the shooting, and Hamas praised the attack as an exceptional heroic operation without explicitly claiming responsibility.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said on Friday he had instructed the military to impose a cordon on the city of Hebron and to prepare for a full closure of the entire occupied West Bank starting today, Deutsche Welle reported. Netanyahu said gunmen attacked Israeli soldiers on Friday morning at a checkpoint near Hebron and that soldiers killed two of the gunmen in a firefight, preventing what he called a major attack, and DW reported at least five people wounded, several critically, without naming a source for that figure. The move comes days before an Israeli election and amid rising settler violence in the West Bank, where DW said dozens of Palestinians have been killed since the start of the year.
