Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said on Friday he had instructed the military to impose a cordon on the city of Hebron and to prepare for a full closure of the entire occupied West Bank starting today, Deutsche Welle reported. Netanyahu said gunmen attacked Israeli soldiers on Friday morning at a checkpoint near Hebron and that soldiers killed two of the gunmen in a firefight, preventing what he called a major attack, and DW reported at least five people wounded, several critically, without naming a source for that figure. The move comes a week before an Israeli election and amid rising settler violence in the West Bank, where DW said dozens of Palestinians have been killed since the start of the year.
