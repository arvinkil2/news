An Israeli strike on a clinic operated by Medecins Sans Frontieres (MSF) in Gaza City killed two watchmen, bringing the medical charity's staff death toll in Gaza to 17, Al Jazeera reported. MSF called the strike a "direct hit," saying its logos were clearly marked and that Israel knew the clinic's location. Israel said both men were members of Hamas's military wing acting as security guards; neither side's account has been independently corroborated.
