Gaza's civil defense agency said an Israeli strike inside the Nasser Medical Complex, west of Khan Younis in southern Gaza, killed six people and wounded more than 20, and the hospital confirmed the six deaths in a statement. The agency's spokesperson, Mahmoud Bassal, gave the toll to AFP, and the bodies and wounded were recovered from a building at the complex.

Israel's military told AFP it was checking for information on the incident when asked for comment. No time for the strike was given in the report, and AFP named no independent verification of it.
