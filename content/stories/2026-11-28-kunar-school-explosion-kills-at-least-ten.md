An explosion at a building storing explosives for mining, beside a school in the Asadabad district of Afghanistan's northeastern Kunar province, killed at least 10 people and injured 110, a spokesman for the Taliban's Interior Ministry said. Children were rushed to Asadabad Provincial Hospital, and video from the aftermath showed bloodied children being pulled from cars at the hospital.

Interior Ministry spokesman Abdul Mateen Qani said early reports indicated the blast was caused by negligence and carelessness at an explosives depot belonging to mining companies near the school, in a province on the border with Pakistan that hosts significant mining operations. Authorities are investigating and will release more details after a government inquiry, he said.
