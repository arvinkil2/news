Russia launched Shahed drones at Kyiv and a string of other regions on Saturday night, with an air alert in the capital from late evening, explosions heard across the city and air defences engaging targets, NV.ua reported. Falling debris from a downed drone set a fire at an infrastructure facility in the Desnianskyi district, Kyiv city military administration head Tymur Tkachenko said.

The Ukrainian Air Force tracked drone groups moving on Kyiv from the north and east and on at least eight other regions through the night. No casualty figures had been reported in the coverage read as the attack continued.
