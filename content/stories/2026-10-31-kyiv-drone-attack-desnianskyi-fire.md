Russia attacked Ukraine with Shahed drones and guided aerial bombs on Saturday evening, with air alerts in Kyiv and a string of other regions and the Air Force tracking drone groups and bomb launches across the country, NV.ua reported. In Kyiv's Desnianskyi district a drone fell on a non-residential area and started a fire that also caught parked cars, the city military administration said; the State Emergency Service said the fire, 20 square metres in a parking lot by one of the markets, was extinguished, and that a second drone fell on open ground at a private house in Darnytskyi district. There were no dead and no injured, the service said.

A presidential technology adviser, Serhii Beskrestnov, said Russia was launching dozens of gasoline-powered Shaheds under the day's solid cloud cover, counting on the cloud making them harder for Ukrainian interceptor crews to detect.
