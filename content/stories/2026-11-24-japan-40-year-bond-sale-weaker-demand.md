Japan's Ministry of Finance sold 300 billion yen of 40-year government bonds on Wednesday at a bid-to-cover ratio of 2.63, down from 3.10 at the previous 40-year sale and below the 12-month average, Bloomberg reported. The highest accepted yield was 4.090 percent, slightly below the previous sale's 4.125 percent, at a price of 94.35.

The softer result lands while investors weigh the government's issuance plans for next year and the Bank of Japan's December policy meeting, where a rate increase is back under discussion.
