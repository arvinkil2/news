Kazakhstan's energy minister, Yerlan Akkenzhenov, announced the start of talks to build a second nuclear power plant in the country with China's National Nuclear Corporation, and said Astana is discussing the parameters and timeline for the plant at Moyinkum with the corporation. The first plant, at Balkhash, will be built with the Russian Rosatom consortium, and Akkenzhenov said the projects should be finished by 2035.

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev had earlier ordered that construction of the Balkhash plant begin next year and that an agreement be signed to accelerate construction of the Moyinkum plant. On Monday, Almasadam Satkaliyev, director of the Atomic Energy Agency, announced plans to create a state strategic uranium reserve to supply fuel to the future plants.
