Israel removed the British consulate sign in occupied East Jerusalem and announced that 20 British diplomats, including the consul general, would have to leave the country, retaliating against a new wave of UK sanctions, Al Jazeera reports. The British government says it will retain its presence in occupied East Jerusalem despite the closure order.
