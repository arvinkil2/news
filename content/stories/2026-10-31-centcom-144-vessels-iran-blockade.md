US Central Command said US forces had, as of October 31, redirected 144 commercial vessels sailing to or from Iranian ports since the naval blockade resumed on July 14, Interfax reported. CENTCOM said more than 3,000 commercial vessels, more than 95 percent of all transits, passed through the Strait of Hormuz in the past six months despite Revolutionary Guard threats and attacks, and that US forces have helped Gulf partners export more than 1.5 billion barrels of oil since the summer. In the earlier blockade phase, from April 13 to June 18, CENTCOM redirected 142 vessels, disabled nine that did not comply and let more than 50 humanitarian aid vessels through, so the current phase has now matched that phase's redirected total.
