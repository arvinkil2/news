The cost of overnight borrowing in Singapore's interbank market rose 11.7 basis points to 1.7446 percent as of Friday, the highest level since July 2025, according to data Bloomberg cited as published on Tuesday. Bloomberg attributed the climb to tighter local liquidity and expectations for further US interest rate hikes. Local markets were shut on Monday for a holiday.
