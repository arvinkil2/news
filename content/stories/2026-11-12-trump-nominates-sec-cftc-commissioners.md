The White House notice names Steven Levine of Maryland for the SEC seat last held by Hester Peirce, for a term expiring June 5, 2030, and Kristin Johnson of Michigan for the seat last held by Caroline Crenshaw, for a term expiring June 5, 2029. For the CFTC it names Nathan Anonick of Virginia for a term expiring June 19, 2031, in the seat last held by Rostin Behnam, and Matthew Rowland of the District of Columbia for the remainder of the term expiring April 13, 2028, in the seat last held by Summer Mersinger.

Bloomberg Law reported the slate is bipartisan: Levine, a Republican who has served as a senior official on the White House Council of Economic Advisers, and Johnson, a Democrat and former CFTC commissioner, for the SEC; Anonick, a Republican and senior counsel on the Senate Agriculture Committee, and Rowland, a Democrat who also serves on that panel's staff, for the CFTC. CoinDesk noted the CFTC is currently operating with Chairman Mike Selig as its sole commissioner, and that the nominations are the first of their kind since 2025, with the Senate in its lame duck session.
