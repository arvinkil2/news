OP Gold increased its proposed additional payment to the state to 2.5 percent from 1.5 percent after Freeport-McMoRan countered at 2 percent, exercising its right as the original bidder to improve its proposal, according to the National Mining Agency's final evaluation. The Cesar block was one of eight copper, gold and polymetallic contracts signed on October 9 with five companies, and OP Gold also secured a second block in the round. Zijin Mining took two blocks at Buriticá in Antioquia without a counteroffer, and Copper Giant Resources said on Monday that it had secured the 16 square kilometre Chaparral block in Tolima, next to its Mocoa copper project.
