Israel's Channel 13 reported on Thursday that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu convened a security meeting the previous day and told it that military action against Iran is "inevitable". A senior Israeli officer told the channel that the Israel Defense Forces and US Central Command have a jointly established and approved operation plan, and that the only remaining issue is timing, awaiting President Trump's decision. Separately, the Washington Post reported, citing four sources, that Netanyahu instructed Mossad director Roman Gofman to prepare a secret operation against Iran without informing the cabinet or the IDF chief of staff, after Gofman lobbied White House officials in Washington last week for a joint airstrike.

The same reporting says IDF chief of staff Eyal Zamir opposed Netanyahu's attack plan, and one Israeli security official denied that an operation plan was being prepared without the IDF's knowledge. What is confirmed by the IDF itself is narrower: Central Command commander Brad Cooper visited Israel this week and held a joint situation assessment with Zamir and senior commanders from both countries, which the IDF said reviewed the regional situation and deepening joint readiness.
