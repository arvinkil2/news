**Bharat Semi lands 24 million dollar seed.** Indian chip startup Bharat Semi has landed a 24 million dollar seed round as it eyes the global AI sensor market, TechCrunch reported. The raise is one of the larger seed rounds for an Indian semiconductor design startup and lands as India pushes fabless design under its semiconductor mission.

**Mount Thor raises 45 million dollars.** Mount Thor has raised 45 million dollars to fund a new AI cloud offering built on Apple chips and software, Data Center Dynamics reported, an unusual hardware bet in a market standardised on Nvidia accelerators.

**Plural raises 1.1 billion dollar fund.** Plural, a backer of defence technology company Helsing, has raised 1.1 billion dollars to fund Europe's next generation of technology companies, TechCrunch reported. The fund is among the largest European venture raises of the year and continues the flow of capital into European defence and dual use technology.
