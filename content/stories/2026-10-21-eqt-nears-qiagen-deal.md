EQT is nearing a 9.6 billion euro deal to acquire Qiagen, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday. Reuters, reporting Bloomberg's earlier account on Tuesday, said EQT had submitted a binding offer of more than 50 dollars a share, KKR had also made a bid, and Qiagen's board is meeting this week to decide, with no certainty the discussions will lead to a sale.

Qiagen, which develops molecular testing and sample preparation technologies used by research and clinical laboratories, had a market capitalisation of 8.57 billion euros in LSEG data cited by Reuters, and its US listed shares rose more than 6 percent on Tuesday. Qiagen said it does not comment on market rumours or speculation.
