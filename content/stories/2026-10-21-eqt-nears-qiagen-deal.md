**Latest:** Qiagen has signed a business combination agreement with EQT Private Equity, the company announced on Wednesday evening. Crown NL Bidco, a holding company indirectly controlled by the EQT XI fund with co-investments from Luxinva, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, and CPP Investment Board Europe, will make a voluntary public tender offer of 46.50 euros a share in cash for all Qiagen shares. The offer values Qiagen at about 10.8 billion euros in enterprise value, about 12.1 billion dollars including about 1.1 billion dollars of net debt, and stands at a 40.3 percent premium to the unaffected share price on July 8, 2026, the last trading day before reports of takeover interest, and 46.9 percent above the three-month volume-weighted average price. Qiagen's supervisory and managing boards unanimously approved the agreement and will recommend that shareholders tender, subject to review of the final offer document. The offer carries a minimum acceptance threshold of 50 percent of the share capital plus one share, needs merger control and foreign investment clearances, and is expected to close in mid-2027, after which Qiagen's Frankfurt and New York listings would end. Qiagen also reported preliminary third-quarter net sales of 548 million dollars and adjusted diluted earnings of 0.65 dollars a share, and suspended its full-year 2026 outlook while the transaction is pending.

Earlier on Wednesday, Bloomberg reported EQT was nearing a deal, and Reuters, reporting Bloomberg's account on Tuesday, said EQT had submitted a binding offer of more than 50 dollars a share and KKR had also made a bid. Qiagen, which develops molecular testing and sample preparation technologies used by research and clinical laboratories, had a market capitalisation of 8.57 billion euros in LSEG data cited by Reuters, and its US listed shares rose more than 6 percent on Tuesday.
