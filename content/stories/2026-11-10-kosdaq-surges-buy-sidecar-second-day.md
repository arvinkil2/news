**Latest.** The Kosdaq closed up 37.49 points, or 5.62 percent, at 705.10 on Wednesday, November 11, recovering the 700 level, and the Kospi closed up 136.23 points, or 2.60 percent, at 5,375.36, Yonhap News Agency reported. Yonhap said the rise came despite heightened concern about a possible large, or big-step, US benchmark rate hike, and was helped by gains in large semiconductor shares; the Kospi had opened down 29.42 points, or 0.56 percent, at 5,209.71 and reached 5,377.99 late in the session.

South Korea's Kosdaq index surged earlier on Wednesday, rising 4.75 percent to 699.34 by 11:32 a.m. in Seoul and then climbing more than 5 percent to recover the 700 level, Yonhap reported. The Kospi was up 1.19 percent at 5,301.46 at the same time.

The Korea Exchange triggered a buy sidecar in the Kosdaq market at 11:28 a.m., suspending program buy orders for five minutes after the Kosdaq 150 futures price rose 6.06 percent and the Kosdaq 150 spot index rose 6.19 percent. It was the second consecutive session with a Kosdaq buy sidecar, after one also sounded on Tuesday.
