South Korea's Kosdaq index surged on Wednesday, November 11, rising 4.75 percent to 699.34 by 11:32 a.m. in Seoul and then climbing more than 5 percent to recover the 700 level, Yonhap News Agency reported. The Kospi was up 1.19 percent at 5,301.46 at the same time.

The Korea Exchange triggered a buy sidecar in the Kosdaq market at 11:28 a.m., suspending program buy orders for five minutes after the Kosdaq 150 futures price rose 6.06 percent and the Kosdaq 150 spot index rose 6.19 percent. It was the second consecutive session with a Kosdaq buy sidecar, after one also sounded on Tuesday.
