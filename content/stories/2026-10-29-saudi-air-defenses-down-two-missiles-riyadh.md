Saudi air defenses shot down two ballistic missiles fired by the Houthi militia in Yemen toward Riyadh on Thursday, and the Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen said the missiles were "intercepted and destroyed," Arab News reported. Civil defense had issued danger warnings for Riyadh, Najran and Al-Kharj before announcing the danger had passed about 20 minutes later, and the report carried no casualties or damage from Thursday's missiles, two days after debris from two intercepted missiles damaged a building, a construction project and a vehicle in Riyadh. Separately, a Yemeni government military spokesman said government forces had launched 982 strikes against Houthi targets in 24 hours across Taiz, Al-Jawf, Marib and Al-Bayda, a figure no independent source has corroborated.
