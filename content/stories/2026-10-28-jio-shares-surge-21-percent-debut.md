Jio Platforms shares opened at 1,358 rupees on Wednesday, up 21 percent from their issue price of 1,119 rupees, after the company raised 302 billion rupees (3.2 billion dollars) in India's largest ever initial public offering. The debut valued Jio at about 12.5 trillion rupees (131.9 billion dollars), India's second most valuable listed company after its parent Reliance Industries, and the offering drew bids for 22.19 times the shares on sale from more than 13.8 million applicants, according to exchange data reported by TechCrunch.

CNBC reported the shares last trading at 1,357 rupees and Nomura opening coverage with a buy rating and a target price of 1,340 rupees. Jio plans to use up to 275 billion rupees of the proceeds to repay debt at its telecom subsidiary Reliance Jio Infocomm, with the remainder for general corporate purposes.
