Digital Nasional Berhad, Malaysia's state-owned 5G wholesale network operator, has secured $1.27 billion in funding for the next phase of the national 5G rollout, Data Center Dynamics reported October 6. Details on the funding structure, lenders and the coverage targets attached to the money were not in the initial report.

The financing matters because Malaysia's 5G model is unusual: a state-owned wholesale network that carriers ride on rather than building competing infrastructure, a structure that has been politically contested for years. One point two seven billion dollars is a substantial commitment to the next phase regardless of the model's critics. For the regional technology picture, the timing coincides with Malaysia's rise as a data-center destination, where reliable high-capacity connectivity is part of the pitch. The figure is single-sourced; treat the exact funding terms as unconfirmed until a second outlet or the company confirms them.
