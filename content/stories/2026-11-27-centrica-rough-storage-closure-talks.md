Centrica, the owner of British Gas, is seeking urgent talks with the government over the future of Rough, its largest gas storage facility, off the Yorkshire coast. In a memo to the site's roughly 400 workers, including contractors, chief executive Chris O'Shea said he would seek talks with ministers, trade unions and the prime minister, and that no decisions on jobs had been made. The government has rebuffed Centrica's request for short-term support to run Rough through the winter, and its response to a consultation on longer-term support for ageing underground stores has been delayed.

Rough is scheduled to close at the end of March unless the government intervenes, because seasonal price spreads no longer cover the cost of running it and the upgrade work it needs. The warning follows a similar one from SSE, which said less than two weeks ago that it would shut its Atwick storage site in east Yorkshire next summer without government support, and a November report from the National Energy System Operator warning that Britain risks a gas supply shortfall in a severe cold spell as North Sea output declines.
