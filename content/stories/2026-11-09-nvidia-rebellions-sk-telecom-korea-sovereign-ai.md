Nvidia, Rebellions and SK Telecom announced on Monday at Nvidia AI Day in Seoul a collaboration to build South Korea's sovereign AI infrastructure, backed by the country's Ministry of Science and ICT. Rebellions will use Nvidia's NVLink Fusion interconnect to connect its next generation R200 neural processing units to Nvidia's platform for rack scale inference, building systems on Nvidia's MGX rack architecture, and SK Telecom plans to deploy the systems in its AI data centres to deliver sovereign AI services, including under the national AI for All project.

Rebellions' R200 systems are expected to be ready for deployment in 2028, and its current ATOM-Max chips already run inside several SK Telecom services. No financial terms were disclosed for Monday's announcement, and the companies gave no purchase commitments or deployment dates beyond the 2028 target for the R200 systems.
