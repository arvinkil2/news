Around 20 people were killed when a Myanmar military airstrike hit a wedding venue in a village in Moe Bye, on the borders of Shan and Karenni states, on Saturday, witnesses told AFP. One witness said he found 17 dead bodies at the scene, where preparations were under way for two displaced people to marry. The leader of the Nway Oo Yaung Chi Foundation, which helps displaced people in camps, said he could confirm 21 deaths, five of them children, and more than 40 wounded, and said a second bomb fell after rescuers arrived.

Pictures from villagers showed six different bodies, and AFP said it was not able to verify the videos or pictures independently. The strike came two days after a bomb, artillery and drone attack centred on a school and monastery in Thayet district in Magway region killed 20 civilians and nine members of the People's Defence Forces, according to the National Unity Government in exile. Myanmar has been in civil war since the 2021 coup, and the military controls Moe Bye town while opposition forces operate in villages outside it.
