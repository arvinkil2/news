President Donald Trump said the United States could potentially allow the sale of Nvidia's Blackwell chips to China in the future, when they are no longer among the most advanced on the market. "In two years or three years, I would, if I were here, I would say, go ahead. You can now sell Blackwells because they'll have something else," he said in an interview with Fox News from Shenzhen, China, where he is attending the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, adding that Nvidia is already working on something that will supersede the chips.

Blackwell chips are currently barred from sale to China under US export controls. The comment follows Nvidia's earnings call on Tuesday, at which interim chief executive Colette Kress said the company has stopped shipping its licensed H200 chips to China, a lower tier of product that Washington had approved for sale there last December.
