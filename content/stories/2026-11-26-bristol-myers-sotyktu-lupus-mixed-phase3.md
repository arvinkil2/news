Bristol Myers Squibb announced mixed topline results on November 25 from two pivotal Phase 3 trials of Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) in adults with moderate to severe systemic lupus erythematosus. POETYK SLE-1 met its primary endpoint, with a significantly greater proportion of patients on Sotyktu achieving a Systemic Lupus Erythematosus Responder Index-4 (SRI-4) response at week 52 than patients on placebo. POETYK SLE-2 did not meet the same endpoint.

The two trials were similarly designed, each testing Sotyktu 3 mg twice daily against placebo on top of background standard care. SLE-1 enrolled 510 adults and SLE-2 enrolled 513. In SLE-1, Sotyktu also showed statistically significant effects on the BICLA composite assessment, on SRI-4 with corticosteroid tapering, and on patient-reported fatigue, while other key secondary endpoints were numerically greater but not statistically significant. In SLE-2, the company said the Sotyktu response was consistent with SLE-1, but a higher placebo response meant the primary endpoint was missed, with numerical improvements on all key secondary endpoints. Bristol Myers disclosed no response rates, p-values or effect sizes for either trial.

Sotyktu was well tolerated in both trials, with a safety profile consistent with its established profile in psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis and low discontinuations due to adverse events, the company said. Sotyktu, an oral selective TYK2 inhibitor, is approved in the United States and other markets for moderate to severe plaque psoriasis and for active psoriatic arthritis. Systemic lupus affects an estimated 3.4 million people worldwide, according to the company, and remains a disease with limited targeted oral options.

Bristol Myers said it will complete a comprehensive evaluation of the Phase 2 and Phase 3 lupus data and engage with health authorities, with detailed results to be presented at a future medical meeting. A Phase 3 trial of Sotyktu in Sjogren disease is ongoing, with results expected in 2027. Whether regulators will accept one positive pivotal trial alongside one miss is the open question the split readout creates.
