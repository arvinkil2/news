Axiom Solutions International, currently a wholly owned Flex subsidiary housing its cloud and power infrastructure business, entered into a credit agreement on November 17, 2026 providing 4.565 billion dollars of senior secured facilities, Flex disclosed in a Form 8-K filed the same day (accession 0001193125-26-465504). Citibank is administrative agent. The package is a 1.265 billion dollar revolving facility, including a 200 million dollar letter of credit sublimit, plus two term loans of 1.65 billion dollars each, maturing three and five years after closing.

Nothing was funded at signing. The facilities become available only when the spinoff of Axiom from Flex substantially concurrently closes, and the commitments terminate if that has not happened by June 30, 2027. The term loan proceeds will fund a dividend from Axiom to Flex, which Flex plans to use to prepay a separate term loan it signed on September 29, 2026. The revolver is for Axiom's general corporate purposes.

Axiom must hold consolidated net leverage at or below 4.5 times, stepping down to 4 times after the first year, with interest coverage of at least 3 times. Flex has guided the separation, which will make Axiom an independent public company holding the EPC Power business and the data center power and cooling operations, to the first quarter of 2027, and said at its November 10 investor day that the segment's revenue is growing rapidly on data center demand.

The filing reached this edition through the SEC filings lane rather than the news wires. It is the financing half of the spinoff: the equity story was set out at the investor day, and the credit agreement now fixes the capital structure Axiom will carry on day one, as well as the outside date by which the separation must happen.
