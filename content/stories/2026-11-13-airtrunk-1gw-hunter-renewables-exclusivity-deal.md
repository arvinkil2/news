AirTrunk and European Energy Australia have signed an exclusivity agreement to progress two renewable energy projects in the Hunter region of New South Wales, inside the Hunter-Central Coast Renewable Energy Zone: a 260 megawatt solar farm with a battery project of more than 800 megawatt hours, and a wind project of up to 350 megawatts, with the solar and battery projects expected to begin operating in 2030 and the wind project in 2032. AirTrunk has committed to matching 100 percent of its electricity use with renewable energy by 2030 under its Net Zero 2030 target, and said about 73 percent of its electricity use was matched with renewable energy across its portfolio in its 2026 financial year.
