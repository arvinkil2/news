The World Bank is weighing up to $5 billion in lending to Venezuela over the next two years, drawn from a mix of its public and private sector arms. Susana Cordeiro Guerra, its vice president for Latin America and the Caribbean, said the lender is assessing how it can support Venezuela's priorities if the conditions are right.

The figure is a possibility under assessment, not an approved commitment. Venezuela is resuming relations with the Washington based lender.
