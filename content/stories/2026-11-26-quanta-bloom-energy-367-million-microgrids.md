Taiwan-based server manufacturer Quanta has acquired two fuel cell microgrid systems from Bloom Energy for a reported $367 million, signing the purchases on behalf of its US subsidiary Quanta Manufacturing Nashville, according to Datacenter Dynamics citing TradingView reports. The transactions, which include technical services, cover a California project valued at about $249.9 million and a Tennessee project valued at about $117.1 million.

The agreement is Quanta's third with Bloom, following an $80 million deal for three microgrid systems in October 2024 and a $20 million deal for two systems that April. Bloom's solid oxide fuel cells have become a standard off-grid power source for the data center industry, with supply agreements also in place with NTT Data, Oracle, Equinix and American Electric Power.
