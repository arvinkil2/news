**Hong Kong results declared, Beijing hails the system.** Hong Kong announced the results of Sunday's Election Committee Subsector Ordinary Elections on Sunday evening: all 723 seats across 31 subsectors were filled, contested by 856 candidates, Xinhua reported. A spokesperson for the Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office of the State Council said on Monday that the process, the second under the revamped electoral system, demonstrated the strengths of the new system. SCMP reported the office described the poll, which drew 5,640 voters, as showing broad representation and fair competition under the "patriots only" framework. The committee will elect Hong Kong's next chief executive in March 2027. See yesterday's full story on the record turnout.

**Jeffries sets out the early House agenda.** Hakeem Jeffries, the Democrats' nominee for speaker when the new Congress convenes in January, told CBS on Sunday that House Democrats will move "very early" in the new Congress to ban members of Congress from trading stocks, as part of an accountability agenda covering Congress, the Supreme Court and the administration. In a separate interview reported on Monday, he put ending the Iran war, addressing President Trump's tariffs and reversing cuts to healthcare and food assistance at the top of the incoming majority's early priorities. No bill text or vote date has been announced for the stock trading ban.

**Ghana launches a non-tariff barrier reporting platform.** The Ghana Shippers' Authority, with the Ministry of Trade, Agribusiness and Industry and support from TradeMark Africa, has launched a Non-Tariff Barriers Reporting and Resolution Platform through which traders can report unofficial charges, delays and excessive paperwork at ports, roads and borders, by web, WhatsApp, social media or a USSD code, chief executive Ransford Gyampo said at a media engagement on November 20. The authority also said it will tighten regulation of shipping charges and enforcement of service standards in 2027.

**Commission speeches on health security and retirement.** Commissioner Hadja Lahbib addressed the HERA Industry Days on Monday on EU health emergency preparedness, and Commissioner Maria Luis Albuquerque opened the sixth European Retirement Week with a keynote on retirement saving, both published by the Commission on November 23. Neither speech announced new funding or legislation; they are recorded here as markers of the Commission's work programme rather than decisions.
