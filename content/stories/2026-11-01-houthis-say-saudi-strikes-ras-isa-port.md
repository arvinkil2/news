Houthi-run Al-Masirah television said Saudi forces carried out two airstrikes on Ras Isa port in Hodeidah province on Sunday, hitting the port pier and a merchant vessel berthed there, Yonhap reported. Houthi media also said the coalition struck the medical faculty of Hodeidah University the same day, and two local residents told AFP they heard explosions in the area. The Saudi-led coalition said the airstrike was a precision operation to neutralise key resources used by the Houthi rebels, after it said it had intercepted a ballistic missile and drones launched toward southern Saudi Arabia.

No casualty figures or damage assessment beyond the pier and the vessel have been published. Ras Isa, north of Hodeidah, is used as an oil import and storage base, and Hodeidah is the largest port under Houthi control and the main entry point for food and relief supplies into Houthi-held territory.
