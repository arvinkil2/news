S&P Global Ratings on Friday raised Zambia's long and short-term foreign and local currency ratings to B-/B from CCC+/C, with a stable outlook, citing supportive copper prices, stronger economic activity and expected narrower fiscal deficits from 2027. The agency said Zambia has reached agreements in principle with creditors representing about 94 percent of the $13.3 billion of debt covered by its restructuring programme, with more than 60 percent fully executed, and that public debt has fallen from nearly 130 percent of GDP in 2020 to an estimated 83.8 percent in 2025. S&P expects growth to average 5.5 percent between 2026 and 2029, including 6.5 percent in 2026, while warning that the fiscal deficit is set to widen to 5.9 percent of GDP in 2026 before narrowing to 3.5 percent by 2029.
