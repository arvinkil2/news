The Competition and Markets Authority said on November 11 that McCormick's acquisition of substantially all of Unilever's global food business gives rise to a realistic prospect of a substantial lessening of competition in the supply of dry ingredient mixes, and separately dry sauces, to retailers in the United Kingdom, because the deal combines the two largest branded suppliers in both markets. The CMA found no competition concern in mustard, where the parties sell different styles under the French's, Colman's and Maille brands.

McCormick, which sells in the UK under Schwartz and French's, is to pay for Unilever Foods in McCormick shares, with Unilever also receiving the right to appoint members of the McCormick board, and the merger is conditional on merger control clearances including the CMA's. The parties have until November 18, 2026 to offer undertakings in lieu of a reference; if none is offered, or the CMA judges an offer insufficient, it will refer the merger for an in-depth phase 2 investigation.
