The Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control published a new round of designations on Thursday, adding seven individuals and dozens of entities to the Specially Designated Nationals list. Most of the additions are Cambodian companies and their associates linked to the Prince Group Transnational Criminal Organization, with further entities in Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, Mauritius and the British Virgin Islands, plus one individual designated under a cyber-related programme.

The same action published a determination identifying the electronics, manufacturing, marine and technology sectors of the Cuban economy under Executive Order 14404, and designated Cuban state firms in those sectors, including the electronics importer CubaElectronica, the technical services firm Tecnosime and the maritime carrier Petroleos Maritimos del Caribe. OFAC also added the Cuba programme tag to three tankers that now fly the Cuban flag and to their Cyprus-based operator, Caroil Transport Marine.
