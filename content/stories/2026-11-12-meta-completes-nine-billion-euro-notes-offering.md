Meta Platforms said it completed the offering on November 12 under its Form S-3 shelf. The six tranches are 1.75 billion euros of 4.300 percent notes due 2031, 1.25 billion euros of 4.650 percent notes due 2033, 1 billion euros of 4.900 percent notes due 2035, 1.75 billion euros of 5.400 percent notes due 2038, 1.75 billion euros of 6.050 percent notes due 2044 and 1.5 billion euros of 6.400 percent notes due 2065.

Citigroup Global Markets Limited and Morgan Stanley & Co. International plc acted as representatives of the underwriters, and the notes were issued under Meta's 2022 base indenture with U.S. Bank Trust Company as trustee, as supplemented on November 12. The completion confirms the full 9 billion euros settled as priced, with maturities running to 2065.
