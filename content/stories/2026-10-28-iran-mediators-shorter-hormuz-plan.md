Iran is discussing with mediators how its seven day plan to open the Strait of Hormuz could be carried out in even less than a week, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said in a post on X quoted by RIA Novosti, adding that Iran continues to "give diplomacy a chance to succeed." The mediators were not named.

The plan, as Iran's Foreign Ministry described it earlier, offers to open the strait over seven days in exchange for lifting the naval blockade and restrictions on Iranian exports, and Tehran restated on October 18 that the offer still stood. RIA reports that President Donald Trump rejected the proposal. Shipping through the strait has close to stopped, and the United States advised ships today to avoid unnecessary Hormuz transits.
