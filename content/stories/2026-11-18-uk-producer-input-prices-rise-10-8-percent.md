UK producer input prices rose by 10.8 percent in the year to October 2026, up from 8.3 percent in the year to September and the largest annual rise since February 2023, the Office for National Statistics said on Wednesday. Producer output, or factory gate, prices rose by 5.1 percent on the year, up from a revised 4.7 percent in September and the largest annual rise since April 2023; on the month, input prices rose 2.2 percent and output prices 0.4 percent.

Crude oil drove both series: crude input prices rose 88.4 percent in the year to October and 22.8 percent on the month, and coke and refined petroleum product output prices rose 72.4 percent on the year. The ONS said the increases were mainly caused by oil-related products, with the data still affected by the conflict in the Middle East; domestic food was the only downward contribution to the annual input rate.
