Brookfield Asset Management has agreed to acquire PGP Glass, the Indian specialty glass packaging manufacturer, from funds managed by Blackstone Private Equity for $1.7 billion, the companies said on November 23. Brookfield will fund the investment through its Brookfield Capital Partners strategy and its affiliate Brookfield Business Corporation. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory and government approvals, and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027.

PGP Glass makes specialty glass packaging for cosmetics, fragrances, pharmaceuticals and consumer products. Blackstone acquired the business, then Piramal Glass, for approximately $1 billion in 2021 and renamed it PGP Glass. The $1.7 billion sale price is a clean sponsor-to-sponsor exit at a higher value, without an IPO window being needed.

Brookfield said it sees opportunity for PGP Glass in premium products and expansion, at a time when glass packaging in North America and Europe faces cost pressures and capacity rationalisation. The deal continues the pattern of Indian manufacturing assets passing between global sponsors at valuations set by export and premium domestic demand rather than by local capital markets.
