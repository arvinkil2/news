S&P Global Ratings revised Meta Platforms' outlook to negative from stable on Friday, affirming the AA- issuer credit rating, and cited significant uncertainty around the company's capital spending and free operating cash flow as it pours money into chips and data centres. Meta last week guided full year capital expenditure to between $135 billion and $145 billion after reporting a third quarter free cash flow deficit of $2.12 billion, and S&P now forecasts free operating cash flow deficits of $39 billion in 2026 and $71 billion in 2027, warning that elevated spending could keep the measure negative beyond 2028 and push leverage above 1.0 times as early as 2027, its threshold for the AA- rating. "Given the lack of clarity around the company's future capex and its very ambitious multi-year AI investment plans, we are less certain that it will curtail its spending as previously anticipated," S&P said.
