MasTec has agreed to acquire Murphy Corporation, a Missouri based mechanical contractor, for approximately $1,250 million in cash, subject to customary purchase price adjustments, under an equity purchase agreement dated November 19, 2026. MasTec's press release adds a potential earnout tied to Murphy's cumulative financial performance over the three years after closing, and projects Murphy will generate approximately $775 million of revenue and approximately $140 million of adjusted EBITDA in calendar 2026, implying a price of about 8.9 times projected EBITDA before any earnout, a calculation from the company's own figures.

The acquisition was financed entirely with bank debt. On November 19 MasTec and MasTec North America entered a senior unsecured delayed draw term loan agreement with Bank of America as administrative agent providing $1,250 million of commitments, and on November 20 MasTec borrowed the full amount to fund the purchase and related fees. The loans do not amortize, mature on November 19, 2027, bear interest at Term SOFR plus 1.000 to 1.500 percent depending on leverage and ratings, and must be prepaid from the proceeds of any permanent refinancing or specified equity issuances.

MasTec estimated pro forma net leverage of 2.7 times at September 30, 2026, falling below 2.0 times during 2027. The term loan caps MasTec's consolidated leverage ratio at 3.50 times, with a temporary increase to 4.00 times permitted around large acquisitions. MasTec said substantially all closing conditions were satisfied and it expects to complete the transaction expeditiously, with Murphy becoming a new operating group reported in its Clean Energy and Infrastructure segment.
