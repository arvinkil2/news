**Zelenskyy objects to a summit he is not part of.** Deutsche Welle reported that Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy was irked by a US-Russia-China summit, a format that discusses the war's future among three powers while Ukraine, whose territory is the subject, sits outside the room. No Ukrainian response beyond the reported irritation was carried in the pool.

**Conservative activists charged over a voting stunt.** The BBC reported that US conservative activists have been charged with unlawful voting in an election stunt. Charges are allegations, not findings, and the item is carried at headline level until court reporting establishes what was actually done and by whom.

**Democrats split in public over impeachment.** The Hill reported that House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries tiptoed around the question of impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, while former Vice President Kamala Harris advised Democrats against attempting to impeach President Trump. No articles of impeachment against either man were reported in the pool; the split is over strategy after the midterms, not over a filed case.

**OpenAI stands up an election war room.** Politico reported that OpenAI is launching an election war room to track interference efforts ahead of November 3, putting a major AI company in the position of monitoring manipulation of an election in which AI generated content is itself one of the suspected tools.

**Nigeria sends a low level delegation to Moscow.** Premium Times reported that Nigeria sent a low level delegation to the Russia-Africa Summit, a notable absence of seniority at a summit the Kremlin is using to measure its pull on the continent, and a contrast with the 22 heads of state Moscow counted. The summit's closing documents are covered in today's updated summit story.
