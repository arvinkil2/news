CBL-514, an experimental injection from Taiwan's Caliway Biopharmaceuticals tested in phase 2 trials by Dr. W. Timothy Garvey of the University of Alabama at Birmingham and Caliway's Yu-Fang Ling, cut average visceral fat volume 12.01% four weeks after the final treatment, against a 5.68% increase on placebo, per Fox News. The drug is injected into the lower abdomen every three weeks for up to 12 weeks at doses up to 600 milligrams and selectively triggers apoptosis in fat cells while sparing nearby tissue, the same mechanism that had already shown reductions in subcutaneous fat. Liposuction cannot reach visceral fat, so the results, if replicated, would target a health-risk category surgery cannot touch.
