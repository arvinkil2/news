The National Bank of Serbia revoked the operating licence of ALTA banka a.d. Beograd at its executive board meeting on Saturday. The regulator said the bank's financial condition had deteriorated sharply over the preceding period, with a risk of further deterioration in its position and liquidity that threatened depositors' interests. ALTA Banka confirmed in its own statement that bankruptcy proceedings have been initiated against it, and said the bankruptcy authorities are acting to protect depositors and creditors.

The Deposit Insurance Agency is to be appointed bankruptcy administrator by court decision and, under the timetable the central bank gave, will open payouts of insured deposits within seven working days. The bank is owned by businessman Davor Macura, whom local media describe as close to Aleksandar Vucic's Serbian Progressive Party; the revocation comes six days after the Student List won the October 25 election and Vucic conceded defeat.
