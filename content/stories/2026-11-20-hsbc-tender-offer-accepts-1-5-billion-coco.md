HSBC Holdings said $1,570,766,000 of its $3,000,000,000 6.000% perpetual subordinated contingent convertible securities was validly tendered by the 5:00 p.m. New York expiry on November 19, above the $1,500,000,000 maximum tender amount, so accepted securities were prorated at a factor of 93.27884 percent. The bank will pay 100.50 percent of principal, $1,507,500,000 in total consideration excluding accrued interest, on the November 24 settlement date, and all accepted securities will be cancelled and retired. The purchase was conditional on a new issue that closed on November 13, a $1,500,000,000 offering of 7.625% perpetual subordinated contingent convertible securities.
