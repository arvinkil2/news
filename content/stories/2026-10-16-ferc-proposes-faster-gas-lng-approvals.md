The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission on Thursday proposed no longer requiring air dispersion modelling in its National Environmental Policy Act reviews of gas projects, in a draft policy statement adopted by a 4 to 1 vote, with Commissioner Judy Chang dissenting. Chair Laura Swett said the modelling had become a source of delay and litigation risk, while Chang said it remained the best tool for assessing local air impacts short of physical monitors.

FERC also proposed a three-tier blanket authorisation framework for LNG projects, under which some activities at existing plants would proceed automatically, others after a 60-day notice and comment period, and larger additions such as liquefaction trains, tanks and marine berths after 90 days.
