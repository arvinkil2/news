Yandex said on Sunday that operations at its data centre in Vladimir, east of Moscow, had been completely suspended after a drone strike, with no injuries reported. The company warned in a Telegram service alert that its platform was operating in emergency mode and advised users to activate disaster-recovery plans, adding that the remaining resource configuration was considered unstable. It is the third strike on Yandex facilities in a week: the company said Ukrainian drones partly disabled its Kaluga data centre on Friday, and a day earlier an attack shut its Sasovo hub, which houses two of the three supercomputers used to develop Yandex's AI model. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Thursday that Kyiv responds in mirror-like fashion to Russian strikes on Ukrainian data centres.
