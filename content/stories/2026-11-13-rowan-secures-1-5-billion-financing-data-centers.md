Rowan Digital Infrastructure announced corporate level financings adding more than 1.5 billion dollars in new capital capacity: a 600 million dollar upsize of its holding company term loan led by Apterra Infrastructure Capital, plus a new 590 million dollar revolving credit facility and a new 355 million dollar letter of credit facility, each led by Citibank, with the facilities available for general pipeline development. The company, founded by Quinbrook Infrastructure Partners and Birch Infrastructure and part owned by Blackstone, says it has raised more than 8 billion dollars from more than 40 lenders over the past 18 months and has more than 1.2 gigawatts already contracted to hyperscale customers.
