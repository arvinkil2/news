Dish Wireless, an EchoStar subsidiary, has reached a $525 million settlement with American Tower over unpaid tower leases, Datacenter Dynamics reported, citing the Wall Street Journal. Dish told a court this week that the agreement will require the Federal Communications Commission to approve making the funds available by the end of the year, from a $2.4 billion creditor trust established using proceeds from AT&T's purchase of EchoStar's spectrum licences. The amount is significantly less than the $2.5 billion American Tower had been seeking.

Dish stopped honouring its tower agreements after EchoStar agreed last year to sell spectrum assets to AT&T and SpaceX for a combined $40 billion, saying it had been forced to sell by the FCC, and Dish filed for bankruptcy this summer. The company says it is working on similar settlements with other creditors, but Crown Castle, which is seeking $3.5 billion over its terminated agreement, has said Dish has not reached out to discuss a settlement.
