A 49 year old worker died on Thursday at Soroka Medical Center in Beersheba from injuries sustained in Wednesday's explosion at Intel's Kiryat Gat fabrication plant, bringing the death toll to two. A gas technician was pronounced dead on Wednesday, and a third worker, a man in his twenties, remains in serious condition in intensive care, sedated and on a ventilator.

Police suspect a gas cylinder exploded during unloading operations at the plant entrance, as one delivery truck disconnected and a second connected to transfer gas into the facility's tanks. The blast sparked a fire and a leak of nitrogen trifluoride, a nonflammable but toxic gas used to clean chipmaking tools; hazardous materials teams monitored the site and authorities later said the threat to nearby residents had been removed. Investigators have identified no suspected criminal activity at this stage, and Intel has not reported any effect on production at Fab 28.
