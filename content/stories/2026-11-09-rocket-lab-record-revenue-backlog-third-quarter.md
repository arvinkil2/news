Rocket Lab's third-quarter revenue of $267.8 million beat the $257 million analyst forecast cited by MarketWatch and rose 73 percent from a year earlier, while the net loss widened to $79.7 million, or 12 cents a share, from $18.3 million a year earlier, including a $27.3 million loss on extinguishing debt. The backlog reached $3.57 billion at September 30, up 52 percent in one quarter, after about $1 billion of new launch contracts took the launch backlog to a record 117 missions, including more than $515 million of Neutron contracts across 16 missions at a base price lifted to $70 million.

The company guided fourth-quarter revenue to between $270 million and $290 million with an adjusted EBITDA loss of $14 million to $20 million, reported positive GAAP operating cash flow of $7.2 million, and said Neutron's first launch is now targeted for no earlier than 2027. Its pending acquisition of Iridium Communications is fully funded through a completed at-the-market equity offering, and Iridium shareholders have approved the deal.
