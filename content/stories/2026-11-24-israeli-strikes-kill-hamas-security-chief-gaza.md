Israeli airstrikes killed a senior Hamas security official and six others, including two police officers, in the Gaza Strip on Tuesday, medics and police said. Naeem Abu Nahel, the director of Hamas's internal security apparatus in Gaza, was killed in an airstrike in western Gaza City. The Israeli military and the Shin Bet domestic security service said in a joint statement that they had struck a senior Hamas militant, without identifying him, and the military told Reuters it had struck militants in both areas where people were killed.

Earlier on Tuesday, medics said three people were killed in an Israeli airstrike at a police post in Khan Younis in southern Gaza, two of them police officers according to the Hamas-run interior ministry, and five other people were wounded. Another airstrike near the Netzarim corridor further north killed another three people, taking Tuesday's death toll to at least seven. An October 2025 US-backed ceasefire halted major fighting in the territory but has not stopped Israeli attacks or secured Hamas's disarmament; at least 1,600 Palestinians and five Israeli soldiers have been killed since then, according to respective Palestinian and Israeli tallies cited by Reuters.
