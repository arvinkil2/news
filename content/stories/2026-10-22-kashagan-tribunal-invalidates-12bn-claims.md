An international court of arbitration invalidated about $12 billion of Kazakhstan's claims against the oil majors for disputed costs at the Kashagan field, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Bloomberg. The tribunal decided the government filed its case too late for costs incurred between 2011 and 2015. Kazakhstan had been seeking to recoup about $20 billion of disputed costs at the field, which came into production years late and well over budget, and about $8 billion of its claims were allowed to proceed.
