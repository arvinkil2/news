Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping agreed that humans should always be involved in any military decisions made with artificial intelligence, and that the technology should not be allowed to be autonomous for major military actions. Bessent, speaking to CNBC after returning from Trump's meeting with Xi in Shenzhen, China, called the agreement one of the major achievements of Trump's trip to Asia.

Bessent said the understanding on military use of the technology, requiring human involvement and other safeguards, would not be affected by the two countries' competition for AI supremacy, adding that no major military operation should be launched without a human being involved on either side. He also said US AI labs needed to maintain their technological lead as the best defense against Chinese AI models he described as dangerous and used in cyberattacks.
