Rio Tinto produced 205,000 tonnes of copper in the third quarter, flat against a year earlier and 4 percent below the second quarter, and left its 2026 guidance unchanged at 800,000 to 870,000 tonnes, with Oyu Tolgoi in Mongolia running at an annualised rate of about 450,000 tonnes and output there up 27 percent year on year in the quarter. Global iron ore sales rose 3 percent to 90.8 million tonnes, Pilbara sales rose 2 percent to 85.7 million tonnes, and the Simandou mine in Guinea reached an annualised run rate of about 10 million tonnes as it ramps toward 60 million tonnes in the second half of 2028.

The company said it has achieved more than $2 billion of capital releases so far this year, against a plan to announce about $5 billion by the end of 2026, and that its major projects remain on schedule and budget.
