Anthropic said on Sunday it will commit 5 percent of its shares to a newly created Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, pending stockholder approval, in an announcement reported by Investing.com. The fund, structured as a wholly owned limited liability company rather than a traditional nonprofit, could gain a further 2 percent at the board's discretion for every 50 percent increase in the company's value above a set baseline, and Anthropic said none of the equity it holds will be used for lobbying or political giving. Combined with earlier commitments, including the co-founders' pledge to donate 80 percent of their equity holdings, roughly 20 percent of Anthropic shares will be dedicated to public benefit purposes, the company said.
