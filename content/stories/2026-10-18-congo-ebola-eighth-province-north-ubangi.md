Congo's National Institute for Biomedical Research confirmed two Ebola cases, including one death, in North Ubangi province on Sunday, confirming Reuters reporting from Thursday that had cited health officials without an official statement. North Ubangi, which borders the Central African Republic, becomes the eighth province affected, alongside North Kivu, Ituri, Haut-Uele, Tshopo, Sud-Kivu, Bas-Uele and South Ubangi. Congo's latest situation report showed 67 new confirmed cases and 26 new deaths in the 24 hours to October 16, bringing the cumulative total to 9,415 cases and 4,536 deaths, with Ituri still reporting the most new cases.
