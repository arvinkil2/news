Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said on Saturday that two oil tankers caught fire after striking naval mines while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on a route Iran does not authorize, with one vessel left immobilized, the official IRNA news agency reported, in a Reuters dispatch that noted there was no independent confirmation of the reports and that state media put the tankers' crude cargoes at about 2 million barrels. US Central Command says it has cleared the strait of naval mines and that vessels are being struck by projectiles, pointing to Thursday's sinking of the container ship GFS Pride, which it said was hit by an Iranian projectile and not a mine. Separately, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency reported a new form of attack on Saturday, a tanker hit by a rocket-propelled grenade fired from a small boat 78 nautical miles northeast of Jubail, Saudi Arabia, with damage to its aft port quarter.
