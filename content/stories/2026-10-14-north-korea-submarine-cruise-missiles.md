North Korea said it had tested two types of submarine launched strategic cruise missiles on Wednesday to verify navy mission capabilities and the reliability of its war deterrents, state media KCNA reported on Thursday, in a report carried by Reuters. Senior military officers observed the tests in the sea off the North's east coast, and KCNA said the tests had no adverse impact on the security of neighbouring countries, without giving further detail on the missiles or the launch platform.

The North has test launched ballistic and cruise missiles from various platforms, including an ageing submarine, but is not yet believed to have a modern missile submarine in full operation, a capability Reuters noted is a top military priority for Kim Jong Un. Whether Wednesday's missiles were fired from an operational submarine or a submerged test platform was not stated in the announcement.
