**South Africa pauses its weekly repo auction.** The South African Reserve Bank issued a notice on October 12 suspending the weekly repo auction, its main one-week liquidity operation conducted at the repo rate, and separately published Directive 5 of 2026 withdrawing previously issued specified directives for banks. The notices appeared in the SARB's official publications feed; no reason was given in the feed metadata, and the full texts were not independently opened, so the operational significance is unclear.

**RBI's daily statement shows heavy absorption.** The Reserve Bank of India's money-market operations statement for October 10 showed banks parking Rs 1,39,360 crore in the standing deposit facility at 5.25%, with net liquidity absorbed from outstanding operations at Rs 3,76,435.90 crore. Scheduled commercial banks' cash balances with the RBI stood at Rs 7,86,097.56 crore against an average daily cash-reserve requirement of Rs 8,17,219 crore for the fortnight ending October 15, while net durable liquidity was last reported as a surplus of Rs 13,93,819 crore as of September 15.

**Gold rises as oil-driven hike bets fade.** Gold edged higher Monday as lower oil prices pared market expectations for further interest-rate increases, Bloomberg reported, with traders also weighing a Russia-Ukraine energy ceasefire. The move fits the morning's pattern: softer energy prices are doing the most to cool the global hiking repricing.

**Copper gains on the same repricing, plus a supply shock.** Copper rose on easing rate-hike expectations and an avalanche at a Chilean mine, Bloomberg reported Monday. The metal is doubly sensitive to the rates story, through both the dollar and the growth outlook.

**Nuveen's Cooper sees yield risks tilted up.** In a Bloomberg interview Monday, Nuveen's Cooper said risks to yields are tilted to the upside, adding a prominent asset-manager voice to the camp that sees 5%+ long-end yields as a floor rather than a peak.

**BoJ December hike nearly fully priced.** Money markets see about a 90% chance the Bank of Japan raises its policy rate by year end, Bloomberg Economics estimates, after Governor Kazuo Ueda's hawkish turn following September's hike to 1.25%. The open question is political: longtime easing supporter Sanae Takaichi's tolerance for faster normalization is untested, and the yen's weakness is keeping pressure on the bank.

**China inflation and the week's speaker slate.** China's September CPI and PPI land Wednesday, a read on whether deflationary pressure is still bleeding into the region's rate outlooks. The rest of the calendar is crowded with central-bank voices: RBA minutes and Fed Governor Waller on Tuesday; ECB President Lagarde, the Fed's Beige Book, Governor Bowman and Bank of Canada Governor Macklem on Wednesday; UK August GDP and BoE Governor Bailey on Thursday and Friday.
