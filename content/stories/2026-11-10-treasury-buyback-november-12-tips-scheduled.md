The US Treasury accepted $556 million in its liquidity support buyback on Thursday, November 12, in Treasury inflation-protected securities in the 1 year to 10 year maturity bucket, against a maximum purchase amount of $750 million. Holders offered $3.224 billion, an offered cover of 5.80 times the amount accepted, and 8 of the 28 eligible issues were accepted. The operation ran at 1:40 p.m. Eastern and settlement is on November 13.
