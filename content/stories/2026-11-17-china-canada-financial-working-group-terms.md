The People's Bank of China, Canada's finance ministry and the Bank of Canada have jointly signed terms of reference for the China-Canada Financial Working Group, the PBOC said, signed by Governor Pan Gongsheng, Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne and Governor Tiff Macklem. The working group, led by the three institutions, was set up to carry out the January leaders' meeting consensus, held its first meeting in Beijing in April, and the terms set out the participating departments, cooperation areas and exchange methods so it operates on a standing basis.

The two sides agreed to strengthen cooperation on testing and discussing use of the recently renewed bilateral local currency swap agreement, exploring wider local currency use in bilateral trade and investment, and encouraging two way investment. Canada welcomed qualified Canadian institutions issuing panda bonds and said it welcomes and supports China's interest in issuing renminbi green sovereign bonds in the Canadian market, and the two sides have already held technical exchanges on panda bond issuance, cross border payment efficiency and anti money laundering.
