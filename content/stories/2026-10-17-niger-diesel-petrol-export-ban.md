Niger has banned exports of diesel and premium petrol, according to a statement by the ministry of trade and industry posted on its Facebook page and reported by Bloomberg. The ministry said the ban is a response to fuel supply challenges the country has faced in recent days, and that domestic production of the two fuels is primarily intended to supply the domestic market. The statement, as reported, gave no start date, duration or export volumes.
