Energy Fuels, which agreed in June to acquire Vacuumschmelze for $1.9 billion, said in SEC documents filed last week that a customer of the South Carolina plant terminated its supply agreement on July 1 and that VAC launched arbitration proceedings against that customer several days later, and GM said in its own filing this week that a supplier had initiated arbitration against it after it terminated the agreement. The deal, signed in 2023, was for permanent magnets for electric vehicle traction motors, and VAC's United States unit characterises the termination as wrongful. GM did not say in its filing why it cancelled.

Energy Fuels said it has no concerns about VAC's commercial success and that there may be adjustments to its purchase price for VAC depending on the outcome of the arbitration. Reuters framed the cancellation against GM's pullback from electric vehicles after the loss of federal incentives, including a $6 billion charge in January to unwind some electric vehicle investments.
