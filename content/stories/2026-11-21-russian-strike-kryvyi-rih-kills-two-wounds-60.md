A Russian strike on the central Ukrainian city of Kryvyi Rih on Friday night killed two people, a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman, and wounded 60 others, including six children, local officials said. About 25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools and businesses were damaged, according to Oleksandr Vilkul, head of the city's administration. Vilkul said Russian forces launched further missiles after emergency workers arrived, hitting rescuers, medical personnel and utility crews at the scene.
