Volkswagen chief Oliver Blume expects European policymakers to create the framework needed to make the German automaker's restructuring a success, he said Sunday at a "For Europe" event on the eve of the Paris motor show, per Reuters. Blume, who called the overhaul "the biggest transformation programme in our history," backed proposed "Made in Europe" rules to incentivize local manufacturing and welcomed an EU-China deal that could cut Chinese plug-in hybrid imports to the bloc by more than half.

VW is battling an EV price war as Chinese competitors expand in Europe, cutting jobs and threatening to close up to four German plants while investors press Blume to prove the turnaround goes beyond downsizing into product and software.
