Thousands of customers at major US banks reported problems accessing accounts online on Monday, according to Downdetector data carried by Al Jazeera and Investing.com, with nearly 5,000 reports for JPMorgan Chase, half of them involving account balances and transactions, and more than 800 for Wells Fargo covering logins, balances and fund transfers. US Bank, Bank of America and the Zelle payments network were also named among the affected institutions.

Chase said a technical issue was making some transactions take longer than usual to complete, that customers still had access to their accounts and funds, and that it was working to resolve the problem; CNBC reported, citing an unnamed source, that bad actors did not appear to be involved. The other named banks had not commented.
