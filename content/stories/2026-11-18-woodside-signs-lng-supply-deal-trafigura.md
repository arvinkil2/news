Woodside Energy has agreed to supply Trafigura, the commodities trader, with one million tonnes of liquefied natural gas a year for up to ten years from 2030, Reuters reported. Woodside did not specify where the LNG will come from, and no pricing or delivery terms were reported. Woodside's chief commercial officer Mark Abbotsford said the agreement showed robust long-term demand for reliable LNG supply, and Trafigura chief executive Richard Holtum said it strengthened the trader's ability to provide reliable and flexible supply.

The deal follows Woodside's agreement last month to supply QatarEnergy with LNG from its Louisiana project for 20 years. Reuters noted LNG prices remain at multi-year highs this year, with shipping through the Strait of Hormuz still disrupted after the conflict in the Middle East.
