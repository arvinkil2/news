Canada's Ivanhoe Mines (TSX: IVN) has signed a memorandum of understanding with Pakistan's Mari Energies (PSX: MARI) to pursue mineral exploration opportunities, chairman Robert Friedland announced on Thursday on the sidelines of the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum in Islamabad, marking the Canadian miner's entry into South Asia beyond its major operations in southern Africa. Friedland said Ivanhoe is in the early stages of establishing a local office, expects to sign another agreement on Friday, and is forming equal partnerships in Pakistan, though he did not confirm that this agreement follows a 50-50 ownership structure, and neither company has disclosed financial terms or specific exploration commitments. Mari Energies' mining subsidiary Mari Minerals holds several exploration projects targeting copper and gold in Pakistan, and Pakistani reporting on the forum put the joint work in Chagai, Balochistan, the district that hosts the Reko Diq copper and gold project.
