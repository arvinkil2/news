The Treasury Department's Office of Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructure Protection released its annual holiday advisory on Wednesday, urging consumers to protect themselves from the seasonal surge in cyber-enabled scams and online fraud. The advisory says fraudsters are increasingly using super intelligence to carry out more sophisticated and convincing scams, including impersonating trusted individuals, businesses and loved ones.

Cory Wilson, Deputy Assistant Secretary for Cyber Security and Critical Infrastructure Protection, said it is more important than ever for Americans to remain vigilant this holiday season, and Treasury encouraged consumers to verify suspicious requests, recognise the warning signs of fraud, and report suspected scams quickly. The department directs victims to report fraud to the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center.
