A United States appeals court has allowed the Trump administration to move forward for now with removing deportation protections for Venezuelans under Temporary Protected Status, cancelling an earlier order by District Judge Edward Chen that required the government to restore the protections, Al Jazeera reported with Reuters and The Associated Press. Chen had blocked the expiration of the protections in September 2025, the Supreme Court paused his ruling in October 2025, and that pause expired this month, with his order set to take effect on Friday. The appeals decision does not end the legal challenges to the revocation; it allows the administration to suspend the protections, which covered about 600,000 Venezuelans, while those challenges proceed.
