Samsung Electronics is expected to report a nearly nine-fold jump in third-quarter operating profit, to 106.1 trillion won ($79.1 billion) from 12.17 trillion won a year earlier, Reuters reported Wednesday, in what would be the fourth straight quarterly record as AI infrastructure demand outstrips memory supply growth. The LSEG SmartEstimate, based on forecasts from 21 analysts weighted toward the strongest track records, has nonetheless been cut 7.7 percent since the end of August on slowing memory price gains and a won that strengthened 14.3 percent in the quarter, its biggest quarterly gain since early 1998. Samsung issues preliminary Q3 results Thursday, with detailed figures later in October. TrendForce expects fourth-quarter conventional DRAM contract prices to rise only 10 to 15 percent, slowing sharply from the second quarter's roughly 60 percent surge, while Samsung is gaining HBM4 ground on SK Hynix and J.P. Morgan estimates its HBM market share rises to 34 percent this year from 20 percent last year.
