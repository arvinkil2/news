**Latest (Thursday, October 8):** Samsung confirmed its fourth straight record quarter, guiding to consolidated sales of about 195 trillion won and operating profit of 107.4 trillion won, within its stated ranges of 194 to 196 trillion won in sales and 107.3 to 107.5 trillion won in profit. The profit print came in slightly above the LSEG SmartEstimate of about 106.6 trillion won, a small beat after analysts had cut forecasts 7.7 percent since late August on a strengthening won and slowing memory price gains. Revenue, however, landed below the roughly 200 trillion won consensus. The market was not impressed enough to bid the stock higher: Samsung shares fell 0.7 percent Thursday morning. The full third-quarter report, with division detail, HBM contribution and any 2027 commentary, is due October 29.

Samsung had entered earnings season as the clearest test of the AI memory trade. Reuters reported Wednesday that the LSEG SmartEstimate, based on forecasts from 21 analysts weighted toward the strongest track records, pointed to a nearly nine-fold jump in operating profit from 12.17 trillion won a year earlier, driven by AI infrastructure demand outstripping memory supply growth. TrendForce expects fourth-quarter conventional DRAM contract prices to rise only 10 to 15 percent, slowing sharply from the second quarter's roughly 60 percent surge, while Samsung is gaining HBM4 ground on SK Hynix and J.P. Morgan estimates its HBM market share rises to 34 percent this year from 20 percent last year.
