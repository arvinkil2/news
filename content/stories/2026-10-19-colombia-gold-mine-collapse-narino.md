At least 14 miners died on Monday when an open-pit gold mine collapsed in the municipality of Santa Bárbara de Iscuandé in Nariño, southwestern Colombia, according to local authorities. Jorge Tovar Castillo, the departmental official responsible for disaster response, said another 12 to 15 miners were reported missing, and municipal authorities were managing the response while communication with the area remained difficult.

Nariño Governor Luis Alfonso Escobar said on X that the mine was an informal one and that saving lives was the priority. Santa Bárbara de Iscuandé is surrounded by dense tropical forest and is accessible only by boat or small aircraft, and officials were awaiting the municipality's next update to decide whether additional support was needed.
