Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul and Russian President Vladimir Putin discussed energy and fertilizer supplies during talks on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen, according to the Thai government. Thailand sought Russian fertilizer supplies, while Moscow offered oil and gas and greater nuclear cooperation, in what the report described as a signal of expanding economic ties between the two countries. No volumes, prices, timelines or signed agreements were reported.
