**Latest (overnight, Friday, October 9):** The full session's damage was sharper than the early read: the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.25 percent to 27,193.34, its worst day since mid-August, the Nasdaq 100 dropped 1.4 percent and the S&P 500 fell 0.5 percent, while the Dow edged up 0.1 percent. Intel fell more than 5 percent, Oracle 5.5 percent, Nvidia 2.9 percent and CoreWeave 7.8 percent; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 3.4 percent, its worst slide since September 14. Overnight, chip and cloud names edged up slightly and some analysts called the reaction overblown, while a source familiar with the documents said the $70 billion figure did not come from OpenAI and likely reflected investors adding partner revenue to compare OpenAI's net revenue with Anthropic's gross.

The Financial Times reported that OpenAI told investors its annualized revenue was reaching $50 billion toward the end of September, $20 billion short of the $70 billion it had previously projected, according to MarketWatch. Shares of Micron, Nvidia and other artificial-intelligence chip makers fell hard on Thursday on the report; MarketWatch noted analysts attribute the gap to differences in how the figure is reported rather than weakness in AI demand.
