President Vladimir Putin has signed a decree placing the Russian assets of French flooring maker Tarkett under the temporary management of a Russian company, Ukrainska Pravda reported, citing the official Russian legal portal. The same decree transferred assets of four other European-owned firms, including paper packaging maker Kappa Rus and hygiene products maker Ontex RU, and the affected companies have combined annual revenue above 50 billion rubles. Tarkett had kept two of its 34 worldwide plants in Russia after the full-scale invasion of Ukraine.
