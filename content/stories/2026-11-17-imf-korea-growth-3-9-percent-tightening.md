IMF staff completing the 2026 Article IV mission to South Korea projected real GDP growth of 3.9 percent in 2026, up from 1.1 percent last year, driven by surging technology exports, rebounding investment and resilient consumption, with growth of 3.3 percent projected for 2027 and inflation of 2.7 percent in 2026 and 2.3 percent in 2027. Mission chief Corinne Deléchat said the global AI upcycle is delivering record-high semiconductor exports and strong corporate earnings, though gains remain concentrated in the technology sector. With the economy operating above potential and inflation above target, staff said further monetary tightening is appropriate and a somewhat more contractionary fiscal stance would help contain demand, while welcoming the new Future Response Fund as a fiscal stabilization vehicle.
