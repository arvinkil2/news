The Reserve Bank of India offered to absorb 50,000 crore rupees for two days in a variable rate reverse repo auction on Tuesday and received offers of just 7,627 crore, all of which it accepted at a cut-off rate of 5.49 percent, with the weighted average rate also 5.49 percent. The same morning's money market release showed banks had placed 1,50,777 crore rupees in the overnight standing deposit facility at 5.25 percent on Monday, leaving net liquidity absorbed through the RBI's outstanding operations at 1,34,094 crore rupees.
