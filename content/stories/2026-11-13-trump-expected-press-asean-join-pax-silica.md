President Trump is expected to attend the ASEAN summit in Manila, which runs November 15 to 17, and urge Southeast Asian countries to join Pax Silica and its AI Spark programme for the region, with several agreements expected to be announced during the summit, including Indonesia joining Pax Silica. Pax Silica is an AI supply chain consultative body launched in December 2025 under US State Department leadership to reduce dependence on China, with 26 member countries including South Korea, Japan, Australia, India and Singapore, and AI Spark provides funding and technical support to countries that adopt US made AI systems in public services. Trump is scheduled to arrive in the Philippines on November 17, shortly before the summit closes with an ASEAN and US special summit, hold bilateral talks with President Ferdinand Marcos on November 18, and then travel to Shenzhen for the APEC summit, and unnamed sources also expected him to raise the South China Sea.
