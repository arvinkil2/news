FAA ground stop, technology angle. United States flights resumed after the nationwide ground stop, the third in the FAA's history, and Datacenter Dynamics reported the cause as a telecommunications outage with a new technology rollout blamed. The lead coverage sits with the politics and commodities beats; the technology record is that a systems rollout, not weather or a security event, is the reported trigger, which puts the FAA's modernization program under the same scrutiny the Microsoft outage put on cloud operators this week.

Microsoft outage closed. Microsoft said on Saturday morning that it had fully restored service to the remaining customers and regions affected by its global outage, closing an incident that began on Thursday and disrupted Microsoft 365, Outlook, Teams and Copilot in more than 70 countries and territories. The full running file is the November 12 Microsoft outage story, updated in place; no new technology story is filed on the closure alone.

Oura follow up sits with life sciences. STAT reported that Oura's acquisition of Counsel Health raises conflict of interest questions for Eli Lilly, according to experts it cited. The acquisition itself, at 625 million dollars, was covered on November 10 in the life sciences beat, and the conflict question is a health care governance angle, so the follow up is left to that beat and cross referenced here.
