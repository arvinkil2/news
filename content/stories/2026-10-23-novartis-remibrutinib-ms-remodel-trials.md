Novartis presented the REMODEL-1 and REMODEL-2 results at the MSToronto2026 congress on Friday, reporting annualized relapse rates of 0.073 and 0.080 on remibrutinib against 0.112 and 0.116 on teriflunomide, Sanofi's Aubagio, in about 2,000 patients with relapsing multiple sclerosis. MRI activity fell sharply, with T2 lesion activity down 65 percent and 70 percent across the two trials and gadolinium-enhancing T1 lesions down 66 percent, and more than 92 percent of remibrutinib patients remained free from six-month confirmed disability progression over 24 months. No liver safety signal appeared, including no Hy's Law cases, liver enzyme elevations above three times the upper limit of normal occurred in 2.0 percent of remibrutinib patients against 7.4 percent on teriflunomide, and Novartis said it plans to seek regulatory approval for relapsing multiple sclerosis globally.
