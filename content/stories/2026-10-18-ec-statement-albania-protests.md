The European Commission condemned the use of violence during protests in Albania, pointing to arson, attacks on state institutions and destruction of public and private property in Tirana and other cities, and said violence has no place in a democratic society. It said those responsible should be held to account through due process, and that any response to the violence must remain within the law and proportionate.

The Commission also affirmed freedom of assembly and expression as fundamental rights and core European values that must be respected and exercised peacefully, and welcomed the Albanian authorities' commitment to protect the right to protest. In a candidate country firmly committed to the EU accession process and making important progress on its EU path, it said, grievances should be handled through constructive dialogue and in a peaceful manner.
