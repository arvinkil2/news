Steenkampskraal. The operator of South Africa's Steenkampskraal monazite mine and France's Remedy Metals have signed a non binding agreement to test whether rare earths can be separated at the mine, starting with ore samples in a laboratory before any decision on a pilot plant at site, Mining Weekly reported on Wednesday. The mine's finance chief, Tyrone Searle, told the Northern Miner in October that Steenkampskraal has lined up buyers in China for its first concentrate sales, expected in early 2027. Separation at source would move the operation beyond concentrate sales into the part of the chain where China holds 91 percent of global refining capacity.
