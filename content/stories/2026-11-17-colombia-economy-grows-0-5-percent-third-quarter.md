Colombia's economy grew 0.5 percent in the third quarter of 2026 from a year earlier, the national statistics office DANE reported, the weakest year on year advance since the first quarter of 2024 and a sharp slowdown from the second quarter's revised 3.6 percent growth. On a seasonally adjusted basis the economy contracted 0.8 percent from the second quarter, and growth for 2026 to date stands at 2.1 percent. The result came in below all six financial institution forecasts DANE compiled for the quarter, which ranged from 0.8 percent at Banco Davivienda to 2.2 percent at Bancolombia, with the central bank and Banco de Bogotá at 1.0 percent.

Agriculture was the main drag: its output fell 6.7 percent and subtracted 0.8 percentage points from value added, more than the whole economy grew, as crop production dropped, coffee fell 11.2 percent and fishing fell 35.4 percent. Manufacturing also fell, down 1.2 percent, while mining grew 7.8 percent on a 43.1 percent rise in gold extraction and entertainment activities grew 5.2 percent. Household consumption rose 1.7 percent and became the main support for demand, but imports rose 9.8 percent and subtracted 2 percentage points from growth, while exports fell 1.8 percent.
