United Auto Workers President Shawn Fain declared victory on Friday in his race for a second term leading the 400,000-member union, taking a majority of the vote and easily topping five challengers in preliminary ballot totals released by a federal monitor overseeing the vote. The Detroit Three automakers were watching the balloting closely ahead of 2028 contract negotiations; Fain said he plans to push to restore pensions for all workers, a benefit cut for new hires in 2007. GM, Ford and Stellantis, already grappling with weaker demand, tariff costs and an expensive rollback of their electric-vehicle plans, are expected to push back hard on any meaningful labor-cost increases.
