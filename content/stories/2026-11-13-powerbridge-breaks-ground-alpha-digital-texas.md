PowerBridge has broken ground on Alpha Digital, its flagship powered land campus in Reeves County near Pecos, West Texas, Datacenter Dynamics reported. The site spans about 3,400 acres and is planned at more than 2 gigawatts of co-located power generation, electrical infrastructure and fibre conduit, close to the Waha natural gas hub, with initial site work having started last month.

The first phase is expected to consist of 300 megawatts of generation capacity, expandable over time, with first power expected in the fourth quarter of 2027 and deployment continuing through the first half of 2028. Liberty Energy's Liberty Power Innovations unit will design, develop and operate modular natural gas generation at the campus under a joint venture with PowerBridge, which is developing the powered campus while future data centre buildings are to be built by other parties; no end users have been announced.
