CoreWeave has signed UK autonomous driving company Wayve to a multi-year agreement under which Wayve will train its foundation models on CoreWeave Cloud using Nvidia Blackwell GPUs. Wayve already used Weights and Biases, the AI developer platform CoreWeave acquired in 2025, and has now expanded onto CoreWeave's cloud itself; the company also remains a Microsoft Azure customer.

Wayve's models are the intelligence layer behind its AI Driver for driverless taxis and self-driving personal cars, and its engineering chief said access to purpose-built training, evaluation and simulation infrastructure should accelerate commercial deployment. No financial terms for the agreement were disclosed.
