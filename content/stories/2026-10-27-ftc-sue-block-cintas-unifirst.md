The Federal Trade Commission has indicated to uniform rental firm Cintas that it will sue to block its proposed 5.5 billion dollar acquisition of rival UniFirst, Semafor reported on Tuesday, citing people familiar with the matter, according to Reuters. The commission has not yet voted on whether to sue, but its staff have privately expressed concerns about the companies' combined market share, the report added.

Cintas agreed in March to buy UniFirst in a cash and stock deal after at least three formal approaches since 2022. Shares of UniFirst fell 8 percent on Tuesday and Cintas was down about 5 percent, Reuters reported, and neither company nor the FTC immediately responded to requests for comment.
