Several explosions were heard on Qeshm Island in southern Iran on Sunday, Iran's state run IRNA news agency reported. An IRNA reporter on the island said it appeared that several places on Qeshm had been attacked, but provincial authorities were not confirming the situation, and no cause, casualties or damage were reported.

The report follows a round of explosions on the island on Friday that President Donald Trump later confirmed was a US strike on an anti-aircraft device. Neither the United States nor any independent source had confirmed a new strike at publication.
