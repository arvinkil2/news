The US Treasury has set the composite rate on Series I savings bonds issued from November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027 at 5.51 percent, up from the 4.26 percent composite on bonds issued in the previous six months. The new rate combines a fixed rate of 1.40 percent, raised from 0.90 percent at the May setting, with a semiannual inflation rate of 2.04 percent, up from 1.67 percent. TreasuryDirect sets the fixed and inflation rates every May 1 and November 1, and the fixed rate applies for the life of every bond issued in the six months that follow.
