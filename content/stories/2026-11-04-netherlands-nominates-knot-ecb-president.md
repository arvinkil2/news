The Netherlands is nominating Klaas Knot as its candidate for president of the European Central Bank, NOS reported. The post is falling vacant because Christine Lagarde is leaving earlier than scheduled, according to the report.

Knot led De Nederlandsche Bank, the Dutch central bank, until 2025, and sat on the ECB's Governing Council throughout that tenure. Finland's Yle also reported the nomination.
