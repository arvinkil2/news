President Volodymyr Zelensky said the United States had proposed holding trilateral talks among the United States, Russia and Ukraine to end the war before Christmas and that he had agreed, the Washington Post reported on Saturday, citing Ukrainian media. He suggested the meeting could take place on the sidelines of the G20 summit on December 14 and 15 at the Trump National Doral resort in Miami, and said the sooner results come, the better.

President Donald Trump has invited President Vladimir Putin, whose country is a G20 member, but has not yet invited Zelensky; Germany has urged Trump to invite him, saying other countries cannot decide Ukraine's fate without Ukraine. The Kremlin has not said whether Putin, who has not attended a G20 summit since 2019, will attend. The three sides last met in Abu Dhabi on November 10 and 11, where Russia and Ukraine agreed their largest prisoner exchange to date but remained far apart on security guarantees and territory, and the timing and format of a next round had not been decided.
