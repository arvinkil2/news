ASML's third quarter came in ahead of the market on every line the company guides. Net sales were a record 11.662 billion euros, toward the high end of guidance and up from 9.326 billion in the second quarter, with net income of 4.960 billion euros and a gross margin of 57.5 percent, above guidance on service and upgrade sales of 3.289 billion euros. The fourth quarter guide, 14.2 to 15.2 billion euros of sales at a gross margin near 57 percent, sits above the expectations Reuters cited, and full year sales are now put at 44 to 45 billion euros.

Chief executive Christophe Fouquet said customers are raising their short and medium term demand forecasts on the size and duration of AI investment, that some are asking about placing orders for 2029 and 2030, and that supply as planned for 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet demand, so the company is studying a capacity increase for 2028 beyond the 30 percent rise already flagged. ASML also bought back about 4.1 billion euros of its own shares in the quarter and set an interim dividend of 1.88 euros a share, payable November 4.
