IDC figures reported Monday put third-quarter PC shipments at 62.7 million units, down from 78.5 million a year earlier, a deterioration from the second quarter's 3.8 percent decline. BusinessDay attributed the slump to chipmakers directing capacity toward high-bandwidth memory for AI systems, tightening conventional DRAM and NAND supply and pushing device prices higher.

The declines were broad: HP shipments fell 30.9 percent, Dell 25 percent, Lenovo 22.6 percent, Apple 11.3 percent and ASUS 8.6 percent, per the report.
