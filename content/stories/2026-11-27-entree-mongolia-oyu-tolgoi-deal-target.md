Entrée Resources and the Mongolian government have agreed to work towards a deal by the end of March 2027 on the Shivee Tolgoi and Javkhlant mining licences, which are held by Entrée's local subsidiary and must transfer to mine operator Oyu Tolgoi LLC before underground development can resume at the Hugo North Extension copper-gold deposit. At issue is whether the state takes a stake of up to 34 percent in the subsidiary or accepts a royalty instead; Mongolia's Parliament has the final say, the next round of talks is scheduled for January, and the two sides can extend beyond March by mutual consent.

Entrée holds a 20 percent carried interest in Hugo North Extension alongside Oyu Tolgoi LLC, which is 34 percent owned by Mongolia and 66 percent by Rio Tinto. Entrée shares rose 3.3 percent to $2.19 in Toronto on Friday, valuing the company at about $486 million.
