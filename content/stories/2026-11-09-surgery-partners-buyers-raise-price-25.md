The consortium taking Surgery Partners private has raised its price. Amendment No. 1 to the merger agreement, dated November 8, 2026 and filed with the SEC on Monday, increases the per share consideration to $25.00, from the $23.50 agreed on October 23, and also raises the company termination fee and provides for reimbursement of certain buyer expenses.

The amendment adds a timing protection for shareholders: if the closing has not occurred by March 1, 2027, holders of common stock receive monthly cash payments, with corresponding payments in respect of company awards, until the deal completes. The buyer group is led by Spyglass Topco, an affiliate of Bon Secours Mercy Health, whose HealthSpan Partners agreed in October to take the ambulatory surgery operator private in a deal then valued at about $5.7 billion including debt.
