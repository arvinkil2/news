**Latest.** The US Treasury accepted $1.183 billion in Tuesday's liquidity support buyback of nominal coupon securities in the 20 year to 30 year maturity bucket, out of $4.548 billion offered against a maximum purchase amount of $6 billion. The operation, run at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, accepted 8 of 35 eligible issues, filled about 20 percent of the maximum, and settles on November 18. The accepted amount deviates about 74 percent from the $4.519 billion mean of the previous four operations in the same bucket.

The US Treasury will buy back nominal coupon securities in the 20 year to 30 year maturity bucket on Tuesday, November 17, in a liquidity support operation with a maximum purchase amount of $6 billion. The operation runs at 1:40 p.m. Eastern, 35 issues are eligible, and settlement is on November 18. Amounts offered and accepted will be published after the operation runs.
