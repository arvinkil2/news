The disbursement is part of the 90 billion euro Ukraine Support Loan adopted in February 2026, split indicatively into 60 billion euros for defence and 30 billion euros for budget support over 2026 and 2027. The Commission said total EU and member state support since 2022 now stands at 231.5 billion euros, including 3.8 billion euros from proceeds of immobilised Russian assets, and that 29.64 billion euros remains available for the rest of 2026 subject to agreed conditions and monitoring.
