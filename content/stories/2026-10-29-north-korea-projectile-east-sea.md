North Korea launched an unidentified projectile toward the East Sea, South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff announced, according to a Yonhap breaking news flash transmitted early on October 29 Korea time. The flash gave no further detail: it did not identify the projectile type, the launch site or where the projectile landed.

## Latest

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said North Korea fired more than ten short-range ballistic missiles toward the East Sea, in a later Yonhap breaking flash. The flash carried no launch time, launch site or flight range, and the Joint Chiefs said it was analysing the projectiles' specifications and range.

Japan's Defence Ministry said an object believed to be a ballistic missile appeared to have fallen outside Japan's exclusive economic zone, Yonhap reported from Tokyo. The Japan Coast Guard said that as of 12:55 p.m. no damage to vessels underway had been confirmed, and the Japanese government set up a crisis management centre at the Prime Minister's Office and convened the relevant ministries to assess any damage.

If confirmed as ballistic, the launch would be North Korea's first in 17 days: on October 12 Pyongyang fired ballistic missiles from the Pyongyang area toward the East Sea, a launch it later described through state media as a tactical ballistic missile test. Reuters noted that North Korea has conducted missile tests on more than 10 occasions this year.
