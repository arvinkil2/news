Chevron is closing in on a deal to buy a majority stake in Beacon Offshore Energy, the Blackstone-backed deepwater producer, in a transaction that values the business at roughly $5 billion, the Financial Times reported on Monday, in an account carried by Reuters. Chevron has drawn ahead of rival bidders for Beacon, which controls 70 deepwater leases across some 400,000 gross acres in the Gulf of Mexico.

The pursuit fits Chevron's stated aim of lifting its US Gulf of Mexico production to 300,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day by the end of this year. Chevron declined to comment on the report and Beacon did not immediately respond to a request for comment, so no agreement has been confirmed by either side.
