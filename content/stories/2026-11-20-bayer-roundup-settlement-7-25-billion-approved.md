Judge Timothy Boyer of a state court in St Louis, Missouri, on Friday approved Bayer's $7.25 billion settlement of Roundup cancer lawsuits, finding it fair, reasonable and adequate, and overruling objections that individual payouts would be too low compared with earlier settlements. The deal, announced in February, aims to resolve nearly all current and future Roundup claims in the United States, where Bayer had faced about 65,000 claims in state and federal courts.

The settlement excludes some lawsuits still pending in federal court, about 200 of which Bayer says remain in litigation. Bayer chief executive Bill Anderson called the final approval a decisive milestone in the company's strategy to mitigate the risks of pending and future Roundup litigation.
