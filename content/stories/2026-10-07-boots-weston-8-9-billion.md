Wittington Investments, the holding company of the Canadian branch of the billionaire Weston family, is buying Boots from private equity owner Sycamore Partners and the Pessina family in a $8.9 billion (£6.7 billion) deal announced on October 7. The purchase covers Boots' retail and pharmacy operations in the UK and Ireland, Boots Opticians, the No7 Beauty Company, and its Thailand and franchise businesses. Galen Weston, chairman of Wittington, will become chairman of Boots.

The deal brings the Westons back to the British high street four years after they sold Selfridges for £4 billion in 2022. Wittington already controls Canadian grocer Loblaw and pharmacy chain Shoppers Drug Mart, giving the family deep retail and pharmacy operating experience. Sycamore had acquired Boots only a year ago through its $23.7 billion takeover of Walgreens Boots Alliance; a sale ends speculation about a London stock market listing for the chain. Boots generated £7.5 billion in revenue in its latest reported financial year, with pre-tax profit up 25 percent to £337 million.
