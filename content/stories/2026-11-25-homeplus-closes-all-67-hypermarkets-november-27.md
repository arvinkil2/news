Homeplus, the South Korean hypermarket chain in court-led rehabilitation, will temporarily suspend operations at all 67 of its stores from November 27, saying funds to purchase goods have run out after sales fell short of target and supplier payments fell behind. Most fresh food deliveries have been cut off since November 20, online delivery stops on November 26, and the company said supply disruption had also made it hard to pay utility bills, though tenant-operated mall sections inside the stores will stay open.

The stores had already closed once this year, reopening on August 13 after the company secured 200 billion won in emergency debtor-in-possession financing, and its rehabilitation plan was approved in September. Homeplus said it has recently received a letter of intent from a prospective buyer, but that reaching a contract would take time for consultation with the court and creditors and for full due diligence.
