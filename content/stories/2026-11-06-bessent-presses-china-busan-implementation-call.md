Latest: China's account of the same video call differs in emphasis from Washington's. Xinhua, cited by Agencia EFE, said He Lifeng conveyed Beijing's serious concern over the latest US economic restrictions, a reference to the sanctions announced on Thursday against CEIEC and Beijing H&C Technologies over alleged supplies of military equipment to Cuba. EFE reported no specific demand or warning from He, and Xinhua described the exchanges as frank, deep and constructive, with both sides agreeing to make fuller use of the consultation mechanism and to manage differences appropriately ahead of the presidents' meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen in two weeks.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and US Trade Representative Jameison Greer spoke with Vice Premier He Lifeng of China on Friday to discuss the bilateral economic relationship and implementation of the Busan arrangement, the Treasury said in a readout. Both sides reaffirmed the value of maintaining direct and candid communication.

Bessent emphasized the importance of China fully and immediately implementing its commitments made in Busan and raised concerns about recent Chinese actions. He underscored that continued progress depends on concrete results and that the United States will closely assess China's implementation of the arrangement, and he reiterated the administration's commitment to trade and economic policies that benefit American workers, farmers and businesses.
