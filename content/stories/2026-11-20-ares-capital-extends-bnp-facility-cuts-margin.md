Ares Capital Corporation and ARCC FB Funding LLC, its wholly owned subsidiary, entered into an eleventh amendment to their revolving credit and security agreement with BNP Paribas on November 18, extending the end of the reinvestment period from March 20, 2028 to September 20, 2028 and the stated maturity date from March 20, 2030 to September 20, 2030. The amendment also cut the applicable margin from 1.90 percent to 1.80 percent during the reinvestment period and from 2.40 percent to 2.30 percent after it. Ares Capital said the facility's other terms remained materially unchanged.
