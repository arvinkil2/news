OFAC designated the Comancheros and the 'Ndrangheta under Executive Order 14059, saying the two organizations provide essential logistical and operational support that helps terrorist cartels expand their international reach, with the Comancheros facilitating multi-hundred-kilogram cocaine shipments for cartels including the Sinaloa Cartel and CJNG and the 'Ndrangheta controlling a significant share of the European cocaine trade. The designations also cover Comanchero leaders Alexandre Varela Villar, Allan John Norman Meehan, Pasilika and Vetekina Naufahu and associate Marco Francis Coffen, and the Toronto 'Ndrangheta leadership, including Angelo and Cosimo Figliomeni, Francesco and Rocco Remo Commisso, Vincenzo DeMaria and Luigi Vescio Sr., along with the Vescio funeral-home companies in Ontario and Vescio family members. Treasury said the action was coordinated with the Homeland Security Task Force, the FBI, the DEA and Homeland Security Investigations, and with the Australian Federal Police, New Zealand Police, Italy's Guardia di Finanza, the RCMP and York Regional Police, and that all property of the designated persons within US jurisdiction is blocked.
