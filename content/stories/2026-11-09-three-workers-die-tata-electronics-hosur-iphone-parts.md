Three contract workers died of asphyxiation at Tata Electronics' factory in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, which makes parts for Apple iPhones, Reuters reported, citing a source with direct knowledge of the matter. The three died during maintenance work on a water tank at the facility, and The New Indian Express reported the deaths happened on Monday.

Tamil Nadu's industries minister said in a post on X that the safety and dignity of every worker must remain paramount and that the incident will be thoroughly investigated, with appropriate action taken on the findings. Tata Electronics did not respond to a Reuters request for comment.
