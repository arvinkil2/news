## Latest (Sunday, October 11, late morning): IOM raises the toll to 139 dead, 84 missing

The International Organization for Migration now says at least 139 people died and 84 were missing after an overcrowded boat carrying an estimated 305 people capsized off the Djibouti coast on Saturday night en route to Yemen across the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Eighty survivors, all Ethiopian nationals, were rescued, and the bodies recovered so far include 52 men, 42 women and 15 children. The figures were given by IOM Chief of Mission in Djibouti Alexandre Coissac in an IOM statement issued Sunday, and Djiboutian authorities are continuing search and rescue.

The IOM says most of those attempting the crossing are from Ethiopia and Somalia, that it detected more than 20,000 crossings from Djibouti to Yemen in the past month, and that more than 900 migrants were killed attempting the passage in 2025, making this one of the deadliest shipwrecks on the route in years. The IOM urged donors to strengthen support for Djibouti's response.
