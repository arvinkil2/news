At least 109 migrants died, among them 15 children, and another 116 were missing after a boat carrying 350 people capsized in the sea north of Obock in Djibouti, according to a statement from the International Organization for Migration. Survivors described the vessel overturning en route to Yemen, a major destination for migrants from East Africa and the Horn of Africa trying to reach Gulf countries for work. The wreck is one of the deadliest on the Djibouti crossing in years, following a series of recent shipwrecks off the same coast.
