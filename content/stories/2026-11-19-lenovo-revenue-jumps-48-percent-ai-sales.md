Lenovo's revenue rose 48 percent in its second quarter to $30.2 billion, ahead of an LSEG consensus estimate of $27.6 billion from six analysts, Reuters reported, driven by AI-related sales of servers and other hardware. AI-related revenue rose 79 percent to $10.8 billion, 36 percent of the group total, and the infrastructure solutions group, its server and data centre business, had its strongest quarter ever, with revenue up 178 percent to $11.3 billion; the PC, tablet and smartphone division, about 60 percent of revenue, grew 15 percent.

On Hong Kong accounting standards Lenovo recorded a net loss of $640 million, against a $340 million profit a year earlier, while adjusted net profit stripping out non-cash items tied to warrants and convertible bonds issued under its partnership with Saudi company Alat rose 166 percent to $1.4 billion. Lenovo shares closed down 5.5 percent at 16.25 Hong Kong dollars.
