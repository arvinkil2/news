Hong Kong's exports surged 65.6 percent year on year in October to a record HK$764.5 billion (about $98 billion), the fastest monthly growth since the 70.7 percent recorded in April 1956, which remains the highest since records began in January 1953, provisional Census and Statistics Department figures showed on Thursday. Imports rose 56.2 percent to HK$783.8 billion, leaving a trade deficit of HK$19.3 billion for the month and HK$438.5 billion for the first ten months of the year, over which exports rose 47.4 percent.

A government spokesman said AI-related electronic products again drove the growth, with exports to Asia up 66.1 percent, led by Taiwan, up 198.3 percent, and Singapore, up 98.9 percent. He said robust global AI investment should keep supporting Hong Kong's merchandise trade, while warning that international trade and technology policy developments, the risks of the rapid AI investment expansion and geopolitical tensions warrant close monitoring, after the United States proposed new tariffs on 14 major economies including China.
