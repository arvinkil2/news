Iron Mountain reported third quarter revenue of $1,991 million, up 13.5 percent from $1,754 million a year earlier, with adjusted EBITDA of $748.0 million and adjusted funds from operations of $458.4 million, or $1.53 per share, up 15.9 percent. The company raised its full year 2026 guidance to revenue of $7,950 million to $8,020 million and AFFO per share of $5.94 to $6.00, and increased its quarterly dividend 15 percent to $0.993 per share.

Data center leasing was 102 megawatts in the quarter, taking signed leases to 137 megawatts for the year to date, and the company said its data center, digital and asset lifecycle management growth businesses together grew more than 30 percent year over year.
