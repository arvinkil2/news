**Commission partially greenlights Romania's 2.67 billion euro recovery payment.** The European Commission partially approved Romania's fifth payment request under NextGenerationEU, worth 2.67 billion euros, after assessing the milestones attached to the request. Partial greenlights release the portion tied to completed reforms and investments while holding back the rest pending delivery.

**Slovakia's 548 million euro request also partially approved.** The Commission likewise partially greenlit Slovakia's eighth payment request, for 548 million euros, under the same recovery fund. Together the two decisions keep the fund's tranche-by-tranche disbursement model, in which payment follows verified milestones rather than budget plans, at the center of EU fiscal transfers this week.

**CMA opens Mari and ATG Entertainment merger inquiry.** The UK Competition and Markets Authority has opened a merger inquiry into Mari and ATG Entertainment, the live entertainment group. The inquiry is the first formal step in the authority's review of the transaction; no findings have been published.

**Senate Republicans unveil nearly $1.1 trillion defense bill.** Senate appropriators released a defense appropriations bill of nearly $1.1 trillion, folding reconciliation priorities into the annual Pentagon spending package. The bill sets the opening Senate position for negotiations with the House and the administration over fiscal 2027 defense funding.
