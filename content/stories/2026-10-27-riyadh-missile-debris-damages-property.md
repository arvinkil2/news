Debris from two intercepted ballistic missiles damaged a building, a construction project and a vehicle in Riyadh on Tuesday, Saudi Civil Defense said, as reported by Arab News. A Civil Defense spokesperson said the missiles were launched by Yemen's Iran backed Houthi militia and destroyed before the debris fell in the capital, and crews responded under established procedures; the spokesperson did not say in the report whether anyone was injured.

Earlier on Tuesday, air defences intercepted and destroyed three ballistic missiles aimed at the Eastern Province and shot down four drones targeting southern Saudi Arabia, coalition spokesperson Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Maliki said in the same report. Civil Defense had issued warnings for the Eastern Province and Al-Kharj at around 3 a.m. local time before giving the all clear.
