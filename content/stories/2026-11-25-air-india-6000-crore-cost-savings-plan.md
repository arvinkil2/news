Air India's chief executive and managing director Tewolde Gebremariam outlined a 6,000 crore rupee cost-saving programme at an employee townhall on Wednesday, targeting savings of 20 percent in non-fuel expenditure and 5 percent in fuel costs, The Hindu BusinessLine reported. Of the target, potential savings of 3,500 crore rupees have been identified and 1,500 crore rupees achieved, and a team of about 11 senior executives is driving the exercise.

Air India and Air India Express will increasingly work as one group, sharing enabling functions such as finance, human resources and information technology while retaining separate air operator certificates, brands and business models. Gebremariam said cost per available seat kilometre is much higher than revenue per available seat kilometre, which is why the group is losing money, and that fuel prices paid this year were almost double last year's levels.
