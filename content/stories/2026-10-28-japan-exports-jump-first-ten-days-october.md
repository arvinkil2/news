Japan's exports rose 30.7 percent from a year earlier to 3,822,613 million yen in the first ten days of October 2026, while imports rose 18.9 percent to 4,162,470 million yen, leaving a trade deficit of 339,857 million yen, the Ministry of Finance said in its provisional release. The deficit was 41.1 percent smaller than the 577,469 million yen shortfall recorded in the same ten days of October 2025.

The early-month figures mark a turn from September, when imports rose faster than exports over the full month and the deficit nearly doubled from a year earlier. Ten-day provisional totals are volatile and subject to revision when the full-month release lands, but the direction is clear: export growth is running well ahead of import growth at the start of the fourth quarter.
