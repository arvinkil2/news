Japan's Ministry of Finance sold 20-year government bonds on Tuesday at a weighted average price of 100.87 per 100 yen, a yield of 3.723 percent, with the lowest accepted price at 100.80, a yield of 3.729 percent. Competitive bids totalled 3,139.0 billion yen against 537.1 billion yen accepted, a bid-to-cover ratio of 5.84, and a further 162.8 billion yen went to JGB market special participants on a non-price-competitive basis, bringing the issue to about 700 billion yen. Bloomberg reported the bidding as the strongest demand on record for a 20-year sale, a sharp turn from the soft 20-year readings earlier this year, and the result lands ahead of the ministry's October 27 liquidity enhancement auction and the Bank of Japan's October 29 to 30 policy meeting.
