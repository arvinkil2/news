Cloudflare announced Friday it is acquiring Deno, the startup behind the Deno programming runtime co-founded by Bert Belder and Node.js creator Ryan Dahl, per TechCrunch. Cloudflare principal engineer Kenton Varda said the deal will improve the Workers programming model and platform for running software on Cloudflare's network, and Dahl said his team is joining to make Workers "a mainstream way to build servers." Deno had raised $26 million including a Sequoia-led Series A; financial terms of the acquisition were not disclosed.
