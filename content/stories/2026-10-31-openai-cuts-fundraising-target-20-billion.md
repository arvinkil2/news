OpenAI is now seeking up to 20 billion dollars in its current funding round, down from an earlier target of 30 billion dollars, at a post-money valuation of 1.25 trillion dollars, CNBC reported on Saturday, confirming the Financial Times report of Friday that the target had been cut by 10 billion dollars and that some investors had reduced their allocations. The Wall Street Journal reported the company has secured 11 billion dollars so far and met a tepid response from some large investors, and TechCrunch reported a first close is expected below the 20 billion dollar target, with a later retail offering for wealthy individuals possibly bringing the total to the target. Investors from the United Arab Emirates are leading the round alongside Coatue, and the cut lands in the week AI-linked stocks sold off on doubts about returns on data centre spending.
