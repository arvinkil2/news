The US-backed Board of Peace, created to oversee the Gaza ceasefire and reconstruction, told the UN Security Council in a report submitted on Tuesday that the truce between Israel and Hamas may be on the brink of collapse without immediate steps to implement the 2025 deal. The board said the distance between what has been agreed and what is happening on the ground remains the principal risk to the process, and that a ceasefire that delivers nothing visible cannot be sustained indefinitely.

The board called on Israel to decide without delay on the conditions for training, deploying and returning civilian police to Gaza, said Israeli restrictions on supplies and equipment were impeding relief efforts ahead of winter, and said neither party may treat the other's delay as a licence for its own inaction. It said both Israel and Hamas have fallen far short of their commitments under the deal.
