Euro zone private sector growth slowed to its weakest pace in four months in October, with the S&P Global flash composite PMI output index falling to 51.9 from 53.1 in September, below every forecast in a Reuters poll that had expected 52.7. Services activity fell to a two-month low of 51.7 and manufacturing eased to 52.4, while France pointed to contraction again and French business confidence hit its lowest since 2012 outside the pandemic. Input costs accelerated for a second month on higher energy, fuel and transport costs linked in part to the Middle East war, and hiring came close to a standstill, leaving sustained growth alongside stubborn inflation ahead of next week's European Central Bank decision.
