Diversified Energy Company completed its previously announced acquisition of Birch Permian Holdings, Inc. and affiliated companies on November 19, 2026, for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $1.8 billion, including the repayment of indebtedness and subject to purchase price adjustments. The acquisition brings approximately 46,000 net mineral acres in the Midland Basin, 500 gross operated wells (480 net), and integrated midstream and water infrastructure assets.

A large part of the price was funded at closing with securitized debt. DP Pecos LLC, a bankruptcy remote special purpose vehicle, issued $1.455 billion of fixed rate asset backed securities in a private placement: $1.0 billion of 6.680 percent Class A-1 notes due 2046, $330 million of 7.341 percent Class A-2 notes due 2046 and $125 million of 10.756 percent Class B notes due 2046. The notes are secured by a first priority interest in the acquired Midland Basin wellbore interests, related accounts and hedge agreements, have an anticipated repayment date of November 2031 and a legal final maturity of November 2046.

The balance of the purchase price came from borrowings under Diversified's revolving credit facility and the $50 million deposit paid in September 2026. Net proceeds of the notes also repaid obligations under the Birch Holdco and Birch Permian II credit facilities and covered transaction costs.

Diversified will file the acquired businesses' financial statements and pro forma financial information by amendment within 71 days. The structure, buying producing assets with long dated securitized debt matched against their decline curves, remains the company's signature financing model, now applied at its largest scale this year.
