Savannah River Site officials declared a Site Area Emergency at 5:39 p.m. on Wednesday, October 28, after a fire was reported at the Savannah River National Laboratory at the site near Aiken, South Carolina, the site's Emergency Response Organization said in its News Release #2, issued at 7:30 p.m. The fire has since been extinguished, and no contamination has been reported outside the affected facility, the release said.

As a precaution, barricades at the site entrances have been closed to incoming traffic, rail, river and air traffic in the vicinity is being restricted, and employees in affected areas who are not involved in emergency response or shutdown operations have been sheltered, the release said. State and local government agencies have been notified, and residents were told to stay tuned to local radio and television for any instructions from state officials.
