Doña Ana County's Board of County Commissioners voted 5-0 on October 27 to authorize a potential notice of breach under the Project Jupiter industrial revenue bond agreements, according to Organ Mountain News. The dispute centers on the proposed El Paso Electric connection for the Santa Teresa campus.

At the commission's October 13 meeting, Oracle's proposed energy services agreement with El Paso Electric, $10 million a year for 10 years with proposed credits of up to $55 million a year for up to three years, was discussed without approval, and commissioners said an amendment to the $165 billion bond agreements would be required. The October 27 vote moves the county from discussion toward a formal breach process. What a notice would demand, and on what timetable, is not in the reporting.

The standing record for the project, including the bond structure and the October 13 meeting, is on the [Project Jupiter page](/projects/project-jupiter/).
