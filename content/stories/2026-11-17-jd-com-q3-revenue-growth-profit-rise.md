JD.com said net revenues rose 3.5 percent to RMB309.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, returning to growth, while income from operations swung to RMB5.2 billion from a loss of RMB1.1 billion a year earlier. Net income attributable to ordinary shareholders rose to RMB6.5 billion from RMB5.3 billion, and diluted net income per ADS rose 35.4 percent to RMB4.59.

JD Retail, the core business, reported operating income of RMB15.1 billion at a margin of 5.8 percent, close to the 5.9 percent recorded a year earlier. Chief executive Sandy Xu said the quarter included the successful closing of the Ceconomy acquisition, and chief financial officer Ian Su Shan said losses in the new businesses, including JD Food Delivery, narrowed both from a year earlier and from the previous quarter.
