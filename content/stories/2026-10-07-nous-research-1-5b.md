Nous Research confirmed Wednesday it has raised $90 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation, bringing the three-year-old startup's total funding to $158 million. The round was led by Robot Ventures and includes Nvidia, Union Square Ventures, Menlo Ventures, Samsung and 1789 Capital, and will fund "Hermes for Businesses," letting companies deploy customized AI agents for multi-step workflows while keeping data private.

The startup says its open-source Hermes agent has been cloned more than 24 million times and accounts for about 2.5 percent of global AI token usage, and the Wall Street Journal reported it was near $36 million in annualized revenue by mid-September with expectations to pass $100 million before year end.
