China's foreign ministry announced sanctions on Monday against Paraguay's communications minister Gustavo Villate and Belize's foreign minister Francis Fonseca over their countries' ties with Taiwan, per Al Jazeera. The measures, effective the same day, freeze any assets the two hold in China, deny them entry visas to Hong Kong and Macau, and prohibit Chinese organisations and individuals from transactions or cooperation with them. Beijing said the ministers had taken a series of egregious actions and statements damaging China's core interests, two days after Taiwan's National Day, which Villate marked with a social media post reaffirming Paraguay's friendship with Taiwan. Paraguay and Belize are two of 12 countries that maintain formal diplomatic relations with Taiwan.
