The Reserve Bank of India's second 3 day variable rate repo auction, held November 20 under the liquidity adjustment facility, allotted 25,007 crore rupees against a notified amount of 25,000 crore. Total bids received were 48,040 crore rupees, nearly twice the amount on offer. The cut off rate was 5.58 percent and the weighted average rate 5.61 percent, with bids at the cut off allotted at 50.24 percent.

The operation was the second 3 day repo of the day, following an earlier auction whose announcement and result the RBI published the same morning. Running two same tenor operations in one day, and then scheduling more, is the central bank meeting a liquidity demand that a single notified amount did not satisfy.

The RBI has announced a further 4 day variable rate repo auction under the LAF for Monday November 23. The sequence, repeated short tenor injections at rates just above 5.5 percent, is liquidity management rather than a policy signal: the repo window is being used to keep overnight conditions orderly while banks' demand for funds runs ahead of the amounts first offered.
