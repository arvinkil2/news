The Financial Stability Board finalised its sound practices for responsible AI adoption in finance on Thursday, following a public consultation launched in June, organising 12 practices around organisation-wide governance and risk management across the full AI lifecycle. The governance group puts boards and senior management in charge of aligning AI adoption with strategy and risk appetite, while the lifecycle group covers use-case risk assessment, data governance, explainability, performance evaluation, human oversight, cyber risk and third-party AI dependencies. The board said it strongly encourages financial institutions to reference the practices in their own frameworks and that it will update them as the technology evolves.
