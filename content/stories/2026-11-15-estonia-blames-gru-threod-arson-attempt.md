Intruders were found at Threod Systems in Viimsi, outside Tallinn, shortly after 3 a.m. on Saturday trying to break into the building to set it on fire and cause an explosion, Prosecutor General Astrid Asi said on Sunday, and the company's security staff stopped the attack before it caused significant damage. Eight suspects, all residents of neighbouring Latvia, were detained and five have been remanded in custody, and Estonia's Internal Security Service said its intelligence showed the attack was commissioned by Russia's GRU military intelligence agency.

Prime Minister Kristen Michal called the attempt part of Russia's broader hybrid war against Europe, aimed at reducing support for Ukraine, and said Estonia is preparing for the possibility of closing its border with Russia. The attempt follows an August arson attack on Milrem Robotics, which supplies unmanned ground vehicles to Ukraine and which Estonian authorities formally blamed on Russian intelligence in September; no Russian response to Sunday's attribution was reported.
