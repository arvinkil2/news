Mapletree Investments has selected Citigroup, DBS Group and Morgan Stanley to work on an initial public offering in Singapore covering part of the company, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified. The offering could raise at least S$1 billion (about $780 million) and a listing could take place as soon as next year. Mapletree has not yet decided which assets would go into the listing, and no company or bank named in the report commented on it.
