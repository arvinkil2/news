The European Commission has published provisional safeguard measures setting import quotas for certain hybrid passenger vehicles, entering into force on December 1, after an investigation initiated on October 30 found that imports have increased significantly since 2023 and that the threat of serious injury to EU manufacturers is imminent. The quotas apply per exporting country, with imports outside them not allowed, and countries that supplied more than 2 percent of EU imports in 2025 receive country-specific quotas set at their 2025 import level increased by a little over 15 percent, while all other origins share a residual quota.

The measures cover full, plug-in and mild hybrid passenger vehicles, apply to imports from all origins, and implement the understanding reached at the EU-China trade consultations on October 9 to moderate China's hybrid exports. The provisional measures run for a maximum of 200 days, and definitive measures may follow within nine months of the investigation's initiation, a timeline that can be extended by up to two months.
