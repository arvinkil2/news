**Latest.** Bahrain said on Sunday that two people were injured in Saturday's attack, a Bahraini citizen and a Pakistani resident, revising the preliminary report of no injuries carried below. The ministry still did not name the facility. Residents told Agence France-Presse they saw smoke rising over Sitra Island, on Bahrain's east coast, where state oil company Bapco runs a refinery among other industrial sites; the report does not confirm that the refinery was the facility hit.

Bahrain's Interior Ministry said on Saturday that an Iranian attack targeted a civilian industrial facility in the kingdom, causing a fire at the site. Civil Defence teams were working to bring the blaze under control, and preliminary reports indicated material damage with no injuries. The ministry did not name the facility or say where it was, and Bahraini authorities had earlier warned residents to move to the nearest safe place.

Iran's semi-official Fars news agency said the Revolutionary Guards had attacked US military bases in Bahrain the same day, an account that names different targets and has not been reconciled with Bahrain's statement. Bahrain, which hosts the headquarters of the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, has reported a series of Iranian attacks since November 13.
