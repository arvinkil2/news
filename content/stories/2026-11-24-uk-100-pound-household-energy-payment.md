Up to 20 million households in England will receive £100 towards their energy costs from January under the Household Energy Payment, the government said on Tuesday as it sent delivery guidance to local authorities. Councils will be asked to begin making payments from 4 January, and 13 million households in council tax bands A to D will receive the payment automatically into their bank accounts where councils hold verified direct debit details.

Households that do not pay by direct debit will be contacted by their council and can choose a bank transfer or a credit on their council tax account, with the scheme open until the end of May. A separate £100 million Discretionary Fund will support vulnerable households facing higher energy costs who fall outside the main scheme, and the Scottish and Welsh governments have confirmed equivalent or similar schemes using Barnett formula funding.
