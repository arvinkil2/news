The Competition and Markets Authority said on Monday it has delivered its report on BT's completed acquisition of TalkTalk Telecommunications and PlatformX Communications to the Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport, meeting the 5pm deadline set under the public interest intervention notice issued on October 5. The report covers whether the deal created a relevant merger situation, whether it has resulted or may result in a substantial lessening of competition, and advice relevant to the Secretary of State's decision, including representations on the public interest grounds of critical national infrastructure, disruption to public services and supply to vulnerable customers. The Secretary of State now decides whether to refer the completed acquisition to a Phase 2 assessment.
