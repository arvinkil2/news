American Tower Corporation said in a Form 8-K filed on November 23 that it has reached a proposed settlement of its claims against Dish Wireless and EchoStar Corporation in litigation in the US Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas and the District Court for the District of Colorado, under which it expects to receive a $525 million cash payment on or before December 31, 2026, from the trust the Federal Communications Commission established as a condition of approving EchoStar's recent spectrum sales, with interest accruing if payment is late. The settlement remains subject to a definitive agreement, Bankruptcy Court approval and changes to the FCC trust procedures, and American Tower said its litigation may continue if those conditions are not met. Datacenter Dynamics reported that Dish is working on settlements with other tower providers, while Crown Castle, which is seeking $3.5 billion from Dish, recently said Dish has not reached out to discuss one.
