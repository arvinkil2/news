Belgian shipping group CMB.TECH said on Tuesday that the market value of a 15-year-old very large crude carrier has reached $165 million, some 25 percent above the $132 million price of a new vessel and more than three and a half times the 10-year average of $46 million for a tanker of that age, according to Clarksons data cited in its third-quarter report. The company said 141 tankers are now dedicated to shuttling crude and products for ship-to-ship transfers in the Gulf of Oman, with round voyages averaging 23 days against an optimal 9 to 10 days.

CMB.TECH reported third-quarter net profit of $373.9 million, against $17.3 million a year earlier, including a $100.2 million gain on the sale of two Suezmax tankers. Its Euronav division recorded average VLCC spot earnings of $149,945 per day in the third quarter, against $30,486 a year earlier, and said fourth-quarter VLCC earnings to date average $635,199 per day on 59 percent of days booked.
