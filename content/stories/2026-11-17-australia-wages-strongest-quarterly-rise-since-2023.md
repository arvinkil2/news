Australia's Wage Price Index rose 1.1 percent in the September quarter 2026, the strongest quarterly wage growth since the December quarter 2023, and rose 3.5 percent over the year, the Australian Bureau of Statistics said. Annual growth edged up from 3.4 percent a year earlier but remains well below the 4.3 percent peak of late 2023.

Private sector wages rose 3.4 percent over the year, up from 3.2 percent a year earlier, while public sector wages rose 3.9 percent, the seventh straight quarter in which public sector growth outpaced the private sector. The bureau said Fair Work Commission wage setting decisions, including the Annual Wage Review increase of 4.75 percent to modern awards, were a key driver of private sector growth this quarter, and that state government enterprise agreement rises for front-line workers such as nurses, midwives and police officers drove the public sector result.
