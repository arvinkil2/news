Democrat Mary Peltola, a former congresswoman, is projected to win Alaska's Senate race, defeating Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan and flipping the seat. Decision Desk HQ put Peltola at 50.12 percent of the vote to Sullivan's 45.79 percent with 94 percent reporting, a lead of 4.33 percentage points, and because she is above 50 percent the state's ranked choice tabulation would not come into play. Peltola, 53, will become the first Alaska Native senator.

The result is not yet certified, and a legal fight may follow. The Justice Department sued in October to block the results if Sullivan lost, arguing that an independent candidate also named Dan Sullivan would confuse voters, so the projection sets up a contest over certification as well as a change in the Senate's composition.
