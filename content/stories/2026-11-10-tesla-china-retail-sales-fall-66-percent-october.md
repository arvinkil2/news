Tesla's retail sales in China fell to 8,817 vehicles in October, down 66 percent from a year earlier and the lowest monthly domestic total since July 2022, according to China Passenger Car Association data reported by Electrek. Giga Shanghai wholesaled 67,829 vehicles in the month, up 10.3 percent year on year, but 59,012 of them were exported, leaving the plant shipping 87 percent of its output abroad. China's wider new energy vehicle retail market fell 10 percent in October, a much smaller decline than Tesla's, and Tesla's domestic sales for the first ten months of 2026 are down 15 percent year on year to 389,905.
