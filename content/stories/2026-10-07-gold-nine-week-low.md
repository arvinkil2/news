Spot gold fell to about $4,102 an ounce on Wednesday, its lowest in nine weeks, after futures markets priced an 86 percent chance the Federal Reserve hikes again in December. The slide came despite Brent crude near $102 a barrel and fresh attacks on Saudi airports, as the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.345 percent and the dollar index held near 102.5. Gold has stopped trading as a haven and is moving tick for tick with the bond market instead.
