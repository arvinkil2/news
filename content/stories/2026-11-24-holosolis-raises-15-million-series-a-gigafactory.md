France-based photovoltaic manufacturer HoloSolis has raised more than €15 million, about $16.5 million, in a Series A funding round to advance its planned 5 GW solar module manufacturing facility at Sarreguemines in the Grand Est region. The financing was structured through several successive investments, with the final closing completed on November 3, 2026.

Crédit Agricole Group joined the shareholder base through four regional banks, and Algebris, Trina Solar and StarCharge also joined as new investors, alongside existing Series A investors Calès Technologies, Forming and Ecolab. Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer advised on the transaction.

The money funds pre-construction work: detailed studies, plans and technical specifications, supplier selection for the production lines, and qualification of module prototypes. The planned factory is a €1 billion project, about $1.1 billion, with construction planned to begin in 2028 and production in 2029. HoloSolis plans a further fundraising round in the coming months to secure construction capital, says it holds more than 30 GW of letters of intent from prospective customers, and has been selected for potential support from the European Commission's Innovation Fund, with the grant agreement being finalised. The company counts total financing and identified public support associated with the project at more than €400 million, at different stages of securing.
