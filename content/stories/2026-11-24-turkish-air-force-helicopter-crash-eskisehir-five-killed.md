A Turkish Air Force AS-532 Cougar helicopter crashed during a training flight in western Turkiye on Tuesday evening, killing five crew members, the Defense Ministry said. The helicopter had taken off from Eskisehir, home to the air force's 1st Main Jet Base Command, and came down near Yorukakcayir, a village around 15 kilometres west of the city centre.

The ministry said the wreckage had been reached and search and rescue operations were continuing, and later confirmed that five air force personnel aboard had been killed. No cause was given. The crash is the second involving the Turkish Armed Forces in two weeks: on November 12 an army Sikorsky S-70 crashed near Malatya in southern Turkiye, killing four crew members.
