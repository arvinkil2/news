E3 Lithium has completed Phase 2 of its demonstration facility at the Clearwater project in Alberta and commenced Phase 3, the final phase of the programme. Phase 2 finished the reservoir testing and brine characterisation work, including a full-scale producer and injector well pair built to the prefeasibility study design.

Phase 3 begins by recommissioning the 30-column direct lithium extraction system, processing brine from the Leduc reservoir produced at the demonstration site through purification and carbonation equipment to make battery-grade lithium carbonate. A second step will build and operate a single larger DLE column to raise flow rate and lithium chloride volume, with a scaled single column targeted for deployment in 2027.

The company describes Phase 3 as the transition from technology development to commercial readiness: its output supports offtake product qualification and generates the operating data that project financiers, strategic partners and offtakers require as Clearwater advances toward being shovel ready. Phase 3 and front-end engineering design are supported by up to $36.5 million in non-repayable funding from Natural Resources Canada's Global Partnerships Initiative.

On the regulatory side, E3 has entered the second phase of the process for its Central Process Facility, with the permit application advancing toward a formal hearing. Front-end engineering deliverables are expected to continue into 2027, which sets the timetable against which a feasibility study and final investment decision would follow.
