Upwind, a San Francisco startup that secures cloud infrastructure in real time, has raised $400 million in a Series C round at a $3.8 billion valuation led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with existing backers Greylock Partners and Craft Ventures also taking part, TechCrunch reported. The round follows a $250 million financing nine months ago at a $1.5 billion valuation.

The funding nearly came apart when Datadog, the listed observability company, tried to buy Upwind, according to a person familiar with the company cited by TechCrunch. Datadog did not offer a premium over the $3.8 billion valuation set by the venture round, and Upwind chose to stay independent. The startup is adding $20 million to $30 million of annualized recurring revenue each quarter and is projected to end the year at roughly $90 million, which puts the valuation at more than 40 times forecast recurring revenue.
