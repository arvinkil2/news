Targa Resources is in talks to acquire Kinetik Holdings and could finalise a deal as soon as next week, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, though no final decision has been made and Kinetik could remain independent or attract a separate bidder. Kinetik shares jumped roughly 11.5 percent in after-hours trading on the report. Kinetik, part-owned by Blackstone, operates gas gathering and processing infrastructure in the Permian Basin of West Texas and New Mexico, along with crude oil and water gathering assets, and has a market value of about $8.9 billion against Targa's $61.5 billion. Neither company has commented, and no price or deal structure has been reported.
