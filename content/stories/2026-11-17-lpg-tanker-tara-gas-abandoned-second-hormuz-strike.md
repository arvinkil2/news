The Panama-flagged LPG tanker Tara Gas was attacked at about 1900 UTC on Monday some seven nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, an attack that disabled the vessel and started an engine-room fire, UK Maritime Trade Operations reported. A maritime security agency reported a second strike on the vessel on Tuesday, causing another fire, and its crew of 25 abandoned ship off the Musandam Peninsula. India's shipping minister Sarbananda Sonowal identified the vessel and said it had been targeted in a missile attack on November 16.

India's Ministry of External Affairs said 21 of the 24 Indian nationals aboard had been brought ashore at Khasab and two injured crew members had been admitted to hospital there, while Sonowal said 24 crew members had been accounted for and one Indian seafarer remained missing, with search and rescue operations continuing. Reuters, citing Oman's Maritime Security Center and maritime security sources, reported that Oman evacuated 24 crew members after the vessel caught fire following a projectile strike. The tanker's owner is listed in databases as Global Gas, based in the Marshall Islands, and could not be reached for comment.

A separate tanker was struck by an unidentified projectile while transiting outbound through the strait early on Monday, also suffering an engine-room fire, with all its crew accounted for, UKMTO reported. Responsibility for either attack has not been independently established.
