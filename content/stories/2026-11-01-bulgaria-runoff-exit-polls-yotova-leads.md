Latest, Sunday evening: Andrey Gyurov has conceded defeat in the presidential runoff, thanking voters, congratulating the winners and calling for unity rather than division, the Novinite agency reported. Bulgarian National Radio put Yotova and her vice-presidential candidate Kiril Vylchev on 58.2 percent of the vote after 100 percent of ballots had been counted, against 38.6 percent for Gyurov, a wider margin than the exit polls published earlier in the evening had suggested.

Exit polls after Sunday's presidential runoff in Bulgaria put acting President Iliana Yotova ahead with 50.7 to 52.5 percent of the vote, against 43.4 to 44 percent for Andrey Gyurov, the former interim prime minister backed by pro-Western opposition parties. Yotova, who led the October 25 first round by a narrow margin, opposes military aid to Ukraine and presents herself as a president of peace, while Gyurov has voiced strong support for Kyiv. If official results confirm the outcome, Yotova would become the first woman elected president of Bulgaria.
