People's Bank of China governor Pan Gongsheng used the Bangkok meetings to argue that global imbalances cannot be resolved through exchange rate adjustments alone and to pledge wider foreign access to Chinese financial markets, wider international acceptance of yuan denominated bonds as collateral, and expanded offshore yuan trading, Reuters reported from the sidelines of the annual meetings. The remarks are Beijing's answer to the imbalance pressure led by Washington this week, and the collateral pledge is the operational part: yuan bonds that global investors can repo are yuan bonds they can hold in size.

The Federal Reserve terminated its enforcement actions against Gardner Bancshares and Small Business Bank of Lenexa, Kansas, ending cease and desist orders from September 2023 and November 2024 and a prompt corrective action from June 2026, all terminated on October 13. It is a small bank cleanup with no read across to system funding conditions, but it closes one of the few live prompt corrective actions in the system.

In South Africa, the Reserve Bank's Prudential Authority amended the designation of Discovery Insurance Group, a supervisory notice with no change to the policy rate or to rand funding conditions. In India, the Reserve Bank set the premature redemption price for the 2020-21 Series VII sovereign gold bond, due October 19, at 14,614 rupees per unit, based on the average gold closing price of October 14 to 16. Japan's Ministry of Finance scheduled the next Treasury discount bill auction for October 23, and the Bank of Japan published the summary of its October meeting on market operations, the routine plumbing through which its reduced JGB purchase path is run.

Two of Friday's largest rates items are not repeated here because this site published them in full on October 16: the final fiscal 2026 US deficit of 1.992 trillion dollars from the Monthly Treasury Statement, and the August Treasury International Capital data showing a net 300.7 billion dollar foreign purchase of long term US securities. India's October 16 government stock auction, which cleared the 2036 bond at 7.3955 percent, was also published in full yesterday.
