Ukraine will need 115 billion euros ($125 billion) in external financial support in 2027, President Volodymyr Zelenskiy told his top regional officials on Tuesday, according to Reuters. Zelenskiy said the government had already secured about 90 billion euros in commitments from the European Union for next year and was holding talks on where to find the remaining 25 billion euros.

The EU is now Ukraine's main financial supporter. Last week it announced a plan to borrow another 45 billion euros to support Ukraine's economy and war effort, on top of a separate 45 billion euros that formed part of 90 billion euros in financing approved in December last year for this year and next. "We need to find another 25 billion euros. I am confident that we will accomplish this task," Zelenskiy said.
