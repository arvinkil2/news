Chinese artificial intelligence stocks have lost a combined .7 trillion in market value since late June, reversing the first half's gains across the Hang Seng Tech, ChiNext and Star 50 indexes, all three of which are now negative for 2026. The Hang Seng Tech Index has fallen to a two-year low and the ChiNext is down 31 percent this year after record highs in the summer. The report ties the rout to stretched valuations and to tighter United States technology restrictions, including proposed rules that would bar Chinese companies from reaching advanced Nvidia processors through overseas data centres; Tencent has warned that chip shortages could delay its next-generation HY4 model, and Alibaba expects cloud revenue growth to slow to between 30 and 45 percent next year before recovering above 50 percent in 2028.
