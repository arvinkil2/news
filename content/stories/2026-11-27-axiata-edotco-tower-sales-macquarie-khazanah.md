Axiata Group is breaking up its Edotco tower business in a set of transactions the company values at 11.8 billion ringgit ($2.9 billion), generating gross proceeds of about 2.2 billion ringgit ($533 million) that Axiata says will strengthen its financial position. Edotco's businesses in the Philippines, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Pakistan and Cambodia will all move to new ownership; Macquarie confirmed it has led a $1.2 billion consortium that signed binding agreements across the five markets, with its fund taking the Philippine unit of about 3,200 towers.

In Malaysia, Axiata will sell an approximately 15.645 percent interest in Edotco Malaysia to sovereign fund Khazanah Nasional, leaving each holding about 47.355 percent with joint control of the country's largest tower platform. Edotco operates more than 55,000 towers across seven Asian markets in total.
