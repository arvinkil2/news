American Express is cutting about 5 percent of its global workforce, CNBC reported on Friday after the company released third-quarter results, in a restructuring it is taking a 336 million dollar charge for in the quarter, a charge of the kind that typically covers severance. The company had almost 77,000 employees at the end of 2025 and did not give an absolute number of jobs to go in the report.

Chief Executive Stephen Squeri told the earnings call the company had good momentum but needed to become more agile and efficient to accelerate innovation and win over the next few years. Chief Financial Officer Christophe Le Caillec told CNBC the aim was to make Amex leaner and more agile and also to prepare for AI, reducing layers and increasing spans of control so the company can automate and take advantage of AI in the coming quarters and years.
