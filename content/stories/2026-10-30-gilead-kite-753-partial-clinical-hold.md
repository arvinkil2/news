Gilead Sciences has placed a partial clinical hold on two trials of KITE-753, its anti-CD19/CD20 CAR-T cell therapy in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, the company said on its third quarter earnings call on October 29. The held studies are the phase 2 second-line PALISADES-1 and the phase 3 PALISADES-2 in third-line and later relapsed or refractory disease. A spokesperson told Fierce Biotech that after an initial voluntary pause the company aligned with the FDA on the partial hold to review the safety data.

Chief medical officer Dietmar Berger said the trials were paused to investigate safety differences noted between phase 1 and phase 2, and Kite head Cindy Perettie pointed specifically to grade 3 and grade 4 ICANS, the immune effector cell neurotoxicity syndrome, emerging in a small number of patients. Perettie said there were no deaths and no grade 5 adverse events. No restart date was given.
