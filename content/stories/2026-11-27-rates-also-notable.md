German employment edged lower in October. About 45.69 million people resident in Germany were in employment, down 21,000 on a seasonally adjusted basis from September and 0.5 percent lower than a year earlier, Destatis reported in press release No. 412 on November 27. The adjusted unemployment rate was unchanged at 4.1 percent, with 1.78 million people unemployed on that basis. Employment has fallen by an average of 18,000 a month from January to September, a slow erosion that sits behind the euro area's weak growth data.

Turkey's economic confidence index rose 1.1 percent in November to 98.4, from 97.3 in October, TurkStat reported. Consumer confidence rose 2.4 percent to 86.8, retail trade confidence rose 0.9 percent to 113.6 and services rose 0.6 percent to 109.9, while construction confidence fell 3.8 percent to 80.2. The index remains below the 100 level that separates optimism from pessimism.

India's foreign exchange reserves rose from 691.11 billion dollars to 737.78 billion dollars over April to September 2026, the Reserve Bank of India said in its 47th half-yearly report on reserve management, released this week. The report is a transparency publication rather than a policy signal, but the size of the buffer is the backdrop to the bank's willingness to resist yield levels it dislikes at auction.

The Netherlands' carbon border levy raised an estimated 121.3 million euros in the first half of 2026, Statistics Netherlands reported. The Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism has applied since January 1, 2026 to carbon-intensive imports from outside the EU. Importers paid 35.4 million euros on goods from China alone, 29 percent of the total, and iron and steel accounted for the largest share of costs, followed by aluminium. Only about 800 of more than 11,000 importing businesses exceeded the 50-tonne threshold, while the top 1 percent of importers accounted for 82 percent of levy-liable imports by weight.

Canadian airlines carried 7.5 million passengers in September, up 4.1 percent from a year earlier, Statistics Canada reported, while total aircraft movements at major and selected small airports slipped 0.7 percent to 544,991. Operating revenue for the major carriers rose 22.5 percent to 3.1 billion Canadian dollars, a combination of fuller aircraft and higher yields rather than more flying.
