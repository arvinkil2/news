President Trump said at a campaign rally in South Carolina on Saturday that Audi will invest $4.2 billion to build a car plant in the United States, presenting it as the German brand's first US factory and as evidence that his tariffs are pulling production onshore, Bloomberg and TASS reported. Neither report carried a location, a timeline or a production start, and neither carried a confirmation from Audi or its parent Volkswagen. Audi said in September that it was considering US production, with a decision expected by the end of the year, and Bloomberg reported then that a plant in Georgia was among the options under consideration.
