President Rodrigo Paz decreed a state of emergency in Bolivia on Saturday to confront the intense droughts and floods associated with Super El Niño, which the report describes as the climate phenomenon causing devastation in various parts of the planet this year. The measure will make it possible to mobilise more economic resources and rescue teams at critical points. In a message to the nation, Paz said his government had done enormous work to preserve the environment so that this year's Super El Niño effect would be as small as possible.
