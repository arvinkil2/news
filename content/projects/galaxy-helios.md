<p>Helios is Galaxy Digital's data center campus in Dickens County, West Texas, converted from a Bitcoin mining site Galaxy acquired from Argo Blockchain in December 2022 for about $65 million. CoreWeave is the sole tenant for Helios I: 526 MW of critical IT load across three phases, using 800 MW of gross power, each phase on a 15-year base lease term.</p>
<p>Phase I (133 MW) was completed and fully delivered to CoreWeave by July 2026. Phase II (260 MW) is under construction, financed in July 2026 with $3.507 billion of 9.875 percent senior secured notes due 2031 issued by Galaxy Helios Data Centers II LLC, with data hall deliveries scheduled to begin in the second quarter of 2027. The campus holds 1.63 GW of ERCOT-approved gross power, including a further 830 MW approved in January 2026 that was not under lease at the third quarter of 2026.</p>
