<p>Sopaipilla is the financing structure for Meta's AI data center campus in northeast El Paso, Texas, announced in October 2025 as a $1.5 billion first phase on about 1,000 acres, designed to scale to 1 GW and come online in 2028. Meta raised the planned investment to about $10 billion in March 2026.</p>
<p>In July 2026 Meta placed the campus into a joint venture owned 80 percent by funds managed by BlackRock, through Global Infrastructure Management and HPS Investment Partners, and 20 percent by Meta, with about $14 billion of total development costs. The venture's issuer, Sopaipilla Investor LLC, sold $12.547 billion of 7.534 percent senior secured notes due November 30, 2048, secured by Meta's lease payments. The campus is planned at 960 MW.</p>
