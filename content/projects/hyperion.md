<p>Hyperion is Meta's flagship AI data center campus in Richland Parish, Louisiana, a 2,250-acre site announced in December 2024 at an initially reported cost above $10 billion. In October 2025 Meta moved the campus into a joint venture owned 80 percent by funds managed by Blue Owl Capital and 20 percent by Meta, with about $27 billion of committed development costs; Blue Owl funds contributed about $7 billion in cash and Meta received a one-time distribution of about $3 billion.</p>
<p>The venture's issuer, Beignet Investor LLC, sold $27.294 billion of 6.581 percent senior secured notes due May 30, 2049 in October 2025, the largest single-tranche private debt offering on record. Meta leases the campus under operating leases with a four-year initial term and extension options, backed by a residual value guarantee covering roughly the first 16 years. In July 2026 Meta announced an expansion to 5 GW, taking total planned investment above $50 billion.</p>
