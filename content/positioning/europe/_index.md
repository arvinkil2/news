<h2>ESMA Article 58 venue reports</h2>
<p class="gm-method">Every EU venue files a weekly position report under MiFID II Article 58: long and short by holder category, percent of open interest, and persons per category. Positions are Friday close, published the following week; the register carries each report's own publication timestamp. A missing report means the venue was below the holder and size thresholds that week; it is not a zero. Positions are summed across the register's risk-reducing and other position categories. Where a venue files both a futures-only and a combined report, the futures-only report is used and labelled.</p>
