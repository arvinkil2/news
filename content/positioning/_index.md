<p class="gm-method">Positioning is what regulators and exchanges publish about who holds what: weekly commitments of traders in the United States and Europe, monthly bank participation, exchange member rankings in China, and open interest where no holder split is published. The section is organised by commodity, in the standard breakdown: Energy, Metals, Agriculture and Other. Each commodity page carries every venue that publishes holder data for that commodity, at full held depth, with the history browsable on the page. Position dates and publication dates print per source; the sources update on different days and are never given one combined stamp.</p>
