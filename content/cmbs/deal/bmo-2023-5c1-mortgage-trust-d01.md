Exchangeable component classes carry no balance of their own; their balance sits in the combination class they exchange into. Ratings are the latest held in the dataset for each class and agency, with the current outlook and watch state under the rating; NR means the agency shows the class as not rated, WR that the rating was withdrawn, PIF that the class has been paid in full. A rating marked (prelim.) is a preliminary rating assigned before closing; the rating value is shown as the agency reports it, with the preliminary marker separated from the value. A + after a CUSIP marks a class with companion CUSIPs in the dataset.</p>
