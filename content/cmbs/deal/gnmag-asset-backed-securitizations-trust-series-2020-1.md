<p class="data-vintage">Credit support is computed from the class stack order in the prospectus: the share of the deal's principal balance in classes junior to the class. Interest-only classes carry a notional balance and no principal support figure. Exchangeable component classes carry no balance of their own; their balance sits in the combination class they exchange into. Ratings are the latest held in the dataset for each class and agency; NR means the agency shows the class as not rated, WR that the rating was withdrawn, PIF that the class has been paid in full. A rating marked (prelim.) is a preliminary rating assigned before closing; the rating value is shown as the agency reports it, with the preliminary marker separated from the value. A + after a CUSIP marks a class with companion CUSIPs in the dataset.</p>
